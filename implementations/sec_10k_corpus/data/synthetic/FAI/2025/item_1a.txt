Risks Related to Our Business and Industry

Our profitability depends on the cost of food, beverage, and labor, which can fluctuate due to factors outside our control, including commodity price volatility, supply chain disruptions, and wage inflation. We may not be able to pass increased costs on to guests without reducing demand.

Our results depend on discretionary consumer spending, which is sensitive to macroeconomic conditions, including inflation, interest rates, and consumer confidence. A decline in discretionary spending on dining out could adversely affect our sales across our portfolio of brands.

We depend on a large workforce of restaurant-level team members. Competition for restaurant labor, wage inflation, and minimum wage increases could increase our operating costs or make it more difficult to maintain adequate staffing, which could adversely affect guest service and sales.

Risks Related to Our Financial Position

We had approximately $2,301.6 million of long-term debt outstanding as of fiscal year end 2025. Our level of indebtedness could limit our financial flexibility and increase our vulnerability to adverse changes in interest rates.

Risks Related to Acquisitions and Goodwill

Goodwill represented approximately 14.5% of our total assets as of fiscal year end 2025, reflecting past acquisitions of restaurant brands. A significant impairment of our goodwill, resulting from a deterioration in the performance of one or more of our brands, could adversely affect our results of operations.

Risks Related to Cybersecurity and Information Technology

Our business depends on information technology systems that support our point-of-sale, supply chain, and guest-facing digital ordering and delivery platforms. As described in Item 1C, "Cybersecurity," while we have not experienced a material cybersecurity incident to date, a future incident affecting our systems or those of our third-party service providers could disrupt our operations, compromise guest or employee data, and result in remediation costs, litigation, or reputational harm.

General Risk Factors

Our business is subject to food safety and health-related risks, including foodborne illness incidents, which could harm our reputation and sales even if such incidents are isolated to a single restaurant or brand.

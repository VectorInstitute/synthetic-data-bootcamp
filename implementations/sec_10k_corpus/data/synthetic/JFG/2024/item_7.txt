Overview

Fieldstone Resources Holdings, Inc. is a distributor of industrial and maintenance, repair, and operations products. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, we generated net sales of $5,668.4 million, operating income of $998.5 million (an operating margin of 17.6%), and net income of $795.0 million (a net margin of 14.0%).

Our gross margin of 44.4% reflects the mix of products we distribute and our ability to manage supplier cost and customer pricing. Selling, general and administrative expenses, which include the cost of our embedded sales and service workforce, represented 26.8% of net sales. Our effective tax rate for the year was 20.4%, modestly below the 21% U.S. federal statutory rate.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

We generated $865.0 million of cash from operating activities during 2024, reflecting net income of $795.0 million and depreciation and amortization of $119.9 million, partly offset by changes in working capital. We used $162.0 million of cash in investing activities, primarily capital expenditures. Financing activities used $608.3 million of cash, primarily reflecting $770.7 million of dividends paid to shareholders, partly offset by other financing inflows. Taken together, our cash and cash equivalents increased by $94.7 million during the year.

As of December 31, 2024, we had total debt of approximately $165.3 million and stockholders' equity of $2,911.9 million. We believe our cash flow from operations, together with access to credit on acceptable terms if needed, will be sufficient to fund our operating needs, capital expenditures, and dividends for at least the next twelve months.

Critical Accounting Estimates

Inventory. Inventory, which represented approximately 37.8% of our total assets as of December 31, 2024, is stated at the lower of cost or market. We make estimates regarding excess or obsolete inventory based on historical experience and current market conditions; actual results could differ from these estimates.

Revenue Recognition. We recognize revenue when control of products transfers to the customer, generally upon delivery. For vending and on-site inventory programs, determining the timing of control transfer can require judgment regarding the terms of individual customer arrangements.

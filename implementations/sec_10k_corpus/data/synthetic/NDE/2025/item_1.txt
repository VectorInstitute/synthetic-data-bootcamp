Introduction

Harborview Logistics Corp. ("Harborview," "we," "our," or "us") is a retailer and distributor of automotive replacement parts and accessories, headquartered in Tennessee. We sell automotive hard parts, maintenance items, accessories, and related non-automotive products through a network of retail stores, substantially all of which also serve commercial customers such as repair garages, dealers, service stations, and fleet operators through a commercial delivery program. We also sell through e-commerce channels directly to consumers and to commercial accounts. We do not perform automotive repair or installation services ourselves.

For the fiscal year ended in 2025, we generated net sales of $19,030.6 million. Our business model depends on maintaining broad and deep product availability across our store network and distribution centers, which requires us to carry substantial inventory; inventory represented approximately 39.6% of our total assets as of fiscal year end.

Store Operations

We operate stores across the United States, organized into geographic divisions supported by a network of regional distribution centers that allow us to replenish store inventory frequently, typically multiple times per week. Each store carries a broad assortment of parts and accessories sized to local vehicle populations, supplemented by access to a larger inventory pool at distribution centers and among nearby stores for items not stocked locally.

Commercial Sales Program

A substantial majority of our stores participate in a commercial sales program that provides prompt delivery of parts and commercial credit to repair garages, dealers, service stations, fleet operators, and other commercial accounts. We believe our commercial program is an important driver of revenue growth, as it extends our addressable market beyond walk-in retail customers to the broader vehicle repair and maintenance industry.

Human Capital Resources

As of fiscal year end 2025, we employed a workforce consisting predominantly of store-level personnel, with the remainder employed in distribution centers and corporate support functions. The substantial majority of our store personnel are part-time employees. We do not have any collective bargaining agreements in the United States and believe our relationships with our employees are good. We focus on retention through competitive compensation and benefits, structured training programs, and internal career-advancement paths from part-time store roles into store management and field leadership positions.

Competition

The retail and distribution of automotive parts and accessories is highly competitive. We compete with other national and regional automotive parts retailers, mass merchandisers, online retailers, and automobile dealers that sell original equipment parts. We believe the principal competitive factors in our industry are product availability, store location and convenience, customer service, and price.

Seasonality

Our business is moderately seasonal, with demand for certain parts and accessories, such as those related to vehicle air conditioning and battery failure, historically higher during warmer and colder months, respectively, in the regions where we operate.

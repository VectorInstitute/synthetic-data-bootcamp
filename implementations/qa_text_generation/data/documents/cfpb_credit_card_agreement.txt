Sample Credit Card Agreement (excerpt for bootcamp scaffolding)

1. Definitions
Annual Percentage Rate (APR): The yearly cost of borrowing expressed as a percentage.
Grace Period: The time between the end of a billing cycle and the payment due date.

2. Interest Rates and Interest Charges
Purchase APR: 19.99% for purchases and balance transfers.
Penalty APR: 29.99% may apply after two late payments within six billing cycles.

3. Fees
Annual Fee: $0.
Late Payment Fee: Up to $40 if a payment is received after the due date.
Returned Payment Fee: Up to $40 if a payment is returned unpaid.

4. Billing Rights Summary
If you think your bill is wrong, write to us within 60 days after the first statement
containing the error. Include your name, account number, the dollar amount in question,
and a description of the error.

5. Payment Allocation
Payments above the minimum due are applied first to balances with the highest APR,
then to balances with lower APRs, unless otherwise required by law.

6. Changes to Terms
We may change APRs, fees, and other terms if we provide advance notice as required by law.

SEC Investor Bulletin: Excerpt for Bootcamp Scaffolding

Purpose
This bulletin provides general educational information for investors. It does not
offer personalized investment advice, recommendations to buy or sell securities, or
portfolio management services.

Scope
The bulletin explains common fraud warning signs, including promises of guaranteed
returns, pressure to act immediately, and requests for payment through untraceable methods.

What This Bulletin Does Not Cover
This bulletin does not analyze individual companies, predict stock prices, or tell
investors which securities to purchase or sell. Questions about specific investments
require consultation with a qualified financial professional.

If You Receive a Suspicious Offer
Verify the promoter through official regulatory resources. Do not send money based solely
on unsolicited messages or social media claims.

Disclaimer
The information here is for educational purposes only. The SEC does not endorse any
particular investment product, strategy, or promoter.

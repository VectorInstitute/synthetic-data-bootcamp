Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

The following discussion and analysis of our financial condition and results of operations should be read in conjunction with our consolidated financial statements and related notes included elsewhere in this Annual Report on Form 10-K. For discussion of 2022 items and year-over-year comparisons between 2023 and 2022 that are not included in this 2024 Form 10-K, refer to Item 7. Management’s Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations found in our Form 10-K for the year ended December 31, 2023, filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission.

Executive Overview

Zennov Medical, Inc. is a medical technology leader focused on the development and commercialization of innovative solutions across the patient care continuum. Headquartered in Denver, Colorado, we operate through three reportable segments: Therapeutic Solutions, Diagnostic Systems, and Patient Management. Our mission is to improve clinical outcomes and healthcare efficiency through advanced engineering and digital health integration.

Our financial performance is driven by several key factors, including the volume of elective and emergency surgical procedures, the adoption of our diagnostic platforms, and the expansion of our remote monitoring services. In fiscal 2024, we continued to execute our strategy of high-intensity research and development, investing $923 million to advance our product pipeline. This commitment to innovation is reflected in our revenue growth and the expansion of our gross margin.

During the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, our revenue increased 8.2% to $2,264 million, compared to $2,093 million in fiscal 2023. This growth was primarily driven by increased procedure volumes in our Therapeutic Solutions segment and a growing installed base of diagnostic instruments which generated recurring consumable revenue. Operating income grew 7.6% to $611 million, while net income increased 5.8% to $386 million.

We maintain a strong focus on capital allocation, balancing investments in organic growth with returns to stockholders. In fiscal 2024, we paid $152 million in dividends and utilized $151 million for share repurchases, net of issuances. We ended the year with $68 million in cash and cash equivalents and total assets of $3,077 million.

Results of Operations

Revenue

Consolidated revenue for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, was $2,264 million, an increase of $171 million, or 8.2%, from $2,093 million in fiscal 2023. The growth in revenue was observed across all three of our reportable segments.

In our Therapeutic Solutions segment, revenue growth was supported by the continued recovery of elective surgical procedure volumes in the United States and Europe. Our portfolio of endovascular grafts and specialized catheters experienced strong demand as healthcare providers addressed patient backlogs. Additionally, the introduction of next-generation bio-absorbable implants in our orthopedic line contributed to market share gains in the ambulatory surgery center channel.

The Diagnostic Systems segment benefited from an increase in the utilization of our molecular testing platforms. Our "razor and blade" business model, which focuses on the placement of diagnostic hardware to drive recurring sales of consumable reagents, performed well as clinical laboratories expanded their testing menus for infectious diseases and oncological markers. The imaging division also saw growth, driven by the adoption of optical coherence tomography systems used to guide complex interventional procedures.

Our Patient Management segment, though smaller in total revenue contribution, represented our fastest-growing area. Revenue was driven by the expansion of our remote monitoring subscriber base. The integration of artificial intelligence into our cloud-based platforms has allowed us to offer higher-value data insights to clinicians, supporting the transition toward value-based care models.

Cost of Revenue and Gross Margin

Cost of revenue for fiscal 2024 was $606 million, compared to $584 million in fiscal 2023. This increase of $22 million, or 3.8%, was primarily due to higher sales volumes and increased raw material costs for certain specialized components.

Gross profit increased to $1,658 million in fiscal 2024 from $1,509 million in fiscal 2023. Our gross margin improved to 73.2% in fiscal 2024, compared to 72.1% in fiscal 2023. The 110-basis point expansion in gross margin was primarily driven by:

A favorable shift in product mix toward high-margin diagnostic consumables and digital health subscriptions. Manufacturing efficiencies achieved at our Denver, Colorado, production facilities through the implementation of automated assembly processes. Improved pricing discipline in our Therapeutic Solutions segment, partially offsetting the pricing pressures exerted by large group purchasing organizations.

Research and Development (R&D)

R&D expense for fiscal 2024 was $923 million, an increase of $69 million, or 8.1%, from $854 million in fiscal 2023. As a percentage of revenue, R&D remained consistent at 40.8% in both fiscal 2024 and 2023.

The increase in absolute R&D spending reflects our ongoing commitment to innovation and the high costs associated with clinical evidence generation. Key areas of investment in 2024 included:

Clinical trials for our advanced wound closure devices and next-generation stents. Development of software algorithms for our Patient Management platforms to enhance predictive analytics. Engineering efforts to miniaturize wearable sensors and improve battery life. Regulatory filing costs for new diagnostic assays in international markets.

We believe that our sustained high level of R&D investment is essential to maintaining our competitive position in an industry characterized by rapid technological change.

Selling, General and Administrative (SG&A)

SG&A expense for fiscal 2024 was $124 million, an increase of $37 million, or 42.5%, from $87 million in fiscal 2023. As a percentage of revenue, SG&A increased to 5.5% in fiscal 2024 from 4.2% in fiscal 2023.

The increase in SG&A expenses was primarily driven by:

Expansion of our specialized direct sales force in the United States to support the launch of new therapeutic products. Increased marketing and clinical education initiatives, including participation in major medical congresses. Higher personnel-related costs, including salaries and benefits for our approximately 2,700 employees. Investments in our corporate information technology infrastructure to enhance cybersecurity and data privacy protections.

Operating Income

Operating income for fiscal 2024 was $611 million, an increase of $43 million, or 7.6%, from $568 million in fiscal 2023. Our operating margin was 27.0% in fiscal 2024, compared to 27.1% in fiscal 2023. The slight decrease in operating margin was due to the significant increase in SG&A spending, which offset the improvements in gross margin.

Interest Expense

Interest expense for fiscal 2024 was $59 million, compared to $54 million in fiscal 2023. The increase of $5 million was primarily due to higher average outstanding debt balances. We increased our long-term debt to $1,321 million as of December 31, 2024, compared to $1,201 million at the end of the prior year, to support our strategic investments and capital return programs.

Provision for Income Taxes

The provision for income taxes was $166 million in fiscal 2024, compared to $149 million in fiscal 2023. Our effective tax rate was 30.1% for fiscal 2024, compared to 29.0% for fiscal 2023. The increase in the effective tax rate was primarily due to a shift in the geographic mix of our earnings and changes in the availability of certain tax credits related to our international operations.

Net Income

Net income for fiscal 2024 was $386 million, an increase of $21 million, or 5.8%, from $365 million in fiscal 2023. The growth in net income reflects our strong top-line performance and gross margin expansion, partially offset by higher operating expenses, interest costs, and a higher effective tax rate. Our net margin for fiscal 2024 was 17.0%, compared to 17.4% in fiscal 2023.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

Our primary sources of liquidity are cash flows from operations and borrowings under our debt facilities. As of December 31, 2024, we had $68 million in cash and cash equivalents, an increase of $8 million from $60 million at the end of fiscal 2023.

We believe that our existing cash balances, anticipated cash flows from operations, and available credit will be sufficient to meet our working capital, capital expenditure, and debt service requirements for at least the next twelve months.

Cash Flows from Operating Activities

Net cash provided by operating activities in fiscal 2024 was $451 million. This was driven by net income of $386 million, adjusted for non-cash items including $62 million in depreciation and amortization. Changes in working capital and other items contributed $3 million to operating cash flow.

The management of our working capital remains a key focus. Accounts receivable, net, increased to $340 million at year-end, reflecting higher sales volumes in the fourth quarter. Inventories increased to $149 million to support anticipated demand for new product launches and to mitigate potential supply chain disruptions. Total current liabilities rose to $710 million, primarily due to higher accrued expenses and accounts payable associated with our expanded operations.

Cash Flows from Investing Activities

Net cash used in investing activities in fiscal 2024 was $260 million. This included:

$60 million for the purchase of property and equipment, focused on expanding our manufacturing capacity and upgrading our laboratory facilities. $200 million for other investing activities, net, which primarily included strategic investments in early-stage medical technology companies and the acquisition of intangible assets to bolster our intellectual property portfolio.

Our capital expenditures are aimed at maintaining our specialized production environments, including cleanrooms required for the manufacture of implantable devices in our Therapeutic Solutions segment.

Cash Flows from Financing Activities

Net cash used in financing activities in fiscal 2024 was $183 million. This consisted of:

A net change in long-term debt of $120 million, representing additional borrowings to fund our growth initiatives and stockholder returns. $152 million in dividends paid to our stockholders. $151 million for share repurchases, net of issuances, as part of our ongoing program to return capital to investors and offset dilution from equity compensation.

Debt and Financing Obligations

As of December 31, 2024, our long-term debt was $1,321 million, up from $1,201 million at the end of 2023. This debt provides the necessary capital to support our R&D-intensive business model. We also recorded other non-current liabilities of $88 million. We maintain compliance with all covenants associated with our debt agreements and believe our leverage remains appropriate given our strong operating cash flows.

Critical Accounting Estimates

The preparation of financial statements in conformity with U.S. GAAP requires management to make estimates and assumptions that affect the reported amounts of assets and liabilities. We base our estimates on historical experience and various other assumptions that we believe to be reasonable under the circumstances.

Revenue Recognition

We recognize revenue when control of the promised goods or services is transferred to our customers, in an amount that reflects the consideration we expect to receive. This requires significant judgment, particularly in our Diagnostic Systems segment, where we may enter into multiple-element arrangements that include hardware, consumables, and service contracts. We allocate the transaction price to each performance obligation based on its relative stand-alone selling price. In our Patient Management segment, revenue from monitoring services is recognized over the period the services are provided, requiring estimates of the expected duration of patient monitoring.

Goodwill and Intangible Assets

As of December 31, 2024, we had $490 million in goodwill and $1,614 million in other non-current assets, which includes intangible assets such as patents and trademarks. We test goodwill for impairment annually, or more frequently if events or changes in circumstances indicate that the asset might be impaired. This process involves estimating the fair value of our reporting units using discounted cash flow models. These models require significant assumptions regarding future revenue growth rates, operating margins, and discount rates. If our actual results differ from these assumptions, we may be required to record impairment charges in the future.

Inventory Valuation

Our inventories, which totaled $149 million at the end of fiscal 2024, are stated at the lower of cost or net realizable value. We regularly review inventory quantities on hand and record provisions for excess and obsolete inventory based on our estimates of future product demand and production requirements. The medical technology industry is subject to rapid technological obsolescence; if a new product launch by a competitor renders our existing inventory obsolete more quickly than anticipated, we may incur additional charges.

Income Taxes

We are subject to income taxes in the United States and various international jurisdictions. The determination of our provision for income taxes requires significant judgment. We recognize deferred tax assets and liabilities for the expected future tax consequences of events that have been included in our financial statements or tax returns. We must assess the likelihood that our deferred tax assets will be recovered from future taxable income. If we determine that it is more likely than not that some portion of these assets will not be realized, we must establish a valuation allowance. As of December 31, 2024, our effective tax rate was 30.1%, influenced by our global operations and the various tax laws in the jurisdictions where we operate.

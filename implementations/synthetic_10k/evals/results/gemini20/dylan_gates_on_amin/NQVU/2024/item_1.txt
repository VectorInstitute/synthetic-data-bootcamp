Item 1. Business

BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Novqua Retail, Inc. (collectively with its subsidiaries, the Company, Novqua, we, us, or our) is a leading multi-channel retailer in the consumer discretionary sector. Incorporated in the State of Delaware and headquartered in New York, New York, the Company operates an extensive network of physical retail locations and integrated digital commerce platforms. Our business is built upon a commitment to providing a broad assortment of high-quality merchandise, ranging from apparel and accessories to home furnishings and lifestyle products, at competitive price points.

We aim to create a compelling shopping experience that combines the convenience of modern e-commerce with the tactile and immediate gratification of brick-and-mortar retail. Our strategy focuses on inventory freshness, operational efficiency, and a deep understanding of consumer trends. By leveraging a sophisticated global supply chain and a robust distribution network, we ensure that our product offerings remain relevant to an evolving customer base. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, the Company generated total revenue of $51,238 million.

The Company’s operations are supported by a large-scale infrastructure that includes regional distribution centers, corporate offices, and a dedicated workforce of approximately 57,400 employees. We believe that our scale, brand recognition, and diversified product portfolio position us as a significant participant in the global retail landscape. Our business model is designed to withstand fluctuations in consumer sentiment by offering a value proposition that appeals to a wide range of demographic segments across various geographic regions.

PRINCIPAL PRODUCTS AND SERVICES

Novqua Retail, Inc. offers a diverse array of consumer products across several core categories. Our merchandise selection is curated to meet the daily needs and aspirational desires of our customers, with a focus on quality, style, and value.

Apparel and Accessories This category represents a significant portion of our business and includes a wide variety of clothing for men, women, and children. Our offerings span from basic essentials and activewear to formal attire and seasonal fashion. We complement our apparel lines with a broad selection of accessories, including footwear, handbags, jewelry, and watches. We source these products from a mix of well-known national brands and our own private-label brands, allowing us to offer a unique assortment that distinguishes our stores from competitors.

Home Fashions and Lifestyle Our home category includes a comprehensive range of products designed to enhance the living environment. This includes furniture, bedding, bath textiles, kitchenware, and decorative accents. We also offer seasonal products, such as outdoor living items and holiday-themed decor. By maintaining a high turnover of home merchandise, we provide customers with a sense of discovery during each visit, encouraging frequent repeat traffic.

Beauty and Personal Care We provide an extensive selection of beauty products, including skincare, cosmetics, fragrances, and hair care. Our beauty departments are designed to offer an immersive experience, featuring both established prestige brands and emerging labels. This category is a key driver of customer engagement, particularly among younger demographics who value innovation and trends in personal care.

REPORTABLE SEGMENTS

The Company manages its business through three reportable segments: Novqua North America, Novqua International, and Novqua Direct. This structure allows us to tailor our operational strategies to the specific needs of different markets and channels.

Novqua North America The Novqua North America segment comprises our physical retail operations in the United States and Canada. This is our largest segment and serves as the foundation of our brand identity. Our stores in this segment are typically located in high-traffic areas, including regional malls, lifestyle centers, and urban hubs. These locations are designed with flexible layouts that can be easily reconfigured to highlight seasonal trends or new product launches. We focus on providing a superior in-store experience through attentive customer service and clear visual merchandising.

Novqua International The Novqua International segment includes our retail operations outside of North America, primarily focusing on established and emerging markets in Europe and Asia. We adapt our international store formats and product assortments to align with local consumer preferences, cultural nuances, and sizing standards. This segment is a key component of our long-term growth strategy, as we seek to increase our global market share and capitalize on the rising middle-class population in developing regions. Our international operations benefit from our global sourcing scale while maintaining local relevance.

Novqua Direct Novqua Direct encompasses our digital commerce operations, including our primary e-commerce websites and mobile applications. This segment is integrated with our physical store network to provide a seamless omnichannel experience. Customers can browse our full catalog online, choose from various delivery options, or utilize in-store pickup and return services. We invest heavily in our digital infrastructure to ensure a secure, intuitive, and personalized shopping experience. Novqua Direct also serves as a platform for testing new products and gathering data-driven insights into customer behavior.

CUSTOMERS

Our customer base is highly diversified, reflecting the broad appeal of our product categories and price points. We serve millions of unique customers annually, ranging from budget-conscious families to trend-seeking individuals. Our core customer typically prioritizes a balance of quality and price, seeking branded merchandise at a significant value.

Through our loyalty programs and digital engagement strategies, we have built a substantial database of repeat shoppers. We use advanced analytics to understand purchasing patterns and preferences, allowing us to deliver targeted promotions and personalized recommendations. Our commitment to customer satisfaction is reflected in our liberal return policies and our focus on maintaining a safe and welcoming shopping environment, both in-person and online.

SALES AND MARKETING

The Company employs a multi-faceted marketing strategy designed to drive brand awareness, increase store traffic, and boost digital conversion rates. Our marketing efforts are centered on our value proposition and the breadth of our merchandise assortment.

Brand Positioning We position the Novqua brand as a reliable destination for quality and value. Our advertising campaigns emphasize the "joy of discovery" and the excitement of finding high-end products at accessible prices. We utilize a mix of traditional media, such as television and print, alongside a robust digital presence.

Digital and Social Media Our digital marketing strategy includes search engine optimization, paid search, email marketing, and active engagement on major social media platforms. We collaborate with influencers and content creators to reach younger audiences and showcase our products in lifestyle contexts. Our mobile app serves as a critical touchpoint, providing customers with early access to sales, personalized offers, and a convenient interface for managing their loyalty rewards.

In-Store Promotions Physical retail locations utilize visual merchandising, window displays, and point-of-sale promotions to encourage unplanned purchases and highlight seasonal arrivals. We frequently refresh our store layouts to keep the shopping experience dynamic and engaging.

COMPETITION

The retail industry is intensely competitive and subject to rapid changes in consumer preferences and shopping habits. We compete with a wide range of retailers across various formats.

Department Stores and Specialty Retailers We compete with traditional department stores that offer similar categories of apparel and home goods. These competitors often emphasize brand heritage and high-touch service. We also face competition from specialty retailers that focus on specific niches, such as athletic wear or home decor.

Discount and Off-Price Retailers Our value-oriented pricing puts us in direct competition with discount stores and other off-price retailers. These competitors focus heavily on price leadership and lean operating models. We differentiate ourselves by offering a more curated selection of premium brands and a superior shopping environment.

Online Marketplaces The rise of large-scale online marketplaces has significantly altered the competitive landscape. These entities offer vast selections and rapid delivery. To compete, we leverage our physical store network for immediate product availability and emphasize the tactile experience of in-person shopping, while simultaneously enhancing our own digital capabilities.

We believe our primary competitive advantages include our global sourcing scale, our established brand reputation, our sophisticated logistics network, and our ability to provide a consistent omnichannel experience.

SEASONALITY

Our business is subject to seasonal fluctuations that are typical of the retail industry. A significant portion of our annual revenue and operating income is generated during the fourth quarter, which includes the traditional holiday shopping season. We also experience increased activity during the back-to-school period in the third quarter and during seasonal transitions in the spring.

To manage this seasonality, we carefully plan our inventory levels and staffing requirements. We increase our marketing efforts and adjust our product assortments to align with peak demand periods. Any significant disruption during the fourth quarter, such as adverse weather conditions or economic downturns, could have a disproportionate impact on our annual financial results.

INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

The Company owns and utilizes a variety of intellectual property assets that are essential to our business. These include trademarks, service marks, brand names, and proprietary software.

Trademarks and Brand Names The "Novqua" name and associated logos are registered trademarks in the United States and many international jurisdictions. We also own several private-label brand names that are exclusive to our stores. These trademarks are critical to our brand identity and customer loyalty. We vigorously protect our intellectual property rights against unauthorized use or infringement.

Proprietary Technology We have developed proprietary software systems for inventory management, supply chain optimization, and customer relationship management. These systems provide us with a competitive edge by improving operational efficiency and enabling data-driven decision-making. We also hold various domain names related to our digital commerce operations.

GOVERNMENT REGULATION

Our operations are subject to a wide range of federal, state, local, and international laws and regulations. Compliance with these regulations is a priority for the Company and requires significant resources.

Labor and Employment As a large employer, we are subject to laws governing wages, hours, overtime, benefits, and workplace safety. We are committed to maintaining fair labor practices and providing a safe environment for our employees.

Trade and Sourcing Our global supply chain is subject to customs regulations, import/export controls, and trade agreements. Changes in tariffs or trade policies can impact our sourcing costs and product availability. We work closely with our suppliers to ensure compliance with ethical sourcing standards and labor laws in the countries where our products are manufactured.

Data Privacy and Security We collect and process a significant amount of personal data from our customers and employees. We are subject to data privacy laws such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) in Europe and various state-level privacy acts in the United States. We maintain robust cybersecurity measures to protect this information from unauthorized access or disclosure.

Environmental Regulation Our operations, particularly our logistics and distribution network, are subject to environmental laws regarding waste management, emissions, and energy use. We are increasingly focused on sustainability initiatives to reduce our environmental footprint and comply with evolving climate-related disclosures.

HUMAN CAPITAL

Our employees are the foundation of our success and the primary interface with our customers. As of December 31, 2024, Novqua Retail, Inc. employed about 57,400 people. Our workforce includes full-time and part-time associates across our retail stores, distribution centers, and corporate offices.

Talent Development and Retention We invest in comprehensive training programs to ensure our employees have the skills necessary to provide excellent customer service and operate our facilities safely and efficiently. We offer competitive compensation and benefits packages designed to attract and retain high-quality talent in a competitive labor market.

Diversity and Inclusion We are committed to fostering a diverse and inclusive workplace that reflects the communities we serve. We believe that a variety of perspectives strengthens our organization and enhances our ability to innovate. Our diversity initiatives focus on recruitment, professional development, and creating a culture of belonging for all employees.

Health and Safety The safety of our associates and customers is a top priority. We maintain rigorous safety protocols in our stores and distribution centers, providing regular training and conducting periodic audits to ensure compliance with internal standards and government regulations.

Item 1C. Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity Risk Management and Strategy

Lonnov has developed and integrated a comprehensive cybersecurity risk management program designed to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of our critical information technology systems, intellectual property, and the sensitive data of our customers and partners. As a provider of foundational enterprise software and cloud infrastructure, we recognize that the security of our products and internal environments is paramount to maintaining the trust of our global customer base. Our cybersecurity program is not a standalone function but is deeply embedded within our broader enterprise risk management framework.

We utilize several industry-recognized standards to guide the design and assessment of our cybersecurity program, including the National Institute of Standards and Technology Cybersecurity Framework and the Secure Software Development Framework. These frameworks provide a structured approach for our security teams to identify, protect against, detect, respond to, and recover from cybersecurity threats. Our strategy is built upon a "secure-by-design" philosophy, ensuring that security considerations are integrated into every stage of the software development lifecycle, from initial architectural planning to final deployment and ongoing maintenance.

Our cybersecurity risk management process includes the following key components:

Identification and Assessment: We conduct regular risk assessments to identify potential vulnerabilities within our internal IT environment and our product offerings. These assessments involve automated scanning, internal security audits, and manual penetration testing. We also monitor the evolving threat landscape through internal research and participation in threat intelligence sharing communities.

Product and Cloud Security: Given our focus on hybrid cloud management and software-defined networking, we implement rigorous security controls for our cloud-native services. This includes multi-factor authentication, data encryption at rest and in transit, and network segmentation. We also perform regular security reviews of our software code to identify and remediate potential vulnerabilities before products are released to customers.

Third-Party Risk Management: We rely on various third-party service providers, including cloud hosting partners and hardware suppliers, to support our global operations. We have established a formal third-party risk management program that evaluates the security posture of these vendors before engagement and on a recurring basis thereafter. This process includes security questionnaires, reviews of independent audit reports such as SOC 2, and contractual requirements for data protection and incident notification.

Incident Response and Resilience: We maintain a documented cybersecurity incident response plan that outlines the procedures for responding to and mitigating the impact of security events. This plan includes defined roles and responsibilities, communication protocols, and escalation paths. We conduct regular tabletop exercises and simulations to test the effectiveness of our response capabilities and ensure that our technical teams and senior leadership are prepared to handle a range of scenarios, including ransomware attacks and data breaches.

Employee Awareness and Training: We recognize that our global workforce of approximately 163,900 employees is a critical line of defense. All employees are required to complete mandatory cybersecurity awareness training upon hiring and annually thereafter. This training covers topics such as phishing identification, password hygiene, and the proper handling of sensitive data. We also provide specialized, role-based security training for our software engineers and IT operations personnel.

Continuous Monitoring: Our security operations center provides continuous monitoring of our networks and systems for signs of unauthorized activity. We utilize advanced security information and event management tools to aggregate and analyze logs from across our enterprise, allowing for the rapid detection of and response to potential threats.

We describe whether and how risks from identified cybersecurity threats, including any previous incidents, have materially affected or are reasonably likely to materially affect our business strategy, results of operations, or financial condition in Part I, Item 1A, "Risk Factors," of this Annual Report on Form 10-K.

Cybersecurity Governance

Governance of cybersecurity risks is a critical component of our corporate management structure. Our Board of Directors oversees our cybersecurity risk management program as part of its overall responsibility for risk oversight. The Board has delegated primary responsibility for this oversight to the Audit Committee.

The Audit Committee is responsible for reviewing our cybersecurity policies, the results of internal and external security audits, and management's efforts to monitor and mitigate significant risks. Our Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) and our Chief Technology Officer (CTO) provide quarterly reports to the Audit Committee regarding the state of our cybersecurity program, current threat trends, and progress on key security initiatives. These reports also include updates on any significant security incidents and the status of remediation efforts. In addition to these quarterly updates, the CISO and CTO provide a comprehensive cybersecurity briefing to the full Board of Directors at least annually.

At the management level, our CISO leads our dedicated security organization and is responsible for the day-to-day execution of our cybersecurity strategy. Our CISO reports directly to the CTO, ensuring that security is prioritized alongside our broader technological innovation and product development goals. The CISO is responsible for defining security standards, managing our security operations center, and overseeing our incident response activities.

Our current CISO has served in this role for several years and possesses extensive experience in the information technology and cybersecurity sectors. Prior to joining Lonnov, our CISO held senior leadership positions at other large, publicly traded enterprise software companies and has a proven track record of managing complex security environments at scale. Our CISO’s professional background includes deep expertise in cloud security, regulatory compliance, and threat intelligence.

To further strengthen our governance, we have established a Cybersecurity Risk Committee composed of senior leaders from across the company, including representatives from our legal, internal audit, finance, and engineering departments. This committee meets regularly to discuss emerging risks, review security policies, and ensure that our cybersecurity initiatives are aligned with our business objectives.

In the event of a significant cybersecurity incident, our governance framework provides for immediate escalation to senior management, including the Chief Executive Officer and the Audit Committee. This ensures that the necessary resources are allocated to respond to the incident and that our disclosure obligations are met in a timely and accurate manner. We also maintain relationships with external legal counsel and specialized cybersecurity forensic firms to assist in our response and recovery efforts when necessary.

By maintaining a robust governance structure and a proactive risk management strategy, we aim to protect our enterprise and provide our customers with the secure software and infrastructure solutions they require to operate in an increasingly complex digital world.

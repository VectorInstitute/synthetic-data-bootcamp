Item 1C. Cybersecurity

Risk Management and Strategy

Kator Minerals, Inc. is committed to maintaining a robust cybersecurity risk management program designed to assess, identify, manage, mitigate, and respond to cybersecurity threats and incidents. Recognizing the critical importance of protecting our information systems and data assets, we have developed a comprehensive approach that integrates cybersecurity into our overall enterprise risk management framework. This program is structured to address material risks from cybersecurity threats through a cross-functional strategy, leveraging both internal and external resources.

Our cybersecurity risk management program is grounded in industry best practices and recognized standards for cybersecurity and information technology. We employ a layered portfolio of technology controls, which includes strategic alliances with external cybersecurity service providers to enhance our defensive capabilities. The collaboration with these third-party experts ensures the deployment of advanced security technologies and processes, reinforcing our ability to detect, prevent, and respond to potential cyber threats.

We conduct regular assessments of the cybersecurity threat landscape and our security controls. These assessments involve rigorous audits, vulnerability testing, penetration tests, and simulations, including tabletop exercises involving senior management. To ensure our cybersecurity measures remain effective and current, we engage external consultants to perform independent reviews of our cybersecurity framework annually. These evaluations provide valuable insights into potential vulnerabilities and the overall effectiveness of our security posture.

Governance

Cybersecurity governance at Kator Minerals is a critical component of our risk management strategy, with oversight responsibilities resting with our Board of Directors and designated committees. Our Board and its Audit Committee are actively involved in the oversight of our cybersecurity risk management efforts. The Audit Committee receives regular updates from management on the effectiveness of our information technology security processes, assessment of cybersecurity threats, and the strategies implemented to mitigate associated risks.

The Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) plays a pivotal role in managing our cybersecurity initiatives. Reporting directly to the Chief Information Officer (CIO), the CISO is responsible for the comprehensive protection of our global information systems against cyber threats. This includes developing and implementing cybersecurity policies, overseeing the deployment of protective measures, and managing a dedicated team of cybersecurity professionals. Our CISO brings extensive experience in information security, having previously held senior roles in the technology and cybersecurity sectors.

The CISO collaborates closely with the CIO, who oversees the strategic direction and operational effectiveness of our technology systems. Together, they ensure that cybersecurity considerations are integrated into our technology strategy and operations. The CISO also provides regular reports to the Audit Committee, highlighting key cybersecurity metrics, threat assessments, and ongoing initiatives to strengthen our security posture.

In addition to management oversight, the Board of Directors is periodically briefed on significant cybersecurity incidents and developments. This ensures that the Board is kept informed of the evolving cybersecurity landscape and the measures taken to address potential risks. By maintaining a proactive approach to cybersecurity governance, Kator Minerals is committed to safeguarding its information assets and maintaining the trust of our stakeholders.

Through these comprehensive risk management and governance practices, Kator Minerals aims to mitigate the risks associated with cybersecurity threats, ensuring the integrity, confidentiality, and availability of our information systems and data. We remain dedicated to evolving our cybersecurity strategies in response to emerging threats and technological advancements, reinforcing our commitment to protecting our business operations and stakeholder interests.

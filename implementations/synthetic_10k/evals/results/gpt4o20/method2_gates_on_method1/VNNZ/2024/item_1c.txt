Item 1C. Cybersecurity

Venzen Industries, Inc. ("Venzen") recognizes the critical importance of cybersecurity in safeguarding our operations, intellectual property, and customer data. As a leading entity in the Industrials sector, we are committed to implementing a robust cybersecurity program that addresses the evolving landscape of cyber threats and ensures the resilience and integrity of our information systems.

Cybersecurity Risk Management and Strategy

Our cybersecurity strategy is built upon a comprehensive risk management framework that is designed to prevent, detect, and respond to cybersecurity threats. This framework is aligned with industry best practices and includes a set of controls to mitigate risks associated with our digital environment. Central to our strategy is the implementation of continuous monitoring, advanced threat detection, and incident response protocols.

We employ a multi-layered approach to cybersecurity, leveraging state-of-the-art technology solutions to protect our critical assets. This includes the use of proactive detection tools, such as intrusion detection systems, firewalls, and endpoint protection solutions, to identify and neutralize potential threats before they can impact our operations. Our cybersecurity infrastructure also incorporates threat intelligence capabilities, enabling us to analyze both internal and external threat data to enhance our situational awareness and preparedness.

In addition to technological measures, we conduct regular internal assessments of our cybersecurity posture, benchmarking against established frameworks such as the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) Cybersecurity Framework. These assessments help us identify potential vulnerabilities and drive continuous improvement in our security measures. We also engage independent third-party auditors to evaluate our cybersecurity program and practices, ensuring that our risk management strategies are aligned with current industry standards and regulatory requirements.

Recognizing the importance of a well-prepared workforce in mitigating cybersecurity risks, we invest in ongoing training and awareness programs for our employees. These initiatives are designed to educate staff about potential cyber threats, phishing attacks, and the importance of adhering to security protocols. By fostering a culture of cybersecurity awareness, we empower our employees to act as the first line of defense against cyber incidents.

Governance and Oversight

Venzen's cybersecurity governance structure is designed to provide comprehensive oversight and ensure accountability at all levels of the organization. Our Board of Directors plays a pivotal role in overseeing cybersecurity risk management, receiving regular updates from our Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) and cybersecurity team on emerging threats, incidents, and the effectiveness of our security measures.

The Audit Committee of the Board provides additional oversight, focusing on the adequacy and effectiveness of our cybersecurity controls and practices. The Committee reviews management's actions in response to identified risks and ensures that appropriate resources are allocated to maintain and enhance our cybersecurity posture.

Our CISO, who possesses extensive experience in cybersecurity and information technology, leads the development and implementation of our cybersecurity strategy. The CISO is responsible for managing day-to-day cybersecurity operations and reports directly to the Chief Information Officer (CIO). This reporting structure facilitates seamless communication and alignment between our cybersecurity efforts and broader IT initiatives.

The CISO is supported by a dedicated cybersecurity team, which includes experts in risk assessment, threat intelligence, and incident response. This team collaborates closely with other departments, such as IT, legal, and compliance, to ensure that cybersecurity considerations are integrated into all aspects of our operations and decision-making processes.

To further strengthen our governance framework, we have established an Enterprise Risk Management Committee, a cross-functional group responsible for monitoring and mitigating risks, including cybersecurity risks, that could impact our strategic objectives. The Committee provides guidance on integrating risk intelligence with business processes, such as strategic planning and investment allocation, to ensure that cybersecurity risks are consistently considered in decision-making.

Additionally, we maintain an Enterprise Cybersecurity Governance Committee, which meets regularly to discuss cybersecurity initiatives, known and emerging issues, and program updates. This Committee includes representatives from senior management and is tasked with ensuring that our cybersecurity strategies are aligned with our business objectives and regulatory obligations.

Third-Party Risk Management

Given the interconnected nature of modern business operations, we recognize the importance of managing cybersecurity risks associated with third-party vendors and partners. Our third-party risk management program evaluates the security posture of vendors that access or host our data and systems, ensuring alignment with our security standards.

The program includes an initial review of third-party security practices, ongoing monitoring, and the establishment of contractual agreements that incorporate cybersecurity requirements. By maintaining rigorous standards for third-party services, we aim to mitigate the risks associated with outsourcing and ensure that our partners uphold the same level of security as our internal operations.

Conclusion

Venzen Industries, Inc. is committed to maintaining a strong cybersecurity program that protects our information assets and supports our business objectives. Through continuous improvement, strategic investments in technology, and a focus on governance and risk management, we strive to safeguard our operations against the ever-evolving landscape of cyber threats. Our Board of Directors, executive management, and dedicated cybersecurity team remain actively engaged in enhancing our cybersecurity posture and ensuring that we are well-prepared to address current and future challenges.

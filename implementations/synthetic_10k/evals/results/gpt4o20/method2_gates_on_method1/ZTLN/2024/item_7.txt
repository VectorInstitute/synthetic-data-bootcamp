Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Overview

This Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations ("MD&A") is intended to provide readers with a comprehensive understanding of the financial performance and condition of Zenaltal Foods, Inc. ("Zenaltal" or the "Company") for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024 ("fiscal 2024"). The discussion should be read in conjunction with the Company's Consolidated Financial Statements and the accompanying notes. Our analysis focuses on the primary factors that have influenced Zenaltal's financial results, highlighting key trends and drivers of performance. We also address the liquidity and capital resources available to the Company and discuss critical accounting estimates that play a pivotal role in financial reporting.

In fiscal 2024, Zenaltal Foods continued to operate within the dynamic landscape of the Consumer Staples sector, characterized by changing consumer preferences and intense competition. The Company faced a challenging environment marked by a decrease in revenue, yet successfully improved net income and operating income compared to the previous fiscal year. Key strategic initiatives, including cost management and operational efficiency improvements, enabled Zenaltal to navigate these challenges effectively. Despite the 6.7% decline in revenue, the Company achieved a significant increase in net income of 120.0%.

Results of Operations

Revenue

Zenaltal Foods reported revenue of $15,185 million in fiscal 2024, which represents a decrease of $1,097 million or 6.7% compared to $16,282 million in fiscal 2023. This decline in revenue was primarily driven by a contraction in consumer spending within certain product categories and heightened competitive pressures in the market. Despite efforts to introduce innovative products and leverage our robust distribution network, shifts in consumer preferences and economic factors impacted overall sales performance. The Company remains focused on adapting its product mix and marketing strategies to address these evolving consumer demands.

Cost of Revenue and Gross Margin

The cost of revenue for fiscal 2024 was $10,552 million, a decrease from $11,321 million in fiscal 2023. This reduction in cost of revenue by $769 million reflects our strategic initiatives aimed at optimizing production processes and supply chain efficiencies. These efforts enabled Zenaltal to mitigate some of the adverse effects of declining revenue. The gross profit for fiscal 2024 was $4,633 million, compared to $4,961 million in fiscal 2023. Despite the decrease in gross profit, the gross margin remained consistent at 30.5% for both fiscal years, indicating effective cost management and pricing strategies.

Operating Expenses

Research and Development

Research and development ("R&D") expenses decreased to $91 million in fiscal 2024 from $128 million in fiscal 2023. This reduction aligns with Zenaltal's focused approach to streamline its innovation pipeline, concentrating on high-impact projects that directly contribute to consumer needs and market trends. As a share of revenue, R&D expenses represented 0.6% in fiscal 2024, compared to 0.8% in the prior year.

Selling, General and Administrative

Selling, general, and administrative ("SG&A") expenses were $3,994 million in fiscal 2024, down from $4,250 million in fiscal 2023. This decrease of $256 million was achieved through strategic cost-cutting measures and operational efficiencies. As a percentage of revenue, SG&A expenses were 26.3% in fiscal 2024, slightly higher than 26.1% in fiscal 2023, reflecting the Company's ongoing investment in marketing and brand-building activities.

Other Operating Expense

Other operating expenses for fiscal 2024 were reported as $0, a significant reduction from $188 million in fiscal 2023. The absence of other operating expenses in fiscal 2024 reflects the Company's successful efforts to eliminate non-recurring charges associated with restructuring and strategic repositioning initiatives that were completed in the prior year.

Operating Income

Operating income for fiscal 2024 was $548 million, an increase of $153 million or 38.7% from $395 million in fiscal 2023. This improvement in operating income is attributed to the Company's disciplined cost management strategies and operational efficiencies that offset the decline in revenue. The operating margin improved to 3.6% in fiscal 2024 from 2.4% in fiscal 2023, demonstrating Zenaltal's ability to enhance profitability despite revenue headwinds.

Interest Expense

Interest expense for fiscal 2024 was $240 million, a decrease from $256 million in fiscal 2023. The reduction in interest expense is primarily due to the Company's strategic initiatives to manage its debt portfolio more effectively, including refinancing activities that lowered interest rates and reduced overall interest obligations.

Effective Tax Rate

The provision for income taxes was $66 million in fiscal 2024, compared to $29 million in fiscal 2023. The effective tax rate for fiscal 2024 was 21.4%, up from 20.9% in the prior year. This slight increase reflects changes in the Company's geographic earnings mix and the impact of certain non-deductible expenses.

Net Income

Net income for fiscal 2024 was $242 million, a significant increase from $110 million in fiscal 2023. The 120.0% growth in net income underscores the effectiveness of Zenaltal's strategic focus on cost management and efficiency improvements, coupled with a lower interest expense burden. The net margin improved to 1.6% in fiscal 2024, compared to 0.7% in fiscal 2023, reflecting the Company's enhanced profitability.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

Cash and Cash Equivalents

As of December 31, 2024, Zenaltal Foods held cash and cash equivalents of $1,747 million, slightly down from $1,764 million at the end of fiscal 2023. The minimal change in cash position indicates stable cash management practices amidst challenging market conditions.

Operating Activities

Net cash provided by operating activities was $722 million in fiscal 2024, reflecting the Company's robust cash flow generation capabilities. This performance was driven by improved net income, effective working capital management, and disciplined cost control measures. The positive cash flow from operations underscores Zenaltal's ability to fund its operational needs and strategic initiatives.

Investing Activities

Net cash used in investing activities was $274 million in fiscal 2024. This includes capital expenditures of $516 million, primarily related to investments in property, plant, and equipment to support production capacity and efficiency improvements. Other investing activities, net, provided $790 million, reflecting proceeds from asset divestitures and strategic investments. Zenaltal remains committed to aligning its capital investments with long-term growth objectives.

Financing Activities

Net cash used in financing activities totaled $1,013 million in fiscal 2024. The Company repaid $349 million of long-term debt, paid dividends amounting to $197 million, and executed share repurchases, net of issuances, of $467 million. These actions highlight Zenaltal's commitment to returning value to shareholders while maintaining a prudent capital structure.

Capital Expenditures

Capital expenditures for fiscal 2024 were $516 million, reflecting the Company's focus on enhancing production capabilities and operational efficiencies. These investments are strategically aligned with Zenaltal's objectives to support product innovation and meet evolving consumer demands.

Debt

Zenaltal's long-term debt decreased to $5,341 million as of December 31, 2024, from $5,690 million at the end of fiscal 2023. The reduction in debt levels is consistent with the Company's strategy to strengthen its balance sheet and reduce financial leverage. Zenaltal continues to evaluate opportunities for refinancing and optimizing its capital structure to support future growth.

Dividends and Share Repurchases

During fiscal 2024, Zenaltal declared and paid dividends totaling $197 million, reflecting the Company's commitment to providing consistent returns to its shareholders. Additionally, the Company engaged in share repurchases, net of issuances, amounting to $467 million, demonstrating confidence in its long-term growth prospects and a focus on enhancing shareholder value.

Critical Accounting Estimates

Zenaltal's Consolidated Financial Statements are prepared in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States ("GAAP"). The preparation of these financial statements requires management to make estimates and assumptions that affect the reported amounts of assets, liabilities, revenue, and expenses. The following critical accounting estimates have a significant impact on the Company's financial statements:

Revenue Recognition

Revenue recognition requires management to make judgments about the timing and amount of revenue to be recognized. Zenaltal recognizes revenue when control of the promised goods is transferred to customers, which typically occurs upon delivery. The Company considers various factors in determining revenue recognition, including the nature of its contracts with customers, the timing of delivery, and the evaluation of variable consideration components.

Inventory Valuation

Inventory is stated at the lower of cost or net realizable value. Management exercises judgment in determining the net realizable value of inventory, considering factors such as market demand, product life cycles, and changes in consumer preferences. Zenaltal regularly reviews its inventory levels and adjusts the carrying value to reflect potential obsolescence or excess inventory.

Goodwill and Intangible Assets

Zenaltal assesses the recoverability of goodwill and intangible assets annually, or more frequently if events or changes in circumstances indicate potential impairment. The evaluation requires management to estimate the fair value of reporting units and intangible assets, considering factors such as future cash flows, discount rates, and market conditions. Changes in these estimates could result in impairment charges that impact the Company's financial results.

Income Taxes

The provision for income taxes involves significant judgment in determining the Company's tax positions and the realizability of deferred tax assets. Zenaltal evaluates the likelihood of recovering deferred tax assets and establishes valuation allowances when necessary. The Company's effective tax rate and deferred tax balances may be affected by changes in tax laws, interpretations, and the resolution of tax audits or disputes.

In summary, Zenaltal Foods, Inc. demonstrated resilience and adaptability during fiscal 2024, navigating a challenging environment with strategic focus and operational discipline. Despite a decline in revenue, the Company achieved significant improvements in profitability and cash flow generation. Zenaltal remains committed to executing its strategic initiatives, driving operational efficiencies, and investing in growth opportunities to enhance shareholder value and maintain its leadership position in the Consumer Staples sector.

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

This discussion contains forward-looking statements that reflect our plans, estimates, beliefs, and expected performance. Actual results and the timing of events may differ materially from those contained in these forward-looking statements due to various factors discussed further in Item 1A. Risk Factors and Cautionary Statement Regarding Forward-Looking Statements of this report.

Overview

Draor Midstream, Inc. ("Draor" or the "Company") is a leading company in the energy sector, focusing on midstream services, which include the transportation, storage, and distribution of energy resources such as natural gas, crude oil, and natural gas liquids (NGLs). Our operations are primarily centered on connecting energy producers with end-users efficiently and reliably through our extensive infrastructure network. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, Draor reported substantial revenue growth, highlighting our robust position in the energy market.

Results of Operations

Revenue

For the fiscal year 2024, Draor generated total revenue of $134,874 million, marking a 7.2% increase from $125,839 million in fiscal 2023. This growth was primarily driven by an increase in demand for midstream services amid favorable market conditions and strategic expansion of our pipeline and storage infrastructure. Enhanced operational efficiencies and strengthened customer relationships further contributed to this revenue growth.

Cost of Revenue

The cost of revenue for fiscal 2024 was $123,572 million, compared to $114,074 million in fiscal 2023. This represents an increase in costs commensurate with the rise in revenue, reflecting higher volumes transported and stored, which required greater operational resources. We also faced increased maintenance and operational costs due to the expansion of our infrastructure and rising energy prices, which impacted the expenses associated with our services.

Gross Margin

Draor’s gross profit for fiscal 2024 was $11,302 million, a slight decrease from $11,765 million in fiscal 2023. The gross margin decreased from 9.3% in fiscal 2023 to 8.4% in fiscal 2024. This decline in margin is attributable to the increased cost of revenue outpacing revenue growth, driven by heightened operational expenditures required to maintain and enhance our infrastructure under increased demand conditions.

Selling, General and Administrative Expenses

Selling, general and administrative (SG&A) expenses decreased to $2,724 million in fiscal 2024 from $2,942 million in fiscal 2023. This reduction reflects our continued efforts to streamline administrative processes and implement cost-saving initiatives across our operations, despite an increase in business activities and expansion efforts.

Other Operating Expense

Other operating expenses were significantly reduced in fiscal 2024, amounting to $1,322 million compared to $3,427 million in fiscal 2023. This decrease resulted from the conclusion of certain non-recurring expenses incurred in the previous fiscal year, including restructuring costs and expenses related to asset divestitures.

Operating Income

Operating income for fiscal 2024 rose to $7,256 million from $5,396 million in fiscal 2023, a substantial increase of 34.5%. This improvement was driven by the combination of revenue growth and effective cost management, particularly in reducing SG&A and other operating expenses. Our focus on enhancing operational efficiency and strategic resource allocation significantly contributed to this positive outcome.

Interest Expense

Interest expense increased slightly to $9,779 million in fiscal 2024 from $9,592 million in fiscal 2023. This increase is primarily due to higher average debt levels as we financed part of our infrastructure expansion and strategic investments through additional borrowings. Despite the increase in interest expenses, we maintained a stable debt management strategy to support our growth initiatives.

Income Before Income Taxes and Net Income

Draor reported a loss before income taxes of $2,523 million for fiscal 2024, compared to a loss of $4,196 million in fiscal 2023. The reduction in pre-tax loss reflects improved operating income and cost control measures. Correspondingly, net income for fiscal 2024 was a loss of $2,523 million, improving by 39.9% from a net loss of $4,196 million in fiscal 2023. The effective tax rate remained at 0.0% for both fiscal years due to the utilization of tax loss carryforwards and other strategic tax planning measures.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

Cash and Cash Equivalents

Cash and cash equivalents increased to $32,169 million as of December 31, 2024, from $29,449 million as of December 31, 2023. This increase in cash reserves was primarily driven by improved cash flows from operating activities, despite significant investments in infrastructure expansion and strategic initiatives.

Operating Activities

Net cash provided by operating activities was $17,883 million for fiscal 2024. This positive cash flow reflects our enhanced operating income and rigorous working capital management, alongside a focus on optimizing operational efficiency. Despite a challenging environment, we successfully managed to convert a substantial portion of our revenue into operating cash flow.

Investing Activities

Net cash used in investing activities was $29,691 million in fiscal 2024. This outflow was primarily due to significant capital expenditures totaling $12,800 million for the expansion of our pipeline and storage infrastructure. Additionally, other investing activities, net, accounted for $16,891 million, reflecting our strategic investments in technology and asset acquisitions aimed at bolstering our market position.

Financing Activities

Net cash used in financing activities was $14,528 million for fiscal 2024. This included net share repurchases of $10,378 million, reflecting our commitment to returning value to shareholders. Furthermore, we raised $4,150 million through new long-term debt to finance part of our expansion activities. We did not declare or pay dividends during the fiscal year, aligning with our strategy to reinvest earnings into growth opportunities and strengthen our financial position.

Capital Expenditures

Capital expenditures for fiscal 2024 amounted to $12,800 million, reflecting our aggressive investment in expanding and upgrading our infrastructure to meet increasing demand. These investments are critical to sustaining our operational capabilities and enhancing our service offerings across the energy value chain.

Debt Management

As of December 31, 2024, our long-term debt stood at $217,306 million, up from $213,156 million at the end of fiscal 2023. This increase reflects our strategic borrowing to fund infrastructure projects and support our growth objectives while maintaining a prudent balance between debt and equity financing.

Share Repurchases

During fiscal 2024, we repurchased shares amounting to $10,378 million, net of issuances. This action demonstrates our commitment to optimizing our capital structure and enhancing shareholder value, while also reflecting confidence in our long-term growth prospects.

Critical Accounting Estimates

Our financial statements are prepared in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP) in the United States, which require us to make estimates and assumptions that affect the reported amounts of assets, liabilities, revenues, and expenses. Our critical accounting estimates are those that are most important to the portrayal of our financial condition and results of operations and require our most difficult, subjective, or complex judgments, often as a result of the need to make estimates about the effect of matters that are inherently uncertain.

Impairment of Long-Lived Assets

We evaluate long-lived assets for impairment when events or changes in circumstances indicate that the carrying amount of an asset may not be recoverable. Our evaluation of long-lived assets requires significant judgment in determining whether indicators of impairment exist and in estimating future cash flows, useful lives, and residual values. Changes in these estimates could materially affect the amounts reported in our financial statements.

Fair Value Measurements

Certain assets and liabilities are reported at fair value in our financial statements. Determining fair value requires significant judgment and the use of estimates, particularly when observable market data is not available. We use various valuation techniques, including market, income, and cost approaches, to estimate fair value. Changes in these estimates could have a significant impact on our financial position and results of operations.

Income Taxes

We are subject to income taxes in various jurisdictions, and significant judgment is required in determining our provision for income taxes. We recognize the effects of uncertain tax positions when it is more likely than not, based on the technical merits, that the position will be sustained upon examination. Changes in tax laws and interpretations, as well as changes in estimated future taxable income, could affect the amounts recorded for income taxes in our financial statements.

In conclusion, Draor Midstream, Inc. experienced significant growth and operational improvements in fiscal 2024, driven by strategic investments and rigorous cost management. Our focus on expanding our infrastructure and optimizing our operations has positioned us well for future growth, despite the challenges inherent in the energy sector. We remain committed to enhancing shareholder value while maintaining a strong financial position and adhering to the highest standards of operational excellence.

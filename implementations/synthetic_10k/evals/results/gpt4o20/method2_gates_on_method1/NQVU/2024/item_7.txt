Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

The following discussion and analysis of our financial condition and results of operations should be read in conjunction with the consolidated financial statements and related notes thereto included elsewhere in this Annual Report on Form 10-K. This discussion contains forward-looking statements that involve risks and uncertainties. Factors that could cause or contribute to such differences include those identified below and those discussed in the section titled “Risk Factors” and other sections of this Annual Report on Form 10-K. Our historical results are not necessarily indicative of the results that may be expected for any period in the future.

In addition, this “Management’s Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations” section generally discusses 2024 and 2023 items and year-to-year comparisons between 2024 and 2023.

Overview

Novqua Retail, Inc. is a prominent player in the consumer discretionary sector, focused on providing quality merchandise across a network of physical stores and an expanding online platform. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, we reported total revenue of $51,238 million, a decrease of 1.4% from $51,976 million in 2023. This slight decline reflects the challenging retail environment and competitive pressures, as well as shifts in consumer spending patterns. Despite these challenges, we remain committed to delivering exceptional value to our customers, supported by a diverse product offering and strategic market positioning.

Our gross margin for 2024 was 29.3%, down from 30.3% in 2023, primarily due to increased competitive pricing strategies and changes in product mix. We continue to focus on maintaining operational efficiencies and cost management to support our profitability objectives. Our operating income for 2024 was $4,155 million, reflecting a decrease of 10.8% from $4,659 million in 2023, primarily driven by a reduction in gross profit and targeted investments in our selling, general, and administrative functions.

Results of Operations

Revenue

For the fiscal year 2024, revenue decreased by 1.4% to $51,238 million from $51,976 million in 2023. This decline was influenced by several factors, including heightened competition in the retail sector and evolving consumer preferences towards e-commerce platforms. While we have made significant strides in expanding our online presence, the transition from traditional retail formats to digital channels continues to be a focal point of our strategic initiatives.

Cost of Revenue and Gross Margin

Cost of revenue remained relatively stable, decreasing slightly to $36,205 million in 2024 from $36,235 million in 2023. This stability reflects our ongoing efforts to manage supplier relationships and optimize inventory levels amid fluctuating market conditions. As a result of the decreased revenue and stable cost of revenue, our gross margin declined to 29.3% in 2024 from 30.3% in the previous year.

Selling, General and Administrative Expenses

Selling, general, and administrative (SG&A) expenses were $7,804 million in 2024, representing a decrease from $7,963 million in 2023. The decrease in SG&A expenses is attributed to cost-control measures and the streamlining of operations to enhance efficiency. These efforts have been integral in offsetting some of the pressures on gross margin and operating income.

Other Operating Expenses

Other operating expenses totaled $3,074 million in 2024, down from $3,119 million in 2023. This decrease was driven by reductions in certain discretionary spending categories and efforts to optimize operational processes. We continue to evaluate our cost structure to ensure alignment with our strategic objectives and market conditions.

Operating Income

Operating income for 2024 was $4,155 million, reflecting a decrease of 10.8% compared to $4,659 million in 2023. The decline in operating income is primarily due to the reduction in gross profit, partially offset by the effective management of operating expenses. We remain focused on driving operational efficiencies and leveraging technology to enhance productivity.

Interest Expense

Interest expense increased slightly to $420 million in 2024 from $413 million in 2023. This increase is attributable to higher borrowing costs associated with our long-term debt, reflecting changes in market interest rates. We continue to monitor our capital structure to ensure optimal financing costs.

Effective Tax Rate

Our effective tax rate for 2024 was 17.6%, consistent with the rate in 2023. We maintain a proactive approach to tax planning and compliance, ensuring alignment with applicable regulations and maximizing tax efficiencies.

Net Income

Net income for 2024 was $3,077 million, representing a decrease of 12.0% from $3,498 million in 2023. This decline reflects the combined impact of decreased operating income and slightly higher interest expenses. We remain committed to enhancing our profitability through strategic initiatives focused on revenue growth and cost management.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

Cash and Cash Equivalents

As of December 31, 2024, we had cash and cash equivalents totaling $2,896 million, compared to $3,008 million at the end of 2023. The decrease in cash and cash equivalents reflects our capital allocation priorities, including investments in growth initiatives and returns to shareholders through dividends and share repurchases.

Operating Activities

Net cash provided by operating activities was $5,751 million in 2024. This cash flow was primarily driven by our net income of $3,077 million and adjustments for non-cash items, including depreciation and amortization of $1,455 million. Effective working capital management also contributed to strong operating cash flows.

Investing Activities

Net cash used in investing activities was $2,135 million in 2024. Our investing activities primarily included capital expenditures of $1,240 million for store renovations and technology upgrades, as well as other investing activities totaling $895 million. These investments are critical to supporting our growth strategy and enhancing operational capabilities.

Financing Activities

Net cash used in financing activities was $3,728 million in 2024. This outflow primarily consisted of dividends paid amounting to $2,179 million and share repurchases, net of issuances, totaling $1,709 million. Additionally, we managed a net change in long-term debt of $160 million to optimize our capital structure.

Capital Expenditures

Capital expenditures for 2024 were $1,240 million, reflecting our commitment to investing in infrastructure and technology to support long-term growth. These expenditures were focused on enhancing our e-commerce capabilities, upgrading our physical store network, and improving supply chain efficiency.

Debt

Our long-term debt increased to $9,341 million as of December 31, 2024, from $9,181 million at the end of 2023. The increase in debt reflects our strategic financing activities aimed at supporting growth initiatives while maintaining financial flexibility.

Dividends and Share Repurchases

In 2024, we returned $2,179 million to shareholders through dividends and executed share repurchases totaling $1,709 million, net of issuances. These actions underscore our commitment to delivering value to shareholders while balancing investment in growth opportunities.

Critical Accounting Estimates

The preparation of financial statements in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP) requires management to make estimates and assumptions that affect the reported amounts of assets, liabilities, revenues, and expenses. Our critical accounting estimates are those that are most important to the portrayal of our financial condition and results of operations and require our most difficult, subjective, or complex judgments.

Inventory Valuation

We value our inventories at the lower of cost or net realizable value. This requires us to make estimates of future demand, market conditions, and product life cycles. Changes in these estimates could impact the valuation of inventory and the associated cost of revenue.

Impairment of Long-Lived Assets

We review long-lived assets for impairment whenever events or changes in circumstances indicate that the carrying amount may not be recoverable. This process involves estimating future cash flows and determining the fair value of the assets. Changes in market conditions or our business strategy could impact these estimates and result in impairment charges.

Income Taxes

We record a provision for income taxes based on our interpretation of tax laws and regulations. This requires us to make estimates about future taxable income and the potential impact of tax planning strategies. Changes in tax laws or our business operations could affect these estimates and result in adjustments to our tax provision.

Goodwill and Intangible Assets

Goodwill and intangible assets are tested for impairment annually or more frequently if events or circumstances indicate that the carrying amount may not be recoverable. This process requires us to estimate the fair value of reporting units and to assess the reasonableness of assumptions used in the valuation process.

In conclusion, while we have experienced challenges during fiscal 2024, we remain focused on executing our strategic initiatives and enhancing our competitive position in the consumer discretionary sector. Our commitment to delivering value to our customers and shareholders, coupled with disciplined financial management, positions us well for future growth opportunities.

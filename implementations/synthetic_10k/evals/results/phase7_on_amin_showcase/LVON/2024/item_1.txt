Item 1. Business

Overview

Lonvenlon Machinery, Inc. is a premier global designer, manufacturer, and service provider of advanced industrial machinery, automation solutions, and critical infrastructure equipment. We serve a diverse array of end markets, providing the foundational equipment and technology that enable our customers to build infrastructure, extract essential resources, and manufacture goods efficiently and sustainably. Our comprehensive portfolio encompasses heavy earthmoving equipment, precision automation systems, and a robust suite of aftermarket parts and lifecycle services. By integrating rugged mechanical engineering with advanced digital analytics and control software, we deliver comprehensive solutions that enhance productivity, improve safety, and reduce total cost of ownership for our customers worldwide.

For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, our total revenue was $15,645 million. This represents an increase from our total revenue of $13,661 million for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2023. Our revenue is generated through a balanced mix of original equipment sales and recurring aftermarket services, reflecting our strategic focus on supporting our customers throughout the entire lifecycle of their capital investments.

Our Company

Incorporated in Delaware and headquartered in Illinois, Lonvenlon Machinery, Inc. has established a legacy of engineering excellence and industrial innovation. From our origins as a regional manufacturer of specialized agricultural and construction implements, we have grown through decades of organic expansion and strategic acquisitions into a multinational enterprise. Our growth trajectory has been defined by a continuous commitment to research and development, allowing us to transition from traditional mechanical equipment to highly sophisticated, digitally connected machinery.

Throughout our history, we have strategically expanded our global footprint, establishing manufacturing facilities, research centers, and distribution hubs across the Americas, Europe, and the Asia-Pacific region. This localized presence enables us to respond rapidly to regional market dynamics, tailor our products to specific environmental and regulatory requirements, and maintain close relationships with our customer base. Today, we operate as a unified organization, leveraging our scale and technological expertise to address the complex challenges facing modern industrial sectors.

Our Business

We operate at the intersection of heavy engineering and digital innovation. Our business model is predicated on delivering high-performance physical assets augmented by intelligent software and comprehensive support services. We manufacture our products using advanced production techniques, including precision machining, automated welding, and lean assembly processes, ensuring the highest standards of quality and durability.

A core component of our business strategy is the integration of telemetry and connected technologies into our machinery. This connectivity allows us and our customers to monitor equipment health in real-time, optimize operational parameters, and deploy predictive maintenance protocols. By transforming raw data into actionable insights, we help our customers minimize unplanned downtime and maximize asset utilization. Furthermore, our business is supported by a resilient global supply chain and a vast network of independent dealers and direct sales professionals who provide localized expertise and support.

Principal Products and Services

Our product and service offerings are engineered to meet the rigorous demands of harsh industrial environments while delivering precise, reliable performance. We categorize our offerings into several key areas:

Heavy Earthmoving and Excavation Equipment: We design and manufacture a wide range of heavy machinery, including hydraulic excavators, articulated dump trucks, wheel loaders, and motor graders. These machines are built to withstand extreme conditions and are utilized extensively in large-scale construction, mining, and quarrying operations. Our equipment features advanced powertrain technologies that optimize fuel efficiency while delivering the necessary torque and breakout force required for demanding earthmoving tasks.

Material Handling and Logistics Systems: To support industrial facilities, ports, and distribution centers, we offer a comprehensive suite of material handling equipment. This includes heavy-duty forklifts, reach stackers, and automated conveyor systems. Our material handling solutions are designed to safely and efficiently move heavy loads, streamline warehouse operations, and integrate seamlessly with modern warehouse management software.

Industrial Automation and Control Technologies: We provide sophisticated automation solutions that enhance the efficiency of manufacturing processes. Our portfolio includes programmable logic controllers, automated guided vehicles, and robotic manipulation arms. These systems are highly customizable and are deployed in automotive assembly, heavy fabrication, and consumer goods manufacturing to improve precision, increase throughput, and reduce labor costs.

Aftermarket Parts and Remanufacturing: A critical element of our value proposition is our comprehensive aftermarket support. We supply a full range of original equipment manufacturer replacement parts, maintenance kits, and consumables. Additionally, we operate extensive remanufacturing facilities where used components, such as engines, transmissions, and hydraulic pumps, are rebuilt to original factory specifications. This remanufacturing program provides customers with cost-effective alternatives to new parts while supporting our environmental sustainability initiatives by reducing waste.

Lifecycle Management and Diagnostic Services: Leveraging the connectivity of our equipment, we offer advanced diagnostic and fleet management services. Customers can subscribe to our proprietary software platforms to track equipment location, monitor fuel consumption, and receive automated alerts regarding maintenance requirements. Our service technicians utilize these platforms to perform remote diagnostics and deploy predictive maintenance strategies, thereby extending the operational life of the machinery.

Reportable Segments

We manage our business and report our financial results through three distinct segments, which are organized based on the nature of the products and services provided.

Heavy Industrial Equipment The Heavy Industrial Equipment segment is responsible for the design, manufacture, and sale of our large-scale machinery utilized primarily in construction, mining, and infrastructure development. This segment encompasses our earthmoving machinery, paving equipment, and heavy material handlers. The operations within this segment are highly capital-intensive and rely on large-scale manufacturing facilities capable of fabricating and assembling massive steel structures and complex hydraulic systems. Success in this segment is driven by continuous innovation in machine durability, operator comfort, and powertrain efficiency. We are actively investing in alternative power solutions within this segment, including battery-electric and hydrogen fuel cell technologies, to meet the evolving emissions requirements of our global customer base.

Automation and Control Systems Our Automation and Control Systems segment focuses on the technologies that drive factory automation and intelligent material movement. This segment designs and produces robotic systems, automated guided vehicles, and the associated control software used in modern manufacturing environments. The products in this segment are characterized by high technological complexity and require significant investment in software engineering and systems integration. This segment serves a diverse range of industries looking to modernize their production lines and improve operational efficiency through automation. Growth in this segment is closely tied to the broader industrial trends of digitization, advanced manufacturing, and the pursuit of higher precision in fabrication processes.

Aftermarket Parts and Services The Aftermarket Parts and Services segment represents a recurring and highly resilient component of our business. This segment manages the global distribution of replacement parts, the operation of our remanufacturing centers, and the delivery of digital fleet management services. By maintaining a vast inventory of critical components and utilizing advanced logistics networks, this segment ensures rapid fulfillment of customer orders to minimize equipment downtime. The Aftermarket segment also includes our extended warranty programs and long-term maintenance contracts. This segment generally exhibits higher profit margins and greater stability during economic downturns compared to our original equipment segments, as customers often choose to maintain and extend the life of existing fleets rather than purchasing new machinery during periods of economic uncertainty.

Our Customers

Our customer base is highly diversified across multiple geographic regions and industrial sectors. We do not rely on any single customer for a material portion of our revenue. We primarily serve customers operating in the following end markets:

Construction and Infrastructure: This market includes general contractors, specialized infrastructure developers, and road building companies. These customers utilize our heavy equipment for site preparation, trenching, grading, and paving. Demand in this sector is heavily influenced by government infrastructure spending, residential and commercial real estate development, and broader macroeconomic conditions.

Mining and Extraction: We serve multinational mining corporations and regional quarry operators who require robust machinery for the extraction and transport of coal, copper, iron ore, aggregates, and precious metals. Customers in this market demand equipment that can operate continuously in some of the most unforgiving environments on earth. Capital expenditures by these customers are often tied to global commodity prices and long-term supply contracts.

Manufacturing and Logistics: This segment comprises automotive manufacturers, heavy fabricators, consumer goods producers, and global logistics providers. These customers rely on our material handling equipment and automation systems to optimize their supply chains, manage inventory, and execute precision manufacturing processes. The drive for supply chain resilience and labor efficiency are key catalysts for customer investment in this market.

Equipment Rental Companies: A significant portion of our machinery is sold to large, independent equipment rental fleets. These companies purchase our equipment to lease to end-users on a daily, weekly, or monthly basis. Rental companies prioritize equipment durability, ease of maintenance, and strong residual values, making our premium brand positioning highly attractive to this customer base.

Sales and Marketing

We employ a multi-channel go-to-market strategy to effectively reach our diverse global customer base. Our primary distribution channel is a network of independent, authorized dealers. These dealers are carefully selected based on their financial strength, industry expertise, and commitment to customer service. They are responsible for the localized sales of new and used equipment, the distribution of aftermarket parts, and the provision of repair and maintenance services. We work closely with our dealer network, providing them with extensive product training, marketing support, and advanced diagnostic tools to ensure a consistent and superior brand experience for the end-user.

In addition to our dealer network, we maintain a direct sales force that focuses on large, multinational accounts, particularly in the mining and complex automation sectors. These direct sales professionals possess deep technical knowledge and work collaboratively with customers to design customized equipment configurations and integrated systems solutions.

Our marketing efforts are designed to reinforce our reputation for reliability, technological leadership, and total lifecycle value. We participate in major international trade shows, conduct targeted digital marketing campaigns, and publish extensive technical literature. We also leverage the data generated by our connected fleet to demonstrate the tangible return on investment our equipment provides, using real-world performance metrics to support our value proposition.

Competition

The markets for industrial machinery, automation systems, and aftermarket services are intensely competitive and highly fragmented on a global scale. We face competition from a variety of large multinational corporations, as well as specialized regional manufacturers.

Competition in our industry is based on several key factors, including product performance, reliability, technological innovation, total cost of ownership, and the quality of aftermarket support. In the heavy equipment sector, competitors often compete aggressively on initial purchase price and financing terms. However, we believe that our strategic focus on durability, fuel efficiency, and comprehensive dealer support provides a competitive advantage over the lifecycle of the machinery.

In the automation and control systems market, competition is driven by software capabilities, integration flexibility, and the speed of technological advancement. We compete by offering open-architecture systems that seamlessly integrate with existing customer infrastructure and by continuously investing in research and development to maintain a technological edge. Across all segments, the ability to ensure rapid parts availability and expert service deployment remains a critical differentiator in winning and retaining customer loyalty.

Seasonality

Our business is subject to moderate seasonal fluctuations. In the Northern Hemisphere, demand for construction and earthmoving equipment is typically higher in the spring and summer months, corresponding with the peak construction season. Conversely, the winter months often see a slowdown in equipment utilization and new purchases in these regions due to adverse weather conditions.

Additionally, our sales patterns are influenced by the capital budgeting cycles of our major customers. We frequently experience a concentration of orders and deliveries in the fourth quarter of the fiscal year as customers finalize their capital expenditures and seek to utilize approved budgets before year-end. To mitigate the impact of seasonality on our manufacturing operations, we actively manage our production schedules and inventory levels, and our aftermarket parts business provides a steady stream of revenue that is less susceptible to seasonal weather patterns.

Raw Materials and Suppliers

The manufacturing of our equipment requires substantial quantities of raw materials, primarily high-strength steel, cast iron, aluminum, and rubber. We also procure a vast array of manufactured components, including diesel engines, hydraulic cylinders, electronic control modules, semiconductors, and specialized tires.

We rely on a global network of suppliers to source these materials and components. To ensure supply chain resilience, we employ a diversified sourcing strategy, seeking to avoid over-reliance on any single vendor or geographic region. We maintain long-term strategic relationships with key suppliers to secure favorable pricing, ensure priority allocation during periods of high demand, and collaborate on the engineering of custom components.

Despite our diversified sourcing, our operations can be impacted by fluctuations in raw material prices, particularly steel and petroleum-based products. We utilize forward contracts and index-based pricing agreements where appropriate to manage our exposure to commodity price volatility. Furthermore, the global availability of electronic components, specifically semiconductors, remains a critical factor in our production capabilities, and we continuously monitor our supply chain to anticipate and mitigate potential disruptions.

Intellectual Property

Intellectual property is a vital asset in maintaining our competitive position and technological leadership. We rely on a comprehensive combination of patents, trademarks, copyrights, and trade secrets to protect our innovations and brand identity.

We actively seek patent protection in the United States and key international jurisdictions for our proprietary mechanical designs, hydraulic systems, control algorithms, and manufacturing processes. Our patent portfolio covers a broad spectrum of technologies, from advanced continuously variable transmissions to the software architectures underlying our autonomous guided vehicles.

In addition to patents, we hold numerous trademarks that protect our corporate name, product brands, and service offerings. These trademarks are recognized globally and are synonymous with quality and reliability in the industrial sector. We also protect our proprietary software, engineering drawings, and manufacturing techniques as trade secrets, implementing strict confidentiality agreements with our employees, suppliers, and strategic partners to prevent unauthorized disclosure.

Government Regulation

Our operations, both domestically and internationally, are subject to a complex and evolving framework of laws and regulations. Compliance with these regulations is a fundamental aspect of our business and requires significant ongoing investment.

Environmental Regulations: Our manufacturing facilities and the products we produce are subject to stringent environmental laws. These regulations govern air emissions, wastewater discharge, solid waste disposal, and the handling of hazardous materials. Notably, our diesel-powered equipment must comply with rigorous emissions standards mandated by the Environmental Protection Agency in the United States and similar regulatory bodies in the European Union and other jurisdictions. We dedicate substantial research and development resources to engineering engines and exhaust aftertreatment systems that meet or exceed these regulatory requirements while maintaining machine performance.

Occupational Health and Safety: We are subject to laws designed to protect the health and safety of our workforce, such as those enforced by the Occupational Safety and Health Administration in the United States. We maintain comprehensive safety protocols, conduct regular training, and perform rigorous audits at all our manufacturing and service facilities to ensure compliance and promote a culture of zero harm.

International Trade and Compliance: As a global enterprise, we must navigate complex international trade regulations, including tariffs, export controls, and economic sanctions. We maintain a robust trade compliance program to ensure that our cross-border transactions adhere to the regulations of the United States and the other countries in which we operate. Changes in trade policy, the imposition of new tariffs, or the escalation of geopolitical tensions can impact our supply chain costs and our ability to access certain international markets.

Data Privacy and Cybersecurity: With the increasing digitization of our products and services, we are subject to global data protection regulations, such as the General Data Protection Regulation in Europe. We implement stringent cybersecurity measures and data governance policies to protect the telemetry data collected from our equipment and the personal information of our customers and employees.

Human Capital

Our workforce is the foundation of our success and the driver of our continuous innovation. As of December 31, 2024, we employed about 27,600 individuals globally. Our employees span a wide range of disciplines, including mechanical and software engineering, skilled manufacturing, supply chain logistics, sales, and executive management.

We are committed to attracting, developing, and retaining top talent in a highly competitive labor market. Our human capital strategy focuses on several key pillars:

Health and Safety: The physical safety and mental well-being of our employees are our highest priorities. We utilize advanced ergonomic assessments in our manufacturing facilities, mandate comprehensive safety training for all operational personnel, and empower every employee with the authority to halt production if they identify a potential safety hazard.

Training and Development: We invest heavily in the continuous education of our workforce. We offer extensive technical training programs for our assembly technicians and service mechanics, ensuring they possess the skills required to work on our increasingly complex machinery. For our engineering and corporate staff, we provide leadership development courses, tuition reimbursement programs, and structured career progression pathways.

Diversity and Inclusion: We believe that a diverse workforce fosters innovation and better reflects the global customer base we serve. We actively recruit from a variety of backgrounds and strive to create an inclusive workplace culture where all employees feel valued and empowered to contribute their unique perspectives.

Compensation and Benefits: We offer competitive compensation packages designed to reward performance and align employee interests with those of our shareholders. Our benefits programs include comprehensive healthcare coverage, retirement savings plans, and performance-based incentive structures.

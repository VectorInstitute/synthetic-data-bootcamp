Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements

Note 1. Goodwill and Intangible Assets
Goodwill and acquired intangible assets totaled $82,502.5 million and $26,203.8 million, respectively, as of fiscal year end, together representing approximately 45.6% of total assets. We test these assets for impairment at least annually; no impairment was recognized during fiscal 2025.

Note 2. Income Taxes
Our effective tax rate for fiscal 2025 was 58.9%, compared with the 21% U.S. federal statutory rate. The difference primarily reflects non-deductible items and other permanent differences, which represent a larger proportion of our pretax income given our thin overall margins.

Note 3. Commitments and Contingencies
We are subject to various claims and legal proceedings, including matters related to our pharmacy, benefits, and health services operations, arising in the ordinary course of business. We do not believe the resolution of these matters, individually or in the aggregate, will have a material adverse effect on our consolidated financial position or results of operations.

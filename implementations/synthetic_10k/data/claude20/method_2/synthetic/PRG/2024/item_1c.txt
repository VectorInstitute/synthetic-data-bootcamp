Cybersecurity Program

We maintain a cybersecurity program informed by ISO/IEC 27001 that is designed to identify, assess, and manage risks from cybersecurity threats to our information systems and the data we and our businesses process. Given the decentralized nature of our operating model, day-to-day implementation of cybersecurity controls is managed locally by each of our operating businesses within a company-wide framework of required controls, while certain monitoring and incident-response capabilities are managed centrally.

Our program includes recurring risk assessments, vulnerability scanning and penetration testing, employee security-awareness training, and a formal incident response plan that establishes procedures for identifying, escalating, containing, and remediating cybersecurity incidents. We also assess the cybersecurity practices of third-party vendors and service providers that have access to our systems or data as part of our vendor management process, and we maintain cybersecurity insurance that may offset a portion of the costs associated with certain incidents.

Cybersecurity Incidents

During fiscal 2024, we identified a ransomware attempt on internal IT systems that was detected and contained before data was exfiltrated. We do not believe this incident has had a material effect on our business strategy, results of operations, or financial condition, although we cannot provide assurance that future incidents will not be more severe. See Item 1A, "Risk Factors," for additional discussion of cybersecurity-related risks.

Governance

Our full Board of Directors, with support from the Audit Committee, oversees our cybersecurity risk management program. Management provides the Board and the Audit Committee with periodic updates on our cybersecurity program, including the results of risk assessments, the status of our incident response capabilities, and material cybersecurity incidents, such as the ransomware attempt described above. We believe that board-level engagement on cybersecurity, rather than delegation to a single committee alone, reflects the importance we place on this area given the decentralized nature of our operations.

Program

We maintain a cybersecurity risk management program designed to identify, assess, and manage risks from cybersecurity threats to our systems, our software products, and the confidential design data our customers entrust to us. Our program is informed by the NIST Cybersecurity Framework, and we periodically assess our practices against that framework.

Our program includes recurring risk assessments, vulnerability scanning and penetration testing, employee security-awareness training, and a formal incident response plan that establishes procedures for identifying, containing, and remediating cybersecurity incidents. Given the sensitivity of the intellectual property our customers share with us during the design process, we maintain contractual and technical safeguards intended to protect that data, and our vendor management process assesses the security practices of third parties that have access to our systems.

Risks

We have not experienced a material cybersecurity incident to date. However, because our customers' chip designs are highly confidential and commercially valuable, we and our customers are targets of cybersecurity threats, and a future incident could compromise sensitive design data, disrupt our operations, and result in remediation costs, litigation, or reputational harm. See Item 1A, "Risk Factors," for additional discussion of cybersecurity-related risks.

Governance

Our full Board of Directors, with support from the Audit Committee, oversees our cybersecurity risk management program. Management provides the Board and the Audit Committee with periodic updates on our cybersecurity program, including the results of risk assessments and the threat landscape we face.

Cybersecurity Program

We maintain a cybersecurity risk management program informed by ISO/IEC 27001, designed to identify, assess, and manage risks from cybersecurity threats to our information systems, including the systems that support our manufacturing, supply chain, and customer order operations.

Our program includes recurring risk assessments, vulnerability scanning and penetration testing, employee security-awareness training, and a formal incident response plan that sets out procedures for identifying, escalating, containing, and remediating cybersecurity incidents. Because our manufacturing operations depend in part on third-party suppliers and service providers, our vendor management process includes an assessment of those providers' security practices before and during the engagement. We maintain cybersecurity insurance that may offset a portion of the costs associated with certain incidents.

Cybersecurity Risks

We have not experienced a material cybersecurity incident to date. However, a future incident affecting our manufacturing, supply chain, or customer order systems, or those of our third-party service providers, could disrupt our production operations, compromise sensitive data, and result in remediation costs, litigation, or reputational harm. See Item 1A, "Risk Factors," for additional discussion of cybersecurity-related risks.

Governance

Our full Board of Directors, with support from the Audit Committee, oversees our cybersecurity risk management program. Management provides the Board and the Audit Committee with periodic updates on our cybersecurity program, including the results of risk assessments, the status of our security initiatives, and the threat landscape we face across our manufacturing operations.

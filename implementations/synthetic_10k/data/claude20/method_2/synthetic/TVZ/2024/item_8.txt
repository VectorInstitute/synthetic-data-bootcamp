Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements

Note 1. Inventory
Inventory, stated at the lower of cost or market, totaled $4,378.4 million as of December 31, 2024, representing approximately 32.8% of total assets.

Note 2. Debt
Long-term debt outstanding totaled $6,748.8 million as of December 31, 2024.

Note 3. Stockholders' Equity
Stockholders' equity totaled $267.4 million as of December 31, 2024, reflecting an accumulated deficit of $3,788.8 million resulting primarily from share repurchases in excess of cumulative net income over our operating history. During fiscal 2024, we used $3,066.9 million of cash for share repurchases and paid no dividends.

Note 4. Income Taxes
Our effective tax rate for fiscal 2024 was 16.8%, below the 21% U.S. federal statutory rate, reflecting discrete tax items in the period.

Note 5. Commitments and Contingencies
We are subject to various claims and legal proceedings, including product liability and general liability matters, arising in the ordinary course of business. We do not believe the resolution of these matters, individually or in the aggregate, will have a material adverse effect on our consolidated financial position or results of operations.

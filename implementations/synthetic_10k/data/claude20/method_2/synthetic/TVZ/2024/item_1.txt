Introduction

Meridian Innovations Inc. ("Meridian," "we," "our," or "us") is a retailer and distributor of automotive replacement parts and accessories, headquartered in Tennessee. We sell automotive hard parts, maintenance items, accessories, and related non-automotive products through a network of retail stores, the majority of which also serve commercial customers such as repair garages, dealers, service stations, and fleet operators through a commercial delivery program. We also sell through e-commerce channels. We do not perform automotive repair or installation services ourselves.

For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, we generated net sales of $16,486.4 million. Our business model depends on maintaining broad and deep product availability across our store network and distribution centers, which requires us to carry substantial inventory; inventory represented approximately 32.8% of our total assets as of fiscal year end.

Store Operations

We operate stores across the United States, organized into geographic divisions supported by a network of regional distribution centers that allow us to replenish store inventory frequently. Each store carries a broad assortment of parts and accessories sized to local vehicle populations, supplemented by access to a larger inventory pool at distribution centers and nearby stores for items not stocked locally.

Commercial Sales Program

A majority of our stores participate in a commercial sales program that provides prompt delivery of parts and commercial credit to repair garages, dealers, service stations, fleet operators, and other commercial accounts. We believe our commercial program is an important driver of revenue growth, extending our addressable market beyond walk-in retail customers.

Human Capital Resources

We employ a workforce consisting predominantly of store-level personnel, with the remainder employed in distribution centers and corporate support functions. The substantial majority of our store personnel are part-time employees. We do not have any collective bargaining agreements in the United States and believe our relationships with our employees are good.

Competition

The retail and distribution of automotive parts and accessories is highly competitive. We compete with other national and regional automotive parts retailers, mass merchandisers, online retailers, and automobile dealers that sell original equipment parts. We believe the principal competitive factors in our industry are product availability, store location and convenience, customer service, and price.

Overview

Lakeshore Solutions Corp. is an aerospace and defense company. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2023, we generated revenue of $17,211.6 million, pretax income of $1,527.6 million, and net income of $1,206.8 million (a net margin of 7.0%).

Our cost structure reflects the program-based nature of our business; the substantial majority of our costs relate to materials, labor, and overhead incurred directly on customer programs, which are not broken out separately from other operating costs above. Our effective tax rate for the year was 21.0%, consistent with the U.S. federal statutory rate.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

We generated $1,388.3 million of cash from operating activities during 2023, reflecting net income of $1,206.8 million and depreciation and amortization of $467.1 million, partly offset by changes in working capital. We used $393.8 million of cash in investing activities. Financing activities used $1,175.6 million of cash, primarily reflecting $1,580.2 million used for share repurchases and $19.0 million of dividends paid, partly offset by other financing inflows. Taken together, our cash and cash equivalents decreased by $181.1 million during the year.

As of December 31, 2023, we had no outstanding short-term or long-term debt and stockholders' equity of $10,394.3 million. We believe our cash flow from operations will be sufficient to fund our operating needs, capital expenditures, and shareholder distributions for at least the next twelve months.

Critical Accounting Estimates

Revenue Recognition on Long-Term Contracts. A substantial portion of our revenue is recognized over time as we perform under long-term government and commercial contracts. Measuring progress toward completion, and estimating total costs to complete a program, requires significant judgment; changes in these estimates could materially affect revenue and profitability in the period of change.

Inventory. Inventory, which represented approximately 23.8% of our total assets as of December 31, 2023, is stated at the lower of cost or net realizable value, including program-specific work in process.

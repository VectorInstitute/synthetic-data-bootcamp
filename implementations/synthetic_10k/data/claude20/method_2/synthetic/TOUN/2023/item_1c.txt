Cybersecurity Program

We maintain a cybersecurity program informed by the NIST Cybersecurity Framework and ISO/IEC 27001 that is designed to identify, assess, and manage risks from cybersecurity threats to our information systems, including information related to our government and defense programs. Given the nature of our business, our program is also designed with reference to applicable U.S. government cybersecurity requirements for defense contractors.

Our program includes continuous monitoring of our information technology environment, periodic risk assessments, vulnerability scanning and penetration testing, employee security-awareness training, and a formal incident response plan that establishes procedures for identifying, escalating, containing, and remediating cybersecurity incidents. We also assess the cybersecurity practices of third-party suppliers and service providers that have access to our systems or data.

Cybersecurity Incidents

During fiscal 2023, we identified a contained phishing-related account compromise affecting a limited number of employee accounts. We detected and contained the incident, and we do not believe it has had a material effect on our business strategy, results of operations, or financial condition, although we cannot provide assurance that future incidents will not be more severe. See Item 1A, "Risk Factors," for additional discussion of cybersecurity-related risks.

Governance

Our Board of Directors has established a dedicated Technology and Cybersecurity Committee responsible for overseeing our cybersecurity risk management program. Management provides the committee with regular updates on our cybersecurity program, including the results of risk assessments, the status of our incident response capabilities, and material cybersecurity incidents, such as the phishing-related incident described above. The committee in turn reports to the full Board on matters it believes warrant the Board's attention.

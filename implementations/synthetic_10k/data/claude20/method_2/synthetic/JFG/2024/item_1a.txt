Risks Related to Our Business and Industry

A significant portion of our revenue depends on commercial, industrial, and construction end markets that are sensitive to broader economic cycles. A sustained downturn in industrial production, construction activity, or capital spending by our customers could reduce demand for our products and adversely affect our sales and profitability.

We depend on our ability to maintain sufficient inventory availability to support our on-site and vending customer programs. Inventory represented approximately 37.8% of our total assets as of December 31, 2024. A failure to accurately forecast demand, or a disruption affecting our suppliers or distribution network, could result in lost sales or, alternatively, excess or obsolete inventory.

We face intense competition from other industrial distributors, manufacturers selling directly to end customers, and online marketplaces. Continued growth in online industrial procurement could pressure our margins or require additional investment in our own digital and vending capabilities.

Our business model depends on our sales and service personnel who are embedded at or near customer sites. Our ability to attract, train, and retain this distributed workforce is important to maintaining the service quality that differentiates our offering; increased competition for this talent, or higher turnover, could adversely affect customer relationships and sales.

Risks Related to Our Financial Position

We have a modest amount of indebtedness, and while our capital structure is conservative relative to many industrial companies, our continued ability to fund working capital, capital expenditures, and shareholder distributions depends on our ability to generate cash flow from operations and maintain access to credit on acceptable terms if needed.

Risks Related to Cybersecurity and Information Technology

Our business depends on information technology systems that support our sales, inventory-management, and vending operations across a large number of customer sites. As described in Item 1C, "Cybersecurity," while we have not experienced a material cybersecurity incident to date, a future incident affecting our systems or those of our third-party service providers could disrupt our operations, compromise customer or employee data, and result in remediation costs, litigation, or reputational harm.

General Risk Factors

Our results depend in part on the pricing and availability of the products we distribute, including the impact of tariffs, raw material cost inflation, and supplier concentration, any of which could increase our costs or disrupt our supply chain if not effectively managed or passed through to customers.

Overview

Harborview Partners Holdings, Inc. is an aerospace and defense technology company. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025, we generated revenue of $58,767.2 million, operating income of $6,422.3 million (an operating margin of 10.9%), and net income of $6,153.0 million (a net margin of 10.5%).

Results of Operations

The following table summarizes our results of operations for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025 (in millions, except percentages; amounts may not sum due to rounding):

Revenue | $58,767.2 | 100.0%
Research and development expenses | 1,381.1 | 2.4%
Other operating costs, net | 50,963.8 | 86.7%
Operating income | 6,422.3 | 10.9%

Net income | 6,153.0 | 10.5%

Our cost structure reflects the program-based nature of our business; the substantial majority of our costs relate to materials, labor, and overhead incurred directly on customer programs, which are not broken out separately from other operating costs above. Our effective tax rate for the year was 4.2%, below the 21% U.S. federal statutory rate, primarily reflecting the impact of research and development tax credits and other tax benefits.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

We generated $7,284.6 million of cash from operating activities during 2025, reflecting net income of $6,153.0 million and depreciation and amortization of $2,327.8 million, partly offset by changes in working capital. We used $1,803.0 million of cash in investing activities, primarily capital expenditures of $2,253.3 million, partly offset by other investing inflows. Financing activities used $4,266.2 million of cash, primarily reflecting $2,599.8 million used for share repurchases and other financing outflows. Taken together, our cash and cash equivalents increased by $1,215.5 million during the year.

As of December 31, 2025, we had total debt of approximately $23,478.0 million and stockholders' equity of $25,288.4 million. We believe our cash flow from operations, together with access to the credit and capital markets, will be sufficient to fund our operating needs, capital expenditures, and shareholder distributions for at least the next twelve months.

Critical Accounting Estimates

Goodwill. Goodwill, which represented approximately 38.0% of our total assets as of December 31, 2025, is tested for impairment at least annually. These tests require us to estimate the fair value of our reporting units, which involves significant judgment regarding future cash flows and discount rates.

Revenue Recognition on Long-Term Contracts. A substantial portion of our revenue is recognized over time as we perform under long-term government and commercial contracts. Measuring progress toward completion requires significant judgment.

Introduction

Harborview Partners Holdings, Inc. ("Harborview," "we," "our," or "us") is an aerospace and defense technology company headquartered in Virginia. We design, develop, and manufacture products and systems for U.S. and international defense and government customers, including aerospace systems, mission systems, and related technologies. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025, we generated revenue of $58,767.2 million.

Our Business

We operate across multiple aerospace and defense product lines, serving primarily the U.S. Department of Defense and intelligence community, supplemented by international defense customers and, to a lesser extent, commercial aerospace customers. A substantial portion of our growth over time has come through the acquisition of complementary technology and systems businesses; goodwill represented approximately 38.0% of our total assets at year end.

Government Contracts

Our government contracts are subject to standard terms allowing for termination for convenience, audit, and renegotiation, and our revenue depends in part on future government budget authorizations and appropriations for national security priorities.

Research and Development

We invest in research and development to support current and future programs, reflecting the technology-intensive nature of our product lines.

Human Capital Resources

We employ a workforce of engineering, manufacturing, and program-management personnel, a portion of which require government security clearances to perform contract work. We believe our relationships with our employees are good.

Competition

The aerospace and defense industry is highly competitive, with a small number of large prime contractors and a broader base of specialized suppliers. We compete on the basis of technical capability, program performance, cost, and past performance.

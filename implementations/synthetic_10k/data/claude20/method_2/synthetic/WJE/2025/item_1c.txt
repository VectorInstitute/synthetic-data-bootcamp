Program

We maintain a cybersecurity risk management program designed to identify, assess, and manage risks from cybersecurity threats to our platform, our software supply chain, and our corporate systems. Our program is informed by the NIST Cybersecurity Framework and ISO/IEC 27001, and because we are ourselves a cybersecurity company, we hold our own practices to a standard consistent with what we ask of our customers.

Our program includes continuous monitoring of our production and development environments, regular vulnerability scanning and penetration testing, rigorous software supply-chain security controls, employee security-awareness training, and a formal incident response plan that establishes procedures for identifying, containing, and remediating cybersecurity incidents. We also maintain cybersecurity insurance that may offset a portion of the costs associated with certain incidents.

Risks

We have not experienced a material cybersecurity incident to date. However, as a cybersecurity company, we and the software we distribute to customers are particularly attractive targets for sophisticated threat actors, and a future incident affecting our platform, our software supply chain, or our third-party service providers could disrupt our business, harm our reputation with security-conscious customers, and result in remediation costs or litigation. See Item 1A, "Risk Factors," for additional discussion of cybersecurity-related risks.

Governance

Our full Board of Directors, with support from the Audit Committee, oversees our cybersecurity risk management program. Management provides the Board and the Audit Committee with periodic updates on our cybersecurity program, including the results of risk assessments, the status of our software supply-chain security controls, and the threat landscape we face.

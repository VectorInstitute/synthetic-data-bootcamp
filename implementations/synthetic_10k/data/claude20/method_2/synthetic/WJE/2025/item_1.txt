Introduction

Fieldstone Partners Holdings, Inc. ("Fieldstone," "we," "our," or "us") is headquartered in Texas and provides a cloud-delivered cybersecurity platform that protects endpoints, cloud workloads, and identities from cyberattacks. Our platform is built on a cloud-native architecture and uses a lightweight software agent together with cloud-based analytics to detect and respond to threats in real time.

We sell our platform primarily through subscription arrangements to organizations of a wide range of sizes across industries. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025, we generated revenue of $4,636.5 million.

Research and Development

The cybersecurity threat landscape evolves continuously, and our ability to compete depends on our continued investment in research and development. Research and development expense was $1,278.6 million for fiscal 2025, representing approximately 27.6% of revenue, reflecting our investment in new detection capabilities, threat intelligence, and platform infrastructure.

Human Capital

Our employees include security researchers, software engineers, threat intelligence analysts, and sales and customer support personnel, located primarily in the United States with additional personnel internationally. Competition for cybersecurity talent is intense, and we focus on compensation, equity-based incentives, and mission-driven culture to attract and retain employees.

Competition

The cybersecurity market is large, competitive, and rapidly evolving. We compete with other dedicated endpoint and cloud security vendors, with broader cybersecurity platform providers, and with point-solution vendors addressing specific threat types. We believe the principal competitive factors in our market are the efficacy of threat detection, the breadth of our platform, and the speed of response to emerging threats.

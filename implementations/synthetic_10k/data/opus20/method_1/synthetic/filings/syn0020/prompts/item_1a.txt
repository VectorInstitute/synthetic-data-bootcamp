Item 1. Business

Overview

Drazennov Medical, Inc. is a global health care technology company that designs, manufactures and sells medical devices, therapies and related services used by physicians, hospitals and patients to diagnose and treat chronic and acute conditions. Our products address cardiovascular disease, neurological disorders, surgical care, diabetes and patient monitoring. We sell them in developed and emerging markets around the world. Unless the context requires otherwise, references in this Annual Report on Form 10-K to "Drazennov," "the Company," "we," "us" and "our" refer to Drazennov Medical, Inc. and its consolidated subsidiaries.

For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, we generated total revenue of $60,579 million, an increase of 3.8% compared with the prior fiscal year. Growth reflected continued adoption of our established therapies, contributions from recently introduced products and expanded access to our technologies in markets outside the United States. We invested 11.4% of revenue in research and development during fiscal 2024. We consider this level of investment central to maintaining a broad and replenishing portfolio of clinically differentiated products.

Our mission is to improve and extend patients' lives by bringing to market technologies that address unmet clinical needs. We aim to deliver meaningful clinical outcomes and economic value to health care systems. Our scale, global manufacturing network and long-standing relationships with clinicians enable us to support patients across the continuum of care, from diagnosis and intervention through long-term disease management.

Drazennov Medical, Inc. is incorporated in the State of Delaware. Our principal executive offices are located in Minneapolis, Minnesota.

Our Strategy

Our strategy centers on four priorities.

First, we seek to lead in innovation. We direct research and development spending toward therapies that can change the standard of care in large and underpenetrated patient populations. We complement internal programs with targeted acquisitions, licensing arrangements and collaborations with academic and clinical institutions.

Second, we seek to expand access to our therapies. We work with health care providers, payers and governments to build the clinical and economic evidence that supports coverage and reimbursement. We also invest in physician training and referral pathways so that more eligible patients are identified and treated.

Third, we pursue operational excellence. We are working to simplify our manufacturing footprint, strengthen supply chain resilience, improve quality systems and apply digital tools to our operations. The goals are higher reliability and more efficient use of capital.

Fourth, we actively manage our portfolio. We periodically evaluate our businesses and product lines against their growth prospects, margin profile and strategic fit. We reallocate resources toward the areas we believe offer the most attractive long-term returns.

Principal Products and Services

Our product portfolio includes implantable and minimally invasive devices, capital equipment, single-use instruments and consumables, software, and services that support the effective use of our technologies. Principal product categories include:

- Cardiac rhythm management devices, including implantable pacemakers, defibrillators, leadless pacing systems and insertable cardiac monitors, together with remote monitoring platforms that transmit device data to clinicians.
- Structural heart and vascular therapies, including transcatheter heart valve systems, coronary and peripheral stents, balloon catheters, aortic stent grafts and devices used to treat venous disease.
- Cardiac ablation solutions, including mapping and navigation systems, ablation catheters and related consumables used to treat atrial fibrillation and other arrhythmias.
- Neuromodulation therapies, including spinal cord stimulation systems for chronic pain, deep brain stimulation systems for movement disorders and sacral nerve stimulation systems for bladder and bowel dysfunction.
- Surgical technologies, including powered surgical instruments, advanced energy devices, surgical staplers, hernia repair products, robotic-assisted surgical platforms and surgical navigation and imaging systems.
- Spine and cranial products, including spinal implants, biologics, neurovascular devices used to treat stroke and aneurysms, and cerebrospinal fluid management products.
- Diabetes management products, including insulin pumps, continuous glucose monitoring sensors and transmitters, infusion sets and software that connects these devices into automated insulin delivery systems.
- Patient monitoring and respiratory products, including pulse oximetry sensors, capnography systems, ventilators, airway management devices and hospital monitoring software.

Beyond our devices, we provide clinical education, technical field support, equipment service contracts, data management platforms and outcomes analytics to hospitals and health systems. We are increasingly offering integrated solutions that combine devices with digital tools and services. We believe these solutions help providers improve procedural efficiency and patient outcomes.

Reportable Segments

We manage our business through three reportable segments, organized by therapeutic area and customer call point. Each segment has its own leadership team responsible for research and development, marketing and commercial strategy. Manufacturing, supply chain, quality, regulatory and other functions are shared across the enterprise. Financial information for our segments is in the notes to the consolidated financial statements included elsewhere in this Annual Report on Form 10-K.

Cardiovascular and Vascular Therapies

The Cardiovascular and Vascular Therapies segment develops and sells products that diagnose, treat and monitor heart rhythm disorders, heart failure, structural heart disease, and coronary, aortic and peripheral vascular disease. The segment's principal customers are electrophysiologists, interventional and general cardiologists, cardiac and vascular surgeons, and the hospitals and ambulatory centers where they practice.

The segment's strategy emphasizes less invasive approaches, such as transcatheter valve replacement and leadless pacing. It also seeks to expand our presence in cardiac ablation, which we consider one of the fastest-growing areas of cardiovascular medicine. Remote monitoring and data connectivity are integral to the segment's implantable device offerings and support ongoing patient management after implantation.

Neuroscience and Surgical Solutions

The Neuroscience and Surgical Solutions segment develops and sells therapies for chronic pain, movement disorders, pelvic health, stroke and spinal conditions, along with a broad range of instruments and platforms used in general, thoracic, bariatric, colorectal, gynecologic and orthopedic surgery. Principal customers include neurosurgeons, orthopedic and spine surgeons, neurologists, pain management specialists, general surgeons, and hospital operating room and supply chain leaders.

The segment's strategy focuses on four areas: expanding the use of minimally invasive and robotic-assisted surgery, enlarging the installed base of our surgical platforms, growing the use of consumable instruments that accompany them, and advancing closed-loop neuromodulation technologies that adapt therapy to individual patient signals.

Diabetes and Patient Monitoring

The Diabetes and Patient Monitoring segment develops and sells insulin delivery systems, continuous glucose monitoring products and related software for people with diabetes. It also sells monitoring, respiratory and airway products used in hospitals, ambulatory settings and the home. Customers include endocrinologists, primary care physicians, diabetes educators, anesthesiologists, critical care clinicians, hospitals, and, for diabetes products, patients themselves through pharmacy and durable medical equipment channels.

The segment's strategy centers on automated insulin delivery, interoperability with third-party devices and digital applications, and connected monitoring solutions. These solutions are intended to help clinicians identify patient deterioration earlier and manage care across settings. Because a significant portion of the segment's revenue comes from recurring purchases of sensors, infusion sets and other disposables, the segment places particular emphasis on patient retention and the user experience.

Customers

Our principal customers are hospitals, integrated delivery networks, group purchasing organizations, ambulatory surgery centers, physician practices, clinics, government health care agencies, distributors, pharmacies and, for certain diabetes products, individual patients. In many markets, purchasing decisions involve both the clinicians who select and use our products and the administrators and procurement organizations that negotiate pricing and contract terms.

In the United States, a substantial portion of our sales is made under contracts with group purchasing organizations and integrated delivery networks. These contracts typically set pricing and terms for member facilities but do not obligate members to purchase specified volumes. Outside the United States, many of our customers are public hospitals or government-funded health systems, and sales often occur through tender processes or national procurement arrangements.

Payment for our products ultimately depends in large part on reimbursement from third-party payers. These include government programs such as Medicare and Medicaid in the United States, national health systems in other countries, and private insurers. No single customer accounted for a material portion of our consolidated revenue in fiscal 2024. We do not believe the loss of any single customer would have a material adverse effect on our consolidated results of operations.

Sales and Marketing

We market and sell our products primarily through a direct sales force, supplemented by independent distributors and sales agents in certain geographies and product lines. Our sales representatives and clinical specialists are typically organized by segment and therapy area. Many of them are trained to provide technical support in the procedure room, assist with device programming and follow-up, and educate clinicians on the appropriate use of our products.

Our marketing activities include:

- Physician education and training programs delivered through our training centers, mobile labs, simulation tools and digital learning platforms.
- Participation in medical congresses and scientific meetings.
- Publication of clinical trial results in peer-reviewed journals.
- Patient awareness programs designed to help people recognize symptoms and seek appropriate care.

For diabetes products, we also maintain direct relationships with patients through customer service, product training and digital support channels.

We work with payers and health technology assessment bodies to demonstrate the clinical and economic value of our therapies. Our health economics and market access teams develop evidence on outcomes, cost-effectiveness and total cost of care to support coverage decisions, reimbursement coding and favorable payment rates.

Item 1. Business

OVERVIEW

Orzenka Midstream, Inc. ("Orzenka," the "Company," "we," "us" or "our") is a Delaware corporation headquartered in Phoenix, Arizona. We own, operate and develop energy infrastructure that connects hydrocarbon supply to demand. Our asset base includes:

- natural gas gathering systems and processing plants;
- natural gas liquids ("NGL") fractionation facilities;
- crude oil, refined product and NGL transportation pipelines;
- above-ground tankage and underground storage caverns; and
- marine, rail and truck terminals.

Our customers include producers, refiners, petrochemical manufacturers, local distribution companies, power generators, marketers and exporters. Through our facilities, we move hydrocarbons from the wellhead and from the refinery gate to end-use markets in the United States and to export points.

Our common stock trades under the ticker symbol "ORZA." For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, total revenue was $52,746 million, an increase of 15.1% from the prior fiscal year. Revenue grew because volumes on our systems increased, assets placed in service during the year contributed to results, and our recently acquired assets contributed for a full year.

We manage our business through three reportable segments:

- Gathering and Processing;
- Transportation and Storage; and
- Terminals and Marketing Services.

Each segment is organized around the part of the hydrocarbon value chain it supports. The segments are described below under "Our Reportable Segments." Additional segment financial information appears in Item 7, Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations, and in the notes to our consolidated financial statements in Item 8, Financial Statements and Supplementary Data.

Our business model rests on long-term commercial agreements. Many of these agreements contain fee-based rate structures, minimum volume commitments, acreage dedications or reservation charges. We structure our contracts this way to limit our direct exposure to commodity price changes and to support cash flows through the commodity cycle. Some of our activities, such as certain percent-of-proceeds processing arrangements and our marketing operations, do expose us to commodity prices. We manage that exposure through contract design, operating practices and, where appropriate, derivative instruments.

STRATEGY AND PRIORITIES

Safe and Reliable Operations

The safety of our workforce, the communities near our assets and the environment comes before every other operating objective. We maintain an integrity management program for our pipelines and facilities. The program includes:

- inline inspection;
- cathodic protection;
- leak detection;
- control room management; and
- preventive maintenance.

We investigate incidents and near misses to identify root causes. We share the lessons learned across our operating regions so that corrective measures are applied consistently.

Disciplined Capital Allocation

We assess growth opportunities against expected returns, the strength and duration of the underlying commercial support, and how well the project fits our existing footprint. We favor projects that extend, debottleneck or connect assets we already own. These projects generally offer lower execution risk and better capital efficiency than greenfield development in new regions. We also pursue acquisitions when we believe a transaction will add to our system, broaden our customer base, or create opportunities to integrate the acquired assets with our own.

Cost Competitiveness and Operational Efficiency

We seek to lower our unit operating costs. Our main tools are:

- standardized engineering and procurement practices;
- centralized control operations;
- predictive maintenance programs; and
- consolidation of support functions.

We regard cost competitiveness as essential to winning new volumes in a market where customers can often choose among several service providers.

Financial Strength and Shareholder Returns

We intend to keep a balance sheet that supports access to capital markets throughout the commodity cycle. We aim to generate cash flow sufficient to fund maintenance capital, a significant share of our growth capital, and the return of capital to stockholders through dividends and share repurchases. Our board of directors reviews our capital allocation framework periodically. It may change dividend and repurchase levels in light of business conditions, investment opportunities and other factors.

Energy Transition Readiness

We evaluate opportunities that use our existing rights-of-way, engineering capabilities and customer relationships to serve lower-carbon energy markets. These include:

- carbon dioxide transportation and sequestration;
- renewable feedstock and renewable fuel logistics; and
- the handling of hydrogen and hydrogen-derived products.

We pursue these opportunities only where commercial structures provide returns consistent with our investment criteria.

PRINCIPAL PRODUCTS AND SERVICES

We provide midstream services across the life of a hydrocarbon molecule, from the point of production or refining to the point of consumption or export.

- Gathering. We provide low- and high-pressure gathering of natural gas and crude oil from wellheads and central delivery points. Related services include compression, dehydration, treating and condensate stabilization.

- Processing and fractionation. We separate natural gas into residue gas and a mixed NGL stream. We then fractionate the mixed NGL stream into purity products: ethane, propane, normal butane, isobutane and natural gasoline.

- Pipeline transportation. We provide interstate and intrastate transportation of natural gas, crude oil, refined products and NGLs. Service is offered under tariffs approved by regulators and under negotiated agreements.

- Storage. We provide storage in above-ground tanks and in solution-mined salt caverns. Customers use our storage to manage operations, balance seasonal demand and take advantage of market opportunities.

- Terminalling. We provide receipt, storage, blending, additive injection and redelivery of crude oil, refined products, NGLs and renewable fuels. These services are offered at inland and marine terminals, with connections to pipelines, rail, barges, ships and trucks.

- Marketing and logistics services. We purchase and resell crude oil, NGLs, natural gas and refined products. These activities help optimize the use of our own assets and give customers market access, scheduling support and supply aggregation.

OUR REPORTABLE SEGMENTS

Gathering and Processing

The Gathering and Processing segment provides wellhead-to-market services to oil and natural gas producers in several onshore resource plays. Its assets include:

- gathering pipelines;
- field and central compression;
- cryogenic and refrigeration processing plants;
- treating facilities that remove carbon dioxide, hydrogen sulfide and other impurities; and
- crude oil and produced water gathering systems in certain areas.

Volumes processed in this segment frequently flow onward into our Transportation and Storage segment. This linkage allows us to capture value at several points in the value chain.

Most contracts in this segment are long-term. Many include acreage dedications or minimum volume commitments. Fee types vary by agreement and include fixed fees per unit of throughput, cost-of-service structures, and percent-of-proceeds arrangements. Under a percent-of-proceeds arrangement, we keep a portion of the sales proceeds from residue gas and NGLs. Segment results depend mainly on producer drilling and completion activity. That activity is shaped by commodity prices, producer capital budgets, and the geology and economics of the basins we serve.

Transportation and Storage

The Transportation and Storage segment owns and operates long-haul and regional pipelines that carry natural gas, crude oil, refined products and NGLs. It also owns and operates the storage assets associated with those pipelines. Its assets include:

- interstate and intrastate natural gas pipelines that serve utilities, industrial users, power generators and liquefied natural gas export facilities;
- crude oil trunklines that connect producing regions with refining centers and export terminals;
- refined product pipelines that serve metropolitan demand markets; and
- NGL pipelines that deliver mixed and purity products to fractionation and petrochemical hubs.

The segment also includes above-ground tank farms and underground salt cavern storage for natural gas, NGLs and refined products.

Revenue in this segment comes mainly from fee-based services. Some services are priced under tariffs filed with federal or state regulators. Others are priced under negotiated rates in firm transportation and storage agreements. Many firm agreements include reservation or demand charges that are payable whether or not the customer ships or stores volumes. These charges add predictability to segment cash flows. Interruptible services and short-term storage arrangements supplement firm revenues and allow us to market available capacity.

Terminals and Marketing Services

The Terminals and Marketing Services segment operates our marine, inland, rail and truck-rack terminals. It also conducts our commodity marketing and logistics activities. The terminals offer:

- storage, blending and handling services for refiners, marketers, wholesalers and exporters; and
- dock capacity for loading and unloading vessels and barges serving domestic and international markets.

Our marketing activities purchase hydrocarbons at receipt points on or near our systems and resell them at downstream markets. In doing so, they raise utilization of our assets and give customers flexible supply and disposition options.

Revenue in this segment includes fees for terminalling throughput, storage and ancillary services. It also includes sales of commodities purchased in our marketing activities. Because marketing revenues reflect the gross value of the commodities sold, segment revenues can rise or fall sharply with commodity price levels, even when the margin we earn changes far less.

CUSTOMERS

Our customers span the energy value chain:

- independent and integrated exploration and production companies;
- refiners;
- petrochemical and industrial manufacturers;
- natural gas utilities and local distribution companies;
- electric power generators;
- liquefied natural gas exporters;
- wholesale fuel marketers; and
- commodity trading firms.

We serve several large customers under long-term agreements. Some of these customers account for a meaningful portion of revenue in individual segments. If a significant customer lost volumes, defaulted or declared bankruptcy, the results of the affected segment could be materially affected. For more information, see Item 1A, Risk Factors.

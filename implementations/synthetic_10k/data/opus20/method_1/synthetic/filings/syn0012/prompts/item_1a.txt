Item 1. Business

General

Venzen Industries, Inc. ("Venzen," the "Company," "we," "us" or "our") is a diversified industrial technology company. We design, manufacture, sell and service engineered products, systems and solutions for commercial, industrial, infrastructure, energy and transportation markets around the world. Our offerings help customers improve the efficiency, safety, reliability and environmental performance of the buildings, plants, networks and fleets they own or operate. We combine a broad base of proprietary hardware with embedded controls, software and lifecycle services. This allows us to support customers from initial system design and installation through long-term operation, maintenance, modernization and replacement.

Venzen is incorporated in the State of Delaware, and our corporate headquarters is located in Charlotte, North Carolina. We have manufacturing, engineering, distribution and service locations across North America, Europe, the Middle East, Africa, Latin America and the Asia Pacific region. We sell to customers in a large number of countries through a combination of direct sales and service personnel, independent distributors, dealers, manufacturers' representatives and original equipment manufacturer relationships.

For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024 ("fiscal 2024"), the Company reported revenue of $48,137 million, an increase of 8.0% compared with the fiscal year ended December 31, 2023 ("fiscal 2023"). Growth in fiscal 2024 reflected demand across our principal end markets, contributions from businesses acquired during the year, pricing actions and continued expansion of our aftermarket and services offerings. A discussion of our results of operations appears in Item 7, "Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations," of this Annual Report on Form 10-K.

Our long-term strategy rests on several priorities:

- Extend our positions in end markets supported by durable secular trends, including energy efficiency, electrification, automation, infrastructure renewal and the decarbonization of buildings, industry and transportation.
- Grow our installed base and the recurring aftermarket, service and software revenue associated with it.
- Invest in research, development and engineering to sustain technology leadership and bring differentiated products to market more quickly.
- Apply our operating system, which emphasizes lean manufacturing, continuous improvement, disciplined pricing and supply chain resilience, to improve productivity and quality across our businesses.
- Allocate capital with discipline among organic investment, targeted acquisitions that complement our existing portfolio, and the return of capital to stockholders.

Portfolio Development

Venzen has developed its current portfolio through a combination of organic growth and acquisitions. These actions have broadened our technology base, expanded our geographic reach and deepened our presence in attractive end markets. We have also divested businesses that we concluded were not core to our long-term strategy or that we believed would be better positioned under different ownership. During fiscal 2024, we completed several acquisitions that strengthened our capabilities in building controls, industrial automation software and power conversion. Their results have been included in our consolidated financial statements from the respective dates of acquisition. We continue to evaluate the composition of our portfolio regularly. We may pursue additional acquisitions, joint ventures, strategic investments or dispositions when we believe they will enhance long-term value for our stockholders.

Business Segments

We manage our operations through three operating segments, each of which is a reportable segment. Our segments are organized around the markets they serve and the technologies they employ. This structure is consistent with the manner in which our chief operating decision maker reviews financial information, evaluates performance and allocates resources.

- Climate and Building Technologies. This segment designs, manufactures, sells, installs and services heating, ventilation and air-conditioning ("HVAC") equipment, commercial refrigeration systems, building automation and controls, energy management software, and fire and life safety products. Its customers include owners and operators of commercial offices, healthcare facilities, educational institutions, data centers, retail and hospitality properties, government buildings and multi-family residential developments. Climate and Building Technologies also provides a broad range of technical services, including preventive maintenance, repair, retrofit, energy audits, remote monitoring and equipment replacement. These services are delivered through a network of company-operated service branches and authorized third-party service providers.

- Industrial Automation and Flow Control. This segment designs and manufactures motion control systems, programmable logic and distributed control systems, sensors and instrumentation, industrial valves, pumps, actuators and fluid handling equipment, together with related software and services. Its products are used in process industries such as chemicals, oil and gas, water and wastewater, pharmaceuticals, food and beverage, and mining. They are also used in discrete manufacturing industries such as automotive, packaging, electronics and general machinery. The segment's offerings help customers automate production, monitor and optimize the performance of critical assets, reduce unplanned downtime and comply with safety and environmental requirements.

- Transportation and Power Systems. This segment designs, manufactures and services power generation and distribution equipment, electrical switchgear, power conversion and energy storage systems, and engineered components and subsystems for rail, commercial vehicle, marine and aerospace applications. Its customers include electric utilities, independent power producers, transit authorities, railcar and locomotive builders, commercial vehicle manufacturers, airframers and their suppliers, and operators of large industrial and commercial facilities. Transportation and Power Systems also provides field services, spare parts, refurbishment and long-term service agreements that support the performance of equipment over extended operating lives.

Financial information about our reportable segments, including revenue and segment operating results, is included in the segment information note in the Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements in Item 8, "Financial Statements and Supplementary Data," of this Annual Report on Form 10-K.

Principal Products and Services

Our products and services span the full lifecycle of the assets our customers depend on. Our principal offerings include the following.

Equipment and systems. We manufacture a wide range of engineered equipment. This includes chillers, rooftop units, air handlers, heat pumps, variable refrigerant flow systems, commercial refrigeration cases and compressors, fire detection panels and suppression equipment, industrial valves and pumps, drives and motors, control hardware, medium- and low-voltage switchgear, transformers, inverters, battery energy storage enclosures, and propulsion, braking and thermal management components for transportation applications. Many of these products are designed to meet stringent customer specifications and industry standards. A significant portion is configured or engineered to order.

Controls and software. We provide embedded controls, building and plant management platforms, and cloud-based analytics applications. These connect our equipment, and in many cases third-party equipment, to provide visibility into system performance. Our software offerings support remote monitoring, fault detection and diagnostics, predictive maintenance, energy optimization, asset performance management and regulatory reporting. Software is increasingly delivered on a subscription basis, which we believe enhances the recurring nature of our revenue and strengthens our relationships with customers.

Aftermarket parts and services. Our large installed base generates ongoing demand for replacement parts, consumables, upgrades and services. We offer scheduled maintenance, emergency repair, inspection and testing, commissioning, retrofit and modernization, training and multi-year service agreements. We believe our service capabilities, technical expertise and proximity to customers are important competitive advantages. We also believe aftermarket activity generally provides a more stable source of revenue than new equipment sales, which tend to be more sensitive to economic and construction cycles.

Integrated solutions. For larger and more complex projects, we combine equipment, controls, software and services into integrated solutions designed to achieve specified outcomes. These outcomes may include energy savings, emissions reductions, uptime commitments or capacity expansions. In certain cases, we enter into performance-based contracts under which we guarantee specified levels of energy or operational savings over a defined period.

Customers

We serve a large and diverse customer base, ranging from small and mid-sized businesses to multinational corporations, utilities and government entities. Our principal customer groups include:

- Commercial and institutional building owners, property managers, general and mechanical contractors, and design engineers.
- Industrial manufacturers and process operators, engineering, procurement and construction firms, and system integrators.
- Electric utilities, independent power producers, renewable energy developers and data center operators.
- Original equipment manufacturers in the rail, commercial vehicle, marine and aerospace industries, together with fleet operators and transit agencies.
- Federal, state, local and foreign government agencies, including military and defense customers.

No single customer accounted for 10% or more of our consolidated revenue in fiscal 2024 or fiscal 2023. We do not believe that the loss of any individual customer would have a material adverse effect on our consolidated results of operations. A portion of our revenue is derived from contracts with government entities, directly or as a subcontractor. Such contracts are generally subject to termination for convenience and to audit, and they impose specific compliance obligations. These matters are described further under "Government Regulation" below and in Item 1A, "Risk Factors."

Item 1. Business

General

Venzen Industries, Inc. ("Venzen," the "Company," "we," "us" or "our") is a diversified industrial technology company. We design, manufacture, sell and service engineered products, systems and solutions for commercial, industrial, infrastructure, energy and transportation markets around the world. Our offerings help customers improve the efficiency, safety, reliability and environmental performance of the buildings, plants, networks and fleets they own or operate. We combine a broad base of proprietary hardware with embedded controls, software and lifecycle services. This allows us to support customers from initial system design and installation through long-term operation, maintenance, modernization and replacement.

Venzen is incorporated in the State of Delaware, and our corporate headquarters is located in Charlotte, North Carolina. We have manufacturing, engineering, distribution and service locations across North America, Europe, the Middle East, Africa, Latin America and the Asia Pacific region. We sell to customers in a large number of countries through a combination of direct sales and service personnel, independent distributors, dealers, manufacturers' representatives and original equipment manufacturer relationships.

For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024 ("fiscal 2024"), the Company reported revenue of $48,137 million, an increase of 8.0% compared with the fiscal year ended December 31, 2023 ("fiscal 2023"). Growth in fiscal 2024 reflected demand across our principal end markets, contributions from businesses acquired during the year, pricing actions and continued expansion of our aftermarket and services offerings. A discussion of our results of operations appears in Item 7, "Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations," of this Annual Report on Form 10-K.

Our long-term strategy rests on several priorities:

- Extend our positions in end markets supported by durable secular trends, including energy efficiency, electrification, automation, infrastructure renewal and the decarbonization of buildings, industry and transportation.
- Grow our installed base and the recurring aftermarket, service and software revenue associated with it.
- Invest in research, development and engineering to sustain technology leadership and bring differentiated products to market more quickly.
- Apply our operating system, which emphasizes lean manufacturing, continuous improvement, disciplined pricing and supply chain resilience, to improve productivity and quality across our businesses.
- Allocate capital with discipline among organic investment, targeted acquisitions that complement our existing portfolio, and the return of capital to stockholders.

Portfolio Development

Venzen has developed its current portfolio through a combination of organic growth and acquisitions. These actions have broadened our technology base, expanded our geographic reach and deepened our presence in attractive end markets. We have also divested businesses that we concluded were not core to our long-term strategy or that we believed would be better positioned under different ownership. During fiscal 2024, we completed several acquisitions that strengthened our capabilities in building controls, industrial automation software and power conversion. Their results have been included in our consolidated financial statements from the respective dates of acquisition. We continue to evaluate the composition of our portfolio regularly. We may pursue additional acquisitions, joint ventures, strategic investments or dispositions when we believe they will enhance long-term value for our stockholders.

Business Segments

We manage our operations through three operating segments, each of which is a reportable segment. Our segments are organized around the markets they serve and the technologies they employ. This structure is consistent with the manner in which our chief operating decision maker reviews financial information, evaluates performance and allocates resources.

- Climate and Building Technologies. This segment designs, manufactures, sells, installs and services heating, ventilation and air-conditioning ("HVAC") equipment, commercial refrigeration systems, building automation and controls, energy management software, and fire and life safety products. Its customers include owners and operators of commercial offices, healthcare facilities, educational institutions, data centers, retail and hospitality properties, government buildings and multi-family residential developments. Climate and Building Technologies also provides a broad range of technical services, including preventive maintenance, repair, retrofit, energy audits, remote monitoring and equipment replacement. These services are delivered through a network of company-operated service branches and authorized third-party service providers.

- Industrial Automation and Flow Control. This segment designs and manufactures motion control systems, programmable logic and distributed control systems, sensors and instrumentation, industrial valves, pumps, actuators and fluid handling equipment, together with related software and services. Its products are used in process industries such as chemicals, oil and gas, water and wastewater, pharmaceuticals, food and beverage, and mining. They are also used in discrete manufacturing industries such as automotive, packaging, electronics and general machinery. The segment's offerings help customers automate production, monitor and optimize the performance of critical assets, reduce unplanned downtime and comply with safety and environmental requirements.

- Transportation and Power Systems. This segment designs, manufactures and services power generation and distribution equipment, electrical switchgear, power conversion and energy storage systems, and engineered components and subsystems for rail, commercial vehicle, marine and aerospace applications. Its customers include electric utilities, independent power producers, transit authorities, railcar and locomotive builders, commercial vehicle manufacturers, airframers and their suppliers, and operators of large industrial and commercial facilities. Transportation and Power Systems also provides field services, spare parts, refurbishment and long-term service agreements that support the performance of equipment over extended operating lives.

Financial information about our reportable segments, including revenue and segment operating results, is included in the segment information note in the Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements in Item 8, "Financial Statements and Supplementary Data," of this Annual Report on Form 10-K.

Principal Products and Services

Our products and services span the full lifecycle of the assets our customers depend on. Our principal offerings include the following.

Equipment and systems. We manufacture a wide range of engineered equipment. This includes chillers, rooftop units, air handlers, heat pumps, variable refrigerant flow systems, commercial refrigeration cases and compressors, fire detection panels and suppression equipment, industrial valves and pumps, drives and motors, control hardware, medium- and low-voltage switchgear, transformers, inverters, battery energy storage enclosures, and propulsion, braking and thermal management components for transportation applications. Many of these products are designed to meet stringent customer specifications and industry standards. A significant portion is configured or engineered to order.

Controls and software. We provide embedded controls, building and plant management platforms, and cloud-based analytics applications. These connect our equipment, and in many cases third-party equipment, to provide visibility into system performance. Our software offerings support remote monitoring, fault detection and diagnostics, predictive maintenance, energy optimization, asset performance management and regulatory reporting. Software is increasingly delivered on a subscription basis, which we believe enhances the recurring nature of our revenue and strengthens our relationships with customers.

Aftermarket parts and services. Our large installed base generates ongoing demand for replacement parts, consumables, upgrades and services. We offer scheduled maintenance, emergency repair, inspection and testing, commissioning, retrofit and modernization, training and multi-year service agreements. We believe our service capabilities, technical expertise and proximity to customers are important competitive advantages. We also believe aftermarket activity generally provides a more stable source of revenue than new equipment sales, which tend to be more sensitive to economic and construction cycles.

Integrated solutions. For larger and more complex projects, we combine equipment, controls, software and services into integrated solutions designed to achieve specified outcomes. These outcomes may include energy savings, emissions reductions, uptime commitments or capacity expansions. In certain cases, we enter into performance-based contracts under which we guarantee specified levels of energy or operational savings over a defined period.

Customers

We serve a large and diverse customer base, ranging from small and mid-sized businesses to multinational corporations, utilities and government entities. Our principal customer groups include:

- Commercial and institutional building owners, property managers, general and mechanical contractors, and design engineers.
- Industrial manufacturers and process operators, engineering, procurement and construction firms, and system integrators.
- Electric utilities, independent power producers, renewable energy developers and data center operators.
- Original equipment manufacturers in the rail, commercial vehicle, marine and aerospace industries, together with fleet operators and transit agencies.
- Federal, state, local and foreign government agencies, including military and defense customers.

No single customer accounted for 10% or more of our consolidated revenue in fiscal 2024 or fiscal 2023. We do not believe that the loss of any individual customer would have a material adverse effect on our consolidated results of operations. A portion of our revenue is derived from contracts with government entities, directly or as a subcontractor. Such contracts are generally subject to termination for convenience and to audit, and they impose specific compliance obligations. These matters are described further under "Government Regulation" below and in Item 1A, "Risk Factors."

Sales, Marketing and Distribution

We go to market through multiple channels tailored to the requirements of each business, product line and geography.

Direct sales and service. Our direct sales organization includes account managers, application engineers and solution specialists. They work closely with customers, consulting engineers and specifiers to design systems that meet project requirements. In our Climate and Building Technologies segment in particular, a network of company-operated sales and service branches provides direct access to building owners and facility managers. This supports both new equipment sales and recurring service relationships.

Distributors, dealers and representatives. We sell a significant portion of our standardized products through independent wholesale distributors, dealers, contractors and manufacturers' representatives. These channel partners extend our geographic coverage, hold local inventory and provide installation and first-line support. We maintain partner programs that provide training, technical support, marketing resources and incentive arrangements to strengthen these relationships.

Original equipment manufacturers and system integrators. Many of our components and subsystems are incorporated into products manufactured by others. We work with original equipment manufacturers on long-term supply programs, often beginning early in the customer's product development cycle. System integrators also specify and install our automation and control products as part of broader industrial projects.

Digital channels. We continue to invest in digital commerce capabilities, online configuration tools and customer portals. These allow customers and partners to select products, check availability, place orders, track shipments, access technical documentation and manage service requests.

Our marketing activities include participation in industry trade shows and technical conferences, digital and print advertising, application and training centers, and thought leadership on topics such as energy efficiency, electrification and industrial productivity. Our major brands are well recognized in the markets they serve. We believe they are associated with quality, reliability and technical expertise.

Competition

The markets in which we compete are highly competitive. In most of our product lines, we face a mix of large multinational industrial conglomerates, specialized global manufacturers and numerous regional and local competitors. In certain markets, we also compete with emerging technology companies, software providers and new entrants offering digital, connected or electrified alternatives to traditional products. In our services businesses, we compete with independent service providers, mechanical and electrical contractors, and the in-house maintenance organizations of our customers.

The principal competitive factors in our markets include product performance and quality, technology and innovation, energy efficiency, price, total cost of ownership, delivery lead times, breadth of product offering, application engineering capability, service coverage and responsiveness, brand reputation, and the ability to provide integrated solutions. For large projects, competitive factors also include project management expertise, financial strength and, in some cases, the availability of financing or performance guarantees. A substantial portion of our business is awarded through competitive bidding or negotiated contracts.

We believe we are well positioned to compete because of the breadth of our portfolio, our installed base, our global manufacturing and service footprint, our engineering and software capabilities, and the strength of our brands and channel relationships. Competitive pressures, however, may affect our pricing, margins and market share. These factors are discussed in Item 1A, "Risk Factors."

Research, Development and Engineering

Innovation is central to our strategy. We conduct research, development and engineering activities at technology centers and engineering facilities in North America, Europe and Asia, as well as within our individual businesses. These activities focus on improving the energy efficiency and environmental performance of our products, transitioning to lower global warming potential refrigerants, advancing electrification and power electronics, developing sensing, connectivity and analytics capabilities, and enhancing the cybersecurity of our connected offerings. We also collaborate with universities, research institutions, customers and suppliers on selected development programs. In addition, we maintain an investment program that evaluates early-stage technologies relevant to our markets.

Raw Materials and Supply Chain

We purchase a wide variety of raw materials, components and services from suppliers around the world. Principal raw materials include steel, aluminum, copper and other metals, plastics and resins, and refrigerants. Key purchased components include semiconductors, electronic assemblies, motors, compressors, castings, forgings and batteries. We generally source materials and components from multiple suppliers, although certain items are available only from a limited number of qualified sources. We seek to mitigate supply risk through dual sourcing, long-term supply agreements, regional manufacturing, strategic inventory positioning and supplier development programs. We also use pricing actions and, in some cases, contractual pass-through mechanisms to manage the effects of commodity price fluctuations. We believe our sources of supply are adequate for our current requirements. However, disruptions in the availability of key materials or components could adversely affect our operations, as discussed in Item 1A, "Risk Factors."

Seasonality

Our business is subject to seasonal patterns that vary by segment and geography. In our Climate and Building Technologies segment, demand for HVAC equipment and related services in the Northern Hemisphere is generally higher in the second and third calendar quarters, reflecting cooling season demand and higher construction activity during warmer months. Demand for heating products and services tends to increase in the fourth and first quarters. In our Industrial Automation and Flow Control and Transportation and Power Systems segments, revenue is influenced by the timing of customer capital spending, project completions and shipments of large engineered systems. These are often concentrated in the fourth quarter as customers seek to complete projects and use available budgets before the end of their fiscal years. Weather conditions, the timing of large orders and shifts in project schedules may cause our quarterly results to vary. Accordingly, results for any interim period are not necessarily indicative of results for the full year.

Intellectual Property

We rely on a combination of patents, trademarks, copyrights, trade secrets, confidentiality procedures and contractual arrangements to protect our intellectual property. We own a substantial portfolio of patents and pending patent applications in the United States and in other countries. These relate to product designs, manufacturing processes, control algorithms, software and system architectures. We also own numerous registered and unregistered trademarks and trade names, many of which are well known in the markets we serve. In addition, we license certain technologies from third parties and license certain of our technologies to others.

We believe our intellectual property, taken as a whole, is important to our business. We do not consider any single patent, trademark or license, or any related group of them, to be material to our business as a whole or to any of our reportable segments. We actively monitor and enforce our intellectual property rights, and we seek to respect the valid intellectual property rights of others. Our ability to protect our intellectual property may be limited in certain jurisdictions, and we may face claims of infringement from third parties, as described in Item 1A, "Risk Factors."

Government Regulation

Our operations and products are subject to a wide range of federal, state, local and foreign laws and regulations. Compliance with these requirements is an integral part of how we design products and conduct our business. Significant areas of regulation include the following.

Environmental, health and safety. We are subject to laws and regulations governing air emissions, wastewater discharges, the generation, handling, storage, transportation and disposal of hazardous materials and wastes, and the investigation and remediation of contaminated sites. We have been, and may in the future be, identified as a potentially responsible party at sites where we or our predecessors disposed of waste or conducted operations. Our facilities are also subject to occupational health and safety requirements. We maintain environmental, health and safety management systems and programs designed to promote compliance and continuous improvement. We do not currently expect the costs of complying with existing environmental, health and safety laws to have a material adverse effect on our capital expenditures, earnings or competitive position. However, future requirements, newly discovered conditions or changes in enforcement could result in additional costs.

Energy efficiency and refrigerants. Many of our products are subject to minimum energy efficiency standards, labeling requirements and testing protocols established by government agencies in the United States and other jurisdictions. Our HVAC and refrigeration products are also subject to regulations that restrict or phase down the production and use of certain refrigerants with high global warming potential. These regulations require us to redesign products, qualify alternative refrigerants, retool manufacturing facilities and train our service personnel and channel partners. While these transitions create compliance costs and execution risk, we believe they also create opportunities to sell higher-efficiency, lower-emission equipment and related services.

Product safety and standards. Our products must comply with applicable product safety regulations and with industry codes and standards, including building, fire, electrical and pressure equipment codes. Many products must be tested and certified by independent laboratories before they may be sold or installed. Products for rail, aerospace and certain energy applications are subject to additional certification and qualification requirements imposed by regulators and customers.

Government contracting. Our contracts with the United States government and other government entities are subject to procurement regulations that govern cost accounting, pricing, socioeconomic requirements, cybersecurity and other matters. These contracts may be subject to audit, investigation and termination for convenience. Violations of government contracting requirements could result in penalties, including suspension or debarment from future government business.

Trade, export controls and sanctions. Because we operate globally, we are subject to customs and import regulations, tariffs, export control laws, and economic sanctions administered by the United States and other governments. Certain of our products, technologies and software are subject to export licensing requirements. Changes in trade policy, including new or increased tariffs and retaliatory measures, may affect the cost of our materials and components and the competitiveness of our products in certain markets.

Anti-corruption and competition. We are subject to the U.S. Foreign Corrupt Practices Act and similar anti-bribery laws in other jurisdictions, as well as to antitrust and competition laws. We maintain policies, training programs and internal controls designed to promote compliance with these laws by our employees and by third parties acting on our behalf.

Data privacy and cybersecurity. Our connected products, software platforms and internal information systems are subject to evolving laws and regulations concerning data privacy, data protection, cybersecurity and the cross-border transfer of data. We maintain a cybersecurity program designed to protect our systems, products and customer data. This program is described in Item 1C, "Cybersecurity."

Climate-related disclosure. We are subject to emerging requirements in several jurisdictions relating to the measurement and disclosure of greenhouse gas emissions and other climate-related information. We continue to monitor these developments and to enhance our data collection and reporting processes accordingly.

Human Capital

As of December 31, 2024, Venzen employed about 72,500 people worldwide. Our workforce includes manufacturing and production employees, field service technicians, engineers and scientists, software developers, sales and marketing professionals, and corporate and administrative personnel. A portion of our employees, primarily outside the United States, are represented by labor unions or works councils or are covered by collective bargaining agreements. We believe our relationships with our employees and their representatives are generally good.

Our people are fundamental to our ability to serve customers and execute our strategy. Our human capital programs are overseen by our senior leadership team. The Compensation and Human Capital Committee of our Board of Directors reviews them periodically. Key elements of our approach include the following.

Health and safety. The safety of our employees, contractors and customers is our highest operational priority. We maintain a global safety management system that establishes common standards for hazard identification, risk assessment, incident reporting and investigation, and corrective action. Our leaders review safety performance metrics regularly, and we encourage every employee to stop work they believe is unsafe. We also provide programs that support the physical and mental well-being of our employees.

Talent acquisition and development. We compete for talent in skilled trades, engineering, software and other specialized fields. To attract and retain qualified employees, we recruit from a broad range of sources, including technical schools, colleges and universities, military transition programs and experienced professional networks. We offer apprenticeship and technician training programs, engineering rotational programs, leadership development curricula and tuition assistance. We also operate an internal learning platform that provides technical, compliance and professional development content to employees across our businesses.

Compensation and benefits. We seek to provide compensation and benefits that are competitive in the markets in which we operate and that reward performance. Our programs include base pay, annual incentive plans, long-term equity incentives for eligible employees, retirement savings plans, health and welfare benefits, and paid time off, with specific offerings varying by country and local practice. We periodically review our pay practices to support fair and consistent compensation.

Inclusion and engagement. We believe that a workforce with a broad range of backgrounds, experiences and perspectives strengthens our ability to innovate and to understand the needs of our customers. We strive to maintain a respectful and inclusive workplace in which all employees can contribute and advance. We conduct periodic employee engagement surveys. Leadership teams use the results to identify areas for improvement and develop action plans.

Ethics and conduct. Our Code of Business Conduct sets out the standards of integrity we expect of all directors, officers and employees. We provide regular training on the Code and on related compliance topics. Employees and third parties may report concerns through multiple channels, including a confidential, independently operated helpline that permits anonymous reporting where allowed by local law. We prohibit retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

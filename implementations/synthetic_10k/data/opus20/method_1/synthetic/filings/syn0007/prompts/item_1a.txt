Item 1. Business

Unless the context indicates otherwise, references in this Annual Report on Form 10-K to "Oral Communications," "ORAL," the "Company," "we," "us" and "our" mean Oral Communications, Inc. and its consolidated subsidiaries. Oral Communications, Inc. is a Delaware corporation, and its principal executive offices are located in Reston, Virginia. Our common stock trades under the ticker symbol "ORAL." Our fiscal year ends on December 31, and references to fiscal 2024 mean the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024.

Overview

Oral Communications is one of the largest providers of communications, connectivity and related technology services in the United States. We own and operate a nationwide wireless network, an extensive fiber-optic and copper wireline network, and a portfolio of data centers, network facilities and other infrastructure assets. Through these assets, we provide voice, data, video and managed services to consumers, small and medium-sized businesses, large enterprises, government agencies, and other carriers and service providers.

Our mission is to connect people, businesses and communities through networks that are reliable, secure and ready to adapt to changing customer demand. We believe that the scale of our network, the breadth of our spectrum holdings, the reach of our distribution channels and the strength of our brand position us to compete effectively across the markets we serve.

For fiscal 2024, we reported total revenue of $119,056 million, which decreased 8.5% compared with fiscal 2023. The decrease reflected continued declines in legacy wireline voice and data services, lower equipment revenue, and competitive pricing pressure in certain markets, partially offset by growth in our fiber broadband, fixed wireless access and wireless data offerings. A more detailed discussion of our results of operations appears in Item 7, "Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations."

Industry Trends

The communications industry continues to undergo significant structural change. Customers are consuming more data across more devices, and demand for high-speed, low-latency connectivity continues to grow in both the consumer and business markets. At the same time, traditional revenue streams such as fixed-line voice, legacy data transport and bundled linear video continue to decline as customers migrate to wireless, internet-based and over-the-top alternatives.

Competition for wireless subscribers remains intense, with providers using device promotions, unlimited data plans, bundled streaming content and multi-line discounts to attract and retain customers. The entry of cable operators into the wireless market through mobile virtual network arrangements, and the growth of fixed wireless access as an alternative to wired broadband, have broadened the competitive field. In the business market, enterprises are increasingly replacing legacy private-line and circuit-based services with software-defined networking, cloud-based communications and managed security offerings, which has compressed revenue from certain traditional products even as it creates opportunities in newer services.

We expect these trends to continue to affect our operations and results. We are responding by investing in fiber deployment, fifth-generation ("5G") wireless capacity and next-generation business solutions, while simplifying our product portfolio and reducing the cost of operating our legacy networks. The ultimate effect of these trends on our business will depend on future developments, including customer adoption patterns, competitor behavior and regulatory actions, which are uncertain and difficult to predict.

Our Strategy

Our strategy is built on the following priorities:

- Network leadership. We intend to maintain a high-quality, high-capacity wireless and wireline network by continuing to deploy 5G spectrum, densify our network in high-traffic areas, and extend our fiber footprint to additional homes and businesses.

- Broadband growth. We seek to expand our share of the home and small business broadband market through fiber-to-the-premises and fixed wireless access, and to offer converged packages that combine mobile and home connectivity.

- Business transformation. We are repositioning our business offerings toward managed network services, private wireless networks, security, edge computing and the Internet of Things ("IoT"), while managing the planned decline of legacy products.

- Operational efficiency. We continue to pursue cost reductions through process automation, digital self-service, real estate consolidation, the retirement of legacy network elements and disciplined procurement.

- Capital discipline. We intend to allocate capital toward investments that we believe will generate attractive long-term returns, while returning capital to stockholders and maintaining a balance sheet that supports our investment-grade credit profile.

Principal Products and Services

We offer a broad portfolio of communications products and services, which we group into the following principal categories.

Wireless services. We provide postpaid and prepaid wireless voice, messaging and data services to consumers and businesses on our nationwide network. Our plans range from entry-level prepaid offerings to premium unlimited plans that include features such as high-definition video streaming, mobile hotspot capability, international roaming and bundled content subscriptions. We also provide connectivity for wearables, tablets, connected vehicles and other IoT devices.

Wireless equipment. We sell smartphones, tablets, wearables, hotspots and accessories manufactured by third parties. Customers may purchase devices outright or through installment plans, and we periodically offer device promotions in connection with service activations and upgrades. Equipment revenue fluctuates with device launch cycles, upgrade rates and promotional activity.

Fixed broadband. We provide residential and small business internet access over our fiber-to-the-premises network, our fixed wireless access service delivered over our 5G and fourth-generation ("4G") networks, and, in certain areas, our legacy copper-based digital subscriber line network. Our fiber offerings provide symmetrical multi-gigabit speeds, and we offer whole-home Wi-Fi equipment and related support services.

Voice and video. We continue to provide traditional landline voice services and internet-based voice services to residential and business customers. In certain markets, we offer video services through partnerships with third-party content distributors and through our own internet-delivered platforms.

Business and enterprise solutions. We offer a comprehensive suite of solutions for business and government customers, including dedicated internet access, ethernet, wavelength and private-line services, software-defined wide area networking, unified communications and collaboration, contact center solutions, managed security, private 5G networks, mobile edge computing, and IoT fleet and asset-management solutions.

Wholesale and infrastructure services. We provide network access, transport, backhaul, roaming and tower and fiber leasing services to other telecommunications carriers, cable operators, mobile virtual network operators, content providers and hyperscale cloud operators.

Segments

As of December 31, 2024, we managed our business through three reportable segments: Consumer, Business, and Wholesale and Network Services. Our segments reflect the manner in which our chief operating decision maker reviews financial information, allocates resources and assesses performance. Financial information for our segments is included in Item 7, "Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations," and in the notes to the consolidated financial statements included in Item 8, "Financial Statements and Supplementary Data."

Consumer

Our Consumer segment provides wireless and wireline communications services and equipment to individual and household customers throughout the United States. Its principal offerings include postpaid and prepaid wireless service, wireless devices and accessories, fiber and fixed wireless broadband, home voice, and related value-added services such as device protection, cloud storage and bundled entertainment subscriptions.

The Consumer segment is our largest segment by revenue and customer count. Its performance is driven primarily by the number of wireless and broadband connections, average revenue per account, customer churn, and the mix of premium and entry-level plans. Our recent focus in this segment has been on migrating customers to higher-value unlimited plans, expanding fiber availability, growing fixed wireless access in areas where fiber is not yet economical, and encouraging customers to combine mobile and home broadband services, which we believe improves customer loyalty.

The Consumer segment also includes our prepaid brands, which serve value-conscious customers through national retail partners and our own stores, and which offer service without a credit check or long-term commitment.

Business

Our Business segment provides wireless and wireline communications, networking and technology solutions to small and medium-sized businesses, large enterprises, federal, state and local government agencies, and educational and healthcare institutions. Offerings include business wireless plans and devices, fixed broadband and dedicated internet access, data networking, managed and professional services, security solutions, private wireless networks, IoT connectivity and platforms, and unified communications.

Revenue in this segment depends on the number of business wireless connections, the volume of network services purchased, contract renewals and pricing, and the adoption of newer solutions. Legacy voice and data products continue to decline as customers move to internet-based and software-defined alternatives. We are seeking to offset these declines by expanding sales of wireless, fixed wireless and fiber connectivity to small businesses, and by growing our portfolio of managed, security and edge computing services for larger customers.

Government customers, including agencies of the federal government, purchase services from us under a variety of contract vehicles. These contracts frequently include terms that are specific to government procurement, such as rights of termination for convenience, and are subject to audit and appropriations risk.

Wholesale and Network Services

Our Wholesale and Network Services segment provides access, transport and infrastructure services to other communications providers and large technology companies. Its principal offerings include special access and ethernet backhaul to wireless towers, long-haul and metro fiber transport, dark fiber and wavelength leasing, colocation, domestic and international roaming, voice termination, and network access for mobile virtual network operators that resell wireless service under their own brands.

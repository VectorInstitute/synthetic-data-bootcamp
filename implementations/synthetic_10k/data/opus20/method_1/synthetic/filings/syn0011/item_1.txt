Item 1. Business

Overview

Novmer Materials, Inc. ("Novmer," the "Company," "we," "us" or "our") supplies construction materials, engineered building products and specialty minerals. Our customers build, maintain and renovate infrastructure, commercial and industrial facilities, and residential communities. In fiscal 2024, which ended December 31, 2024, we generated total revenue of $20,255 million.

Our business rests on long-lived, strategically located mineral reserves and manufacturing facilities. We combine these assets with technical know-how, logistics networks and close relationships with customers in the communities we serve. We extract, process, manufacture and deliver materials that are essential to modern construction. Many of these materials are heavy and costly to transport relative to their value. As a result, local market position, proximity to customers and dependable supply are central to how we compete.

We serve customers in the following end markets:

- Public infrastructure, including highways, streets, bridges, airports, rail, water and wastewater systems.
- Non-residential construction, including industrial, manufacturing, warehouse, data center, energy, healthcare, education and commercial projects.
- Residential construction, including new single-family and multi-family housing as well as repair and remodeling activity.

Demand across these end markets is shaped by public funding levels, private investment, interest rates, population growth, household formation and general economic conditions. The mix of these factors varies across our geographic markets.

Our strategy has four parts:

- Strengthen our position in our core local markets through disciplined operations and customer service.
- Expand our portfolio of higher-value engineered products.
- Invest in reserves, plants and logistics assets that support long-term growth.
- Pursue acquisitions that complement our existing footprint and can be integrated efficiently.

During fiscal 2024, we continued to invest in property, plant and equipment and to pursue complementary acquisitions. Our objective is to broaden our geographic reach and deepen our product offering in markets where we see attractive long-term demand.

Novmer Materials, Inc. was incorporated in Delaware. Our corporate headquarters is in Atlanta, Georgia. Our common stock trades under the ticker symbol "NOMV."

Principal Products and Services

We organize our offerings into three broad categories that together cover a large part of the construction value chain.

Construction Materials

Our construction materials business produces:

- Crushed stone, sand and gravel, which together make up construction aggregates.
- Cement and supplementary cementitious materials.
- Ready-mixed concrete.
- Asphalt mix.

Aggregates are the primary raw material for concrete and asphalt and are also used as base material for roads, building foundations and railroad ballast. Cement is the binding agent in concrete. We supply it in bulk and in bags to concrete producers, contractors and distributors. Our ready-mixed concrete and asphalt operations use our own aggregates and cement wherever practical. This vertical integration helps secure supply, improve plant utilization and capture value across successive stages of production. In many markets we also provide paving and related construction services, mainly for public road agencies and private developers.

Engineered Building Products

Our engineered building products business designs and manufactures products for both above-ground and below-ground construction applications, including:

- Precast and prestressed concrete structures.
- Concrete and polymer pipe.
- Drainage and stormwater management systems.
- Utility vaults and enclosures.
- Architectural and structural masonry.
- Building envelope components.

Many of these products are engineered to customer or project specifications. Our technical staff frequently work with engineers, architects and contractors during the design phase to recommend solutions that cut installation time, improve durability or meet regulatory and performance requirements. This business also includes a range of products sold through distribution channels to professional contractors and, to a lesser extent, to do-it-yourself consumers. These include pavers, retaining wall systems and packaged concrete mixes.

Specialty Minerals

Our specialty minerals business extracts and processes industrial minerals for applications that require consistent chemical and physical properties. Products include:

- Lime and limestone-based products.
- Industrial sands.
- Ground and precipitated calcium carbonate.
- Other mineral fillers and additives.

These products are used in steelmaking, environmental remediation, flue gas treatment, water treatment, glass manufacturing, paper and packaging, plastics, paints and coatings, and agriculture. Sales in this business depend more on reliable quality, technical service and long-term supply arrangements than on construction cycles. This provides a measure of diversification against the cyclicality of our construction-oriented businesses.

Reportable Segments

We manage our operations through three reportable segments: Construction Materials, Engineered Products and Specialty Minerals. Each segment is overseen by its own leadership team. Our chief operating decision maker reviews each segment's performance and allocates resources among them. Financial information about our reportable segments is presented in the notes to our consolidated financial statements included elsewhere in this Annual Report on Form 10-K.

Construction Materials Segment

The Construction Materials segment operates quarries, sand and gravel pits, cement plants, ready-mixed concrete plants and asphalt plants. These are supported by a network of rail, barge and truck distribution terminals. This segment is our most asset-intensive business. Its performance depends heavily on:

- The quality, quantity and location of our mineral reserves.
- The efficiency of our extraction and processing operations.
- The cost of energy, fuel and freight.

We seek to keep a reserve position that supports operations for many years at current production levels in each local market. Permitting new reserves is often lengthy, complex and uncertain, which can create significant barriers to entry. Revenue in this segment is closely tied to public infrastructure spending and to commercial and residential construction volumes in the regions where we operate. Pricing is set mostly at the local level and reflects supply and demand conditions, transportation costs and the competitive landscape in each market.

Engineered Products Segment

The Engineered Products segment manufactures precast concrete, pipe, drainage, utility infrastructure, masonry and building envelope products at plants generally located near major metropolitan areas and growth corridors. Value in this segment comes from three sources:

- Engineering and design capabilities.
- Manufacturing quality and consistency.
- On-time delivery to project sites, often coordinated closely with construction schedules.

A substantial portion of this segment's sales is linked to specific projects and governed by project specifications. Specifications can be influenced by early collaboration with designers and owners. The segment also includes branded products sold through home improvement retailers, building supply dealers and landscape distributors. Demand drivers include water and stormwater infrastructure investment, utility and telecommunications expansion, non-residential construction and residential activity, including repair and remodeling.

Specialty Minerals Segment

The Specialty Minerals segment mines and processes high-purity mineral deposits. It manufactures lime, industrial sand and calcium carbonate products for a diverse group of industrial customers. Many customers buy under multi-year supply agreements, and some of our facilities are located next to or near customer plants. Success in this segment depends on:

- Access to high-quality deposits.
- Process technology and product consistency.
- Energy efficiency, since lime production in particular is energy intensive.
- The ability to offer technical support that helps customers optimize their own processes.

End markets for this segment include steel, environmental services, water treatment, glass, paper and packaging, plastics and agriculture.

Customers

Our customer base is broad and diverse. It includes:

- Federal, state and local governmental agencies and the contractors that perform public works projects for them.
- General contractors, subcontractors and specialty contractors.
- Commercial, industrial and residential developers and homebuilders.
- Ready-mixed concrete producers, precast producers and other building materials manufacturers.
- Building materials distributors, dealers and home improvement retailers.
- Utilities and telecommunications companies.
- Industrial manufacturers in the steel, glass, paper, chemical and agricultural sectors.

In our Construction Materials and Engineered Products segments, most customers buy for specific projects. Purchases are typically made under purchase orders or short-term contracts for an individual project, although some larger customers place recurring orders throughout the year. Public infrastructure work is generally awarded through competitive bidding by our contractor customers. We then supply materials to the winning bidders under quoted prices that may be fixed for the duration of a project. In the Specialty Minerals segment, a larger share of revenue is generated under longer-term supply agreements. These agreements may include provisions for price adjustments tied to energy or other input costs.

No single customer accounted for a material portion of our consolidated revenue in fiscal 2024. We believe our broad base of customers across end markets and regions reduces our dependence on any single customer or project. However, the loss of certain large customers could have a meaningful effect on the results of individual operations or local markets.

Sales and Marketing

We sell our products mainly through our own direct sales organization. It is structured around local markets, customer types and product lines. Our sales professionals maintain long-standing relationships with contractors, developers, engineers, architects, public agencies and industrial customers. Because construction materials are typically sourced locally, our local sales and operations teams have significant autonomy to respond quickly to customer needs, quote prices, schedule deliveries and coordinate with project managers.

In our Engineered Products segment, we supplement direct sales with technical sales and engineering support teams. These teams work with project designers to develop specifications and recommend product solutions. They also provide design assistance, product data, installation guidance and field support. For branded products sold through retail and distribution channels, we use dedicated account management teams, merchandising programs and promotional activities to support sell-through. In the Specialty Minerals segment, our sales personnel typically have technical backgrounds. They work with customer operations staff to tailor product characteristics to specific process requirements.

We deliver products using a combination of:

- Company-operated truck fleets and third-party haulers.
- Rail and barge transportation.
- A network of distribution yards and terminals.

Efficient logistics are critical to competitiveness, especially for aggregates and cement, where transportation can represent a significant portion of the delivered cost. We continue to invest in dispatch, fleet management and digital ordering tools to improve service levels, asset utilization and customer convenience.

Competition

The markets in which we operate are highly competitive. Because transportation costs limit the economic shipping distance for most of our products, competition takes place mainly at the local and regional level. In each local market we compete with a mix of:

- Large national and multinational building materials producers.
- Regional companies.
- Numerous smaller, privately held operators.
- In some cases, governmental entities that operate their own quarries or plants.

We also face competition from imported cement and from alternative products and materials. Examples include steel, wood, plastic and composite products that may substitute for concrete-based solutions in certain applications.

The principal competitive factors in our businesses are:

- Price.
- Proximity of production facilities and reserves to customers.
- Product quality and consistency.
- Reliability and timeliness of delivery.
- Technical service and engineering support.
- Breadth of product offering.
- The ability to supply large and complex projects.

In the Engineered Products segment, design capabilities and the ability to have our products specified on projects are also important. In the Specialty Minerals segment, chemical purity, particle characteristics, process know-how and security of supply are important differentiators.

We believe we compete effectively because of our well-located reserves and plants, our vertically integrated operations in many markets, our breadth of products and services, our technical expertise and our long-standing customer relationships. Our competitors may nonetheless have greater resources, lower costs or stronger positions in particular markets. In addition, new capacity, imports or aggressive pricing by competitors could adversely affect our results.

Raw Materials and Energy

Our most important raw materials are the mineral deposits we own or lease, including limestone, granite, sand, gravel and other minerals. We also purchase:

- Cement and supplementary cementitious materials to supplement our own production in certain markets.
- Liquid asphalt for our asphalt operations.
- Steel reinforcing materials, resins and admixtures for our engineered products.
- Substantial quantities of energy, including diesel fuel, natural gas, coal, petroleum coke, electricity and alternative fuels.

Prices for energy and certain purchased materials can be volatile. We seek to manage these exposures through supplier diversification, procurement programs, contractual pricing mechanisms and, in some cases, fixed-price purchase arrangements. Raw materials and energy have generally been available in adequate quantities. However, supply disruptions or significant price increases could affect our operations and margins.

Seasonality

Our business is seasonal. The construction industry operates at reduced levels during the winter months in many of our markets because of cold temperatures, snow and shorter daylight hours. Heavy rainfall and other adverse weather can disrupt construction activity at any time of year. As a result, demand for our construction materials and engineered products is typically lower in the first and fourth calendar quarters and higher in the second and third quarters. Our revenues, operating results and cash flows therefore vary from quarter to quarter.

We often use the winter months to perform scheduled maintenance at our plants and to build inventories ahead of the peak construction season, which affects working capital requirements during the year. Severe weather events, such as hurricanes, flooding, wildfires and droughts, can disrupt our operations and our customers' projects. They can also, in some cases, generate later demand for reconstruction and repair. Our Specialty Minerals segment is less seasonal than our construction-oriented segments, although certain of its end markets, such as agriculture, have their own seasonal patterns.

Intellectual Property

We own or license a range of intellectual property that supports our businesses, including:

- Patents, trademarks and trade names.
- Trade secrets and proprietary process know-how.
- Product designs and software.

Our trademarks and brand names are important to the marketing of certain products in our Engineered Products segment, especially those sold through retail and distribution channels. Patents and proprietary designs protect certain of our precast, drainage, stormwater management and building envelope products. Our Specialty Minerals segment relies significantly on proprietary process technologies and production know-how.

We protect our intellectual property through a combination of patent, trademark, copyright and trade secret laws, as well as confidentiality and license agreements with employees, customers, suppliers and other third parties. While our intellectual property is valuable in the aggregate, we do not believe any single patent, trademark or license is material to our business as a whole. We continue to pursue product and process improvements, including lower-carbon cement and concrete formulations and the increased use of recycled and alternative materials. Some of these may result in new intellectual property.

Government Regulation

Our operations are subject to extensive and frequently changing federal, state and local laws and regulations. These include laws governing land use, zoning, mining and reclamation, environmental protection, health and safety, transportation and employment. Compliance requires significant management attention and the commitment of capital and operating resources. We believe we are in substantial compliance with applicable laws and regulations. However, the costs of compliance and potential liabilities under these laws could increase in the future.

Environmental Matters

Our operations are subject to environmental laws and regulations relating to:

- Air emissions, including particulate matter, nitrogen oxides, sulfur dioxide and greenhouse gases.
- Water discharges and stormwater management.
- The handling, storage and disposal of solid and hazardous materials and wastes.
- The remediation of contaminated sites.
- The protection of wetlands, endangered species and other natural resources.

Cement and lime manufacturing are energy-intensive processes that generate significant carbon dioxide emissions. They are subject to air permitting requirements under the federal Clean Air Act and comparable state laws. Existing and potential future laws, regulations, disclosure requirements and international agreements relating to climate change could increase our operating costs or capital expenditures, affect demand for certain products, or create opportunities for lower-carbon products. We monitor these developments closely. We are working to reduce the emissions intensity of our operations through energy efficiency improvements, alternative fuels, supplementary cementitious materials and blended cement products.

Under certain environmental laws, including the federal Comprehensive Environmental Response, Compensation, and Liability Act and analogous state laws, we may be liable for the costs of investigating and remediating contamination at properties we currently or formerly owned or operated, or at third-party sites where wastes from our operations were sent. Liability may arise regardless of fault.

Mining, Permitting and Reclamation

Our quarrying and mining operations require numerous governmental permits and approvals. These cover the extraction of minerals, the use of land, blasting, noise, dust, traffic and the protection of groundwater. Obtaining permits for new reserves or expansions of existing sites is often time-consuming and costly, and may face opposition from local communities or other stakeholders. We are generally required to reclaim mined property after extraction is complete and to provide financial assurance for those reclamation obligations. We recognize asset retirement obligations for these reclamation costs in accordance with applicable accounting standards.

Health and Safety

Our quarrying and mining operations are subject to the federal Mine Safety and Health Act of 1977, as amended, which is administered by the Mine Safety and Health Administration. Our other operations are subject to the federal Occupational Safety and Health Act and comparable state laws. These laws impose requirements on workplace safety, training, equipment and recordkeeping. Our facilities are subject to periodic inspections, and violations can result in citations, fines or orders to suspend operations. Information about mine safety violations and other regulatory matters required by Section 1503(a) of the Dodd-Frank Wall Street Reform and Consumer Protection Act is included in an exhibit to this Annual Report on Form 10-K.

Transportation and Other Matters

Our truck fleets are subject to regulation by the U.S. Department of Transportation and state transportation authorities regarding vehicle weight, driver qualifications, hours of service and safety. Our rail and barge shipments depend on third-party carriers whose operations are also regulated. In addition, a significant portion of our revenue depends on public infrastructure spending. That spending is influenced by federal, state and local funding legislation, budget appropriations and procurement rules.

Human Capital

As of December 31, 2024, we employed about 65,500 people. Our employees include:

- Equipment operators, drivers and plant production workers.
- Maintenance technicians.
- Engineers, geologists and technical specialists.
- Sales professionals.
- Administrative and management personnel.

A portion of our workforce is represented by labor unions under collective bargaining agreements, which are negotiated locally and expire at various times. We consider our relations with our employees and their representatives to be good.

Safety

Safety is our foremost operating priority and a core value across the Company. Our operations involve heavy mobile equipment, blasting, high-temperature kilns, moving machinery and heavy loads. We maintain comprehensive safety management systems, including:

- Hazard identification and risk assessment processes.
- Mandatory training programs.
- Incident investigation and corrective action procedures.
- Contractor safety requirements.

Leaders at every level are accountable for safety performance. We encourage every employee to stop work they believe to be unsafe. We track leading and lagging safety indicators, and our senior leadership team and board of directors review them regularly.

Talent Development and Engagement

We seek to attract, develop and retain a skilled and engaged workforce. Our programs include technical and skills training, apprenticeships, leadership development, tuition assistance and structured career paths for operations and maintenance roles. Many of these positions are in high demand across the industrial economy. We conduct employee engagement surveys and use the results to identify areas for improvement at the local and enterprise level.

Compensation and Benefits

We offer competitive compensation, incentive programs, health and welfare benefits, and retirement savings plans that vary by location and employee group.

Inclusion and Community

We are committed to fostering an inclusive workplace where employees are treated with respect and have opportunities to contribute and advance. Many of our facilities have operated in their communities for decades, and our employees are often long-tenured members of those communities. We support local engagement through volunteer programs, charitable giving, educational partnerships and community advisory processes at our operating sites.

Item 1. Business

General Development of Business

Selzennov Materials, Inc. is a Delaware corporation headquartered in Charlotte, North Carolina. In this Annual Report on Form 10-K, "Selzennov," the "Company," "we," "us" and "our" refer to Selzennov Materials, Inc. and its consolidated subsidiaries unless the context requires otherwise. Our fiscal year is the calendar year, and the fiscal year discussed in this report ended December 31, 2024. Our common stock trades under the ticker symbol SOLV.

Over its history, the Company has grown from a regional producer of construction aggregates into a diversified materials enterprise. That growth has come through investment in our own production facilities and through acquisitions of complementary businesses. We continue to evaluate acquisitions, joint ventures and divestitures as part of the ongoing management of our portfolio. We pursue transactions that strengthen our positions in markets where we believe we have durable cost or logistical advantages, and we exit operations that no longer fit our long-term objectives.

Narrative Description of Business

Overview

Selzennov is a producer of essential materials used in construction, infrastructure, packaging and a broad range of industrial processes. Our products are basic inputs to the built environment and to the manufacture and distribution of consumer and industrial goods. These products include crushed stone, sand and gravel, cement, ready-mixed concrete, containerboard, corrugated packaging and processed industrial minerals. In fiscal 2024, our consolidated revenue was $14,240 million, compared with $13,521 million in fiscal 2023. As of December 31, 2024, we employed about 18,000 people.

Our business model rests on three foundations: control of long-lived natural resource reserves, operation of large-scale and efficient production assets, and proximity to the customers we serve. Many of our products are heavy and relatively low in value per unit of weight. For this reason, transportation costs often represent a significant share of the delivered price, and production facilities located close to demand centers can hold a meaningful advantage over more distant competitors. We have built our network of quarries, plants, mills and distribution terminals with this dynamic in mind.

We operate in a capital-intensive industry. Our results depend on sustained investment in property, plant and equipment, on the disciplined maintenance of our reserves and operating permits, and on prudent management of our capital structure. We seek to generate value over the full economic cycle by improving operating efficiency, by managing pricing in line with input cost trends, and by allocating capital to projects and acquisitions that we expect to earn returns above our cost of capital.

Strategy

Our strategy centers on several long-term priorities:

- Operational excellence. We pursue continuous improvement in plant reliability, energy efficiency, yield and logistics. We use standardized operating practices and performance metrics across our facilities to identify and spread best practices.

- Commercial discipline. We price our products to reflect the value of reliable supply, consistent quality and technical support, and we seek to recover inflation in energy, labor, freight and raw material costs through our pricing actions.

- Portfolio focus. We concentrate investment in geographic markets and product categories where we hold leading positions or have a clear path to developing them. We regularly review assets that do not meet our return expectations.

- Sustainability. We work to reduce the environmental footprint of our operations, including energy use, emissions and water consumption, and to expand our offering of lower-carbon and recycled-content products that help our customers meet their own environmental objectives.

- Financial stewardship. We balance reinvestment in our business, strategic acquisitions and returns to stockholders while maintaining a capital structure appropriate for a cyclical, asset-intensive enterprise.

Principal Products and Services

Our principal products fall into several broad categories.

Construction aggregates, consisting of crushed stone, sand and gravel, are the most widely used construction materials. They are the primary component of concrete and asphalt and are also used as base material for roads, building foundations, railroad ballast and drainage systems. We extract aggregates from quarries and pits that we own or lease, process them by crushing, screening and washing, and sell them to customers in a range of sizes and specifications.

Cement is a binding agent produced by heating limestone and other raw materials at high temperatures to form clinker, which is then ground with gypsum and other additives. We produce portland and blended cements, including formulations that replace part of the clinker with supplementary cementitious materials to reduce the carbon intensity of the finished product.

Ready-mixed concrete and other downstream products combine our aggregates and cement with water and admixtures. They are delivered to job sites by our fleet of mixer trucks and by third-party carriers. In selected markets we also produce asphalt mix and provide paving services.

Containerboard and corrugated packaging include linerboard and corrugating medium manufactured at our mills from virgin wood fiber and recycled fiber. These materials are converted at our box plants into corrugated shipping containers, displays and protective packaging.

Industrial minerals include processed calcium carbonate, lime and specialty silica sands. They serve as functional inputs in steelmaking, environmental remediation, water treatment, glass manufacturing, plastics, coatings and agriculture.

Beyond product supply, we provide technical services. These include mix design assistance, quality testing, packaging design and application support, which help customers specify and use our materials effectively.

Reportable Segments

Our operating segments are aggregated into three reportable segments: Building Materials, Packaging Solutions and Industrial Minerals. We aggregated operating segments based on similarities in products, production processes, customer types, distribution methods and economic characteristics. Financial information about our reportable segments is included in the notes to our consolidated financial statements in Item 8 of Part II of this Form 10-K.

Building Materials

The Building Materials segment produces and sells construction aggregates, cement, ready-mixed concrete, asphalt and related products and services. It is our largest segment by revenue and asset base. Its operations are organized around regional market areas, each anchored by quarries and production facilities that serve customers within an economic delivery radius.

Our aggregates business is supported by reserves that we believe are sufficient to sustain operations at our principal sites for many years. Obtaining new quarry permits can be a lengthy and uncertain process because of zoning requirements, environmental review and community concerns. We therefore regard our permitted reserves as long-lived strategic assets and seek to extend them through land acquisition, lease renewals and permit expansions.

Our cement plants supply our own ready-mixed concrete operations as well as unaffiliated concrete producers, precast and block manufacturers, and distributors. We supplement plant production with a network of terminals served by rail and water, which allows us to reach markets beyond the immediate vicinity of our plants. We have invested in alternative fuels, process efficiency and blended cement products to reduce energy consumption and carbon dioxide emissions per unit of output.

Demand for Building Materials products comes from three principal end markets: public infrastructure, including highways, bridges, airports and water systems; nonresidential construction, including warehouses, data centers, manufacturing facilities and commercial buildings; and residential construction. Public infrastructure demand is influenced by federal, state and local funding programs. Private construction demand responds to interest rates, employment, population growth and business investment.

Packaging Solutions

The Packaging Solutions segment manufactures containerboard and converts it into corrugated packaging products. Our mills produce linerboard and corrugating medium using both virgin and recycled fiber. Most of that output is consumed by our own converting plants, and the remainder is sold to independent converters and other customers. Our converting network produces a broad range of corrugated products, from standard shipping containers to customized, high-graphics retail displays and protective packaging for e-commerce distribution.

Our customers in this segment include producers of food and beverages, consumer products, agricultural goods, building products and industrial goods, as well as retailers and e-commerce fulfillment operations. Demand for corrugated packaging generally tracks consumption and shipments of nondurable and durable goods. It is also influenced by inventory levels in customer supply chains and by changes in retail and distribution channels.

Fiber is the principal raw material for this segment. We source wood fiber through purchases of timber and wood chips from independent landowners and suppliers. We procure recovered fiber, principally old corrugated containers, from retailers, collectors and recycling operations. Our mills also consume significant amounts of energy, chemicals and water, and several of them operate cogeneration facilities that use biomass by-products of the pulping process.

We compete in this segment by providing dependable supply, consistent quality, design capability and responsive service. We seek to optimize the integration between our mills and converting operations to balance capacity utilization, freight efficiency and customer service.

Industrial Minerals

The Industrial Minerals segment extracts and processes calcium carbonate, lime and specialty silica sands. These materials are valued for their chemical and physical properties rather than as bulk construction inputs. They are used in steel production, flue gas treatment, drinking water and wastewater treatment, glass and fiberglass manufacturing, paper and plastics filling, coatings, building products and agricultural soil conditioning.

Products in this segment are typically manufactured to tight specifications for particle size, purity and brightness. They often require close technical collaboration with customers to qualify for use in their processes. As a result, customer relationships in this segment are frequently long-standing and governed by supply agreements of varying duration.

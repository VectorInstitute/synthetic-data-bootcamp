Item 1. Business

OVERVIEW

Lonnov Software, Inc. ("Lonnov," the "Company," "we," "us" or "our") is a global provider of enterprise software, cloud infrastructure and technology services. Organizations across industries use our offerings to modernize their information technology environments, run mission-critical workloads, protect their data and manage complex digital operations at scale. We were incorporated in the State of Delaware, and our corporate headquarters are located in Austin, Texas. Our common stock trades under the ticker symbol "LNON."

Our offerings span the technology lifecycle of our customers. We help them plan and design their technology architectures, deploy and integrate software and cloud platforms, migrate legacy systems, and operate and secure their environments on an ongoing basis. We combine proprietary software platforms with a large, globally distributed services organization. This allows us to serve customers who want packaged software, customers who want fully managed outcomes, and customers who want a combination of the two.

For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024 ("fiscal 2024"), our total revenue was $80,053 million, an increase of 3.9% compared with the fiscal year ended December 31, 2023 ("fiscal 2023"). We believe the breadth of our portfolio, the recurring nature of a substantial portion of our revenue, and the long-standing relationships we maintain with large enterprise and public sector customers provide a durable foundation for the business.

Enterprise technology environments have grown more complex. Organizations operate hybrid estates that combine on-premises data centers, multiple public cloud environments and a growing number of software-as-a-service applications. At the same time, they face higher expectations for cybersecurity and data protection, scarce specialized technical talent, and pressure to adopt artificial intelligence and advanced analytics in a responsible and cost-effective manner. We believe these trends increase demand for partners that can provide integrated platforms together with the engineering and operational capacity to implement and run them. Our strategy is to be that partner for the largest and most demanding organizations in the world.

REPORTABLE SEGMENTS

As of December 31, 2024, we managed our business through three operating and reportable segments, which are organized around the nature of the offerings we deliver and the way our chief operating decision maker evaluates performance and allocates resources:

- Cloud and Data Platforms ("CDP"), which includes our hybrid cloud infrastructure software, data management and analytics platforms, and artificial intelligence development and deployment tools.
- Enterprise Applications and Security ("EAS"), which includes our business applications, workflow automation software, identity and access management products, and threat detection and response solutions.
- Managed and Professional Services ("MPS"), which includes consulting, systems integration, application development and maintenance, cloud migration, and outsourced infrastructure and application management services.

The CDP segment focuses on the foundational software layers on which customers build and run their workloads. Its offerings are typically sold as subscriptions or consumption-based arrangements and are designed to operate consistently across customer data centers and third-party public cloud environments.

The EAS segment focuses on software that supports specific business processes and on protecting the applications, users and data that underpin those processes. Its offerings are sold primarily as multi-year subscriptions, both as cloud-delivered services and as software deployed in customer environments.

The MPS segment provides the people, processes and operational tooling that customers rely on to design, implement and run technology environments. Engagements range from discrete advisory and implementation projects to multi-year managed services agreements under which we assume operational responsibility for significant portions of a customer's technology estate. The MPS segment employs the majority of our workforce and accounts for a substantial portion of our cost of revenue, reflecting the labor-intensive nature of services delivery.

Our segments work closely together. A significant portion of our largest customer relationships involves offerings from more than one segment, and we design our go-to-market approach to encourage customers to adopt integrated solutions. Additional information about our segments is included in Item 7, "Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations," and in the notes to the consolidated financial statements in Item 8, "Financial Statements and Supplementary Data."

PRINCIPAL PRODUCTS AND SERVICES

Cloud and Data Platforms

Our hybrid cloud platform gives customers a common control plane for provisioning, orchestrating and governing compute, storage and networking resources across private and public cloud environments. The platform includes container orchestration, virtualization management, policy-based automation and cost governance capabilities. Customers use it to standardize operations, reduce dependence on any single infrastructure provider, and move workloads between environments as business needs change.

Our data management portfolio includes data integration, data cataloging, master data management and data quality tools, along with a cloud-native analytics engine that supports structured and unstructured data. These products help customers consolidate fragmented data sources, enforce governance and lineage requirements, and make trusted data available to analysts, applications and machine learning models.

Our artificial intelligence tools allow customers to build, fine-tune, evaluate and deploy machine learning and generative models on their own data, inside environments they control. The tools include model lifecycle management, monitoring for model drift and performance, and guardrail capabilities intended to support responsible use. We have embedded these capabilities into many products across our portfolio, and we continue to expand them in response to customer demand.

Enterprise Applications and Security

Our enterprise applications automate and digitize core operational processes, including IT service management, field service management, procurement workflows, and human resources service delivery. The applications are built on a common low-code platform, so customers can extend standard functionality and build custom workflows without extensive software development.

Our security portfolio includes identity governance, privileged access management, multi-factor authentication, endpoint and cloud workload protection, and a security analytics platform that correlates telemetry across customer environments to detect and prioritize threats. We also offer managed detection and response services in which our security operations personnel monitor customer environments and respond to incidents around the clock. These services are delivered in collaboration with our MPS segment.

Managed and Professional Services

Our consulting and systems integration practices advise customers on technology strategy, enterprise architecture, and operating model design. They also implement our own software as well as third-party platforms. Our application services teams develop, modernize, test and maintain custom and packaged applications. Our cloud migration services help customers assess application portfolios, plan migration waves, and move workloads to the target environments.

Our managed services offerings include infrastructure management, application management, end-user computing support, network operations, and service desk operations. These engagements are generally governed by multi-year contracts with defined service levels. We deliver them through a combination of onshore, nearshore and offshore delivery centers, supported by our proprietary automation and operational analytics platforms. We increasingly use artificial intelligence to automate routine operational tasks, predict and prevent incidents, and improve the productivity of our delivery personnel.

Revenue Models

We generate revenue through software subscriptions, consumption-based cloud services, perpetual software licenses with associated support, time-and-materials and fixed-price professional services engagements, and multi-year managed services arrangements. A substantial portion of our revenue is recurring in nature, which we believe gives us good visibility into future results. Our revenue recognition policies are described in the notes to the consolidated financial statements in Item 8.

STRATEGY

Our strategy is designed to deepen our role as a long-term technology partner to the world's largest organizations. Its principal elements are as follows.

Integrated Solutions. We intend to keep integrating our platforms with our services capabilities so that customers can buy outcomes rather than individual components. We believe customers increasingly want fewer, more strategic vendors that can take end-to-end accountability, and that our combination of proprietary software and delivery scale sets us apart.

Innovation in Artificial Intelligence and Automation. We are investing in artificial intelligence across our portfolio, both as product capabilities we offer customers and as tools that make our own services delivery more efficient. In fiscal 2024, research and development expense represented 13.8% of revenue, compared with 13.7% in fiscal 2023. This reflects our continued commitment to product innovation.

Hybrid and Multi-Cloud Neutrality. We design our platforms to run consistently across customer data centers and multiple public cloud providers. We believe this neutrality is valuable to customers seeking to avoid concentration risk, meet data residency requirements, and optimize costs.

Operational Excellence. We continuously work to improve delivery productivity, the utilization of our workforce, and the efficiency of our operations. Our efforts include standardizing delivery methodologies, expanding automation, rationalizing facilities, and optimizing the geographic mix of our delivery resources.

Disciplined Capital Allocation. We aim to balance investment in organic growth with selective acquisitions that add technology, talent or market access, while returning capital to stockholders through dividends and share repurchases.

CUSTOMERS

We serve a broad and diversified customer base that includes large multinational enterprises, mid-sized organizations, and government agencies at the national, state and local levels. Our customers operate in a wide range of industries, including financial services, insurance, healthcare and life sciences, manufacturing, retail and consumer goods, telecommunications, media, energy and utilities, transportation, and the public sector.

Our largest customers typically purchase offerings from more than one of our segments and are often party to long-term master agreements under which they procure software and services over multiple years. We focus on building enduring relationships and expanding them over time through additional products, services and geographies. Many of our largest relationships have lasted well over a decade.

Item 1C. Cybersecurity

Overview

Venzen relies on information technology systems, operational technology and connected products to design, manufacture, sell and service our offerings. These systems also support our internal operations. Our controls and software, including remote monitoring and subscription-based analytics, are increasingly linked to customer buildings, plants, networks and fleets. As a result, cybersecurity is a core element of our enterprise risk management. Our program aims to protect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of our systems and data. It also aims to protect the security of the products and services we deliver to customers.

Risk Management and Strategy

Our cybersecurity program is governed by a set of enterprise policies and standards. These are aligned with recognized industry frameworks, including the cybersecurity framework published by the National Institute of Standards and Technology, and with standards applicable to industrial automation and control systems. The program is organized around the core functions of identifying, protecting against, detecting, responding to and recovering from cybersecurity threats. Its principal elements include the following:

Continuous security monitoring through a centralized security operations function. This function aggregates and analyzes activity across our corporate networks, cloud environments and endpoints, using automated tools supplemented by analysts who investigate potential threats. Identity and access management controls designed to restrict access to systems and data based on role and need. These include multifactor authentication and privileged access safeguards. Vulnerability management practices, including periodic scanning, prioritized remediation and penetration testing of critical systems and applications. A dedicated operational technology security program. It addresses the specific risks of our manufacturing facilities, test laboratories and service operations, including segmentation of plant networks from enterprise systems. A product security program that applies secure-by-design principles throughout the development lifecycle of our embedded controls, building and plant management platforms, and cloud-based software. It includes threat modeling, code review, security testing and a coordinated process for receiving and addressing vulnerability reports from customers and researchers. Collection and evaluation of threat intelligence from internal sources, industry information-sharing organizations, government agencies and commercial providers, to identify threats relevant to our industry and operations. Security awareness training for employees, with additional role-based training for personnel in higher-risk functions. Simulated phishing exercises reinforce this training.

Incident Response and Recovery

We maintain a written cybersecurity incident response plan. It defines roles, escalation paths and procedures for evaluating, containing, remediating and communicating about incidents. Under the plan, incidents are assessed for severity and potential business impact. Incidents that meet defined thresholds are escalated promptly to senior management. A cross-functional group that includes legal, finance, communications and business leadership evaluates whether an incident may be material and whether disclosure or notification obligations apply. We conduct tabletop exercises periodically to test the plan, and some of these exercises involve senior executives. Our business continuity and disaster recovery programs include recovery planning and testing for systems that support manufacturing, order fulfillment, field service and financial reporting.

Third-Party Risk

We engage independent assessors, consultants and auditors to evaluate the maturity and effectiveness of our cybersecurity program, test our controls and support incident response when needed. Management reviews their findings and tracks remediation actions through completion.

We depend on many suppliers, service providers, channel partners and software vendors, some of which host or have access to our data and systems. Our third-party risk management process evaluates the security practices of these parties before engagement and periodically afterward, scaled to the sensitivity of the data or access involved. Contracts with relevant providers generally include security and incident notification requirements. Businesses we acquire are assessed for cybersecurity risk during due diligence. We then integrate them into our security standards and monitoring over a planned transition period.

Some of our contracts with government entities, including defense customers, impose specific cybersecurity and data protection requirements. We maintain processes designed to comply with those obligations.

Assessment of Risks from Cybersecurity Threats

Cybersecurity risks are incorporated into our enterprise risk management process. Through that process, management identifies, evaluates and prioritizes risks that could affect our strategic and operating objectives. Like other companies of our size and complexity, we face threats such as ransomware, phishing, credential theft, malicious code, denial-of-service attacks and intrusions by sophisticated actors, including actors that may be state-sponsored. We also face risks arising from vulnerabilities in our suppliers' systems and in the connected products we sell.

We have experienced cybersecurity incidents in the past. As of the date of this report, however, we are not aware of any cybersecurity threats or incidents, including those resulting from previous incidents, that have materially affected or are reasonably likely to materially affect the Company, including our business strategy, results of operations or financial condition. We cannot provide assurance that we will not experience such an incident in the future. For further discussion, see Item 1A, "Risk Factors."

Governance

Board Oversight

Our Board of Directors oversees our enterprise risks, including cybersecurity risks. The Board has delegated primary oversight of cybersecurity and information security risk to its Audit Committee. The Audit Committee receives regular reports from the Chief Information Security Officer on the threat environment, program performance, assessment results, significant incidents and the progress of key initiatives. The Audit Committee reports on these matters to the full Board, which also receives periodic cybersecurity briefings directly from management. Under our incident response plan, management notifies the Audit Committee chair, and the full Board as appropriate, of incidents that meet defined escalation criteria.

Management's Role

Our Chief Information Security Officer leads our enterprise cybersecurity organization. This organization is responsible for information technology, operational technology and product security strategy, as well as security operations and incident response. The Chief Information Security Officer has extensive experience leading cybersecurity programs at large, multinational industrial and technology organizations, with a background in security architecture, threat management and operational technology protection. The individual holds recognized professional certifications in information security. The Chief Information Security Officer reports to our Chief Information Officer, who reports to our Chief Executive Officer. Security leaders embedded within each of our three segments support the Chief Information Security Officer and work with business, engineering and plant leadership to integrate security requirements into operations and product development.

We also maintain a Cybersecurity Risk Council, a cross-functional management committee chaired by the Chief Information Officer. Its members include senior leaders from legal, finance, internal audit, compliance, operations and each of our segments. The Council reviews cybersecurity risks, sets program priorities, monitors remediation of significant findings and helps ensure that cybersecurity considerations inform strategic planning, budgeting and investment decisions across the Company.

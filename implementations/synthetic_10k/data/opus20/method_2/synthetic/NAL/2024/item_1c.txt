# ITEM 1C. CYBERSECURITY

## Overview

Information technology is integral to nearly every aspect of our business. Our restaurants depend on point-of-sale and kitchen display systems to serve guests. Our brand-owned digital channels support online ordering, reservations, waitlists, and loyalty programs. Our Restaurant Support Center relies on enterprise systems for finance, payroll, human resources, and supply chain functions. In the ordinary course of business, we collect and process personal information about guests, team members, and other parties, as well as payment card data.

We recognize that protecting these systems and this information is essential to maintaining the trust of our guests, team members, franchisees, and shareholders. We have therefore established a cybersecurity risk management program that is integrated into our broader enterprise risk management processes and overseen by our Board of Directors through its Audit Committee.

## Risk Management and Strategy

Our information security program is designed to:

- protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of our systems and data;
- comply with applicable data protection laws and the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard;
- identify, assess, and respond to cybersecurity threats.

We use the NIST Cybersecurity Framework to structure the program and to assess its maturity. Using the Framework does not mean that we meet any particular technical standard or requirement. Rather, it guides how we organize our activities to identify, protect, detect, respond to, and recover from cybersecurity threats.

**Risk identification and assessment.** Our information technology organization periodically assesses cybersecurity risks across our corporate systems, restaurant technology, and digital platforms. These assessments consider:

- the threats facing the restaurant industry and consumer-facing businesses;
- the sensitivity of the data we hold, particularly payment card data and guest and team member personal information;
- the potential operational effect of disruptions to systems shared across our brands.

Results are incorporated into our enterprise risk management process. That process evaluates cybersecurity alongside other principal risks, including food safety, severe weather, and supply chain disruption.

**Protective and detective controls.** Our program includes a range of administrative, technical, and physical safeguards, such as:

- network segmentation and security controls for restaurant payment environments;
- identity and access management, including multi-factor authentication for designated systems;
- endpoint protection and monitoring for malicious activity;
- vulnerability management and patching processes;
- encryption and tokenization of payment card data where appropriate;
- data backup and recovery capabilities.

**Workforce awareness.** Our large, distributed restaurant footprint and the high turnover of our hourly workforce create particular exposure to social engineering and credential misuse. We therefore provide security awareness training to Restaurant Support Center and restaurant management team members, with content adapted to their roles and access. We also conduct periodic phishing simulations to reinforce that training.

**Use of third parties.** We engage external assessors, consultants, and other service providers to support our program. These parties may perform penetration testing, assess our compliance with payment card industry requirements, evaluate the maturity of our program against the NIST Cybersecurity Framework, and provide incident response support when needed. Their findings help us prioritize improvements.

**Third-party service provider risk.** We rely on numerous vendors, including:

- cloud service providers;
- payment processors;
- digital ordering and loyalty platforms;
- third-party delivery service providers;
- payroll and benefits administrators.

We seek to manage the associated risks through security reviews during vendor selection, contractual provisions addressing data protection and incident notification, and ongoing oversight that is commensurate with the sensitivity of the data or systems involved. Franchisees are independent businesses responsible for their own operations. However, franchise agreements require them to adhere to our brand technology standards, including standards relating to payment systems.

**Incident response and business continuity.** We maintain a cybersecurity incident response plan. The plan defines roles and responsibilities, escalation procedures, and the steps for containing, investigating, and remediating incidents. It provides for the escalation of significant incidents to senior management and, as appropriate, to the Audit Committee. It also provides for coordination with our legal, compliance, communications, and finance functions so that incidents can be evaluated for materiality and any required disclosure or notification. We periodically test the plan through tabletop exercises. We also coordinate cybersecurity recovery planning with our broader business continuity and disaster recovery plans, which address the risk that severe weather could affect our Florida-based Restaurant Support Center and the shared services it provides to all of our brands.

**Insurance.** We maintain cyber liability insurance as part of our comprehensive insurance program. This coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses from a significant incident.

**Material effects of cybersecurity threats.** As of the date of this report, we are not aware of any cybersecurity incidents that have materially affected, or are reasonably likely to materially affect, the Company, including our business strategy, results of operations, or financial condition. Like other companies, however, we face ongoing threats, and we cannot provide assurance that our measures will prevent or detect every incident. Item 1A, "Risk Factors," describes the risks associated with cybersecurity threats, including the section titled "A cybersecurity incident or other breach of our information systems could result in liability, regulatory penalties, operational disruption, and reputational harm."

## Governance

**Board oversight.** Our Board of Directors oversees risk management as part of its general oversight responsibilities. It has delegated primary oversight of cybersecurity risk to the Audit Committee. The Audit Committee receives periodic reports from management on matters such as:

- the threat environment;
- the status of our information security program and key initiatives;
- the results of assessments and testing;
- compliance with payment card industry and data protection requirements;
- significant incidents, if any, and our response.

The Audit Committee reports to the full Board on these matters as appropriate. The full Board also considers cybersecurity as part of its review of our enterprise risk management process and our technology strategy.

**Management's role.** Within management, our information security function is responsible for assessing and managing cybersecurity risk day to day. That function is housed within the information technology organization at our Restaurant Support Center and is led by a senior information security leader. This leader reports through our technology leadership and has relevant experience in information security, risk management, and the protection of payment and personal data. The function is supported by internal security personnel and by the external service providers described above.

Our information security leader keeps informed about the prevention, detection, mitigation, and remediation of cybersecurity incidents through regular reporting from security monitoring tools, internal personnel, and service providers. This leader works closely with our legal and compliance, internal audit, and finance functions and with brand leadership. Cybersecurity matters are also reviewed through our enterprise risk management process, in which members of our senior leadership team participate, and they are escalated to the Audit Committee in accordance with our incident response plan and governance practices.

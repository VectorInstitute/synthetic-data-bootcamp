# Item 1C. Cybersecurity

## Overview

As a key player in the energy sector and specifically within the oil and gas exploration and production sub-industry, Silverlake Instruments Holdings, Inc. ("Silverlake" or the "Company") recognizes the vital importance of cybersecurity in safeguarding our operational integrity, sensitive data, and stakeholder trust. With the increasing digitalization of our operations, cyber threats represent a significant and evolving risk to our business.

## Cybersecurity Framework and Strategy

Silverlake employs the NIST Cybersecurity Framework as a cornerstone of our cybersecurity strategy. This widely respected framework guides our efforts to identify, protect, detect, respond, and recover from cybersecurity incidents. Our approach encompasses a comprehensive array of policies, procedures, and controls designed to secure both our information technology (IT) and operational technology (OT) environments.

Our cybersecurity measures are crafted to address threats unique to the oil and gas industry, including those targeting industrial control systems and field operations. By aligning our strategy with industry best practices, we strive to fortify our defenses while accommodating the dynamic nature of cyber threats.

## Governance and Oversight

The cybersecurity oversight at Silverlake is driven by the full Board of Directors, with dedicated support from the Audit Committee. This leadership structure ensures a top-down prioritization of cybersecurity, reflecting our commitment to protect our digital assets and maintain operational resilience. Regular cybersecurity risk assessments, updates, and scenario analyses are presented to the Board to facilitate informed decision-making and strategic planning.

## Risk Management

Silverlake's risk management approach integrates cybersecurity considerations into our broader enterprise risk management framework. This integration allows us to evaluate and address cyber risks in the context of overall business operations and strategic objectives. Specifically, we focus on identifying potential threats, assessing vulnerabilities, and implementing responsive controls that lessen the impact of potential cybersecurity incidents.

## Incident Response and Recovery

Although Silverlake has not experienced a disclosed cybersecurity incident to date, we maintain a robust incident response plan designed to promptly address any potential breaches. This plan includes predefined response protocols, roles and responsibilities, and communication strategies that facilitate swift identification and containment of threats. In the event of a cybersecurity incident, our capabilities are aimed at minimizing operational disruption and protecting sensitive information.

Additionally, Silverlake conducts regular simulation exercises to test and refine our incident response mechanisms, enhancing our readiness to handle real-world cyber threats effectively.

## Workforce and Training

Employee awareness and training are integral components of Silverlake's cybersecurity posture. We conduct ongoing training programs designed to educate employees about cyber risks, security best practices, and their role in safeguarding company assets. By fostering a culture of cybersecurity, Silverlake empowers employees to act as the first line of defense against potential threats.

## Technology and Infrastructure

The Company continues to invest in advanced cybersecurity technologies to detect and neutralize potential threats before they materialize into significant incidents. Our security architecture includes next-generation firewalls, intrusion detection and prevention systems, and endpoint protection solutions. These tools are complemented by 24/7 monitoring and intelligence gathering to provide comprehensive visibility into our IT and OT environments.

## Vendor and Third-Party Risk Management

Recognizing the interconnected nature of modern operations, Silverlake extends its cybersecurity efforts to include rigorous vendor and third-party risk management. We assess the cybersecurity practices of partners and service providers to ensure they align with our standards and obligations. This evaluation extends to contractual requirements, data protection agreements, and audits to verify compliance and mitigate third-party risks.

## Continuous Improvement

Silverlake is committed to the continuous improvement of our cybersecurity capabilities. This commitment involves regular reviews and updates to our cybersecurity policies, as well as investment in new technologies and methodologies. Feedback from incident debriefs, threat intelligence, and industry developments inform our strategy, ensuring that our defenses evolve alongside emerging threats.

## Summary

In conclusion, Silverlake Instruments Holdings, Inc. remains vigilant and proactive in its approach to cybersecurity. By embedding rigorous cybersecurity practices across all levels of the organization and aligning with respected frameworks, we strive to protect our technology assets and ensure the secure, reliable operation of our business. Our comprehensive strategy reflects our dedication to maintaining stakeholder confidence and supporting the sustainable growth of Silverlake in an increasingly connected world.

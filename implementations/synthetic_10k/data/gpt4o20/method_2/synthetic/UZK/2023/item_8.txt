# Item 8. Financial Statements and Supplementary Data

## Financial Statements

The financial statements of Silverlake Instruments Holdings, Inc. ("Silverlake" or the "Company") for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2023, provide a comprehensive overview of the Company's financial position. These consolidated financial statements have been prepared in accordance with Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (GAAP) and include the Balance Sheet, Income Statement, and Cash Flow Statement, reflecting our robust financial performance within the energy sector.

### Consolidated Balance Sheet

As of the end of fiscal year 2023, Silverlake reported total assets amounting to $58,017,279,538. The asset base is predominantly composed of cash and equivalents of $1,251,692,766, alongside significant accounts receivable of $2,727,170,575 and inventory valued at $1,756,637,753. The Company's infrastructure investments are reflected in other assets totaling $52,281,778,444, underscoring Silverlake's strategic commitment to resource development and advanced technological integration.

Total liabilities for the Company stand at $35,021,283,686, with key components including accounts payable of $3,200,282,125 and accrued liabilities summing to $2,758,494,375. Long-term debt amounts to $13,776,238,125, representing a strategic balance in leveraging opportunities for expansion. This results in a total stockholders' equity of $22,995,995,852, underpinned by additional paid-in capital and retained earnings, reflecting stable shareholder investments and accumulated profits.

### Consolidated Income Statement

For the fiscal year 2023, Silverlake reported total revenues of $19,366,186,883, a testament to effective resource extraction and operational efficiencies. The cost of revenue totaled $2,524,758,516, with additional operating expenses including selling, general, and administrative costs of $852,618,989 and other operating expenses of $11,480,228,724. This resulted in a pretax income of $4,508,580,654.

The income tax expense for the year amounted to $946,801,937, leading to a net income of $3,561,778,717. Silverlake's financial outcomes underscore our strategic emphasis on cost discipline and tactical investment in growth initiatives, aligning with shareholder interests.

### Consolidated Cash Flow Statement

Silverlake's cash flow from operating activities reflects a positive inflow of $11,726,708,150, driven by strong net income and significant contributions from depreciation and amortization, which totaled $4,819,174,601. Changes in working capital and other adjustments contributed an additional inflow of $3,345,754,832.

Investing activities resulted in cash outflows of $6,361,543,108, with capital expenditures amounting to $5,360,991,270, reflecting our strategic investment in enhancing exploration and production capacities. Other investing activities accounted for $1,000,551,838, further supporting long-term asset growth and technological upgrades.

Financing activities generated outflows of $4,543,123,678, primarily due to share repurchases totaling $1,662,897,947 and other financing-related transactions of $2,880,225,731. These activities reflect our commitment to optimizing capital structure and delivering value to shareholders.

## Notes to Financial Statements

### Note 1: Basis of Presentation

Silverlake's consolidated financial statements comprise the accounts of Silverlake Instruments Holdings, Inc. and its subsidiaries. All intercompany accounts and transactions have been eliminated in consolidation. The financial statements are prepared using GAAP and present the operations of the Company on a continuing basis.

### Note 2: Summary of Significant Accounting Policies

#### Revenue Recognition

Revenues are recognized when control of the promised goods or services is transferred to customers in an amount that reflects the consideration we expect to receive in exchange for those goods or services.

#### Inventory Valuation

Inventories are stated at the lower of cost or market, using the first-in, first-out (FIFO) method of inventory accounting. Inventory values are regularly assessed to ensure they are carried at appropriate amounts.

#### Property and Equipment

Property and equipment are recorded at cost, net of accumulated depreciation. Depreciation is computed using the straight-line method over the estimated useful lives of the assets.

#### Income Taxes

Silverlake accounts for income taxes under the asset and liability method, recognizing deferred tax assets and liabilities for future tax consequences attributable to differences between the financial statement carrying amounts of existing assets and liabilities and their respective tax bases.

### Other Notes

#### Commitments and Contingencies

Silverlake has entered into various commitments and contingencies, including legal and environmental obligations, which are considered in our comprehensive evaluation of operational risks and financial impacts.

#### Subsequent Events

Management has evaluated subsequent events occurring after the balance sheet date, which have been properly reflected and disclosed, ensuring the accuracy and completeness of our reporting.

# Item 1C. Cybersecurity

Amberwood Partners Co. recognizes the critical importance of cybersecurity in safeguarding its operations, customer data, and reputation within the dynamic consumer discretionary sector, specifically the restaurant sub-industry. As a company deeply committed to providing exceptional dining experiences, we also emphasize the protection and security of our digital infrastructure. This commitment is fundamental to maintaining trust among our customers, employees, and partners.

## Cybersecurity Governance

Amberwood Partners Co. maintains a robust cybersecurity governance framework led by our Audit Committee of the Board of Directors. This committee bears the primary responsibility for overseeing the company's cybersecurity risk management strategy and ensuring it aligns with our overall business objectives. We have adopted the NIST Cybersecurity Framework, a strategic guide that allows us to manage and mitigate cybersecurity risks effectively while fostering a culture of security across the organization.

## Strategy and Risk Management

Our cybersecurity strategy is designed to anticipate, prevent, and respond to a diverse range of cyber threats that could impact our operations and brand integrity. Key components of our strategy include:

1. **Risk Assessment**: Continuous evaluation of potential cyber threats is central to our operations. We conduct regular risk assessments to identify vulnerabilities within our systems and implement necessary countermeasures to safeguard our infrastructure.

2. **Incident Response Planning**: While Amberwood Partners Co. has yet to experience a disclosed cybersecurity incident, we maintain a comprehensive incident response plan that ensures readiness should such an event occur. This plan is routinely tested and updated to reflect the evolving threat landscape, with the aim of minimizing potential impacts on our operations.

3. **Awareness and Training**: We recognize that our workforce plays a crucial role in cybersecurity. To this end, we invest in tailored training programs designed to enhance employee awareness and equip them with the knowledge necessary to identify and respond to potential threats. This proactive approach mitigates human error and strengthens our overall security posture.

## Technology and Infrastructure

Our commitment to cybersecurity is reflected in the strategic investments we make in advanced technologies that enhance the resilience of our systems. Key initiatives include:

1. **Network Security**: We have implemented state-of-the-art firewalls, intrusion detection systems, and encryption protocols to protect sensitive customer and employee data. These measures help ensure confidentiality, integrity, and availability of critical information across all our digital platforms.

2. **Data Protection**: Amberwood Partners Co. prioritizes the safeguarding of personal and financial information. Our data protection measures include robust access controls, regular audits, and encryption technologies that prevent unauthorized access and data breaches.

3. **Vendor Management**: The security of our supply chain is closely monitored through rigorous vendor assessments. We collaborate with third-party partners who meet our stringent cybersecurity standards, thereby minimizing potential risks inherent in vendor relationships.

## Continuous Improvement

The cybersecurity landscape is continually evolving, necessitating an adaptive approach to risk management. Amberwood Partners Co. is dedicated to continuous improvement through:

1. **Monitoring and Reporting**: We utilize advanced monitoring tools to provide real-time visibility into network activities. Anomalies are promptly reported and investigated, facilitating rapid incident response and mitigation.

2. **Innovation and Adaptation**: We actively explore emerging cybersecurity technologies and methodologies to stay ahead of potential threats. By embracing innovation, we enhance our ability to counteract new attack vectors and reinforce our defensive measures.

3. **Executive Oversight**: Regular updates on cybersecurity initiatives and risks are presented to the Executive Management Team and the Audit Committee. This ensures that cybersecurity remains at the forefront of our strategic decision-making processes.

## Regulatory Compliance

Compliance with regional, national, and international cybersecurity regulations is a core component of our strategy. Amberwood Partners Co. is committed to maintaining compliance with applicable data protection laws, including those governing the collection, storage, and processing of personal information. Our adherence to these regulations not only minimizes legal risks but also reinforces the trust our stakeholders place in us.

## Conclusion

Amberwood Partners Co. is resolute in its commitment to maintaining the highest standards of cybersecurity. By leveraging the NIST Cybersecurity Framework and overseeing our efforts through the Audit Committee, we have established a resilient defense against cyber threats. As we continue to expand and innovate, our focus remains on safeguarding our digital assets while delivering exceptional value to our stakeholders. Our proactive and comprehensive approach to cybersecurity ensures that we are well-equipped to face the challenges of an increasingly digital world.

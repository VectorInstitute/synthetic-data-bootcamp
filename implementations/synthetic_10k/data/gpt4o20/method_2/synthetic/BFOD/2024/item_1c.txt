# Item 1C. Cybersecurity

Highmark Industries Co. ("Highmark" or "the Company") recognizes the critical importance of maintaining robust cybersecurity measures to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of our data and systems. As a leading provider in the Property & Casualty Insurance sector, headquartered in Connecticut and operating within the highly regulated financials industry, cybersecurity is a foundational component of our operational framework and risk management strategy.

## Cybersecurity Governance and Oversight

Highmark implements the NIST Cybersecurity Framework as the blueprint for our cybersecurity practices. This structured approach enables us to manage cybersecurity risk by organizing our efforts into core functions, including Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, and Recover. These functions guide our strategies to address the complex landscape of threats and vulnerabilities in today's digital environment.

Oversight of Highmark's cybersecurity program is conducted by our full Board of Directors, supported by the Audit Committee. This governance structure ensures comprehensive review and guidance on cybersecurity matters, enabling long-term strategic alignment with our business goals while emphasizing the importance of cyber resilience in achieving sustained customer trust and operational reliability.

## Cybersecurity Strategy

Highmark's cybersecurity strategy is built on several core pillars: risk assessment, incident prevention, detection and response, and continuous improvement. By integrating these elements into our broader technology and innovation strategies, we ensure a well-rounded approach to safeguarding our information assets.

**1. Risk Assessment and Management:**
Highmark carries out continuous risk assessments to identify potential cyber threats and vulnerabilities across our network and systems. This proactive approach allows us to prioritize cybersecurity investments and allocate resources effectively to mitigate identified risks. We employ advanced analytical tools and threat intelligence to enhance situational awareness and ensure our defenses are aligned with the evolving threat landscape.

**2. Incident Prevention:**
Preventing cybersecurity incidents is paramount to sustaining our operational integrity and protecting customer data. Highmark invests in modern security technologies, including firewalls, intrusion detection and prevention systems, and encryption protocols, to establish a robust defense-in-depth strategy. Employee training and awareness programs complement our technical defenses by fostering a culture of cybersecurity vigilance across the organization.

**3. Detection and Response:**
Highmark's detection mechanisms are designed to promptly identify suspicious activities and anomalous behaviors on our network. We maintain a state-of-the-art Security Operations Center (SOC) that operates continuously to monitor, analyze, and respond to potential threats in real-time. Our incident response protocols are thoroughly documented and tested regularly to ensure rapid and coordinated actions, minimizing the impact of any cybersecurity events on our business operations and customer commitments.

## Information Security Program

Central to Highmark's cybersecurity practices is our comprehensive Information Security Program, which encompasses a diverse range of policies, procedures, and technical controls designed to protect our information systems and the sensitive data we manage. This program is anchored by our commitment to compliance with industry standards and regulatory requirements, reflecting our dedication to maintaining the highest levels of security and privacy.

**Data Protection:**
Highmark prioritizes the protection of personal and proprietary information through data classification, access controls, and encryption technologies. Rigorous data governance protocols ensure that sensitive information is accessed only by authorized personnel and handled securely throughout its lifecycle.

**Vulnerability Management:**
Our vulnerability management program involves regular scanning and testing to identify potential security weaknesses within our infrastructure. We adopt a risk-based approach to remediation, concentrating efforts on addressing vulnerabilities that pose the greatest threat to our operations and data.

## Third-Party Risk Management

As Highmark relies on a network of third-party service providers, managing third-party cybersecurity risk is a key element of our strategy. We perform comprehensive due diligence during vendor selection and maintain ongoing assessments to ensure that third parties adhere to our security standards and contractual requirements.

Highmark collaborates closely with third-party partners to share threat intelligence and coordinate on incident response efforts when necessary, reinforcing our collective resilience against cyber threats.

## Incident History and Lessons Learned

To date, Highmark has not disclosed any cybersecurity incidents that have resulted in a material impact on our operations or financial condition. This track record is a testament to the effectiveness of our cybersecurity programs and the dedication of our teams.

Nonetheless, Highmark acknowledges the dynamic nature of cybersecurity risks and remains vigilant in our efforts to continuously enhance our defenses. Lessons learned from industry developments and emerging threats are systematically incorporated into our cybersecurity practices, ensuring ongoing adaptation to new challenges.

## Future Initiatives

Looking forward, Highmark is committed to maintaining and advancing our cybersecurity posture through strategic investments in emerging technologies, such as artificial intelligence and machine learning, for threat detection and response automation. We will continue to strengthen our partnerships with industry peers, government agencies, and security organizations to foster a collective approach to cybersecurity resilience.

By integrating cybersecurity deeply into our risk management and operational strategies, Highmark aims to uphold our reputation for reliability and trustworthiness in the Property & Casualty Insurance sector, ensuring secure and seamless service delivery to our valued clients.

# Item 1A. Risk Factors

Investors should carefully consider the risks described below before making an investment decision regarding Highmark Industries Co. (“Highmark” or “the Company”). The risks and uncertainties described here are not the only ones facing the Company. Additional risks and uncertainties not currently known to us, or that we currently deem immaterial, may also impair our business operations. If any of the following risks materialize, our business, financial condition, results of operations, and cash flows could be materially adversely affected, and the market value of our securities could decline.

## Economic and Market Volatility

**1. Economic Conditions and Cyclical Nature of the Insurance Business:** Our operations are subject to economic conditions and cyclicality in the financial markets. Economic downturns or volatility can adversely impact consumer spending and investment activities, leading to decreased demand for our insurance products. Additionally, they can affect the performance of our investment portfolio, which could negatively impact our financial results.

**2. Interest Rate Fluctuations:** As a company operating in the Financials sector, changes in interest rates can significantly affect our ability to earn predictable returns on our investments and could lead to corresponding adjustments in premium rates, impacting competitiveness and profitability.

## Operational Risks

**3. Underwriting and Pricing Risks:** Our underwriting results and financial condition are highly dependent on our ability to accurately assess and price risk. Any inaccurate pricing or inadequately estimated reserves could result in underwriting losses.

**4. Technological Disruptions:** We rely heavily on information technology systems and data analytics for risk assessment, underwriting, and product distribution. Technological failures, cyber attacks, or disruption in our technological infrastructure could adversely affect our business operations and customer relationships.

**5. Dependency on Distribution Channels:** Our distribution network is primarily composed of independent agents and brokers. Any changes in relationships with these agents or a failure to effectively manage these channels could adversely affect our ability to attract and retain customers.

## Regulatory and Legal Risks

**6. Compliance with Regulatory Requirements:** Highmark Industries operates in a heavily regulated sector, and changes in laws or regulations could increase operational complexity and compliance costs. Non-compliance with regulatory standards could result in sanctions, penalties, or reputational damage.

**7. Litigation:** As a provider of insurance products, we are subject to litigation risk, including claims from policyholders, regulatory actions, or other legal proceedings. Such litigation could result in significant legal expenses, settlements, or damage awards.

## Competitive and Market Risks

**8. Competitive Pressure:** The Property & Casualty Insurance market is highly competitive, with companies competing on the basis of price, product offerings, and service quality. Increased competition could lead to pricing pressures, reduced margins, and loss of market share.

**9. Changes in Customer Preferences:** Evolving customer preferences and expectations for insurance products and services could require us to adapt our offerings and business strategies. Failure to meet changing customer expectations could impact our ability to retain and acquire customers.

## Financial and Credit Risks

**10. Investment Performance:** Our financial performance is affected by the results of our investment portfolio. Market volatility, poor investment choices, or credit risk can impact investment income and, consequently, our overall financial condition.

**11. Liquidity Risk:** While we strive to manage our liabilities to ensure liquidity, unforeseen claims or other cash flow challenges could impact our ability to meet obligations, affecting our operational capabilities.

## Catastrophic Events and Climate Change

**12. Exposure to Catastrophic Events:** Natural disasters, severe weather conditions, or other catastrophic events could result in large-scale claims, impacting our financial condition and capital resources.

**13. Climate Change Risks:** Climate change introduces uncertainties regarding the frequency and severity of natural events impacting our underwriting results. Changes in environmental regulations related to climate change can also introduce additional costs or operational limitations.

Highmark Industries Co. strives to proactively manage these risks through strategic planning, risk management practices, and continuous monitoring of the economic and regulatory environment. Nevertheless, we cannot assure that we will successfully mitigate all risks. Each of these risk factors should be carefully considered in conjunction with the other information contained in our Annual Report on Form 10-K.

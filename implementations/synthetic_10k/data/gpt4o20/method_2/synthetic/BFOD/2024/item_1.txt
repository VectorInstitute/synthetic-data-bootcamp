# Item 1. Business

## General

Highmark Industries Co. (“Highmark” or “the Company”) is a leading provider in the Property & Casualty Insurance sector, committed to delivering comprehensive risk management solutions to its clientele. Established in the financial hub of Connecticut, Highmark leverages its strategic location and industry expertise to ensure robust and reliable financial protection and peace of mind for its customers. As of the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, Highmark reported revenues of $2,999,501,569, underscoring its strong market position and consistent growth trajectory in the competitive insurance landscape.

## Our Mission

The mission of Highmark Industries Co. is to empower our clients with innovative insurance products tailored to meet their unique risks and protect their future financial security. By adhering to our core values of integrity, client-focus, and innovation, we aim to establish enduring relationships with our stakeholders, including customers, employees, and shareholders.

## Business Segments

Highmark operates exclusively within the Property & Casualty Insurance segment, offering a wide variety of products designed to meet the needs of individuals and businesses. Our diversified portfolio covers multiple lines of insurance, which are discussed in detail below:

### Personal Insurance

Within our personal insurance division, Highmark provides coverage solutions that include homeowner’s insurance, automobile insurance, and personal liability insurance. These products are designed to safeguard individuals and families against unforeseen events, protecting their properties and personal assets.

### Commercial Insurance

Highmark specializes in comprehensive commercial insurance solutions. These offerings cater to small, medium, and large enterprises and encompass property coverage, liability insurance, and specialty insurance products. Our commercial lines are crafted to ensure businesses can operate with confidence, minimizing financial disruptions from potential claims or losses.

## Risk Management and Underwriting

Risk assessment and underwriting are the cornerstone of Highmark's operational strategy. With a team of highly skilled underwriters and advanced analytical tools, the Company employs a disciplined approach to evaluating risks, setting premiums, and ensuring profitability while maintaining competitive pricing. Highmark rigorously monitors external economic conditions and adjusts its strategies accordingly to mitigate adverse impacts.

## Distribution Channels

Highmark distributes its insurance products primarily through independent agents and brokers, fostering extensive networks that enable the Company to reach a broad customer base. Through these channels, Highmark ensures high-quality service and personalized, locally-attuned support that enhances customer satisfaction and loyalty.

## Technology and Innovation

Recognizing the importance of technological advancement, Highmark is committed to continuous innovation. By investing in modern information technology systems, the Company enhances its operational efficiency, customer service, and analytical capabilities. Highmark utilizes cutting-edge data analytics to better evaluate risk parameters and customer behavior, thereby tailoring its offerings to more precisely meet market demands.

## Regulatory Environment

Operating within a highly regulated sector, Highmark adheres to the regulatory requirements established by insurance regulators in the jurisdictions where it operates. The Company is committed to maintaining compliance with all pertinent regulations, thereby protecting consumer interests while ensuring operational integrity.

## Competitive Strengths

Highmark's competitive strengths lie in its strong underwriting capabilities, comprehensive product portfolio, and superior customer service. The Company's focus on innovation and strategic partnerships further augments its ability to maintain a competitive edge in the dynamic insurance ecosystem.

## Employees

Highmark’s success is driven by a team of dedicated professionals who are experts in their respective fields. The Company fosters a culture of growth and learning, providing training and development opportunities that empower employees to excel in delivering exceptional service and solutions to our clients.

## Future Outlook

Looking ahead, Highmark Industries Co. remains committed to growth and innovation, steering the Company towards new opportunities and expanding our market presence in the Property & Casualty Insurance industry. By constantly evaluating market trends and adapting to changes, Highmark is poised to continue its legacy of trust, resilience, and excellence in service delivery.

Highmark Industries Co. remains dedicated to its mission of providing top-tier insurance solutions, ensuring both our clients’ and the Company’s prosperity in the evolving global economy.

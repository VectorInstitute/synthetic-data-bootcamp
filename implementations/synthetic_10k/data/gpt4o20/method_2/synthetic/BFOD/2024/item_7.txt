# Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

The following discussion and analysis provides information that Highmark Industries Co. (“Highmark” or the “Company”) believes is relevant to an assessment and understanding of its consolidated financial condition and results of operations. This section should be read in conjunction with Highmark’s financial statements and related notes for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024.

## Overview

Highmark Industries Co. is a leading entity within the Property & Casualty Insurance sector, headquartered in Connecticut. The Company is dedicated to delivering comprehensive risk management solutions tailored to meet the needs of its diverse clientele. For the fiscal year 2024, Highmark reported revenues of $2,999,501,569, highlighting the Company’s strong performance and competitive positioning within the insurance industry.

## Results of Operations

### Revenues

In fiscal 2024, Highmark generated revenues of $2,999,501,569. This robust revenue performance underscores the effectiveness of our strategic initiatives to expand market share and enhance customer value through diversified product offerings and superior service quality.

### Operating Expenses

For the year ended December 31, 2024, operating expenses totaled $993,412,419, primarily driven by significant investments in technology and personnel aimed at bolstering our underwriting capabilities and customer service operations. A notable reduction in other operating expenses, recorded at $(10,735,306,346), reflects a streamlined operational strategy and effective cost-control measures.

### Net Income

Highmark achieved a net income of $2,699,551,412 in fiscal 2024, evidencing strong financial health and operational efficiency. This positive net income reflects both our disciplined approach to expense management and successful execution of growth strategies.

## Financial Condition

### Balance Sheet

As of December 31, 2024, Highmark's total assets stood at $59,990,031,374. The significant asset base is supported by a balanced composition of cash and equivalents ($167,160,660), strategic investments in property, plant, and equipment ($668,621,268), and intangible assets ($4,890,489,23), including goodwill valued at $1,556,779,528.

Total liabilities amounted to $47,557,166,451, aligning with prudent financial management to sustain operations and fund strategic initiatives. The leverage is comfortably managed, with a long-term debt of $3,588,091,206, supported by ample equity.

Stockholders' equity remains strong at $12,432,864,923, driven by retained earnings of $16,942,245,075, reflecting the profitability and sound financial stewardship underpinning Highmark’s growth strategy.

### Liquidity and Capital Resources

#### Cash Flow Summary

**Operating Activities:** Highmark generated cash flows from operations amounting to $11,346,702,583, substantiated by robust revenue streams and efficient management of working capital.

**Investing Activities:** The Company’s investing activities resulted in a net cash outflow of $(8,067,337,827), primarily due to capital expenditures ($274,391,448) and investments in technology to enhance future competitiveness and operational efficiency.

**Financing Activities:** Financing activities resulted in a net outflow of $(4,097,708,957), attributable mainly to share repurchases ($2,741,879,459) aimed at returning capital to shareholders and optimizing our capital structure.

Overall, Highmark recorded a net decrease in cash of $(818,344,201) during the fiscal year 2024. The Company maintains substantial liquidity to support ongoing operations and strategic initiatives.

## Segment Review

Highmark operates exclusively within the Property & Casualty Insurance segment. The Company’s diversified portfolio addresses both personal and commercial insurance needs, ensuring comprehensive coverage for individuals and organizations alike. The balanced suite of products and risk mitigation strategies continues to underpin our market leadership.

## Market Environment

The fiscal year 2024 presented a challenging environment with economic fluctuations and regulatory developments. However, Highmark’s proactive risk management and strategic planning enabled the Company to navigate these challenges effectively, supporting sustainable growth and profitability.

## Strategic Initiatives and Future Outlook

Highmark remains committed to pursuing innovations that propel growth and strengthen market position. Key strategic initiatives focus on enhancing digital capabilities, expanding distribution networks, and optimizing operations to deliver superior customer experiences. Looking forward, Highmark is well-positioned to capitalize on emerging trends and opportunities within the Property & Casualty Insurance sector.

In conclusion, Highmark Industries Co. demonstrated strong operational resilience and financial performance in fiscal 2024. The Company remains focused on leveraging its strengths to drive future growth and deliver sustained value to stakeholders.

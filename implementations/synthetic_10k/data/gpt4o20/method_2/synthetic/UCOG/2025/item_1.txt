Item 1. Business

Introduction

Amberwood Robotics Co. ("Amberwood," the "Company," "we," "our," or "us") is a pioneering entity within the Consumer Discretionary sector, focused specifically on the Automotive Retail sub-industry. Headquartered in the vibrant state of Tennessee, we have dedicated ourselves to revolutionizing the automotive retail experience through advanced robotic technologies. Our operations, which span nationally, aim to offer unparalleled value to both retail customers and commercial partners.

Our innovative product lines include robotics-integrated automotive parts and accessories, designed to enhance vehicle performance and user convenience. We pride ourselves on a seamless omnichannel approach, ensuring our clients have access to our offerings through our expanding network of retail outlets and our comprehensive online platform. As of the end of our fiscal year 2025, we achieved total revenues of $16,591,900,553, underlining our strong market position and continuous growth trajectory.

Human Capital Resources

At Amberwood, our workforce is the cornerstone of our sustained innovation and market success. Guided by our core values — Innovation, Integrity, Collaboration, and Excellence — we strive to cultivate a work environment that inspires creativity and nurtures talent. Our team is encouraged to be forward-thinking and proactive, contributing to the continuous development of cutting-edge solutions for our customers.

We are committed to being the employer of choice within our sector by offering competitive compensation, robust benefits packages, and ongoing professional development opportunities. Our training programs are designed to equip employees with the skills necessary to thrive in an evolving technological landscape. We believe these initiatives are vital in attracting, retaining, and motivating top-tier professionals who are passionate about shaping the future of automotive retail.

Our Operations

Amberwood's operations are strategically aligned to deliver exceptional customer experiences and drive operational efficiency. Our Tennessee headquarters serves as the central hub for our research and development activities, focusing on the integration of robotics in automotive applications. This location also houses our primary distribution center, supporting our nationwide network.

Our retail strategy emphasizes both physical and digital touchpoints, ensuring that customers have access to our products whenever and wherever they choose to engage with us. We leverage advanced data analytics to optimize inventory management and streamline supply chain operations, enhancing the overall efficiency and responsiveness of our network.

Innovation and Technology

Innovation remains at the heart of Amberwood's business strategy. Our commitment to research and development is unwavering, with a significant portion of our resources dedicated to pioneering technologies that redefine automotive retail. By integrating robotics with traditional auto parts and services, we create unique value propositions that differentiate us from competitors.

Our technological advancements are protected by a robust portfolio of patents, fostering a competitive edge in the market. This technological leadership not only drives our product development but also enhances the operational capabilities of our commercial partners, ensuring mutual growth and success.

Corporate Responsibility

Amberwood recognizes the importance of corporate responsibility and sustainability in its operations. We are dedicated to minimizing our environmental footprint by adopting sustainable practices across our supply chain. Our initiatives focus on reducing waste, optimizing energy use, and promoting responsible sourcing of materials.

We actively engage with our local communities through various outreach programs and partnerships, reinforcing our commitment to making a positive impact on society. These efforts are integral to our corporate ethos and demonstrate our dedication to ethical business practices.

Conclusion

As we continue to expand our footprint in the automotive retail sector, Amberwood remains steadfast in delivering value through innovation, exceptional customer service, and responsible business practices. Our forward-looking approach prepares us to navigate the evolving landscape of technology and consumer expectations, ensuring that we meet the dynamic needs of our customers and stakeholders.

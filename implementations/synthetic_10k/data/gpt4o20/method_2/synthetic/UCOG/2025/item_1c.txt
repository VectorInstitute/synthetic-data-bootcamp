**Item 1C. Cybersecurity**

**Risk Management and Strategy**

At Amberwood Robotics Co. ("Amberwood," "we," "our," or "us"), cybersecurity is a cornerstone of our commitment to protecting our information assets, ensuring the privacy of our customers, and maintaining the integrity of our operations. Recognizing the critical nature of cybersecurity in the automotive retail industry, our approach is proactive, comprehensive, and aligned with the evolving threat landscape.

Our cybersecurity strategy is guided by the principles outlined in the NIST Cybersecurity Framework. This framework enables us to identify, protect, detect, respond to, and recover from cybersecurity events and potential threats. We are devoted to continuously improving our defenses through an integrated approach that encompasses people, processes, and technology.

**Program**

Amberwood has developed a multi-layered cybersecurity program that leverages cutting-edge technologies, rigorous protocols, and industry best practices. Our program focuses on threat mitigation, risk assessment, and the resilience of our systems to safeguard against cybersecurity threats.

We utilize a suite of advanced cybersecurity tools, including intrusion detection systems, firewalls, and encryption technologies to protect our digital assets. Regular vulnerability assessments, penetration testing, and threat simulations are conducted to evaluate and enhance the effectiveness of our security controls.

Our incident response plan provides a structured approach for identifying, analyzing, and mitigating cybersecurity incidents. This plan includes detailed procedures for response coordination, mitigation strategies, and communication protocols. Our cross-functional response team, which includes representatives from IT, legal, communications, and operations, ensures a swift and comprehensive handling of any incidents.

Vendor risk management is also a critical component of our cybersecurity strategy. Before onboarding any third-party service providers, we conduct thorough assessments of their cybersecurity measures to ensure compliance with industry standards and our requirements. Ongoing monitoring is carried out to maintain a strong security posture and mitigate potential risks associated with third-party engagements.

Our commitment to fostering a culture of cybersecurity awareness is evident through our Information Security Awareness program. This program educates our employees about cybersecurity risks and best practices, using a combination of training sessions, internal communications, and awareness campaigns.

While Amberwood has not experienced any material cybersecurity incidents, we maintain robust cybersecurity insurance to mitigate potential financial impacts from such events.

**Cybersecurity Risks**

The ever-evolving nature of cybersecurity threats poses significant risks to our operations. Despite our comprehensive security measures, the potential for unauthorized access, data theft, or system disruptions remains. Such events could impair our ability to operate effectively, damage our reputation, and result in financial losses. The potential impacts of these threats are discussed further in Item 1A, "Risk Factors."

**Governance**

Our cybersecurity risk management program is an integral part of Amberwood's broader enterprise risk management framework. This integration allows us to maintain a holistic view of risk exposure and prioritize our responses effectively.

Oversight of our cybersecurity strategy and initiatives is provided by our Board of Directors, with support from the Audit Committee. The full Board receives regular updates on cybersecurity matters, ensuring that they are informed of potential risks and the progress of our security initiatives.

Our Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) plays a key role in implementing our cybersecurity program. The CISO reports directly to the Chief Information Officer and works closely with the executive leadership team. Regular meetings with the Audit Committee ensure transparency and accountability in managing cybersecurity risks.

The Audit Committee reviews and discusses cybersecurity concerns quarterly, engaging directly with the CISO to understand potential threats, progress on risk mitigation strategies, and the evolution of our cybersecurity roadmap. This governance structure ensures that Amberwood remains vigilant and prepared to address the dynamic cybersecurity landscape.

In conclusion, Amberwood's comprehensive cybersecurity strategy underscores our commitment to protecting our digital infrastructure, customer information, and overall business operations. As we continue to innovate and expand our capabilities, cybersecurity will remain a top priority, driving our efforts to ensure a secure and resilient ecosystem for our stakeholders.

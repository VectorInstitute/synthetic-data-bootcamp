**Item 7. Management’s Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations**

**Overview**

Amberwood Robotics Co. ("Amberwood," "we," "our," or "us") is situated at the forefront of the Consumer Discretionary sector, nestled in the Automotive Retail sub-industry. Based in Tennessee, our mission is to transform the automotive retail experience by infusing advanced robotic technologies into our product offerings. These integrated solutions, available through our extensive retail outlets and digital platform, continue to redefine customer interactions and positioned us as a market leader.

Our innovative pursuits in robotics-integrated automotive parts have resonated with consumers and commercial partners alike, evidenced by total revenues of $16,591,900,553 for fiscal year 2025. This achievement underscores our commitment to enhancing vehicle performance and user convenience while ensuring a seamless omnichannel shopping experience.

**Results of Operations**

For the fiscal year ended 2025, Amberwood witnessed a remarkable financial performance reflective of our strategic initiatives and market demand. Total revenues reached $16,591,900,553, driven by robust sales across our product lines and operational geographies.

**Revenue and Operating Income**

Revenues stood at $16,591,900,553, showcasing our successful penetration into emerging segments and fortified customer relationships. Operating income reached $2,997,683,663, driven by cost-effective operations and strategic investments in technology and talent.

Operating expenses were characterized by the following:
- Cost of revenue was $8,763,850,438, consistent with our focus on quality and innovation in sourcing and production.
- Selling, General and Administrative expenses (SG&A) amounted to $6,243,469,387, reflecting investments in marketing, technology integration, and workforce development.
- A notable component of our financial strategy was efficient management of other operating expenses, resulting in a credit of $1,413,102,935 arising from positive adjustments in asset valuations and strategic divestitures.

**Net Income and Taxation**

Our fiscal prowess translated to a net income of $2,225,755,391. The income tax expense incurred was $771,928,272, positioning our tax strategy to effectively support sustainable profitability.

**Liquidity and Capital Resources**

Amberwood’s liquidity and capital resource strategy is designed to ensure financial flexibility and support our long-term growth ambitions. Our cash flow from operations reached $2,442,726,186, propelled by efficient working capital management and operational leverage.

Despite capital expenditures amounting to $1,279,507,113, primarily directed towards upgrading our technological capabilities and expanding our operational footprint, our cash from investing activities registered a deficit of $1,089,768,404. On the financing front, share repurchases valued at $1,416,846,858 exemplify our commitment to enhancing shareholder value, culminating in net cash used in financing activities of $1,442,329,486.

The comprehensive focus on strategic capital allocation resulted in a minimal decrease in cash and equivalents by $89,371,704, ensuring ample reserves to meet short-term liabilities and invest in future growth.

**Balance Sheet Analysis**

Our balance sheet at the close of fiscal year 2025 presents a robust asset base of $15,883,805,319. Notably, property, plant, and equipment accounted for $6,267,201,993, underlining our investment in cutting-edge facilities that drive our operational excellence.

Liabilities aggregated to $15,566,129,213, with accounts payable constituting $6,115,802,914, indicative of efficient supply chain management. Meanwhile, stockholders' equity stood at $317,676,106, reflecting our strategic focus on maintaining optimal capital structure and shareholder returns.

**Strategic Initiatives and Outlook**

Looking ahead, Amberwood is poised to capitalize on evolving market dynamics through continued innovation, strategic partnerships, and expansion into untapped markets. By leveraging our technological prowess and enhancing consumer engagement, we aim to sustain our growth trajectory and reinforce our leadership position in automotive retail.

In conclusion, Amberwood remains committed to achieving long-term success through strategic investments in innovation, effective risk management, and a robust framework for financial stewardship. Our forward-looking strategies ensure preparedness for industry changes and consumer trends, aligning us firmly with sustained growth and enhanced stakeholder value.

# ITEM 1C. CYBERSECURITY

## Overview

Ironwood Logistics Group, Inc. ("Ironwood" or the "Company") acknowledges the critical importance of cybersecurity in safeguarding the integrity of our operations and the trust our clients place in our life and health insurance offerings. In an industry marked by the handling of sensitive personal and financial information, robust cybersecurity measures are paramount. As such, Ironwood is dedicated to maintaining comprehensive cybersecurity protocols and infrastructure to protect against data breaches, unauthorized access, and cyber threats.

## Cybersecurity Framework and Governance

Ironwood's cybersecurity policy framework aligns with the NIST Cybersecurity Framework and ISO/IEC 27001 standards. These internationally recognized frameworks guide our practices to ensure a systematic approach to managing sensitive information and mitigating potential cyber risks. Our adherence to these frameworks underscores our commitment to achieving the highest levels of data security, integrity, confidentiality, and availability.

Oversight of Ironwood's cybersecurity strategy and risk management processes is provided by a dedicated Technology and Cybersecurity Committee of the Board. This committee plays a pivotal role in overseeing our cybersecurity initiatives, ensuring accountability, and establishing strategic priorities to address evolving cyber threats. Regular updates on cybersecurity issues and initiatives are presented to the Board, reinforcing a culture of security awareness and proactive governance.

## Cybersecurity Policies and Procedures

Ironwood has implemented a robust suite of policies and procedures designed to protect sensitive information. These include, but are not limited to, data encryption both in transit and at rest, multi-factor authentication for accessing systems, and comprehensive access control mechanisms. Our protocols are regularly reviewed and updated to accommodate new threats and technologies, ensuring our defenses remain resilient and adaptive.

Employee training is a critical component of our cybersecurity strategy. We conduct regular training programs and simulation exercises to enhance employee awareness of cybersecurity risks and empower individuals to identify and report suspicious activities. By fostering a culture of vigilance and responsibility, we aim to minimize human error, one of the most common vectors for cyber incidents.

## Risk Assessment and Incident Response

Ironwood conducts ongoing risk assessments to evaluate vulnerabilities and potential exposures within our information technology systems. These assessments enable us to prioritize and address high-risk areas, applying appropriate controls and safeguards to mitigate identified threats. Our risk management approach is iterative, incorporating lessons learned and industry best practices to fortify our defenses continuously.

In the event of a cybersecurity incident, Ironwood has developed comprehensive incident response protocols to manage and mitigate any potential impact swiftly. Our incident response strategy includes identifying and containing breaches, eradicating threats, restoring systems to normal operations, and communicating with affected stakeholders transparently. Although we have not disclosed any incidents as of the fiscal year 2023, our preparedness ensures rapid responses should any cybersecurity events arise.

## Third-Party Vendors and Partner Security

Ironwood recognizes that cybersecurity extends beyond our internal systems to include third-party vendors and partners who have access to our data and systems. Accordingly, we have established rigorous vendor management protocols to assess the cybersecurity posture of third-party service providers. Contracts with vendors incorporate stringent cybersecurity requirements, and we engage in ongoing monitoring to ensure compliance with these standards.

## Technological Investments and Innovations

As part of our commitment to maintaining cutting-edge cybersecurity capabilities, Ironwood invests significantly in advanced technologies and innovations designed to enhance our cyber defenses. This includes deploying advanced threat detection systems, leveraging artificial intelligence and machine learning to identify anomalous activities, and automating routine security tasks to enhance response times.

Our investment in cybersecurity technology is complemented by participation in cybersecurity information-sharing networks. These collaborations allow us to stay abreast of emerging threats and industry trends, fostering a proactive approach to cybersecurity risk management.

## Future Outlook

Looking forward, Ironwood will continue to evolve our cybersecurity strategies to meet the demands of an ever-changing threat landscape. Our focus will remain on strengthening our cybersecurity infrastructure, enhancing employee awareness, and cultivating partnerships with industry leaders and regulatory bodies to improve our defenses continuously.

We anticipate that the complexity of cyber threats will grow, driven by advances in technology and the increasing interconnectedness of global systems. As such, we are committed to remaining vigilant, investing in our cybersecurity framework, and adapting to new challenges to ensure the security and trust that underpin our mission to provide peace of mind to our policyholders. Through these efforts, Ironwood aims to position ourselves as a cybersecurity leader within the life and health insurance industry, safeguarding the privacy and security of our clients and stakeholders.

# ITEM 1A. RISK FACTORS

Ironwood Logistics Group, Inc. ("Ironwood" or the "Company") operates within the life and health insurance industry, a sector characterized by rapid technological advancements, dynamic regulatory environments, and evolving customer expectations. Our ability to successfully execute our business strategy depends on addressing various risks, which could adversely impact our operations, financial condition, and results of operations. We have identified the following material risks:

## INDUSTRY AND MARKET RISKS

### Competitive Pressure

The life and health insurance industry is highly competitive. Ironwood faces competition from established national and international insurers, new market entrants, and insurers with deep financial resources. This intense competition could pressure our pricing models, force increased investment in customer acquisition, and reduce our market share and profitability.

### Economic Volatility

Economic conditions, both nationally and globally, significantly impact the purchasing power of our clients and investment income from our asset portfolio. Uncertainty or downturns in the economy could lead to a decline in policy sales and increased claims rates, adversely affecting our revenues and capital adequacy.

## OPERATIONAL RISKS

### Underwriting and Pricing Risks

The failure to accurately underwrite and price our insurance products commensurate with the risk and potential claims could significantly harm our financial results. Our underwriting processes rely on numerous factors, analyses, and assumptions, and errors or inaccurate assumptions can negatively impact our profit margins and reserve adequacy.

### Technology and Cybersecurity Risks

Ironwood's reliance on technological systems and digital platforms for operations, customer engagement, and data management introduce risks related to cybersecurity threats and technology failures. Cyberattacks, data breaches, or technical disruptions could compromise sensitive customer information, disrupt business continuity, and damage our reputation.

## REGULATORY AND LEGAL RISKS

### Regulatory Compliance

Ironwood operates in a highly regulated environment, with oversight by state insurance departments, the National Association of Insurance Commissioners (NAIC), and other bodies. Changes in laws, regulations, or regulatory interpretations can impact our product offerings, sales practices, and capital requirements, increasing operational complexity and compliance costs.

### Litigation and Claims

The nature of life and health insurance exposes us to claims and potential litigation. Disputes over coverage, policy terms, or claim denials can result in prolonged legal proceedings, increased costs, and reputational harm. Unfavorable outcomes may necessitate significant financial settlements or increased loss reserves.

## FINANCIAL RISKS

### Investment Portfolio Risks

Our investment portfolio's performance is critical to generating returns and meeting policyholder obligations. Adverse market conditions, interest rate fluctuations, or credit events can impair our investment income, lead to unrealized losses, and affect the fair value of our portfolio, impacting our financial statements and equity.

### Liquidity and Capital Adequacy

Sustaining adequate liquidity and capital reserves is vital for both regulatory compliance and financial stability. Any depletion of liquidity reserves due to unexpected claims or regulatory charges may necessitate capital raising at unfavorable terms, potentially diluting stockholder value.

## STRATEGIC AND REPUTATIONAL RISKS

### Customer Satisfaction and Retention

Ironwood's commitment to customer satisfaction is core to our strategy. Any failure to meet customer expectations or manage relationships effectively can result in dissatisfied clients, increased policy lapses, and turnover, undermining our efforts to retain and expand our client base.

### Brand and Reputation

Our brand and reputation are key assets. Any adverse publicity from operational missteps, negative customer experiences, or ethical lapses can erode trust and impede our ability to attract and retain customers, partners, and employees, ultimately hindering growth initiatives.

## HUMAN CAPITAL RISKS

### Talent Management

Our success relies significantly on attracting, developing, and retaining skilled personnel. Failure to manage talent through effective recruitment, training, and retention strategies may impair our operational capabilities, innovative potential, and market competitiveness.

### Workforce Diversity and Inclusion

Ironwood emphasizes diversity and inclusion as strategic priorities. Any failure to foster an inclusive corporate culture or allegations of workplace discrimination could damage our reputation, hurt employee morale, and hinder productivity.

---

The risks described herein are not exhaustive. Additional risks and uncertainties may arise or become material, potentially impacting Ironwood's future performance and financial condition. We continue to invest in risk management practices to anticipate, manage, and mitigate these challenges and ensure long-term resilience and growth.

# ITEM 7. MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS OF FINANCIAL CONDITION AND RESULTS OF OPERATIONS

This discussion contains forward-looking statements that involve risks and uncertainties. ## Overview

Ironwood Logistics Group, Inc. ("Ironwood" or the "Company"), operates within the life and health insurance sub-industry under the broad financial sector umbrella. Our headquarters in Georgia provides a strategic base to serve our diverse clientele across the nation. As a key player in the industry, Ironwood experienced robust performance in fiscal year 2023, generating revenues amounting to $21,006,220,039. This achievement is indicative of our unwavering dedication to innovation and our customer-centric approach that caters to the evolving needs of our policyholders.

Our fiscal year 2023 showed a net income of $5,411,069,160 as we successfully managed our operating expenses while strategically investing in growth initiatives. The Company's total asset base stood at $136,747,486,447, reflecting our solid financial foundation and capacity to address future opportunities and challenges.

## Financial Condition

### Assets

Ironwood's total assets at the end of fiscal year 2023 were $136,747,486,447, comprising primarily other assets valued at $130,997,706,005. Cash and equivalents were recorded at $4,954,272,868, providing us with a robust liquidity position to meet operational and strategic needs. Property, plant, and equipment, net of depreciation, were valued at $506,375,254, illustrating continued investment in our operational infrastructure.

### Liabilities

Our total liabilities amounted to $113,004,344,000, stemming exclusively from other liabilities. This structure underscores our strategic decision to operate without long-term debt, enhancing our financial flexibility and reducing interest-related expenditures.

### Stockholders' Equity

The total stockholders' equity stood at $23,743,142,447, a reflection of our profitability and prudent capital management strategies. Retained earnings reached $53,979,187,121, providing a cushion for future growth investments and dividends. However, accumulated other comprehensive income was negative at $5,262,465,075, largely due to market volatility impacting unrealized investment gains.

## Results of Operations

### Revenue

Ironwood generated total revenues of $21,006,220,039 in fiscal year 2023, driven by increased demand for our life and health insurance products. Our diversified product offerings and strengthened distribution channels contributed to sustained growth despite competitive pressures.

### Operating Expenses

Operating expenses totaled $13,308,191,168, consisting of selling, general, and administrative expenses of $3,485,565,096 and other operating expenses of $10,455,204,072. Our ability to manage these costs effectively underpins our operational efficiency and enhanced profit margins.

### Interest Expense

Interest expense for the year was $215,996,238, reflecting interest on funding activities related to portfolio and operational financing requirements. Our disciplined approach to managing interest obligations supports the Company's liquidity and capital position.

### Net Income

Net income for the fiscal year 2023 was $5,411,069,160, an increase over the previous year due to higher revenues and efficient cost management. This outcome underscores Ironwood's strategic strength and scalability in navigating the dynamic insurance market landscape.

## Liquidity and Capital Resources

Ironwood concluded fiscal year 2023 with a net cash position change of -$286,506,765. Net cash provided by operating activities amounted to $3,583,852,443, reflecting successful profit conversion into cash flow, notwithstanding changes in working capital requirements.

Investing activities resulted in cash inflows of $829,238,060, representing strategic reinvestment of resources to support business growth. Notably, capital expenditures were zero, aligning with our strategic focus on optimizing existing assets.

Conversely, cash flow from financing activities was negative at $4,699,597,268, primarily driven by dividends paid of $1,207,424,837 and share repurchases totaling $2,922,938,765. These actions illustrate our commitment to returning value to shareholders while balancing capital needs for future growth.

## Outlook

Moving into fiscal year 2024, Ironwood remains committed to strengthening our market position within the life and health insurance sector. We will continue focusing on enhancing our technological capabilities, expanding our product offerings, and optimizing our distribution network to improve customer engagement and satisfaction.

Our strategic initiatives will emphasize risk management and underwriting innovation, which are critical to sustaining profitability in a competitive environment. We anticipate continued economic fluctuations and regulatory changes, which will be met with proactive strategies to mitigate potential impacts on our operations.

As we advance, Ironwood remains dedicated to maintaining a strong balance sheet, ensuring liquidity, and upholding our commitment to shareholders through continued dividends and prudent capital management.

Our future efforts will remain aligned with our mission to deliver peace of mind and financial security to our customers, positioning Ironwood as a resilient and responsive leader in the life and health insurance industry.

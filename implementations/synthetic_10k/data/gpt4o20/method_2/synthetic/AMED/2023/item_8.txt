# Item 8. Financial Statements and Supplementary Data

The following financial statements and supplementary data provide a comprehensive view of Riverton Energy Group, Inc.'s ("Riverton" or "the Company") financial performance and position for the fiscal year ended 2023. These statements aim to offer insights into the financial condition, operational results, liquidity, and capital resources, reinforcing transparency and facilitating stakeholder decision-making processes.

## Consolidated Balance Sheets

The consolidated balance sheet analysis highlights Riverton's asset management effectiveness and financial stability. As of the end of fiscal year 2023, the Company reported total assets of $4,049,858,186, marking substantial investment in strategic resources to support growth and innovation.

### Assets

- **Cash and Cash Equivalents:** Riverton maintained a robust liquidity position with cash and equivalents totaling $740,770,190, providing significant flexibility for strategic initiatives and operational demands.
- **Inventory:** Inventory levels amounted to $274,888,695, reflecting efficient inventory management aligned with production and sales strategies across the Aerospace & Defense sub-industry.
- **Property, Plant, and Equipment (Net):** Net property, plant, and equipment stood at $222,141,365, underscoring the Company's commitment to maintaining state-of-the-art facilities to bolster its manufacturing and research capabilities.
- **Goodwill:** Goodwill was reported at $63,118,771, highlighting strategic acquisitions and alliances aimed at enhancing market presence and technological expertise.
- **Intangible Assets (Net):** Intangible assets, net of amortization, totaled $24,857,570, illustrating Riverton's investments in proprietary technologies and intellectual property essential for competitive differentiation.
- **Other Assets:** Other assets were valued at $2,724,081,595, capturing investments in technology infrastructure, advanced research platforms, and strategic partnerships.

### Liabilities

- **Accounts Payable and Accrued Liabilities:** Together, accounts payable and accrued liabilities amounted to $339,349,522, reflecting the Company's efficient management of short-term obligations to sustain operational flow.
- **Long-term Debt:** Riverton's prudent financial strategy is evidenced by long-term debt of $785,521,524, maintaining a balance between leveraging opportunities and preserving financial health.
- **Other Liabilities:** Other liabilities, totaling $1,256,055,965, comprise strategic and operational commitments requisite for continuing the Company's growth trajectory.

### Equity

Riverton's total stockholders' equity was robust at $1,668,931,175, reflecting a well-capitalized structure positioning the Company favorably for future initiatives.

- **Additional Paid-in Capital:** The Company reported substantial additional paid-in capital of $1,374,842,094, testament to its strong equity financing foundation.
- **Retained Earnings:** Retained earnings amounted to $473,109,467, underscoring the Company's profitability and its capacity to reinvest earnings into future growth.
- **Accumulated Other Comprehensive Income and Other Equity:** The negative balance in accumulated other comprehensive income and other equity portions reflects market variances, aligning with comprehensive risk management practices to support strategic aspirations.

# Item 1C. Cybersecurity

## Overview

Riverton Energy Group, Inc. ("Riverton" or "the Company") recognizes that the integrity of its operations and the trust of its stakeholders are inseparably linked to the security of its information technology systems. As a leading entity within the Aerospace & Defense sub-industry, safeguarding our digital assets and sensitive data is critical to maintaining competitive advantage, ensuring operational continuity, and fulfilling contractual obligations. In fiscal year 2023, Riverton's commitment to robust cybersecurity practices was underscored by adherence to industry-leading standards and vigilant oversight mechanisms.

## Cybersecurity Framework and Governance

In alignment with the best practices for risk management and resilience, Riverton's cybersecurity framework is guided by both the NIST Cybersecurity Framework and ISO/IEC 27001 standards. This dual adherence ensures comprehensive coverage of security principles, including risk assessment, control implementation, continuous monitoring, and incident response. 

Oversight of the Company's cybersecurity initiatives is exercised by the Audit Committee of the Board of Directors. This committee is tasked with evaluating risks, authorizing strategic priorities, and ensuring that Riverton's cybersecurity posture is appropriately aligned with its business objectives. Regular updates on cybersecurity matters are presented to the Board, facilitating informed decision-making and strategic alignment.

## Cybersecurity Incident

During the 2023 fiscal year, Riverton experienced a cybersecurity incident involving a ransomware attempt on its internal IT systems. Although this event posed a potential risk to our operations, prompt action was taken by Riverton's cybersecurity team to detect and contain the threat. Notably, no data was exfiltrated, underscoring the effectiveness of our preventive measures and rapid incident response mechanisms.

The Company's robust approach to cybersecurity management involves not just incident response but also the fostering of a proactive security culture across the organization. Lessons learned from this incident have been integrated into ongoing training programs and security enhancements, ensuring continuous improvement of our defenses against evolving cyber threats.

## Risk Management and Threat Mitigation

Riverton's cybersecurity risk management strategy encompasses a multi-layered defense approach to protect against unauthorized access, theft, and damage. The Company conducts comprehensive penetration testing and vulnerability assessments, aiming to preemptively identify potential weaknesses in its IT infrastructure. Additionally, security audits and evaluations are performed regularly by both internal teams and third-party experts, ensuring compliance with regulatory requirements and industry best practices.

Crucially, Riverton maintains a proactive threat intelligence program that leverages advanced analytics and artificial intelligence to predict and combat emerging cyber threats. Our collaborative partnerships with governmental and industry-specific cybersecurity organizations further enhance our capability to anticipate and mitigate risks.

## Data Protection and Privacy

Recognizing the importance of confidentiality and integrity of data, Riverton has implemented rigorous data protection protocols. Encryption is widely used for sensitive data, both in transit and at rest, adding an additional layer of security to our information assets. Moreover, access controls and user authentication mechanisms are strictly enforced to manage data access rights effectively.

To uphold privacy standards, Riverton routinely reviews its data handling practices against applicable data protection laws and regulations. This commitment to data privacy is integral to maintaining trust with clients, partners, and stakeholders, reinforcing our reputation as a responsible and ethical leader in the Aerospace & Defense sector.

## Employee Training and Awareness

A key component of Riverton's cybersecurity strategy is fostering an informed and alert workforce. Cybersecurity training programs are regularly conducted to enhance employee awareness and preparedness, ensuring that each member of the Riverton team is equipped to recognize and respond to potential cyber threats. These programs are updated to reflect the latest in security developments and industry insights.

Phishing simulations and security workshops provide practical training experiences, helping to reinforce the importance of cybersecurity at every level of the organization. By embedding cybersecurity consciousness into our corporate culture, Riverton strives to empower its employees to be the frontline defense in securing our digital landscape.

## Business Continuity and Recovery Planning

Business continuity planning is an essential aspect of Riverton's cybersecurity framework, ensuring minimal disruption in the event of a cyber incident. Contingency plans, including comprehensive disaster recovery protocols, are in place to facilitate rapid restoration of operations and data. Redundant systems and data backup procedures ensure that critical business functions can continue with minimal downtime.

Regular drills and recovery exercises are conducted to test and refine our response strategies, honing our ability to maintain operations under adverse conditions. This proactive approach not only mitigates risk but also instills confidence in our ability to sustain business continuity amidst unforeseen challenges.

## Continuous Improvement and Future Outlook

As the cybersecurity landscape continues to evolve, Riverton remains committed to advancing its security measures and frameworks. Continuous improvement initiatives ensure that our cybersecurity posture adapts to new challenges and threats. Ongoing investments in technology, personnel, and processes are vital to maintaining our resilience and protective capabilities.

Looking forward, Riverton is committed to leveraging emerging technologies, such as machine learning and quantum computing, to enhance security responses and counteract sophisticated cyber threats. By fostering a culture of innovation and vigilance, the Company aims to maintain its leadership in cybersecurity within the Aerospace & Defense industry, safeguarding its operations and stakeholders as we advance our mission.

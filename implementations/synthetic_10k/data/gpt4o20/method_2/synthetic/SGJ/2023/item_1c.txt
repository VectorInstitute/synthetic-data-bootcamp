**Item 1C. Cybersecurity**

### Cybersecurity Overview

At Northbridge Robotics Holdings, Inc. ("Northbridge" or "the Company"), we recognize that robust cybersecurity measures are integral to protecting our assets, as well as safeguarding the sensitive health care data and personal information that our systems handle. Given our pivotal role in the health care services sub-industry, which involves the deployment of advanced robotic systems within clinical settings, we are committed to utilizing comprehensive cybersecurity protocols to protect against increasing cyber threats.

### Cybersecurity Framework

Northbridge employs the ISO/IEC 27001 framework as the cornerstone of our cybersecurity strategy. This internationally recognized standard for information security management systems (ISMS) guides our efforts to identify, prevent, and manage information security risks. Our adherence to this framework demonstrates our proactive approach in continually assessing and enhancing our security posture to comply with best practices and industry standards.

### Board Oversight and Governance

Northbridge's cybersecurity governance framework is overseen by the full Board of Directors and supported by the Audit Committee. This structured oversight ensures that our cybersecurity strategies are robust, up-to-date, and aligned with our organizational objectives. The Board, alongside the Audit Committee, reviews our cybersecurity policies and procedures regularly to ensure that they address evolving cyber threats effectively and that necessary resources are allocated towards sustaining and advancing our cybersecurity initiatives.

### Risk Assessment and Management

As a company deeply integrated into the health care sector, we acknowledge the inherent cybersecurity risks associated with processing and storing health-related data. To mitigate these risks, we conduct regular risk assessments employing advanced analytical tools to identify potential vulnerabilities within our systems. These assessments enable us to implement targeted security measures, strengthening our defenses against cyber attacks while ensuring compliance with regulatory mandates.

### Cybersecurity Measures

Northbridge has established a multi-layered security architecture designed to protect our information systems and data assets. Key components of this architecture include:

- **Network Security:** Implementation of firewalls, intrusion detection/prevention systems (IDPS), and virtual private networks (VPNs) to control and monitor data traffic and prevent unauthorized access.
  
- **Data Encryption:** Utilization of encryption protocols for data both in transit and at rest, particularly for health care data, to maintain confidentiality and integrity.

- **Access Controls:** Strong authentication mechanisms and role-based access controls restrict data access to authorized personnel, thereby reducing insider threats.

- **Incident Response:** A dedicated team manages the incident response protocol, prepared to execute rapid responses to contain and mitigate security breaches while minimizing damage to our operations and reputation.

### Employee Training and Awareness

We recognize that employees are vital to our cybersecurity defenses. Our comprehensive training program ensures that all personnel are aware of cybersecurity risks and are well-equipped to respond to potential threats. Regular workshops, simulations, and updates on emerging cybersecurity challenges are standard practices to maintain a high level of awareness across the organization.

### Third-Party Risk Management

Our commitment to cybersecurity extends beyond internal operations. We recognize the significance of securing our supply chain and partnering with vendors who share our cybersecurity standards. Our third-party risk management process involves rigorous assessments and audits of vendors' cybersecurity measures, ensuring they align with our requirements before any engagement.

### Reporting and Disclosure

Northbridge is committed to transparency in cybersecurity practices. Although we have not disclosed any cybersecurity incidents in the fiscal year 2023, our policy mandates the thorough investigation and documentation of any cyber events, should they occur. This policy is part of a broader commitment to maintaining stakeholder trust by ensuring openness and accountability in handling cybersecurity-related matters.

### Continuous Improvement and Future Outlook

Cybersecurity threats are constantly evolving; thus, Northbridge remains dedicated to improving our cybersecurity posture. In line with our focus on fostering innovation, we continually invest in advancing our cybersecurity technologies and protocols. Future initiatives include the integration of artificial intelligence and machine learning in our threat detection systems, providing enhanced real-time monitoring and analytic capabilities to preemptively identify and respond to threats.

Northbridge Robotics Holdings, Inc. remains steadfast in our mission to lead the health care services industry not only through technological innovation in robotics but also by setting a benchmark in robust cybersecurity practices. Our continuous dedication to security enhancements ensures that we are well-prepared to protect both our operations and the sensitive data entrusted to us, maintaining our reputation as a responsible and forward-thinking leader in the industry.

**Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations**

### Overview

Northbridge Robotics Holdings, Inc. ("Northbridge" or the "Company") delivered a robust performance for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2023, underpinned by strategic initiatives aimed at advancing our position in the health care services sub-industry. As an innovator in integrating robotics into health care, our focus remains on enhancing patient outcomes and operational efficiency through cutting-edge technology. The following discussion provides an analysis of our financial condition, operating results, and cash flow activities for the fiscal year, highlighting key factors influencing our performance and the strategies implemented to maintain our growth trajectory.

### Financial Performance Summary

For the fiscal year 2023, Northbridge recorded total revenues of $258.4 billion, reflecting our ongoing commitment to delivering value-driven solutions in the health care sector. Our ability to adapt and innovate within a dynamic market environment has been crucial to these results. Net income for the year was $5.39 billion, a testament to our effective cost management and strategic growth initiatives.

### Results of Operations

#### Revenues

Total revenues for 2023 reached $258,399,461,658, driven by increased adoption of our robotic systems across diverse health care settings. The surge in demand for surgical and patient assistance robots contributed significantly to our top-line growth. We observed substantial uptake in the adoption of robotic process automation (RPA) tools, which reflects the increasing need for streamlined administrative operations in health care facilities.

#### Operating Expenses

Operating expenses totaled $248,345,188,125, comprising cost of revenue at $164,872,574,156 and other operating expenses at $83,472,613,969. The cost of revenue represents expenses directly tied to the production and delivery of our robotic solutions, which included material costs, manufacturing overhead, and direct labor. Our operational focus was on optimizing supply chain efficiencies and controlling costs without sacrificing quality or innovation.

#### Operating Income

Our operating income was $10,054,273,533, highlighting our efficiency in managing operating expenses relative to revenue growth. This performance indicator reflects the success of our strategic initiatives aimed at cost optimization and process improvement.

#### Interest Expense and Net Income

Interest expenses for the fiscal year totaled $1,804,524,678, reflective of our ongoing efforts to manage debt effectively. Pretax income amounted to $8,249,748,855, with an income tax expense of $2,862,303,618, resulting in a net income of $5,387,445,237.

### Liquidity and Capital Resources

Our liquidity position remains strong with cash and equivalents at the year-end totaling $6,079,925,037. However, cash flow from investing and financing activities indicated a net decrease in cash, primarily due to significant investments in R&D and strategic financing activities.

#### Operating Activities

Net cash provided by operating activities was $11,146,788,099. This outcome is anchored in our robust net income and efficient management of working capital, including positive shifts in accounts receivable and inventories.

#### Investing Activities

Net cash used in investing activities was $(17,332,188,965), attributed to continued capital investment in R&D and infrastructure enhancements, ensuring our technological edge in health care robotics.

#### Financing Activities

Financing activities contributed $1,689,688,734, mainly driven by proactive engagements in capital markets, despite dividend distributions and share repurchases.

### Financial Position

As of December 31, 2023, Northbridge maintained a solid balance sheet with total assets of $178,846,953,758. Total liabilities stood at $116,537,187,910, supported by a total stockholders' equity of $62,309,765,848, reinforcing our financial stability and resilience.

### Strategic Initiatives

Our strategic focus remains on innovation, market expansion, and partnership cultivation. Key initiatives included enhancing our R&D capabilities, building alliances with health care institutions, and expanding into emerging geographic markets to capitalize on the growing demand for health care robotics.

### Outlook

Looking ahead, Northbridge is committed to driving growth through continued innovation and the expansion of our robotic solutions portfolio. We expect to harness opportunities in telemedicine and home health care, capitalizing on our market expertise and technological advancements to widen our impact. Our strategy includes continuous investment in research and development while maintaining operational efficiency to sustain long-term growth and profitability.

### Conclusion

The fiscal year 2023 marked significant milestones for Northbridge Robotics Holdings, Inc., as we continued to set benchmarks in integrating robotics into health care services. Our financial discipline, strategic execution, and innovation-driven approach have positioned us well for sustainable growth, and we remain focused on delivering long-term shareholder value.

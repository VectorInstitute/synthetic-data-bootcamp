**Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations**

This section provides a narrative interpretation of the results of operations and the financial condition of Ironwood Resources Corp. ("Ironwood," "the Company," "we," "our," or "us"). It is intended to offer insights into our financial performance over the fiscal year 2023, highlighting the dynamics in revenue streams, expenditures, and cash flow changes. This analysis should be read in conjunction with the consolidated financial statements and notes.

**Overview of Fiscal Year 2023**

During fiscal year 2023, Ironwood Resources Corp. demonstrated resilience and growth, cementing its position as a leader in the Asset Management & Custody Banks sub-industry. Headquartered in Illinois, our Company generated revenues totaling approximately $8.04 billion. Our financial results reflect robust operational execution amid a challenging macroeconomic environment characterized by volatile interest rates and market fluctuations.

**Financial Performance Highlights**

1. **Revenue Analysis**  
   Ironwood Resources Corp. reported total revenues of $8,038,599,569. This revenue was primarily driven by our strong performance in the Asset Management segment, where demand for customized investment solutions and client-focused advisory services remained strong. The Custody and Fund Services segment also contributed significantly, highlighting our capacity to deliver value-added services for institutional clients seeking robust asset safeguarding and administration services.

2. **Operating Expenses**  
   While we sustained our revenue growth, controlling operating expenses was pivotal. The major components of our operating expenses include personnel costs, technology investments, and regulatory compliance expenses. Strategic investments in technology facilitated our operational efficiency improvements. These investments are aligned with our commitment to providing innovative digital products and enhancing client experiences.

3. **Net Income**  
   Our reported net income for 2023 stood at $2,299,536,868, signaling strong bottom-line performance. This figure accounts for an interest expense of $4,823,159,741, underscoring our ability to manage debt obligations effectively while enhancing profitability.

4. **Income Tax Expense**  
   Our income tax expense for the year amounted to $915,902,960, reflecting an effective tax strategy in line with prevailing tax legislation and our proactive tax planning measures.

**Balance Sheet Analysis**

1. **Asset Composition**  
   Our total assets at the fiscal year-end were valued at $160,771,991,387. Key components include cash and equivalents at $5,787,758,603, and goodwill at $707,192,941, evidencing continued growth and strategic acquisitions that enhance our market position.

2. **Liabilities and Stockholders' Equity**  
   Total liabilities amounted to $146,654,991,211, primarily driven by other liabilities. Our total stockholders' equity was robust at $14,117,000,176, showcasing a well-capitalized structure that supports our growth initiatives.

**Cash Flow Analysis**

1. **Operating Activities**  
   Ironwood generated $4,494,443,551 in cash from operating activities. This figure signifies our efficient operational core, driven by a strong net income and substantive cash flow enhancements from depreciation and working capital changes.

2. **Investing Activities**  
   Cash provided by investing activities totaled $9,040,614,656, primarily fueled by proceeds from other investing activities, offset by capital expenditures of $198,384,173. These investments demonstrate a strategic focus on growth and capability expansion.

3. **Financing Activities**  
   Cash used in financing activities was $14,817,025,073, impacted by share repurchases and significant cash deployments in other financing activities. Careful management of financial resources is pivotal in maintaining shareholder value and supporting future capital requirements.

**Liquidity and Capital Resources**

Ironwood continues to maintain a strong liquidity position, supported by an ample cash-equivalent reserve. Our capital resources are positioned to support ongoing investment, strategic acquisition opportunities, and shareholder value initiatives. We foresee no liquidity constraints impacting our operational capabilities or strategic pursuits.

**Strategic Outlook**

The fiscal year 2023 illustrated our adaptability within a fluctuating market environment. Leveraging traditional strengths in asset management while investing in technology positions Ironwood for continued growth. Our strategic focus remains on innovation, client-centric services, and operational efficiency to capitalize on market opportunities and navigate potential economic headwinds effectively.

**Item 1A. Risk Factors**

Our business, financial condition, and results of operations are subject to various risks and uncertainties. Discussed below are the significant risk factors that could have a material adverse effect on our business. These risk factors should be read in conjunction with the other information contained in this report, including our consolidated financial statements and related notes.

**Market Risks**

1. **Economic and Market Conditions**  
   Ironwood Resources Corp. is significantly influenced by macroeconomic conditions, such as interest rates, inflation, and the performance of financial markets. Economic downturns, fluctuating interest rates, and volatility in financial markets can adversely affect our asset management revenues and the value of assets under custody, leading to decreased client activity and diminished fee income.

2. **Regulatory Changes**  
   As part of the financial sector, our operations must comply with a stringent regulatory environment. Changes in laws or regulations, or increased enforcement actions, could necessitate costly adjustments to our operations, affect the types of products and services we offer, and potentially lead to sanctions or fines, impacting our financial performance.

**Operational Risks**

3. **Cybersecurity and Data Privacy**  
   Our reliance on technology exposes Ironwood to risks related to cybersecurity threats and data breaches, which could compromise sensitive client information and disrupt our operations. Failures in our technology infrastructure to prevent and respond to cyber-attacks could result in financial loss, reputational damage, and legal liability.

4. **Operational Efficiency and Technological Integration**  
   Inadequacies in our operational processes and technology integration can result in inefficiencies, increased costs, and service disruptions. Maintaining and upgrading technological infrastructure is essential for operational efficiency and client satisfaction. Failure to effectively implement technology or manage operational processes could adversely impact our growth and competitive edge.

**Client-Related Risks**

5. **Client Preferences and Retention**  
   The financial needs and preferences of our clients can shift rapidly. A failure to adapt to changing client preferences, deliver innovative solutions, or provide superior client service can lead to client dissatisfaction and attrition. Losing a significant client or group of clients can materially impact our revenue base and reputation.

6. **Reputation and Brand Management**  
   Our reputation is a fundamental component of our success. Negative publicity, whether true or not, can severely damage our brand, resulting in diminished client trust and business opportunities. Maintaining a strong reputation requires continuous focus on service quality, ethical practices, and stakeholder relations.

**Financial Risks**

7. **Asset Impairment**  
   Our balance sheet reflects significant tangible and intangible assets. Fluctuations in market conditions or operational challenges can lead to impairments, negatively impacting our financial statements. An impairment of assets could reduce our total assets and stockholders' equity, affecting financial ratios and investor perception.

8. **Credit Risks**  
   As an entity operating largely in asset management and custody banks, we are exposed to credit risks from counterparties and clients, especially regarding non-performing assets or defaults. A significant decline in asset quality or rise in default rates can impair our financial results and limit our lending and investing capabilities.

**Competitive Risks**

9. **Intense Industry Competition**  
   We operate in an intensely competitive industry with numerous established players and new entrants. Our ability to sustain and grow market share relies on offering superior products and services and addressing client needs effectively. Failure to remain competitive could adversely affect our market position and earnings.

**Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) Risks**

10. **Sustainability and ESG Practices**  
    Increasing focus on environmental, social, and governance issues presents risks if Ironwood fails to align our practices with stakeholder expectations. Non-compliance or ineffectiveness in addressing these concerns might lead to reputational and financial setbacks, impacting long-term shareholder value.

**Conclusion**

While the above risks represent significant challenges, Ironwood Resources Corp. is dedicated to implementing comprehensive risk management strategies to mitigate potential impacts. We recognize that timely identification and management of risks are crucial for sustaining our operational resilience and delivering lasting value to our clients and shareholders.

**Item 1C. Cybersecurity**

**Overview**

Ironwood Resources Corp. ("Ironwood," "the Company," "we," "our," or "us") recognizes the critical importance of cybersecurity in securing our assets and maintaining the trust of our clients. As a leader in the financial sector, specifically within the Asset Management & Custody Banks sub-industry, our operations require the protection of sensitive financial data and client information. Cybersecurity is integral to our business operations and is aligned with our mission to deliver secure and innovative financial solutions. Our cybersecurity framework is grounded in the principles of risk management, continuous monitoring, and proactive threat mitigation.

**Cybersecurity Framework**

In securing our operational and client data, Ironwood adheres to widely recognized standards including the NIST Cybersecurity Framework and ISO/IEC 27001. This multi-layered strategy ensures comprehensive coverage of all potential threat vectors, including threats related to emerging technologies and evolving cyber attack tactics. Our cybersecurity approach is dynamically designed to adapt to the rapidly changing threat landscape and ensures the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of our data.

**Governance and Oversight**

Cybersecurity oversight at Ironwood is under the direct supervision of the Audit Committee of the Board of Directors. The committee is tasked with assessing our cybersecurity policies and strategies, ensuring they are not only robust but also aligned with the strategic goals of the Company. Regular updates and presentations on cybersecurity initiatives are made to the Board, enhancing transparency and enabling informed decision-making related to security investments and incident responses.

**Incident Management**

During fiscal year 2023, Ironwood disclosed a cybersecurity incident involving a phishing-related account compromise, which affected a limited number of employee accounts. Thanks to our preemptive risk management and response protocols, the incident was swiftly contained with no material impact on our operations or client data. Our incident response strategy emphasizes quick identification, isolation, and remediation measures, supported by forensic analysis to prevent recurrence and enhance our threat detection capabilities.

**Risk Assessment and Mitigation**

Our risk assessment process involves a comprehensive identification and evaluation of potential cybersecurity threats across our technology ecosystem. Regularly conducted by our dedicated cybersecurity team, these assessments guide the prioritization of risks and the development of targeted mitigation strategies. We employ advanced tools and techniques, including machine learning and artificial intelligence, to monitor network activity and identify anomalies indicative of potential threats.

**Employee Training and Awareness**

At Ironwood, cybersecurity is a shared responsibility. We have implemented rigorous training programs that educate employees about cybersecurity best practices, phishing awareness, and data protection policies. Mandatory training sessions and simulated phishing exercises enhance our workforce's readiness to identify and mitigate potential threats.

**Third-Party Risk Management**

Given the interconnected nature of the financial industry, Ironwood places significant emphasis on the cybersecurity practices of third-party vendors and partners. Our vendor management policy includes stringent requirements for cybersecurity compliance, regular audits, and collaborative incident response planning. This proactive stance minimizes risks arising from external entities and ensures that our cybersecurity standards extend beyond our direct operations.

**Technology Investments**

Ironwood’s commitment to cybersecurity is reflected in our ongoing investment in cutting-edge security technologies. These investments are directed towards enhancing our threat intelligence capabilities, secure data handling, and implementing strong authentication measures. We continuously evaluate our technology stack to ensure that it is robust against current threats and scalable to address future challenges.

**Data Privacy**

Data privacy goes hand-in-hand with cybersecurity at Ironwood, underscoring our commitment to protecting client information. We adhere to applicable data privacy laws and regulations, embedding privacy controls within our cybersecurity framework. Regular audits and assessments ensure compliance and reinforce our reputation as a trusted custodian of client data.

**Industry Collaboration**

Ironwood engages in active collaboration with industry peers, governmental agencies, and cybersecurity experts to share insights and intelligence on emerging threats and best practices. Participation in financial sector cybersecurity forums and information-sharing platforms strengthens our threat intelligence and provides valuable resources to enhance our defense strategies.

**Conclusion**

Ironwood Resources Corp. remains vigilant in the face of ever-evolving cybersecurity threats. By adhering to established frameworks, investing in advanced security technologies, and fostering a culture of security awareness, we are well-positioned to protect our clients and maintain the integrity of our operations. Our focus on continuous improvement and strategic resilience ensures that our cybersecurity posture supports our business objectives and sustains stakeholder trust.

As the cyber threat landscape continues to evolve, Ironwood is dedicated to adapting our cybersecurity measures to protect the interests of all stakeholders, ensuring the long-term security and success of our enterprise.

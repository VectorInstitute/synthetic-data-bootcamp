### Supplementary Data

#### Notes to the Consolidated Financial Statements

**1. Basis of Presentation**  
The consolidated financial statements include the accounts of Ironwood Resources Corp. and its subsidiaries. All significant intercompany accounts and transactions have been eliminated in consolidation.

**2. Summary of Significant Accounting Policies**  
Ironwood applies its significant accounting policies consistently in the preparation and presentation of these financial statements, adhering to U.S. GAAP, recognizing revenues when earned, and recording expenses when incurred.

**3. Revenue Recognition**  
Revenue is recognized through various streams correlating to our business segments, primarily derived from asset management fees, custody services, and financial advisory offerings. Revenues are presented net of rebates and allowances.

**4. Goodwill**  
Goodwill represents the excess of purchase price over the fair value of net identifiable assets acquired and identified liabilities. It is tested annually for impairment, with no impairment recognized in the fiscal year 2023.

**5. Stockholders’ Equity**  
Changes in equity components reflect adjustments from comprehensive income elements, net income additions, and stock repurchase activities. Our equity structure underpins our capital resource framework and strategic flexibility.

**6. Income Taxes**  
The Company is subject to federal and state income taxes based in Illinois. Our effective tax rate benefits from strategic tax planning and compliance with prevailing tax provisions, with deferred taxes arising from timing differences.

**Conclusion**

The consolidated financial statements of Ironwood Resources Corp. capture our robust fiscal performance and stability throughout the fiscal year 2023. Our results underscore our strategic focus on leveraging financial strength to support innovation, client-centric services, and sustainable growth, ensuring strategic resilience in a dynamic market environment.

ITEM 1C. CYBERSECURITY

Overview and Strategy  
At Fieldstone Solutions Holdings, Inc., the safeguarding of our digital landscape is of paramount importance. In an era where cybersecurity threats are increasingly sophisticated and pervasive, our approach is designed to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of our critical systems and information. Although we have not disclosed any incidents, we continuously align our cybersecurity measures with recognized frameworks like the NIST Cybersecurity Framework to stay ahead of emerging threats.

We integrate cybersecurity risk management into our overall enterprise risk management efforts, acknowledging it as a critical component of our strategic operations. Our cybersecurity program encompasses comprehensive measures for identifying, assessing, and managing potential risks to ensure robust defense mechanisms are in place. 

Our cybersecurity strategy consists of multiple layers, focusing on prevention, detection, and response capabilities to mitigate potential cyber threats effectively. These efforts are spearheaded by our dedicated team of cybersecurity professionals within our Global Security Group (GSG).

Key Elements of Our Cybersecurity Program

1. Holistic Risk Management  
Our risk management framework is designed to address unresolved and emerging cybersecurity threats. Continuous evaluations and updates of our security processes are essential as the cyber threat landscape evolves. The implementation of proactive risk assessment processes ensures that we can identify potential security risks to our IT environments, products, services, and broader operations promptly.

2. Employee and Contractor Training  
Understanding that human error is often a vulnerability, we mandate annual cybersecurity and data privacy training for all employees and contractors. Newly onboarded team members must also undergo this crucial training to heighten awareness and foster a security-conscious culture throughout the organization.

3. External Engagement and Validation  
Independent assessments conducted by external service providers offer an unbiased review of our cybersecurity practices. These external partners engage in activities such as penetration testing, threat analysis, incident response optimization, and advisory roles to further fortify our defenses and enhance our awareness.

4. Vendor and Third-Party Risk Management  
Our operations rely on numerous third-party service providers and vendors. To mitigate associated risks, we have a stringent vendor risk assessment process. This ensures that partners adhere to industry standards and meet our contractual cybersecurity requirements with proactive incident management and notification protocols.

5. Incident Simulation Exercises  
We conduct regular tabletop exercises simulating potential threat scenarios to validate our response strategies and reinforce our incident management readiness. These rehearsals help refine our practices and improve collaboration among stakeholders at all levels.

Cybersecurity Governance

Our governance structure is comprehensive, ensuring meticulous oversight of our cybersecurity strategies. The Fieldstone Solutions Holdings, Inc. Board of Directors is actively involved in supervising our cybersecurity posture. Working closely with the Audit Committee, they ensure that all cybersecurity-related activities align with our business goals and regulatory requirements.

1. Role of the Board and Audit Committee  
Both the full Board of Directors and the Audit Committee maintain oversight of our cybersecurity management processes. The Audit Committee meets quarterly—or as necessary—to review updates provided by our executive leadership team, including our Chief Information Officer (CIO) and Chief Information Security Officer (CISO). 

2. Executive Leadership  
Our CIO and CISO are crucial to steering our cybersecurity framework. With over 25 years of combined experience in global IT operations and cybersecurity, they bring deep expertise in deploying strategies that advance the maturity of our cybersecurity defenses. The CIO oversees the Global Security Group and is a direct report to the CEO, ensuring executive-level insight and accountability.

3. Incident Reporting Protocols  
Effective channels are in place to escalate incidents, enabling swift reporting and coordination. The Audit Committee and Board receive regular updates on our cybersecurity initiatives, trends, and any changes in risk exposure, providing them with the data necessary to make informed decisions about overall corporate security postures.

Conclusion and Forward-Looking Views

Fieldstone Solutions Holdings, Inc. is committed to advancing our cybersecurity maturity. We continue to invest in robust infrastructures, cutting-edge technologies, and skilled personnel to protect our digital assets. While we maintain rigorous preventive measures, we understand that no system is invulnerable. Thus, we approach cybersecurity with vigilance, constantly striving for enhancement in our processes.

As the landscape evolves, we remain proactive, ensuring that our cybersecurity and business strategies adapt to meet the demands of ever-changing technological environments. For additional insights on our cybersecurity-related risks, kindly refer to Item 1A. Risk Factors in this Annual Report on Form 10-K.

Our pledge is to maintain trust through transparency, resilience, and an unwavering commitment to securing our technological landscape.

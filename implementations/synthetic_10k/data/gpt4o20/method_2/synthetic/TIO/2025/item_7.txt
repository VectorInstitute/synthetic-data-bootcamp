ITEM 7. MANAGEMENT’S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS OF FINANCIAL CONDITION AND RESULTS OF OPERATIONS

This Management’s Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations (“MD&A”) has been prepared to provide an overview of Fieldstone Solutions Holdings, Inc.'s financial performance during our fiscal year ended December 31, 2025. The MD&A should be read in conjunction with our consolidated financial statements and accompanying notes, which form part of this Annual Report on Form 10-K. This discussion may include forward-looking statements based upon our current expectations, forecasts, and assumptions that are subject to risks and uncertainties. Actual results could differ materially from those anticipated in these forward-looking statements due to numerous factors, including those included under “Risk Factors.”

Overview

Fieldstone Solutions Holdings, Inc., headquartered in California within the Information Technology sector and specifically in the semiconductor sub-industry, is renowned for creating leading-edge semiconductor solutions. With a dynamic focus on designing and supplying high-performance digital and mixed-signal semiconductor products, we cater to an extensive array of applications including advanced data center infrastructures, telecommunications, and AI systems.

In fiscal year 2025, we reported revenues of $96,117.2 million, reflecting our robust market presence and commitment to innovation. Our strong financial performance aligns with our strategic initiatives to expand our product portfolio and secure strategic partnerships in high-growth markets such as AI and machine learning.

Our operational effectiveness is demonstrated through our substantial operating income of $35,016.7 million, reflecting efficient management of operating expenses and a commitment to delivering value. During fiscal year 2025, our operational accomplishments were underpinned by strategic investments in research and development that totaled $15,046.3 million.

Performance Analysis

Revenues

During fiscal year 2025, our revenue totaled $96,117.2 million, representing a strong affirmation of our product offerings' market relevance across our defined industry segments. This revenue growth is primarily attributed to our expanding footprint in data center and AI applications, tapping into the growing demand for high-efficiency computing solutions. Our revenue base benefited from a strategic focus on AI-driven companies and hyperscale data center operators, ensuring a broad and diversified customer base.

Operating Expenses

Our operating expenses for fiscal year 2025 were effectively managed, totaling $61,168.3 million. This figure includes our substantial investment in research and development, where $15,046.3 million was directed towards bolstering our technological leadership and introducing innovative products. Selling, general, and administrative expenses accounted for $6,748.9 million, signifying prudent management of our corporate resources. Other operating expenses amounted to $7,348.9 million, ensuring our operational infrastructure supports sustained business growth.

Net Income

Our net income for fiscal year 2025 was particularly strong, reaching $38,197.1 million. This signifies a remarkable operational capability and successful execution of our business strategies. Notably, our net income was positively influenced by a tax benefit situation, as demonstrated by an income tax expense credit of $3,180.4 million, enhancing our effective tax management strategies.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

Fieldstone Solutions Holdings, Inc. maintains a robust liquidity position. At the end of 2025, our cash and cash equivalents stood at $22,909.0 million, significant enough to support future strategic initiatives and buffer against potential market volatility. Our cash flow from operating activities was particularly strong at $46,466.9 million. This substantial cash inflow underscores the underlying strength of our core business operations and our ability to generate substantial returns for our stakeholders.

During the fiscal year, our cash flows from investing activities showed an outflow of $963.2 million, largely influenced by capital expenditures directed towards upgrading our infrastructure to support future growth. Additionally, in our financing activities, we returned $15,576.1 million through dividends and undertook share repurchases totaling $3,673.3 million, reflecting our commitment to returning value to shareholders.

Financial Position

As of fiscal year-end 2025, our balance sheet remains strong with total assets of $231,579.4 million, bolstered by goodwill at $138,866.0 million, reflecting our strategic acquisitions that have enhanced our market and technological position. Our total liabilities amount to $123,732.6 million, with accounts payable of $1,879.9 million displaying our efficient supply chain and payment procedures.

Our stockholders' equity of $107,846.8 million signifies a resilient economic foundation, underscoring our financial stability and providing a robust platform for future growth initiatives.

Outlook

Looking ahead, Fieldstone Solutions Holdings, Inc. remains committed to fostering sustainable growth through continuous innovation and customer engagement. We anticipate leveraging our advanced semiconductor solutions to seize opportunities in burgeoning markets, such as AI, IoT, and 5G. Our strategy will consistently focus on driving technological evolution and expanding our global market presence.

In closing, we maintain a vigilant focus on operational excellence, financial discipline, and strategic investments to reinforce our industry leadership and deliver consistent value to our stakeholders.

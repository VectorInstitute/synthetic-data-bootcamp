**Supplementary Data**

**Critical Accounting Policies and Estimates:**  
The preparation of our consolidated financial statements in conformity with GAAP requires management to make estimates and assumptions that affect the reported amounts of assets, liabilities, revenues, and expenses. Our critical accounting estimates relate to revenue recognition, the valuation of goodwill and intangible assets, income taxes, stock-based compensation, inventory valuation, and accounting for business combinations.

**Revenue Recognition:**  
Revenue is recognized when obligations under the terms of a contract with our customers are satisfied. Generally, this satisfaction occurs when we transfer control over a product to the customer, which can be at a point in time or over time, depending on the nature of the arrangement.

**Goodwill and Intangible Assets:**  
Goodwill arising from acquisitions represents the excess of the purchase price over the fair value of net identifiable assets acquired. It is not amortized but is assessed annually for impairment. Intangible assets are amortized over their useful lives and also evaluated for impairment when events or changes in circumstances indicate that their carrying amounts may not be recoverable.

**Income Taxes:**  
Our provision for income taxes is driven by statutory tax rates in the jurisdictions in which we operate and is affected by certain permanent and temporary differences between financial reporting and tax bases of assets and liabilities. Deferred tax assets and liabilities are recognized for the expected future tax consequences of events that have been included in the financial statement or tax returns.

**Commitments and Contingencies:**  
We are subject to claims and assessments arising in the ordinary course of our business, including challenges to our intellectual property, general liability, and employment matters. We believe that adequate provisions have been made for such claims and assessments, where appropriate, based on information available to us.

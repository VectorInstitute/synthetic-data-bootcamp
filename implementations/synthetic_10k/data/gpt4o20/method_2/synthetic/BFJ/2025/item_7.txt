**Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations**

**Overview**

Stonegate Systems Holdings, Inc. ("Stonegate" or "the Company"), a cornerstone in the financial sector's multi-line insurance sub-industry, provides diversified insurance solutions from its headquarters in New York. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025, Stonegate generated revenues totaling $18.3 billion, reinforcing its strong market position amidst an increasingly competitive environment. 

**Executive Summary**

The following discussion provides detailed insights into Stonegate's financial condition, operating results, and cash flows. Our focus is on understanding the impact of significant financial metrics, evaluating operational strategies, and assessing future perspectives in a dynamic insurance landscape.

**Financial Condition**

Stonegate's financial position remains robust, with total assets of $125.3 billion at fiscal year-end, a slight increase indicative of strategic asset management. The balance sheet's composition reflects substantial investments in goodwill and other assets totaling $23.54 billion and $120.48 billion, respectively, signifying our strategic acquisitions and investments aimed at enhancing our service offerings.

**Assets and Liabilities**

The Company maintains a rigorous balance between cash preservation and strategic investments. Cash and equivalents stand at approximately $1.14 billion, ensuring liquidity for operational needs and potential growth opportunities. Long-term investments in property, plant, and equipment, valued at $1.13 billion, align with our commitment to sustaining infrastructure and technological capabilities.

Our liabilities amount to $92.3 billion, including $6.4 billion in long-term debt. Stonegate's priority remains optimizing debt levels, ensuring maintainable leverage while supporting capital needs. Other liabilities, primarily constituted by insurance contract obligations and related activities, reflect our core operational commitments and risk management strategies.

**Equity Position**

Total stockholders' equity at year-end amounts to $32.9 billion. Our equity structure includes substantial additional paid-in capital of $66.6 billion, with retained earnings of $27.5 billion, emphasizing sustained profitability and shareholder reinvestment. Notably, our accumulated other comprehensive income presents a negative balance, primarily due to market volatility's effect on investments.

**Results of Operations**

For the fiscal year 2025, Stonegate exhibited a robust operating performance, highlighted by net income of $2.16 billion, representing an effective tax strategy and operational efficiencies. Revenues increased due to strategic pricing, product diversification, and enhanced customer engagement.

**Operating Revenues and Expenses**

Total revenues for the year reached $18.3 billion. Diverse product offerings and strategic market penetration have been pivotal in maintaining revenue streams. Operating expenses, totaling $15.70 billion, include $3.04 billion in selling, general, and administrative expenses, and $12.25 billion in other operating expenses, reflecting our investment in human capital and technological infrastructure critical to supporting operations and customer service.

**Interest and Tax Expenses**

Interest expenses of $299.2 million reflect our debt management strategies, while our income tax expense totaled $574.4 million, indicative of effective tax planning reflecting the geographical diversification of our operations.

**Cash Flow Analysis**

Stonegate continues to generate strong cash flows from operations, amounting to $2.42 billion, driven by solid net income and efficient capital management strategies. Investing activities provided cash inflows of $2.21 billion, underlining the return on strategic asset sales and investment realignment.

**Financing Overview**

Cash used in financing activities totaled $5.11 billion, primarily due to share repurchases amounting to $4.36 billion, underscoring our commitment to enhancing shareholder value. Despite these outflows, our liquidity position, supported by substantial cash reserves and operational cash inflows, remains resilient.

**Liquidity and Capital Resources**

Managing liquidity and maintaining capital resources are central to Stonegate's financial strategy. Our strong balance sheet and access to diverse funding sources enhance our capacity to meet operational needs and pursue strategic initiatives. Although we experienced a net cash outflow of $482 million, our strategic reserves and cash flow from operations provide a stable foundation for future commitments.

**Future Prospects and Risks**

Looking forward, Stonegate is poised to capitalize on growth prospects through innovation, market expansion, and strategic partnerships. However, potential economic fluctuations, regulatory changes, and evolving competitive dynamics present ongoing challenges. Our vigilant risk management framework will remain integral in navigating these uncertainties.

**Conclusion**

Stonegate Systems Holdings, Inc. remains committed to sustaining its leadership in the financial and insurance markets. Through disciplined financial practices, strategic resource allocation, and a focus on innovation, we continue to strive for excellence in delivering value to our shareholders and clients. As we adapt to the evolving insurance landscape, our robust financial standing positions us well to seize future opportunities and tackle emerging challenges effectively.

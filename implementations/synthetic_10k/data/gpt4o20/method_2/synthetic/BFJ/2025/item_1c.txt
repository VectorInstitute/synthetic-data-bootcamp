**Item 1C. Cybersecurity**

**Overview**

Stonegate Systems Holdings, Inc. ("Stonegate" or "the Company") operates in the highly interconnected and digital financial sector, delivering multi-line insurance solutions. Given the nature of our business and the sensitive data we handle, cybersecurity is not only a technological priority but a fundamental component of our operational integrity and customer trust. As cyber threats evolve globally, we are committed to ensuring robust protection of our information systems and assets through comprehensive risk management and governance frameworks.

**Cybersecurity Framework and Strategy**

To fortify our cybersecurity posture, Stonegate adheres to the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) Cybersecurity Framework alongside the ISO/IEC 27001 standards. These established frameworks provide a flexible, performance-based approach to managing cybersecurity risks, enabling us to implement effective protective measures, respond to incidents swiftly, and recover from disruptions effectively.

Our cybersecurity strategy focuses on the following key areas:

1. **Risk Identification and Assessment**: Stonegate conducts regular assessments of its digital landscape to identify vulnerabilities and potential threats. We prioritize risks based on impact and probability, allocating resources efficiently to mitigate those priorities.

2. **Mitigation and Protection**: Proactive measures, including multi-layered security systems, encryption technologies, and access control protocols, are employed to safeguard sensitive data from unauthorized access and cyber threats. Systems are continuously monitored and updated to address emerging vulnerabilities.

3. **Detection and Response**: Our Security Operations Center (SOC) operates round-the-clock, utilizing advanced analytics and threat intelligence tools to identify suspicious activities. Immediate response protocols are in place to contain and investigate threats effectively, minimizing potential impact.

4. **Recovery and Continuity**: In the event of a cybersecurity incident, our recovery plans are designed to restore operations swiftly and securely. Regularly tested and updated, these plans ensure continuity of our services and the preservation of customer confidence.

**Governance and Oversight**

Stonegate places significant emphasis on cybersecurity governance, recognizing its critical role in protecting our business and client interests. Oversight of cybersecurity initiatives is provided by our full Board of Directors, with specialized guidance and support from the Audit Committee. This structure ensures high-level visibility and accountability, promoting a culture of security awareness and compliance.

The Audit Committee assists the Board in reviewing cybersecurity policies, risk management strategies, and incident response plans, ensuring alignment with organizational goals and regulatory requirements. Regular reports from the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) and internal audits provide the Board and Committee with insights into the effectiveness of cybersecurity measures and areas for improvement.

**Employee Awareness and Training**

Our employees play a crucial role in our cybersecurity ecosystem. Stonegate implements comprehensive training and awareness programs to equip staff with the knowledge and skills necessary to recognize and mitigate cyber threats. Annual training sessions, phishing simulations, and regular security updates are conducted to reinforce a culture of vigilance and responsibility across the organization.

**Third-Party and Supply Chain Security**

Recognizing the potential risks associated with third-party partnerships, Stonegate rigorously evaluates the cybersecurity practices of our vendors and partners. Risk assessments and contractual agreements with clear cybersecurity requirements are employed to ensure that third-party operations align with our security standards and reduce supply chain vulnerabilities.

**Incident Reporting and Transparency**

Stonegate has established transparent incident reporting protocols to ensure timely disclosure of relevant information. While the Company has not disclosed any significant cybersecurity incidents in the fiscal year 2025, we maintain readiness to communicate promptly with affected stakeholders should an incident occur, in compliance with regulatory requirements and best practice guidelines.

**Continuous Improvement and Innovation**

Our commitment to cybersecurity is ongoing. Stonegate continuously seeks to enhance its cybersecurity capabilities by monitoring industry trends, adopting innovative technologies, and participating in industry forums and collaborations. These efforts enable us to anticipate changes in the cybersecurity landscape and adjust our strategies accordingly.

**Conclusion**

In the digital age, maintaining a resilient cybersecurity infrastructure is paramount. At Stonegate Systems Holdings, Inc., we are dedicated to preserving the integrity of our systems, protecting the sensitive data entrusted to us by our clients, and upholding our reputation as a trusted leader in the financial sector. Through strategic investment, proactive risk management, and organizational commitment, Stonegate is well-equipped to address the cybersecurity challenges of today and the future.

**Overview**

The following financial statements and supplementary data for Stonegate Systems Holdings, Inc. ("Stonegate" or "the Company") provide a comprehensive view of the Company's financial position, operating results, and cash flows for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025. These statements have been prepared in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP) and audited by our independent registered public accounting firm to ensure accuracy and transparency for our stakeholders.

**Consolidated Balance Sheets**

As of December 31, 2025, Stonegate reported total assets of $125.3 billion. The asset composition reflects strategic investments and sound financial practices. Key components include:

- **Cash and Equivalents**: $1.14 billion, providing necessary liquidity and flexibility for operational needs.
- **Property, Plant, and Equipment, Net**: Valued at $1.13 billion, underpinning our operational capabilities and infrastructure.
- **Goodwill**: $2.54 billion, representing the value attributed to our strategic acquisitions that enhance service offerings.
- **Other Assets**: $120.48 billion, consisting primarily of diverse investments and insurance-related assets.

The total liabilities reported were $92.3 billion, including:

- **Long-Term Debt**: $6.4 billion, reflecting strategic leverage aligned with capital needs.
- **Other Liabilities**: $85.9 billion, comprising insurance contract obligations and other operationally significant liabilities.

Stockholders' equity stood at $32.9 billion, highlighting our solid financial foundation:

- **Additional Paid-In Capital**: $66.6 billion, underscoring strong investor support.
- **Retained Earnings**: $27.5 billion, reflecting sustained profitability and a commitment to reinvestment.
- **Accumulated Other Comprehensive Income**: A negative balance of $3.4 billion, showing the impact of market volatility.

**Consolidated Income Statements**

Stonegate achieved a robust financial performance in 2025, with total revenues of $18.3 billion. This was facilitated by strategic pricing, product diversification, and enhanced customer engagement. Key highlights include:

- **Net Income**: $2.16 billion, demonstrating effective tax planning and operational efficiencies.
- **Operating Expenses**: Totaling $15.70 billion, which includes $3.04 billion in selling, general, and administrative expenses, and $12.25 billion in other operating expenses.

Interest expense amounted to $299.2 million, and income tax expense was $574.4 million. These figures reflect our prudent financial management and strategic corporate tax strategies.

**Consolidated Statements of Cash Flows**

For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025, Stonegate's cash flow from operations was strong at $2.42 billion, driven by solid net income and efficient capital management. Cash flows from investing activities generated $2.21 billion, primarily from strategic asset realignments. Conversely, financing activities used $5.11 billion, with significant cash outflows for share repurchases totaling $4.36 billion, emphasizing our focus on enhancing shareholder value.

The net change in cash reflected an outflow of $482 million, attributable to the substantial investments in share repurchases and other financing activities. Despite this outflow, Stonegate's liquidity remains robust, supported by operating cash flows and substantial cash reserves.

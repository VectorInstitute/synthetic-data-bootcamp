**Item 1C. Cybersecurity**

**Introduction**

Clearwater Technologies Co. ("Clearwater" or "the Company") recognizes that in the increasingly digital landscape of the financial services and insurance sectors, the ability to manage cybersecurity threats is vital to maintaining stakeholder trust. As a leading provider in the multi-line insurance industry, the Company is committed to protecting sensitive data, maintaining the integrity of its systems, and ensuring seamless service delivery to clients. This section outlines our approach to cybersecurity, including oversight mechanisms, risk management strategies, and incident response protocols.

**Governance and Oversight**

Clearwater has established a robust governance framework to manage cybersecurity risks, adhering to industry best practices and regulatory requirements. The Company's cybersecurity strategy is guided by the principles of the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) Cybersecurity Framework. This comprehensive framework provides a structured approach to managing cybersecurity risks, focusing on five key functions: Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, and Recover.

Oversight of Clearwater's cybersecurity initiatives is the responsibility of the Technology and Cybersecurity Committee of the Board. This dedicated committee provides strategic direction, oversight, and evaluation of cybersecurity policies and practices. It is also responsible for ensuring that cybersecurity measures align with the Company's strategic objectives and regulatory obligations. Regular updates and reports are submitted by our Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) to this committee, ensuring continued alignment and proactive risk management.

**Risk Management Strategy**

As a multi-line insurance provider, Clearwater processes substantial amounts of sensitive personal, financial, and business data. The Company implements a multi-layered cybersecurity strategy designed to protect its infrastructure from increasingly sophisticated cyber threats. Our risk management strategy includes:

1. **Comprehensive Risk Assessments:** Regular and thorough risk assessments help identify vulnerabilities within Clearwater's information systems. These assessments are essential for understanding current threat landscapes and for prioritizing remediation efforts.

2. **Advanced Threat Detection Systems:** Clearwater employs state-of-the-art intrusion detection and prevention systems to monitor network traffic and identify suspicious activities. Real-time monitoring allows for the swift identification and containment of potential security breaches.

3. **Data Encryption and Protection:** Data encryption is applied both at rest and in transit, safeguarding sensitive information against unauthorized access. Additionally, we employ strict access controls and use multi-factor authentication to ensure that only authorized personnel can access critical systems and data.

4. **Regular Security Audits and Penetration Testing:** The Company conducts regular internal and external audits, as well as penetration testing, to evaluate our cybersecurity posture. These efforts provide ongoing insights into our security measures' effectiveness and help guide future enhancements.

**Incident Response and Recovery**

Clearwater is committed to a swift and effective response to any cybersecurity incidents, with clear protocols guided by predefined incident response plans. Our capabilities in this area are critical for minimizing impacts and preventing future incidents:

1. **Incident Response Team:** A dedicated team, led by the CISO, oversees incident management activities. This cross-functional team includes specialists in IT, legal, communications, and business continuity planning, ensuring a coordinated and comprehensive response to cybersecurity threats.

2. **Training and Simulation Exercises:** Regular training and simulation exercises are conducted to prepare staff for potential security incidents. These exercises test the effectiveness of the incident response plans and facilitate continuous improvement.

3. **Post-Incident Review and Improvement:** After the resolution of any incident, a thorough review is conducted to determine root causes, assess the impact, and identify opportunities for improvement. Lessons learned from each incident help to refine our cybersecurity policies and procedures.

**Recent Cybersecurity Incident**

During the fiscal year 2023, Clearwater experienced an unauthorized access incident involving a third-party vendor's system. This incident briefly exposed limited customer contact information. Following a thorough investigation, Clearwater took immediate steps to mitigate the breach. We enhanced security measures with the vendor, including strengthening access controls and conducting additional risk assessments to prevent future occurrences.

The incident response demonstrated Clearwater's commitment to transparency and swift action. Customers whose information was potentially affected were promptly notified and provided with guidance to safeguard their details. Additionally, there was no significant financial or operational impact on the Company due to the proactive measures in place.

**Building a Culture of Cybersecurity Awareness**

Clearwater believes that fostering a culture of cybersecurity awareness among employees is essential for effective risk management. Our cybersecurity training program emphasizes the importance of security best practices and equips employees with the knowledge to recognize and report potential threats. The program includes:

1. **Annual Training and Certification:** All employees are required to participate in yearly cybersecurity training, which covers the latest threat landscape, security policies, and best practices. Certification is mandatory to reinforce commitment to cybersecurity.

2. **Phishing Simulations:** Regular phishing simulations are conducted to test employee awareness and ability to identify social engineering attacks. Feedback from these exercises helps strengthen individual and organizational security measures.

**Conclusion**

Clearwater Technologies Co. remains resolute in its commitment to safeguarding the information entrusted by its clients and stakeholders. Our proactive cybersecurity strategy, aligned with the NIST framework, ensures that we manage risks effectively while maintaining high service standards. As the cybersecurity landscape continues to evolve, Clearwater will adapt and enhance its strategies, ensuring that the Company's operations remain secure and resilient.

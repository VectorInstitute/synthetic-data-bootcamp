# Item 1C. Cybersecurity

In today's evolving digital landscape, cybersecurity is a crucial component of Redcanyon Enterprises Co. ("Redcanyon" or the "Company"), particularly in the Aerospace & Defense sub-industry in which we operate. Effective cybersecurity measures are essential to safeguard the integrity, confidentiality, and availability of our systems and the sensitive information that underpins our operations. At Redcanyon, we recognize that robust cybersecurity practices are fundamental to our mission of delivering advanced aerospace and defense solutions that enhance global security.

## Cybersecurity Framework and Governance

Redcanyon employs the NIST Cybersecurity Framework, an established standard that guides our risk management practices. This framework enables us to effectively identify, protect, detect, respond to, and recover from cybersecurity threats. By adopting the NIST Cybersecurity Framework, we ensure a structured approach to managing cybersecurity risk, which is critical given the complex regulatory environment in which we operate.

Cybersecurity oversight at Redcanyon is exercised by the full Board of Directors, supported by the Audit Committee. This governance structure ensures that cybersecurity is an integral part of our strategic decision-making process. With ongoing reviews and assessments, the Board provides critical oversight and direction, while the Audit Committee supports monitoring activities and evaluates the effectiveness of our cybersecurity controls.

## Incident Management and Response

In fiscal year 2025, Redcanyon maintained a vigilant focus on incident management and response to protect our assets and reputation. In line with our proactive approach, we identified and contained a ransomware attempt on our internal IT systems. Swift detection and response measures ensured that this incident was managed without any data exfiltration, preserving the security of our sensitive information and maintaining the trust of our stakeholders.

Our incident response capabilities are continually tested and refined through simulations and real-time evaluations. This ensures that our cybersecurity infrastructure remains resilient against potential cyber threats. By prioritizing incident readiness, Redcanyon enhances its ability to rapidly address and neutralize cybersecurity incidents, minimising impact.

## Cybersecurity Risk Management

Managing cybersecurity risk is a core component of Redcanyon’s operational strategy. Our risk management practices are designed to anticipate and mitigate potential vulnerabilities within our IT architectures and networks. Regular risk assessments and penetration testing provide critical insights into our security posture, allowing us to address vulnerabilities before they can be exploited by malicious actors.

We also invest in the continuous training and education of our workforce. By fostering a culture of security awareness, employees at all levels of the organization contribute to reinforcing our cybersecurity defenses. Phishing awareness programs, cybersecurity workshops, and regular security updates form part of our comprehensive effort to elevate cybersecurity awareness across the Company.

## Data Protection and Privacy

Redcanyon is committed to upholding the highest standards of data protection and privacy. Our data protection strategies encompass strict access controls, data encryption, and secure communication protocols. These measures ensure that both our operational data and customers' information are rigorously protected against unauthorized access or exposure.

Compliance with data privacy regulations is paramount to maintaining trust and integrity in our customer relationships. We have implemented robust privacy policies and procedures to meet regulatory requirements and uphold the confidentiality of stakeholder data.

## Technology and Innovation

As a technology-driven company, Redcanyon leverages cutting-edge cybersecurity technologies to fortify our defenses. Our investments span a range of advanced cybersecurity tools, including threat intelligence platforms, intrusion detection systems, and machine learning analytics. These technologies provide us with actionable insights, enabling the identification and prevention of potential threats in a timely manner.

Furthermore, our collaborative approach to cybersecurity includes partnerships with leading cybersecurity firms and participation in industry consortiums. These collaborations ensure that Redcanyon remains at the forefront of emerging cybersecurity trends and technological advancements.

## Future Outlook

Looking ahead, Redcanyon is dedicated to enhancing its cybersecurity capabilities in alignment with evolving industry standards and threat landscapes. Our commitment to continuous improvement and innovation will guide our efforts to strengthen our cybersecurity posture. Key initiatives for future consideration include expanding our threat intelligence resources, fostering industry collaborations, and advancing our incident response coordination.

In conclusion, Redcanyon remains resolute in its commitment to cybersecurity excellence. By integrating cybersecurity into the fabric of our business operations and strategic planning, we are well-positioned to navigate the challenges of the digital age, protecting our critical assets and delivering on our promise to safeguard national and global security.

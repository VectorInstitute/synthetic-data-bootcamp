# Item 1. Business

## Overview

Redcanyon Enterprises Co. ("Redcanyon" or the "Company") is a leading entity in the Industrials sector, specifically within the Aerospace & Defense sub-industry. Headquartered in the state of Virginia, Redcanyon specializes in the design, manufacture, and support of advanced aerospace and defense systems, delivering cutting-edge technology to secure peace and safety globally. With a keen focus on innovation and reliability, we serve a diverse range of customers, including government agencies, military organizations, and commercial aviation sectors.

In the fiscal year 2025, Redcanyon generated revenues amounting to $29,209,366,257, demonstrating our significant scale and industry leadership. Our financial performance reflects solid operational execution and a robust product pipeline.

## Industry Context

The aerospace and defense industry is characterized by high technological demands, long development cycles, and a complex regulatory environment. As a critical component of national security and global economic stability, the sector requires continuous innovation and adaptation to evolving threats and opportunities. Redcanyon is strategically positioned within this environment to leverage its capabilities and drive forward strategic initiatives in advanced aerospace systems and defense technologies.

## Products and Services

### Aerospace Systems

Redcanyon's Aerospace Systems division focuses on the development of state-of-the-art aircraft, unmanned aerial systems (UAS), and associated technologies. We provide comprehensive solutions tailored to the unique needs of our military and commercial clients, encompassing:

- **Advanced Aircraft**: We design and produce aircraft that meet stringent military and commercial specifications, emphasizing performance, safety, and reliability.
- **Unmanned Aerial Systems**: Our UAS offerings cater to both surveillance and tactical operations, providing critical intelligence-gathering capabilities.

### Defense Technologies

Our Defense Technologies division offers innovative systems and solutions for modern warfare and national security applications. This includes:

- **Missile Defense Systems**: Redcanyon develops sophisticated missile defense solutions that enhance the strategic capabilities of defense forces.
- **Cybersecurity Solutions**: To counter ever-evolving digital threats, we provide robust cybersecurity tools and services that safeguard sensitive defense infrastructure and data.

With operations spanning several strategic locations within Virginia and across the globe, Redcanyon maintains a broad geographic footprint to support our global customer base.

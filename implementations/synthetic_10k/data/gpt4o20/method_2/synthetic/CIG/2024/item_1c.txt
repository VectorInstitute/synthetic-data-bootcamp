# Item 1C: Cybersecurity

## Overview

In the realm of Information Technology, where data forms the backbone of operations, Crestline Energy Inc. recognizes the critical importance of robust cybersecurity measures. As a leader in the Electronic Equipment & Instruments sub-industry, we are steadfast in our commitment to safeguarding our proprietary data and that of our clients against increasingly sophisticated cyber threats. Understanding that cybersecurity is pivotal not only to protecting our operational integrity but also to maintaining the trust of our stakeholders, Crestline Energy Inc. has adopted a comprehensive and proactive approach to cybersecurity management.

## Cybersecurity Framework

Crestline Energy Inc. has integrated the ISO/IEC 27001 framework into our cybersecurity strategy to ensure that our systems, processes, and protocols meet international standards for information security. This choice of framework underscores our dedication to implementing best practices for data protection, risk management, and incident response. By adhering to this framework, we systematically identify vulnerabilities, manage risks, and enhance the resilience of our digital infrastructure.

Our approach involves regular audits, systematic risk assessments, and the continuous improvement of security protocols, ensuring that our cybersecurity strategy evolves in step with emerging threats and technological advancements. This rigorous approach to cybersecurity extends throughout our organization, including all operations and processes, thus encapsulating a comprehensive security ecosystem.

## Governance and Oversight

Cybersecurity governance at Crestline Energy Inc. falls under the purview of the full Board of Directors, with operational support from the Audit Committee. This governance structure ensures that cybersecurity receives the strategic oversight necessary to prioritize and allocate resources effectively. The Audit Committee facilitates a deep dive into cybersecurity risks, recommending adjustments to strategies and controls to mitigate potential threats.

This strategic oversight is complemented by an executive-led cybersecurity council within the company, tasked with the ongoing assessment of cybersecurity policies and ensuring compliance with both the ISO/IEC 27001 framework and any applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

## Risk Assessment and Management

In our ongoing endeavor to protect organizational assets, Crestline Energy Inc. implements systematic and periodic risk assessments. These assessments are designed to identify and evaluate potential security threats across our networks, systems, and applications. Being a critical component of our risk management process, these assessments form the basis for updating and refining our cybersecurity protocols to address identified vulnerabilities.

By leveraging advanced analytical tools and threat intelligence, we can anticipate potential cyber-attacks and develop preemptive measures to counteract them. This proactive risk stance is a crucial aspect of maintaining the integrity and security of the sensitive data in our possession, including proprietary product designs and client information.

## Incident Response and Continuity

Although Crestline Energy Inc. has not experienced a disclosed cybersecurity incident, we maintain a robust incident response plan to swiftly address any potential breaches. Our incident response strategy encompasses detailed procedures for containment, eradication, recovery, and lessons learned, ensuring that any security event is managed promptly and efficiently to minimize its impact.

Part of our commitment to uninterrupted operations includes a comprehensive business continuity plan that incorporates strategies for managing disruptions, whether digital or physical. Regular testing and updating of our incident response and business continuity plans are integral to our preparedness.

## Employee Training and Awareness

Recognizing that human error is often a contributor to cybersecurity breaches, Crestline Energy Inc. invests significantly in employee training and awareness programs. These programs are designed to instruct employees across all levels in cybersecurity best practices, secure data handling, phishing identification, and the importance of vigilance.

Our security awareness initiatives promote a culture of security mindfulness, ensuring that every member of our organization is equipped to act as a frontline defense against cyber threats.

## Data Privacy and Protection

At Crestline Energy Inc., data privacy is as crucial as data security. Our robust framework ensures that compliance with data privacy laws and regulations is upheld, reinforcing the trust placed in us by our clients and partners. This includes the implementation of controls to govern data access, retention, and disposal.

Our privacy protocols are designed to align with global standards, ensuring that irrespective of geographical location, data is managed with the utmost care and security.

## Continuous Improvement

Keeping pace with an evolving threat landscape requires us to continuously reassess and refine our cybersecurity strategies. Innovations in security technology, responses to global security trends, and feedback from internal audits all inform our strategy updates.

Our commitment to continuous improvement means that as Crestline Energy Inc. grows and expands, so too does the sophistication of our cybersecurity measures, ensuring ongoing protection of our digital assets.

## Conclusion

Crestline Energy Inc.'s dedication to cybersecurity is unwavering. With a robust framework, strong governance, and a culture of continuous improvement, we stand resolute in safeguarding our digital assets and ensuring the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of the information entrusted to us. Looking ahead, as we advance our technological innovations and expand our market presence, cybersecurity will remain a cornerstone of our operational excellence and risk management strategy, cementing our role as a responsible and reliable industry leader.

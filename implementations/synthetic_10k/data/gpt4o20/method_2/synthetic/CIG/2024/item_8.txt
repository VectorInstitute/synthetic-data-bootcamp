# Item 8: Financial Statements and Supplementary Data

## Financial Statements

Crestline Energy Inc. is pleased to present its audited financial statements for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024. These statements have been prepared in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP) and provide a comprehensive overview of our financial condition, performance, and cash flows for the year, reinforcing our position as a robust player in the Information Technology sector.

### Consolidated Balance Sheet

Our balance sheet reflects the robust financial standing of Crestline Energy Inc., with total assets reaching $29,921,956,490. A substantial portion of these assets is composed of goodwill ($19,261,194,429) and net intangible assets ($8,934,374,934), showcasing investments in technology and brand equity. Current assets such as cash and equivalents are $172,715,767, while accounts receivable are outstanding at $771,537,803, highlighting our strong liquidity position. Inventory levels are managed at $107,289,672, supporting operational efficiency in line with demand forecasts.

Total liabilities stand at $13,943,582,430, underscored by our balanced leverage strategy. Short-term debt is reported at $1,064,539,201 while long-term debt is maintained at $5,648,764,304, ensuring an optimal capital structure. Other liabilities, including accrued liabilities of $470,116,566, reflect our disciplined financial management.

Stockholders’ equity is a robust $15,978,374,060, showcasing the company's financial resilience. Key components include retained earnings of $17,560,625,189, highlighting consistent profitability and our capacity for reinvestment and shareholder returns.

### Consolidated Income Statement

Crestline Energy Inc.'s income statement for fiscal 2024 is a testament to our operational efficiency and market success, with total revenues of $7,425,482,275. Operating income surged to $2,084,884,454, reflecting our targeted strategies in cost management and efficiency improvements across operations.

Operating expenses were effectively monitored, with cost of revenue at $2,318,426,046 due to strategic procurement initiatives. Selling, General and Administrative expenses totaled $3,207,972,259, and R&D costs were $870,321,394, underscoring our commitment to innovation. Other operating expenses were favorably influenced, with an accounting gain of $1,056,121,878, further boosting our bottom line.

Our net income is reported at $1,876,085,370 after accounting for an income tax expense of $208,799,084. This exemplar performance is aligned with our strategic focus on revenue expansion and maintaining tight control over expenses.

### Consolidated Cash Flow Statement

The cash flow statement details our liquidity strength, with net cash provided by operating activities amounting to $2,384,760,713. This is primarily driven by our net income and supported by significant non-cash adjustments for depreciation and amortization of $928,396,068, alongside efficient working capital management resulting in cash outflows of $419,720,725.

Regarding investing activities, net cash used was $3,403,897,499, reflecting our strategic investments in enhancing technological capabilities. Financing activities contributed $1,030,369,893 to cash flow, driven by judicious debt management and a focus on enhancing shareholder value through dividend payments of $311,962,844.

The net change in cash for the year was an increase of $11,233,107, affirming our financial strategy's effectiveness in generating cash while supporting growth-oriented investments.

## Notes to the Financial Statements

### Basis of Presentation

The financial statements are presented in U.S. dollars and have been prepared according to GAAP. These notes provide additional details on accounting policies, financial disclosures, and other pertinent information to offer a clear understanding of our financial health and business operations.

### Goodwill and Intangible Assets

Goodwill and intangible assets account for a significant portion of our asset base. These arise from past acquisitions and the strategic investment in intellectual property and technology capabilities. These assets are tested for impairment annually and when events or changes in circumstances indicate that the carrying value may not be recoverable.

### Revenue Recognition

Crestline Energy Inc. recognizes revenue primarily from the sale of electronic equipment and the provision of related services. Revenue is recognized when control of the product or service is transferred to the customer and the company satisfies its performance obligations. This process aligns with our commitment to transparency and regulatory compliance.

### Income Taxes

Tax expense calculations are based on the geographical mix of earnings, applicable tax rates, and tax planning strategies. We carefully manage our tax positions to ensure compliance with relevant tax legislation, acknowledging the complexities and variabilities involved in international tax obligations.

### Risk Management

Our approach to risk management is multifaceted, focusing on financial hedging, insurance, and operational contingencies. This includes currency risk mitigation due to our international exposure and strategic supply chain risk management to ensure product availability against global disruptions.

The financial position and results of operations for Crestline Energy Inc. in fiscal 2024 reaffirm our business acumen and strategic focus on technological excellence, operational efficiency, and sustainable growth.

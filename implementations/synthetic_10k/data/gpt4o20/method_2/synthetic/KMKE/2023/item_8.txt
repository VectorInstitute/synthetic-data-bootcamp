# Item 8: Financial Statements and Supplementary Data

## Overview

The following section provides the comprehensive financial statements and supplementary data for Stonegate Networks Group, Inc. ("Stonegate" or the "Company") for the fiscal year ending 2023. As a leader in the financial sector, specifically within the transaction and payment processing services sub-industry, Stonegate demonstrates financial strength and resilience rooted in its robust balance sheet and effective financial management practices. All figures are reported in U.S. dollars and are prepared in accordance with the generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP).

## Consolidated Financial Statements

### Consolidated Balance Sheet

As of December 31, 2023, Stonegate's total assets amounted to $55,243,583,038. The Company maintains a diversified asset base with significant holdings in cash and cash equivalents, accounts receivable, property, plant, and equipment, as well as goodwill and intangible assets. Notably, cash and cash equivalents stood at an impressive $12,288,864,079, reflecting strong liquidity and financial flexibility which supports both operational needs and strategic initiatives.

The balance sheet also reflects total liabilities of $44,939,949,922, including long-term debt obligations of $20,127,341,520, which manageably leverage Stonegate's growth endeavors. Equity stands strong at $10,303,633,116, underscoring the Company's sound capital position and commitment to shareholder value.

#### Key Balance Sheet Highlights
- **Total Assets**: $55,243,583,038
- **Total Liabilities**: $44,939,949,922
- **Total Stockholders' Equity**: $10,303,633,116

### Consolidated Income Statement

For the fiscal year 2023, Stonegate's total revenues reached $34,182,102,995, showcasing the Company's ability to capture market demand and maintain a formidable competitive position. Operating income amounted to $18,829,982,675, driven by effective cost management and operational efficiencies, resulting in a robust operating margin of approximately 55.1%.

The Company recorded a net income of $16,343,775,370 after accounting for interest and tax expenses, yielding a strong net profit margin of 47.8%. This profitability underscores Stonegate's focus on financial discipline and strategic investments that drive sustained growth.

#### Key Income Statement Highlights
- **Revenues**: $34,182,102,995
- **Operating Income**: $18,829,982,675
- **Net Income**: $16,343,775,370

### Consolidated Cash Flow Statement

Stonegate's cash flow from operating activities totaled $16,508,452,354, indicative of robust cash generation capabilities. #### Key Cash Flow Highlights

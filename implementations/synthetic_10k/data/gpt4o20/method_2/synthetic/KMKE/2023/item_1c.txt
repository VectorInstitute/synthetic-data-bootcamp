# Item 1C: Cybersecurity

## Overview

As a leading provider of transaction and payment processing services, Stonegate Networks Group, Inc. ("Stonegate" or the "Company"), recognizes that cybersecurity is paramount to protecting our clients' sensitive financial data and maintaining the integrity of our operations. Our commitment to cybersecurity is embedded in every aspect of our business, supported by an advanced cybersecurity infrastructure and a comprehensive risk management framework.

## Cybersecurity Framework

Stonegate employs an internal enterprise risk management framework aligned with the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) cybersecurity framework. This alignment ensures that our approach to cybersecurity is systematic, robust, and scalable. We incorporate industry-leading practices to identify, protect, detect, respond to, and recover from cyber threats, continually updating our protocols to address emerging risks.

Our cybersecurity program is designed to address the complex challenges of the financial sector, particularly within transaction and payment processing services, where protecting consumer information is critical. The program encompasses technological solutions, operational processes, and personnel training, ensuring a holistic defense against cyber risks.

## Oversight and Governance

The Audit Committee of the Board of Directors is responsible for overseeing Stonegate's cybersecurity risk management practices. This governance structure ensures that cybersecurity receives strategic attention at the highest levels of the organization. The Audit Committee routinely reviews and assesses cybersecurity policies, incident response strategies, and risk assessments to ensure compliance with regulatory requirements and industry standards.

Additionally, Stonegate's executive leadership team, including our Chief Information Security Officer (CISO), is tasked with implementing cybersecurity initiatives across the company. By fostering a culture of security awareness, Stonegate empowers employees to recognize and report potential threats, thereby enhancing our overall cybersecurity posture.

## Cybersecurity Measures

### Advanced Threat Protection

Stonegate has invested extensively in advanced cybersecurity technologies to protect our systems and data from a broad spectrum of threats. Our multi-layered cybersecurity defenses include intrusion detection and prevention systems, endpoint protection, network security protocols, and encryption technologies. These measures are designed to safeguard against malware, data breaches, and unauthorized access attempts.

### Continuous Monitoring and Response

To ensure the resilience of our cybersecurity defenses, Stonegate continuously monitors network activities across our infrastructure. The Security Operations Center (SOC) provides round-the-clock surveillance, allowing us to detect anomalies and potential incidents in real-time. Our incident response team is equipped to respond promptly to any cyber incidents, minimizing potential impacts on our operations and clients.

Our approach to cybersecurity is proactive rather than reactive. Regular vulnerability assessments and penetration testing are conducted to identify and address system weaknesses before threats can exploit them. This strategy mitigates risks and ensures an ongoing state of preparedness.

## Cybersecurity Incident History

For fiscal year 2023, Stonegate reported no disclosed cybersecurity incidents, reflecting the efficacy of our comprehensive cybersecurity program. However, we recognize that the absence of past incidents does not equate to future immunity. As cyber threats evolve, our commitment to cybersecurity remains steadfast, with continuous enhancements to our defenses and adaptive strategies to counter potential threats.

## Cybersecurity Risk Management

While no system can be entirely invulnerable to cyber threats, Stonegate's risk management practices focus on minimizing the likelihood and impact of any potential incidents. The risk management framework involves regular risk assessments, collaboration with industry partners, and active engagement with regulatory bodies to remain at the forefront of cybersecurity developments.

Furthermore, Stonegate maintains a robust cybersecurity insurance policy to mitigate the financial risk associated with potential data breaches or other cyber incidents. This coverage demonstrates our proactive stance in managing cybersecurity risks and our dedication to protecting stakeholders' interests.

## Employee Training and Awareness

Understanding that humans are often the first line of defense against cyber threats, Stonegate prioritizes employee training and awareness. Our comprehensive training programs are tailored to educate employees about cybersecurity best practices, phishing scams, and social engineering tactics. By empowering our workforce with knowledge, we fortify our defenses and minimize the risk of human error compromising our systems.

## Conclusion

As a leader in transaction and payment processing services, Stonegate Networks Group, Inc. is unwavering in its commitment to cybersecurity excellence. Our systematic approach, robust governance framework, and continuous investment in advanced technologies position us to navigate the complex cybersecurity landscape effectively. We remain focused on enhancing our cybersecurity measures, protecting client data, and ensuring the integrity of our operations, thereby maintaining the trust and confidence of our stakeholders.

Stonegate's ongoing dedication to cybersecurity not only addresses immediate threats but also anticipates future challenges, allowing us to adapt and evolve in a rapidly changing digital environment. Our cybersecurity strategy is a cornerstone of our business, integral to safeguarding the interests of our clients and supporting our long-term success.

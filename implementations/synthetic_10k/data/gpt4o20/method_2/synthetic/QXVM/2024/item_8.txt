# Item 8: Financial Statements and Supplementary Data

## Financial Statements Overview

Bluestone Analytics Inc. ("Bluestone" or the "Company") presents the consolidated financial statements for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024. Our financial statements have been prepared in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP) in the United States, providing a comprehensive view of our financial position, operational performance, and cash flow activities for the specified period.

### Balance Sheet Highlights

As of December 31, 2024, Bluestone's total assets amounted to $15,139,779,410, reflecting our robust strategic investments and operational dynamics. Our cash and cash equivalents stand at $296,432,117, ensuring adequate liquidity to manage short-term obligations and strategic opportunities. Notably, property, plant, and equipment net valuations rose to $5,426,829,563, underscoring our commitment to maintaining and enhancing our infrastructure, pivotal for operational efficiency and growth.

Our total liabilities are $14,836,983,822, anchored primarily by accounts payable of $6,290,996,188 and long-term debt totaling $7,665,483,624. This liability structure showcases our strategic use of leverage to fund operations and growth while maintaining fiscal discipline. Stockholders' equity is measured at $302,795,588, comprised of components including additional paid-in capital and retained earnings, indicating prudent equity management amidst aggressive market dynamics.

### Income Statement Overview

For the fiscal year 2024, Bluestone recorded revenues of $18,529,777,843, stemming from our expansive client base and enhanced service offerings in the Automotive Retail sub-industry. Our efficient cost management is exemplified by total operating expenses amounting to $14,381,779,054. Key components include a cost of revenue of $8,574,432,061 and selling, general and administrative expenses of $5,901,686,575. Consequently, our operating income reached an impressive $4,294,588,789.

The absence of interest expense facilitated a direct conversion of operating income to pretax income, leading to a net income of $2,625,875,247 after accounting for income tax expenses amounting to $1,668,713,542. This performance underscores Bluestone's ability to maintain profitability in a competitive marketplace.

### Cash Flow Statement Summary

Bluestone's cash flow from operating activities totaled $3,207,792,907, reflecting our capability to generate cash efficiently from operational activities, with net income being the primary contributor. Adjustments for non-cash charges include $626,514,674 in depreciation and amortization, indicating ongoing investment in property and technology.

Investing activities resulted in a cash outflow of $1,155,738,520, driven by capital expenditures totaling $1,234,760,180, aligned with our strategy to bolster infrastructure for sustained competitive advantage. Offsetting these outflows was $79,021,660 from other investing sources, showcasing our balanced approach to asset management.

Financing activities yielded a net cash outflow of $1,584,427,266. Key activities included strategic share repurchases amounting to $3,200,289,873, signifying our commitment to enhance shareholder value. Proceeds from other financing activities partially offset this outflow, showing judicious capital structure management.

### Notes to Financial Statements

Revenue from contracts with customers is recognized when control of the promised goods or services is transferred to clients, reflecting our adherence to the revenue recognition standard. This is primarily at the point of sale for product-related revenues or over time for service-oriented contracts, ensuring accurate and timely revenue reflection.

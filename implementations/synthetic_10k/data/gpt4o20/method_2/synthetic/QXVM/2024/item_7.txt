# Item 7: Management’s Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

## Overview

Bluestone Analytics Inc. ("Bluestone" or the "Company") is pleased to report robust financial performance for the fiscal year 2024, marked by strategic achievements across our core operations in the Automotive Retail sub-industry. In an evolving market landscape, our emphasis on data analytics and customer-centric solutions has enabled us to achieve revenues of $18,529,777,843 and a net income of $2,625,875,247. Amidst a backdrop of intense competition and rapidly shifting consumer preferences, Bluestone's fiscal strategies have driven substantial growth and further solidified our market position.

## Results of Operations

### Revenues

Bluestone achieved a revenue of $18,529,777,843, reflecting our continued success in expanding our client base and enhancing our service offerings within the Consumer Discretionary sector. Our data analytics solutions and customer engagement platforms have seen increased adoption, contributing significantly to our revenue growth. We continue to focus on delivering innovative solutions tailored to the automotive retail market, addressing the diverse needs of our clients.

### Operating Income

Our operating income reached $4,294,588,789, a testament to our efficient cost management and strategic allocation of resources. Operating expenses were kept in check, with a total of $14,381,779,054. Notably, the cost of revenue, at $8,574,432,061, was meticulously managed to maintain healthy profit margins even as we scaled our operations.

### Net Income

Net income for the fiscal year 2024 was $2,625,875,247, underscoring our ability to translate operational success into bottom-line results. The absence of interest expenses facilitated a direct translation of operating and pre-tax income into net proceeds, thereby optimizing returns for our stakeholders.

## Financial Condition

### Balance Sheet

Bluestone's balance sheet reflects a stable financial structure, with total assets amounting to $15,139,779,410. Our liquidity position remains robust, as evidenced by cash and equivalents of $296,432,117. We continued to invest strategically in property, plant, and equipment, with a net valuation of $5,426,829,563 supporting our operational infrastructure and future growth initiatives.

Total liabilities stand at $14,836,983,822, with accounts payable and accrued liabilities constituting a significant portion, at $6,290,996,188 and $811,166,793 respectively. Our equity balance, totaling $302,795,588, reflects careful management of equity and debt, ensuring financial stability and flexibility.

### Cash Flow

Our cash flow management in fiscal year 2024 demonstrates our operational efficiency and strategic financial planning. Cash from operating activities was a robust $3,207,792,907, driven by strong operational performance and prudent working capital management. Despite significant capital expenditures of $1,234,760,180, our disciplined approach in investing activities resulted in manageable net cash usage of $1,155,738,520.

Cash flows from financing activities reported an outflow of $1,584,427,266, primarily due to strategic share repurchases totaling $3,200,289,873, offset by inflows from other financing activities. The overall net change in cash was positive, at $467,627,121, reinforcing our solid cash position.

Fiscal 2024 saw continued investment in digital platforms to enhance user experience and meet diverse client requirements. Bluestone remains committed to integrating sustainable practices across our operations, aligning with industry standards and societal expectations. Recognizing the inherent risks in the automotive retail sector, Bluestone employs a comprehensive risk management framework to identify and mitigate potential challenges. Our proactive approach to managing economic, operational, and financial risks ensures resilience and sustainability, safeguarding our assets and stakeholder interests.

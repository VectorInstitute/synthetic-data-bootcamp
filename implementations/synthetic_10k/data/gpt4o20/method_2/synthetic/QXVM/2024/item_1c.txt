# Item 1C: Cybersecurity

## Overview

At Bluestone Analytics Inc. ("Bluestone" or the "Company"), maintaining robust cybersecurity practices is a fundamental aspect of our operations. Given our significant reliance on data analytics and technology platforms within the automotive retail industry, we are acutely aware of the critical importance of safeguarding the integrity, confidentiality, and availability of our data assets. Our cybersecurity strategy is designed to protect our systems against unauthorized access, data breaches, and other cyber threats while ensuring compliance with relevant regulations and industry standards.

## Cybersecurity Governance

The governance of our cybersecurity program is overseen by our full Board of Directors, with support from the Audit Committee. This governance structure ensures that cybersecurity is prioritized at the highest levels within the Company, fostering a culture of vigilance and proactive management. Regular updates are provided to the Board, which includes reviews of our risk mitigation strategies and the effectiveness of our cybersecurity measures. By integrating cybersecurity oversight into our corporate governance framework, we align our information security policy with our overall business objectives, reinforcing our commitment to maintaining a secure and resilient operational environment.

## Cybersecurity Framework

Bluestone's cybersecurity initiatives are grounded in the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) Cybersecurity Framework. Utilizing this industry-recognized framework enables us to systematically manage and mitigate cybersecurity risks, ensuring alignment with best practices. The framework guides our approach across five key functions: Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, and Recover. By establishing a comprehensive cybersecurity strategy, we not only fortify our defenses against potential threats but also enhance our ability to respond promptly and effectively to incidents, thereby minimizing potential disruptions to our business operations.

### Identify

The first step in our cybersecurity strategy involves identifying and understanding our critical assets, as well as the risks associated with them. We conduct regular assessments of our information systems and processes to gain insights into potential vulnerabilities. This allows us to prioritize the implementation of risk management strategies and allocate resources efficiently to address the most significant threats.

### Protect

To safeguard our systems and data, we implement protective measures that include user access controls, encryption, network security, and cybersecurity training for employees. Our multi-layered defense strategy is designed to prevent unauthorized access and ensure data confidentiality and integrity. Regular training sessions raise employee awareness about security best practices and phishing threats to help mitigate risks related to human error.

### Detect

Prompt detection of cybersecurity incidents is crucial to minimizing their impact. We have established advanced monitoring systems and protocols to identify suspicious activities and potential threats in real-time. Our threat detection capabilities are continuously updated and enhanced with new technologies, allowing us to swiftly detect and address anomalies before they escalate into more significant issues.

### Respond

In the event of a cybersecurity incident, our incident response team is prepared to act swiftly and efficiently to contain and remediate the threat. Our incident response plan includes predefined steps for communication, containment, eradication, recovery, and post-incident analysis. By maintaining a state of readiness and regularly evaluating our response procedures through simulated exercises, we ensure a quick and effective response to any potential breach.

### Recover

Following any cybersecurity event, our focus shifts to recovering and restoring normal operations as efficiently as possible. We prioritize the affected areas and leverage backup and recovery strategies to minimize downtime and data loss. Post-incident evaluations allow us to learn from each event, refine our processes, and enhance our overall cybersecurity posture.

## Cybersecurity Risk Management

We recognize that cybersecurity risks are dynamic and require continuous vigilance and adaptation. Our risk management approach involves the proactive identification, assessment, and mitigation of potential cyber threats. The ever-evolving nature of cybersecurity threats demands that we remain agile and responsive, enhancing our capabilities through regular audits, testing, and improvement of our security measures. Our investments in cybersecurity infrastructure and human capital are indicative of our commitment to maintaining a secure digital environment.

## Incident History

As of the fiscal year 2024, Bluestone Analytics Inc. has not disclosed any cybersecurity incidents. While this track record is commendable, we remain conscious of the potential threats to our systems and continue to bolster our defenses. Our ongoing efforts to improve our cybersecurity frameworks and practices are aimed at safeguarding our data and maintaining the trust of our clients and stakeholders.

## Future Outlook

Looking ahead, Bluestone is committed to advancing our cybersecurity strategy to meet the demands of an increasingly digital and interconnected marketplace. Our focus will be on integrating emerging technologies, enhancing threat intelligence capabilities, and fostering a culture of cybersecurity awareness. By remaining steadfast in our efforts to secure our digital assets and infrastructure, we aim to sustain our competitive advantage and continue to add value for our clients in the ever-evolving automotive retail environment.

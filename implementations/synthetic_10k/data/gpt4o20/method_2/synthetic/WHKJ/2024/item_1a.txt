# Item 1A. Risk Factors

Investing in Stonegate Materials Corp. ("Stonegate" or the "Company") involves inherent risks that could impact our business operations, financial condition, and future prospects. In this section, we outline significant factors that could adversely affect our industry position and market performance. The risks described below are not exhaustive, but represent relevant concerns that might cause actual results to differ materially from expected outcomes. Potential investors should carefully consider these risks before deciding to invest in our securities.

## Industry and Market Risks

### Intense Competition
The Paper & Plastic Packaging Products & Materials sub-industry is highly competitive, with numerous global players vying for market share. Stonegate faces competitive pressures from companies with comparable or greater resources, wider product offerings, and more extensive distribution networks. Our ability to maintain market position and profitability is contingent upon our capacity to innovate, effectively price our products, and execute marketing strategies across geographies.

### Economic Fluctuations
Our financial performance is closely tied to global and regional economic conditions. Economic downturns could lead to decreased demand for our products, particularly as they influence the primary sectors we serve, such as food and beverage, consumer goods, and industrial markets. Currency fluctuations, interest rate changes, and inflation also present risks that could adversely affect our operating results.

## Operational Risks

### Raw Material Costs and Availability
Stonegate's products depend on the availability of raw materials, including sustainably sourced fibers and polymers. Any disruptions in supply, increases in raw material costs, or challenges in sustainable sourcing could negatively impact our margins and production capabilities. We actively engage in long-term contracts and partnerships to mitigate supply risks, but these may not completely insulate us from market volatility.

### Environmental and Regulatory Compliance
Compliance with environmental laws and regulations in various jurisdictions is critical to our operations. Material changes in such regulations, increased enforcement, or failure to comply could result in significant operational disruptions and financial penalties. Furthermore, our sustainability commitments necessitate continuous innovation to meet rising environmental standards, which might require substantial investments and could affect our cost structure.

### Production Disruptions
Natural disasters, pandemics, and geopolitical events could lead to operational disruptions at our manufacturing facilities. Any significant downtime or reduction in capacity could impair our ability to deliver products to customers, impacting sales and profitability. While we maintain contingency planning and disaster recovery processes, unforeseen events may still adversely affect our operations.

## Financial Risks

### Credit and Liquidity Risk
Stonegate manages significant assets and liabilities, with total assets valued at approximately $38.22 billion and total liabilities at $23.53 billion as of the end of fiscal year 2024. Our liquidity and financial flexibility are critical to sustain growth and meet obligations. Deterioration in credit markets, changes in our credit ratings, or failure to maintain access to capital could diminish our financial stability and restrict investments in strategic initiatives.

### Foreign Exchange Risk
Operating in diverse markets exposes us to currency exchange fluctuations, which can impact our financial results and competitive position. Changes in the value of foreign currencies relative to the Euro, the currency of our headquarters in Ireland, may affect our reported earnings, costs, and margins. While we employ hedging strategies to manage foreign currency exposure, these measures may not completely eliminate the related financial risks.

## Strategic and Growth Risks

### Innovation and Technological Advancement
Our success largely depends on our ability to innovate and develop new products that meet evolving customer demands and regulatory requirements. The failure to anticipate or respond to new technological developments in the packaging industry could impair our competitive edge, affect customer satisfaction, and hinder market growth. We invest in R&D, yet there is no assurance that these investments will result in commercially viable products.

### Expansion into Emerging Markets
Stonegate seeks opportunities in high-growth, emerging markets, which introduces risks related to cultural differences, regulatory complexity, and market dynamics. Unforeseen difficulties or costs associated with international expansion could adversely affect projected returns and hinder our ability to achieve strategic objectives. Successfully navigating these challenges is crucial to realizing the anticipated benefits of market diversification.

## Governance and Compliance Risks

### Cybersecurity and Data Privacy
As we integrate advanced technologies into our operations, cybersecurity and data privacy risks increase. Unauthorized access, cyber-attacks, or data breaches could compromise sensitive information, disrupt business activities, and damage our reputation. We implement robust security measures and protocols, but evolving threats necessitate continuous vigilance and resource allocation.

### Legal and Ethical Compliance
Stonegate operates in a highly regulated industry where non-compliance with laws and ethical standards can result in legal proceedings or reputational damage. Allegations of non-compliance, irrespective of their merit, might require significant time and resources to resolve, impacting our financial performance and stakeholder trust.

In summary, Stonegate Materials Corp. faces a range of risks associated with our industry, operations, finances, strategic initiatives, and regulatory environment. While we strive to mitigate these risks through comprehensive strategies and governance practices, potential investors should consider these factors as they evaluate investment decisions.

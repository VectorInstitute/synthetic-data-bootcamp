# Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

The following discussion and analysis of financial condition and results of operations should be read in conjunction with the consolidated financial statements and related notes of Stonegate Materials Corp. ("Stonegate" or the "Company") for the fiscal year ending 2024. This analysis reflects our assessment of the principal contours of our financial performance and the key factors affecting the results of our operations. All financial figures are presented in U.S. dollars.

## Executive Overview

Stonegate Materials Corp., a key player in the Materials sector focusing on Paper & Plastic Packaging Products & Materials, continues to anchor its success on innovation and sustainability. Based in Ireland, our operations span widely to cover critical markets across Europe, North America, and Asia, allowing us to serve sectors such as food and beverage, consumer goods, healthcare, and industrial products effectively. For the fiscal year 2024, Stonegate reported a revenue of $20.49 billion and a net income of $270.67 million, showcasing our ability to navigate industry challenges while capitalizing on market opportunities.

## Financial Condition

### Balance Sheet Analysis

As of the end of fiscal year 2024, Stonegate's total assets stand at approximately $38.22 billion, highlighting our strong asset base which is essential for business continuity and expansion strategies. Key components include:

- **Cash and Equivalents:** The cash and equivalents position is robust at $640.25 million. This level of liquidity is vital for supporting our ongoing operations and strategic investments, particularly in innovation and international expansion.
  
- **Goodwill and Intangible Assets:** Representing a significant portion of our assets, goodwill and intangible assets amount to $6.73 billion and $1.06 billion, respectively. These figures reflect our strategic acquisitions and the strong brand reputation we maintain across our operational geographies.

Our total liabilities are $23.53 billion. Management remains focused on optimizing the balance between debt and equity to sustain financial stability. Notable liabilities include:

- **Long-Term Debt:** At $10.60 billion, our long-term debt primarily supports strategic capital expenditures and initiatives aiming to enhance operational efficiencies.

Equity stands at $14.68 billion, further empowering us to invest in sustainable practices and technological advancements, vital to maintaining our competitive edge.

### Liquidity Position

Stonegate is committed to sustaining a strong liquidity position, with net cash provided by operating activities at $1.57 billion, driven by solid cash flow from operations. This cash flow, combined with prudent cash management, has enabled strategic investments with a focus on long-term growth.

During the fiscal year, the net change in cash was $187.74 million, underscoring the net positive impact of operating, investing, and financing activities.

## Results of Operations

### Revenue Analysis

Stonegate achieved total revenues of approximately $20.49 billion in 2024. Our diversified portfolio of paper and plastic packaging solutions continues to drive revenue growth. 

- **Paper Packaging Solutions:** Strong demand persists across major segments, notably food and beverage, due to our innovative designs and sustainable offerings.
- **Plastic Packaging Solutions:** Despite challenges in raw material procurement, this segment realized growth from the introduction of environmentally friendly plastic solutions.

### Operating Expenses

Total operating expenses amounted to $19.03 billion, slightly narrower than total revenues, resulting in an operating income of $962.54 million. Operating costs and efficiency remain a priority, facilitated by:

- **Cost of Revenue:** Standing at $18.18 billion, reflecting high material costs but partially offset by improved operational efficiencies.
- **Selling, General, and Administrative Expenses:** Amounting to $2.44 billion. Investments in marketing and administrative systems were pivotal as we scaled operations.

### Net Income

The net income for 2024 was $270.67 million, reflecting resilient operational performance and effective cost management despite industry challenges. The income tax expense was $691.86 million.

## Cash Flow Analysis

### Operating Activities

Generated cash from operating activities was $1.57 billion, primarily driven by net income adjusted for depreciation and amortization. Working capital management, including accounts receivable and inventory turnover, positively contributed to cash flow.

### Investing Activities

Investing activities resulted in a net cash outflow of $1.88 billion, mainly owing to portfolio reinvestments and R&D expenditure aimed at future growth. No capital expenditures were reported this year, reflecting efficient use of existing facilities.

### Financing Activities

Financing activities contributed $504.99 million to cash flows, including dividend disbursements of $623.40 million and share repurchases of $24.68 million, showing our commitment to returning value to shareholders while balancing financial growth initiatives.

## Outlook

For the forthcoming fiscal year, Stonegate remains focused on leveraging its strategic market position and advancing its sustainability agenda. Emphasis on R&D continues to be a cornerstone of our strategy, promoting innovations such as biodegradable plastics and enhanced paper options to meet evolving market needs. Strategic growth into emerging markets remains a priority, targeting partnerships and infrastructure development in regions like Asia, where demand for packaging solutions is expected to rise.

Stonegate assures stakeholders of its commitment to maintaining financial discipline, managing costs efficiently, and delivering innovative, sustainable packaging solutions. As we navigate a challenging economic landscape, our strategic direction remains anchored in stability, growth, and innovation.

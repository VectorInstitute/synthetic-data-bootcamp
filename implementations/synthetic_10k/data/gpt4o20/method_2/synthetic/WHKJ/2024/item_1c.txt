# Item 1C. Cybersecurity

## Overview

Stonegate Materials Corp. ("Stonegate" or the "Company") understands that in today’s digital era, cyber threats are a critical risk to financial and operational integrity. As a leader in the Paper & Plastic Packaging Products & Materials industry, we are committed to safeguarding our operational technology, proprietary information, customer data, and stakeholder interests. Our cybersecurity program is designed to proactively address potential cyber threats and ensure comprehensive protection against data breaches and cyber-attacks.

## Cybersecurity Framework and Governance

Stonegate has implemented the ISO/IEC 27001 framework, which establishes best practices for an information security management system (ISMS). This framework guides our policies and procedures in protecting our information assets, ensuring their confidentiality, integrity, and availability.

Cybersecurity oversight is maintained by the Audit Committee of the Board of Directors, which assesses the effectiveness of our cybersecurity strategy, oversees risk management protocols, and monitors compliance with our cybersecurity policy. The Board receives regular updates on cybersecurity metrics, threat landscape evaluations, and incident response exercises.

## Risk Management and Mitigation Strategies

Stonegate uses a multi-layered approach to cybersecurity risk management, integrating technology, processes, and education to prevent unauthorized network access and data exposure. This proactive risk management strategy includes the following key elements:

- **Advanced Threat Detection and Prevention:** We employ cutting-edge technology such as firewalls, intrusion detection systems, and AI-powered threat intelligence platforms to identify and mitigate cyber risks in real-time.

- **Access Controls and Encryption:** Multifactor authentication, role-based access controls, and data encryption at rest and in transit are integral to our data protection strategy. These measures ensure that sensitive information is accessible only to authorized personnel.

- **Regular Security Audits and Penetration Testing:** Stonegate schedules frequent internal and external security audits and penetration tests to identify vulnerabilities and strengthen defenses. Findings are used to update and refine our cybersecurity practices.

## Incident Response and Preparedness

Although Stonegate has not experienced any publicly disclosed cybersecurity incidents, we recognize the importance of preparedness. Our incident response plan is designed to minimize the impact of any potential cyber incidents. Key components include:

- **Incident Response Team (IRT):** Our IRT is composed of cybersecurity experts who are prepared to execute rapid response protocols in the event of a cyber incident, minimizing harm and restoring operations swiftly.

- **Crisis Management Exercises:** Regular simulations and tabletop exercises strengthen our response capabilities, ensuring all team members are adept at managing diverse cyber threat scenarios.

- **Communication Protocols:** Transparent and timely communication with stakeholders, including customers, regulators, and the Board, is crucial during any cybersecurity event. Our protocol ensures that all parties receive accurate information efficiently.

## Employee Training and Awareness

Employee awareness is a cornerstone of effective cybersecurity. Stonegate invests in comprehensive training programs to educate staff about potential cyber risks and safe computing practices. Programs include:

- **Regular Training Sessions:** These sessions cover topics such as phishing awareness, secure password practices, and recognizing social engineering attacks to mitigate human errors that could lead to security breaches.

- **Continuous Education:** Updates and refreshers are provided to all employees to inform them about the latest cyber threats and emerging best practices in cybersecurity.

## Technological Investments and Partnerships

Stonegate allocates significant resources to adopt innovative cybersecurity technologies that enhance our protective capabilities. Our approach involves:

- **Partnerships with Cybersecurity Experts:** Collaborating with leading cybersecurity vendors and consultants helps Stonegate integrate cutting-edge solutions and stay informed about emerging threats.

- **Investment in Research and Development:** We invest in the development of proprietary security technologies and tools to tailor our cyber defense strategies specific to our industry needs.

## Regulatory Compliance and Data Privacy

Stonegate complies with global cybersecurity and data privacy regulations in all jurisdictions where we operate. Our ISMS ensures adherence to regulatory requirements, demonstrating our commitment to data protection. Compliance highlights include:

- **Data Privacy Regulations:** We are vigilant in our adherence to data privacy laws including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other applicable data protection laws and standards.

- **Continuous Monitoring Systems:** These allow us to respond dynamically to changes in regulations and incorporate new compliance requirements swiftly.

## Conclusion

Cybersecurity remains a top priority for Stonegate Materials Corp., ensuring robust protection against an evolving array of cyber threats. By diligently adhering to international standards, utilizing state-of-the-art cybersecurity technologies, fostering a culture of awareness, and ensuring rigorous oversight by our Board of Directors, Stonegate is well-equipped to safeguard its information assets and maintain stakeholder trust.

Stonegate remains committed to continuous improvement and innovation as we navigate the complex cybersecurity landscape, sustaining operational resilience and enhancing our cybersecurity posture to support our strategic business objectives.

# Item 8. Financial Statements and Supplementary Data

## Consolidated Financial Statements

The consolidated financial statements of Stonegate Materials Corp. ("Stonegate" or the "Company") for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, present a comprehensive overview of the Company’s financial position, performance, and cash flows. These statements have been prepared in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP) and reflect the financial condition of the Company and its subsidiaries.

### Consolidated Balance Sheets

The balance sheet as of December 31, 2024, provides a snapshot of Stonegate's assets, liabilities, and equity. Key highlights include:

- **Total Assets:** Stonegate reported total assets of approximately $38.22 billion. Significant components include cash and equivalents of $640.25 million and goodwill of $6.73 billion, which underscores investments in strategic acquisitions and the intrinsic value of the Company's brand.

- **Liabilities:** Total liabilities amounted to $23.53 billion, primarily composed of long-term debt totaling $10.60 billion. This debt supports capital investments aimed at driving operational efficiencies and sustaining future growth.

- **Equity:** The Company maintains a robust equity position, with total stockholders’ equity of $14.69 billion. This reflects Stonegate's commitment to sustainable business practices and its capacity to fund strategic initiatives.

### Consolidated Income Statements

For the fiscal year 2024, Stonegate demonstrated solid financial performance with the following results captured in the income statement:

- **Revenues:** Total revenues reached approximately $20.49 billion, driven by sustained demand across the Company’s diverse packaging solutions, emphasizing both paper and plastic segments.

- **Operating Income:** Reported operating income was $962.54 million, representing strong operational performance despite the current industry challenges.

- **Net Income:** Stonegate achieved a net income of $270.67 million, highlighting effective cost management and strategic focus on core business activities.

### Consolidated Statements of Cash Flows

The statement of cash flows reflects Stonegate’s cash movements and liquidity position for fiscal year 2024:

- **Operating Activities:** Net cash provided by operating activities was $1.57 billion, a result of solid net income figures alongside strategic adjustments for non-cash items like depreciation.

- **Investing Activities:** The net cash used in investing activities amounted to $1.88 billion, largely driven by strategic reinvestments in research and development to foster future growth and innovation.

- **Financing Activities:** Financing activities produced a net cash flow of $504.99 million. This included significant shareholder returns through dividends amounting to $623.40 million alongside share repurchases, reflecting a balanced approach to capital management.

- **Note 2: Business Combinations and Divestitures**

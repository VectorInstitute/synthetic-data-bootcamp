**Item 1C: Cybersecurity**

**Overview**

Silverlake Materials Holdings, Inc. recognizes the paramount importance of cybersecurity in protecting our operations, assets, and stakeholders in an increasingly digital world. As a leading entity in the Materials sector, specializing in the Metal, Glass & Plastic Containers sub-industry, our goal is to advance our cybersecurity frameworks and protocols to preemptively identify and neutralize potential threats. Although there have been no disclosed incidents to date, we maintain an adaptive and robust cybersecurity approach to address evolving risks.

**Cybersecurity Governance**

The governance of our cybersecurity program is entrusted to the full Board of Directors, with significant support from the Audit Committee. This structure ensures comprehensive oversight and accountability, aligning our cybersecurity strategies with our overall corporate objectives. The board's commitment is epitomized through periodic reviews and assessments of cybersecurity policies, response plans, and risk management frameworks, thereby fortifying our defensive posture against potential cyber incidents.

**Cybersecurity Framework and Strategy**

Our cybersecurity strategy is anchored around the ISO/IEC 27001 framework, which provides a systematic approach to managing sensitive company information, ensuring data integrity, confidentiality, and accessibility. Adhering to this internationally recognized standard facilitates the establishment of a robust Information Security Management System (ISMS), incorporating all facets of risk management, including physical, technical, and procedural controls.

Key components of our cybersecurity strategy include:

1. **Risk Assessment and Management:**
   - Regular risk evaluations and threat modeling are conducted across the organization to identify vulnerabilities and prioritize remediation efforts. We employ advanced analytical tools and threat intelligence data to anticipate and mitigate potential cyber threats.

2. **Incident Response Planning:**
   - We maintain a comprehensive incident response plan designed to provide swift and effective responses to any detected cyber threats or breaches. This plan includes predefined procedures for incident identification, containment, eradication, and recovery, minimizing potential disruptions to our business operations.

3. **Employee Training and Awareness:**
   - Recognizing employees as the first line of defense against cyber threats, we implement continuous training programs designed to heighten awareness and foster a culture of cybersecurity vigilance across the organization. These programs cover an array of topics, including recognizing phishing attempts, secure password practices, and safeguarding sensitive information.

4. **Data Protection and Privacy:**
   - Our data protection initiatives focus on protecting both company and customer data by implementing strong encryption practices and secure data transmission protocols. Compliance with applicable regulations regarding data privacy provides an additional layer of security, building trust with our stakeholders.

**Technical Safeguards**

Silverlake Materials Holdings deploys multiple technical safeguards to protect our information technology infrastructure and digital assets:

- **Network Security Measures:**
  - Our network infrastructure is secured using firewalls, intrusion detection, and prevention systems, which are continuously monitored and updated to protect against unauthorized access and intrusions.

- **Endpoint Security:**
  - We utilize endpoint protection solutions, including antivirus and malware detection software, that operate in real-time to safeguard company devices from emerging cyber threats.

- **Access Controls and Authentication:**
  - Stringent access control policies are enforced to ensure that only authorized personnel can access sensitive information. Multi-factor authentication and role-based access protocols enhance the security of our systems.

**Continuous Improvement and Monitoring**

Cybersecurity is a dynamic field, and Silverlake Materials Holdings remains committed to continuous improvement through regular assessments and audits of our cybersecurity programs. Our dedicated cybersecurity team is tasked with monitoring the threat landscape, ensuring that our defenses are responsive to evolving threats and that our cybersecurity infrastructure is state-of-the-art.

To enhance our cybersecurity posture, we partner with industry experts and leverage external resources, such as cybersecurity forums and threat intelligence services, to gain insights into latest trends and technologies.

**Summary**

Silverlake Materials Holdings is dedicated to maintaining the highest standards of cybersecurity to protect our operational integrity and uphold stakeholder confidence. By implementing a comprehensive and proactive cybersecurity strategy, we aim to mitigate risks and safeguard our business against potential cyber threats in an ever-evolving digital landscape. While no system can guarantee absolute security, our commitment to rigorous cybersecurity practices and continuous improvement underscores our dedication to resilience and forward-thinking in addressing cybersecurity challenges.

**Item 1C. Cybersecurity**

As a leader in the industrial conglomerate sector, Meridian Technologies Co. ("Meridian" or the "Company") recognizes the fundamental importance of maintaining robust cybersecurity measures to protect our assets, operations, and the sensitive data of our stakeholders. Our commitment to cybersecurity forms an integral part of our strategic operations, ensuring that we remain resilient and secure in an increasingly digital business environment. The following outlines our approach to cybersecurity management, including governance, operational strategies, and potential risks.

**Cybersecurity Governance and Framework**

Meridian has established a comprehensive cybersecurity framework guided by the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) Cybersecurity Framework. This framework provides us with a robust structure to identify, protect against, detect, respond to, and recover from cyber threats. Our cybersecurity efforts are supervised by the full Board of Directors, with significant support from the Audit Committee, underscoring the high priority we assign to safeguarding our information systems and data infrastructure.

**Strategic Cybersecurity Initiatives**

**1. Risk Identification and Assessment**

Meridian routinely conducts thorough risk assessments to identify potential vulnerabilities across our network and systems. Leveraging advanced threat intelligence and analytics, we evaluate our threat landscape to anticipate and mitigate risks proactively. These assessments ensure that our cybersecurity measures are both current and relevant, facilitating timely upgrades and adjustments as necessary.

**2. Protective Measures**

Our protective measures are designed to shield our critical assets from unauthorized access or breaches. We employ multi-layered defenses, including encryption, firewalls, and intrusion detection and prevention systems. Comprehensive access control protocols and robust identity management solutions ensure that only authorized personnel can access sensitive information.

**3. Advanced Monitoring and Detection**

Meridian utilizes state-of-the-art monitoring tools to detect anomalies and potential security incidents in real-time. Our Security Operations Center (SOC) operates 24/7 to analyze data traffic and network activities, enabling us to promptly identify and address potential threats or vulnerabilities.

**4. Response and Recovery Plans**

Our Incident Response Team is equipped to manage and mitigate the impact of security incidents efficiently. In the event of a cyber incident, our predefined response and recovery plans minimize disruption to our operations and ensure business continuity. These plans are regularly tested and refined to improve their effectiveness and resilience against evolving threats.

**5. Continuous Improvement and Training**

Meridian is committed to continuous improvement in our cybersecurity practices. We invest in regular training and awareness programs for our employees to ensure a security-conscious workforce. By fostering a culture of cybersecurity awareness, we empower our employees to act as the first line of defense against potential threats.

**Potential Cybersecurity Risks**

Despite our comprehensive cybersecurity framework, Meridian acknowledges that no system can be completely immune to cyber threats. Potential risks include:

- **Cyber Attacks and Data Breaches**: The risk of sophisticated cyberattacks remains significant, with potential consequences including data breaches and unauthorized access to sensitive information. Such incidents could impair our operations, compromise customer data, and lead to reputational damage and financial loss.

- **Third-Party Vulnerabilities**: As part of a global supply chain, we are interconnected with third-party vendors and partners. Vulnerabilities in third-party systems could introduce risks to our operations, emphasizing the need for stringent vendor risk management practices.

- **Evolving Threat Landscape**: Cyber threats are continually evolving, driven by technological advancements and increased sophistication among cybercriminals. Maintaining vigilance and adaptability is crucial to address these dynamic challenges effectively.

- **Regulatory Compliance and Data Privacy**: As data privacy regulations evolve, ensuring compliance with international standards, such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), presents ongoing challenges. Non-compliance could result in regulatory penalties and impact our business operations.

**Conclusion**

Meridian Technologies Co. remains dedicated to upholding the highest standards of cybersecurity as part of our operational integrity and strategic growth. We continue to enhance our cybersecurity measures, aligned with industry best practices and regulatory requirements, to safeguard our operations and stakeholder interests. Through diligent governance, advanced technologies, and a proactive security culture, Meridian positions itself to navigate the complexities of today's digital landscape while maintaining trust and confidence among our stakeholders.

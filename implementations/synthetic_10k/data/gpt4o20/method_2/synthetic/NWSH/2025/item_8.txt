**Item 8. Financial Statements and Supplementary Data**

**Consolidated Financial Statements**

The following consolidated financial statements of Meridian Technologies Co. are prepared in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles in the United States of America (GAAP). These statements provide a detailed account of our financial position, encompassing our assets, liabilities, equity, and results of operations as of and for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025.

**Balance Sheet Overview**

As of December 31, 2025, Meridian Technologies Co. reported total assets amounting to $41.88 billion. The major components of our asset structure include:

- **Cash and Equivalents:** $6.44 billion, showcasing strong liquidity and emphasizing our capability to fund operations and strategic initiatives.
- **Accounts Receivable:** $3.42 billion, primarily reflective of robust sales activities and efficient credit management.
- **Inventory:** $3.74 billion aligned with our operational dynamics across the different segments, facilitating enhanced service delivery and inventory optimization.
- **Property, Plant, and Equipment, Net:** $8.28 billion, underscoring our commitment to ongoing infrastructure investments.
- **Goodwill and Intangible Assets:** Goodwill stood at $6.51 billion, which primarily arose from various strategic acquisitions enhancing our competitive positioning, alongside intangible assets valued at $1.09 billion reflecting proprietary technologies and patents. 

The total liabilities equate to $36.68 billion, supported by:

- **Accounts Payable and Short-Term Debt:** At $3.39 billion and $1.89 billion respectively, these figures represent operational expenditures and short-term financial obligations associated with our diverse industrial activities.
- **Long-Term Debt:** At $12.03 billion, indicating leverage used to finance expansion projects and strategic growth opportunities.
- **Other Liabilities:** Totaling $19.37 billion, encompassing various operational and strategic contingencies.

Our equity stands robust with the total stockholders’ equity valued at $5.19 billion, indicating sustained shareholder wealth accumulation and company growth.

**Income Statement Summary**

Meridian Technologies Co. realized revenues amounting to $26.03 billion for fiscal year 2025, driven by strong performance across our business segments. Our cost management measures led to:

- **Operating Income:** $5.12 billion, reflecting our operational efficiency and capacity to deliver shareholder value.
- **Net Income:** Achieved at $3.69 billion, after accounting for tax expenses of $1.43 billion, demonstrating our fiscal discipline and strategic financial stewardship.

**Cash Flow Statement Analysis**

For 2025, we experienced a net cash inflow of $458.86 million, reflecting efficient cash management strategies through operations, investments, and financing activities:

- **Cash from Operating Activities:** Generated $2.53 billion, driven largely by positive operational inputs, efficient working capital adjustments, and revenue generation.
- **Cash from Investing Activities:** Positive cash flow of $1.61 billion supported by strategic divestitures and optimized asset management, despite capital expenditures of $939.94 million, continuously enhancing our production capabilities and technological infrastructure.
- **Cash from Financing Activities:** Witnessed an outflow of $3.68 billion, primarily from share repurchases aimed at maximizing shareholder value and strategic capital management objectives without compromising our leverage ratios.

**Segment Analysis**

Meridian's diversified operations are segmented into Infrastructure Solutions, Advanced Manufacturing, and Energy Systems:

- **Infrastructure Solutions:** Contributed significantly to the revenue mix, driven by high-demand projects in civil and technological infrastructure, leveraging smart city integration and comprehensive service offerings.

- **Energy Systems:** Demonstrated strong performance, with a focus on sustainability and energy efficiency, capturing market share in both renewable and grid management projects, contributing materially to our top-line growth.

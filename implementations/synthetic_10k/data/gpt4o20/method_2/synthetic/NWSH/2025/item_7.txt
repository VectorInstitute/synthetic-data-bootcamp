**Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations**

**Overview**

Meridian Technologies Co. ("Meridian" or the "Company"), a leading industrial conglomerate headquartered in Minnesota, experienced a year of strategic successes and robust financial outcomes in fiscal year 2025. Despite a challenging macroeconomic environment, our diversified operations across the Infrastructure Solutions, Advanced Manufacturing, and Energy Systems segments drove substantial value creation and supported a resilient financial performance.

**Financial Performance Summary**

In 2025, Meridian reported total revenues of $26.03 billion, underpinned by strong demand across all business segments. Our operating income stood at $5.12 billion, with net income reaching $3.69 billion, reflecting effective cost management and strategic execution. The Company maintained its strong balance sheet with total assets of $41.88 billion and generated positive cash flow from operations, which was instrumental in supporting growth initiatives and shareholder returns.

**Revenues and Segment Performance**

**Infrastructure Solutions**

The Infrastructure Solutions segment capitalized on escalating investments in civil and industrial projects, contributing significantly to our top-line growth. The integration of smart technologies and sustainability-oriented solutions bolstered our competitive position and facilitated the capture of high-value contracts. This segment accounted for a substantial portion of our revenues, reflecting our leadership in delivering comprehensive infrastructure services.

**Advanced Manufacturing**

Our Advanced Manufacturing segment witnessed robust growth, driven by demand for customized solutions and advancements in manufacturing technologies. Investments in state-of-the-art production facilities and the adoption of additive manufacturing techniques enhanced our ability to serve diverse industries efficiently. The segment's focus on innovation and quality sustained its contribution to Meridian's revenue mix and fortified our market standing.

**Energy Systems**

The Energy Systems segment showcased impressive performance as global momentum toward renewable energy solutions and energy efficiency accelerated. Meridian's expertise in renewable technologies and intelligent grid systems positioned us favorably in the market, resulting in increased demand and project wins. This segment's results underscore our commitment to fostering sustainable energy developments and contributing to environmental goals.

**Operational Efficiency and Cost Management**

Meridian's operating expenses, including the cost of revenue ($13.75 billion) and selling, general, and administrative expenses ($4.08 billion), were prudently managed to sustain profitability and support strategic investments. Our focused approach on operational efficiency enabled us to deliver a strong operating margin and absorb market fluctuations. The disciplined execution of cost control measures allowed us to maintain robust operating leverage throughout the year.

**Liquidity and Capital Resources**

The Company upheld a resilient financial position with cash and cash equivalents of $6.44 billion at year-end, ensuring ample liquidity to meet operational expenditure and invest in growth trajectories. The net increase in cash of $458.86 million primarily resulted from cash generated from operating activities, supplemented by positive cash flows from investing activities while strategically balancing capital expenditures and share repurchases.

**Investing Activities**

Investments were strategically allocated to enhance core capabilities and capture new opportunities. Capital expenditures of $939.94 million were directed towards expanding our manufacturing capacity and advancing our technological competencies. Meanwhile, proceeds of $2.55 billion from other investing activities further bolstered our liquidity position, supporting ongoing and future strategic initiatives.

**Financing Activities**

Meridian remained committed to maximizing shareholder value, evidenced by share repurchases totaling $3.62 billion during the fiscal year. We also managed our capital structure prudently, maintaining net equity and leverage at optimal levels to ensure financial flexibility. The absence of dividends paid reflects our strategic decision to reinvest within the business for continued growth and expansion.

**Outlook**

Looking ahead to fiscal year 2026, Meridian anticipates operating in a macroeconomic environment marked by continued volatility and uncertainty. However, our strategic positioning and diversified business model equip us to navigate these challenges effectively. We remain focused on innovation and technology integration across our segments, anticipating sustained demand for our solutions and services.

In conclusion, Meridian Technologies Co. delivered a robust financial and operational performance in fiscal year 2025, demonstrating the strength of our business model and strategic execution capability. We are committed to maintaining our trajectory of growth and value creation, guided by our vision of technological leadership, operational excellence, and sustainable impact.

# Item 1C. Cybersecurity

## Overview

Bluestone Systems Co. ("Bluestone" or "the Company") acknowledges that the integrity, reliability, and security of our information technology infrastructure and data are paramount to maintaining operational excellence and customer trust. As a pivotal provider of electric utility services, our commitment to robust cybersecurity practices is critical to safeguarding our assets, confidential data, and the seamless delivery of electricity to millions of customers across the United States. 

## Cybersecurity Framework

Our cybersecurity strategy is anchored by adherence to the NIST Cybersecurity Framework and ISO/IEC 27001 standards. These globally recognized frameworks guide our information security management system and outline a comprehensive approach for protecting our cyber assets. By leveraging these standards, we implement and continuously refine risk management processes that help us identify, protect, detect, respond to, and recover from cybersecurity incidents, ensuring a resilient security posture.

## Management and Oversight

To reinforce our commitment to strong cybersecurity governance, Bluestone has established a dedicated Technology and Cybersecurity Committee of the Board of Directors. This committee provides strategic oversight, reviewing our cyber risk management strategies and policies to ensure they align with both business objectives and regulatory requirements. The technology team, led by the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO), is responsible for implementing our cybersecurity strategy and reporting on critical metrics and developments to the committee.

## Threat Landscape and Risk Mitigation

The electric utilities sector faces unique cybersecurity threats, including those from state and non-state actors seeking to disrupt critical infrastructure. Bluestone undertakes ongoing threat assessments to understand and mitigate potential vulnerabilities across our network. Our efforts include employing intrusion detection systems, conducting regular penetration testing, and implementing state-of-the-art encryption protocols to safeguard sensitive data.

**Incident Response and Recovery**

While Bluestone has not experienced any disclosed cybersecurity incidents to date, we recognize that the potential for such occurrences necessitates robust incident response capabilities. Our incident response plan is regularly updated to reflect emerging threats and industry best practices. It outlines the procedures for containing breaches, maintaining operational continuity, and reducing the impact of any potential incidents on our services and stakeholders. 

## Employee Training and Awareness

Human error remains a significant threat vector in cybersecurity. To mitigate this risk, Bluestone places heavy emphasis on workforce education and awareness. We conduct regular training sessions to enhance employee understanding of cyber threats and promote secure practices in handling data and responding to potential phishing or ransomware attacks. This proactive approach fosters a culture of vigilance and responsibility across our organization.

## Technology Investments

To ensure proactive defense against evolving cyber threats, Bluestone invests in advanced security technologies. These include next-generation firewalls, endpoint protection systems, and security information and event management (SIEM) solutions, which allow for real-time monitoring and analysis of security events. Our strategic focus on incorporating artificial intelligence and machine learning capabilities into our security framework further strengthens our ability to detect and mitigate sophisticated cyber threats in a timely manner.

## Compliance and Regulatory Considerations

Operating in the electric utilities sector, Bluestone is subject to stringent regulatory requirements pertaining to cybersecurity. We are committed to fulfilling these obligations, maintaining compliance with industry standards and legislation, and collaborating with regulatory bodies to ensure our cybersecurity practices keep pace with evolving guidelines. Our commitment is underscored by proactive engagement in industry forums and partnerships, fostering collective resilience against cyber threats.

## Continual Improvement

The cybersecurity landscape is dynamic, necessitating an adaptive approach. Bluestone continually evaluates and audits our security measures, implementing lessons learned from incident simulations and industry developments. Our commitment to continual improvement ensures that our cybersecurity strategy remains robust, enabling us to effectively manage risks while advancing our business objectives.

## Conclusion

Bluestone Systems Co.'s commitment to cybersecurity is unwavering. Through strategic investments, continuous improvement practices, and comprehensive management oversight, we aim to protect our critical infrastructure, maintain stakeholder trust, and deliver uninterrupted utility services. Our proactive approach ensures we remain resilient in the face of evolving threats, safeguarding both our customers and the communities we serve.

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Overview

Zennov Medical, Inc. ("Zennov" or "the Company") is a leading healthcare company specializing in the development, manufacturing, and marketing of innovative medical devices and diagnostic products. Headquartered in Denver, Colorado, Zennov is committed to addressing significant healthcare challenges by advancing medical innovation and improving patient outcomes across various therapeutic areas. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, Zennov Medical reported total revenue of $2,264 million, marking a significant milestone in our continuous growth trajectory.

Our operations are driven by a strong focus on research and development, which accounts for a substantial portion of our investment strategy. This focus supports our goal of introducing cutting-edge solutions to the market, thereby enhancing the quality of care provided by healthcare professionals worldwide. The fiscal year 2024 demonstrated our ability to deliver sustained growth while navigating a competitive and dynamic healthcare environment.

Results of Operations

Revenue

For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, Zennov Medical reported revenue of $2,264 million, an increase of 8.2% compared to $2,093 million in fiscal 2023. This growth was primarily driven by increased demand for our innovative medical devices and diagnostic products across key markets. The expansion of our product portfolio and the successful launch of new products contributed significantly to the revenue increase. Furthermore, strategic partnerships and enhanced distribution channels facilitated greater market penetration, bolstering our revenue growth.

Cost of Revenue and Gross Margin

The cost of revenue for fiscal 2024 was $606 million, compared to $584 million in fiscal 2023. Despite the increase in cost, our gross profit improved to $1,658 million from $1,509 million in the prior year. The gross margin for fiscal 2024 stood at 73.2%, compared to 72.1% in fiscal 2023. The enhancement in gross margin reflects our ongoing efforts to optimize manufacturing processes and supply chain efficiencies, as well as the introduction of higher-margin products. These initiatives have enabled us to absorb cost increases while maintaining a robust margin profile.

Operating Expenses

Research and Development

Investment in research and development (R&D) is a cornerstone of our growth strategy. In fiscal 2024, R&D expenses totaled $923 million, compared to $854 million in fiscal 2023, representing 40.8% of our revenue for both years. The increase in R&D expenditure underscores our commitment to advancing medical innovation and expanding our product offerings. Key areas of focus included the development of next-generation medical technologies and diagnostic solutions, which are critical to maintaining our competitive edge and addressing emerging healthcare needs.

Selling, General and Administrative

Selling, general and administrative (SG&A) expenses increased to $124 million in fiscal 2024, up from $87 million in fiscal 2023. As a percentage of revenue, SG&A expenses rose to 5.5% from 4.2% in the prior year. This increase reflects strategic investments in our sales and marketing capabilities, aimed at enhancing brand awareness and supporting the launch of new products. Additionally, we expanded our sales teams and strengthened our customer engagement initiatives to drive market expansion and customer satisfaction.

Operating Income

Operating income for fiscal 2024 was $611 million, a 7.6% increase from $568 million in fiscal 2023. The growth in operating income was driven by higher revenue and improved gross margins, offsetting increased R&D and SG&A expenses. Our operating margin was 27.0% in fiscal 2024, slightly below the prior year's margin of 27.1%, highlighting our ability to maintain operational efficiency while investing in future growth.

Interest Expense

Interest expense for fiscal 2024 was $59 million, compared to $54 million in fiscal 2023. The increase in interest expense is attributable to higher levels of long-term debt, which rose to $1,321 million from $1,201 million in the prior year. The additional debt was primarily used to finance strategic investments and support our R&D initiatives. We continue to manage our debt levels prudently to ensure financial flexibility and support our growth objectives.

Effective Tax Rate

Our provision for income taxes was $166 million for fiscal 2024, compared to $149 million in fiscal 2023, resulting in an effective tax rate of 30.1%, up from 29.0% in the prior year. The increase in the effective tax rate reflects changes in the geographic mix of our earnings and adjustments related to tax credits and incentives. We remain focused on optimizing our tax strategy to enhance shareholder value while complying with applicable tax regulations.

Net Income

Net income for fiscal 2024 was $386 million, representing a 5.8% increase from $365 million in fiscal 2023. The growth in net income was driven by higher operating income and revenue growth, partially offset by increased interest expense and a higher effective tax rate. Our net margin for fiscal 2024 was 17.0%, compared to 17.4% in the previous year, reflecting our continued focus on delivering profitable growth.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

Cash and Cash Equivalents

As of December 31, 2024, cash and cash equivalents totaled $68 million, up from $60 million at the end of fiscal 2023. The increase in cash reserves reflects our strong operating cash flow generation, which provides the foundation for strategic investments and financial flexibility.

Operating Activities

Net cash provided by operating activities in fiscal 2024 was $451 million, driven by robust net income and effective management of working capital. Our ability to generate strong cash flows from operations is critical to funding our R&D initiatives, capital expenditures, and other strategic priorities.

Investing Activities

Net cash used in investing activities amounted to $260 million for fiscal 2024. This included $60 million in capital expenditures for the expansion and enhancement of our manufacturing facilities and research infrastructure. Additionally, we invested $200 million in other investing activities, primarily focused on strategic acquisitions and partnerships to enhance our product portfolio and market presence.

Financing Activities

Net cash used in financing activities was $183 million in fiscal 2024. This included a net change in long-term debt of $120 million, reflecting our efforts to optimize our capital structure and support growth initiatives. We also paid dividends totaling $152 million and engaged in share repurchases, net of issuances, amounting to $151 million. These actions reflect our commitment to returning value to shareholders while maintaining financial strength.

Capital Expenditures

Capital expenditures for fiscal 2024 were $60 million, compared to $48 million in fiscal 2023. The increase in capital spending reflects our continued investment in infrastructure to support our growth and innovation objectives. We remain focused on deploying capital efficiently to enhance our operational capabilities and drive long-term value creation.

Debt

Our long-term debt increased to $1,321 million as of December 31, 2024, from $1,201 million at the end of fiscal 2023. The additional debt supports our strategic investments and provides the financial flexibility necessary to pursue growth opportunities. We continue to prioritize debt management and maintain a balanced capital structure to support our financial health and operational agility.

Dividends and Share Repurchases

In fiscal 2024, we returned $303 million to shareholders through dividends and share repurchases. Dividends paid totaled $152 million, while share repurchases, net of issuances, amounted to $151 million. These actions are aligned with our commitment to delivering shareholder value and reflect our confidence in the Company's future prospects.

Critical Accounting Estimates

The preparation of financial statements in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP) requires the use of estimates and assumptions that affect the reported amounts of assets, liabilities, revenue, and expenses. Our management evaluates these estimates on an ongoing basis, considering historical experience and other factors. While we believe our accounting estimates are appropriate and reasonable, actual results could differ from these estimates under different assumptions or conditions.

Revenue Recognition

Revenue from the sale of medical devices and diagnostic products is recognized when control of the product is transferred to the customer, typically upon delivery. The determination of revenue recognition requires judgment, particularly in assessing the timing of transfer of control and the impact of variable consideration such as rebates and discounts. We continually evaluate these factors to ensure accurate revenue reporting.

Inventory Valuation

Inventory is stated at the lower of cost or net realizable value. We assess the recoverability of inventory based on historical sales trends, product lifecycles, and market conditions. Provisions for excess and obsolete inventory are recorded when necessary. Changes in market demand or technological advancements could impact our inventory valuation and result in adjustments to our financial results.

Goodwill and Intangible Assets

Goodwill and intangible assets are subject to annual impairment testing or more frequently if indicators of impairment arise. The assessment involves estimating the fair value of our reporting units and comparing it to their carrying value. This process requires judgment in estimating future cash flows, growth rates, and discount rates. Changes in market conditions or business performance could impact these estimates and result in impairment charges.

Income Taxes

The provision for income taxes involves the application of complex tax laws and regulations, requiring significant judgment in determining the recognition and measurement of tax positions. We evaluate our tax positions and establish provisions for uncertain tax positions where necessary. Changes in tax laws or interpretations, as well as the outcome of tax audits, could impact our tax provisions and effective tax rate.

Conclusion

Fiscal 2024 was a year of significant achievement for Zennov Medical, marked by strong revenue growth, strategic investments, and continued innovation. Our financial performance reflects our commitment to advancing healthcare solutions and delivering value to our stakeholders. As we look ahead, we remain focused on leveraging our strengths, addressing market opportunities, and navigating challenges to sustain our growth trajectory and enhance our competitive position in the healthcare industry.

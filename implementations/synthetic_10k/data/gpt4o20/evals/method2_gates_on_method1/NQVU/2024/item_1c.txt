Item 1C. Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity Governance

Novqua Retail, Inc. is committed to maintaining a robust cybersecurity framework to safeguard our information assets and ensure business continuity. Our cybersecurity governance structure is led by the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO), who brings extensive experience in managing complex security environments. The CISO is responsible for overseeing the Company's cybersecurity strategy and risk management efforts. This role is supported by a dedicated Information Security team, which operates across several key functional areas, including network security, application security, incident response, and governance, risk, and compliance (GRC).

Our Board of Directors holds ultimate responsibility for overseeing cybersecurity risk management. The Board is supported by its Audit Committee, which is tasked with monitoring cybersecurity risks and strategies. The Audit Committee receives regular updates from the CISO and the Company's Chief Information Officer (CIO), ensuring that the Board is informed of cybersecurity risks, incidents, and mitigation strategies. These updates cover a range of topics, including threat intelligence, incident response readiness, and compliance with cybersecurity policies and regulations.

Cybersecurity Strategy and Risk Management

The Information Security team at Novqua Retail employs a comprehensive risk management approach to identify, assess, and mitigate cybersecurity threats. We utilize industry-standard frameworks to evaluate potential risks, determining their likelihood and potential impact on our operations. Risk assessments are conducted regularly, and the results are used to prioritize resources and efforts to address the most significant threats.

An essential component of our cybersecurity strategy is the implementation of a GRC platform, which enables continuous monitoring of identified risks and the effectiveness of mitigation strategies. This platform facilitates the tracking of risk assessments, control measures, and any remediation activities necessary to address vulnerabilities.

To strengthen our cybersecurity posture, we conduct regular security reviews and penetration tests. These assessments, often performed with the assistance of third-party consultants, help us identify vulnerabilities in our systems and applications. The insights gained from these reviews inform our security enhancements and strategic planning.

In the event of a potential cybersecurity incident, we have established a detailed Cybersecurity Incident Response Plan (CIRP). This plan outlines the criteria for identifying a qualifying cybersecurity incident, the process for escalation, and the roles and responsibilities of the incident response team. The team includes representatives from the Information Security team, senior management, and the legal department, ensuring a coordinated response to any incident.

Should an incident be classified as material, it is escalated to the appropriate leadership team, including the general counsel and the Audit Committee. The CIRP provides guidelines for determining the materiality of an incident and outlines the necessary steps for communication and remediation. All incidents, regardless of their materiality, are logged and tracked within our incident response platform to ensure continuous monitoring and improvement of our security practices.

We recognize the importance of managing cybersecurity risks associated with third-party service providers. To this end, we have established a process for evaluating the security posture of our third-party partners. This process involves assessing the vendor's security practices, compliance with industry standards, and their ability to protect our data. While we work diligently to ensure that our third-party partners maintain strong security measures, we acknowledge that we cannot control all aspects of their operations.

Our cybersecurity strategy includes ongoing efforts to educate and train our employees on security best practices. All employees are required to complete annual cybersecurity training and certify their understanding of the Company's Cybersecurity Policy. This policy mandates that employees report any potential security incidents immediately to the Information Security team. By fostering a culture of security awareness, we aim to empower our employees to act as the first line of defense against cyber threats.

Cybersecurity Threat Landscape

The threat landscape in which Novqua Retail operates is constantly evolving, with cyber threats growing in sophistication and frequency. We are vigilant in monitoring emerging threats and adapting our security measures accordingly. Our threat intelligence capabilities are bolstered by partnerships with industry organizations and government agencies, which allow us to stay informed of the latest threat vectors and trends.

Despite our robust cybersecurity measures, we acknowledge that no system is entirely immune to cyber threats. As such, we continually evaluate and enhance our security controls to mitigate potential risks to our systems and data. Our commitment to cybersecurity resilience is unwavering, and we remain dedicated to protecting our Company's assets and ensuring the trust of our customers and stakeholders.

As of the date of this report, there are no known cybersecurity incidents that have materially impacted Novqua Retail. However, as highlighted in our "Risk Factors" section, the potential for cyber-attacks to disrupt our operations or compromise sensitive information remains a significant risk. We are prepared to respond swiftly and effectively to any such incidents, minimizing their impact on our business.

In conclusion, Novqua Retail is dedicated to maintaining a proactive and comprehensive approach to cybersecurity risk management. Through vigilant oversight, strategic investment in security technologies, and ongoing education and training, we strive to protect our information assets and uphold the integrity of our operations. Our commitment to cybersecurity is integral to our mission of delivering exceptional value to our customers and sustaining our position as a leading retailer in the consumer discretionary sector.

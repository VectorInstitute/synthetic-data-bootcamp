Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Overview

Novix Aerospace, Inc. is a prominent entity within the aerospace and defense sector, known for its dedication to delivering advanced technological solutions for both commercial and military applications. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, Novix Aerospace reported consolidated revenue of $21,485 million, representing a 7.5% increase from $19,982 million in the previous fiscal year. This growth underscores our ongoing commitment to innovation and our ability to meet the evolving needs of our diverse customer base.

In fiscal 2024, the company navigated a dynamic market environment characterized by technological advancements and competitive pressures. Our performance reflects the successful execution of strategic initiatives aimed at enhancing operational efficiency and expanding our market presence. The year was marked by continued investment in research and development, underscoring our commitment to maintaining a leading edge in aerospace technology.

Results of Operations

Revenue

For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, Novix Aerospace achieved revenue of $21,485 million, a growth of 7.5% from $19,982 million in fiscal 2023. This increase was primarily driven by heightened demand across both our Defense Systems and Commercial Aerospace segments. The growth in revenue is attributed to the successful introduction of new products, increased sales from existing programs, and the expansion of our customer base in both domestic and international markets.

Cost of Revenue and Gross Margin

The cost of revenue for fiscal 2024 was $14,831 million, an increase from $13,958 million in the prior year. This represents an increase driven by higher production volumes and elevated costs associated with raw materials and supply chain disruptions. Despite these challenges, Novix Aerospace maintained a robust gross profit of $6,654 million, up from $6,024 million in fiscal 2023. Our gross margin improved to 31.0% from 30.1% in the previous year, reflecting our efforts in cost management and operational efficiencies.

Research and Development

Research and development (R&D) expenses were $1,328 million in fiscal 2024, compared to $1,234 million in fiscal 2023. This expenditure accounted for 6.2% of our revenue, consistent with the previous year. Our continued investment in R&D is crucial for developing innovative aerospace solutions that meet the future demands of our customers and maintain our competitive position in the industry.

Selling, General and Administrative Expenses

Selling, general, and administrative (SG&A) expenses increased to $2,024 million in fiscal 2024 from $1,805 million in fiscal 2023, representing 9.4% of revenue compared to 9.0% in the prior year. The increase in SG&A expenses reflects strategic investments in our sales and marketing capabilities, aimed at strengthening customer relationships and expanding market reach.

Other Operating Expenses

Other operating expenses were $1,289 million in fiscal 2024, up from $1,199 million in fiscal 2023. The increase is attributable to higher costs associated with operational expansion and the integration of new technologies. These expenses are part of our strategic initiatives to enhance operational capabilities and support long-term growth.

Operating Income

Operating income rose to $2,013 million in fiscal 2024, an increase from $1,786 million in the prior year. This represents a growth of 12.7%, reflecting improved gross margins and effective cost management. Our operating margin improved to 9.4% from 8.9% in fiscal 2023, demonstrating our commitment to operational efficiency and profitability.

Interest Expense

Interest expense increased to $213 million in fiscal 2024 from $187 million in fiscal 2023. The rise in interest expense is primarily due to an increase in long-term debt, as we strategically leveraged financing to support capital expenditures and strategic initiatives.

Provision for Income Taxes

The provision for income taxes was $319 million in fiscal 2024, up from $286 million in the prior year. Our effective tax rate decreased slightly to 17.7% from 17.9% in fiscal 2023, reflecting tax efficiencies and strategic tax planning initiatives undertaken during the fiscal year.

Net Income

Net income for fiscal 2024 was $1,481 million, representing a 12.8% increase from $1,313 million in fiscal 2023. This improvement is attributed to higher operating income and effective tax management, resulting in a net margin of 6.9%, up from 6.6% in the previous year. The growth in net income underscores the successful execution of our strategic priorities and financial discipline.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

Cash and Cash Equivalents

As of December 31, 2024, Novix Aerospace had cash and cash equivalents totaling $435 million, compared to $393 million at the end of fiscal 2023. The increase in cash reflects improved operational cash flows and strategic management of capital resources.

Operating Activities

Net cash provided by operating activities was $2,525 million in fiscal 2024. This cash flow was driven by strong net income and effective working capital management. We continue to focus on optimizing our cash conversion cycle to enhance liquidity and support strategic investments.

Investing Activities

Net cash used in investing activities amounted to $2,190 million in fiscal 2024, reflecting continued investment in our future growth. This included $397 million for purchases of property and equipment, aimed at expanding production capacity and enhancing technological capabilities. Additionally, other investing activities, net of proceeds, consumed $1,793 million, representing investments in new technologies and strategic acquisitions to bolster our competitive position.

Financing Activities

Net cash used in financing activities was $293 million in fiscal 2024. This includes a net change in long-term debt of $573 million, which supported our capital expenditure program and strategic initiatives. We also returned value to shareholders through $136 million in dividends paid and $730 million in net share repurchases. These actions reflect our commitment to delivering shareholder value while maintaining a balanced capital structure.

Capital Expenditures

Capital expenditures totaled $397 million in fiscal 2024, directed towards expanding our manufacturing capabilities and enhancing technological infrastructure. These investments are crucial for supporting future growth and maintaining our competitive edge in the aerospace industry.

Debt

As of December 31, 2024, Novix Aerospace's long-term debt was $4,734 million, up from $4,161 million in the previous year. The increase in debt supports our strategic investments in technology and capacity expansion. We remain committed to maintaining a prudent capital structure and managing our debt levels effectively.

Dividends and Share Repurchases

During fiscal 2024, we paid dividends totaling $136 million and repurchased shares worth $730 million, net of issuances. These actions are part of our ongoing strategy to return value to shareholders while balancing the need for investment in growth initiatives.

Critical Accounting Estimates

Our financial statements are prepared in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles in the United States (GAAP), which require us to make estimates and assumptions that affect the reported amounts of assets, liabilities, revenue, and expenses. Critical accounting estimates are those that are most important to the portrayal of our financial condition and results of operations and require management's most difficult, subjective, or complex judgments.

Revenue Recognition

Revenue from sales of products is recognized when control of the products is transferred to the customer, which typically occurs upon shipment or delivery depending on the terms of the contract. The estimation of sales returns, discounts, and allowances is based on historical experience and market trends, and adjustments are made as necessary to ensure accuracy.

Inventory Valuation

Inventories are stated at the lower of cost or net realizable value. We regularly review inventory levels and record provisions for excess or obsolete inventory based on historical usage and projected future demand. Changes in market conditions, technology, or customer preferences could impact these estimates and result in adjustments to inventory balances.

Impairment of Long-Lived Assets

We assess the recoverability of the carrying value of our long-lived assets, including goodwill, whenever events or changes in circumstances indicate that the carrying amount may not be recoverable. An impairment loss is recognized when the carrying amount exceeds the estimated future undiscounted cash flows expected to result from the use of the asset. The determination of impairment involves significant management judgment and estimates regarding future cash flows and market conditions.

Income Taxes

The provision for income taxes is based on our interpretation of complex tax laws and regulations. We have recorded deferred tax assets and liabilities based on differences between the financial reporting and tax bases of assets and liabilities. Realization of deferred tax assets is dependent on future taxable income, and we regularly assess the need for a valuation allowance. Changes in tax laws, regulations, or interpretations could impact our tax expense and deferred tax balances.

Conclusion

In conclusion, fiscal year 2024 was a year of growth and strategic execution for Novix Aerospace, Inc. Our financial performance reflects our ability to navigate a competitive landscape, driven by innovation and operational efficiency. We remain committed to delivering long-term value to our shareholders, investing in our future, and maintaining a strong financial position to support our strategic objectives. As we move forward, we will continue to focus on enhancing our capabilities, expanding our market presence, and driving sustainable growth in the aerospace and defense industry.

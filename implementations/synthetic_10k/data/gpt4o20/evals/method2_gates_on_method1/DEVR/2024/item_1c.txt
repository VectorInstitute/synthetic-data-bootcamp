Item 1C. Cybersecurity

Risk Management and Strategy

Drazennov Medical, Inc. ("Drazennov" or the "Company") recognizes the critical importance of cybersecurity in safeguarding its operations and sensitive information. The Company has established comprehensive cybersecurity policies and standards designed to address potential threats and protect its information systems. These policies encompass a multi-faceted approach to security, including the deployment of advanced security tools that support the protection, detection, and response to cybersecurity incidents.

The Company employs a proactive risk assessment process to continuously evaluate potential cybersecurity threats. This process involves regular monitoring and analysis of the cybersecurity landscape to identify emerging risks and vulnerabilities. By leveraging threat intelligence and industry best practices, Drazennov ensures that its cybersecurity measures are both current and effective in mitigating potential threats.

To enhance its cybersecurity posture, Drazennov has implemented a robust incident response plan that outlines procedures for addressing actual or attempted cybersecurity incidents. This plan is designed to facilitate a swift and coordinated response, minimizing potential disruptions to operations and protecting the Company's reputation.

Drazennov places significant emphasis on managing third-party risks, recognizing that its network of suppliers and service providers can introduce additional vulnerabilities. The Company has instituted a structured third-party risk management program that includes thorough assessments of data security practices and supply chain integrity. This program ensures that all third-party engagements adhere to Drazennov’s stringent cybersecurity standards, thereby reducing the risk of breaches originating from external partners.

An essential component of the Company’s cybersecurity strategy is its commitment to employee education and awareness. Drazennov maintains a formal information security training program that is mandatory for all employees. This program covers a wide range of topics, including phishing awareness, email security best practices, and data privacy. By fostering a culture of cybersecurity awareness, Drazennov empowers its workforce to act as a first line of defense against potential threats.

To continually refine its cybersecurity program, Drazennov engages with third-party experts to conduct periodic assessments of its information security maturity. These evaluations are instrumental in identifying areas for improvement and ensuring that the Company’s cybersecurity practices align with industry standards. Drazennov's proactive approach to cybersecurity has, to date, successfully mitigated significant incidents that could materially impact its business operations.

Governance - Management's Responsibility

Drazennov adopts a risk-based approach to cybersecurity, integrating it into the broader risk management framework. The Chief Information Security Officer (CISO), who is a key member of the executive leadership team, is tasked with overseeing the Company’s cybersecurity program. The CISO's responsibilities include the development and implementation of security strategies, incident detection and response, and the continuous improvement of security measures.

The current CISO at Drazennov brings extensive experience in information security, with a background that spans technical expertise, strategic planning, and leadership roles within the cybersecurity domain. This experience is instrumental in guiding the Company’s cybersecurity initiatives and ensuring that they are aligned with both the operational needs and strategic objectives of Drazennov.

The CISO operates in close collaboration with the Chief Information Officer (CIO) to align IT and cybersecurity strategies. This synergy ensures that Drazennov’s technology infrastructure is resilient against threats while also supporting the Company’s innovation and growth objectives. The CISO provides regular updates to senior management on the status of cybersecurity initiatives, emerging threats, and any incidents that require executive attention.

Governance - Board Oversight

Cybersecurity governance at Drazennov is reinforced by the oversight of the Board of Directors, which is actively involved in the Company’s overall risk management processes. The Board has delegated specific oversight responsibilities to the Audit and Compliance Committee, which is charged with monitoring cybersecurity risks and ensuring compliance with applicable laws, regulations, and Company policies.

The Audit and Compliance Committee conducts regular reviews of the Company’s cybersecurity posture, receiving detailed reports from the CISO on key risk areas, cybersecurity initiatives, and the regulatory landscape. These reports provide the Committee with insights into the threat environment, the effectiveness of the Company’s cybersecurity measures, and any significant incidents affecting Drazennov or the industry at large.

Through its oversight activities, the Board ensures that Drazennov maintains a strong cybersecurity framework that is capable of adapting to the evolving threat landscape. The Board's commitment to cybersecurity is reflected in its dedication to providing the necessary resources and support for the continuous enhancement of the Company’s information security program.

In conclusion, Drazennov Medical, Inc. remains vigilant in its approach to cybersecurity, recognizing the potential risks and impacts associated with cybersecurity breaches. The Company’s comprehensive strategy, supported by experienced leadership and board oversight, positions Drazennov to effectively manage cybersecurity risks and protect its stakeholders' interests. While no system can be completely immune to threats, Drazennov's proactive measures and commitment to cybersecurity excellence help mitigate potential impacts and maintain the trust of its customers, partners, and shareholders.

Item 1C. Cybersecurity

RISK MANAGEMENT AND STRATEGY

Selzenqua Semiconductor, Inc. ("Selzenqua" or the "Company") recognizes the paramount importance of cybersecurity in safeguarding sensitive and proprietary data critical to our operations and stakeholder trust. In alignment with this priority, we have established a comprehensive cybersecurity program designed to protect our information systems and data assets. This program incorporates a multi-layered approach, integrating administrative, technical, and physical controls to mitigate cybersecurity threats and ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of our information systems.

Our cybersecurity strategy is built on a cross-functional framework that emphasizes proactive threat prevention, timely incident response, and continuous improvement. This strategy is aimed at minimizing the risk of cyber incidents that could adversely impact our business operations, reputation, and financial condition. To achieve these objectives, we employ a range of processes and controls.

The cybersecurity program is spearheaded by designated information security personnel led by our Chief Information Security Officer (CISO). Our CISO brings extensive experience in cybersecurity leadership and information technology, ensuring that our security posture remains robust and adaptive to evolving threats. The CISO is supported by a dedicated team responsible for implementing and maintaining our cybersecurity initiatives.

Central to our cybersecurity efforts are risk assessments, which are conducted regularly to identify and prioritize potential cybersecurity risks. Our risk assessment process involves the evaluation of internal and external threats, leveraging proprietary and third-party threat intelligence. This process allows us to stay informed of emerging threats and align our defenses accordingly. We incorporate industry standards and best practices to guide our assessment and response strategies.

To enhance our cybersecurity posture, we engage external service providers and consultants who offer specialized expertise and conduct independent assessments of our security controls. These third-party assessments provide valuable insights into our cybersecurity maturity and areas for improvement. We also collaborate with industry partners and participate in information-sharing initiatives to keep abreast of the latest cybersecurity trends and threat landscapes.

Our cybersecurity program includes a vulnerability management component, ensuring that potential vulnerabilities in our systems are promptly identified, assessed, and mitigated. We prioritize vulnerability remediation based on risk impact and employ automated tools for continuous monitoring and patch management.

Incident response is a critical element of our cybersecurity strategy. Selzenqua has established a comprehensive incident response plan, supplemented by scenario-specific playbooks to address various types of cyber incidents. These playbooks outline the procedures for identifying, containing, and mitigating incidents, as well as communication protocols to ensure timely reporting to relevant stakeholders.

Additionally, we have developed business continuity and disaster recovery plans to support rapid recovery in the event of a significant cybersecurity incident or other disruption. These plans are regularly tested and updated to reflect changes in our operational environment and threat landscape.

GOVERNANCE

Governance of Selzenqua's cybersecurity program is provided by the Board of Directors and its Audit and Risk Committee, which exercise oversight of the Company's cybersecurity risk management efforts. The Audit and Risk Committee receives regular updates from the CISO on the status of our cybersecurity initiatives, including key metrics, threat intelligence, and incident response activities.

The CISO maintains a direct reporting line to the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) and provides monthly briefings that cover cybersecurity trends, security events, and ongoing initiatives to enhance our cyber defenses. These briefings ensure that the CEO and executive leadership team remain informed of the cybersecurity landscape and are engaged in strategic decision-making related to risk management.

The full Board of Directors provides ultimate oversight of Selzenqua's cybersecurity strategy and ensures that it aligns with the Company's broader risk management framework. The Board's oversight role includes reviewing the effectiveness of our cybersecurity controls and ensuring that appropriate resources are allocated to support our security objectives.

As part of our governance structure, we have implemented a security awareness and training program for employees and contractors. This program is designed to promote a culture of security, providing role-based training tailored to specific job functions and responsibilities. Our training initiatives aim to enhance the cybersecurity acumen of our workforce and foster vigilance against potential threats.

In addition to internal governance, we have established a third-party risk management framework to address cybersecurity risks associated with our suppliers and partners. This framework involves conducting due diligence on third parties' cybersecurity capabilities and implementing contractual security requirements to safeguard our data and systems. Ongoing monitoring of third-party compliance is conducted to ensure that our security standards are consistently met.

In conclusion, Selzenqua remains steadfast in its commitment to maintaining a robust cybersecurity program that protects our operations and stakeholders from evolving cyber threats. Through comprehensive risk management practices, governance oversight, and a culture of continuous improvement, we strive to uphold the highest standards of cybersecurity excellence.

Item 1C. Cybersecurity

Draor Midstream, Inc. ("Draor" or the "Company") recognizes the critical importance of cybersecurity in safeguarding its operational and informational assets. As a pivotal entity in the energy sector, Draor's extensive information technology (IT) and operational technology (OT) systems are integral to its success and continuity. The Company is committed to protecting these systems from cyber threats through a comprehensive cybersecurity program designed to identify, assess, and mitigate risks, ensuring business resilience and continuity.

Cybersecurity Risk Management and Strategy

Draor's cybersecurity strategy is an essential component of its overall risk management framework. The strategy is structured to manage and mitigate threats while maintaining robust operational capabilities. The program is aligned with leading industry standards, including the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) Cybersecurity Framework, which provides a structured approach to identify, protect, detect, respond, and recover from cybersecurity threats.

The Company's cybersecurity risk management process involves continuous monitoring and assessment of its IT and OT networks. Draor employs a combination of internal resources and external partnerships to enhance its threat detection and response capabilities. This multi-layered approach ensures that the Company can quickly identify potential vulnerabilities and address them proactively before they can be exploited.

Draor's cybersecurity team conducts regular risk assessments, which include evaluating the security posture of the Company's technologies, third-party suppliers, and critical infrastructure. These assessments help prioritize the most significant risks and focus resources on protecting Draor's most valuable assets. Furthermore, the Company leverages automated threat intelligence to maintain awareness of the evolving threat landscape and take preemptive actions to mitigate risks.

Third-party risk management is a critical aspect of Draor's cybersecurity strategy. As the Company relies on third-party vendors and service providers for various operational needs, it is essential to ensure that these partners adhere to the same high standards of cybersecurity. Draor's risk management process includes rigorous qualification and monitoring of third-party vendors to prevent potential security breaches that could arise from these external relationships.

Governance

Cybersecurity governance at Draor is characterized by a structured approach to oversight and accountability, involving multiple levels of the organization, including senior management and the Board of Directors. The Board plays a crucial role in overseeing the Company's cybersecurity program, ensuring that adequate resources are allocated to manage cybersecurity risks effectively.

The Audit Committee of the Board is responsible for reviewing and assessing the Company's cybersecurity policies and practices. This Committee receives regular updates from the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) and the Chief Information Officer (CIO) on the state of cybersecurity, including risk assessments, incident responses, and strategic initiatives. At least annually, the Board receives a comprehensive briefing on cybersecurity risks and the Company's efforts to address them.

The CISO, who reports directly to the CIO, leads Draor's global cybersecurity team. The CISO is charged with developing and implementing the Company's cybersecurity strategy, ensuring that it aligns with business objectives and the dynamic threat environment. The CISO brings extensive experience in cybersecurity leadership, with a background in managing cybersecurity programs for large, complex organizations. This expertise is instrumental in guiding Draor's strategic approach to managing cyber risks.

Under the CISO's leadership, the cybersecurity organization at Draor is responsible for executing the cybersecurity program and coordinating incident response efforts. The team is equipped with advanced tools and technologies to detect, analyze, and respond to cyber threats. In the event of a significant cybersecurity incident, the CISO has the authority to mobilize a cross-functional response team that includes external cybersecurity experts, if necessary.

The Company's cybersecurity program also emphasizes the importance of cybersecurity awareness and training. Draor conducts regular training sessions for employees at all levels to ensure they are aware of potential cyber threats and are equipped to recognize and respond to suspicious activities. This training is an integral part of Draor's efforts to foster a culture of cybersecurity awareness and accountability across the organization.

In addition to internal measures, Draor engages with external cybersecurity consultants to conduct periodic assessments of its cybersecurity program. These assessments provide an independent evaluation of the Company's cybersecurity posture and offer recommendations for enhancing its capabilities. Draor's commitment to excellence in cybersecurity is reflected in its willingness to continuously improve and adapt to the changing threat landscape.

In conclusion, Draor Midstream, Inc. is dedicated to maintaining a robust cybersecurity program that effectively manages risks and protects its critical assets. Through comprehensive risk management processes, strategic governance, and ongoing employee training, Draor aims to safeguard its operations and maintain the trust of its stakeholders. The Company's proactive approach to cybersecurity, combined with strong leadership and governance, positions it to effectively navigate the complexities of the evolving cyber threat landscape.

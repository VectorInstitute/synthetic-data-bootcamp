Item 1C. Cybersecurity

Management's Role in Assessing and Managing Cybersecurity Risk

Zenaltal Foods, Inc. ("Zenaltal" or the "Company") recognizes the critical importance of cybersecurity in safeguarding its operations, intellectual property, and consumer trust. The Company has established a comprehensive risk management framework designed to identify, assess, and mitigate cybersecurity risks that could adversely affect its strategic and operational objectives. Central to this framework is the Enterprise Risk Management ("ERM") Committee, which is chaired by our Chief Executive Officer and includes senior executives such as the Chief Financial Officer, Chief Legal Officer, and Chief Information Officer. This committee is responsible for monitoring both current and emerging risks, including those related to cybersecurity, and meets regularly to prioritize and address these risks.

Zenaltal has identified cybersecurity as a crucial area of focus. The Chief Information Officer oversees the management of information technology-related risks, including cybersecurity threats. Within this structure, our Chief Information Security Officer ("CISO") plays a pivotal role in managing cybersecurity risk. The CISO reports directly to the Chief Information Officer and is tasked with implementing strategies to protect the Company's digital assets and infrastructure.

Cybersecurity Strategy and Processes

Zenaltal's cybersecurity strategy is built on a foundation of industry best practices and frameworks, such as the National Institute of Standards and Technology ("NIST") cybersecurity framework. This approach ensures that the Company develops a comprehensive understanding of cybersecurity risks and implements appropriate safeguards and response plans to mitigate these threats.

The information security organization, led by the CISO, is responsible for assessing and managing cybersecurity risks. This team employs cutting-edge technologies to detect and monitor potential vulnerabilities and threats, ensuring the resilience of the Company's information systems. The organization conducts regular assessments, including penetration testing and vulnerability scans, to identify areas for improvement and strengthen the Company's security posture.

Zenaltal also prioritizes third-party cybersecurity risk management. The Company has established a rigorous process to evaluate and monitor the cybersecurity practices of third-party vendors and partners. This process follows industry standards and helps ensure that third parties meet Zenaltal's stringent security requirements, minimizing risks associated with external collaborations.

Governance and Oversight

Zenaltal's Board of Directors places significant emphasis on cybersecurity oversight, with specific responsibilities delegated to the Audit Committee. The Audit Committee oversees the ERM process and ensures that the Company's risk management systems align with its strategic objectives. The full Board is actively engaged in cybersecurity discussions, attending Audit Committee meetings and participating in risk management deliberations.

The CISO, who leads the cybersecurity efforts, brings extensive experience in information technology and security management. With a background in leading global security programs, the CISO is well-equipped to guide Zenaltal's information security organization in protecting the Company's digital assets. The CISO's responsibilities include developing and implementing security policies, conducting threat assessments, and leading incident response efforts.

Zenaltal maintains a comprehensive Data Security Incident Response Plan, which outlines the procedures for responding to and resolving security incidents that may compromise sensitive information. This plan is reviewed and updated regularly to adapt to evolving threats and ensure preparedness. The information security organization also collaborates with the Internal Audit function to evaluate the effectiveness of cybersecurity controls and identify areas for enhancement.

To foster a culture of security awareness, Zenaltal provides mandatory cybersecurity training to all employees. This training covers topics such as identifying and reporting cybersecurity incidents, recognizing phishing attempts, and adhering to security protocols. The Company also conducts simulations and exercises to test its incident response capabilities and ensure compliance with security policies.

In conclusion, Zenaltal Foods, Inc. is committed to maintaining a robust cybersecurity framework that protects the Company's operations and assets. By leveraging industry best practices, engaging in continuous risk assessment, and fostering a culture of security awareness, Zenaltal aims to safeguard its business from cybersecurity threats and deliver value to its stakeholders.

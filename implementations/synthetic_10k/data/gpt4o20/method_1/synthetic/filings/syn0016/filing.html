Item 1. Business

Company Overview

Zenaltal Foods, Inc. ("Zenaltal" or the "Company") is a leading entity in the Consumer Staples sector, primarily engaged in the production, marketing, and distribution of high-quality food products. Incorporated in Delaware and headquartered in Princeton, New Jersey, Zenaltal leverages its strategic location to optimize its operations across various markets. The Company's mission is to nourish and delight consumers with innovative food solutions that meet the evolving demands of modern lifestyles.

Zenaltal operates with a commitment to sustainability and quality, ensuring that its products not only meet but exceed consumer expectations. The Company’s dedication to sustainability is reflected in its production processes, which are designed to minimize environmental impact while maximizing efficiency and product quality.

Principal Products and Services

Zenaltal Foods offers an extensive portfolio of food products that cater to a diverse range of consumer preferences. This includes staple foods, packaged meals, snacks, and specialty dietary products. The Company is recognized for its focus on developing products that align with current consumer trends, including health-conscious and convenience-oriented offerings.

The Company’s product innovation pipeline is robust, driven by a commitment to research and development that ensures the introduction of new and improved products. This pipeline is supported by Zenaltal’s strategic investments in technology and consumer insights, which enable the Company to anticipate and effectively respond to market changes.

Reportable Segments

Zenaltal Foods organizes its operations into three primary reportable segments: Packaged Foods, Snacks, and Specialty Nutrition.

1. Packaged Foods: This segment encompasses a wide array of ready-to-eat and easy-to-prepare meal solutions. Zenaltal focuses on providing convenience without compromising on nutrition or taste, catering to busy consumers seeking quality meals that fit into their active lifestyles.

2. Snacks: Within the Snacks segment, Zenaltal offers a diverse range of products designed to meet the increasing demand for healthier snack alternatives. The Company emphasizes the use of natural ingredients and innovative flavors to appeal to a broad consumer base, from children to adults.

3. Specialty Nutrition: This segment is dedicated to products that address specific dietary needs and preferences, including allergen-free, plant-based, and low-calorie options. Zenaltal is at the forefront of developing solutions that support consumers in achieving their health and wellness goals.

Customers

Zenaltal Foods serves a wide array of customers, ranging from major retail chains to foodservice providers and specialty stores. The Company's customer base is geographically diverse, allowing it to mitigate risks associated with regional economic fluctuations. Zenaltal’s ability to adapt its product offerings to meet the unique preferences of different markets is a key competitive advantage.

Sales and Marketing

The Company employs a multi-channel sales strategy designed to maximize reach and efficiency. This includes direct sales to retailers, partnerships with distributors, and an increasing focus on e-commerce platforms. Zenaltal’s marketing efforts are centered around building strong brand equity and consumer loyalty through targeted advertising campaigns, promotions, and consumer engagement initiatives.

Zenaltal also emphasizes the use of data analytics to tailor its marketing strategies, ensuring they resonate with targeted consumer segments. The Company’s investment in digital marketing technologies enables it to effectively track consumer trends and preferences, refining its approach to maximize impact.

Competition

The food industry is highly competitive, characterized by numerous domestic and international companies vying for market share. Zenaltal competes on the basis of product quality, innovation, brand recognition, and distribution capabilities. The Company’s focus on sustainability and product differentiation provides it with a competitive edge in a market where consumers are increasingly conscious of the environmental and health impacts of their food choices.

Seasonality

Zenaltal’s operations are subject to seasonal variations, with sales typically experiencing an uplift during holiday periods and other peak consumer buying times. The Company strategically plans its production and inventory management to align with these seasonal trends, ensuring it can meet increased demand without compromising on service levels.

Intellectual Property

Zenaltal Foods places a strong emphasis on protecting its intellectual property, which includes trademarks, patents, and proprietary technologies. The Company’s commitment to innovation is supported by its investment in R&D, which fosters the development of new products and processes. Zenaltal actively defends its intellectual property rights to maintain its competitive position in the market.

Government Regulation

Zenaltal operates in a highly regulated industry, subject to numerous federal, state, and local regulations concerning food safety, labeling, and environmental standards. The Company is committed to compliance with all applicable regulations and has established robust internal processes to ensure adherence. Zenaltal’s proactive approach to regulatory compliance not only mitigates risk but also enhances its reputation as a responsible corporate citizen.

Human Capital

Zenaltal Foods recognizes that its employees are its most valuable asset. The Company employs approximately 22,700 individuals across various functions, including production, research and development, sales, and administration. Zenaltal is dedicated to fostering a diverse and inclusive workplace culture, offering competitive compensation packages, and investing in employee development programs that promote career growth and satisfaction.

Available Information

Zenaltal Foods is committed to maintaining transparency and open communication with its stakeholders. Investors and other interested parties can obtain additional information about Zenaltal's business operations, financial performance, and strategic initiatives through these channels.

Management's Role in Assessing and Managing Cybersecurity Risk

Zenaltal Foods, Inc. ("Zenaltal" or the "Company") recognizes the critical importance of cybersecurity in safeguarding its operations, intellectual property, and consumer trust. The Company has established a comprehensive risk management framework designed to identify, assess, and mitigate cybersecurity risks that could adversely affect its strategic and operational objectives. Central to this framework is the Enterprise Risk Management ("ERM") Committee, which is chaired by our Chief Executive Officer and includes senior executives such as the Chief Financial Officer, Chief Legal Officer, and Chief Information Officer. This committee is responsible for monitoring both current and emerging risks, including those related to cybersecurity, and meets regularly to prioritize and address these risks.

Zenaltal has identified cybersecurity as a crucial area of focus. The Chief Information Officer oversees the management of information technology-related risks, including cybersecurity threats. Within this structure, our Chief Information Security Officer ("CISO") plays a pivotal role in managing cybersecurity risk. The CISO reports directly to the Chief Information Officer and is tasked with implementing strategies to protect the Company's digital assets and infrastructure.

Cybersecurity Strategy and Processes

Zenaltal's cybersecurity strategy is built on a foundation of industry best practices and frameworks, such as the National Institute of Standards and Technology ("NIST") cybersecurity framework. This approach ensures that the Company develops a comprehensive understanding of cybersecurity risks and implements appropriate safeguards and response plans to mitigate these threats.

The information security organization, led by the CISO, is responsible for assessing and managing cybersecurity risks. This team employs cutting-edge technologies to detect and monitor potential vulnerabilities and threats, ensuring the resilience of the Company's information systems. The organization conducts regular assessments, including penetration testing and vulnerability scans, to identify areas for improvement and strengthen the Company's security posture.

Zenaltal also prioritizes third-party cybersecurity risk management. The Company has established a rigorous process to evaluate and monitor the cybersecurity practices of third-party vendors and partners. This process follows industry standards and helps ensure that third parties meet Zenaltal's stringent security requirements, minimizing risks associated with external collaborations.

Governance and Oversight

Zenaltal's Board of Directors places significant emphasis on cybersecurity oversight, with specific responsibilities delegated to the Audit Committee. The Audit Committee oversees the ERM process and ensures that the Company's risk management systems align with its strategic objectives. The full Board is actively engaged in cybersecurity discussions, attending Audit Committee meetings and participating in risk management deliberations.

The CISO, who leads the cybersecurity efforts, brings extensive experience in information technology and security management. With a background in leading global security programs, the CISO is well-equipped to guide Zenaltal's information security organization in protecting the Company's digital assets. The CISO's responsibilities include developing and implementing security policies, conducting threat assessments, and leading incident response efforts.

Zenaltal maintains a comprehensive Data Security Incident Response Plan, which outlines the procedures for responding to and resolving security incidents that may compromise sensitive information. This plan is reviewed and updated regularly to adapt to evolving threats and ensure preparedness. The information security organization also collaborates with the Internal Audit function to evaluate the effectiveness of cybersecurity controls and identify areas for enhancement.

To foster a culture of security awareness, Zenaltal provides mandatory cybersecurity training to all employees. This training covers topics such as identifying and reporting cybersecurity incidents, recognizing phishing attempts, and adhering to security protocols. The Company also conducts simulations and exercises to test its incident response capabilities and ensure compliance with security policies.

In conclusion, Zenaltal Foods, Inc. is committed to maintaining a robust cybersecurity framework that protects the Company's operations and assets. By leveraging industry best practices, engaging in continuous risk assessment, and fostering a culture of security awareness, Zenaltal aims to safeguard its business from cybersecurity threats and deliver value to its stakeholders.

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Overview

This Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations ("MD&A") is intended to provide readers with a comprehensive understanding of the financial performance and condition of Zenaltal Foods, Inc. ("Zenaltal" or the "Company") for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024 ("fiscal 2024"). In fiscal 2024, Zenaltal Foods continued to operate within the dynamic landscape of the Consumer Staples sector, characterized by changing consumer preferences and intense competition. The Company faced a challenging environment marked by a decrease in revenue, yet successfully improved net income and operating income compared to the previous fiscal year. Key strategic initiatives, including cost management and operational efficiency improvements, enabled Zenaltal to navigate these challenges effectively. Despite the 6.7% decline in revenue, the Company achieved a significant increase in net income of 120.0%.

Results of Operations

Revenue

Zenaltal Foods reported revenue of $15,185 million in fiscal 2024, which represents a decrease of $1,097 million or 6.7% compared to $16,282 million in fiscal 2023. This decline in revenue was primarily driven by a contraction in consumer spending within certain product categories and heightened competitive pressures in the market. Despite efforts to introduce innovative products and leverage our robust distribution network, shifts in consumer preferences and economic factors impacted overall sales performance. The Company remains focused on adapting its product mix and marketing strategies to address these evolving consumer demands.

Cost of Revenue and Gross Margin

The cost of revenue for fiscal 2024 was $10,552 million, a decrease from $11,321 million in fiscal 2023. This reduction in cost of revenue by $769 million reflects our strategic initiatives aimed at optimizing production processes and supply chain efficiencies. These efforts enabled Zenaltal to mitigate some of the adverse effects of declining revenue. The gross profit for fiscal 2024 was $4,633 million, compared to $4,961 million in fiscal 2023. Despite the decrease in gross profit, the gross margin remained consistent at 30.5% for both fiscal years, indicating effective cost management and pricing strategies.

Operating Expenses

Research and Development

Research and development ("R&D") expenses decreased to $91 million in fiscal 2024 from $128 million in fiscal 2023. This reduction aligns with Zenaltal's focused approach to streamline its innovation pipeline, concentrating on high-impact projects that directly contribute to consumer needs and market trends. As a share of revenue, R&D expenses represented 0.6% in fiscal 2024, compared to 0.8% in the prior year.

Selling, General and Administrative

Selling, general, and administrative ("SG&A") expenses were $3,994 million in fiscal 2024, down from $4,250 million in fiscal 2023. This decrease of $256 million was achieved through strategic cost-cutting measures and operational efficiencies. As a percentage of revenue, SG&A expenses were 26.3% in fiscal 2024, slightly higher than 26.1% in fiscal 2023, reflecting the Company's ongoing investment in marketing and brand-building activities.

Other Operating Expense

Other operating expenses for fiscal 2024 were reported as $0, a significant reduction from $188 million in fiscal 2023. The absence of other operating expenses in fiscal 2024 reflects the Company's successful efforts to eliminate non-recurring charges associated with restructuring and strategic repositioning initiatives that were completed in the prior year.

Operating Income

Operating income for fiscal 2024 was $548 million, an increase of $153 million or 38.7% from $395 million in fiscal 2023. This improvement in operating income is attributed to the Company's disciplined cost management strategies and operational efficiencies that offset the decline in revenue. The operating margin improved to 3.6% in fiscal 2024 from 2.4% in fiscal 2023, demonstrating Zenaltal's ability to enhance profitability despite revenue headwinds.

Interest Expense

Interest expense for fiscal 2024 was $240 million, a decrease from $256 million in fiscal 2023. The reduction in interest expense is primarily due to the Company's strategic initiatives to manage its debt portfolio more effectively, including refinancing activities that lowered interest rates and reduced overall interest obligations.

Effective Tax Rate

The provision for income taxes was $66 million in fiscal 2024, compared to $29 million in fiscal 2023. The effective tax rate for fiscal 2024 was 21.4%, up from 20.9% in the prior year. This slight increase reflects changes in the Company's geographic earnings mix and the impact of certain non-deductible expenses.

Net Income

Net income for fiscal 2024 was $242 million, a significant increase from $110 million in fiscal 2023. The 120.0% growth in net income underscores the effectiveness of Zenaltal's strategic focus on cost management and efficiency improvements, coupled with a lower interest expense burden. The net margin improved to 1.6% in fiscal 2024, compared to 0.7% in fiscal 2023, reflecting the Company's enhanced profitability.

Item 1. Business

Oral Communications, Inc. (the "Company," "we," "us," or "our") is a leading provider of communication services, offering a comprehensive range of products and services designed to meet the diverse needs of our customers. Incorporated in Delaware and headquartered in Reston, Virginia, we operate predominantly within the United States, serving a broad customer base that includes both individual consumers and businesses.

Overview

In fiscal year 2024, Oral Communications, Inc. generated total revenue of $119,056 million. As a key player in the Communication Services sector, our focus is on providing innovative and reliable communication solutions that enhance connectivity for our customers. Our offerings include a variety of communication services such as voice, data, and internet services.

Principal Products and Services

Our service portfolio is designed to facilitate seamless communication experiences. The principal products and services include:

  • Voice Services: We offer a range of voice communication services, which include traditional landline telephony as well as VoIP (Voice over Internet Protocol) services. These services cater to both residential and commercial customers, ensuring high-quality voice communication.
  • Data and Internet Services: Our data services encompass high-speed internet access and data transmission solutions. We provide reliable broadband services that support the increasing demand for data-driven applications and streaming services.
  • Integrated Communication Solutions: For business customers, we offer integrated communication solutions that combine voice, data, and internet services. These solutions are tailored to enhance business productivity and efficiency.

Segments

Oral Communications, Inc. operates through three primary reportable segments, each focusing on distinct aspects of our business operations:

  • Consumer Services: This segment focuses on providing communication services to individual consumers. Our consumer services include voice and internet offerings, with an emphasis on delivering high-speed connectivity and superior customer service. We continuously invest in network infrastructure to enhance the quality and reliability of our services.
  • Business Solutions: The Business Solutions segment caters to small, medium, and large enterprises, offering a suite of communication products that are essential for business operations. Our offerings include advanced data solutions, voice services, and customized communication packages designed to meet specific business needs.
  • Wholesale and Carrier Services: This segment provides communication services to other telecommunications carriers and service providers. By leveraging our extensive network infrastructure, we offer wholesale voice and data services that enable other carriers to expand their reach and improve service offerings.

Customers

Our customer base is diverse, spanning multiple sectors and demographics. We serve individual consumers seeking reliable residential communication services and businesses across various industries that require robust communication solutions. Additionally, we collaborate with other telecommunications providers through our wholesale services, enhancing their service capabilities.

Sales and Marketing

Our marketing strategy is centered on understanding customer needs and delivering value-driven communication solutions. We employ a multi-channel approach, utilizing direct sales, digital marketing, and partnerships to reach and engage our target audiences. Our customer service teams are dedicated to providing exceptional support, ensuring customer satisfaction and fostering long-term relationships.

Competition

The communication services industry is highly competitive, with several established players and new entrants vying for market share. We face competition from traditional telecommunications companies as well as emerging digital service providers. Our competitive advantage lies in our extensive network infrastructure, innovative service offerings, and commitment to customer service excellence.

Seasonality

Our business is subject to certain seasonal trends, with variations in customer demand typically observed in line with economic cycles and technological advancements. Intellectual Property

Oral Communications, Inc. holds a portfolio of intellectual property, including trademarks, patents, and proprietary technologies that support our service offerings. Our intellectual property is integral to maintaining our competitive edge and ensuring the continued innovation of our product and service lines.

Government Regulation

Item 1C. Cybersecurity

Oral Communications, Inc. ("Oral" or the "Company") is committed to maintaining the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of our information assets through a comprehensive cybersecurity program. Our approach to cybersecurity is integral to our enterprise risk management framework, ensuring that we effectively identify, assess, and mitigate potential risks that could impact our operations and reputation.

Risk Management and Strategy

Our cybersecurity risk management strategy encompasses both technical and non-technical controls aimed at safeguarding our digital assets. We employ a multi-layered defense approach, incorporating advanced intrusion detection and prevention systems to secure our network perimeter. Endpoint protection through antivirus solutions and endpoint detection and response systems further enhances our ability to identify and mitigate threats at the device level.

In addition to technical defenses, we emphasize building a cybersecurity-aware culture within Oral. This includes mandatory annual cybersecurity training for all employees, frequent dissemination of security awareness information, and simulated phishing exercises to enhance employee vigilance against cyber threats. By fostering a culture of security awareness, we aim to empower our employees as a line of defense against potential threats.

We engage independent third-party organizations to conduct regular penetration testing and assessments of our cybersecurity practices. These evaluations are guided by industry standards and frameworks, such as those from the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST). Independent assessments enable us to benchmark our cybersecurity posture against best practices and implement improvements where necessary.

Our Cybersecurity Operations Center plays a critical role in monitoring and responding to security incidents. This facility provides continuous surveillance of our network environment to detect anomalies and potential threats. In the event of a cybersecurity incident, we have a well-defined Cybersecurity Incident Response Plan that establishes roles, responsibilities, and communication protocols to manage and mitigate the impact effectively.

Third-party risk management is an essential component of our cybersecurity strategy. We conduct thorough cybersecurity assessments of our vendors and partners during onboarding and contract renewals to ensure they meet our security standards. This process helps us manage risks associated with third-party service providers and ensures that they notify us promptly in case of any incidents that could impact our operations.

Governance

Cybersecurity governance at Oral is structured to ensure appropriate oversight and accountability. Our Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) is responsible for managing cybersecurity risks and strategies. The CISO leads a global team focused on protecting our information assets and maintaining the resilience of our systems.

The CISO reports directly to our Chief Information Officer and provides regular updates to our executive management team and board of directors. The board of directors, through its designated committee, plays an active role in overseeing our cybersecurity risk management efforts. This committee receives quarterly updates on our cybersecurity posture, threat environment, and progress on key initiatives to enhance our security framework.

The CISO at Oral brings extensive expertise in cybersecurity and risk management, with a background in leading security programs across various industries. This expertise is instrumental in guiding our strategic direction and ensuring that we remain at the forefront of cybersecurity practices.

Our governance framework also includes periodic engagements with external cybersecurity experts to provide additional insights and validation of our cybersecurity strategies. These collaborations enhance our understanding of the evolving threat landscape and allow us to adapt our defenses accordingly.

In conclusion, Oral Communications, Inc. is dedicated to protecting our digital assets and ensuring the security of our operations through a robust cybersecurity program. By integrating strong governance, proactive risk management, and a culture of security awareness, we are well-positioned to address the challenges posed by the dynamic cyber threat landscape. Our commitment to continuous improvement and adherence to best practices underpins our ability to safeguard our business and maintain trust with our stakeholders.

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Overview

Oral Communications, Inc. ("Oral" or the "Company") is a premier provider of communication services, headquartered in Reston, Virginia. The Company offers a wide array of communication solutions to both individual and enterprise customers. Our performance in fiscal year 2024 reflects the challenges and opportunities within the communication services industry, characterized by rapid technological changes and competitive pressures.

For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, the Company's total revenue amounted to $119,056 million, a decrease of 8.5% from the previous year's revenue of $130,116 million. The decline in revenue was primarily driven by competitive pressures and shifts in customer demand patterns. Our gross profit decreased to $39,860 million from $43,002 million in fiscal 2023, resulting in a gross margin of 33.5%, slightly up from the previous year's 33.0%.

Despite these challenges, we remain focused on leveraging our extensive network infrastructure and enhancing our service offerings to meet evolving customer needs. Our commitment to operational excellence and customer satisfaction continues to guide our strategic initiatives.

Results of Operations

Revenue

In fiscal year 2024, our total revenue was $119,056 million, down from $130,116 million in fiscal 2023, reflecting a decrease of 8.5%. This decline in revenue was influenced by several factors, including intensified competition from both traditional telecommunications providers and new market entrants, as well as shifts in customer preferences towards digital communication solutions. The competitive landscape has driven price pressures, impacting our revenue streams across various segments.

Cost of Revenue and Gross Margin

The cost of revenue for fiscal year 2024 was $79,196 million, compared to $87,114 million in fiscal 2023. The decrease in cost of revenue is primarily attributable to our ongoing efforts to streamline operations and enhance cost efficiency, including optimization of network infrastructure and renegotiation of supplier contracts. Our gross profit for fiscal year 2024 was $39,860 million, down from $43,002 million in the prior year. The gross margin increased slightly to 33.5% from 33.0% in fiscal 2023, indicating improved cost management despite the decrease in revenue.

Selling, General and Administrative Expenses

Selling, general and administrative (SG&A) expenses decreased marginally to $16,846 million in fiscal year 2024 from $16,973 million in fiscal 2023. This decrease was achieved through strategic cost control measures, including optimization of administrative functions and a reduction in discretionary spending. SG&A expenses as a percentage of revenue increased to 14.1% in fiscal 2024 from 13.0% in fiscal 2023, reflecting the impact of the revenue decline on operating leverage.

Operating Income

Operating income for fiscal year 2024 was $23,014 million, a decrease of 11.6% compared to $26,029 million in fiscal 2023. The decline in operating income was primarily due to the decrease in revenue, partially offset by cost management initiatives. Our operating margin decreased to 19.3% in fiscal 2024 from 20.0% in the previous year, reflecting the challenges faced in maintaining profitability amidst a competitive environment.

Interest Expense

Interest expense for fiscal year 2024 amounted to $3,372 million, down from $3,576 million in fiscal 2023. The decrease in interest expense is attributable to our strategic debt management efforts, including refinancing of high-interest obligations and reduction of overall debt levels. These actions have enabled us to manage our interest costs effectively, despite fluctuations in interest rates.

Provision for Income Taxes and Effective Tax Rate

The provision for income taxes for fiscal year 2024 was $4,596 million, compared to $5,185 million in fiscal 2023. Our effective tax rate increased slightly to 23.4% in fiscal 2024 from 23.1% in fiscal 2023. The increase in the effective tax rate is attributable to changes in the mix of income across jurisdictions and the impact of certain non-deductible expenses.

Net Income

Net income for fiscal year 2024 was $15,046 million, representing a decrease of 12.9% from $17,268 million in fiscal 2023. The decrease in net income reflects the impact of lower operating income, partially offset by reduced interest expenses and effective tax management. Our net margin decreased to 12.6% in fiscal 2024 from 13.3% in fiscal 2023, indicating the challenges faced in sustaining profitability amidst revenue pressures.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

Cash and Cash Equivalents

At the end of fiscal year 2024, our cash and cash equivalents totaled $10,708 million, down from $11,340 million at the end of fiscal 2023. The decrease in cash and cash equivalents reflects the impact of our strategic capital allocation priorities, including investments in network infrastructure and share repurchases.

Operating Activities

Net cash provided by operating activities for fiscal year 2024 was $36,309 million. This reflects our ability to generate strong operating cash flows, driven by effective working capital management and cost containment measures. The consistent cash flow generation underscores the resilience of our business model, despite challenges in the broader market environment.

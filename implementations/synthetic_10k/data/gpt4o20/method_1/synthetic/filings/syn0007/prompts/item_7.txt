Item 1. Business

Oral Communications, Inc. (the "Company," "we," "us," or "our") is a leading provider of communication services, offering a comprehensive range of products and services designed to meet the diverse needs of our customers. Incorporated in Delaware and headquartered in Reston, Virginia, we operate predominantly within the United States, serving a broad customer base that includes both individual consumers and businesses.

Overview

In fiscal year 2024, Oral Communications, Inc. generated total revenue of $119,056 million. As a key player in the Communication Services sector, our focus is on providing innovative and reliable communication solutions that enhance connectivity for our customers. Our offerings include a variety of communication services such as voice, data, and internet services.

Principal Products and Services

Our service portfolio is designed to facilitate seamless communication experiences. The principal products and services include:

- Voice Services: We offer a range of voice communication services, which include traditional landline telephony as well as VoIP (Voice over Internet Protocol) services. These services cater to both residential and commercial customers, ensuring high-quality voice communication.

- Data and Internet Services: Our data services encompass high-speed internet access and data transmission solutions. We provide reliable broadband services that support the increasing demand for data-driven applications and streaming services.

- Integrated Communication Solutions: For business customers, we offer integrated communication solutions that combine voice, data, and internet services. These solutions are tailored to enhance business productivity and efficiency.

Segments

Oral Communications, Inc. operates through three primary reportable segments, each focusing on distinct aspects of our business operations:

- Consumer Services: This segment focuses on providing communication services to individual consumers. Our consumer services include voice and internet offerings, with an emphasis on delivering high-speed connectivity and superior customer service. We continuously invest in network infrastructure to enhance the quality and reliability of our services.

- Business Solutions: The Business Solutions segment caters to small, medium, and large enterprises, offering a suite of communication products that are essential for business operations. Our offerings include advanced data solutions, voice services, and customized communication packages designed to meet specific business needs.

- Wholesale and Carrier Services: This segment provides communication services to other telecommunications carriers and service providers. By leveraging our extensive network infrastructure, we offer wholesale voice and data services that enable other carriers to expand their reach and improve service offerings.

Customers

Our customer base is diverse, spanning multiple sectors and demographics. We serve individual consumers seeking reliable residential communication services and businesses across various industries that require robust communication solutions. Additionally, we collaborate with other telecommunications providers through our wholesale services, enhancing their service capabilities.

Sales and Marketing

Our marketing strategy is centered on understanding customer needs and delivering value-driven communication solutions. We employ a multi-channel approach, utilizing direct sales, digital marketing, and partnerships to reach and engage our target audiences. Our customer service teams are dedicated to providing exceptional support, ensuring customer satisfaction and fostering long-term relationships.

Competition

The communication services industry is highly competitive, with several established players and new entrants vying for market share. We face competition from traditional telecommunications companies as well as emerging digital service providers. Our competitive advantage lies in our extensive network infrastructure, innovative service offerings, and commitment to customer service excellence.

Seasonality

Our business is subject to certain seasonal trends, with variations in customer demand typically observed in line with economic cycles and technological advancements. While the demand for communication services generally remains stable, periods of economic uncertainty can influence consumer spending and business investment in communication infrastructure.

Intellectual Property

Oral Communications, Inc. holds a portfolio of intellectual property, including trademarks, patents, and proprietary technologies that support our service offerings. Our intellectual property is integral to maintaining our competitive edge and ensuring the continued innovation of our product and service lines.

Government Regulation

The communication services industry is subject to extensive regulation by federal, state, and local authorities. Regulatory compliance is a critical aspect of our operations, impacting areas such as service offerings, pricing, and customer privacy. We work closely with regulatory bodies to ensure adherence to applicable laws and regulations, maintaining the highest standards of operational integrity.

Human Capital

As of December 31, 2024, Oral Communications, Inc. employed approximately 205,300 people. Our workforce is our most valuable asset, driving innovation, customer satisfaction, and operational excellence. We are committed to fostering a diverse and inclusive workplace, providing our employees with opportunities for professional growth and development.

Available Information

We are committed to transparency and timely communication with our stakeholders. Our website provides access to a wide range of information, including financial reports, press releases, and updates on company initiatives. Stakeholders can access our Annual Report on Form 10-K, quarterly reports on Form 10-Q, and current reports on Form 8-K, along with other filings made with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), through the SEC's website or our investor relations page.

In conclusion, Oral Communications, Inc. remains focused on delivering high-quality communication services that meet the evolving needs of our customers. Through strategic investments, operational excellence, and a commitment to innovation, we aim to drive long-term value for our stakeholders and maintain our position as a leader in the communication services industry.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Investing in our securities carries certain risks. The following discussion outlines the key risks that could adversely affect our business operations, financial condition, and results. These risks are not the only ones we face. Additional risks that are not known at this time or that are currently deemed immaterial could also impact our business. Consider these risks carefully before making any investment decisions regarding our securities.

Risks Related to Our Business and Industry

Highly Competitive Industry

We operate in a highly competitive communication services industry. Competition arises from both established telecommunications companies and emerging digital service providers offering similar services such as voice, data, and internet connectivity. Our ability to maintain and grow our market share depends on our capacity to provide innovative, high-quality services at competitive prices. Failure to effectively compete could result in reduced revenue and adversely affect our profitability.

Rapid Technological Change

The communication services sector is characterized by rapid technological advancements. To remain competitive, we must continuously invest in new technologies and infrastructure. This requires significant financial resources and strategic foresight. If we fail to anticipate or keep pace with technological changes, our products and services may become obsolete, potentially leading to a loss of customers and market share.

Dependence on Key Suppliers and Partners

Our operations rely on a network of suppliers and partners for essential components and services. Disruptions in the supply chain, whether due to geopolitical tensions, natural disasters, or other factors, could impede our ability to deliver services to customers. Additionally, if our partners fail to meet their contractual obligations, our service quality and reputation could suffer, impacting customer satisfaction and retention.

Risks Related to Our Operations

Network Infrastructure Reliability

Our business depends on the reliability and capacity of our network infrastructure. Any significant disruption, whether due to system failures, natural disasters, or cyber-attacks, could impair our ability to provide services, leading to customer dissatisfaction and potential financial losses. Ensuring the security and resilience of our network is critical to maintaining customer trust and protecting our revenue streams.

Human Capital Management

Our workforce of approximately 205,300 employees is integral to our success. Attracting, retaining, and motivating talented employees is essential to our operations. Failure to effectively manage human capital, including addressing skill gaps and fostering a diverse and inclusive workplace, could hinder our ability to execute our business strategies and achieve our objectives.

Operational Costs and Efficiency

Managing operational costs is crucial to maintaining our profitability. If we are unable to control expenses, particularly in areas such as network maintenance, employee compensation, and regulatory compliance, our financial performance could be adversely affected. Additionally, inefficiencies in our operations could lead to increased costs and reduced competitiveness.

Risks Related to Financing and Capital Structure

Leverage and Debt Servicing

We have a significant amount of outstanding debt, which requires substantial interest payments. Our ability to meet these obligations depends on our operating performance and cash flow generation. Adverse changes in market conditions or our financial performance could impact our ability to service our debt, potentially leading to increased borrowing costs or financial distress.

Capital Allocation and Investment

Effective capital allocation is essential for sustaining our growth and competitiveness. Decisions regarding investments in network infrastructure, technology, and strategic initiatives must be carefully considered to ensure they align with our long-term objectives. Poor investment decisions could result in financial losses or impair our ability to achieve desired returns on investment.

Access to Capital Markets

Our ability to finance operations and growth initiatives depends on access to capital markets. Economic downturns, changes in investor sentiment, or regulatory developments could restrict our access to financing or increase the cost of capital. Any inability to secure necessary funding could impede our growth plans and adversely affect our financial condition.

Risks Related to Legal and Regulatory Matters

Regulatory Compliance

The communication services industry is subject to extensive regulation by federal, state, and local authorities. Compliance with these regulations is critical to our operations and can affect various aspects of our business, including service offerings and pricing. Changes in regulatory requirements or failure to comply with existing regulations could result in fines, penalties, or other adverse consequences.

Data Privacy and Security

We handle a significant amount of customer data, making data privacy and security paramount. Any breach of our data protection measures could lead to unauthorized access to sensitive information, potentially resulting in legal liability, reputational damage, and loss of customer trust. Ensuring robust data security measures is essential to safeguarding our business and maintaining customer confidence.

Litigation and Legal Proceedings

We are subject to various legal proceedings and claims, which could result in substantial costs and divert management attention from our business operations. The outcome of litigation is inherently uncertain, and adverse rulings could have a material impact on our financial condition and operational results.

General Risks

Economic Conditions

Our business is sensitive to general economic conditions. Economic downturns or financial instability could lead to reduced consumer and business spending on communication services, adversely affecting our revenue and profitability. Additionally, fluctuations in foreign exchange rates and interest rates could impact our financial performance.

Natural Disasters and External Events

Natural disasters, pandemics, and geopolitical events could disrupt our operations or affect demand for our services. These events can have wide-ranging impacts, including supply chain disruptions, increased operational costs, and changes in consumer behavior. Our ability to effectively manage and mitigate these risks is crucial to maintaining business continuity.

Reputation and Brand Management

Our reputation is a valuable asset, influencing customer loyalty and brand strength. Any event that damages our reputation, such as service outages, data breaches, or negative publicity, could lead to customer attrition and financial losses. Proactively managing our reputation and ensuring consistent delivery of high-quality services is essential to maintaining customer trust and sustaining our competitive position.

In conclusion, while we strive to mitigate these risks through effective management and strategic initiatives, there can be no assurance that our efforts will be successful. Investors should carefully consider these risks, along with other information in this Annual Report on Form 10-K, before making investment decisions regarding our securities.

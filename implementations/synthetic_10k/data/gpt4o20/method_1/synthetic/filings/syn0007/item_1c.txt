Item 1C. Cybersecurity

Oral Communications, Inc. ("Oral" or the "Company") is committed to maintaining the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of our information assets through a comprehensive cybersecurity program. Our approach to cybersecurity is integral to our enterprise risk management framework, ensuring that we effectively identify, assess, and mitigate potential risks that could impact our operations and reputation.

Risk Management and Strategy

Our cybersecurity risk management strategy encompasses both technical and non-technical controls aimed at safeguarding our digital assets. We employ a multi-layered defense approach, incorporating advanced intrusion detection and prevention systems to secure our network perimeter. Endpoint protection through antivirus solutions and endpoint detection and response systems further enhances our ability to identify and mitigate threats at the device level.

In addition to technical defenses, we emphasize building a cybersecurity-aware culture within Oral. This includes mandatory annual cybersecurity training for all employees, frequent dissemination of security awareness information, and simulated phishing exercises to enhance employee vigilance against cyber threats. By fostering a culture of security awareness, we aim to empower our employees as a line of defense against potential threats.

We engage independent third-party organizations to conduct regular penetration testing and assessments of our cybersecurity practices. These evaluations are guided by industry standards and frameworks, such as those from the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST). Independent assessments enable us to benchmark our cybersecurity posture against best practices and implement improvements where necessary.

Our Cybersecurity Operations Center plays a critical role in monitoring and responding to security incidents. This facility provides continuous surveillance of our network environment to detect anomalies and potential threats. In the event of a cybersecurity incident, we have a well-defined Cybersecurity Incident Response Plan that establishes roles, responsibilities, and communication protocols to manage and mitigate the impact effectively.

Third-party risk management is an essential component of our cybersecurity strategy. We conduct thorough cybersecurity assessments of our vendors and partners during onboarding and contract renewals to ensure they meet our security standards. This process helps us manage risks associated with third-party service providers and ensures that they notify us promptly in case of any incidents that could impact our operations.

Governance

Cybersecurity governance at Oral is structured to ensure appropriate oversight and accountability. Our Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) is responsible for managing cybersecurity risks and strategies. The CISO leads a global team focused on protecting our information assets and maintaining the resilience of our systems.

The CISO reports directly to our Chief Information Officer and provides regular updates to our executive management team and board of directors. The board of directors, through its designated committee, plays an active role in overseeing our cybersecurity risk management efforts. This committee receives quarterly updates on our cybersecurity posture, threat environment, and progress on key initiatives to enhance our security framework.

The CISO at Oral brings extensive expertise in cybersecurity and risk management, with a background in leading security programs across various industries. This expertise is instrumental in guiding our strategic direction and ensuring that we remain at the forefront of cybersecurity practices.

Our governance framework also includes periodic engagements with external cybersecurity experts to provide additional insights and validation of our cybersecurity strategies. These collaborations enhance our understanding of the evolving threat landscape and allow us to adapt our defenses accordingly.

In conclusion, Oral Communications, Inc. is dedicated to protecting our digital assets and ensuring the security of our operations through a robust cybersecurity program. By integrating strong governance, proactive risk management, and a culture of security awareness, we are well-positioned to address the challenges posed by the dynamic cyber threat landscape. Our commitment to continuous improvement and adherence to best practices underpins our ability to safeguard our business and maintain trust with our stakeholders.

Item 1. Business

BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Novqua Retail, Inc. (together with its subsidiaries, "Novqua Retail," the "Company," "we," or "our") is a prominent entity in the consumer discretionary sector, focusing on delivering superior value in apparel and home goods. Headquartered in New York, New York, and incorporated in Delaware, Novqua Retail operates with a strategic emphasis on adaptability and customer satisfaction. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, we achieved total revenue of $51,238 million, demonstrating our commitment to providing exceptional products and services to our diverse customer base.

Our business model revolves around offering an extensive array of quality merchandise at competitive prices through a network of physical stores and our emerging online platform. This approach enables us to maintain a dynamic presence in the retail market, catering to varying consumer preferences and trends. Our mission is to meet the evolving demands of our customers by delivering innovative solutions and a compelling shopping experience.

PRINCIPAL PRODUCTS AND SERVICES

Novqua Retail specializes in a broad spectrum of consumer goods, primarily focusing on fashion apparel, accessories, and home furnishings. Our product offerings are designed to encompass a wide range of styles, preferences, and price points, allowing us to appeal to a broad demographic. We emphasize quality, style, and affordability across our product lines, ensuring that customers have access to desirable merchandise that aligns with current market trends.

In addition to our core product categories, we continuously explore new product introductions and lines to diversify our offerings and enhance our market position. This strategy allows us to remain competitive and relevant in an ever-evolving retail environment.

The Apparel and Accessories segment is a cornerstone of our business, offering a comprehensive range of clothing, footwear, and fashion accessories for men, women, and children. By offering a wide selection of furniture, lighting, textiles, and decorative pieces, we aim to inspire creativity and personalization in home design. E-commerce Segment

Item 1C. Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity Governance

Novqua Retail, Inc. is committed to maintaining a robust cybersecurity framework to safeguard our information assets and ensure business continuity. Our cybersecurity governance structure is led by the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO), who brings extensive experience in managing complex security environments. The CISO is responsible for overseeing the Company's cybersecurity strategy and risk management efforts. This role is supported by a dedicated Information Security team, which operates across several key functional areas, including network security, application security, incident response, and governance, risk, and compliance (GRC).

Our Board of Directors holds ultimate responsibility for overseeing cybersecurity risk management. The Board is supported by its Audit Committee, which is tasked with monitoring cybersecurity risks and strategies. The Audit Committee receives regular updates from the CISO and the Company's Chief Information Officer (CIO), ensuring that the Board is informed of cybersecurity risks, incidents, and mitigation strategies. These updates cover a range of topics, including threat intelligence, incident response readiness, and compliance with cybersecurity policies and regulations.

Cybersecurity Strategy and Risk Management

The Information Security team at Novqua Retail employs a comprehensive risk management approach to identify, assess, and mitigate cybersecurity threats. We utilize industry-standard frameworks to evaluate potential risks, determining their likelihood and potential impact on our operations. Risk assessments are conducted regularly, and the results are used to prioritize resources and efforts to address the most significant threats.

An essential component of our cybersecurity strategy is the implementation of a GRC platform, which enables continuous monitoring of identified risks and the effectiveness of mitigation strategies. This platform facilitates the tracking of risk assessments, control measures, and any remediation activities necessary to address vulnerabilities.

To strengthen our cybersecurity posture, we conduct regular security reviews and penetration tests. These assessments, often performed with the assistance of third-party consultants, help us identify vulnerabilities in our systems and applications. The insights gained from these reviews inform our security enhancements and strategic planning.

In the event of a potential cybersecurity incident, we have established a detailed Cybersecurity Incident Response Plan (CIRP). This plan outlines the criteria for identifying a qualifying cybersecurity incident, the process for escalation, and the roles and responsibilities of the incident response team. The team includes representatives from the Information Security team, senior management, and the legal department, ensuring a coordinated response to any incident.

Should an incident be classified as material, it is escalated to the appropriate leadership team, including the general counsel and the Audit Committee. The CIRP provides guidelines for determining the materiality of an incident and outlines the necessary steps for communication and remediation. All incidents, regardless of their materiality, are logged and tracked within our incident response platform to ensure continuous monitoring and improvement of our security practices.

We recognize the importance of managing cybersecurity risks associated with third-party service providers. To this end, we have established a process for evaluating the security posture of our third-party partners. This process involves assessing the vendor's security practices, compliance with industry standards, and their ability to protect our data. While we work diligently to ensure that our third-party partners maintain strong security measures, we acknowledge that we cannot control all aspects of their operations.

Our cybersecurity strategy includes ongoing efforts to educate and train our employees on security best practices. All employees are required to complete annual cybersecurity training and certify their understanding of the Company's Cybersecurity Policy. This policy mandates that employees report any potential security incidents immediately to the Information Security team. By fostering a culture of security awareness, we aim to empower our employees to act as the first line of defense against cyber threats.

Cybersecurity Threat Landscape

The threat landscape in which Novqua Retail operates is constantly evolving, with cyber threats growing in sophistication and frequency. We are vigilant in monitoring emerging threats and adapting our security measures accordingly. Our threat intelligence capabilities are bolstered by partnerships with industry organizations and government agencies, which allow us to stay informed of the latest threat vectors and trends.

Despite our robust cybersecurity measures, we acknowledge that no system is entirely immune to cyber threats. As such, we continually evaluate and enhance our security controls to mitigate potential risks to our systems and data. Our commitment to cybersecurity resilience is unwavering, and we remain dedicated to protecting our Company's assets and ensuring the trust of our customers and stakeholders.

As of the date of this report, there are no known cybersecurity incidents that have materially impacted Novqua Retail. However, as highlighted in our "Risk Factors" section, the potential for cyber-attacks to disrupt our operations or compromise sensitive information remains a significant risk. We are prepared to respond swiftly and effectively to any such incidents, minimizing their impact on our business.

In conclusion, Novqua Retail is dedicated to maintaining a proactive and comprehensive approach to cybersecurity risk management. Through vigilant oversight, strategic investment in security technologies, and ongoing education and training, we strive to protect our information assets and uphold the integrity of our operations. Our commitment to cybersecurity is integral to our mission of delivering exceptional value to our customers and sustaining our position as a leading retailer in the consumer discretionary sector.

Overview

Novqua Retail, Inc. is a prominent player in the consumer discretionary sector, focused on providing quality merchandise across a network of physical stores and an expanding online platform. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, we reported total revenue of $51,238 million, a decrease of 1.4% from $51,976 million in 2023. This slight decline reflects the challenging retail environment and competitive pressures, as well as shifts in consumer spending patterns. Despite these challenges, we remain committed to delivering exceptional value to our customers, supported by a diverse product offering and strategic market positioning.

Our gross margin for 2024 was 29.3%, down from 30.3% in 2023, primarily due to increased competitive pricing strategies and changes in product mix. We continue to focus on maintaining operational efficiencies and cost management to support our profitability objectives. Our operating income for 2024 was $4,155 million, reflecting a decrease of 10.8% from $4,659 million in 2023, primarily driven by a reduction in gross profit and targeted investments in our selling, general, and administrative functions.

Results of Operations

Revenue

For the fiscal year 2024, revenue decreased by 1.4% to $51,238 million from $51,976 million in 2023. This decline was influenced by several factors, including heightened competition in the retail sector and evolving consumer preferences towards e-commerce platforms. While we have made significant strides in expanding our online presence, the transition from traditional retail formats to digital channels continues to be a focal point of our strategic initiatives.

Cost of Revenue and Gross Margin

Cost of revenue remained relatively stable, decreasing slightly to $36,205 million in 2024 from $36,235 million in 2023. This stability reflects our ongoing efforts to manage supplier relationships and optimize inventory levels amid fluctuating market conditions. As a result of the decreased revenue and stable cost of revenue, our gross margin declined to 29.3% in 2024 from 30.3% in the previous year.

Selling, General and Administrative Expenses

Selling, general, and administrative (SG&A) expenses were $7,804 million in 2024, representing a decrease from $7,963 million in 2023. The decrease in SG&A expenses is attributed to cost-control measures and the streamlining of operations to enhance efficiency. These efforts have been integral in offsetting some of the pressures on gross margin and operating income.

Other Operating Expenses

Other operating expenses totaled $3,074 million in 2024, down from $3,119 million in 2023. This decrease was driven by reductions in certain discretionary spending categories and efforts to optimize operational processes. We continue to evaluate our cost structure to ensure alignment with our strategic objectives and market conditions.

Operating Income

Operating income for 2024 was $4,155 million, reflecting a decrease of 10.8% compared to $4,659 million in 2023. The decline in operating income is primarily due to the reduction in gross profit, partially offset by the effective management of operating expenses. We remain focused on driving operational efficiencies and leveraging technology to enhance productivity.

Interest Expense

Interest expense increased slightly to $420 million in 2024 from $413 million in 2023. This increase is attributable to higher borrowing costs associated with our long-term debt, reflecting changes in market interest rates. We continue to monitor our capital structure to ensure optimal financing costs.

Effective Tax Rate

Our effective tax rate for 2024 was 17.6%, consistent with the rate in 2023. We maintain a proactive approach to tax planning and compliance, ensuring alignment with applicable regulations and maximizing tax efficiencies.

Net Income

Net income for 2024 was $3,077 million, representing a decrease of 12.0% from $3,498 million in 2023. This decline reflects the combined impact of decreased operating income and slightly higher interest expenses. We remain committed to enhancing our profitability through strategic initiatives focused on revenue growth and cost management.

As of December 31, 2024, we had cash and cash equivalents totaling $2,896 million, compared to $3,008 million at the end of 2023. The decrease in cash and cash equivalents reflects our capital allocation priorities, including investments in growth initiatives and returns to shareholders through dividends and share repurchases.

This cash flow was primarily driven by our net income of $3,077 million and adjustments for non-cash items, including depreciation and amortization of $1,455 million. Effective working capital management also contributed to strong operating cash flows.

These investments are critical to supporting our growth strategy and enhancing operational capabilities.

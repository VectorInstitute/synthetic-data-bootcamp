Item 1C. CYBERSECURITY
We maintain a cybersecurity program and framework as set forth in our cybersecurity policies and standards. The foundation of our cybersecurity program is based on the National Institute of Standards and Technology ("NIST") Cybersecurity Framework, which includes a set of controls to prevent, detect, and respond to cybersecurity threats and incidents. These controls include constant monitoring, log collection and analysis, threat hunting and intelligence surveillance, and regular vulnerability scans/penetration tests. Additionally, in furtherance of assessing, identifying and managing material cybersecurity risks, we:
•Leverage technology solutions, including proactive detection tools, to protect our assets and detect threats in our environment;
•Perform regular internal assessments of our cybersecurity program against the NIST Cybersecurity Framework. The results of these assessments are then reviewed and, based on such findings, action plans are developed and progress tracked through completion;
•Analyze both internal and external cybersecurity incidents and related threat intelligence to determine applicability to our environment and industry. Findings from such analyses are then reviewed and utilized to create action plans where applicable and relevant to our environment and industry;
•Maintain an enterprise-wide disaster recovery governance program, which includes cybersecurity-related disaster recovery standards and compliance procedures related thereto;
•Regularly perform cybersecurity-related disaster recovery testing to ensure that the Company's mission-critical systems are recoverable, in support of the business continuity needs of our various business lines;
•Maintain an operational technology (OT) security program to address cyber risks that are inherent and unique to our industry and manufacturing environment;
•Maintain a centralized product security program that unifies company-wide strategy to ensure our customer-facing products and services are secure by design; and
•Integrate each of our business and corporate groups with our internal cybersecurity team to ensure cybersecurity requirements are embedded into operating environments as appropriate, which drives business strategies, budgeting, and similar processes. In addition, senior and executive management, as well as our Board of Directors, regularly review our financial planning processes for these areas, inclusive of our cybersecurity programs.
Any changes or additions to our cybersecurity program and related practices and procedures described above in response to cybersecurity needs are reviewed by our executive management, Board of Directors and Audit Committee.
We regularly engage independent third-parties and auditors to assess our cybersecurity program and practices and assist in the mitigation of risk. The effectiveness of our cybersecurity environment is regularly tested by internal personnel and these third-parties. These assessments are performed in connection with standards and requirements under the Payment Card Industry (PCI) data security standard, Sarbanes-Oxley Act (SOX), and the U.S. Department of Defense, cybersecurity capability maturity benchmarking and voluntary certifications by us, such as the Service Organization Control Type 2 (SOC 2). The results of these audits and assessments are promptly reviewed and enhancements are made to our cybersecurity program and practices based on such findings as appropriate. We also maintain a cybersecurity third party risk management program which evaluates third parties that either host or have access to our data and/or systems to ensure that they are aligned with our security requirements. The program includes initial review, ongoing monitoring and contractual agreements with cybersecurity requirements to ensure third party services meet our standards for such providers, and the cybersecurity risks associated with the use of these services is acceptable.
Like other comparable-sized companies, our information technology systems, networks and infrastructure and technology embedded in certain of our control products have been and may continue to be vulnerable to cyber-attacks and unauthorized security intrusions. These types of attacks may include computer viruses, malicious code, unauthorized access, phishing attempts, denial-of-service attacks, among others. For more information about these and other cybersecurity risks faced by us, see Part IA, Item 1A, "Risk Factors - Risks Related to Cybersecurity and Technology."
Our Board of Directors has ultimate oversight for risks relating to our cybersecurity program and practices and receives regular updates from our internal cybersecurity team on cybersecurity risks and threats. In addition, our Audit Committee provides Board-level oversight for management's actions with respect to practices, procedures and controls used to identify, assess and manage our key cybersecurity programs and risks. Item 1. Business

General

Venzen Industries, Inc., headquartered in Charlotte, North Carolina, is a prominent entity in the Industrials sector, specializing in the design, manufacture, and distribution of advanced industrial solutions and equipment. Founded in the state of Delaware, Venzen Industries has consistently maintained its commitment to innovation, quality, and customer satisfaction. The company operates with a strategic focus on providing cutting-edge solutions that cater to a diverse range of industrial applications.

With an extensive portfolio that includes products and services aimed at enhancing operational efficiency and reliability, Venzen Industries is dedicated to delivering superior value to its stakeholders. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, the company reported a total revenue of $48,137 million, reflecting its strong market position and the effective execution of its business strategies.

Principal Products and Services

Venzen Industries offers a comprehensive suite of industrial solutions, which are categorized into several principal areas:

1. Industrial Equipment: The company provides a wide range of machinery and equipment designed for various industrial applications, including manufacturing, construction, and energy production. These products are engineered to deliver high performance, durability, and cost-effectiveness.

2. Automation Solutions: Venzen Industries is at the forefront of integrating automation technologies into industrial operations. The company offers advanced control systems and software solutions that enhance productivity, streamline processes, and ensure precision in critical applications.

3. Maintenance and Support Services: Recognizing the importance of operational continuity, Venzen Industries provides extensive maintenance and support services. These services encompass preventive maintenance, repair, and technical support to ensure the optimal performance of its products throughout their lifecycle.

Venzen Industries conducts its operations through three primary reportable segments, each tailored to address specific market needs and opportunities:

2. Recovery from economic recessions can be slow and may not coincide with improvements in our business.

The industrial sector in which we operate is highly competitive, with numerous companies vying for market share. We face competition from both established players and emerging companies that offer similar products and services. Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Overview

Venzen Industries, Inc. ("Venzen" or the "Company"), headquartered in Charlotte, North Carolina, is a leading player in the Industrials sector, providing innovative solutions across a broad spectrum of industrial applications. The fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, was marked by significant achievements and challenges as we navigated through a dynamic market environment. Our commitment to operational excellence, strategic investments, and customer-centric solutions enabled us to achieve strong financial performance, as reflected in our improved revenue, profitability, and cash flow metrics.

In fiscal 2024, Venzen generated revenue of $48,137 million, representing an 8.0% increase over the previous year. This growth was driven by robust demand across our segments, particularly in Manufacturing Solutions and Infrastructure and Construction, as well as our continued focus on innovation and customer engagement. Our diversified product portfolio and strategic market positioning allowed us to capitalize on emerging opportunities while mitigating the impact of external challenges, such as supply chain disruptions and macroeconomic uncertainties.

Results of Operations

Revenue

In fiscal 2024, Venzen recorded revenue of $48,137 million, an increase of $3,570 million, or 8.0%, from $44,567 million in fiscal 2023. This growth was primarily attributed to strong sales performance in our key segments, driven by increased demand for industrial equipment and automation solutions. The Manufacturing Solutions segment experienced significant growth due to heightened investment in production capacity by industrial clients. Additionally, the Infrastructure and Construction segment benefited from increased infrastructure spending, leading to higher demand for our construction equipment and services.

Cost of Revenue and Gross Margin

The cost of revenue for fiscal 2024 was $30,909 million, up from $28,726 million in fiscal 2023. This increase of $2,183 million was largely in line with the growth in revenue, reflecting higher production volumes and increased raw material costs. Despite these cost pressures, our gross profit improved to $17,228 million in fiscal 2024, compared to $15,841 million in the prior year. The gross margin for fiscal 2024 was 35.8%, a slight increase from 35.5% in fiscal 2023. This improvement was driven by operational efficiencies, strategic sourcing initiatives, and favorable product mix, partially offset by rising input costs.

Operating Expenses

Research and Development

Our investment in research and development (R&D) increased to $2,036 million in fiscal 2024, up from $1,912 million in the previous year. This 6.5% increase underscores our commitment to innovation and maintaining a competitive edge in the market. R&D expenses accounted for 4.2% of total revenue in fiscal 2024, reflecting our focus on developing advanced technologies and enhancing our product offerings to meet evolving customer needs.

Selling, General and Administrative

Selling, general and administrative (SG&A) expenses were $6,292 million in fiscal 2024, compared to $5,812 million in fiscal 2023. The increase of $480 million, or 8.3%, was primarily due to higher personnel costs, expanded marketing efforts, and investments in digital transformation initiatives. SG&A expenses represented 13.1% of revenue in fiscal 2024, slightly up from 13.0% in the prior year, as we continued to invest in enhancing our customer engagement and operational capabilities.

Other Operating Expense

Other operating expenses amounted to $2,888 million in fiscal 2024, an increase from $2,674 million in fiscal 2023. This 8.0% increase was driven by higher costs associated with compliance and regulatory requirements, as well as increased provisions for contingencies and warranties. We continue to closely monitor these expenses to ensure alignment with our operational objectives and risk management strategies.

Operating Income

Operating income for fiscal 2024 was $6,012 million, an increase of $569 million, or 10.5%, compared to $5,443 million in fiscal 2023. The improvement in operating income was driven by revenue growth and disciplined cost management across our business segments. Our operating margin improved to 12.5% in fiscal 2024, up from 12.2% in the prior year, reflecting our efforts to enhance operational efficiency and profitability.

Interest Expense

Interest expense increased to $420 million in fiscal 2024, compared to $367 million in fiscal 2023. This increase of 14.4% was primarily due to higher levels of borrowings to support strategic investments and capital expenditures. We continue to manage our interest expense through effective debt structuring and favorable interest rate negotiations.

Provision for Income Taxes and Effective Tax Rate

The provision for income taxes was $1,186 million in fiscal 2024, compared to $1,142 million in fiscal 2023. Our effective tax rate decreased to 21.2% in fiscal 2024 from 22.5% in the prior year. The reduction in the effective tax rate was primarily due to favorable changes in tax legislation and the implementation of tax optimization strategies. We remain committed to ensuring tax compliance while optimizing our overall tax position.

Net Income

Net income for fiscal 2024 was $4,406 million, an increase of $472 million, or 12.0%, compared to $3,934 million in fiscal 2023. This improvement reflects our strong operating performance and effective cost management, as well as the benefits of a lower effective tax rate. Our net margin increased to 9.2% in fiscal 2024, up from 8.8% in the prior year, underscoring our ability to deliver sustainable profitability.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

Our cash and cash equivalents at the end of fiscal 2024 were $13,825 million, compared to $12,038 million at the end of fiscal 2023. The increase of $1,787 million reflects our strong cash generation from operating activities, as well as our prudent approach to cash management and capital allocation.

Net cash provided by operating activities in fiscal 2024 was $5,817 million, driven by strong net income and effective working capital management. Depreciation and amortization expenses contributed $785 million to cash flow, while changes in working capital and other items provided an additional $626 million. Our focus on optimizing cash flow from operations remains a key priority to support our strategic initiatives and enhance shareholder value.

Investing Activities

Net cash used in investing activities was $3,782 million in fiscal 2024, primarily reflecting investments in property, plant, and equipment of $823 million, as well as other investing activities totaling $2,959 million. These investments are aligned with our strategic priorities to enhance production capacity, drive innovation, and support long-term growth. We continue to evaluate investment opportunities with a focus on maximizing returns and supporting our business objectives.

Financing Activities

Net cash used in financing activities was $248 million in fiscal 2024. Key components included net changes in long-term debt, which increased by $1,188 million, and dividends paid of $2,397 million. Additionally, we repurchased shares, net of issuances, totaling $961 million.

Item 1. Business

BUSINESS

Overview

Merqua Land, Inc. ("Merqua" or the "Company"), incorporated in the state of Delaware, is a prominent entity in the real estate sector. Headquartered in Chicago, Illinois, Merqua specializes in the acquisition, development, and management of a diverse range of real estate properties across North America. The Company is dedicated to generating sustainable value for its shareholders through a strategic focus on quality assets, operational excellence, and disciplined financial management. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, Merqua reported total revenues of $9,373 million.

Principal Products and Services

Merqua's primary business activities encompass the development and management of residential, commercial, and mixed-use properties. The Company engages in the leasing, management, and operation of these properties to ensure optimal performance and tenant satisfaction. Merqua's portfolio includes a variety of property types such as residential complexes, office buildings, retail centers, and industrial properties. By offering a comprehensive suite of real estate services, Merqua is able to meet the evolving needs of its diverse clientele.

Reportable Segments

Merqua operates through three reportable segments: Residential Properties, Commercial Properties, and Mixed-Use Developments. Each segment is strategically designed to leverage Merqua's core competencies and capitalize on market opportunities.

Residential Properties

The Residential Properties segment focuses on the development and management of multi-family housing units. These properties include high-rise and mid-rise apartment buildings, condominium complexes, and townhouses. Merqua aims to provide high-quality living environments that cater to a wide range of demographics, from young professionals to families and retirees. The Company emphasizes modern design, amenities, and sustainability in its residential projects to enhance tenant satisfaction and retention.

Commercial Properties

In the Commercial Properties segment, Merqua invests in and manages office buildings, retail spaces, and industrial facilities. These properties are strategically located in urban and suburban areas to attract a diverse range of tenants, including corporate clients, retailers, and logistics companies. Merqua is committed to maintaining high occupancy rates and maximizing the value of its commercial assets through proactive leasing strategies and efficient property management practices.

Mixed-Use Developments

The Mixed-Use Developments segment integrates residential, commercial, and recreational spaces into cohesive communities. These developments are designed to create vibrant, self-sustaining environments that offer convenience and connectivity for residents and businesses alike. Merqua's mixed-use projects often include features such as retail shops, dining establishments, office spaces, and public amenities. By fostering a sense of community and promoting urban living, Merqua enhances the appeal and value of its mixed-use developments.

Customers

Merqua's customer base is diverse, encompassing a wide range of tenants and property buyers. The Company's residential tenants include individuals and families seeking quality housing in desirable locations. Commercial tenants range from small businesses to large corporations seeking office or retail space. Additionally, Merqua's customers include property investors and developers seeking to acquire or partner in new real estate projects. The Company's commitment to customer satisfaction and long-term relationships is a cornerstone of its business strategy.

Sales and Marketing

Merqua employs a multi-faceted sales and marketing strategy to attract and retain tenants across its property portfolio. The Company utilizes digital marketing, social media, and traditional advertising channels to reach potential tenants and buyers. Additionally, Merqua leverages partnerships with real estate brokers and agents to expand its market reach. The Company's sales and marketing efforts are supported by a dedicated team that focuses on customer engagement, brand building, and market analysis to ensure optimal property performance.

Competition

The real estate industry is highly competitive, with numerous players vying for market share. Merqua competes with other real estate developers, property management firms, and investment companies in acquiring, developing, and leasing properties. Competitors range from large national firms to regional and local players. Merqua differentiates itself through its commitment to quality, strategic location selection, and innovative property designs. The Company's ability to adapt to market trends and customer preferences is critical to maintaining its competitive edge.

Seasonality

Merqua's business operations are subject to seasonal fluctuations, which can impact leasing activity and property management. Residential leasing typically experiences higher demand during the spring and summer months, while commercial leasing activity may vary based on economic conditions and corporate planning cycles. Merqua actively manages its leasing strategies and operational schedules to mitigate the effects of seasonality and optimize property performance throughout the year.

Intellectual Property

Merqua's intellectual property consists of proprietary property management systems, design templates, and operational processes developed to enhance efficiency and tenant satisfaction. The Company continually invests in technology and innovation to improve its real estate offerings and maintain a competitive advantage. Merqua's intellectual property is protected through industry-standard measures, including confidentiality agreements and trademark registrations where applicable.

Government Regulation

The real estate industry is subject to extensive government regulation at the federal, state, and local levels. Regulations affecting Merqua's operations include zoning laws, building codes, environmental regulations, and property tax policies. Compliance with these regulations is essential to the successful development and management of Merqua's properties. The Company maintains a dedicated compliance team to ensure adherence to applicable laws and regulations and to navigate the complexities of the regulatory environment.

Human Capital

Merqua recognizes that its employees are vital to the Company's success. As of December 31, 2024, Merqua employed approximately 16,800 individuals across various functions, including property management, development, sales, marketing, finance, and administration. The Company is committed to fostering a diverse and inclusive workforce that supports professional growth and development. Merqua invests in training programs, employee engagement initiatives, and talent retention strategies to cultivate a motivated and skilled workforce.

Available Information

Merqua Land, Inc. provides access to its financial and operational information through various channels. The Company files annual, quarterly, and current reports with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission ("SEC"), which are accessible on the SEC's website. Additionally, Merqua maintains a corporate website where investors, analysts, and the general public can access press releases, investor presentations, and other relevant information. The Company is committed to transparency and timely communication with its stakeholders.

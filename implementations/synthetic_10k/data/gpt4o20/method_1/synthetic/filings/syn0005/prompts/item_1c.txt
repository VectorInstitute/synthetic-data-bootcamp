Item 1. Business

BUSINESS

Overview

Merqua Land, Inc. ("Merqua" or the "Company"), incorporated in the state of Delaware, is a prominent entity in the real estate sector. Headquartered in Chicago, Illinois, Merqua specializes in the acquisition, development, and management of a diverse range of real estate properties across North America. The Company is dedicated to generating sustainable value for its shareholders through a strategic focus on quality assets, operational excellence, and disciplined financial management. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, Merqua reported total revenues of $9,373 million.

Principal Products and Services

Merqua's primary business activities encompass the development and management of residential, commercial, and mixed-use properties. The Company engages in the leasing, management, and operation of these properties to ensure optimal performance and tenant satisfaction. Merqua's portfolio includes a variety of property types such as residential complexes, office buildings, retail centers, and industrial properties. By offering a comprehensive suite of real estate services, Merqua is able to meet the evolving needs of its diverse clientele.

Reportable Segments

Merqua operates through three reportable segments: Residential Properties, Commercial Properties, and Mixed-Use Developments. Each segment is strategically designed to leverage Merqua's core competencies and capitalize on market opportunities.

Residential Properties

The Residential Properties segment focuses on the development and management of multi-family housing units. These properties include high-rise and mid-rise apartment buildings, condominium complexes, and townhouses. Merqua aims to provide high-quality living environments that cater to a wide range of demographics, from young professionals to families and retirees. The Company emphasizes modern design, amenities, and sustainability in its residential projects to enhance tenant satisfaction and retention.

Commercial Properties

In the Commercial Properties segment, Merqua invests in and manages office buildings, retail spaces, and industrial facilities. These properties are strategically located in urban and suburban areas to attract a diverse range of tenants, including corporate clients, retailers, and logistics companies. Merqua is committed to maintaining high occupancy rates and maximizing the value of its commercial assets through proactive leasing strategies and efficient property management practices.

Mixed-Use Developments

The Mixed-Use Developments segment integrates residential, commercial, and recreational spaces into cohesive communities. These developments are designed to create vibrant, self-sustaining environments that offer convenience and connectivity for residents and businesses alike. Merqua's mixed-use projects often include features such as retail shops, dining establishments, office spaces, and public amenities. By fostering a sense of community and promoting urban living, Merqua enhances the appeal and value of its mixed-use developments.

Customers

Merqua's customer base is diverse, encompassing a wide range of tenants and property buyers. The Company's residential tenants include individuals and families seeking quality housing in desirable locations. Commercial tenants range from small businesses to large corporations seeking office or retail space. Additionally, Merqua's customers include property investors and developers seeking to acquire or partner in new real estate projects. The Company's commitment to customer satisfaction and long-term relationships is a cornerstone of its business strategy.

Sales and Marketing

Merqua employs a multi-faceted sales and marketing strategy to attract and retain tenants across its property portfolio. The Company utilizes digital marketing, social media, and traditional advertising channels to reach potential tenants and buyers. Additionally, Merqua leverages partnerships with real estate brokers and agents to expand its market reach. The Company's sales and marketing efforts are supported by a dedicated team that focuses on customer engagement, brand building, and market analysis to ensure optimal property performance.

Competition

The real estate industry is highly competitive, with numerous players vying for market share. Merqua competes with other real estate developers, property management firms, and investment companies in acquiring, developing, and leasing properties. Competitors range from large national firms to regional and local players. Merqua differentiates itself through its commitment to quality, strategic location selection, and innovative property designs. The Company's ability to adapt to market trends and customer preferences is critical to maintaining its competitive edge.

Seasonality

Merqua's business operations are subject to seasonal fluctuations, which can impact leasing activity and property management. Residential leasing typically experiences higher demand during the spring and summer months, while commercial leasing activity may vary based on economic conditions and corporate planning cycles. Merqua actively manages its leasing strategies and operational schedules to mitigate the effects of seasonality and optimize property performance throughout the year.

Intellectual Property

Merqua's intellectual property consists of proprietary property management systems, design templates, and operational processes developed to enhance efficiency and tenant satisfaction. The Company continually invests in technology and innovation to improve its real estate offerings and maintain a competitive advantage. Merqua's intellectual property is protected through industry-standard measures, including confidentiality agreements and trademark registrations where applicable.

Government Regulation

The real estate industry is subject to extensive government regulation at the federal, state, and local levels. Regulations affecting Merqua's operations include zoning laws, building codes, environmental regulations, and property tax policies. Compliance with these regulations is essential to the successful development and management of Merqua's properties. The Company maintains a dedicated compliance team to ensure adherence to applicable laws and regulations and to navigate the complexities of the regulatory environment.

Human Capital

Merqua recognizes that its employees are vital to the Company's success. As of December 31, 2024, Merqua employed approximately 16,800 individuals across various functions, including property management, development, sales, marketing, finance, and administration. The Company is committed to fostering a diverse and inclusive workforce that supports professional growth and development. Merqua invests in training programs, employee engagement initiatives, and talent retention strategies to cultivate a motivated and skilled workforce.

Available Information

Merqua Land, Inc. provides access to its financial and operational information through various channels. The Company files annual, quarterly, and current reports with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission ("SEC"), which are accessible on the SEC's website. Additionally, Merqua maintains a corporate website where investors, analysts, and the general public can access press releases, investor presentations, and other relevant information. The Company is committed to transparency and timely communication with its stakeholders.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Merqua Land, Inc. ("Merqua" or the "Company") faces a variety of risks in its business operations and financial endeavors. These risks, which are inherent to the real estate industry and the Company's specific business model, could adversely affect Merqua's financial condition, results of operations, and cash flows. Potential investors should carefully consider these risks before making any investment decisions. The following discussion outlines the material risks identified by the Company.

Risks Related to Our Business and Industry

Fluctuations in Real Estate Market Conditions 
The real estate market is inherently cyclical and influenced by a variety of factors, including economic conditions, interest rates, and consumer confidence. Downturns in the real estate market can lead to decreased demand for leasing and property sales, which may adversely impact the Company's revenue and profitability. Merqua's success depends on its ability to navigate these market fluctuations and adjust its strategies accordingly.

Intense Competition in the Real Estate Sector
The real estate industry is highly competitive, with numerous firms vying for market share in property acquisition, development, and management. Merqua faces competition from both large national companies and smaller regional firms. The Company's ability to maintain its competitive edge depends on its capacity to offer unique, high-quality properties and maintain strong relationships with tenants and investors. Failure to compete effectively could result in decreased occupancy rates and rental income.

Dependence on Strategic Property Locations
Merqua's success is significantly influenced by the location of its properties. Properties in desirable locations typically attract more tenants and higher rental rates. If Merqua is unable to acquire properties in strategic locations or if there is a shift in demand away from its current property locations, the Company's financial performance could suffer. Additionally, changes in local economic conditions or demographics can impact property values and rental demand.

Risks Related to Operations

Operational Challenges in Property Management
The efficient management of properties is critical to maintaining tenant satisfaction and maximizing revenue. Operational challenges, such as maintenance issues, tenant disputes, or disruptions in utility services, could negatively affect the Company's reputation and financial performance. Merqua must ensure that its property management processes are robust and responsive to potential issues to minimize operational risks.

Dependence on Key Personnel
Merqua's operations rely heavily on the expertise and efforts of its management team and key personnel. The loss of key executives or the inability to attract and retain skilled employees could disrupt operations and hinder the Company's strategic initiatives. Merqua's ability to achieve its business objectives depends on having a talented and motivated workforce aligned with the Company's goals.

Risks Related to Financing and Capital Structure

Substantial Debt Obligations
Merqua's capital structure includes significant debt obligations, which require regular interest and principal payments. Adverse changes in interest rates or the Company's financial performance could increase borrowing costs or limit access to additional capital. High levels of debt may also restrict the Company's operational flexibility and ability to pursue new investment opportunities.

Reliance on External Financing
The Company's growth strategy is dependent on securing external financing for property acquisitions and developments. An inability to obtain financing on favorable terms or a deterioration in credit market conditions could adversely affect Merqua's ability to execute its growth plans. The Company's future success depends on its ability to access sufficient capital to fund its strategic initiatives.

Risks Related to Legal and Regulatory Matters

Compliance with Government Regulations
The real estate industry is subject to extensive regulation at federal, state, and local levels. Compliance with zoning laws, environmental regulations, and building codes is essential for the successful development and management of properties. Changes in regulations or non-compliance could result in fines, legal liabilities, or project delays, adversely impacting the Company's operations and financial results.

Environmental Liability Risks
Merqua is exposed to environmental risks associated with its real estate holdings. Contamination or violations of environmental laws could result in significant remediation costs and legal liabilities. The Company must actively manage environmental risks through regular assessments and compliance with applicable regulations to mitigate potential impacts on its business.

General Risks

Economic Volatility and Global Uncertainty
Merqua's business is influenced by broader economic conditions, including inflation, unemployment rates, and geopolitical events. Economic volatility can impact consumer spending and business investment, affecting demand for real estate. Global uncertainties, such as trade tensions or political instability, could also indirectly affect the Company's operations and financial performance.

Impact of Pandemics and Health Crises
Pandemics and health crises, such as the COVID-19 pandemic, can have far-reaching impacts on the real estate industry. Such events may lead to decreased leasing activity, rental income disruptions, and increased operational costs due to health and safety measures. Merqua must be prepared to address such challenges and adapt its business practices to ensure continuity and resilience.

Technological Changes and Cybersecurity Risks
The increasing reliance on technology in real estate operations and management exposes Merqua to cybersecurity risks. Cyberattacks or data breaches could compromise sensitive information, disrupt operations, and damage the Company's reputation. Merqua must invest in robust cybersecurity measures and stay abreast of technological advancements to protect its operations and maintain a competitive edge.

This comprehensive overview of risk factors outlines the various challenges that Merqua Land, Inc. faces in its business environment. The Company remains committed to identifying, assessing, and mitigating these risks to ensure sustained growth and value creation for its shareholders.

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

The following discussion and analysis should be read in conjunction with the consolidated financial statements and related notes included in this Annual Report. The discussion provides insights into the financial condition and results of operations of Merqua Land, Inc. ("Merqua" or the "Company") for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, with comparisons to the previous fiscal year ended December 31, 2023.

Overview

Merqua is a leader in the real estate sector, focusing on the acquisition, development, and management of residential, commercial, and mixed-use properties in North America. The Company is committed to delivering sustainable value to its shareholders by leveraging high-quality assets, operational excellence, and disciplined financial management. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, Merqua reported total revenues of $9,373 million, a decrease of 2.2% compared to the prior year. This decrease in revenue reflects the challenges and opportunities encountered during the year.

Results of Operations

Revenue

For the fiscal year 2024, Merqua reported revenue of $9,373 million, a decrease of 2.2% from $9,587 million in fiscal 2023. This decline in revenue was primarily driven by a reduction in leasing activity in certain segments, influenced by competitive pressures and shifts in tenant preferences. Despite a challenging market environment, the Company maintained its focus on optimizing tenant retention and pursuing strategic leasing opportunities across its diverse portfolio.

Cost of Revenue and Gross Margin

Cost of revenue for fiscal 2024 was $7,118 million, representing a decrease of $155 million, or 2.1%, from the previous year's cost of $7,273 million. This reduction was achieved through cost management initiatives and operational efficiencies across the property management functions. The gross profit for fiscal 2024 was $2,255 million, a decrease of 2.6% compared to $2,314 million in fiscal 2023. The gross margin remained stable at 24.1% in both fiscal 2024 and 2023, indicating consistent operational performance amidst revenue fluctuations.

Selling, General and Administrative Expenses

Selling, general and administrative (SG&A) expenses totaled $1,469 million in fiscal 2024, a decrease of $12 million or 0.8% from $1,481 million in fiscal 2023. This reduction was primarily due to cost-saving measures and a streamlined administrative structure. SG&A expenses as a percentage of revenue remained relatively stable at 15.7% in fiscal 2024 compared to 15.4% in the prior year.

Other Operating Expenses

Other operating expenses amounted to $562 million for fiscal 2024, a decrease of $13 million or 2.3% from $575 million in fiscal 2023. The decline in other operating expenses was attributed to reduced costs associated with property maintenance and management-related activities. The Company continues to focus on optimizing its operational processes to enhance cost efficiency.

Operating Income

Operating income for fiscal 2024 was $224 million, a decrease of $34 million, or 13.2%, from $258 million in fiscal 2023. This reduction in operating income is reflective of the lower revenue base, partially offset by effective cost management strategies. The operating margin decreased to 2.4% in fiscal 2024 from 2.7% in fiscal 2023, indicating the impact of the revenue decline on profitability.

Interest Expense

Interest expense for fiscal 2024 was $213 million, a slight decrease of $5 million, or 2.3%, compared to $218 million in fiscal 2023. This reduction in interest expense was primarily due to debt repayment activities during the year, which helped lower the Company's overall interest burden.

Income Before Income Taxes

Income before income taxes was $11 million for fiscal 2024, a decrease of 72.5% from $40 million in fiscal 2023. The decline in pre-tax income was mainly due to the decrease in operating income, compounded by relatively stable interest expenses.

Provision for Income Taxes

The provision for income taxes in fiscal 2024 was $3 million, compared to $9 million in fiscal 2023. The effective tax rate for fiscal 2024 was 27.3%, higher than the 22.5% effective tax rate in the prior year. The increase in the effective tax rate was attributable to changes in the geographic mix of earnings and adjustments in deferred tax liabilities.

Net Income

Net income for fiscal 2024 was $8 million, a decrease of 74.2% from $31 million in fiscal 2023. The net margin declined to 0.1% from 0.3% in the previous year, reflecting the challenges in maintaining profitability in a competitive market environment.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

Cash and cash equivalents at the end of fiscal 2024 were $2,034 million, compared to $2,141 million at the end of fiscal 2023. The decrease in cash and cash equivalents was influenced by various operating, investing, and financing activities throughout the year.

Operating Cash Flows

Net cash provided by operating activities was $1,558 million for fiscal 2024. This figure reflects the Company's robust operating cash flow generation, driven by strong working capital management and operational efficiencies. Depreciation and amortization expenses totaling $1,619 million contributed positively to the operating cash flows.

Investing Cash Flows

Net cash used in investing activities amounted to $331 million in fiscal 2024. The primary use of cash was for purchases of property and equipment totaling $990 million, which was partially offset by $659 million received from other investing activities. The Company remains committed to strategic investments aimed at enhancing its property portfolio and operational capabilities.

Financing Cash Flows

Net cash used in financing activities was $1,334 million for fiscal 2024. The significant outflow was primarily due to share repurchases, net of issuances, amounting to $1,215 million. The Company did not pay dividends during the year, choosing instead to focus on share buybacks as a means of returning value to shareholders. Additionally, the net change in long-term debt resulted in a cash outflow of $119 million, reflecting the Company's efforts to manage its debt obligations prudently.

Capital Expenditures

Capital expenditures for fiscal 2024 totaled $990 million, reflecting the Company's ongoing commitment to property development and maintenance. These expenditures were strategically allocated towards enhancing the quality and competitiveness of Merqua's real estate portfolio.

Debt

As of December 31, 2024, Merqua's total long-term debt was $4,724 million, a decrease from $4,843 million as of December 31, 2023. The reduction in debt levels was a result of the Company's disciplined approach to debt management and repayment efforts. Maintaining a manageable debt profile continues to be a strategic priority for Merqua, ensuring financial flexibility and stability.

Dividends and Share Repurchases

Merqua did not declare or pay any dividends during fiscal 2024. The Company focused on share repurchases as a key component of its capital return strategy. During the year, Merqua repurchased shares totaling $1,215 million, reflecting management's confidence in the Company's long-term prospects and commitment to enhancing shareholder value.

BUSINESS

Overview

Merqua Land, Inc. ("Merqua" or the "Company"), incorporated in the state of Delaware, is a prominent entity in the real estate sector. Headquartered in Chicago, Illinois, Merqua specializes in the acquisition, development, and management of a diverse range of real estate properties across North America. The Company is dedicated to generating sustainable value for its shareholders through a strategic focus on quality assets, operational excellence, and disciplined financial management. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, Merqua reported total revenues of $9,373 million.

Principal Products and Services

Merqua's primary business activities encompass the development and management of residential, commercial, and mixed-use properties. The Company engages in the leasing, management, and operation of these properties to ensure optimal performance and tenant satisfaction. Merqua's portfolio includes a variety of property types such as residential complexes, office buildings, retail centers, and industrial properties. By offering a comprehensive suite of real estate services, Merqua is able to meet the evolving needs of its diverse clientele.

Reportable Segments

Merqua operates through three reportable segments: Residential Properties, Commercial Properties, and Mixed-Use Developments. Each segment is strategically designed to leverage Merqua's core competencies and capitalize on market opportunities.

Residential Properties

The Residential Properties segment focuses on the development and management of multi-family housing units. These properties include high-rise and mid-rise apartment buildings, condominium complexes, and townhouses. Merqua aims to provide high-quality living environments that cater to a wide range of demographics, from young professionals to families and retirees. The Company emphasizes modern design, amenities, and sustainability in its residential projects to enhance tenant satisfaction and retention.

Commercial Properties

In the Commercial Properties segment, Merqua invests in and manages office buildings, retail spaces, and industrial facilities. These properties are strategically located in urban and suburban areas to attract a diverse range of tenants, including corporate clients, retailers, and logistics companies. Merqua is committed to maintaining high occupancy rates and maximizing the value of its commercial assets through proactive leasing strategies and efficient property management practices.

Mixed-Use Developments

The Mixed-Use Developments segment integrates residential, commercial, and recreational spaces into cohesive communities. These developments are designed to create vibrant, self-sustaining environments that offer convenience and connectivity for residents and businesses alike. Merqua's mixed-use projects often include features such as retail shops, dining establishments, office spaces, and public amenities. By fostering a sense of community and promoting urban living, Merqua enhances the appeal and value of its mixed-use developments.

Customers

Merqua's customer base is diverse, encompassing a wide range of tenants and property buyers. The Company's residential tenants include individuals and families seeking quality housing in desirable locations. Commercial tenants range from small businesses to large corporations seeking office or retail space. Additionally, Merqua's customers include property investors and developers seeking to acquire or partner in new real estate projects. The Company's commitment to customer satisfaction and long-term relationships is a cornerstone of its business strategy.

Merqua employs a multi-faceted sales and marketing strategy to attract and retain tenants across its property portfolio. Additionally, Merqua leverages partnerships with real estate brokers and agents to expand its market reach. The real estate industry is highly competitive, with numerous players vying for market share. Competitors range from large national firms to regional and local players. Merqua differentiates itself through its commitment to quality, strategic location selection, and innovative property designs. Cybersecurity Risk Management and Strategy

Merqua Land, Inc. ("Merqua" or the "Company") recognizes the critical importance of cybersecurity in safeguarding the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of its information systems. The Company has developed comprehensive cybersecurity risk management processes designed to address the evolving landscape of cyber threats and to protect its digital infrastructure and data assets.

Merqua employs the National Institute of Standards and Technology Cybersecurity Framework (NIST CSF) as a guiding tool to inform the design and assessment of its cybersecurity processes. This framework aids the Company in identifying, assessing, and managing cybersecurity risks pertinent to its real estate operations. While the adoption of the NIST CSF provides a structured approach, it does not signify compliance with any specific technical standards or requirements.

The Company has integrated its cybersecurity risk management processes into its broader risk management program. This integration is achieved through the alignment of methodologies, reporting structures, and governance processes, ensuring that cybersecurity risks are managed alongside other business risks.

Key components of Merqua's cybersecurity risk management processes include:

  • Conducting independent maturity assessments to identify significant cybersecurity risks affecting the Company's information technology environment and systems. These assessments provide valuable insights into potential vulnerabilities and areas for improvement.
  • Establishing a cyber resilience team that plays a central role in managing the Company's cybersecurity risk assessment processes, security controls, and incident response strategies. This team is tasked with monitoring the cybersecurity landscape, implementing protective measures, and ensuring swift responses to any incidents.
  • Engaging external service providers to assist with various aspects of cybersecurity. These providers are utilized for tasks such as security assessments, penetration testing, and the enhancement of security controls, allowing the Company to benefit from specialized expertise and tools.
  • Providing comprehensive cybersecurity awareness training for employees, incident response personnel, and senior management. This training is designed to foster a culture of cybersecurity vigilance and equip individuals with the knowledge needed to recognize and respond to potential threats.
  • Developing and maintaining a cybersecurity incident response plan that outlines procedures for responding to cybersecurity incidents. This plan is regularly updated to reflect the latest threat intelligence and best practices, ensuring the Company is prepared to handle incidents effectively.
  • Implementing a risk management process for service providers, suppliers, and vendors that aligns with Merqua's compliance requirements. This process helps ensure that third-party partners adhere to the Company's cybersecurity standards and do not introduce additional risks to its operations.

To date, Merqua has not experienced any cybersecurity incidents that have materially affected its operations, business strategy, results of operations, or financial condition. However, the Company recognizes the complexity of cybersecurity threats and the potential for such threats to have a significant impact if realized. Therefore, Merqua remains vigilant in its efforts to continuously enhance its cybersecurity posture.

Merqua's Board of Directors plays an integral role in overseeing the Company's cybersecurity risk management efforts. The Board considers cybersecurity risks as a key aspect of its risk management and compliance oversight responsibilities. To this end, the Board receives regular reports from management on cybersecurity risks, with updates provided at least twice a year. In addition, management informs the Board of any material cybersecurity incidents, as well as incidents with lesser impact potential, ensuring that the Board remains informed about the Company's cybersecurity status.

The Board is further educated on cybersecurity topics through briefings and presentations from Merqua's Chief Information Security Officer (CISO), Chief Technology Officer, and other key personnel. These sessions provide the Board with insights into the Company's cyber risk management processes and the broader cybersecurity landscape affecting public companies.

Responsibility for executing Merqua's cybersecurity risk management processes lies with the Company's management team. The CISO, in collaboration with the Chief Technology Officer and other senior leaders, is responsible for assessing and managing material risks arising from cybersecurity threats. The CISO possesses extensive experience in information technology, compliance, and cybersecurity program design and management, contributing to a robust cybersecurity strategy.

The cybersecurity operations team, under the leadership of the CISO, includes individuals with industry certifications such as Certified Information Systems Security Professional (CISSP) and Certified Information Security Manager (CISM). This team's expertise is critical in implementing and maintaining effective cybersecurity measures across the Company's operations.

Merqua's management team works closely with the cybersecurity operations team to stay informed about efforts to prevent, detect, mitigate, and remediate cybersecurity risks and incidents. This collaboration involves regular briefings from internal security personnel, the analysis of threat intelligence from public and private sources, and the utilization of alerts and reports generated by security tools. By leveraging these resources, Merqua is able to proactively address potential threats and ensure that its cybersecurity defenses remain robust.

In conclusion, Merqua Land, Inc. is committed to maintaining a strong cybersecurity posture that protects its information systems and data assets against a range of cyber threats. Overview

Merqua is a leader in the real estate sector, focusing on the acquisition, development, and management of residential, commercial, and mixed-use properties in North America. The Company is committed to delivering sustainable value to its shareholders by leveraging high-quality assets, operational excellence, and disciplined financial management. This decrease in revenue reflects the challenges and opportunities encountered during the year.

Results of Operations

Revenue

For the fiscal year 2024, Merqua reported revenue of $9,373 million, a decrease of 2.2% from $9,587 million in fiscal 2023. This decline in revenue was primarily driven by a reduction in leasing activity in certain segments, influenced by competitive pressures and shifts in tenant preferences. Despite a challenging market environment, the Company maintained its focus on optimizing tenant retention and pursuing strategic leasing opportunities across its diverse portfolio.

Cost of Revenue and Gross Margin

Cost of revenue for fiscal 2024 was $7,118 million, representing a decrease of $155 million, or 2.1%, from the previous year's cost of $7,273 million. This reduction was achieved through cost management initiatives and operational efficiencies across the property management functions. The gross profit for fiscal 2024 was $2,255 million, a decrease of 2.6% compared to $2,314 million in fiscal 2023. The gross margin remained stable at 24.1% in both fiscal 2024 and 2023, indicating consistent operational performance amidst revenue fluctuations.

Selling, General and Administrative Expenses

Selling, general and administrative (SG&A) expenses totaled $1,469 million in fiscal 2024, a decrease of $12 million or 0.8% from $1,481 million in fiscal 2023. This reduction was primarily due to cost-saving measures and a streamlined administrative structure. SG&A expenses as a percentage of revenue remained relatively stable at 15.7% in fiscal 2024 compared to 15.4% in the prior year.

Other Operating Expenses

Other operating expenses amounted to $562 million for fiscal 2024, a decrease of $13 million or 2.3% from $575 million in fiscal 2023. The decline in other operating expenses was attributed to reduced costs associated with property maintenance and management-related activities. The Company continues to focus on optimizing its operational processes to enhance cost efficiency.

Operating Income

Operating income for fiscal 2024 was $224 million, a decrease of $34 million, or 13.2%, from $258 million in fiscal 2023. This reduction in operating income is reflective of the lower revenue base, partially offset by effective cost management strategies. The operating margin decreased to 2.4% in fiscal 2024 from 2.7% in fiscal 2023, indicating the impact of the revenue decline on profitability.

Interest Expense

Interest expense for fiscal 2024 was $213 million, a slight decrease of $5 million, or 2.3%, compared to $218 million in fiscal 2023. This reduction in interest expense was primarily due to debt repayment activities during the year, which helped lower the Company's overall interest burden.

Income Before Income Taxes

Income before income taxes was $11 million for fiscal 2024, a decrease of 72.5% from $40 million in fiscal 2023. The decline in pre-tax income was mainly due to the decrease in operating income, compounded by relatively stable interest expenses.

Provision for Income Taxes

The provision for income taxes in fiscal 2024 was $3 million, compared to $9 million in fiscal 2023. The effective tax rate for fiscal 2024 was 27.3%, higher than the 22.5% effective tax rate in the prior year. The increase in the effective tax rate was attributable to changes in the geographic mix of earnings and adjustments in deferred tax liabilities.

Net Income

Net income for fiscal 2024 was $8 million, a decrease of 74.2% from $31 million in fiscal 2023. The net margin declined to 0.1% from 0.3% in the previous year, reflecting the challenges in maintaining profitability in a competitive market environment.

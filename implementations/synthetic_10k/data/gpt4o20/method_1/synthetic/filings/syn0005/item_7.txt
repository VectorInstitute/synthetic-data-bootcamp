Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

The following discussion and analysis should be read in conjunction with the consolidated financial statements and related notes included in this Annual Report. The discussion provides insights into the financial condition and results of operations of Merqua Land, Inc. ("Merqua" or the "Company") for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, with comparisons to the previous fiscal year ended December 31, 2023.

Overview

Merqua is a leader in the real estate sector, focusing on the acquisition, development, and management of residential, commercial, and mixed-use properties in North America. The Company is committed to delivering sustainable value to its shareholders by leveraging high-quality assets, operational excellence, and disciplined financial management. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, Merqua reported total revenues of $9,373 million, a decrease of 2.2% compared to the prior year. This decrease in revenue reflects the challenges and opportunities encountered during the year.

Results of Operations

Revenue

For the fiscal year 2024, Merqua reported revenue of $9,373 million, a decrease of 2.2% from $9,587 million in fiscal 2023. This decline in revenue was primarily driven by a reduction in leasing activity in certain segments, influenced by competitive pressures and shifts in tenant preferences. Despite a challenging market environment, the Company maintained its focus on optimizing tenant retention and pursuing strategic leasing opportunities across its diverse portfolio.

Cost of Revenue and Gross Margin

Cost of revenue for fiscal 2024 was $7,118 million, representing a decrease of $155 million, or 2.1%, from the previous year's cost of $7,273 million. This reduction was achieved through cost management initiatives and operational efficiencies across the property management functions. The gross profit for fiscal 2024 was $2,255 million, a decrease of 2.6% compared to $2,314 million in fiscal 2023. The gross margin remained stable at 24.1% in both fiscal 2024 and 2023, indicating consistent operational performance amidst revenue fluctuations.

Selling, General and Administrative Expenses

Selling, general and administrative (SG&A) expenses totaled $1,469 million in fiscal 2024, a decrease of $12 million or 0.8% from $1,481 million in fiscal 2023. This reduction was primarily due to cost-saving measures and a streamlined administrative structure. SG&A expenses as a percentage of revenue remained relatively stable at 15.7% in fiscal 2024 compared to 15.4% in the prior year.

Other Operating Expenses

Other operating expenses amounted to $562 million for fiscal 2024, a decrease of $13 million or 2.3% from $575 million in fiscal 2023. The decline in other operating expenses was attributed to reduced costs associated with property maintenance and management-related activities. The Company continues to focus on optimizing its operational processes to enhance cost efficiency.

Operating Income

Operating income for fiscal 2024 was $224 million, a decrease of $34 million, or 13.2%, from $258 million in fiscal 2023. This reduction in operating income is reflective of the lower revenue base, partially offset by effective cost management strategies. The operating margin decreased to 2.4% in fiscal 2024 from 2.7% in fiscal 2023, indicating the impact of the revenue decline on profitability.

Interest Expense

Interest expense for fiscal 2024 was $213 million, a slight decrease of $5 million, or 2.3%, compared to $218 million in fiscal 2023. This reduction in interest expense was primarily due to debt repayment activities during the year, which helped lower the Company's overall interest burden.

Income Before Income Taxes

Income before income taxes was $11 million for fiscal 2024, a decrease of 72.5% from $40 million in fiscal 2023. The decline in pre-tax income was mainly due to the decrease in operating income, compounded by relatively stable interest expenses.

Provision for Income Taxes

The provision for income taxes in fiscal 2024 was $3 million, compared to $9 million in fiscal 2023. The effective tax rate for fiscal 2024 was 27.3%, higher than the 22.5% effective tax rate in the prior year. The increase in the effective tax rate was attributable to changes in the geographic mix of earnings and adjustments in deferred tax liabilities.

Net Income

Net income for fiscal 2024 was $8 million, a decrease of 74.2% from $31 million in fiscal 2023. The net margin declined to 0.1% from 0.3% in the previous year, reflecting the challenges in maintaining profitability in a competitive market environment.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

Cash and cash equivalents at the end of fiscal 2024 were $2,034 million, compared to $2,141 million at the end of fiscal 2023. The decrease in cash and cash equivalents was influenced by various operating, investing, and financing activities throughout the year.

Operating Cash Flows

Net cash provided by operating activities was $1,558 million for fiscal 2024. This figure reflects the Company's robust operating cash flow generation, driven by strong working capital management and operational efficiencies. Depreciation and amortization expenses totaling $1,619 million contributed positively to the operating cash flows.

Investing Cash Flows

Net cash used in investing activities amounted to $331 million in fiscal 2024. The primary use of cash was for purchases of property and equipment totaling $990 million, which was partially offset by $659 million received from other investing activities. The Company remains committed to strategic investments aimed at enhancing its property portfolio and operational capabilities.

Financing Cash Flows

Net cash used in financing activities was $1,334 million for fiscal 2024. The significant outflow was primarily due to share repurchases, net of issuances, amounting to $1,215 million. The Company did not pay dividends during the year, choosing instead to focus on share buybacks as a means of returning value to shareholders. Additionally, the net change in long-term debt resulted in a cash outflow of $119 million, reflecting the Company's efforts to manage its debt obligations prudently.

Capital Expenditures

Capital expenditures for fiscal 2024 totaled $990 million, reflecting the Company's ongoing commitment to property development and maintenance. These expenditures were strategically allocated towards enhancing the quality and competitiveness of Merqua's real estate portfolio.

Debt

As of December 31, 2024, Merqua's total long-term debt was $4,724 million, a decrease from $4,843 million as of December 31, 2023. The reduction in debt levels was a result of the Company's disciplined approach to debt management and repayment efforts. Maintaining a manageable debt profile continues to be a strategic priority for Merqua, ensuring financial flexibility and stability.

Dividends and Share Repurchases

Merqua did not declare or pay any dividends during fiscal 2024. The Company focused on share repurchases as a key component of its capital return strategy. During the year, Merqua repurchased shares totaling $1,215 million, reflecting management's confidence in the Company's long-term prospects and commitment to enhancing shareholder value.

Critical Accounting Estimates

The preparation of financial statements in conformity with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States requires management to make estimates and assumptions that affect the reported amounts of assets, liabilities, revenues, and expenses. The most significant estimates and assumptions made by management relate to the valuation of real estate properties, goodwill impairment assessments, and the estimation of income tax liabilities.

Real Estate Property Valuation

Merqua's real estate properties are recorded at cost and are subject to periodic reviews for impairment. The determination of fair value involves significant estimates and assumptions regarding market conditions, property performance, and future cash flow projections. Changes in these estimates could materially impact the Company's financial results.

Goodwill Impairment

Goodwill is reviewed for impairment annually, or more frequently if events or changes in circumstances indicate that the carrying value may not be recoverable. The impairment assessment involves estimating the fair value of reporting units and comparing it to their carrying value. Any necessary impairment charges could significantly affect the Company's financial performance.

Income Tax Liabilities

The provision for income taxes involves estimating income tax obligations in each jurisdiction where Merqua operates. The Company is required to assess the likelihood of the realization of deferred tax assets and may establish valuation allowances if it is deemed more likely than not that some portion of the deferred tax assets will not be realized. These assessments require judgment and could be impacted by changes in tax laws and the Company's future taxable income.

In conclusion, Merqua Land, Inc. navigated a challenging fiscal 2024 with a focus on strategic cost management, operational efficiency, and prudent capital allocation. The Company's commitment to enhancing its property portfolio, maintaining financial flexibility, and returning value to shareholders positions it well for future growth opportunities. Management remains vigilant in monitoring market conditions and adapting its strategies to ensure sustained success in the competitive real estate sector.

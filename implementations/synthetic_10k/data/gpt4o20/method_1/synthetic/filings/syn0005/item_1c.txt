Item 1C. Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity Risk Management and Strategy

Merqua Land, Inc. ("Merqua" or the "Company") recognizes the critical importance of cybersecurity in safeguarding the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of its information systems. The Company has developed comprehensive cybersecurity risk management processes designed to address the evolving landscape of cyber threats and to protect its digital infrastructure and data assets.

Merqua employs the National Institute of Standards and Technology Cybersecurity Framework (NIST CSF) as a guiding tool to inform the design and assessment of its cybersecurity processes. This framework aids the Company in identifying, assessing, and managing cybersecurity risks pertinent to its real estate operations. While the adoption of the NIST CSF provides a structured approach, it does not signify compliance with any specific technical standards or requirements.

The Company has integrated its cybersecurity risk management processes into its broader risk management program. This integration is achieved through the alignment of methodologies, reporting structures, and governance processes, ensuring that cybersecurity risks are managed alongside other business risks.

Key components of Merqua's cybersecurity risk management processes include:

- Conducting independent maturity assessments to identify significant cybersecurity risks affecting the Company's information technology environment and systems. These assessments provide valuable insights into potential vulnerabilities and areas for improvement.

- Establishing a cyber resilience team that plays a central role in managing the Company's cybersecurity risk assessment processes, security controls, and incident response strategies. This team is tasked with monitoring the cybersecurity landscape, implementing protective measures, and ensuring swift responses to any incidents.

- Engaging external service providers to assist with various aspects of cybersecurity. These providers are utilized for tasks such as security assessments, penetration testing, and the enhancement of security controls, allowing the Company to benefit from specialized expertise and tools.

- Providing comprehensive cybersecurity awareness training for employees, incident response personnel, and senior management. This training is designed to foster a culture of cybersecurity vigilance and equip individuals with the knowledge needed to recognize and respond to potential threats.

- Developing and maintaining a cybersecurity incident response plan that outlines procedures for responding to cybersecurity incidents. This plan is regularly updated to reflect the latest threat intelligence and best practices, ensuring the Company is prepared to handle incidents effectively.

- Implementing a risk management process for service providers, suppliers, and vendors that aligns with Merqua's compliance requirements. This process helps ensure that third-party partners adhere to the Company's cybersecurity standards and do not introduce additional risks to its operations.

To date, Merqua has not experienced any cybersecurity incidents that have materially affected its operations, business strategy, results of operations, or financial condition. However, the Company recognizes the complexity of cybersecurity threats and the potential for such threats to have a significant impact if realized. Therefore, Merqua remains vigilant in its efforts to continuously enhance its cybersecurity posture.

Cybersecurity Governance

Merqua's Board of Directors plays an integral role in overseeing the Company's cybersecurity risk management efforts. The Board considers cybersecurity risks as a key aspect of its risk management and compliance oversight responsibilities. To this end, the Board receives regular reports from management on cybersecurity risks, with updates provided at least twice a year. In addition, management informs the Board of any material cybersecurity incidents, as well as incidents with lesser impact potential, ensuring that the Board remains informed about the Company's cybersecurity status.

The Board is further educated on cybersecurity topics through briefings and presentations from Merqua's Chief Information Security Officer (CISO), Chief Technology Officer, and other key personnel. These sessions provide the Board with insights into the Company's cyber risk management processes and the broader cybersecurity landscape affecting public companies.

Responsibility for executing Merqua's cybersecurity risk management processes lies with the Company's management team. The CISO, in collaboration with the Chief Technology Officer and other senior leaders, is responsible for assessing and managing material risks arising from cybersecurity threats. The CISO possesses extensive experience in information technology, compliance, and cybersecurity program design and management, contributing to a robust cybersecurity strategy.

The cybersecurity operations team, under the leadership of the CISO, includes individuals with industry certifications such as Certified Information Systems Security Professional (CISSP) and Certified Information Security Manager (CISM). This team's expertise is critical in implementing and maintaining effective cybersecurity measures across the Company's operations.

Merqua's management team works closely with the cybersecurity operations team to stay informed about efforts to prevent, detect, mitigate, and remediate cybersecurity risks and incidents. This collaboration involves regular briefings from internal security personnel, the analysis of threat intelligence from public and private sources, and the utilization of alerts and reports generated by security tools. By leveraging these resources, Merqua is able to proactively address potential threats and ensure that its cybersecurity defenses remain robust.

In conclusion, Merqua Land, Inc. is committed to maintaining a strong cybersecurity posture that protects its information systems and data assets against a range of cyber threats. Through a combination of strategic risk management processes, comprehensive governance oversight, and the expertise of its cybersecurity team, the Company is well-positioned to navigate the dynamic cybersecurity landscape and safeguard its operations and stakeholders.

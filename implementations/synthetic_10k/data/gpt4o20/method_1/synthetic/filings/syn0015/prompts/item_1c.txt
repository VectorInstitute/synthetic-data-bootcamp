Cybersecurity Strategy and Risk Management
Our comprehensive cybersecurity program is supported by policies and procedures designed to protect our systems and operations as well as the sensitive personal information and data of our clients and customers from foreseeable cybersecurity threats. This program is an integral component of our enterprise risk management program.
Core to our security model is our defense-in-depth framework, comprising multiple layers of processes and technologies that help prevent, detect and respond to threats. Our approach to safeguarding against external threats incorporates a suite of preventive technologies, including malicious email blocking, defenses against automated attacks and multifactor authentication. Event monitoring technologies run continuously, detecting suspected intrusion attempts and alerting our Cybersecurity Incident Response Team. We undertake a number of critical security processes to mitigate and protect against cybersecurity risks, which include but are not limited to (i) identity and access management; (ii) security awareness and training; (iii) security operations and monitoring; (iv) change

Cybersecurity risk assessment results are used by senior management to make informed decisions about where to allocate resources to reduce cybersecurity risks and improve overall security posture. We examine our entire program annually with third parties and measure the program against generally accepted industry standards and frameworks, such as an internationally recognized security control framework established by the NIST and used by companies to assess and improve their ability to prevent, detect and respond to cyberattacks. Our cybersecurity policies and standards are reviewed annually and are mainly guided by the NIST 800-53 Cybersecurity Framework. The GIRP is reviewed quarterly at a minimum but may be updated as needed based on lessons learned, changes in key teams or processes or other circumstances as warranted, and the procedures therein are tested annually. Item 1. Business

OVERVIEW

Zennov Medical, Inc. (ZONE) is a leading healthcare company, incorporated in Delaware and headquartered in Denver, Colorado. With a commitment to advancing medical innovation, we focus on the development, manufacturing, and marketing of medical devices and diagnostic products that address significant healthcare challenges. Our comprehensive product portfolio and robust research initiatives aim to improve patient outcomes across a range of therapeutic areas.

For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, Zennov Medical, Inc. reported total revenue of $2,264 million, reflecting our continuous growth and market expansion. Our dedication to improving healthcare delivery is evident through our sustained investment in research and development, which remains a cornerstone of our strategy to introduce cutting-edge solutions to the market.

PRINCIPAL PRODUCTS AND SERVICES

Our key product lines consist of advanced medical devices and diagnostic solutions that cater to the needs of healthcare professionals and patients globally. These products are designed to address complex medical conditions, enhance diagnostic accuracy, and improve treatment efficacy. We focus on several therapeutic areas, including cardiology, neurology, and oncology, ensuring that our offerings are at the forefront of medical technology.

Our medical devices are known for their precision and reliability, helping healthcare providers deliver better care. Coupled with our diagnostic solutions, which offer rapid and accurate results, we are committed to supporting healthcare systems in delivering timely interventions and improving patient management.

SEGMENTS

Zennov Medical operates through two primary reportable segments: Medical Devices and Diagnostic Products.

- Medical Devices: This segment is dedicated to the development and commercialization of innovative medical equipment used in various clinical settings. Our devices are engineered to meet the highest standards of quality and safety, addressing the needs of both healthcare providers and patients. Through continuous innovation, we strive to enhance the functionality and usability of our products, ensuring they can adapt to the evolving demands of modern healthcare.

- Diagnostic Products: The diagnostic products segment focuses on providing state-of-the-art solutions that aid in the early detection and monitoring of diseases. Our diagnostic tools are designed to deliver accurate and timely results, supporting clinicians in making informed decisions about patient care. This segment emphasizes research and development to advance diagnostic technologies and expand our product offerings.

CUSTOMERS

Our customers include hospitals, clinics, diagnostic laboratories, and healthcare professionals across the globe. We are dedicated to building strong relationships with our clients by understanding their needs and providing tailored solutions that enhance their ability to deliver quality care. Our customer-centric approach ensures that we remain responsive to market demands and continue to deliver value through our products and services.

SALES AND MARKETING

Zennov Medical employs a comprehensive sales and marketing strategy that emphasizes the promotion of our products through direct sales forces, distributors, and strategic partnerships. Our sales teams are highly trained and equipped with extensive knowledge about our product offerings, enabling them to effectively communicate the benefits of our solutions to healthcare providers. We also engage in targeted marketing campaigns to increase brand awareness and demonstrate the impact of our innovations on patient care.

COMPETITION

The healthcare industry is highly competitive, with numerous companies striving to introduce new and improved medical technologies. Zennov Medical competes with a wide range of companies in the medical device and diagnostic sectors, from large multinational corporations to emerging start-ups. We differentiate ourselves through our commitment to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction, ensuring that our products remain competitive in terms of performance, reliability, and cost-effectiveness.

SEASONALITY

Our business is subject to certain seasonal fluctuations, primarily driven by the purchasing cycles of healthcare providers and institutions. These cycles can be influenced by budgetary allocations, fiscal year-end spending, and policy changes within healthcare systems. While we strive to mitigate the impact of seasonality through strategic planning and inventory management, fluctuations in demand can affect our sales and operational performance.

INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

Zennov Medical places significant importance on protecting our intellectual property to maintain our competitive edge. We hold a robust portfolio of patents and trademarks that safeguard our proprietary technologies and innovations. Our intellectual property strategy is designed to protect our research and development investments, support our business objectives, and enable us to bring groundbreaking solutions to market.

GOVERNMENT REGULATION

As a healthcare company, Zennov Medical is subject to extensive government regulation in the jurisdictions where we operate. Our products and manufacturing processes must comply with stringent regulatory standards to ensure safety and efficacy. We work closely with regulatory agencies to ensure our products meet all necessary approvals and maintain compliance with applicable laws and regulations. This commitment to regulatory excellence is fundamental to our mission of delivering safe and effective medical solutions.

HUMAN CAPITAL

Zennov Medical's success is driven by our talented team of approximately 2,700 employees who are dedicated to advancing healthcare solutions. Our workforce is comprised of skilled professionals across various disciplines, including research and development, manufacturing, sales, and support services. We are committed to fostering a dynamic work environment that encourages innovation, collaboration, and professional growth. Our employees are our most valuable asset, and we invest in their development to ensure they are equipped to meet the challenges of the healthcare industry.

AVAILABLE INFORMATION

Zennov Medical is committed to transparency and providing stakeholders with timely and accurate information about our business operations and financial performance. We regularly file reports with the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), including our annual report on Form 10-K, quarterly reports on Form 10-Q, and current reports on Form 8-K. We encourage stakeholders to utilize these resources to stay informed about our company's progress and strategic initiatives.

We may also be adversely impacted by other risks not presently known to us or that we currently consider immaterial.

Business and Industry Risks

Healthcare Industry Competition

The healthcare industry, particularly the medical device and diagnostics sectors, is intensely competitive. Zennov Medical faces significant competition from both large multinational corporations and smaller, innovative start-ups. Competitors with greater financial resources may develop superior products, technologies, or services, which could diminish the market share and pricing power of our products. Our ability to maintain a competitive edge depends on continual innovation, effective marketing, and the delivery of high-quality products. Failure to keep pace with technological advancements or shifts in consumer preferences could adversely affect our business and financial performance.

Dependence on Key Products and Technologies

Our business is heavily reliant on the continued success and acceptance of our key products and technologies. A significant portion of our revenue is generated from a limited number of products, particularly in the medical devices and diagnostics segments. If these products fail to maintain market acceptance, or if we are unable to successfully launch new products that meet customer needs and regulatory requirements, our financial performance could suffer. Additionally, unforeseen issues with product safety, efficacy, or quality could lead to recalls, tarnish our reputation, and impact our business operations.

Economic and Market Conditions

Global Economic Volatility

The global economic environment can significantly impact our operations and financial results. Economic downturns, inflationary pressures, and fluctuating foreign exchange rates may affect the purchasing power of our customers, leading to reduced demand for our products. Economic instability in key markets could also impact our supply chain, increase production costs, and create challenges in pricing our products competitively. Adverse economic conditions can further affect healthcare spending and budgets, potentially leading to decreased sales and profitability.

Our operations rely on a complex global supply chain for the procurement of raw materials, components, and manufacturing services. We may face difficulties in sourcing alternative suppliers or materials on a timely or cost-effective basis, which may disrupt our ability to meet customer demand and harm our financial performance.

Product Development and Innovation

Innovation is critical to our success in the healthcare industry. Additionally, the development of new technologies involves uncertainties related to regulatory approvals, intellectual property protection, and market acceptance. The production and distribution of our products are subject to stringent quality control standards and regulatory requirements. Our products must comply with extensive regulatory requirements in the jurisdictions where we operate. Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Overview

Zennov Medical, Inc. ("Zennov" or "the Company") is a leading healthcare company specializing in the development, manufacturing, and marketing of innovative medical devices and diagnostic products. Headquartered in Denver, Colorado, Zennov is committed to addressing significant healthcare challenges by advancing medical innovation and improving patient outcomes across various therapeutic areas. Our operations are driven by a strong focus on research and development, which accounts for a substantial portion of our investment strategy. This focus supports our goal of introducing cutting-edge solutions to the market, thereby enhancing the quality of care provided by healthcare professionals worldwide. The fiscal year 2024 demonstrated our ability to deliver sustained growth while navigating a competitive and dynamic healthcare environment.

Results of Operations

Revenue

For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, Zennov Medical reported revenue of $2,264 million, an increase of 8.2% compared to $2,093 million in fiscal 2023. This growth was primarily driven by increased demand for our innovative medical devices and diagnostic products across key markets. The expansion of our product portfolio and the successful launch of new products contributed significantly to the revenue increase. Furthermore, strategic partnerships and enhanced distribution channels facilitated greater market penetration, bolstering our revenue growth.

The cost of revenue for fiscal 2024 was $606 million, compared to $584 million in fiscal 2023. These initiatives have enabled us to absorb cost increases while maintaining a robust margin profile.

Operating Expenses

Research and Development

Investment in research and development (R&D) is a cornerstone of our growth strategy. In fiscal 2024, R&D expenses totaled $923 million, compared to $854 million in fiscal 2023, representing 40.8% of our revenue for both years. Selling, General and Administrative

As a percentage of revenue, SG&A expenses rose to 5.5% from 4.2% in the prior year. Operating Income

Operating income for fiscal 2024 was $611 million, a 7.6% increase from $568 million in fiscal 2023. Our operating margin was 27.0% in fiscal 2024, slightly below the prior year's margin of 27.1%, highlighting our ability to maintain operational efficiency while investing in future growth.

Interest Expense

Interest expense for fiscal 2024 was $59 million, compared to $54 million in fiscal 2023. The increase in interest expense is attributable to higher levels of long-term debt, which rose to $1,321 million from $1,201 million in the prior year. The additional debt was primarily used to finance strategic investments and support our R&D initiatives. We continue to manage our debt levels prudently to ensure financial flexibility and support our growth objectives.

Effective Tax Rate

Our provision for income taxes was $166 million for fiscal 2024, compared to $149 million in fiscal 2023, resulting in an effective tax rate of 30.1%, up from 29.0% in the prior year. The increase in the effective tax rate reflects changes in the geographic mix of our earnings and adjustments related to tax credits and incentives. We remain focused on optimizing our tax strategy to enhance shareholder value while complying with applicable tax regulations.

Net Income

Net income for fiscal 2024 was $386 million, representing a 5.8% increase from $365 million in fiscal 2023. The growth in net income was driven by higher operating income and revenue growth, partially offset by increased interest expense and a higher effective tax rate. Our net margin for fiscal 2024 was 17.0%, compared to 17.4% in the previous year, reflecting our continued focus on delivering profitable growth.

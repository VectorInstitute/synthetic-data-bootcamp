Item 1. Business

OVERVIEW

Zennov Medical, Inc. (ZONE) is a leading healthcare company, incorporated in Delaware and headquartered in Denver, Colorado. With a commitment to advancing medical innovation, we focus on the development, manufacturing, and marketing of medical devices and diagnostic products that address significant healthcare challenges. Our comprehensive product portfolio and robust research initiatives aim to improve patient outcomes across a range of therapeutic areas.

For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, Zennov Medical, Inc. reported total revenue of $2,264 million, reflecting our continuous growth and market expansion. Our dedication to improving healthcare delivery is evident through our sustained investment in research and development, which remains a cornerstone of our strategy to introduce cutting-edge solutions to the market.

PRINCIPAL PRODUCTS AND SERVICES

Our key product lines consist of advanced medical devices and diagnostic solutions that cater to the needs of healthcare professionals and patients globally. These products are designed to address complex medical conditions, enhance diagnostic accuracy, and improve treatment efficacy. We focus on several therapeutic areas, including cardiology, neurology, and oncology, ensuring that our offerings are at the forefront of medical technology.

Our medical devices are known for their precision and reliability, helping healthcare providers deliver better care. Coupled with our diagnostic solutions, which offer rapid and accurate results, we are committed to supporting healthcare systems in delivering timely interventions and improving patient management.

SEGMENTS

Zennov Medical operates through two primary reportable segments: Medical Devices and Diagnostic Products.

  • Medical Devices: This segment is dedicated to the development and commercialization of innovative medical equipment used in various clinical settings. Our devices are engineered to meet the highest standards of quality and safety, addressing the needs of both healthcare providers and patients. Through continuous innovation, we strive to enhance the functionality and usability of our products, ensuring they can adapt to the evolving demands of modern healthcare.
  • Diagnostic Products: The diagnostic products segment focuses on providing state-of-the-art solutions that aid in the early detection and monitoring of diseases. Our diagnostic tools are designed to deliver accurate and timely results, supporting clinicians in making informed decisions about patient care. This segment emphasizes research and development to advance diagnostic technologies and expand our product offerings.

CUSTOMERS

Our customers include hospitals, clinics, diagnostic laboratories, and healthcare professionals across the globe. We are dedicated to building strong relationships with our clients by understanding their needs and providing tailored solutions that enhance their ability to deliver quality care. Our customer-centric approach ensures that we remain responsive to market demands and continue to deliver value through our products and services.

SALES AND MARKETING

Zennov Medical employs a comprehensive sales and marketing strategy that emphasizes the promotion of our products through direct sales forces, distributors, and strategic partnerships. Our sales teams are highly trained and equipped with extensive knowledge about our product offerings, enabling them to effectively communicate the benefits of our solutions to healthcare providers. We also engage in targeted marketing campaigns to increase brand awareness and demonstrate the impact of our innovations on patient care.

COMPETITION

The healthcare industry is highly competitive, with numerous companies striving to introduce new and improved medical technologies. Zennov Medical competes with a wide range of companies in the medical device and diagnostic sectors, from large multinational corporations to emerging start-ups. We differentiate ourselves through our commitment to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction, ensuring that our products remain competitive in terms of performance, reliability, and cost-effectiveness.

SEASONALITY

Our business is subject to certain seasonal fluctuations, primarily driven by the purchasing cycles of healthcare providers and institutions. These cycles can be influenced by budgetary allocations, fiscal year-end spending, and policy changes within healthcare systems. While we strive to mitigate the impact of seasonality through strategic planning and inventory management, fluctuations in demand can affect our sales and operational performance.

INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

Zennov Medical places significant importance on protecting our intellectual property to maintain our competitive edge. We hold a robust portfolio of patents and trademarks that safeguard our proprietary technologies and innovations. Our intellectual property strategy is designed to protect our research and development investments, support our business objectives, and enable us to bring groundbreaking solutions to market.

GOVERNMENT REGULATION

As a healthcare company, Zennov Medical is subject to extensive government regulation in the jurisdictions where we operate. Our products and manufacturing processes must comply with stringent regulatory standards to ensure safety and efficacy. We work closely with regulatory agencies to ensure our products meet all necessary approvals and maintain compliance with applicable laws and regulations. This commitment to regulatory excellence is fundamental to our mission of delivering safe and effective medical solutions.

HUMAN CAPITAL

Zennov Medical's success is driven by our talented team of approximately 2,700 employees who are dedicated to advancing healthcare solutions. Our workforce is comprised of skilled professionals across various disciplines, including research and development, manufacturing, sales, and support services. We are committed to fostering a dynamic work environment that encourages innovation, collaboration, and professional growth. Our employees are our most valuable asset, and we invest in their development to ensure they are equipped to meet the challenges of the healthcare industry.

Item 1C. Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity Strategy and Risk Management

At Zennov Medical, Inc., cybersecurity is an integral part of our enterprise risk management framework. Our approach is underpinned by comprehensive policies and procedures designed to protect our systems, operations, and the sensitive data of our clients and customers from potential cybersecurity threats. This commitment is reflected in our defense-in-depth strategy, which leverages multiple layers of technology and processes to prevent, detect, and respond to cyber threats effectively.

Our defense strategy incorporates a range of preventive technologies, such as advanced threat intelligence, endpoint protection, and network security measures. We actively monitor for suspicious activities using sophisticated event monitoring systems that operate continuously. These systems are designed to identify potential threats and trigger alerts to our dedicated Cybersecurity Incident Response Team, ensuring prompt response and mitigation efforts.

We implement several critical security processes to manage and mitigate cybersecurity risks. These include identity and access management, security awareness training, and regular security assessments. We also focus on maintaining robust security operations and monitoring capabilities, change management protocols, and disaster recovery planning. Our vulnerability management program emphasizes timely patching and updates to safeguard against known threats. Additionally, we conduct thorough third-party vendor security reviews to ensure that our partners adhere to our stringent security standards.

Our cybersecurity risk assessment framework is designed to identify, evaluate, treat, and monitor risks continuously. These assessments inform our senior management's decision-making regarding resource allocation to reduce risks and enhance our security posture. We engage external experts to conduct annual evaluations of our cybersecurity program, measuring it against industry standards such as the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) Cybersecurity Framework and International Organization for Standardization (ISO) standards. This ensures our practices remain aligned with best-in-class security controls.

To ensure preparedness and strengthen our incident response capabilities, we conduct regular tabletop exercises involving leaders, stakeholders, and subject matter experts. These exercises are developed in collaboration with external security professionals and simulate realistic incident scenarios. The goal is to enhance our ability to respond effectively to cybersecurity incidents through practical training and scenario-based learning.

We also engage third-party firms to evaluate components of our cybersecurity program. These evaluations include control assessments for specific business areas and reviews aligned with industry-specific standards. Such evaluations ensure that our cybersecurity measures are robust and capable of addressing evolving threats.

Cybersecurity Governance

The governance of our cybersecurity program is overseen by our Board of Directors, which is responsible for ensuring that we have effective risk management policies and processes in place. The Board's oversight is executed through the Audit Committee, which is regularly briefed by our Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) on cybersecurity matters. These briefings provide insight into critical risks, audit findings, and management's strategies for risk mitigation, ensuring that the Board remains informed about the cybersecurity landscape and our preparedness.

Our CISO plays a pivotal role in leading our cybersecurity initiatives. With extensive experience in information security, the CISO collaborates closely with senior management to develop and implement our cybersecurity strategy. This role involves defining security policies, overseeing the execution of security measures, and ensuring alignment with industry standards and regulatory requirements. The CISO's reporting lines ensure direct communication with executive leadership and the Board, facilitating timely decision-making and response to emerging threats.

Our cybersecurity governance structure also includes a dedicated Incident Handling Team, which is responsible for managing validated cybersecurity events. This team maintains our Global Incident Response Plan, which outlines procedures for incident handling, including the formation of a cross-functional response team and post-incident reviews. The plan is regularly reviewed and updated to incorporate lessons learned and changes in personnel or processes.

Zennov Medical is committed to maintaining the highest standards of cybersecurity to protect our stakeholders and sustain trust in our operations. As the cybersecurity landscape evolves, we will continue to adapt our strategies and invest in technologies to safeguard our systems and data effectively.

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Overview

Zennov Medical, Inc. ("Zennov" or "the Company") is a leading healthcare company specializing in the development, manufacturing, and marketing of innovative medical devices and diagnostic products. Headquartered in Denver, Colorado, Zennov is committed to addressing significant healthcare challenges by advancing medical innovation and improving patient outcomes across various therapeutic areas. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, Zennov Medical reported total revenue of $2,264 million, marking a significant milestone in our continuous growth trajectory.

Our operations are driven by a strong focus on research and development, which accounts for a substantial portion of our investment strategy. This focus supports our goal of introducing cutting-edge solutions to the market, thereby enhancing the quality of care provided by healthcare professionals worldwide. The fiscal year 2024 demonstrated our ability to deliver sustained growth while navigating a competitive and dynamic healthcare environment.

Results of Operations

Revenue

For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, Zennov Medical reported revenue of $2,264 million, an increase of 8.2% compared to $2,093 million in fiscal 2023. This growth was primarily driven by increased demand for our innovative medical devices and diagnostic products across key markets. The expansion of our product portfolio and the successful launch of new products contributed significantly to the revenue increase. Furthermore, strategic partnerships and enhanced distribution channels facilitated greater market penetration, bolstering our revenue growth.

Cost of Revenue and Gross Margin

The cost of revenue for fiscal 2024 was $606 million, compared to $584 million in fiscal 2023. Despite the increase in cost, our gross profit improved to $1,658 million from $1,509 million in the prior year. The gross margin for fiscal 2024 stood at 73.2%, compared to 72.1% in fiscal 2023. The enhancement in gross margin reflects our ongoing efforts to optimize manufacturing processes and supply chain efficiencies, as well as the introduction of higher-margin products. These initiatives have enabled us to absorb cost increases while maintaining a robust margin profile.

Operating Expenses

Research and Development

Investment in research and development (R&D) is a cornerstone of our growth strategy. In fiscal 2024, R&D expenses totaled $923 million, compared to $854 million in fiscal 2023, representing 40.8% of our revenue for both years. The increase in R&D expenditure underscores our commitment to advancing medical innovation and expanding our product offerings. Key areas of focus included the development of next-generation medical technologies and diagnostic solutions, which are critical to maintaining our competitive edge and addressing emerging healthcare needs.

Selling, General and Administrative

Selling, general and administrative (SG&A) expenses increased to $124 million in fiscal 2024, up from $87 million in fiscal 2023. As a percentage of revenue, SG&A expenses rose to 5.5% from 4.2% in the prior year. This increase reflects strategic investments in our sales and marketing capabilities, aimed at enhancing brand awareness and supporting the launch of new products. Additionally, we expanded our sales teams and strengthened our customer engagement initiatives to drive market expansion and customer satisfaction.

Operating Income

Operating income for fiscal 2024 was $611 million, a 7.6% increase from $568 million in fiscal 2023. The growth in operating income was driven by higher revenue and improved gross margins, offsetting increased R&D and SG&A expenses. Our operating margin was 27.0% in fiscal 2024, slightly below the prior year's margin of 27.1%, highlighting our ability to maintain operational efficiency while investing in future growth.

Interest Expense

Interest expense for fiscal 2024 was $59 million, compared to $54 million in fiscal 2023. The increase in interest expense is attributable to higher levels of long-term debt, which rose to $1,321 million from $1,201 million in the prior year. The additional debt was primarily used to finance strategic investments and support our R&D initiatives. We continue to manage our debt levels prudently to ensure financial flexibility and support our growth objectives.

Effective Tax Rate

Our provision for income taxes was $166 million for fiscal 2024, compared to $149 million in fiscal 2023, resulting in an effective tax rate of 30.1%, up from 29.0% in the prior year. The increase in the effective tax rate reflects changes in the geographic mix of our earnings and adjustments related to tax credits and incentives. We remain focused on optimizing our tax strategy to enhance shareholder value while complying with applicable tax regulations.

Net Income

Net income for fiscal 2024 was $386 million, representing a 5.8% increase from $365 million in fiscal 2023. The growth in net income was driven by higher operating income and revenue growth, partially offset by increased interest expense and a higher effective tax rate. Our net margin for fiscal 2024 was 17.0%, compared to 17.4% in the previous year, reflecting our continued focus on delivering profitable growth.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

Cash and Cash Equivalents

As of December 31, 2024, cash and cash equivalents totaled $68 million, up from $60 million at the end of fiscal 2023. The increase in cash reserves reflects our strong operating cash flow generation, which provides the foundation for strategic investments and financial flexibility.

Operating Activities

Net cash provided by operating activities in fiscal 2024 was $451 million, driven by robust net income and effective management of working capital. Our ability to generate strong cash flows from operations is critical to funding our R&D initiatives, capital expenditures, and other strategic priorities.

Investing Activities

Net cash used in investing activities amounted to $260 million for fiscal 2024. This included $60 million in capital expenditures for the expansion and enhancement of our manufacturing facilities and research infrastructure. Additionally, we invested $200 million in other investing activities, primarily focused on strategic acquisitions and partnerships to enhance our product portfolio and market presence.

Financing Activities

Net cash used in financing activities was $183 million in fiscal 2024. This included a net change in long-term debt of $120 million, reflecting our efforts to optimize our capital structure and support growth initiatives. We also paid dividends totaling $152 million and engaged in share repurchases, net of issuances, amounting to $151 million. These actions reflect our commitment to returning value to shareholders while maintaining financial strength.

Capital Expenditures

Capital expenditures for fiscal 2024 were $60 million, compared to $48 million in fiscal 2023. The increase in capital spending reflects our continued investment in infrastructure to support our growth and innovation objectives. We remain focused on deploying capital efficiently to enhance our operational capabilities and drive long-term value creation.

Debt

Our long-term debt increased to $1,321 million as of December 31, 2024, from $1,201 million at the end of fiscal 2023. The additional debt supports our strategic investments and provides the financial flexibility necessary to pursue growth opportunities. We continue to prioritize debt management and maintain a balanced capital structure to support our financial health and operational agility.

Dividends and Share Repurchases

In fiscal 2024, we returned $303 million to shareholders through dividends and share repurchases. Dividends paid totaled $152 million, while share repurchases, net of issuances, amounted to $151 million. These actions are aligned with our commitment to delivering shareholder value and reflect our confidence in the Company's future prospects.

Executive Overview
General
Humana Inc., headquartered in Louisville, Kentucky, is committed to putting health first – for our teammates, our customers, and our company. Through our Humana insurance services, and our CenterWell health care services, we make it easier for the millions of people we serve to achieve their best health – delivering the care and service they need, when they need it. These efforts are leading to a better quality of life for people with Medicare, Medicaid, families, individuals, military service personnel, and communities at large.
Our industry relies on two key statistics to measure performance. The benefit ratio, which is computed by taking total benefits expense as a percentage of premiums revenue, represents a statistic used to measure underwriting profitability. The operating cost ratio, which is computed by taking total operating costs, excluding depreciation and amortization, as a percentage of total revenue less investment income, represents a statistic used to measure administrative spending efficiency.
Employer Group Commercial Medical Products Business Exit
In February 2023, we announced our planned exit from the Employer Group Commercial Medical Products business, which includes all fully insured, self-funded and Federal Employee Health Benefit medical plans, as well as associated wellness and rewards programs. No other Humana health plan offerings are materially affected. Following a strategic review, we determined the Employer Group Commercial Medical Products business was no longer positioned to sustainably meet the needs of commercial members over the long term or support our long-term strategic plans. We anticipate the exit of this line of business to be finalized in the first half of 2025.
Value Creation Initiatives and Impairment Charges
In order to create capacity to fund growth and investment in our Medicare Advantage business and further expansion of our healthcare services capabilities beginning in 2022, we committed to drive additional value for the enterprise through cost saving, productivity initiatives, and value acceleration from previous investments. As a result of these initiatives, we recorded charges of $281 million and $436 million in 2024 and 2023, respectively, primarily within operating costs in the consolidated statements of income.
The value creation initiative charges primarily relate to $256 million and $237 million in asset impairments in 2024 and 2023, respectively, as well as $25 million and $199 million in severance charges in connection with workforce optimization in 2024 and 2023, respectively.
In addition, we recorded impairment charges of $200 million, relating to indefinite-lived intangible assets, in 2024 and $91 million, including $55 million relating to indefinite-lived intangible assets, in 2023. The indefinite-lived intangible asset impairment charges were included within operating costs in our consolidated statements of income with the remaining impairment charges included within investment income.
Further, we recorded severance charges of $70 million in 2023 within operating costs in our consolidated statement of income as a result of our exit from the Employer Group Commercial Medical Products business.

COVID-19
The emergence and spread of the novel coronavirus, or COVID-19, beginning in the first quarter of 2020 has impacted our business. Initially during periods of increased incidences of COVID-19, a reduction in non-COVID-19 hospital admissions for non-emergent and elective medical care resulted in lower overall healthcare system utilization. At the same time, COVID-19 treatment and testing costs increased utilization. During 2022, we experienced lower overall utilization of the healthcare system than anticipated, as the reduction in COVID-19 utilization following the increased incidence associated with the Omicron variant outpaced the increase in non-COVID-19 utilization.
The COVID-19 National Emergency declared in 2020 was terminated on April 10, 2023 and the Public Health Emergency expired on May 11, 2023.
Business Segments
Our two reportable segments, Insurance and CenterWell, are based on a combination of the type of health plan customer and adjacent businesses centered on well-being solutions for our health plans and other customers, as described below. Our Chief Executive Officer, the Chief Operating Decision Maker, utilizes these segment groupings and results of each segment, measured by income (loss) from operations, to assess performance and allocate resources primarily during our annual budget process and periodic forecast updates. For segment financial information, refer to Note 18 to the audited Consolidated Financial Statements included in Part II, Item 8, "Financial Statements and Supplementary Data" of this Form 10-K.
The Insurance segment consists of Medicare benefits, marketed to individuals or directly via group Medicare accounts, as well as our contract with CMS to administer the Limited Income Newly Eligible Transition, or LI-NET, prescription drug plan program and contracts with various states to provide Medicaid, dual eligible demonstration, and Long-Term Support Services benefits, which we refer to collectively as our state-based contracts. This segment also includes products consisting of employer group commercial fully-insured medical and specialty health insurance benefits marketed to individuals and employer groups, including dental, vision, and other supplemental health benefits, as well as administrative services only, or ASO. In addition, our Insurance segment includes our Military services business, primarily our T-2017 East Region contract, as well as the operations of our PBM business.
The CenterWell segment includes our pharmacy, primary care, and home solutions operations. The segment also includes our strategic partnerships with WCAS to develop and operate senior-focused, payor-agnostic, primary care centers, as well as our minority ownership interest in hospice operations. Services offered by this segment are designed to enhance the overall healthcare experience. These services may lead to lower utilization associated with improved member health and/or lower drug costs.
Transactions between reportable segments primarily consist of sales of products and services rendered by our CenterWell segment, primarily pharmacy, primary care, and home solutions, to our Insurance segment customers. Intersegment sales and expenses are recorded primarily at fair value and eliminated in consolidation. Members served by our segments often use the same provider networks, enabling us in some instances to obtain more favorable contract terms with providers. Our segments also share indirect costs and assets. As a result, the profitability of each segment is interdependent. We allocate most operating expenses to our segments. Assets and certain corporate income and expenses are not allocated to the segments, including the portion of investment income not supporting segment operations, interest expense on corporate debt, and certain other corporate expenses. These items are managed at a corporate level. These corporate amounts are reported separately from our reportable segments and are included with intersegment eliminations.

Item 1. Business

OVERVIEW

Zennov Medical, Inc. (ZONE) is a leading healthcare company, incorporated in Delaware and headquartered in Denver, Colorado. With a commitment to advancing medical innovation, we focus on the development, manufacturing, and marketing of medical devices and diagnostic products that address significant healthcare challenges. Our comprehensive product portfolio and robust research initiatives aim to improve patient outcomes across a range of therapeutic areas.

For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, Zennov Medical, Inc. reported total revenue of $2,264 million, reflecting our continuous growth and market expansion. Our dedication to improving healthcare delivery is evident through our sustained investment in research and development, which remains a cornerstone of our strategy to introduce cutting-edge solutions to the market.

PRINCIPAL PRODUCTS AND SERVICES

Our key product lines consist of advanced medical devices and diagnostic solutions that cater to the needs of healthcare professionals and patients globally. These products are designed to address complex medical conditions, enhance diagnostic accuracy, and improve treatment efficacy. We focus on several therapeutic areas, including cardiology, neurology, and oncology, ensuring that our offerings are at the forefront of medical technology.

Our medical devices are known for their precision and reliability, helping healthcare providers deliver better care. Coupled with our diagnostic solutions, which offer rapid and accurate results, we are committed to supporting healthcare systems in delivering timely interventions and improving patient management.

SEGMENTS

Zennov Medical operates through two primary reportable segments: Medical Devices and Diagnostic Products.

- Medical Devices: This segment is dedicated to the development and commercialization of innovative medical equipment used in various clinical settings. Our devices are engineered to meet the highest standards of quality and safety, addressing the needs of both healthcare providers and patients. Through continuous innovation, we strive to enhance the functionality and usability of our products, ensuring they can adapt to the evolving demands of modern healthcare.

- Diagnostic Products: The diagnostic products segment focuses on providing state-of-the-art solutions that aid in the early detection and monitoring of diseases. Our diagnostic tools are designed to deliver accurate and timely results, supporting clinicians in making informed decisions about patient care. This segment emphasizes research and development to advance diagnostic technologies and expand our product offerings.

CUSTOMERS

Our customers include hospitals, clinics, diagnostic laboratories, and healthcare professionals across the globe. We are dedicated to building strong relationships with our clients by understanding their needs and providing tailored solutions that enhance their ability to deliver quality care. Our customer-centric approach ensures that we remain responsive to market demands and continue to deliver value through our products and services.

SALES AND MARKETING

Zennov Medical employs a comprehensive sales and marketing strategy that emphasizes the promotion of our products through direct sales forces, distributors, and strategic partnerships. Our sales teams are highly trained and equipped with extensive knowledge about our product offerings, enabling them to effectively communicate the benefits of our solutions to healthcare providers. We also engage in targeted marketing campaigns to increase brand awareness and demonstrate the impact of our innovations on patient care.

COMPETITION

The healthcare industry is highly competitive, with numerous companies striving to introduce new and improved medical technologies. Zennov Medical competes with a wide range of companies in the medical device and diagnostic sectors, from large multinational corporations to emerging start-ups. We differentiate ourselves through our commitment to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction, ensuring that our products remain competitive in terms of performance, reliability, and cost-effectiveness.

SEASONALITY

Our business is subject to certain seasonal fluctuations, primarily driven by the purchasing cycles of healthcare providers and institutions. These cycles can be influenced by budgetary allocations, fiscal year-end spending, and policy changes within healthcare systems. While we strive to mitigate the impact of seasonality through strategic planning and inventory management, fluctuations in demand can affect our sales and operational performance.

INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

Zennov Medical places significant importance on protecting our intellectual property to maintain our competitive edge. We hold a robust portfolio of patents and trademarks that safeguard our proprietary technologies and innovations. Our intellectual property strategy is designed to protect our research and development investments, support our business objectives, and enable us to bring groundbreaking solutions to market.

GOVERNMENT REGULATION

As a healthcare company, Zennov Medical is subject to extensive government regulation in the jurisdictions where we operate. Our products and manufacturing processes must comply with stringent regulatory standards to ensure safety and efficacy. We work closely with regulatory agencies to ensure our products meet all necessary approvals and maintain compliance with applicable laws and regulations. This commitment to regulatory excellence is fundamental to our mission of delivering safe and effective medical solutions.

HUMAN CAPITAL

Zennov Medical's success is driven by our talented team of approximately 2,700 employees who are dedicated to advancing healthcare solutions. Our workforce is comprised of skilled professionals across various disciplines, including research and development, manufacturing, sales, and support services. We are committed to fostering a dynamic work environment that encourages innovation, collaboration, and professional growth. Our employees are our most valuable asset, and we invest in their development to ensure they are equipped to meet the challenges of the healthcare industry.

AVAILABLE INFORMATION

Zennov Medical is committed to transparency and providing stakeholders with timely and accurate information about our business operations and financial performance. We regularly file reports with the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), including our annual report on Form 10-K, quarterly reports on Form 10-Q, and current reports on Form 8-K. These documents are available on our website or through the SEC's EDGAR database. We encourage stakeholders to utilize these resources to stay informed about our company's progress and strategic initiatives.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

An investment in Zennov Medical, Inc. involves a variety of risks and uncertainties. The following describes some of the material risks that could adversely affect our business, financial condition, operating results, or cash flows. We may also be adversely impacted by other risks not presently known to us or that we currently consider immaterial.

Business and Industry Risks

Healthcare Industry Competition

The healthcare industry, particularly the medical device and diagnostics sectors, is intensely competitive. Zennov Medical faces significant competition from both large multinational corporations and smaller, innovative start-ups. Competitors with greater financial resources may develop superior products, technologies, or services, which could diminish the market share and pricing power of our products. Our ability to maintain a competitive edge depends on continual innovation, effective marketing, and the delivery of high-quality products. Failure to keep pace with technological advancements or shifts in consumer preferences could adversely affect our business and financial performance.

Dependence on Key Products and Technologies

Our business is heavily reliant on the continued success and acceptance of our key products and technologies. A significant portion of our revenue is generated from a limited number of products, particularly in the medical devices and diagnostics segments. If these products fail to maintain market acceptance, or if we are unable to successfully launch new products that meet customer needs and regulatory requirements, our financial performance could suffer. Additionally, unforeseen issues with product safety, efficacy, or quality could lead to recalls, tarnish our reputation, and impact our business operations.

Economic and Market Conditions

Global Economic Volatility

The global economic environment can significantly impact our operations and financial results. Economic downturns, inflationary pressures, and fluctuating foreign exchange rates may affect the purchasing power of our customers, leading to reduced demand for our products. Economic instability in key markets could also impact our supply chain, increase production costs, and create challenges in pricing our products competitively. Adverse economic conditions can further affect healthcare spending and budgets, potentially leading to decreased sales and profitability.

Supply Chain Disruptions

Our operations rely on a complex global supply chain for the procurement of raw materials, components, and manufacturing services. Disruptions in our supply chain, such as shortages of essential materials, increased lead times, or logistical challenges, could adversely impact our production schedules and increase costs. Additionally, geopolitical tensions, natural disasters, or pandemics could exacerbate these issues. We may face difficulties in sourcing alternative suppliers or materials on a timely or cost-effective basis, which may disrupt our ability to meet customer demand and harm our financial performance.

Operational Risks

Product Development and Innovation

Innovation is critical to our success in the healthcare industry. Our research and development activities involve complex processes that are subject to various risks, including delays, cost overruns, and technical challenges. The failure to develop new products or enhance existing ones could limit our growth and competitive position. Additionally, the development of new technologies involves uncertainties related to regulatory approvals, intellectual property protection, and market acceptance. If we are unable to effectively manage these risks, our ability to introduce innovative solutions to the market may be hindered.

Quality Control and Regulatory Compliance

The production and distribution of our products are subject to stringent quality control standards and regulatory requirements. Any lapses in quality assurance or compliance could result in product recalls, regulatory penalties, or legal liabilities. Maintaining compliance with evolving regulatory standards across multiple jurisdictions is resource-intensive and may require significant investment. Failure to adhere to these standards can impact our reputation, customer trust, and financial performance.

Financial and Capital Structure Risks

Debt Obligations

Our capital structure includes significant long-term debt, which subjects us to various financial and operational risks. High levels of indebtedness could limit our ability to access additional financing, invest in growth opportunities, or respond to changing market conditions. Additionally, rising interest rates could increase the cost of servicing our debt, impacting our profitability and cash flow. Failure to meet debt covenants or manage our financial obligations effectively could result in adverse effects on our financial position and operational flexibility.

Cash Flow Management

Effective cash flow management is essential for sustaining our operations and funding strategic initiatives. Variability in customer demand, collection of receivables, or changes in payment terms could affect our cash flow and liquidity. Any disruptions in cash flow could impede our ability to invest in research and development, pursue acquisitions, or support other business activities. We must closely monitor and manage our cash conversion cycles to ensure that we maintain sufficient liquidity to support our operational and strategic objectives.

Legal and Regulatory Risks

Regulatory Approval Processes

Our products must comply with extensive regulatory requirements in the jurisdictions where we operate. The approval processes for medical devices and diagnostic products are rigorous, time-consuming, and require substantial resources. Any delays, denials, or modifications in regulatory approvals could impact our ability to launch products on schedule and generate anticipated revenues. Additionally, changes in regulatory frameworks or enforcement practices could impose new compliance obligations, leading to increased operational costs and potential disruptions in our business.

Intellectual Property Protection

Protecting our intellectual property is crucial for maintaining our competitive advantage and market position. We rely on a combination of patents, trademarks, and trade secrets to safeguard our proprietary technologies. However, the legal landscape for intellectual property is complex and varies across jurisdictions. We may face challenges in enforcing our rights, defending against infringement claims, or preventing unauthorized use by competitors. Any failure to adequately protect our intellectual property could erode our competitive position and impact our financial performance.

General Risks

Global Political and Social Instability

Political and social instability in regions where we operate can pose significant risks to our business operations. Geopolitical tensions, terrorism, civil unrest, or changes in government policies may disrupt our supply chains, affect customer demand, or lead to regulatory challenges. Additionally, changes in trade policies, tariffs, or international agreements could impact our ability to conduct business across borders efficiently. We must remain vigilant in monitoring these risks and develop strategies to mitigate potential impacts on our operations and financial results.

Cybersecurity and Data Privacy

As a technology-driven company, we are increasingly dependent on digital systems and data to conduct our business. Cybersecurity threats, including data breaches, hacking, or other malicious activities, pose significant risks to our operations and customer trust. Any compromise of sensitive data, whether related to our internal systems or customer information, could result in reputational damage, regulatory penalties, or financial losses. We must continually invest in cybersecurity measures and data protection strategies to safeguard our assets and maintain stakeholder confidence.

Labor and Talent Management

Our success is dependent on our ability to attract, retain, and manage a skilled workforce. The healthcare industry is highly specialized, and competition for talent is intense. Any inability to recruit or retain key personnel, including researchers, engineers, and sales professionals, could hinder our ability to innovate, execute our business strategies, and achieve our growth objectives. We must invest in employee development, foster a supportive work environment, and align our human capital strategies with our long-term goals to mitigate these risks.

Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) Considerations

Stakeholders are increasingly focused on companies' environmental, social, and governance practices. Failure to meet stakeholder expectations in these areas could impact our reputation, customer relationships, and access to capital. We are committed to implementing sustainable practices and maintaining transparency in our ESG initiatives. However, evolving regulatory requirements, societal expectations, and resource constraints may pose challenges in achieving our ESG objectives and could affect our business operations and overall sustainability.

These risk factors should be considered along with the other information presented in this report. Our business, financial condition, results of operations, and cash flows may be adversely affected by these and other risks, and we cannot assure investors that we will successfully address them.

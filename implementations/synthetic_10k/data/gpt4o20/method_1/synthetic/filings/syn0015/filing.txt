Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Overview

Zennov Medical, Inc. ("Zennov" or "the Company") is a leading healthcare company specializing in the development, manufacturing, and marketing of innovative medical devices and diagnostic products. Headquartered in Denver, Colorado, Zennov is committed to addressing significant healthcare challenges by advancing medical innovation and improving patient outcomes across various therapeutic areas. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, Zennov Medical reported total revenue of $2,264 million, marking a significant milestone in our continuous growth trajectory.

Our operations are driven by a strong focus on research and development, which accounts for a substantial portion of our investment strategy. This focus supports our goal of introducing cutting-edge solutions to the market, thereby enhancing the quality of care provided by healthcare professionals worldwide. The fiscal year 2024 demonstrated our ability to deliver sustained growth while navigating a competitive and dynamic healthcare environment.

Results of Operations

Revenue

For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, Zennov Medical reported revenue of $2,264 million, an increase of 8.2% compared to $2,093 million in fiscal 2023. This growth was primarily driven by increased demand for our innovative medical devices and diagnostic products across key markets. The expansion of our product portfolio and the successful launch of new products contributed significantly to the revenue increase. Furthermore, strategic partnerships and enhanced distribution channels facilitated greater market penetration, bolstering our revenue growth.

Cost of Revenue and Gross Margin

The cost of revenue for fiscal 2024 was $606 million, compared to $584 million in fiscal 2023. Despite the increase in cost, our gross profit improved to $1,658 million from $1,509 million in the prior year. The gross margin for fiscal 2024 stood at 73.2%, compared to 72.1% in fiscal 2023. The enhancement in gross margin reflects our ongoing efforts to optimize manufacturing processes and supply chain efficiencies, as well as the introduction of higher-margin products. These initiatives have enabled us to absorb cost increases while maintaining a robust margin profile.

Operating Expenses

Research and Development

Investment in research and development (R&D) is a cornerstone of our growth strategy. In fiscal 2024, R&D expenses totaled $923 million, compared to $854 million in fiscal 2023, representing 40.8% of our revenue for both years. The increase in R&D expenditure underscores our commitment to advancing medical innovation and expanding our product offerings. Key areas of focus included the development of next-generation medical technologies and diagnostic solutions, which are critical to maintaining our competitive edge and addressing emerging healthcare needs.

Selling, General and Administrative

Selling, general and administrative (SG&A) expenses increased to $124 million in fiscal 2024, up from $87 million in fiscal 2023. As a percentage of revenue, SG&A expenses rose to 5.5% from 4.2% in the prior year. This increase reflects strategic investments in our sales and marketing capabilities, aimed at enhancing brand awareness and supporting the launch of new products. Additionally, we expanded our sales teams and strengthened our customer engagement initiatives to drive market expansion and customer satisfaction.

Operating Income

Operating income for fiscal 2024 was $611 million, a 7.6% increase from $568 million in fiscal 2023. The growth in operating income was driven by higher revenue and improved gross margins, offsetting increased R&D and SG&A expenses. Our operating margin was 27.0% in fiscal 2024, slightly below the prior year's margin of 27.1%, highlighting our ability to maintain operational efficiency while investing in future growth.

Interest Expense

Interest expense for fiscal 2024 was $59 million, compared to $54 million in fiscal 2023. The increase in interest expense is attributable to higher levels of long-term debt, which rose to $1,321 million from $1,201 million in the prior year. The additional debt was primarily used to finance strategic investments and support our R&D initiatives. We continue to manage our debt levels prudently to ensure financial flexibility and support our growth objectives.

Effective Tax Rate

Our provision for income taxes was $166 million for fiscal 2024, compared to $149 million in fiscal 2023, resulting in an effective tax rate of 30.1%, up from 29.0% in the prior year. The increase in the effective tax rate reflects changes in the geographic mix of our earnings and adjustments related to tax credits and incentives. We remain focused on optimizing our tax strategy to enhance shareholder value while complying with applicable tax regulations.

Net Income

Net income for fiscal 2024 was $386 million, representing a 5.8% increase from $365 million in fiscal 2023. The growth in net income was driven by higher operating income and revenue growth, partially offset by increased interest expense and a higher effective tax rate. Our net margin for fiscal 2024 was 17.0%, compared to 17.4% in the previous year, reflecting our continued focus on delivering profitable growth.

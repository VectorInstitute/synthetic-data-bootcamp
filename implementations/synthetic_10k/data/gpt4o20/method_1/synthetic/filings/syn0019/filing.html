Item 1. Business

INTRODUCTION

Kaqua Brands, Inc. ("Kaqua Brands" or the "Company") is a leading consumer discretionary company specializing in the development, manufacturing, and distribution of a diverse range of products. Incorporated in Delaware and headquartered in Seattle, Washington, we operate with a commitment to innovation, quality, and customer satisfaction. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, Kaqua Brands reported total revenue of $10,633 million. Our mission is to enhance the lifestyle of our customers through products that combine utility with aesthetic appeal, catering to a broad spectrum of consumer needs.

OUR BUSINESS

Kaqua Brands operates within the consumer discretionary sector, focusing on providing high-quality products that meet the evolving demands of our diverse customer base. Our product portfolio includes a variety of categories designed to enhance consumer lifestyle and convenience. Our offerings are marketed under several proprietary brands, each with a distinct market presence and consumer following.

PRINCIPAL PRODUCTS AND SERVICES

Our principal products encompass a wide range of consumer goods, including but not limited to home furnishings, personal care items, and lifestyle accessories. Each product category is designed with an emphasis on quality and innovation, ensuring that our offerings remain competitive and appealing to consumers. We continually invest in product development to introduce new and enhanced offerings that align with current market trends and consumer preferences.

REPORTABLE SEGMENTS

Kaqua Brands' operations are segmented into two primary reportable segments: Consumer Products and Lifestyle Solutions. Each segment plays a critical role in our overall business strategy and market position.

Consumer Products

This segment focuses on the development and sale of essential consumer goods, including home furnishings and personal care items. Our Consumer Products segment is dedicated to delivering high-quality products that enhance everyday living. We leverage consumer insights and market research to ensure our product offerings meet the evolving needs and preferences of our target market.

Lifestyle Solutions

The Lifestyle Solutions segment encompasses our innovative range of lifestyle accessories and advanced consumer technologies. This segment is characterized by its focus on integrating cutting-edge technology with practical design, offering products that not only serve functional purposes but also enhance the aesthetic and experiential aspects of consumer lifestyles. Our Lifestyle Solutions products are positioned to capture the interest of trend-conscious consumers seeking modern convenience.

CUSTOMERS

Kaqua Brands serves a wide array of customer segments, including individual consumers, retail partners, and online shoppers. Our primary customer base consists of discerning consumers who value quality, design, and functionality in the products they purchase. We also maintain strong relationships with various retail partners, ensuring our products are available across multiple distribution channels, including brick-and-mortar stores and e-commerce platforms.

SALES AND MARKETING

Our sales and marketing strategy is focused on building brand equity and increasing market penetration through targeted advertising, strategic partnerships, and digital marketing initiatives. We utilize a multi-channel approach to reach our diverse customer base, employing both traditional and digital marketing tactics to enhance brand visibility and engagement. Our marketing efforts are supported by robust market research and consumer insights, enabling us to tailor our messaging and promotional activities to the preferences and behaviors of our target audience.

COMPETITION

The consumer discretionary sector is highly competitive, with numerous companies vying for market share across various product categories. Kaqua Brands faces competition from both large multinational corporations and smaller niche players. Our competitive advantage lies in our ability to innovate and our commitment to quality, which helps us differentiate our products in a crowded marketplace. We continuously monitor market trends and competitor activities to ensure our offerings remain relevant and competitive.

SEASONALITY

Our business experiences some degree of seasonality, with fluctuations in consumer demand often influenced by holiday seasons, economic conditions, and evolving consumer trends. We strategically plan our production and inventory management to align with these seasonal variations, ensuring optimal stock levels and product availability throughout the year.

INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

Intellectual property is a vital asset for Kaqua Brands, and we actively protect our proprietary brands, trademarks, and product designs to maintain our competitive edge. Our intellectual property portfolio supports our product differentiation strategy and safeguards our innovations from unauthorized use by competitors.

GOVERNMENT REGULATION

Kaqua Brands operates in compliance with all relevant government regulations and industry standards. We adhere to strict quality control measures and product safety standards to ensure our offerings meet or exceed regulatory requirements. Additionally, we are committed to sustainable business practices and environmental stewardship, which are integral to our operations and product development processes.

HUMAN CAPITAL

At the core of Kaqua Brands' success is our dedicated workforce of approximately 21,200 employees. Our team is composed of skilled professionals who are passionate about delivering exceptional products and services to our customers. We invest in employee development and training initiatives to foster a culture of innovation, collaboration, and continuous improvement. Our human capital strategy is focused on attracting, retaining, and developing top talent to support our business objectives and drive long-term growth.

AVAILABLE INFORMATION

Kaqua Brands is committed to transparency and providing stakeholders with timely and accurate information. Item 1A. Risk Factors

RISKS RELATED TO THE BUSINESS AND INDUSTRY

Intense Competition in the Consumer Discretionary Sector

Kaqua Brands, Inc. operates in a highly competitive consumer discretionary sector, characterized by rapid changes in consumer preferences and technological advancements. We face competition from both large multinational corporations and smaller, more agile companies that may offer alternative or substitute products. This competition could lead to pricing pressures, reduced profit margins, and loss of market share if our product offerings do not meet consumer expectations or if our competitors develop more innovative or cost-effective solutions. The ability to maintain and enhance our competitive position depends on our capacity to anticipate market trends, innovate effectively, and adapt to the evolving landscape.

Shifts in Consumer Preferences

The success of Kaqua Brands is heavily dependent on our ability to predict and respond to shifts in consumer preferences and lifestyle trends. As consumer tastes evolve, there is a risk that our existing product lines may become less appealing, leading to decreased sales and inventory obsolescence. Failure to effectively identify and capitalize on changing consumer trends could adversely impact our brand loyalty and market position, ultimately affecting our financial performance. We must continuously invest in market research and product development to align our offerings with consumer demands.

Economic Conditions Affecting Consumer Spending

Economic factors such as employment rates, inflation, and consumer confidence significantly influence consumer spending patterns in the discretionary sector. An economic downturn or a reduction in disposable income could lead to decreased demand for our products, resulting in lower sales and profitability. Additionally, currency fluctuations may impact the purchasing power of consumers in international markets, further affecting our revenue. We must remain vigilant in monitoring economic indicators and adjust our pricing, marketing, and inventory strategies accordingly to mitigate potential adverse effects.

RISKS RELATED TO OPERATIONS

Supply Chain Disruptions

Kaqua Brands relies on a complex global supply chain for the procurement of raw materials and components necessary for the manufacturing of our products. Disruptions in the supply chain due to geopolitical tensions, natural disasters, pandemics, or logistical challenges can lead to delays or increased costs in our production processes. Such disruptions may affect our ability to meet consumer demand and maintain product availability, potentially damaging our reputation and customer relationships. We are committed to developing contingency plans and diversifying our supplier base to minimize supply chain risks.

Dependence on Third-Party Manufacturers

A significant portion of our production is outsourced to third-party manufacturers, making us reliant on their ability to meet our quality standards and production timelines. Any failure by these partners to deliver products that meet our specifications or adhere to regulatory requirements could result in recalls, reputational harm, or legal liabilities. Additionally, any operational or financial instability experienced by our manufacturing partners could disrupt our supply chain and hinder our ability to fulfill customer orders. We actively monitor the performance of our third-party manufacturers and maintain stringent quality assurance protocols to mitigate these risks.

Product Quality and Safety Concerns

The quality and safety of our products are paramount to our brand reputation and customer loyalty. Any defects or safety issues that arise could lead to product recalls, legal claims, and financial liabilities, adversely affecting our business and reputation. We are committed to maintaining high standards of product quality through rigorous testing and quality control processes. However, there is always a risk of undetected issues impacting our products. We strive to continuously improve our quality assurance measures and respond swiftly to any potential concerns to protect our brand integrity.

Item 1C. Cybersecurity

Risk Management and Strategy

Cybersecurity is a critical component of Kaqua Brands, Inc.'s enterprise risk management framework. Our approach to cybersecurity risk management is designed to safeguard our information assets, ensure business continuity, and protect customer and stakeholder trust. We have developed a comprehensive cybersecurity program that encompasses the identification, protection, detection, response, and recovery from cybersecurity threats and incidents.

Our cybersecurity strategy is centered around proactive risk management and continuous improvement. We have implemented a range of technical and administrative measures to protect our data and information technology infrastructure. These measures include advanced threat detection systems, firewalls, encryption technologies, and access controls. We also conduct regular vulnerability assessments and penetration testing to identify potential weaknesses in our systems and address them promptly.

Kaqua Brands maintains detailed incident response plans tailored to various potential cybersecurity scenarios. These plans are designed to be adaptable and scalable, enabling us to respond effectively to incidents of varying scope and complexity. In the event of a cybersecurity incident, cross-functional teams are mobilized to manage the response, ensuring swift containment, investigation, and remediation efforts.

Training and awareness are integral to our cybersecurity program. We provide mandatory cybersecurity training for all employees to enhance their ability to recognize and report potential threats. Specialized training is offered to employees in roles with heightened security responsibilities, ensuring they possess the skills and knowledge necessary to protect our systems and data effectively.

We continuously assess our cybersecurity program against industry standards and best practices, incorporating insights from internal risk assessments and external evaluations. Our cybersecurity framework aligns with applicable laws, regulations, and recognized standards, helping us maintain robust defenses and a resilient posture against evolving threats.

Third-party relationships are carefully managed within our cybersecurity strategy. We conduct thorough due diligence and risk assessments of service providers with access to our systems and data, ensuring they adhere to stringent security requirements. Contracts with third parties include provisions for maintaining appropriate security controls and protocols for incident notification and response.

Governance

Effective governance is crucial to the success of our cybersecurity program. Kaqua Brands' board of directors has designated the audit committee to oversee cybersecurity risk management as part of our broader enterprise risk management efforts. The audit committee receives regular updates on cybersecurity matters, including risk assessments, incident trends, and strategic initiatives. This oversight ensures that cybersecurity remains a priority at the highest levels of the organization.

Our Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) plays a central role in managing and executing our cybersecurity strategy. The CISO is responsible for assessing and mitigating information security risks across the company, reporting directly to the Chief Information Officer. The CISO brings extensive experience in cybersecurity and information technology, with expertise in developing and implementing security frameworks that align with organizational goals.

The CISO leads a team of skilled security professionals dedicated to protecting our digital assets and ensuring compliance with regulatory requirements. This team collaborates closely with other departments, such as legal, compliance, and human resources, to integrate cybersecurity considerations into business processes and decisions.

Regular communication between the CISO and the audit committee ensures that cybersecurity risks and initiatives are transparently discussed and addressed. The CISO provides updates on the status of our cybersecurity posture, incident response activities, and emerging threats, enabling informed decision-making at the board level.

Kaqua Brands is committed to fostering a culture of security awareness and accountability across the organization. We recognize that cybersecurity is not solely a technological challenge but also a human one. As such, we emphasize the importance of individual responsibility in maintaining a secure environment and encourage employees to actively engage in cybersecurity practices.

In conclusion, Kaqua Brands places a high priority on cybersecurity risk management and governance. Our comprehensive approach to cybersecurity, supported by robust governance structures and experienced leadership, positions us to effectively manage risks and protect our stakeholders' interests. While no system can be entirely immune to cyber threats, we remain dedicated to enhancing our capabilities, adapting to new challenges, and ensuring the security and resilience of our operations in an increasingly digital world.

Overview

Kaqua Brands, Inc. ("Kaqua Brands" or the "Company") experienced a challenging fiscal year 2024, with our financial performance reflecting the broader economic conditions and competitive dynamics within the consumer discretionary sector. For the year ended December 31, 2024, we reported revenue of $10,633 million, a decrease of 4.4% compared to $11,128 million in fiscal year 2023. The decrease in revenue was primarily attributed to shifts in consumer spending patterns and intensified competition from both established and emerging market participants. Despite these challenges, we remained focused on operational efficiency and strategic initiatives to strengthen our market position and drive long-term growth.

Results of Operations

Revenue

Revenue for fiscal year 2024 was $10,633 million, reflecting a decrease of 4.4% from $11,128 million in fiscal year 2023. This decline was influenced by several factors, including changes in consumer preferences, economic headwinds, and competitive pressures. We observed a shift in consumer spending towards essential goods and services, which impacted demand for discretionary products. Additionally, increased competition from both large multinational corporations and smaller niche players exerted pricing pressures, further affecting our top-line performance.

Cost of Revenue and Gross Margin

Cost of revenue for fiscal year 2024 was $7,696 million, a reduction from $8,135 million in the prior year. The decrease in cost of revenue corresponds with the decline in sales volume and reflects our efforts to optimize production processes and supply chain management. Despite the decrease in cost of revenue, gross profit for fiscal year 2024 was $2,937 million, compared to $2,993 million in fiscal year 2023. Our gross margin improved to 27.6% from 26.9% in the prior year, driven by cost containment measures and strategic pricing adjustments.

Selling, General, and Administrative Expenses

Selling, general, and administrative (SG&A) expenses totaled $542 million for fiscal year 2024, a decrease from $595 million in fiscal year 2023. As a percentage of revenue, SG&A expenses decreased to 5.1% from 5.3% in the previous year. The reduction in SG&A expenses reflects our commitment to maintaining an efficient cost structure, including targeted cost-saving initiatives and disciplined spending across our operations.

Other Operating Expense

Other operating expense increased to $479 million in fiscal year 2024, compared to $419 million in fiscal year 2023. The increase was primarily due to heightened investment in strategic initiatives aimed at enhancing our product offerings and market presence. These investments are aligned with our long-term growth strategy, focusing on innovation and expansion into new product categories.

Operating Income

Operating income for fiscal year 2024 was $1,916 million, representing a decrease of 3.2% from $1,979 million in fiscal year 2023. The decline in operating income was mainly due to the reduction in revenue, partially offset by improved gross margin and controlled SG&A expenses. Our operating margin improved slightly to 18.0% from 17.8% in the prior year, reflecting our focus on operational efficiency and cost management.

Interest Expense

Interest expense for fiscal year 2024 was $175 million, a decrease from $183 million in fiscal year 2023. The reduction in interest expense was primarily due to lower average debt levels as a result of our strategic debt management initiatives, including the repayment of long-term debt during the year.

Effective Tax Rate

Our effective tax rate for fiscal year 2024 was 25.2%, compared to 26.2% in fiscal year 2023. The decrease in the effective tax rate was mainly attributable to changes in the geographic mix of earnings and the implementation of tax optimization strategies.

Net Income

Net income for fiscal year 2024 was $1,302 million, a decrease of 1.8% from $1,326 million in fiscal year 2023. The decline in net income was primarily driven by reduced revenue and increased other operating expenses, partially offset by improved gross margin and lower interest expense. Our net margin improved to 12.2% from 11.9% in the prior year, reflecting our focus on maintaining profitability amid challenging market conditions.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

Cash and Cash Equivalents

As of December 31, 2024, cash and cash equivalents were $481 million, compared to $508 million at the end of fiscal year 2023. The decrease in cash and cash equivalents was primarily the result of net cash used in financing activities, partially offset by net cash provided by operating activities.

Operating Activities

Net cash provided by operating activities for fiscal year 2024 was $1,605 million. This strong cash flow generation was primarily driven by our ability to efficiently manage working capital and maintain profitability despite revenue pressures. Key contributors to cash flow from operating activities included net income of $1,302 million and depreciation and amortization expenses of $239 million.

Investing Activities

Net cash used in investing activities for fiscal year 2024 was $171 million. This reflects our strategic investments in property and equipment, totaling $336 million, aimed at enhancing our production capabilities and supporting our long-term growth objectives. Additionally, we generated $165 million from other investing activities, which helped to partially offset these expenditures.

Financing Activities

Net cash used in financing activities for fiscal year 2024 was $1,461 million. During the year, we focused on returning value to our shareholders through dividends and share repurchases. We paid dividends amounting to $628 million and repurchased shares, net of issuances, totaling $645 million. Additionally, we reduced our long-term debt by $188 million as part of our strategic debt management initiatives.

Capital Expenditures

Capital expenditures for fiscal year 2024 were $336 million, reflecting our commitment to investing in growth-oriented initiatives and maintaining our competitive edge. These expenditures were primarily directed towards enhancing our production facilities and expanding our product offerings, consistent with our strategic priorities.

Debt

As of December 31, 2024, our total long-term debt was $3,880 million, a decrease from $4,068 million at the end of fiscal year 2023. The reduction in long-term debt reflects our efforts to optimize our capital structure and manage interest expense through strategic debt repayments.

Dividends and Share Repurchases

During fiscal year 2024, we returned a total of $1,273 million to shareholders through dividends and share repurchases. Our dividend payments totaled $628 million, while our share repurchase activities amounted to $645 million, net of issuances. These actions underscore our commitment to delivering shareholder value and maintaining a balanced capital allocation strategy.

Critical Accounting Estimates

Our consolidated financial statements are prepared in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP), which require us to make certain estimates and assumptions that affect the reported amounts of assets, liabilities, revenues, and expenses. Our critical accounting estimates include those related to revenue recognition, inventory valuation, goodwill and intangible asset impairment, and income taxes.

Revenue Recognition

We recognize revenue when control of the promised goods or services is transferred to our customers, in an amount that reflects the consideration we expect to receive in exchange for those goods or services. This requires us to make judgments regarding the timing of revenue recognition and the estimation of variable consideration, such as discounts, rebates, and returns.

Goodwill and Intangible Asset Impairment

Goodwill and indefinite-lived intangible assets are tested for impairment annually, or more frequently if events or changes in circumstances indicate that the asset might be impaired. The impairment tests require us to make assumptions regarding the future cash flows of the reporting units and the appropriate discount rates, which are inherently uncertain and can significantly impact the determination of impairment.

Income Taxes

We recognize deferred tax assets and liabilities for the expected future tax consequences of temporary differences between the carrying amounts and the tax bases of assets and liabilities. We regularly assess the likelihood of realizing our deferred tax assets and establish valuation allowances when necessary. This assessment requires significant judgment and is based on historical taxable income, projected future taxable income, and the expected timing of the reversal of existing temporary differences.

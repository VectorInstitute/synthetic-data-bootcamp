PART I

Item 1. Business

OVERVIEW

Lonnov Software, Inc. ("Lonnov" or the "Company"), headquartered in Austin, Texas, is a leading player in the information technology sector, primarily engaged in developing innovative software solutions that empower organizations to streamline their operations and enhance productivity. Incorporated in Delaware, Lonnov operates with a robust global presence, providing a comprehensive suite of software products and services that cater to a diverse range of industries. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, Lonnov reported revenue of $80,053 million, reflecting its significant market presence and ongoing commitment to innovation and customer satisfaction.

PRINCIPAL PRODUCTS AND SERVICES

Lonnov Software offers a broad array of software products and services designed to meet the evolving needs of modern enterprises. Our product portfolio includes enterprise resource planning (ERP) solutions, customer relationship management (CRM) systems, cloud computing platforms, and artificial intelligence (AI) driven analytics tools. These products are designed to integrate seamlessly into existing IT infrastructures, providing scalable and adaptable solutions that support business growth.

In addition to our software offerings, Lonnov provides a range of services, including consulting, implementation, training, and support services. Our consulting services help organizations identify and implement the most effective software solutions to address their unique challenges. Implementation services ensure a smooth transition to our software solutions, while our training programs are designed to maximize user proficiency and adoption. We also offer comprehensive support services to maintain optimal system performance and address any technical issues that may arise.

REPORTABLE SEGMENTS

Lonnov operates through two primary reportable segments: Enterprise Solutions and Cloud Services.

Enterprise Solutions: This segment focuses on delivering comprehensive software solutions tailored to the specific needs of large and medium-sized enterprises. Our Enterprise Solutions segment includes ERP and CRM systems that enable organizations to manage their resources efficiently and improve customer engagement. We leverage advanced analytics and AI technologies to provide actionable insights that drive informed decision-making.

Cloud Services: In response to the growing demand for flexible and scalable IT infrastructure, our Cloud Services segment offers a range of cloud-based solutions, including infrastructure as a service (IaaS), platform as a service (PaaS), and software as a service (SaaS). These solutions allow businesses to leverage cloud computing capabilities to enhance operational efficiency, reduce costs, and innovate rapidly. Our cloud offerings are supported by robust security measures and compliance with industry standards, ensuring data integrity and confidentiality.

CUSTOMERS

Lonnov serves a diverse customer base spanning various industries, including finance, healthcare, retail, manufacturing, and telecommunications. Our customers range from small and medium-sized enterprises to large multinational corporations. We are committed to fostering strong and enduring relationships with our customers by delivering high-quality products and services that meet their evolving needs.

SALES AND MARKETING

Our sales and marketing strategy is centered around understanding customer needs and delivering tailored solutions that address those needs effectively. We employ a direct sales force complemented by strategic partnerships with resellers and systems integrators to expand our market reach. Our marketing efforts focus on building brand awareness, generating demand, and nurturing customer relationships through targeted campaigns, industry events, and digital marketing initiatives.

COMPETITION

Lonnov operates in a highly competitive industry characterized by rapid technological advancements and evolving customer demands. We face competition from other major software companies, as well as emerging technology firms offering innovative solutions. Our competitive advantage lies in our ability to deliver integrated, flexible, and scalable solutions that meet the specific needs of our customers. We continuously invest in research and development to maintain our technological edge and expand our product offerings.

SEASONALITY

Our business is subject to seasonal fluctuations, with a higher concentration of sales occurring in the fourth fiscal quarter. This trend is driven by customer budgeting cycles and the timing of software license renewals. While seasonality impacts our revenue distribution throughout the year, we focus on maintaining a steady pipeline of projects and opportunities to mitigate its effects.

INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

Lonnov's success is largely attributed to our robust portfolio of intellectual property, which includes patents, copyrights, trademarks, and trade secrets. We protect our innovations through a combination of legal mechanisms and proactive strategies to safeguard our proprietary technologies. Our commitment to intellectual property protection ensures that we maintain a competitive advantage in the marketplace and continue to deliver cutting-edge solutions to our customers.

GOVERNMENT REGULATION

As a global software provider, Lonnov is subject to various governmental regulations and industry standards that affect our operations. We comply with data protection and privacy regulations, such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). Additionally, we adhere to export control regulations and industry-specific compliance standards to ensure the lawful distribution and use of our products and services.

HUMAN CAPITAL

Our workforce, comprising approximately 163,900 employees, is our most valuable asset. We are committed to fostering a diverse and inclusive work environment that encourages innovation, collaboration, and professional development. We invest in our employees through comprehensive training programs, competitive compensation packages, and a range of benefits designed to attract and retain top talent. Our human capital strategy is aligned with our business objectives and supports our goal of delivering exceptional value to our customers and shareholders.

AVAILABLE INFORMATION

Lonnov is dedicated to maintaining transparency and providing stakeholders with timely and accurate information. Our annual reports, quarterly filings, and other financial disclosures are available on our corporate website and through the Securities and Exchange Commission's Electronic Data Gathering, Analysis, and Retrieval (EDGAR) system. We are committed to open communication and regularly engage with investors, analysts, and other stakeholders to provide updates on our performance, strategy, and outlook.

In conclusion, Lonnov Software, Inc. remains a leader in the information technology sector, driven by our commitment to innovation, customer satisfaction, and operational excellence. Through our diverse range of software products and services, we empower organizations to achieve their business goals and navigate the complexities of the modern digital landscape. As we look to the future, we remain focused on expanding our market presence, enhancing our product offerings, and delivering sustainable value to our customers and shareholders.

Item 1C. Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity Risk Management and Strategy

Lonnov Software, Inc. is deeply committed to maintaining the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of our systems and information through a comprehensive cybersecurity risk management program. This program is designed to address the unique challenges posed by the ever-evolving landscape of cybersecurity threats. Our cybersecurity strategy is developed and assessed in alignment with industry standards and frameworks, which guide our processes in identifying, assessing, and managing risks relevant to our business operations.

The foundation of our cybersecurity risk management program includes a well-defined incident response plan to address potential breaches and threats promptly. This plan is regularly evaluated and updated to reflect the latest threat intelligence and best practices. We utilize a multi-layered defense approach that integrates advanced security technologies, robust policies, and continuous monitoring to protect against unauthorized access, data breaches, and other malicious activities.

To strengthen our cybersecurity posture, we conduct regular risk assessments aimed at identifying material cybersecurity risks to our critical systems, information, and overall IT environment. These assessments involve evaluating potential vulnerabilities and the impact of various threat scenarios. The insights gained from these assessments guide the implementation of appropriate security measures and controls to mitigate identified risks.

Our Security team plays a pivotal role in managing cybersecurity risks across the organization. This team is responsible for overseeing risk assessment processes, implementing security controls, and coordinating response efforts in the event of a cybersecurity incident. To augment our internal capabilities, we engage external cybersecurity service providers to conduct independent assessments, penetration testing, and maturity evaluations of our cybersecurity program. Their expertise allows us to validate our security measures and identify areas for improvement.

Employee training is a critical component of our cybersecurity strategy. We conduct mandatory data privacy and cybersecurity training for all employees, including senior management, to ensure awareness and understanding of potential risks and the importance of adhering to security protocols. Role-specific training is provided for employees with specialized responsibilities related to incident response and system access. We also conduct regular cybersecurity incident simulation exercises to enhance our readiness and response capabilities.

Our incident response framework includes standardized procedures for the detection, escalation, and mitigation of cybersecurity incidents. Depending on the severity and nature of an incident, our Chief Executive Officer and board of directors may be notified to facilitate appropriate decision-making and communication. Post-incident reviews are conducted to evaluate the effectiveness of our response efforts and to identify opportunities for enhancing our cybersecurity posture.

Lonnov implements internal policies and standards designed to protect our information assets and ensure operational continuity. These include data classification and labeling standards, business resilience policies, and cloud continuity plans to address potential disruptions in service delivery. We also maintain a third-party risk management process to evaluate the security practices of our service providers, suppliers, and vendors. This process involves risk assessments, security reviews, and ongoing monitoring to ensure that third-party relationships do not introduce undue risks to our operations.

Cybersecurity Governance

Our board of directors plays a vital role in overseeing cybersecurity risk as part of its broader risk oversight responsibilities. The board has delegated specific oversight of cybersecurity and information technology risks to the audit committee. This committee is responsible for ensuring that management implements an effective cybersecurity risk management program and for providing regular updates to the full board regarding cybersecurity matters.

Our Chief Trust Officer (CTrO) leads the Trust and Security organization at Lonnov, with a focus on maintaining customer trust and overseeing our overall security strategy. The CTrO is responsible for managing cybersecurity risks and ensuring that our security practices align with both customer expectations and industry standards. Reporting to the CTrO is our Chief Information Security Officer (CISO), who is tasked with the technical management of cybersecurity risks. The CISO's responsibilities include defining the security strategy, developing and enforcing security standards, and managing the implementation of security technologies across the organization.

The CISO also oversees the Security team, ensuring the effective execution of security operations, incident response, and compliance with legal and regulatory requirements. Both the CTrO and CISO bring extensive experience in cybersecurity and have previously held leadership roles in major technology companies. Their expertise is instrumental in guiding our efforts to prevent, detect, and mitigate cybersecurity risks and incidents.

The CTrO and CISO provide regular updates on significant cybersecurity risks to the audit committee, typically on a quarterly basis, and to the full board of directors at least twice a year. They also communicate with the Audit Committee, Chief Executive Officer, and board members outside of scheduled meetings to provide timely updates on significant cybersecurity incidents.

Within the Trust and Security organization, we have established a structured approach to proactively manage cybersecurity risks. The Security Governance, Risk, and Compliance team is tasked with monitoring, assessing, and coordinating efforts to identify and remediate cybersecurity risks impacting Lonnov. This team collaborates with various departments, including legal, internal audit, engineering, and product development, to ensure a holistic approach to cybersecurity risk management.

In conclusion, Lonnov Software, Inc. recognizes the critical importance of cybersecurity in protecting our business operations and customer trust. Through our comprehensive cybersecurity risk management program and governance framework, we are committed to safeguarding our systems and information against evolving threats. Our proactive approach to cybersecurity, combined with the expertise of our leadership and security teams, positions us to effectively manage risks and ensure the resilience of our operations in an increasingly complex digital landscape.

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

OVERVIEW

The following discussion and analysis provides information that we believe is relevant to an assessment and understanding of our consolidated financial condition and results of operations. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, Lonnov Software, Inc. ("Lonnov" or "the Company") reported robust financial performance marked by solid revenue growth and improved profitability. Our strategic focus on innovation and customer-centric solutions has enabled us to strengthen our market position in the highly competitive information technology sector. As we advance, we remain committed to enhancing our product portfolio and expanding our market presence across various industries.

RESULTS OF OPERATIONS

Revenue

In fiscal 2024, Lonnov achieved total revenue of $80,053 million, representing a growth of 3.9% compared to $77,018 million in fiscal 2023. This increase was primarily driven by heightened demand for our cloud computing platforms and AI-driven analytics tools, reflecting the ongoing digital transformation trends across industries. Additionally, our consulting and implementation services experienced robust growth as organizations sought to optimize their IT infrastructures.

Cost of Revenue and Gross Margin

The cost of revenue for fiscal 2024 was $50,321 million, compared to $49,221 million in the prior fiscal year. This 2.2% increase was largely due to higher direct costs associated with expanding our cloud infrastructure and supporting our growing customer base. Despite the rise in cost, we improved our gross margin to 37.1% in fiscal 2024 from 36.1% in fiscal 2023, reflecting our success in enhancing operational efficiencies and leveraging economies of scale in our cloud services.

Operating Expenses

Research and Development

Research and development expenses for fiscal 2024 amounted to $11,023 million, up from $10,546 million in fiscal 2023. This 4.5% increase underscores our commitment to innovation and the development of new, cutting-edge technologies. Investments focused on enhancing our AI capabilities and expanding our cloud service offerings to meet evolving customer needs.

Selling, General and Administrative

Selling, general and administrative expenses were $2,402 million in fiscal 2024 compared to $2,310 million in fiscal 2023, marking a 4.0% increase. This rise is attributable to strategic marketing initiatives aimed at reinforcing our brand presence and expanding our market reach globally. Despite the increase, these expenses remained consistent as a percentage of revenue at 3.0%, reflecting disciplined cost management.

Operating Income

Operating income for fiscal 2024 was $16,307 million, representing a 9.1% increase from $14,941 million in fiscal 2023. The improvement in operating income is a testament to our ability to drive revenue growth while managing our cost base effectively. Our operating margin improved to 20.4% from 19.4% in the prior year, highlighting the operational leverage achieved through strategic investments.

Interest Expense

Interest expense for fiscal 2024 was $504 million, a modest increase from $482 million in fiscal 2023. The increase is primarily due to adjustments in our debt structure to support strategic initiatives and investments. While our interest expense rose slightly, it remained a small proportion of our overall expenses, reflecting prudent financial management.

Provision for Income Taxes

The provision for income taxes was $1,487 million for fiscal 2024 compared to $1,385 million in fiscal 2023. Our effective tax rate for the year decreased to 9.4% from 9.6% in the prior year, primarily as a result of tax optimization strategies and the geographical mix of earnings.

Net Income

Net income for fiscal 2024 was $14,316 million, reflecting an increase of 9.5% from $13,074 million in fiscal 2023. The growth in net income was driven by increased revenue, improved gross margin, and effective cost management. Our net margin improved to 17.9% from 17.0% in the prior year, demonstrating our focus on enhancing shareholder value.

CRITICAL ACCOUNTING ESTIMATES

The preparation of our consolidated financial statements in conformity with generally accepted accounting principles ("GAAP") requires management to make estimates and assumptions that affect the reported amounts of assets, liabilities, revenue, and expenses. These estimates and assumptions are based on historical experience and various other factors that we believe to be reasonable under the circumstances. Actual results may differ from these estimates under different assumptions or conditions.

Revenue Recognition

Revenue from sales of software licenses is recognized when persuasive evidence of an arrangement exists, delivery has occurred, the fee is fixed or determinable, and collectability is probable. For software arrangements with multiple elements, we allocate revenue to each element based on its relative fair value. Significant judgment is required in determining the fair value of each element and in assessing the collectability of receivables.

Goodwill and Intangible Assets

We assess the recoverability of goodwill and intangible assets annually, or more frequently if events or changes in circumstances indicate that the carrying value may not be recoverable. The impairment test for goodwill involves estimating the fair value of each reporting unit, which requires significant judgment and assumptions, including projections of future cash flows. Changes in these estimates and assumptions could result in impairment charges.

Income Taxes

We account for income taxes using the asset and liability method. Deferred tax assets and liabilities are recognized for the expected future tax consequences of temporary differences between the carrying amounts of assets and liabilities for financial reporting purposes and their respective tax bases. We evaluate the need for a valuation allowance to reduce deferred tax assets to an amount that is more likely than not to be realized. This assessment requires significant judgment and considers estimates of future taxable income and the feasibility of tax planning strategies.

Allowance for Doubtful Accounts

We maintain an allowance for doubtful accounts for estimated losses resulting from the inability of our customers to make required payments. We evaluate the adequacy of this allowance based on factors such as historical collection experience and the creditworthiness of specific customers. Changes in these factors could impact the amount of the allowance and our financial results.

Stock-Based Compensation

We measure stock-based compensation expense for stock options and other equity awards at the grant date, based on the estimated fair value of the award, and recognize it over the vesting period. The determination of the fair value of stock options involves significant judgment and estimates, including assumptions about expected volatility, expected life, and risk-free interest rates. Changes in these assumptions could affect the amount of stock-based compensation expense recognized.

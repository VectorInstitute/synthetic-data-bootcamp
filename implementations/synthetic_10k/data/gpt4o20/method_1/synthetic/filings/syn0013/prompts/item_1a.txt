Item 1. Business

Overview

Draor Midstream, Inc. ("Draor" or the "Company"), headquartered in Seattle, Washington, is a prominent player in the energy sector, specializing in the transportation and distribution of energy resources. Incorporated in Delaware, Draor is a key provider of midstream services, linking suppliers with consumers through its extensive network of pipelines and storage facilities. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, Draor reported total revenue of $134,874 million, reflecting its significant presence in the energy market.

Principal Products and Services

Draor's core operations involve the transportation, storage, and distribution of natural gas, crude oil, and natural gas liquids (NGLs). The Company operates a robust infrastructure that supports the seamless movement of these resources from production sites to end-users. This infrastructure includes a vast network of pipelines, strategically located storage facilities, and advanced logistics systems that ensure efficient and reliable delivery. Draor's services are critical in maintaining the balance between supply and demand in the energy market, providing essential connectivity across the energy value chain.

Segments

Draor's operations are organized into two primary reportable segments: Transportation and Storage.

Transportation

The Transportation segment encompasses Draor's extensive pipeline network, which is crucial for the movement of natural gas, crude oil, and NGLs. These pipelines traverse key energy-producing regions, connecting them to major consumption hubs across the United States. Draor's strategic focus on maintaining and expanding its pipeline infrastructure ensures the reliability and efficiency of its transportation services. The Company's pipelines are designed to accommodate varying volumes and types of energy products, allowing for flexible operations tailored to market conditions.

Storage

The Storage segment is centered around Draor's comprehensive portfolio of storage facilities. These facilities are strategically located near production areas and major distribution points, providing vital storage capacity for natural gas, crude oil, and NGLs. Draor's storage services offer flexibility in managing supply fluctuations, enabling customers to optimize their inventory levels and mitigate market volatility. The Company's commitment to safety and environmental stewardship is evident in its state-of-the-art storage infrastructure, which adheres to stringent industry standards.

Customers

Draor serves a diverse customer base that includes energy producers, refiners, marketers, and industrial end-users. The Company maintains long-term relationships with its customers, which underscores its reputation for reliability and operational excellence. Draor's ability to provide tailored solutions and responsive service has positioned it as a preferred partner for many leading entities in the energy sector.

Sales and Marketing

Draor's sales and marketing efforts are focused on fostering strong customer relationships and expanding its market reach. The Company's marketing team works closely with customers to understand their unique needs and develop customized service offerings. Draor leverages its extensive industry knowledge and expertise to identify growth opportunities and enhance its competitive position in the market.

Competition

The midstream energy sector is highly competitive, with numerous companies vying for market share. Draor competes with other midstream service providers, including those with integrated operations across the energy value chain. The Company's ability to differentiate itself through its extensive infrastructure, operational efficiency, and customer-centric approach is critical in maintaining its competitive edge. Draor continuously assesses market trends and technological advancements to enhance its service offerings and strengthen its market position.

Seasonality

Draor's operations are subject to seasonal variations, primarily driven by changes in energy demand. The demand for natural gas typically increases during colder months due to heating needs, while demand for crude oil and NGLs can fluctuate based on industrial activity and transportation needs. Draor's flexible infrastructure and adaptive operational strategies enable it to manage these seasonal variations effectively, ensuring consistent service delivery to its customers.

Intellectual Property

Draor's success is supported by its intellectual property portfolio, which includes proprietary technologies and processes that enhance the efficiency and safety of its operations. The Company invests in research and development to innovate and improve its service offerings, although it does not engage in formal research and development activities. Draor's commitment to technological advancement is evident in its adoption of cutting-edge technologies for pipeline monitoring, data analytics, and facility management.

Government Regulation

As a participant in the energy sector, Draor is subject to a comprehensive regulatory framework that governs its operations. Regulatory compliance is critical in ensuring the safety and reliability of Draor's services. The Company works closely with regulatory bodies to ensure adherence to all applicable laws and regulations, including those related to environmental protection, safety standards, and operational practices. Draor's proactive approach to regulatory compliance reflects its commitment to responsible and sustainable operations.

Human Capital

Draor recognizes the importance of its workforce in achieving its operational and strategic goals. The Company employs approximately 202,000 individuals, whose expertise and dedication drive its success. Draor is committed to fostering a diverse and inclusive work environment that promotes professional growth and development. The Company invests in training and development programs to equip its employees with the necessary skills and knowledge to excel in their roles. Draor's focus on employee well-being and engagement is integral to maintaining a motivated and high-performing workforce.

Available Information

Draor is committed to transparency and open communication with its stakeholders. The Company provides timely and accurate information through various channels, including its corporate website and investor relations platform. Stakeholders can access financial reports, regulatory filings, and other relevant information to gain insights into Draor's operations and performance. The Company adheres to best practices in corporate governance and strives to maintain the highest standards of integrity and accountability in all its interactions.

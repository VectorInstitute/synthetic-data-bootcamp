Item 1C. Cybersecurity

Risk Management and Strategy

Novix Aerospace, Inc. recognizes the critical importance of cybersecurity in safeguarding our operational integrity and supporting our strategic objectives in the aerospace and defense industry. As a company engaged in the development and manufacturing of advanced aerospace systems, we are exposed to a wide array of cybersecurity threats. These include common industry attacks such as phishing and ransomware, as well as sophisticated threats from highly organized adversaries, including nation-state actors targeting the defense industrial base. The potential impact of a cybersecurity incident on our operations, performance, and reputation necessitates our commitment to being at the forefront of cybersecurity practices.

Our corporate information security organization has implemented a comprehensive governance structure and processes aimed at identifying, assessing, and managing cybersecurity risks. We employ a proactive approach to cybersecurity, integrating advanced threat detection and response capabilities to mitigate risks effectively. Our cybersecurity framework is aligned with industry best practices and governmental standards, ensuring compliance with regulatory requirements and enhancing our resilience against cyber threats.

In addition to internal measures, we collaborate with third-party cybersecurity experts to evaluate our security infrastructure. This collaboration includes penetration testing, independent audits, and consulting on emerging cybersecurity challenges. Through these engagements, we test the strength of our security controls and enhance our ability to respond to potential threats. We also share threat intelligence with industry peers and government agencies, fostering a collaborative environment to counteract cybersecurity risks.

Our supply chain is pivotal to delivering products and services, and a cybersecurity incident affecting our suppliers could materially impact us. To address this, we assess the cybersecurity posture of third-party vendors through comprehensive evaluations and require adherence to stringent cybersecurity standards in our contracts. These measures ensure that our suppliers implement necessary security controls and report any cybersecurity incidents, allowing us to assess potential impacts promptly.

Governance

The governance of cybersecurity at Novix Aerospace is a key component of our risk management strategy. Our Board of Directors plays an active role in overseeing the management of cybersecurity risks, ensuring alignment between our risk exposure and strategic objectives. The board is regularly briefed on our cybersecurity posture and is informed of any incidents with significant business impact. This oversight ensures that cybersecurity considerations are integrated into our broader business strategy.

Our Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) leads the corporate information security organization, overseeing our cybersecurity strategy, policy development, security engineering, and operations. The CISO's extensive experience in information technology and cybersecurity is instrumental in steering our efforts to protect against and respond to cyber threats. The CISO reports directly to senior leadership and the Board of Directors, providing insights into our cybersecurity initiatives and the evolving threat landscape.

The corporate information security organization is tasked with managing a robust security infrastructure, with the primary goal of preventing cybersecurity incidents while enhancing system resilience. Central to this organization is our computer incident response team (CIRT), which is responsible for the detection and response to security incidents. The CIRT operates with agility, leveraging advanced tools and intelligence to safeguard Novix Aerospace's data and networks.

Cybersecurity risk management is integrated into our enterprise risk management (ERM) process, ensuring a holistic approach to identifying and mitigating risks. Through the ERM process, cybersecurity risks are evaluated annually, and risk owners are assigned to develop and implement mitigation plans. These plans are monitored closely to ensure effective risk reduction and compliance with regulatory standards.

Our commitment to cybersecurity extends to educating and training our workforce. Employees are immersed in a corporate culture that prioritizes security, improving our overall cybersecurity posture. Regular training programs and awareness campaigns are conducted to keep employees informed of the latest cyber threats and best practices for safeguarding information.

Despite our comprehensive approach to cybersecurity, the dynamic nature of cyber threats means that no system can be entirely immune to incidents. As such, we maintain detailed incident response protocols to guide our actions in the event of a cybersecurity breach. These protocols encompass steps from detection to mitigation, recovery, and communication with relevant stakeholders, including legal, regulatory, and senior leadership teams.

While Novix Aerospace invests significantly in cybersecurity, we acknowledge that residual risks remain. To address potential financial impacts of cybersecurity incidents, we maintain cybersecurity insurance, although coverage may not fully offset all costs. Our focus remains on continually enhancing our cybersecurity capabilities to protect our operations and maintain stakeholder trust.

In conclusion, cybersecurity is a priority for Novix Aerospace, Inc., vital to our mission of delivering advanced aerospace solutions. Through robust risk management strategies, governance structures, and a culture of security, we strive to mitigate cybersecurity risks effectively. Our proactive stance and commitment to continuous improvement position us to meet the evolving challenges of the cybersecurity landscape in the aerospace and defense industry.

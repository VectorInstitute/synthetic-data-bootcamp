PART I

Item 1. Business

OVERVIEW

Orzenka Midstream, Inc. (ticker ORZA), incorporated in Delaware and headquartered in Phoenix, Arizona, operates as a key player in the energy sector, focusing on midstream energy infrastructure. We are engaged in the transportation, storage, and distribution of energy products, contributing to the efficient movement of resources throughout the United States. Our operations are structured into distinct business segments: Crude Oil and Products Logistics, Natural Gas and NGL Services, and Energy Storage Solutions. Each segment is designed to leverage our comprehensive network of assets to meet the diverse needs of our customers.

PRINCIPAL PRODUCTS AND SERVICES

Our principal products and services encompass the transportation and storage of crude oil, refined products, natural gas, and natural gas liquids (NGLs). Through our extensive pipeline and storage infrastructure, we facilitate the efficient transfer of these commodities from production sites to end-user markets. Our operations are supported by a robust network of pipelines, terminals, and storage facilities strategically located to optimize logistics and supply chain efficiencies across the energy landscape.

REPORTABLE SEGMENTS

Crude Oil and Products Logistics

The Crude Oil and Products Logistics segment is primarily involved in the gathering, transportation, and storage of crude oil and refined products. This segment is integral to the energy supply chain, ensuring the seamless movement of crude oil from production sites to refineries and the subsequent distribution of refined products to markets. Our network of pipelines and terminals provides critical infrastructure for the distribution of gasoline, diesel, jet fuel, and other petroleum-based products.

Natural Gas and NGL Services

Our Natural Gas and NGL Services segment focuses on the gathering, processing, and transportation of natural gas and NGLs. This segment connects natural gas production sites to processing facilities and markets, playing a vital role in the delivery of energy resources. We offer comprehensive services including gas gathering, processing, fractionation, and transportation, ensuring that natural gas and NGLs reach their intended destinations efficiently and reliably.

Energy Storage Solutions

The Energy Storage Solutions segment is dedicated to the storage and management of energy commodities. With facilities equipped to handle large volumes of hydrocarbons and renewables, this segment offers flexibility and logistical options to our customers. Our storage solutions provide market participants with the ability to optimize inventory levels, manage supply and demand fluctuations, and capitalize on market opportunities.

CUSTOMERS

Our customer base is diverse, encompassing major oil and gas producers, refiners, and industrial users. We maintain long-term relationships with these customers through fee-based contracts that provide stability and predictability in our revenue streams. Our ability to offer integrated logistics solutions and extensive market coverage makes us a preferred partner for energy companies seeking reliable and efficient midstream services.

SALES AND MARKETING

Sales and marketing efforts are centered around building and maintaining strong relationships with our customers. Our marketing strategy focuses on highlighting the value of our comprehensive service offerings, extensive infrastructure, and commitment to safety and reliability. By understanding the unique needs of each customer, we tailor our services to provide optimal solutions that enhance their business operations.

COMPETITION

The midstream energy market is competitive, with numerous companies offering similar services. We compete based on the reach and reliability of our infrastructure, the quality of our customer service, and our ability to provide cost-effective solutions. Our strategic location and comprehensive network give us a competitive advantage, allowing us to serve key energy-producing regions and major consumption markets efficiently.

SEASONALITY

Our operations are subject to seasonal fluctuations in demand, driven by variations in energy consumption patterns. For instance, demand for refined products typically increases during the summer months due to higher travel activity, while natural gas demand rises in the winter months for heating purposes. We manage these seasonal variations through effective inventory management and operational flexibility.

INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

While intellectual property is not a primary focus in the midstream energy sector, we leverage proprietary technologies and processes to enhance our operational efficiencies and maintain competitive advantage. We continually invest in technology to improve our service offerings, optimize our operations, and ensure the safety and reliability of our infrastructure.

GOVERNMENT REGULATION

Our operations are subject to extensive federal, state, and local regulations related to environmental protection, safety standards, and transportation. Compliance with these regulations is critical to our business, and we are committed to maintaining the highest standards of health, safety, and environmental performance. We actively engage with regulatory authorities to ensure adherence to applicable laws and regulations, mitigating any potential risks to our operations.

HUMAN CAPITAL

Our workforce is a key asset in driving the success of Orzenka Midstream, Inc. As of December 31, 2024, we employed approximately 177,300 individuals who are integral to executing our strategic initiatives and maintaining our operational excellence. We prioritize the safety, development, and well-being of our employees, fostering a culture of collaboration, innovation, and continuous improvement.

AVAILABLE INFORMATION

We are committed to providing transparency and accessibility of information to our stakeholders. Our corporate website serves as a central resource for accessing financial reports, regulatory filings, and other relevant information about our business operations. We encourage investors, analysts, and other interested parties to visit our website for the latest updates and insights into our company.

In conclusion, Orzenka Midstream, Inc. remains dedicated to delivering value through our comprehensive energy infrastructure and services. We continue to focus on operational excellence, customer satisfaction, and strategic growth to enhance our position as a leading provider in the midstream energy sector. Through our robust network, commitment to innovation, and focus on sustainability, we are well-positioned to meet the evolving needs of the energy industry and deliver long-term value to our stakeholders.

Item 1C. Cybersecurity

Risk Management and Strategy

Orzenka Midstream, Inc. is committed to safeguarding our information systems, data, and infrastructure from cybersecurity threats and risks. We have established a comprehensive strategy designed to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of our digital assets. Our cybersecurity framework is built upon a set of policies, practices, and standards that guide our approach to identifying, assessing, and managing material cybersecurity risks.

Our strategy includes implementing rigorous controls to limit physical and network access to our information technology (IT) and operating technology (OT) systems. We employ a layered defense methodology, integrating advanced security technologies and practices to detect, prevent, and respond to cybersecurity threats. These layers are designed to enhance cyber resilience, minimize vulnerabilities, and provide flexibility in addressing emerging threats.

A centralized cybersecurity operations center is instrumental in our threat detection efforts. This center collects and analyzes data across our network, allowing for real-time monitoring and rapid response to potential threats. We utilize threat intelligence to stay informed about the latest cybersecurity trends and potential risks, ensuring our defenses remain robust and adaptive.

Employee and contractor awareness is a critical component of our cybersecurity strategy. We provide regular training programs to educate our workforce on recognizing and responding to cybersecurity threats. This training is tailored to different roles within the organization, ensuring that all employees are equipped with the knowledge necessary to maintain security standards.

To assess our cybersecurity resilience, we conduct regular testing, including penetration tests and tabletop exercises. These assessments are designed to simulate cybersecurity incidents and evaluate the effectiveness of our incident response plans. The scenarios include a range of potential threats and involve participants from various levels of the organization, ensuring comprehensive preparedness.

We also engage third-party experts to evaluate our cybersecurity practices. These independent assessments provide valuable insights into our cybersecurity posture and help identify areas for improvement. Maintaining strong relationships with external cybersecurity resources, such as federal agencies and industry groups, allows us to stay informed about regulatory changes and emerging threats.

Governance

Our Board of Directors provides oversight of cybersecurity risks, with the Audit Committee specifically tasked with monitoring these threats as part of our enterprise risk management (ERM) program. This committee receives regular updates on our cybersecurity strategy and risk management efforts. The ERM program integrates cybersecurity risk management into our overall risk assessment processes, ensuring alignment with our strategic objectives.

The Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) plays a pivotal role in our cybersecurity governance. The CISO is responsible for implementing our cybersecurity program, which encompasses Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC); Cybersecurity Architecture; Engineering and Operations; and a Cyber Fusion Center that includes Threat Intelligence, Vulnerability Management, and Incident Response. The CISO has extensive experience in the energy sector, having served in leadership roles across IT and cybersecurity functions. This experience is complemented by advanced degrees in cybersecurity and computer science.

Reporting directly to our Chief Digital Officer (CDO), the CISO collaborates closely with senior management to ensure that cybersecurity strategies are integrated with our broader business objectives. The CISO provides regular briefings to the Board of Directors, including the Audit Committee, on the status of cybersecurity initiatives, emerging threats, and risk mitigation efforts.

The CDO, with a strong background in IT leadership, oversees our digital and information technology functions. This role is critical in ensuring that our technology infrastructure supports our cybersecurity objectives while enabling operational efficiency and innovation. The CDO and CISO work together to align cybersecurity initiatives with our strategic priorities, ensuring that our technology investments enhance both security and business performance.

In addition to formal briefings, the CISO and CDO maintain open lines of communication with the Board and senior management, providing updates on cybersecurity trends, incidents, and strategy adjustments as needed. This proactive communication ensures that our leadership remains informed and engaged in our cybersecurity efforts, supporting informed decision-making and strategic alignment.

We recognize the importance of managing cybersecurity risks associated with our third-party service providers. To this end, we have implemented a comprehensive third-party risk management process. This process includes evaluating the cybersecurity controls of our service providers, conducting risk assessments, and ensuring that contractual agreements reflect our cybersecurity expectations.

As of the date of this report, we have not identified any cybersecurity incidents that have materially impacted our business operations, financial condition, or results. However, we acknowledge that the cybersecurity landscape is constantly evolving, and there is no guarantee that our defenses will prevent all potential incidents. We remain vigilant in our efforts to enhance our cybersecurity posture and mitigate risks associated with cybersecurity threats.

In summary, Orzenka Midstream, Inc. is dedicated to maintaining robust cybersecurity defenses. Through strategic investments in technology, continuous risk assessment, and proactive governance, we strive to protect our digital assets and support our business objectives. Our commitment to cybersecurity is an integral part of our overall strategy to deliver value to our stakeholders and ensure the long-term success of our organization.

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Overview

The following discussion and analysis should be read in conjunction with our Consolidated Financial Statements and accompanying notes. This section provides an overview of our financial condition and results of operations for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, with comparative analysis to the fiscal year ended December 31, 2023. Our performance in fiscal 2024 reflects our strategic focus on optimizing operations and capitalizing on the growing demand for energy infrastructure services. We experienced robust growth across our business segments, driven by increased utilization of our midstream assets and enhanced operational efficiencies. Our commitment to maintaining strong customer relationships and our ability to deliver reliable, cost-effective solutions have been central to our success. Additionally, strategic investments in infrastructure and technology have bolstered our capacity to meet the evolving needs of the energy market.

Results of Operations

Revenue

In fiscal 2024, our revenue increased by 15.1% to $52,746 million from $45,826 million in fiscal 2023. This growth was primarily driven by higher throughput volumes across our pipeline and storage operations, supported by favorable market conditions and increased customer demand. The expansion of our infrastructure capabilities and optimization of existing assets contributed to this revenue enhancement. Additionally, the continued recovery of global energy markets, following previous disruptions, further supported revenue growth.

Cost of Revenue and Gross Margin

The cost of revenue for fiscal 2024 was $27,191 million, reflecting an increase from $23,802 million in fiscal 2023. This 14.2% increase was largely attributable to higher operational costs associated with increased volumes, including transportation and storage expenses. Despite the rise in cost, our gross profit improved to $25,555 million from $22,024 million, with the gross margin slightly increasing to 48.4% from 48.1% in the prior year. This improvement in gross margin demonstrates our ability to manage costs effectively while expanding our operational capacity.

Selling, General and Administrative Expenses

Selling, general and administrative expenses increased to $1,313 million in fiscal 2024 from $1,095 million in fiscal 2023, representing a growth of 19.9%. This increase was due to strategic investments in expanding our sales and marketing efforts to capture new business opportunities and enhance customer engagement. Additionally, inflationary pressures on administrative costs and investments in workforce development contributed to the rise in expenses. As a percentage of revenue, these expenses constituted 2.5%, up from 2.4% in the previous year.

Other Operating Expenses

Other operating expenses for fiscal 2024 totaled $3,165 million, compared to $2,750 million in fiscal 2023, representing a 15.1% increase. This rise was driven by higher maintenance and operational support costs as we expanded our network and undertook several strategic projects to enhance service delivery. These projects included upgrades to our existing infrastructure and the integration of advanced technologies to improve operational efficiency.

Operating Income

Our operating income for fiscal 2024 was $21,077 million, up from $18,179 million in fiscal 2023, reflecting a 15.9% increase. This growth was primarily due to our enhanced revenue streams and effective cost management strategies, which offset the increases in operating expenses. Our ability to drive operational efficiencies and leverage our expanded infrastructure contributed significantly to the improvement in operating income.

Interest Expense

Interest expense rose to $3,832 million in fiscal 2024 from $3,302 million in fiscal 2023, an increase of 16.0%. This was primarily due to the issuance of additional debt to finance our capital expansion projects and infrastructure investments. While the increase in interest expenses reflects higher borrowing costs, the investments funded by this debt are expected to generate substantial returns in the future, supporting our long-term growth objectives.

Provision for Income Taxes and Effective Tax Rate

The provision for income taxes in fiscal 2024 was $4,089 million, compared to $3,552 million in fiscal 2023. Our effective tax rate slightly decreased to 23.7% from 23.9% in the prior year. The reduction in the effective tax rate was largely due to the implementation of tax optimization strategies and the geographical diversification of our operations, which provided some tax efficiencies.

Net Income

Net income for fiscal 2024 increased by 16.2% to $13,156 million from $11,325 million in fiscal 2023. This growth was primarily driven by higher operating income and an improved tax position. The increase in net income reflects our successful execution of strategic initiatives aimed at enhancing operational performance and optimizing our capital structure.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

Cash and Cash Equivalents

As of December 31, 2024, our cash and cash equivalents were $10,049 million, compared to $8,785 million at the end of fiscal 2023. The increase in cash reserves was primarily due to strong cash flow generation from operations, which supported both our growth initiatives and shareholder return programs.

Operating Activities

Net cash provided by operating activities for fiscal 2024 was $17,777 million. This reflects robust operational performance and effective working capital management. The increase in cash flow from operations enabled us to fund our capital expenditures and support strategic investments without compromising our financial flexibility.

Investing Activities

The primary use of cash was for purchases of property and equipment, totaling $6,198 million, as we continued to expand and upgrade our infrastructure. Additionally, other investing activities, net of proceeds, accounted for $14,027 million, reflecting our strategic acquisitions and investments aimed at enhancing our operational capabilities and market presence.

Financing Activities

This reflects our strategic approach to managing our capital structure, which included the net issuance of long-term debt amounting to $11,775 million to finance growth initiatives. We also returned value to our shareholders through dividends paid of $3,564 million and share repurchases, net of issuances, totaling $4,499 million. These actions align with our commitment to delivering shareholder value while maintaining financial flexibility.

Capital Expenditures

Our disciplined approach to capital spending supports our strategic growth objectives and positions us to capitalize on future opportunities in the energy market.

The increase in debt levels was primarily due to financing activities related to our capital expansion projects. While this increase has raised our leverage, we remain committed to maintaining a balanced capital structure. Dividends and Share Repurchases

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Overview

The following discussion and analysis should be read in conjunction with our Consolidated Financial Statements and accompanying notes. This section provides an overview of our financial condition and results of operations for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, with comparative analysis to the fiscal year ended December 31, 2023. Our performance in fiscal 2024 reflects our strategic focus on optimizing operations and capitalizing on the growing demand for energy infrastructure services. We experienced robust growth across our business segments, driven by increased utilization of our midstream assets and enhanced operational efficiencies. Our commitment to maintaining strong customer relationships and our ability to deliver reliable, cost-effective solutions have been central to our success. Additionally, strategic investments in infrastructure and technology have bolstered our capacity to meet the evolving needs of the energy market.

Results of Operations

Revenue

In fiscal 2024, our revenue increased by 15.1% to $52,746 million from $45,826 million in fiscal 2023. This growth was primarily driven by higher throughput volumes across our pipeline and storage operations, supported by favorable market conditions and increased customer demand. The expansion of our infrastructure capabilities and optimization of existing assets contributed to this revenue enhancement. Additionally, the continued recovery of global energy markets, following previous disruptions, further supported revenue growth.

Cost of Revenue and Gross Margin

The cost of revenue for fiscal 2024 was $27,191 million, reflecting an increase from $23,802 million in fiscal 2023. This 14.2% increase was largely attributable to higher operational costs associated with increased volumes, including transportation and storage expenses. Despite the rise in cost, our gross profit improved to $25,555 million from $22,024 million, with the gross margin slightly increasing to 48.4% from 48.1% in the prior year. This improvement in gross margin demonstrates our ability to manage costs effectively while expanding our operational capacity.

Selling, General and Administrative Expenses

Selling, general and administrative expenses increased to $1,313 million in fiscal 2024 from $1,095 million in fiscal 2023, representing a growth of 19.9%. This increase was due to strategic investments in expanding our sales and marketing efforts to capture new business opportunities and enhance customer engagement. Additionally, inflationary pressures on administrative costs and investments in workforce development contributed to the rise in expenses. As a percentage of revenue, these expenses constituted 2.5%, up from 2.4% in the previous year.

Other Operating Expenses

Other operating expenses for fiscal 2024 totaled $3,165 million, compared to $2,750 million in fiscal 2023, representing a 15.1% increase. This rise was driven by higher maintenance and operational support costs as we expanded our network and undertook several strategic projects to enhance service delivery. These projects included upgrades to our existing infrastructure and the integration of advanced technologies to improve operational efficiency.

Operating Income

Our operating income for fiscal 2024 was $21,077 million, up from $18,179 million in fiscal 2023, reflecting a 15.9% increase. This growth was primarily due to our enhanced revenue streams and effective cost management strategies, which offset the increases in operating expenses. Our ability to drive operational efficiencies and leverage our expanded infrastructure contributed significantly to the improvement in operating income.

Interest Expense

Interest expense rose to $3,832 million in fiscal 2024 from $3,302 million in fiscal 2023, an increase of 16.0%. This was primarily due to the issuance of additional debt to finance our capital expansion projects and infrastructure investments. While the increase in interest expenses reflects higher borrowing costs, the investments funded by this debt are expected to generate substantial returns in the future, supporting our long-term growth objectives.

Provision for Income Taxes and Effective Tax Rate

The provision for income taxes in fiscal 2024 was $4,089 million, compared to $3,552 million in fiscal 2023. Our effective tax rate slightly decreased to 23.7% from 23.9% in the prior year. The reduction in the effective tax rate was largely due to the implementation of tax optimization strategies and the geographical diversification of our operations, which provided some tax efficiencies.

Net Income

Net income for fiscal 2024 increased by 16.2% to $13,156 million from $11,325 million in fiscal 2023. This growth was primarily driven by higher operating income and an improved tax position. The increase in net income reflects our successful execution of strategic initiatives aimed at enhancing operational performance and optimizing our capital structure.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

Cash and Cash Equivalents

As of December 31, 2024, our cash and cash equivalents were $10,049 million, compared to $8,785 million at the end of fiscal 2023. The increase in cash reserves was primarily due to strong cash flow generation from operations, which supported both our growth initiatives and shareholder return programs.

Operating Activities

Net cash provided by operating activities for fiscal 2024 was $17,777 million. This reflects robust operational performance and effective working capital management. The increase in cash flow from operations enabled us to fund our capital expenditures and support strategic investments without compromising our financial flexibility.

Investing Activities

Net cash used in investing activities was $20,225 million in fiscal 2024. The primary use of cash was for purchases of property and equipment, totaling $6,198 million, as we continued to expand and upgrade our infrastructure. Additionally, other investing activities, net of proceeds, accounted for $14,027 million, reflecting our strategic acquisitions and investments aimed at enhancing our operational capabilities and market presence.

Financing Activities

Net cash used in financing activities amounted to $3,712 million in fiscal 2024. This reflects our strategic approach to managing our capital structure, which included the net issuance of long-term debt amounting to $11,775 million to finance growth initiatives. We also returned value to our shareholders through dividends paid of $3,564 million and share repurchases, net of issuances, totaling $4,499 million. These actions align with our commitment to delivering shareholder value while maintaining financial flexibility.

Capital Expenditures

Our capital expenditures in fiscal 2024 were $6,198 million, reflecting our ongoing investment in infrastructure development and maintenance. These expenditures were focused on enhancing our pipeline and storage capabilities and integrating advanced technologies to improve operational efficiency. Our disciplined approach to capital spending supports our strategic growth objectives and positions us to capitalize on future opportunities in the energy market.

Debt and Leverage

As of December 31, 2024, our total long-term debt was $85,152 million, compared to $73,377 million at the end of fiscal 2023. The increase in debt levels was primarily due to financing activities related to our capital expansion projects. While this increase has raised our leverage, we remain committed to maintaining a balanced capital structure. Our strong cash flow generation and prudent financial management provide us with the flexibility to manage our debt obligations effectively.

Dividends and Share Repurchases

In fiscal 2024, we paid dividends totaling $3,564 million and repurchased shares amounting to $4,499 million, net of issuances. These actions reflect our confidence in the long-term prospects of our business and our commitment to returning value to our shareholders. Our capital allocation strategy prioritizes a balanced approach to growth investments and shareholder returns, ensuring sustainable value creation.

Critical Accounting Estimates

Our consolidated financial statements are prepared in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles, which require us to make estimates and assumptions that affect the reported amounts of assets, liabilities, revenues, expenses, and related disclosures. We review these estimates regularly, relying on historical experience and other factors that we believe to be reasonable under the circumstances. Our critical accounting estimates include the assessment of goodwill impairment, fair value measurements, asset retirement obligations, and income taxes.

Goodwill Impairment

We assess goodwill for impairment annually or more frequently if events or circumstances indicate that goodwill may be impaired. This assessment involves estimating the fair value of our reporting units, which requires significant judgment and assumptions regarding market conditions, future cash flows, and discount rates. Changes in these assumptions could result in an impairment charge, affecting our financial position and results of operations.

Fair Value Measurements

We utilize fair value measurements for certain financial instruments and assets, including derivative instruments and asset impairments. Determining the fair value of these instruments requires judgment and the use of significant assumptions, including market data and pricing models. Changes in market conditions or assumptions used in these models can affect the fair value estimates and result in fluctuations in our financial results.

Asset Retirement Obligations

We recognize asset retirement obligations for the future dismantlement and removal of our infrastructure assets. Estimating these obligations involves assumptions regarding the timing, cost, and extent of future dismantlement activities, as well as discount rates. Changes in these estimates can affect the recorded liability and the corresponding asset retirement cost.

Income Taxes

Our provision for income taxes and effective tax rate are influenced by numerous factors, including changes in tax laws, regulations, and interpretations. We evaluate the realizability of deferred tax assets and recognize tax benefits only if it is more likely than not that they will be realized. This assessment requires judgment and assumptions about future taxable income and the timing of reversals of temporary differences. Changes in these assumptions can affect our tax provision and financial results.

In conclusion, fiscal 2024 was a year of significant growth and operational achievement for Orzenka Midstream, Inc. Our financial performance reflects our strategic focus on expanding our infrastructure, optimizing operations, and delivering value to our stakeholders. We remain committed to leveraging our strengths and capabilities to capitalize on opportunities in the evolving energy market, ensuring sustainable growth and resilience in the years to come.

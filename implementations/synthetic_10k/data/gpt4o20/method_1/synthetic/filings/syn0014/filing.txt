Item 1. Business

Overview

Kator Minerals, Inc. (Kator or the Company) is a leading enterprise in the materials sector, primarily engaged in the extraction, processing, and distribution of essential mineral resources. Incorporated in Delaware and headquartered in Reston, Virginia, Kator Minerals has carved a significant presence in the market by offering a broad array of mineral products that are critical to various industries. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, Kator recorded total revenue of $11,823 million, underscoring its robust operational capabilities and market demand for its products.

Principal Products and Services

Kator Minerals specializes in the production and supply of a diverse range of mineral resources. The Company’s principal products include a variety of metallic and non-metallic minerals that are integral to construction, manufacturing, and technological applications. Our portfolio is designed to meet the needs of a wide spectrum of industries, providing essential materials that serve as the foundation for infrastructure projects, industrial manufacturing, and technological advancements.

Reportable Segments

Kator Minerals operates through several key segments, each contributing to the Company's overall strategy and market presence.

1. Base Metals Segment

This segment focuses on the extraction and processing of base metals such as copper, aluminum, and zinc. These metals are crucial for electrical wiring, construction, and the production of industrial machinery. The segment is characterized by its extensive mining operations and state-of-the-art processing facilities, which ensure high-quality output and efficient supply chains.

2. Industrial Minerals Segment

The Industrial Minerals Segment is dedicated to the production of non-metallic minerals, including aggregates, clays, and gypsum, which are vital for construction and manufacturing applications. This segment supports infrastructure development and industrial processes by supplying materials that enhance product durability and performance.

3. Specialty Minerals Segment

Our Specialty Minerals Segment focuses on high-value minerals that are critical in advanced technologies and specialized applications. This includes rare earth elements and other strategic minerals that are essential for electronics, renewable energy technologies, and advanced manufacturing processes. This segment is at the forefront of innovation, driving the development of new applications and supporting technological progress.

Customers

Kator Minerals serves a diversified customer base, including construction companies, manufacturing firms, and technology developers. Our customers range from small enterprises to large multinational corporations, each relying on the quality and reliability of Kator’s mineral products. The Company’s ability to cater to a wide array of industries underscores its flexibility and commitment to meeting the diverse needs of its clientele.

Sales and Marketing

Kator employs a comprehensive sales and marketing strategy that integrates traditional and digital channels to reach its customers effectively. The Company’s marketing efforts are focused on showcasing the quality and reliability of its products, as well as the technical expertise and customer service that distinguish Kator in the market. Strategic partnerships and long-term contracts with key industry players further enhance our sales efforts, ensuring sustained demand and market presence.

Competition

The materials sector is highly competitive, with numerous players vying for market share. Kator Minerals competes with both domestic and international companies that provide similar mineral products. The Company differentiates itself through its commitment to quality, extensive resource base, and advanced processing capabilities. Continuous investment in technology and innovation also positions Kator favorably in the competitive landscape, enabling it to meet evolving customer demands and maintain a leading market position.

Seasonality

Peak demand typically occurs during the warmer months when construction activity is at its highest. The Company manages these fluctuations through strategic inventory management and flexible production schedules, ensuring timely delivery and customer satisfaction throughout the year.

Intellectual Property

Kator Minerals places significant emphasis on research and development, leading to the creation of proprietary technologies and processes that enhance operational efficiency and product quality. While the Company does not hold extensive patents, its intellectual property strategy focuses on trade secrets and process innovations that provide a competitive advantage in the production of high-quality mineral products.

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

(the "Company") for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, compared with fiscal year 2023. Overview

Kator Minerals, Inc. is a prominent entity in the materials sector, focusing on the extraction, processing, and distribution of essential mineral resources. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, Kator recorded total revenue of $11,823 million, reflecting a 13.9% increase over the previous year. This growth underscores our robust operational capabilities and the sustained market demand for our diverse range of mineral products. Our operational structure is defined by strategic segments that cater to different aspects of the mineral market, including base metals, industrial minerals, and specialty minerals.

Results of Operations

Revenue

For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, Kator Minerals reported revenue of $11,823 million, compared to $10,379 million in the prior year. The 13.9% increase in revenue is attributable to higher demand across all product segments, bolstered by strong market conditions in the construction and technology sectors. The expansion of our customer base and strategic pricing initiatives also contributed positively to revenue growth.

Cost of Revenue and Gross Margin

The cost of revenue for fiscal 2024 was $11,097 million, an increase from $9,823 million in fiscal 2023. This 13.0% rise was primarily driven by increased production volumes and higher input costs, including energy and raw materials. Despite the rise in costs, gross profit improved to $726 million from $556 million in the prior year, reflecting a gross margin of 6.1%, up from 5.4% in fiscal 2023. The improvement in gross margin is indicative of our effective cost management strategies and operational efficiencies.

Operating Expenses

Selling, General and Administrative Expenses

Selling, general and administrative (SG&A) expenses increased to $227 million in fiscal 2024 from $214 million in fiscal 2023. The 6.1% increase was mainly due to expanded marketing efforts and enhanced customer engagement initiatives aimed at supporting revenue growth. Despite the increase, SG&A expenses as a percentage of revenue decreased from 2.1% in fiscal 2023 to 1.9% in fiscal 2024, demonstrating improved operational efficiency.

Other Operating Expense

Other operating expenses rose to $709 million in fiscal 2024, compared with $623 million in fiscal 2023. This increase of 13.8% was largely due to higher maintenance costs associated with our expanded production capacity and increased environmental compliance expenditures. We continue to invest in our operational infrastructure to support future growth and sustainability initiatives.

Operating Income

The Company reported an operating loss of $210 million for fiscal 2024, an improvement from the operating loss of $281 million in fiscal 2023. This 25.3% reduction in operating loss reflects the positive impact of increased gross profit and disciplined cost management, partially offset by higher operating expenses.

Interest Expense

Interest expense increased to $333 million in fiscal 2024 from $292 million in fiscal 2023, an increase of 14.0%. The rise in interest expense was primarily due to higher levels of long-term debt used to finance capital expenditures and strategic growth initiatives. Our focus remains on optimizing our capital structure to manage interest obligations effectively.

Effective Tax Rate

For both fiscal 2024 and fiscal 2023, the provision for income taxes was zero. This outcome is consistent with the Company's net loss position and available tax loss carryforwards, resulting in an effective tax rate of 0.0% for both years.

Net Income

Kator Minerals reported a net loss of $543 million for fiscal 2024, compared to a net loss of $573 million in fiscal 2023. The 5.2% improvement in net income was primarily driven by reduced operating losses and effective cost management, partially offset by increased interest expenses. Our strategic initiatives to enhance operational efficiency and expand market presence are expected to contribute positively to future profitability.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

Cash and Cash Equivalents

As of December 31, 2024, the Company held cash and cash equivalents of $1,223 million, compared to $1,143 million at the end of the prior year. The increase in cash and cash equivalents reflects our strong cash flow generation from operating activities, despite significant investments in capital expenditures and strategic financing activities.

Operating Activities

Net cash provided by operating activities was $629 million in fiscal 2024. This positive cash flow was primarily driven by improved operational performance and effective working capital management. Depreciation and amortization expenses of $1,062 million contributed significantly to cash flow, offsetting the net loss for the year.

Investing Activities

Net cash used in investing activities totaled $2,700 million in fiscal 2024. This substantial outflow was largely attributable to purchases of property and equipment amounting to $283 million and other investing activities amounting to $2,417 million. These investments were focused on expanding production capacity, enhancing operational efficiencies, and supporting long-term growth strategies.

Financing Activities

Net cash used in financing activities amounted to $2,151 million for fiscal 2024. This cash outflow was primarily due to share repurchases, net of issuances, totaling $1,249 million, and a net change in long-term debt of $902 million. The Company did not pay dividends during this period, aligning with our capital allocation strategy focused on reinvestment in growth opportunities and return of capital to shareholders through share buybacks.

Capital Expenditures

Capital expenditures for fiscal 2024 amounted to $283 million, reflecting our commitment to maintaining and expanding our operational infrastructure. These expenditures were strategically directed towards enhancing production capabilities and supporting sustainable practices across our operations.

Debt

Long-term debt increased to $7,399 million as of December 31, 2024, compared to $6,497 million at the end of fiscal 2023. The additional debt was primarily used to finance capital investments and strategic initiatives aimed at supporting our growth objectives. We remain focused on managing our debt levels to maintain financial flexibility and support long-term value creation.

Dividends and Share Repurchases

During fiscal 2024, Kator Minerals did not pay any dividends, in line with our strategy to prioritize reinvestment in the business and share repurchases. The Company repurchased shares totaling $1,249 million, reflecting our commitment to enhancing shareholder value through capital return initiatives.

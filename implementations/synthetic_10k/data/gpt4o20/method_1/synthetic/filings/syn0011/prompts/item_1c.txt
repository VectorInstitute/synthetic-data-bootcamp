Risk Management and Strategy
We maintain a cyber risk management program designed to assess, identify, manage, mitigate and respond to cybersecurity threats and incidents. We seek to address material risks from cybersecurity threats through a cross-functional approach, and we utilize various processes to inform our identification, assessment and management of material risks from cybersecurity threats. Our cyber risk management program is integrated into our overall enterprise risk management (ERM) program. Cybersecurity risks are identified and assessed through our ERM program, which is designed to provide cross-functional executive insight across the business to identify and monitor risks, opportunities and emerging trends that can impact our strategic business objectives. The underlying controls of our cyber risk management program are based on recognized best practices and standards for cybersecurity and information technology, including the National Institute of Standards and Technology Cybersecurity Framework.
We utilize dedicated internal and external cybersecurity personnel to focus on assessing, detecting, identifying, managing, preventing and responding to cybersecurity threats and incidents. Our approach to cybersecurity incorporates a layered portfolio of technology controls, including strategic partnerships for our cybersecurity platforms, documented policies and procedures, periodic end user training, including cybersecurity awareness training for employees and certain contractors, and dedicated resources to manage and monitor the evolving threat landscape, including through the gathering of actionable threat intelligence. We maintain and periodically evaluate and, as needed, update our information security policy and an incident response plan, which describes the processes we use to prepare for, detect, respond to and recover from a cybersecurity incident, including processes to assess severity, escalate, contain, investigate and remediate an incident, as well as to comply with potentially applicable legal and disclosure obligations.
We regularly evaluate and assess the threat landscape and our security controls, including through audits and assessments, regular network and endpoint monitoring, vulnerability testing, penetration testing and tabletop exercises that include senior management. To assess the design and effectiveness of our cybersecurity controls, we engage with assessors, consultants, auditors and other third parties, including through independent third-party reviews of our information technology security program conducted on at least an annual basis. We also have processes to oversee and identify material cybersecurity risks associated with our use of third-party service providers, including utilizing safeguards to protect sensitive data, performing diligence on certain third parties that have access to our systems, data or facilities that store such systems or data, continually monitoring cybersecurity threat risks identified through such diligence and contracting to manage cybersecurity risks in specified ways such as requiring agreements to be subject to periodic cybersecurity audits.
We have experienced targeted and non-targeted cybersecurity incidents in the past, including an incident in August 2023 that affected certain of our information systems and resulted in temporary disruptions to parts of our operations. However, prior cybersecurity incidents, including the August 2023 incident, have not materially affected us. Notwithstanding our cyber risk management program, we may not be successful in preventing or mitigating a cybersecurity incident that could materially affect us, including our business strategy, results of operations or financial condition. Item 1. Business

Overview

Novmer Materials, Inc. ("Novmer" or "the Company"), incorporated in Delaware and headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia, is a prominent entity in the materials sector. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, Novmer generated $20,255 million in revenue, reflecting its robust position in the industry. The Company is dedicated to delivering high-quality materials and innovative solutions that advance the market's evolving needs, contributing significantly to various sectors that depend on reliable material supply.

Principal Products and Services

Novmer specializes in the production and distribution of an extensive range of materials essential for numerous applications across diverse industries. These materials are categorized into key offerings that serve multiple segments, ensuring a strong market presence and competitive edge. Novmer's principal products include advanced polymers, industrial metals, and specialty chemicals, each tailored to meet specific market demands and regulatory standards. The Company's commitment to quality and innovation drives the continuous enhancement of its product portfolio, ensuring alignment with customer expectations and industry trends.

Reportable Segments

Novmer operates through several strategic business segments that address distinct market needs, leveraging the Company's expertise and resources to maximize value creation. The core segments include:

1. Polymers Segment: This segment focuses on the production of high-performance polymers used in applications ranging from automotive components to consumer goods. Novmer's polymers are known for their durability, versatility, and environmental sustainability, meeting the increasing demand for materials that support eco-friendly manufacturing processes.

2. Metals Segment: Specializing in industrial metals, this segment supplies essential materials for construction, automotive, and aerospace industries. Novmer's metals are characterized by their strength and reliability, contributing to infrastructure projects and technological advancements globally.

3. Specialty Chemicals Segment: Novmer's specialty chemicals are integral to various industrial processes, offering solutions that enhance performance and efficiency. These chemicals support sectors such as agriculture, pharmaceuticals, and energy, where precision and quality are paramount.

Customers

Novmer's customer base is diverse, encompassing entities ranging from large multinational corporations to small and medium-sized enterprises. The Company's materials are integral to the operations of customers involved in manufacturing, construction, transportation, and consumer goods production. By maintaining strong relationships and providing tailored solutions, Novmer ensures high customer satisfaction and loyalty.

Sales and Marketing

Novmer employs a multifaceted sales and marketing strategy designed to effectively reach and engage its diverse customer base. The Company utilizes direct sales forces, distribution networks, and digital platforms to promote its products and services. Marketing efforts focus on highlighting product quality, sustainability, and innovation, reinforcing Novmer's reputation as a trusted supplier in the materials sector. The Company's sales strategy is supported by robust market research and customer feedback mechanisms, enabling it to adapt to changing market dynamics and customer preferences.

Competition

The materials industry is highly competitive, characterized by numerous domestic and international players. Novmer competes with companies that offer similar products and services, emphasizing the importance of innovation, cost efficiency, and customer service. The Company differentiates itself through its comprehensive product portfolio, commitment to sustainability, and ability to deliver customized solutions. By continuously investing in research and development, Novmer maintains its competitive advantage and adapts to emerging industry trends.

Seasonality

Novmer's business experiences some degree of seasonality, influenced by factors such as construction cycles and agricultural demands. The demand for certain materials may fluctuate based on weather conditions and economic cycles, impacting sales patterns throughout the year. The Company manages these variations through strategic inventory management and flexible production scheduling, ensuring consistent customer supply and minimizing the effects of seasonal changes.

Intellectual Property

Human Capital

Novmer is dedicated to fostering a diverse and inclusive work environment, offering opportunities for professional growth and development. Item 1A. Risk Factors

Novmer Materials, Inc. ("Novmer" or "the Company") is exposed to various risks that could materially and adversely affect its business, financial condition, results of operations, and cash flows. These risk factors are outlined below and are grouped under specific categories to provide a comprehensive understanding of the potential challenges the Company may face.

Industry and Market Risks

Cyclical Nature of the Materials Industry
The materials industry, in which Novmer operates, is inherently cyclical, with demand closely tied to economic conditions. Fluctuations in global economic activity, including downturns or recessions, could lead to decreased demand for Novmer's products. Sectors such as construction, automotive, and manufacturing, which rely heavily on materials, are particularly sensitive to economic changes. A prolonged economic slowdown could result in reduced customer orders, lower sales volumes, and diminished revenue streams, adversely impacting the Company's profitability and financial stability.

Intense Competition and Market Dynamics
Novmer operates in a highly competitive industry characterized by numerous domestic and international competitors. The Company faces competition from other material producers that offer similar products, often competing on price, quality, and service. The emergence of new competitors, advancements in alternative materials, and shifts in consumer preferences could erode Novmer's market share. To maintain its competitive edge, the Company must continuously innovate, effectively manage costs, and offer superior customer service. Failure to do so may result in lost customers and reduced profitability.

Raw Material Price Volatility
The costs of raw materials significantly influence Novmer's production expenses and overall profitability. Prices for key inputs, such as metals, polymers, and chemicals, can be volatile due to factors beyond the Company's control, including geopolitical events, supply chain disruptions, and changes in global demand. Any substantial increase in raw material costs without a corresponding ability to pass these costs onto customers could adversely affect Novmer's margins. Additionally, any challenges in securing a stable supply of raw materials could disrupt production and impact the Company's ability to fulfill customer orders.

The Company must actively manage supplier relationships and develop contingency plans to mitigate these risks.

Capital-Intensive Operations
Novmer's business model requires substantial capital investment in machinery, facilities, and technology to maintain and enhance its production capabilities. The Company's ability to finance these investments is critical to sustaining competitiveness and operational efficiency. Economic downturns, rising interest rates, or limited access to capital markets could constrain Novmer's ability to fund necessary capital expenditures, potentially impacting the Company's growth prospects and operational effectiveness.

Dependence on Skilled Workforce
The success of Novmer's operations relies heavily on the expertise and dedication of its workforce. Attracting, retaining, and developing skilled employees are essential to maintaining the Company's competitive position and driving innovation. Labor shortages, demographic shifts, and increased competition for talent in the industry could challenge Novmer's ability to secure the human capital necessary for its operations. The Company must continuously invest in employee development and foster a positive work environment to mitigate these risks.

Exposure to Interest Rate Fluctuations
Novmer's financial performance is sensitive to changes in interest rates, which can affect the Company's borrowing costs and overall financial condition. Novmer's capital structure includes significant long-term debt obligations. As a global company, Novmer is exposed to risks related to fluctuations in foreign exchange rates. Adverse movements in exchange rates could lead to reduced revenue, increased costs, and diminished profitability. Legal and Regulatory Risks

Compliance with Environmental Regulations
Novmer operates in an industry subject to stringent environmental regulations and standards. Changes in environmental laws, increased regulatory scrutiny, or new sustainability requirements could impose additional compliance costs and necessitate operational modifications. Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Overview

The following Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations ("MD&A") provides an overview of the financial condition and operational performance of Novmer Materials, Inc. ("Novmer" or "the Company") for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024. This analysis should be read in conjunction with the Company's consolidated financial statements and accompanying notes included in Item 8 of this Form 10-K. The purpose of this section is to provide a narrative explanation of the financial statements, detailing the significant factors influencing Novmer's financial performance, liquidity, and capital resources.

Novmer operates within the materials sector, focusing on the production and distribution of polymers, industrial metals, and specialty chemicals. The Company leverages its extensive product portfolio and technological expertise to cater to diverse industries, including automotive, construction, and consumer goods. For the fiscal year 2024, Novmer reported revenue of $20,255 million, an increase of $1,175 million, or 6.2%, over fiscal year 2023. Despite global economic uncertainties and competitive pressures, Novmer demonstrated resilience by enhancing operational efficiencies and expanding its market presence.

Results of Operations

Revenue

For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, Novmer’s revenue increased by 6.2% to $20,255 million from $19,080 million in fiscal year 2023. This growth was primarily driven by increased sales volume across key segments, particularly in the polymers and specialty chemicals sectors. The heightened demand for sustainable materials contributed significantly to the revenue increase, reflecting the Company's strategic focus on eco-friendly product innovations.

Cost of Revenue and Gross Margin

The cost of revenue for fiscal year 2024 was $15,825 million, an increase from $14,858 million in the prior year. This 6.5% increase was closely aligned with revenue growth and was primarily attributable to higher raw material costs and increased production volumes. Novmer's gross profit for 2024 was $4,430 million, compared to $4,222 million in 2023, resulting in a gross margin of 21.9% for 2024, slightly down from 22.1% in 2023. The slight decrease in gross margin reflects the impact of rising input costs, which were partially offset by operational efficiencies and price adjustments.

Selling, General and Administrative Expenses

Selling, general and administrative expenses ("SG&A") for fiscal year 2024 were $2,856 million, representing 14.1% of revenue, compared to $2,629 million, or 13.8% of revenue, in 2023. The increase in SG&A expenses was primarily due to higher personnel costs and investments in digital marketing initiatives aimed at enhancing customer engagement and expanding market reach. Novmer continues to focus on cost management strategies to optimize SG&A expenditures while supporting growth initiatives.

Other Operating Expenses

Other operating expenses for fiscal year 2024 amounted to $1,215 million, up from $1,145 million in 2023. This 6.1% increase reflects higher expenses related to the integration of new technologies and regulatory compliance costs. The Company remains committed to maintaining operational excellence and ensuring compliance with evolving industry standards.

Operating Income

Operating income for fiscal year 2024 was $359 million, a decrease of 19.9% from $448 million in 2023. The decline in operating income was primarily due to the increased cost of revenue and operating expenses, which outpaced revenue growth. Despite these challenges, Novmer continues to focus on strategic initiatives aimed at improving operational efficiency and profitability.

Interest Expense

Interest expense for fiscal year 2024 increased to $689 million from $652 million in 2023. This rise was primarily due to higher average debt levels incurred to support capital investments and strategic initiatives. The Company actively manages its debt portfolio to optimize interest expenses and enhance financial flexibility.

Effective Tax Rate

Novmer's effective tax rate remained at 0.0% for both fiscal years 2024 and 2023. The absence of a provision for income taxes is attributed to the utilization of tax assets and other tax planning strategies. The Company continually assesses its tax position to ensure compliance and optimize tax efficiency.

Net Income

Net income for fiscal year 2024 was a loss of $330 million, compared to a loss of $204 million in fiscal year 2023. The 61.8% decrease in net income reflects the challenges posed by increased operating expenses and interest costs, which more than offset revenue growth. Novmer remains focused on enhancing profitability through cost management and strategic initiatives to drive long-term value creation.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

Cash and Cash Equivalents

The increase is primarily due to the net cash provided by operating activities, which was partly offset by significant cash used in investing and financing activities.

Net cash provided by operating activities for fiscal year 2024 was $1,274 million. This increase was driven by improvements in working capital management and the Company's ability to generate cash from operations despite reporting a net loss. Effective inventory management and timely collection of accounts receivable contributed positively to cash flow from operations.

Investing Activities

Net cash used in investing activities for fiscal year 2024 was $4,546 million. This substantial outflow was primarily due to other investing activities, net, amounting to $4,025 million, and purchases of property and equipment totaling $521 million. These investments reflect Novmer's commitment to enhancing production capabilities and expanding its product offerings to meet market demand.

Overview

Principal Products and Services

Reportable Segments

Novmer operates through several strategic business segments that address distinct market needs, leveraging the Company's expertise and resources to maximize value creation. The core segments include:

Novmer's polymers are known for their durability, versatility, and environmental sustainability, meeting the increasing demand for materials that support eco-friendly manufacturing processes.

2. Metals Segment: Specializing in industrial metals, this segment supplies essential materials for construction, automotive, and aerospace industries. Novmer's metals are characterized by their strength and reliability, contributing to infrastructure projects and technological advancements globally.

3. Specialty Chemicals Segment: Novmer's specialty chemicals are integral to various industrial processes, offering solutions that enhance performance and efficiency. These chemicals support sectors such as agriculture, pharmaceuticals, and energy, where precision and quality are paramount.

Risk Management and Strategy

Novmer Materials, Inc. ("Novmer" or "the Company") is committed to maintaining a robust cybersecurity risk management program designed to identify, assess, manage, and respond to cybersecurity threats and incidents. The program is an integral component of our enterprise risk management (ERM) framework, which provides a comprehensive approach to identifying and mitigating risks that could impact our business objectives, operations, and reputation.

Our cybersecurity risk management strategy encompasses a cross-functional approach, leveraging insights and expertise from various departments to address potential material risks arising from cybersecurity threats. We employ a layered defense model that includes a combination of technology controls, strategic partnerships, policies, and procedures to protect our information systems and data assets.

We engage dedicated internal cybersecurity personnel alongside external experts to continuously assess, detect, and respond to cybersecurity threats. This collaboration ensures that we remain vigilant and proactive in our efforts to protect against evolving threats. Our cybersecurity strategy incorporates the use of advanced technologies and threat intelligence to monitor, prevent, and mitigate potential security breaches.

The Company has established documented policies and procedures to guide our cybersecurity practices. These include our information security policy, which outlines the standards and controls we have in place to protect our digital assets, and an incident response plan, which provides a structured approach to managing and recovering from cybersecurity incidents. Our incident response plan encompasses processes for assessing the severity of incidents, escalating them appropriately, and executing containment, investigation, and remediation efforts.

We conduct regular evaluations of the cybersecurity threat landscape and the effectiveness of our security controls. This includes periodic audits, vulnerability assessments, penetration testing, and tabletop exercises involving senior management. These evaluations are essential for identifying potential vulnerabilities and ensuring that our defenses remain robust and adaptive to new threats.

To further enhance our cybersecurity posture, we engage with third-party assessors, consultants, and auditors to conduct independent reviews of our information technology security program. These external assessments provide valuable insights into the design and effectiveness of our cybersecurity controls and help us align with industry best practices and standards.

We also recognize the importance of managing cybersecurity risks associated with our use of third-party service providers. To this end, we have implemented processes to oversee and evaluate the cybersecurity practices of third parties that have access to our systems or data. This includes conducting due diligence, requiring adherence to our cybersecurity standards, and performing regular audits to ensure compliance.

Governance

The governance of our cybersecurity risk management program is overseen by our Board of Directors, which plays an active role in reviewing and discussing the adequacy and effectiveness of our cybersecurity processes. The Board's oversight is facilitated through its Audit Committee, which receives regular reports from management on cybersecurity matters, including assessments of risks, incidents, and the effectiveness of our controls.

Our Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) leads the implementation and management of our cybersecurity strategy. The CISO reports directly to our Chief Information Officer (CIO), who is responsible for the overall strategy, deployment, and risk management of our technology systems. The CISO brings extensive experience in cybersecurity and risk management, with a proven track record of protecting complex technology infrastructures from cyber threats.

The CISO's responsibilities include overseeing the deployment of cybersecurity controls, managing a team of cybersecurity professionals, and ensuring that our security measures align with industry standards and best practices. The CISO also plays a critical role in coordinating incident response efforts and reporting on cybersecurity matters to senior management and the Audit Committee.

In addition to the CISO, our ERM management committee provides input and oversight on our cybersecurity risk management processes. This committee comprises senior leaders from across the organization, including our Chief Innovation Officer, who contribute diverse perspectives and expertise to the identification and management of cybersecurity risks.

Our Board and its Audit Committee engage in ongoing discussions with management to review the risks and threats to our information technology systems. These discussions include assessments of the internal controls we have implemented to safeguard against cybersecurity risks, as well as the steps management has taken to monitor and mitigate these risks.

The Audit Committee also receives updates on notable cybersecurity incidents and potential risks, ensuring that the Board remains informed and engaged in the oversight of our cybersecurity program. This governance structure supports our commitment to maintaining a strong security posture and protecting our business operations, stakeholder interests, and reputation.

In conclusion, Novmer Materials, Inc. is dedicated to maintaining a comprehensive and effective cybersecurity risk management program. Through robust governance, continuous risk assessment, and proactive threat management, we strive to safeguard our information systems and data assets from cybersecurity threats. We recognize that the evolving threat landscape requires constant vigilance and adaptability, and we remain committed to enhancing our cybersecurity capabilities to address emerging challenges and protect our business.

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Overview

The following Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations ("MD&A") provides an overview of the financial condition and operational performance of Novmer Materials, Inc. ("Novmer" or "the Company") for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024. This analysis should be read in conjunction with the Company's consolidated financial statements and accompanying notes included in Item 8 of this Form 10-K. The purpose of this section is to provide a narrative explanation of the financial statements, detailing the significant factors influencing Novmer's financial performance, liquidity, and capital resources.

Novmer operates within the materials sector, focusing on the production and distribution of polymers, industrial metals, and specialty chemicals. The Company leverages its extensive product portfolio and technological expertise to cater to diverse industries, including automotive, construction, and consumer goods. For the fiscal year 2024, Novmer reported revenue of $20,255 million, an increase of $1,175 million, or 6.2%, over fiscal year 2023. Despite global economic uncertainties and competitive pressures, Novmer demonstrated resilience by enhancing operational efficiencies and expanding its market presence.

Results of Operations

Revenue

For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, Novmer’s revenue increased by 6.2% to $20,255 million from $19,080 million in fiscal year 2023. This growth was primarily driven by increased sales volume across key segments, particularly in the polymers and specialty chemicals sectors. The heightened demand for sustainable materials contributed significantly to the revenue increase, reflecting the Company's strategic focus on eco-friendly product innovations.

Cost of Revenue and Gross Margin

The cost of revenue for fiscal year 2024 was $15,825 million, an increase from $14,858 million in the prior year. This 6.5% increase was closely aligned with revenue growth and was primarily attributable to higher raw material costs and increased production volumes. Novmer's gross profit for 2024 was $4,430 million, compared to $4,222 million in 2023, resulting in a gross margin of 21.9% for 2024, slightly down from 22.1% in 2023. The slight decrease in gross margin reflects the impact of rising input costs, which were partially offset by operational efficiencies and price adjustments.

Selling, General and Administrative Expenses

Selling, general and administrative expenses ("SG&A") for fiscal year 2024 were $2,856 million, representing 14.1% of revenue, compared to $2,629 million, or 13.8% of revenue, in 2023. The increase in SG&A expenses was primarily due to higher personnel costs and investments in digital marketing initiatives aimed at enhancing customer engagement and expanding market reach. Novmer continues to focus on cost management strategies to optimize SG&A expenditures while supporting growth initiatives.

Other Operating Expenses

Other operating expenses for fiscal year 2024 amounted to $1,215 million, up from $1,145 million in 2023. This 6.1% increase reflects higher expenses related to the integration of new technologies and regulatory compliance costs. The Company remains committed to maintaining operational excellence and ensuring compliance with evolving industry standards.

Operating Income

Operating income for fiscal year 2024 was $359 million, a decrease of 19.9% from $448 million in 2023. The decline in operating income was primarily due to the increased cost of revenue and operating expenses, which outpaced revenue growth. Despite these challenges, Novmer continues to focus on strategic initiatives aimed at improving operational efficiency and profitability.

Interest Expense

Interest expense for fiscal year 2024 increased to $689 million from $652 million in 2023. This rise was primarily due to higher average debt levels incurred to support capital investments and strategic initiatives. The Company actively manages its debt portfolio to optimize interest expenses and enhance financial flexibility.

Effective Tax Rate

Novmer's effective tax rate remained at 0.0% for both fiscal years 2024 and 2023. The absence of a provision for income taxes is attributed to the utilization of tax assets and other tax planning strategies. The Company continually assesses its tax position to ensure compliance and optimize tax efficiency.

Net Income

Net income for fiscal year 2024 was a loss of $330 million, compared to a loss of $204 million in fiscal year 2023. The 61.8% decrease in net income reflects the challenges posed by increased operating expenses and interest costs, which more than offset revenue growth. Novmer remains focused on enhancing profitability through cost management and strategic initiatives to drive long-term value creation.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

Cash and Cash Equivalents

The increase is primarily due to the net cash provided by operating activities, which was partly offset by significant cash used in investing and financing activities.

The Company continues to prioritize investments that align with its strategic objectives and deliver long-term value.

Debt

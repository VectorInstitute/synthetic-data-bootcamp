Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

You should read the following discussion and analysis of our financial condition and results of operations in conjunction with our consolidated financial statements and related notes included in Part II, Item 8 of this report. This discussion contains forward-looking statements that involve risks and uncertainties. Our actual results could differ materially from those discussed below. Factors that could cause or contribute to such differences include, but are not limited to, those identified below and those discussed in "Risk Factors" included elsewhere in this report.

Overview

Drazennov Medical, Inc. ("Drazennov" or the "Company") is a leading entity in the health care sector, specializing in the development, production, and distribution of medical devices and health care solutions. The Company is incorporated in Delaware with headquarters in Minneapolis, Minnesota, and operates globally. In the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, Drazennov achieved a total revenue of $60,579 million, representing a revenue growth of 3.8% compared to the previous fiscal year. The Company's diverse product portfolio and strategic market approach have positioned it for continued growth and success in the competitive health care industry.

Results of Operations

Revenue

For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, Drazennov recorded revenue of $60,579 million, an increase of $2,223 million, or 3.8%, from $58,356 million in the fiscal year ended December 31, 2023. This growth was driven by a combination of factors, including an increase in the adoption of our innovative medical devices and health care solutions, as well as expanded market reach. The demand for our advanced diagnostic imaging systems and health care IT solutions contributed significantly to this revenue growth, reflecting the Company's commitment to addressing the evolving needs of health care providers and institutions.

Cost of Revenue and Gross Margin

The cost of revenue for fiscal 2024 was $34,924 million, compared to $33,744 million in fiscal 2023, reflecting an increase of $1,180 million. This increase is primarily attributable to higher production volumes and the associated costs of materials and labor. The gross profit for fiscal 2024 was $25,655 million, up from $24,612 million in the previous year. Our gross margin for fiscal 2024 was 42.3%, slightly higher than the 42.2% reported for fiscal 2023. This improvement in gross margin reflects our continued focus on operational efficiency and cost management, enabling us to maintain profitability despite rising input costs.

Research and Development Expenses

Research and development (R&D) expenses in fiscal 2024 were $6,882 million, an increase of $195 million from $6,687 million in fiscal 2023. R&D remains a cornerstone of our growth strategy, and this increase underscores our commitment to innovation and the development of new products. The investment in R&D during this period supported advancements in our product lines and the enhancement of existing technologies, ensuring that Drazennov remains at the forefront of medical technology.

Selling, General and Administrative Expenses

Selling, general and administrative (SG&A) expenses amounted to $7,148 million in fiscal 2024, compared to $6,853 million in fiscal 2023, representing an increase of $295 million. The increase in SG&A expenses is mainly due to expanded sales and marketing efforts to support the launch of new products and enhance brand presence in key markets. Additionally, investments in digital marketing and customer engagement initiatives have been instrumental in driving customer acquisition and retention.

Other Operating Expenses

Other operating expenses for fiscal 2024 were $3,635 million, an increase of $134 million from $3,501 million in fiscal 2023. This increase is primarily attributed to costs associated with the enhancement of our global supply chain infrastructure and the integration of new technologies into our operations. These strategic investments are intended to improve our operational resilience and agility in response to market demands.

Operating Income

Operating income for fiscal 2024 was $7,990 million, an increase of $419 million or 5.5% from $7,571 million in fiscal 2023. The improvement in operating income reflects the Company's ability to effectively manage operating expenses while achieving revenue growth. Our strategic initiatives, including cost optimization and process improvements, have contributed to enhanced operational performance.

Interest Expense

Interest expense for fiscal 2024 was $835 million, compared to $751 million in fiscal 2023, representing an increase of $84 million. This increase is primarily due to higher average debt levels during the fiscal year, as the Company utilized additional financing to support strategic initiatives and capital investments. Managing our debt obligations effectively remains a priority to ensure financial flexibility.

Income Before Income Taxes and Provision for Income Taxes

Income before income taxes for fiscal 2024 was $7,155 million, an increase of $335 million from $6,820 million in fiscal 2023. The provision for income taxes in fiscal 2024 was $1,827 million, compared to $1,804 million in the prior year. The effective tax rate for fiscal 2024 was 25.5%, slightly lower than the 26.5% reported in fiscal 2023. The reduction in the effective tax rate is primarily attributable to favorable changes in the geographic mix of earnings and tax planning initiatives.

Net Income

Net income for fiscal 2024 was $5,328 million, an increase of $312 million or 6.2% from $5,016 million in fiscal 2023. The growth in net income reflects our strong operational performance and disciplined financial management. The increase in net income also highlights our ability to deliver value to shareholders while investing in growth opportunities.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

Cash and Cash Equivalents

As of December 31, 2024, cash and cash equivalents were $28,917 million, an increase of $2,258 million from $26,659 million at the end of fiscal 2023. The increase in cash and cash equivalents is primarily due to robust cash flow generation from operating activities, which has enabled us to fund strategic investments while maintaining a strong liquidity position.

Operating Activities

Net cash provided by operating activities in fiscal 2024 was $12,628 million. The strong cash flow from operations reflects the Company's continued focus on efficient working capital management and the generation of higher earnings. Changes in working capital, including an increase in accounts receivable and inventory, were effectively managed to support business growth.

Investing Activities

Net cash used in investing activities for fiscal 2024 was $8,413 million. This reflects our continued investment in property, plant, and equipment, amounting to $1,321 million, as well as other investing activities totaling $7,092 million. These investments are aligned with our strategy to enhance production capabilities and support the development of new technologies, ensuring that we remain competitive in the evolving health care landscape.

Financing Activities

Net cash used in financing activities for fiscal 2024 was $1,957 million. The primary components of financing cash flows include the net change in long-term debt, which increased by $1,872 million, and the payment of dividends totaling $822 million. Additionally, share repurchases, net of issuances, amounted to $3,007 million. These activities are consistent with our capital allocation strategy, balancing debt management, shareholder returns, and strategic investments.

Capital Expenditures

Capital expenditures in fiscal 2024 amounted to $1,321 million, reflecting our commitment to maintaining and expanding our production facilities and enhancing our technological infrastructure. These investments are critical to supporting our growth initiatives and ensuring the delivery of high-quality products to our customers.

Debt Management

As of December 31, 2024, our long-term debt stood at $18,562 million, an increase from $16,690 million at the end of fiscal 2023. The increase in debt is primarily due to financing activities undertaken to support strategic expansion and capital investments. We remain focused on managing our debt levels prudently to maintain financial flexibility and support our growth objectives.

Dividends and Share Repurchases

During fiscal 2024, we paid dividends totaling $822 million, reflecting our commitment to providing consistent returns to our shareholders. Additionally, we repurchased shares, net of issuances, amounting to $3,007 million. Our capital return strategy is designed to optimize shareholder value while ensuring the Company has the necessary resources to pursue growth opportunities.

Critical Accounting Estimates

Our consolidated financial statements are prepared in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP) in the United States. The preparation of these financial statements requires management to make estimates and assumptions that affect the reported amounts of assets, liabilities, revenues, and expenses. We consider an accounting policy to be critical if it is important to our financial condition and results of operations, and it requires significant judgment and estimation by management in its application.

Revenue Recognition

Revenue recognition is a critical accounting estimate that involves judgment in determining when and how much revenue should be recognized. We recognize revenue when control of the promised goods or services is transferred to our customers, in an amount that reflects the consideration we expect to receive. This process involves assessing the timing of revenue recognition and estimating variable consideration, which requires management judgment and estimation.

Inventory Valuation

Our inventory is valued at the lower of cost or net realizable value. The determination of net realizable value involves judgment regarding future demand, market conditions, and the potential for obsolescence. We regularly review inventory levels to identify excess or obsolete items and adjust the carrying value as necessary. Changes in these estimates could have a material impact on our financial results.

Goodwill and Intangible Assets

We evaluate goodwill and intangible assets for impairment annually, or more frequently if events or changes in circumstances indicate that an impairment may have occurred. The impairment analysis requires management judgment regarding the fair value of reporting units and the identification of potential indicators of impairment. Changes in assumptions, such as discount rates or projected cash flows, could result in impairment charges that affect our financial condition and results of operations.

Income Taxes

Income taxes involve judgment, including the evaluation of uncertainties in the application of complex tax laws. We assess the realizability of deferred tax assets, which requires management to make assumptions about future taxable income and the potential impact of tax planning strategies. Significant changes in these assumptions could affect the recorded amounts of deferred tax assets and liabilities and our provision for income taxes.

In conclusion, Drazennov Medical, Inc. achieved solid financial performance in fiscal 2024, driven by revenue growth, effective cost management, and strategic investments. Our focus on innovation and operational excellence positions us for continued success in the competitive health care industry. We remain committed to delivering value to our shareholders while ensuring that we have the resources necessary to pursue growth opportunities and navigate potential challenges in the market.

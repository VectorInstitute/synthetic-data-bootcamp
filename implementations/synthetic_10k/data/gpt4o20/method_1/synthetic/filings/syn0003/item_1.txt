Item 1. Business

General Development of Business

Selzennov Materials, Inc. ("Selzennov" or the "Company") was incorporated in Delaware and maintains its headquarters in Charlotte, North Carolina. As a leading entity in the materials sector, Selzennov operates with a fiscal year ending on December 31. The consolidated financial statements include the activities of both domestic and international operations, adhering to U.S. GAAP (accounting principles generally accepted in the United States of America). The Company's operations span across multiple geographical regions, reflecting Selzennov's commitment to meeting the diverse needs of its global customer base.

Narrative Description of Business

General

Selzennov Materials, Inc. is a prominent provider of advanced materials solutions, catering to various industries worldwide. With annual revenues of $14,240 million for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, Selzennov is dedicated to supplying high-quality products and services that enhance the operational efficiency and sustainability of its customers. Employing approximately 18,000 individuals, Selzennov's workforce is integral to the Company's ongoing success and innovation.

Principal Products and Services

Selzennov specializes in the production and distribution of a wide array of materials that serve critical functions in industrial applications. The Company's portfolio includes specialty chemicals, performance materials, and other advanced solutions designed to meet the stringent demands of sectors such as automotive, construction, electronics, and more. By leveraging cutting-edge technology and a deep understanding of market requirements, Selzennov delivers products that contribute to the improved performance and longevity of its customers' operations.

Reportable Segments

The operations of Selzennov are organized into distinct reportable segments, each tailored to address specific market needs and opportunities. These segments include:

1. Performance Materials

The Performance Materials segment focuses on the development and supply of high-performance materials that are essential in enhancing product durability and efficiency. This segment serves a diverse range of industries, including automotive, aerospace, and electronics. By providing materials that withstand extreme conditions, Selzennov supports its customers in achieving superior product performance.

2. Specialty Chemicals

The Specialty Chemicals segment is dedicated to the production of chemical solutions that optimize industrial processes. These chemicals are utilized in various applications, from improving manufacturing efficiencies to ensuring environmental compliance. The segment's offerings are critical in addressing the evolving regulatory and performance requirements of industries such as manufacturing, water treatment, and energy.

3. Advanced Solutions

The Advanced Solutions segment encompasses innovative products and services that facilitate the transition to more sustainable and efficient industrial practices. This segment offers tailored solutions that aid customers in reducing their environmental footprint while maintaining operational excellence. Key industries served include renewable energy, sustainable construction, and eco-friendly manufacturing.

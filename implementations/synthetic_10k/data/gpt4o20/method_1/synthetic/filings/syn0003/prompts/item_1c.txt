We also maintain third-party management processes to identify and manage the cybersecurity risks associated with third-party service providers. We periodically evaluate and improve the effectiveness of our cybersecurity program internally and by engaging with consultants and other third-party advisors to conduct reviews and assessments of our program. Overseeing the assessment and management of our exposure to various risks, including cybersecurity, is a key oversight responsibility for the Board of Directors. We have an enterprise risk management (ERM) program that includes the processes used to identify, assess and manage our most significant enterprise risks and uncertainties that could materially impact the long-term health of the Company or prevent the achievement of strategic objectives. Our chief financial officer (CFO) facilitates the Company’s ERM program, which includes a formal assessment of the Company’s risk environment at least once per year. Our CFO reviews the ERM program with the Audit Committee at least once per year, including reviewing existing risks and significant emerging risks across the Company’s key risk categories. Item 1A. Risk Factors

This section highlights the significant risks that could adversely affect Selzennov Materials, Inc. ("Selzennov" or the "Company"). Additional risks not presently known to us or that we currently consider immaterial may also adversely affect our business, financial condition, operating results, and cash flows. Risks Related to the Business and Industry

Volatility in Raw Material Costs

The cost of raw materials significantly affects our financial performance. Selzennov relies on a stable supply of specialty chemicals and performance materials to manufacture its products. Fluctuations in the prices of these raw materials, driven by changes in global supply and demand, geopolitical tensions, or natural disasters, could lead to increased production costs that we may not be able to pass on to our customers. Such cost variations could materially impact our profit margins and financial results.

Intense Competition in the Materials Sector

The materials sector is highly competitive, with numerous companies vying for market share. Selzennov faces competition from both established players and new entrants in the industry. Competitors may offer products at lower prices or develop superior technologies, which could erode our market position. Our ability to maintain a competitive edge depends on our capacity to innovate, maintain product quality, and respond swiftly to market changes.

Dependence on Key Customers

A significant portion of our revenue is derived from a limited number of key customers across industries such as automotive, aerospace, and electronics. The loss of any major customer, or a significant reduction in orders from them, could adversely impact our sales and profitability. Additionally, if these customers experience financial difficulties or changes in demand for their products, it could have a ripple effect on our operations.

Risks Related to Operations

Operational Disruptions

Selzennov's manufacturing operations are subject to risks of disruption due to equipment failure, industrial accidents, natural disasters, or other unforeseen events. Such disruptions could result in production delays, increased costs, and an inability to meet customer demands. Operational inefficiencies or prolonged downtime could significantly affect our ability to generate revenue and maintain market reputation.

Supply Chain Constraints

Our operations are heavily dependent on a complex global supply chain. Disruptions in the supply chain, whether due to transportation issues, supplier insolvency, or geopolitical factors, could lead to shortages of essential components and materials. These constraints may result in increased costs and delays in production, adversely affecting our ability to fulfill customer orders on time.

As a materials company, Selzennov must adhere to stringent environmental and safety regulations. Non-compliance with these regulations could result in significant fines, penalties, or legal liabilities. Additionally, changes in environmental laws or increased enforcement could lead to higher costs for compliance and operational adjustments. Our commitment to environmental stewardship is critical not only for legal compliance but also for maintaining our corporate reputation.

Selzennov's financial stability is influenced by its level of indebtedness. Our substantial long-term debt obligations require significant cash flow for interest and principal payments. High debt levels may limit our ability to invest in growth opportunities, respond to market changes, or withstand economic downturns. Changes in interest rates can significantly impact our financial results. Our ability to meet financial obligations and pursue strategic investments depends on maintaining adequate liquidity. Selzennov operates in multiple jurisdictions, subjecting us to various international trade laws and regulations. Non-compliance with these laws, whether related to tariffs, export controls, or trade sanctions, could result in substantial fines and operational restrictions. Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

The following Management’s Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations of Selzennov Materials, Inc. (“Selzennov” or the “Company”) should be read in conjunction with the consolidated financial statements of the Company and the accompanying notes to the consolidated financial statements. This discussion covers the fiscal years ended December 31, 2024, and December 31, 2023.

Overview

In fiscal year 2024, Selzennov Materials, Inc. experienced a solid performance amid evolving market conditions within the materials sector. The Company reported revenue growth of 5.3%, reflecting a strong demand across its diversified portfolio, particularly in the Performance Materials and Specialty Chemicals segments. The growth in revenue was supported by strategic initiatives aimed at enhancing operational efficiency and expanding market reach. Despite challenges such as increased raw material costs and competitive pressures, Selzennov maintained a robust financial position, evidenced by an increase in net income by 33.9% over the prior year.

The Company’s focus on maintaining competitive pricing and optimizing its supply chain contributed to an improved operating income. Such strategic measures allowed Selzennov to navigate the complexities of the global materials market effectively. Additionally, the Company continued to advance its commitment to sustainability and innovation, which are pivotal to its long-term growth strategy.

Results of Operations

Revenue

For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, Selzennov recorded revenue of $14,240 million, compared to $13,521 million in fiscal 2023, representing an increase of 5.3%. This growth was driven by higher sales volumes across key segments, underpinned by robust demand in the automotive and electronics industries. Moreover, the Company’s strategic expansion into emerging markets contributed positively to the revenue trajectory.

Cost of Revenue and Gross Margin

Cost of revenue for fiscal 2024 was $11,829 million, up from $11,290 million in fiscal 2023. This increase in cost was primarily attributable to the rising prices of raw materials and logistical expenses. Despite the increase in costs, Selzennov’s gross profit improved to $2,411 million from $2,231 million in the previous year. Consequently, the gross margin for fiscal 2024 stood at 16.9%, a slight improvement from 16.5% in fiscal 2023. The margin improvement was achieved through strategic cost management initiatives and operational efficiencies.

Selling, General and Administrative Expenses

Selling, general and administrative expenses decreased to $365 million in fiscal 2024 from $378 million in fiscal 2023. This reduction was largely due to the Company’s ongoing efforts to streamline administrative processes and optimize workforce deployment. As a percentage of revenue, these expenses decreased from 2.8% in fiscal 2023 to 2.6% in fiscal 2024, reflecting enhanced operational efficiencies.

Other Operating Expenses

Other operating expenses increased from $811 million in fiscal 2023 to $854 million in fiscal 2024. The increase was primarily due to higher depreciation expenses associated with recent investments in manufacturing infrastructure and technology upgrades. These investments are part of Selzennov’s strategy to bolster production capabilities and ensure long-term competitiveness.

Operating Income

Operating income for fiscal 2024 was $1,192 million, representing a 14.4% increase from $1,042 million in fiscal 2023. The improvement in operating income was driven by higher gross margins and effective cost management strategies. The operating margin for fiscal 2024 was 8.4%, compared to 7.7% in fiscal 2023, indicating a stronger operational performance.

Interest Expense

Interest expense increased to $697 million in fiscal 2024 from $665 million in fiscal 2023. The increase in interest expense was primarily due to higher average debt levels during the year, as the Company financed strategic capital investments aimed at enhancing its production and technological capabilities.

Effective Tax Rate

The provision for income taxes was $112 million for fiscal 2024, compared to $91 million in fiscal 2023. The effective tax rate decreased to 22.6% in fiscal 2024 from 24.1% in fiscal 2023. This reduction in the effective tax rate was primarily due to tax planning strategies and the utilization of available tax credits.

Net Income

Net income for fiscal 2024 increased to $383 million, from $286 million in fiscal 2023, reflecting a growth of 33.9%. The improvement in net income was driven by higher operating income and a lower effective tax rate. The net margin improved to 2.7% in fiscal 2024 from 2.1% in the prior year, underscoring the Company’s enhanced profitability.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

Cash and Cash Equivalents

Selzennov’s cash and cash equivalents increased to $3,038 million as of December 31, 2024, from $2,831 million at the end of fiscal 2023. The increase in cash reserves was primarily the result of strong operating cash flows and prudent financial management.

Operating Activities

Net cash provided by operating activities was $1,186 million for fiscal 2024. This was driven by robust net income and effective working capital management, which included optimized inventory levels and efficient accounts receivable collections.

Investing Activities

Net cash used in investing activities amounted to $1,900 million in fiscal 2024. This was primarily due to capital expenditures of $779 million for property and equipment purchases, as well as other strategic investments amounting to $1,121 million. These investments were focused on enhancing production capacity and technological advancements to support future growth.

Financing Activities

This included a net increase in long-term debt of $699 million, dividend payments of $116 million, and share repurchases net of issuances amounting to $338 million. The Company’s capital allocation strategy reflects its commitment to returning value to shareholders while maintaining financial flexibility.

Capital Expenditures

Selzennov’s capital expenditures for fiscal 2024 were $779 million. These expenditures were directed towards expanding manufacturing capabilities and upgrading technology infrastructure, which are critical to meeting increased demand and improving operational efficiencies.

Debt

As of December 31, 2024, Selzennov’s long-term debt stood at $15,480 million, compared to $14,781 million at the end of fiscal 2023. The increase in debt was primarily used to finance capital investments and strategic initiatives.

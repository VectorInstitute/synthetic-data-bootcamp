Item 1C. Cybersecurity

Selzennov Materials, Inc. ("Selzennov" or the "Company") understands the criticality of robust cybersecurity measures in safeguarding our operations and maintaining stakeholder trust. Our cybersecurity strategy is carefully designed to align with our business objectives and to effectively manage risks associated with cyber threats. This strategy is integrated within our broader enterprise risk management framework and is subject to ongoing evaluation and enhancement to ensure resilience against evolving threats.

Cybersecurity Risk Management

Our cybersecurity risk management program encompasses a comprehensive set of policies, standards, and practices aimed at mitigating risks associated with cyber threats. This program is built on a foundation of industry best practices and frameworks, including the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) Cybersecurity Framework. Selzennov employs a multi-layered approach to cybersecurity, which includes preventive, detective, and response mechanisms to protect our digital assets.

Key components of our cybersecurity strategy include:

- Implementation of Advanced Security Technologies: Selzennov utilizes a variety of security technologies to protect against unauthorized access and potential data breaches. These technologies include firewalls, intrusion detection and prevention systems, encryption, and endpoint protection solutions. By leveraging advanced technologies, we aim to minimize vulnerabilities and enhance our ability to detect and respond to threats promptly.

- Threat Monitoring and Intelligence: Our dedicated cybersecurity team continuously monitors for potential threats and vulnerabilities through a sophisticated security operations center. We employ threat intelligence tools that provide real-time insights into emerging cyber risks, enabling us to proactively address potential threats before they materialize.

- Incident Response and Recovery: In the event of a cybersecurity incident, Selzennov has established response protocols to contain, mitigate, and recover from the incident efficiently. These protocols include defined roles and responsibilities, escalation procedures, and communication plans to ensure a coordinated response across the organization.

Third-Party Risk Management

Recognizing the importance of third-party relationships, Selzennov has implemented a rigorous third-party management process to assess and manage cybersecurity risks associated with our suppliers and service providers. This process involves conducting thorough risk assessments, establishing security requirements in contracts, and performing regular audits and reviews to ensure compliance with our security standards. We work closely with our partners to reinforce security measures and mitigate potential vulnerabilities in our supply chain.

Assessment and Evaluation

Selzennov is committed to the continuous improvement of our cybersecurity posture. We conduct regular assessments and evaluations of our cybersecurity program through internal audits and external reviews by independent consultants. These evaluations include penetration testing, vulnerability assessments, and simulated cyber attack exercises to test our defenses and identify areas for enhancement. Feedback from these assessments informs our strategy and enables us to adapt to the changing cybersecurity landscape.

Governance and Oversight

Cybersecurity governance at Selzennov is a key element of our risk management framework. Our Board of Directors actively oversees the management of cybersecurity risks, supported by the Audit Committee, which is tasked with the oversight of our cybersecurity program. The Audit Committee receives regular updates on cybersecurity matters, threats, and incidents, ensuring that the Board is informed of the Company's risk exposure and mitigation strategies.

Role of the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)

The Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) plays a pivotal role in Selzennov's cybersecurity efforts. The CISO is responsible for leading the Company's global cybersecurity program and managing cybersecurity risks. Our CISO possesses extensive experience in information security and has a proven track record of leadership in cybersecurity management. Reporting directly to the Chief Financial Officer (CFO), the CISO ensures that cybersecurity considerations are integrated into strategic business decisions and operational processes.

The CISO leads a team of cybersecurity professionals who are experts in various domains of information security. This team is responsible for implementing security measures, monitoring threats, and responding to incidents. The CISO also collaborates with other business units to promote a culture of security awareness and ensure that cybersecurity practices are embedded throughout the organization.

Cybersecurity Training and Awareness

Selzennov places great emphasis on fostering a culture of cybersecurity awareness among all employees. We provide regular training and educational programs to equip our workforce with the knowledge and skills necessary to recognize and respond to cyber threats. These programs include general cybersecurity training, role-based education for employees with specific responsibilities, and targeted phishing simulations to reinforce good security practices.

Challenges and Future Outlook

Despite our comprehensive cybersecurity efforts, Selzennov acknowledges that no system is entirely immune to cyber threats. The cybersecurity landscape is constantly evolving, with new threats and vulnerabilities emerging regularly. We remain vigilant and committed to investing in our cybersecurity capabilities to ensure the protection of our digital assets and the continuity of our operations.

Our future focus includes expanding our cybersecurity initiatives to address emerging technologies and threats, enhancing our incident response capabilities, and strengthening our collaboration with industry partners to share intelligence and best practices. Selzennov is dedicated to maintaining a robust cybersecurity posture that supports our business objectives and safeguards the interests of our stakeholders.

In conclusion, Selzennov Materials, Inc. is committed to maintaining a resilient cybersecurity framework that effectively manages risks and adapts to the dynamic threat environment. Through strategic investments, governance oversight, and a culture of awareness, we aim to protect our operations and uphold the trust of our customers, partners, and shareholders.

Item 1. Business

Kalondra Machinery, Inc., together with its subsidiaries ("Kalondra" or the "Company"), is a leading provider in the industrial sector, headquartered in San Jose, California, and incorporated in the state of Delaware. The Company specializes in the design, manufacturing, and distribution of a wide range of machinery products and technology solutions for diverse industries, playing a crucial role in advancing industrial capabilities and efficiencies across the globe. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, Kalondra reported total revenue of $82,769 million.

Overview

Kalondra Machinery, Inc. is committed to delivering innovative and reliable machinery solutions that enhance productivity and operational efficiency for its customers. The Company’s operations are supported by an extensive network of manufacturing facilities, research and development centers, and sales and distribution channels worldwide. Kalondra's strategic focus is on maintaining a robust portfolio of products while continually investing in cutting-edge technology to meet the evolving needs of its clients.

Principal Products and Services

Kalondra offers a comprehensive range of machinery products, including industrial automation systems, construction machinery, and precision tools. These products are designed to serve various sectors such as construction, mining, agriculture, and manufacturing. The Company also provides aftermarket services, including maintenance, repair, and parts replacement, ensuring longevity and optimal performance of its machinery.

Reportable Segments

Kalondra operates through three principal segments: Industrial Automation, Construction Equipment, and Aftermarket Services.

- Industrial Automation: This segment focuses on providing advanced automation solutions that enhance production capabilities across various industries. Products include robotic systems, control systems, and software solutions that integrate seamlessly into customers’ existing operations, reducing labor costs and increasing production efficiency.

- Construction Equipment: Kalondra's construction machinery segment offers a broad range of equipment designed for the construction and mining industries. Key products include excavators, loaders, and cranes, which are renowned for their durability and efficiency. This segment emphasizes innovation and robust design to meet the demanding requirements of construction projects worldwide.

- Aftermarket Services: The Aftermarket Services segment provides comprehensive support to ensure the sustained operation of Kalondra's machinery. This includes scheduled maintenance, parts supply, and technical support. The Company’s global network ensures rapid response times and high availability of parts, minimizing downtime for customers.

Customers

Kalondra serves a diverse customer base, ranging from small businesses to large multinational corporations across various industries. Its products are integral to sectors such as construction, agriculture, energy, and manufacturing. The Company prides itself on building strong relationships with its customers, offering tailored solutions that meet specific operational needs and providing extensive support throughout the product lifecycle.

Sales and Marketing

Kalondra employs a multifaceted sales and marketing approach, utilizing both direct sales teams and a network of authorized distributors. The Company leverages its strong brand reputation and extensive industry knowledge to effectively market its products. Additionally, Kalondra invests in digital marketing strategies and participates in industry trade shows and exhibitions to enhance brand visibility and engage with potential clients.

Competition

The industrial machinery market is highly competitive, with numerous players offering a wide array of products and services. Kalondra competes with other machinery manufacturers on the basis of product quality, technological innovation, customer service, and price. The Company differentiates itself through its comprehensive product range, commitment to innovation, and strong customer support services.

Seasonality

Kalondra's business is subject to certain seasonal fluctuations, primarily influenced by the construction and agricultural cycles. Demand for construction equipment typically peaks during warmer months when construction activity is at its highest. Similarly, agricultural machinery sales are influenced by planting and harvesting seasons. Kalondra manages these fluctuations through strategic inventory management and flexible production schedules.

Intellectual Property

Innovation is central to Kalondra's operations, and the Company holds numerous patents and trademarks that protect its proprietary technology and brand. Kalondra's intellectual property portfolio is a critical asset that supports its competitive advantage and enables the Company to deliver cutting-edge solutions to its customers.

Government Regulation

Kalondra operates in a highly regulated environment, complying with numerous laws and regulations relating to product safety, environmental standards, and labor practices. The Company is committed to maintaining the highest standards of compliance and actively monitors regulatory developments to ensure adherence to applicable laws.

Human Capital

As of December 31, 2024, Kalondra Machinery, Inc. employed approximately 126,100 individuals worldwide. The Company values its workforce as a pivotal component of its success and invests in comprehensive training and development programs to enhance employee skills and expertise. Kalondra is dedicated to fostering a diverse and inclusive work environment that promotes innovation and collaboration.

Available Information

Kalondra Machinery, Inc. makes its annual report on Form 10-K, quarterly reports on Form 10-Q, current reports on Form 8-K, and amendments to those reports available on its website as soon as reasonably practicable after filing them with the Securities and Exchange Commission ("SEC"). These reports, and other information about the Company, can be accessed through the SEC's website and the investor section of Kalondra’s corporate website.

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

The following Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations ("MD&A") is intended to help the reader understand the financial results and condition of Kalondra Machinery, Inc. ("Kalondra" or the "Company") for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024. The MD&A should be read in conjunction with the consolidated financial statements and notes thereto included in Part II, Item 8 of this Form 10-K.

Overview

Kalondra Machinery, Inc., headquartered in San Jose, California, is a leader in the industrial sector, specializing in the design, manufacturing, and distribution of a wide range of machinery products and technology solutions. Our commitment to innovation and quality has driven our financial performance, enabling us to serve diverse industries including construction, mining, agriculture, and manufacturing. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, the Company reported total revenue of $82,769 million, reflecting a revenue growth of 6.7% compared to the prior fiscal year.

Results of Operations

Revenue

For fiscal 2024, revenue was $82,769 million, an increase of $5,197 million, or 6.7%, from $77,572 million in fiscal 2023. This growth was primarily driven by increased demand across all major segments, particularly in industrial automation and construction equipment, as global industrial activities rebounded post-pandemic. Additionally, strategic pricing initiatives and enhanced product offerings contributed to the revenue increase.

Cost of Revenue and Gross Margin

The cost of revenue for fiscal 2024 was $57,989 million, compared to $55,129 million in fiscal 2023, representing an increase of $2,860 million. Despite the rise in cost of revenue, the gross margin improved to 29.9% from 28.9% in 2023. This improvement was mainly due to operational efficiencies, cost management strategies, and favorable pricing dynamics. The Company also benefited from economies of scale and a focus on high-margin products.

Research and Development

Research and development expenses were $1,986 million in fiscal 2024, up from $1,899 million in 2023. The increase of $87 million reflects our continued investment in innovation to maintain competitive advantage and meet evolving customer needs. These expenses represented 2.4% of total revenue for both fiscal 2024 and 2023, consistent with our strategic emphasis on technological advancements.

Selling, General and Administrative Expenses

Selling, general and administrative (SG&A) expenses totaled $6,406 million in fiscal 2024, an increase of $530 million from $5,876 million in 2023. This increase was due to higher sales activity, increased marketing efforts to support brand expansion, and investments in strengthening our global distribution network. SG&A expenses as a percentage of revenue increased slightly from 7.6% in 2023 to 7.7% in 2024, indicating efficient cost management amid expanding operations.

Other Operating Expenses

Other operating expenses were $4,966 million in fiscal 2024, up from $4,654 million in 2023. The increase of $312 million was primarily due to higher operational costs associated with expanded manufacturing capacity and enhanced logistics to meet growing customer demand. Despite this increase, the Company's focus on operational efficiency helped mitigate the impact on overall profitability.

Operating Income

Operating income for fiscal 2024 was $11,422 million, representing an increase of $1,408 million, or 14.1%, from $10,014 million in 2023. The improvement in operating income was driven by higher revenue, improved gross margin, and effective cost management strategies. The operating margin improved to 13.8% in 2024 from 12.9% in the prior year, reflecting our ability to leverage operational efficiencies and strategic pricing.

Interest Expense

Interest expense remained consistent at $103 million for both fiscal 2024 and 2023. The stability in interest expense reflects the Company's disciplined approach to managing its capital structure and debt obligations, despite increased investments in growth initiatives.

Effective Tax Rate

The effective tax rate for fiscal 2024 was 20.9%, compared to 20.4% in 2023. The slight increase in the effective tax rate was due to changes in the geographic mix of pre-tax income and adjustments related to tax legislation in certain jurisdictions. The Company continues to focus on optimizing its global tax structure to enhance shareholder value.

Net Income

Net income for fiscal 2024 was $8,951 million, an increase of $1,059 million, or 13.4%, from $7,892 million in fiscal 2023. The net income growth was primarily driven by higher operating income and improved gross margin, partially offset by a marginally higher effective tax rate. The net margin improved to 10.8% in 2024 from 10.2% in the prior year, underscoring the Company's strong financial performance and ability to deliver shareholder value.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

Cash and Cash Equivalents

As of December 31, 2024, cash and cash equivalents totaled $897 million, an increase of $31 million from $866 million at the end of 2023. The increase in cash and cash equivalents reflects the Company's strong cash generation capabilities, despite significant investments in growth initiatives and shareholder returns.

Operating Activities

Net cash provided by operating activities was $13,653 million in fiscal 2024. This strong cash flow was driven by higher net income and efficient working capital management, including improved collections on accounts receivable and optimized inventory levels. The positive cash flow from operations underscores the Company's robust operational performance and ability to fund strategic initiatives.

Investing Activities

Net cash used in investing activities was $5,242 million for fiscal 2024. This included capital expenditures of $2,210 million, reflecting investments in capacity expansion, technology upgrades, and infrastructure enhancements to support future growth. Additionally, other investing activities, net, accounted for $3,032 million, primarily related to strategic acquisitions and investments in innovation.

Financing Activities

Net cash used in financing activities was $8,380 million for fiscal 2024. This included the repurchase of shares totaling $7,803 million, demonstrating the Company's commitment to returning value to shareholders. Dividends paid amounted to $567 million, reflecting the Company's consistent dividend policy. The net change in long-term debt was a decrease of $10 million, highlighting the Company's focus on maintaining a balanced capital structure.

Capital Expenditures

Capital expenditures in fiscal 2024 were $2,210 million, reflecting the Company's strategic investments in enhancing production capabilities and technological advancements. These investments are critical to supporting our growth trajectory, ensuring operational excellence, and maintaining our competitive position in the industry.

Debt

As of December 31, 2024, the Company's long-term debt was $2,278 million, a slight decrease from $2,288 million at the end of 2023. The Company continues to manage its debt prudently, balancing growth investments with financial stability. The leverage ratio remains within the targeted range, providing flexibility for future strategic initiatives.

Dividends and Share Repurchases

In fiscal 2024, the Company returned significant value to shareholders through dividends and share repurchases. Dividends paid totaled $567 million, consistent with our commitment to delivering stable and predictable returns. Additionally, the Company repurchased shares worth $7,803 million, reflecting confidence in our long-term growth prospects and commitment to enhancing shareholder value.

Critical Accounting Estimates

The preparation of financial statements in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles requires management to make estimates and assumptions that affect the reported amounts of assets, liabilities, revenues, and expenses. The most significant estimates include those related to revenue recognition, impairment of goodwill and intangible assets, and inventory valuation.

Revenue from product sales is recognized when control of the product is transferred to the customer, which generally occurs upon shipment or delivery, depending on the terms of the sale. The amount of revenue recognized reflects the consideration to which the Company expects to be entitled in exchange for the products, taking into account any variable consideration and applicable discounts.

Impairment of Goodwill and Intangible Assets

The Company performs annual impairment assessments of goodwill and indefinite-lived intangible assets, or more frequently if events or changes in circumstances indicate that the carrying value may not be recoverable. The impairment test involves comparing the fair value of the reporting unit to its carrying value. If the carrying value exceeds the fair value, an impairment loss is recognized.

Inventory Valuation

Inventories are valued at the lower of cost or net realizable value. The Company regularly reviews inventories for excess or obsolete items and writes down inventory to its estimated net realizable value when necessary. Management's estimates are based on historical usage rates, estimated future demand, and market conditions.

Conclusion

Kalondra Machinery, Inc. delivered strong financial results for fiscal 2024, driven by robust revenue growth, improved margins, and disciplined cost management. Our strategic investments in technology and capacity expansion, coupled with a focus on operational excellence, position us well for sustainable growth. The Company remains committed to enhancing shareholder value through strategic initiatives, efficient capital allocation, and strong financial performance.

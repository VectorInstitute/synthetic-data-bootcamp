Item 1C. Cybersecurity

Kalondra Machinery, Inc. ("Kalondra" or the "Company") prioritizes cybersecurity as a fundamental component of its operational strategy, recognizing the critical importance of protecting its information systems, data, and the privacy of its stakeholders. This commitment is reflected in our comprehensive cybersecurity management and governance program, designed to address the evolving landscape of cyber threats and safeguard the integrity and confidentiality of our information assets.

Cybersecurity Risk Management and Strategy

Kalondra's approach to cybersecurity is structured around a robust risk management framework, informed by industry best practices and international standards, such as the National Institute of Standards and Technology Cybersecurity Framework (NIST CSF). This framework guides our efforts to identify, assess, and mitigate cybersecurity risks across the organization.

Our cybersecurity strategy is underpinned by a multi-layered defense model, incorporating advanced detection and prevention technologies to protect against unauthorized access, data breaches, and other cyber threats. We conduct regular penetration testing and vulnerability assessments, employing both internal resources and third-party cybersecurity experts to rigorously evaluate our security posture. These assessments are pivotal in identifying potential vulnerabilities, enabling us to implement timely and effective remediation measures.

Kalondra leverages threat intelligence services to maintain awareness of emerging threats and trends in the cybersecurity landscape. This proactive approach allows us to anticipate and defend against potential attacks, minimizing the impact on our operations and stakeholders. The Company also employs state-of-the-art encryption technologies to secure sensitive data, ensuring that information remains protected both in transit and at rest.

In addition to technical controls, Kalondra places a strong emphasis on employee awareness and training. We conduct regular cybersecurity training sessions to educate employees about security best practices and the importance of vigilance in identifying and reporting suspicious activities. Phishing simulations and other awareness exercises are conducted periodically to reinforce our security culture and enhance employee readiness.

Third-party risk management is another critical aspect of our cybersecurity strategy. Kalondra conducts comprehensive security assessments of third-party vendors and service providers to ensure they meet our stringent cybersecurity standards. This includes evaluating their security policies, incident response capabilities, and data protection measures. Where gaps are identified, we work collaboratively with our partners to address and mitigate risks to an acceptable level.

Governance

The governance of cybersecurity at Kalondra is anchored by the active involvement of our Board of Directors and executive management. Oversight of cybersecurity risk and strategy is vested in the Board's Audit Committee, which receives regular updates on the Company's cybersecurity posture, risk assessments, and strategic initiatives.

The Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) plays a pivotal role in Kalondra's cybersecurity governance structure. With extensive experience in information security and risk management, the CISO is responsible for leading the development and execution of our cybersecurity strategy. The CISO reports directly to the Chief Executive Officer, ensuring that cybersecurity remains a top priority at the highest levels of the organization.

The CISO oversees a dedicated cybersecurity team, tasked with implementing security policies, managing incident response efforts, and ensuring compliance with relevant regulations and industry standards. This team collaborates closely with other departments, including IT, legal, and human resources, to integrate cybersecurity considerations into all aspects of our operations.

Kalondra's Incident Response Plan outlines our procedures for managing and mitigating the impact of cybersecurity incidents. This plan is regularly tested and updated to reflect changes in the threat environment and lessons learned from previous incidents. In the event of a security breach, our incident response team is activated to contain and remediate the incident, working swiftly to minimize disruption and protect sensitive information.

The Company is committed to transparency in its cybersecurity practices and maintains open lines of communication with stakeholders. We provide regular reports to the Board of Directors on cybersecurity risks, initiatives, and incidents, ensuring that they remain informed and engaged in our efforts to protect the organization.

Board members and senior executives receive ongoing education on cybersecurity trends and risks, enabling them to make informed decisions and provide effective oversight. This commitment to continuous learning and improvement is central to our ability to adapt to the dynamic cybersecurity landscape and maintain the trust of our stakeholders.

In conclusion, Kalondra Machinery, Inc. is steadfast in its dedication to cybersecurity excellence, recognizing the vital role it plays in ensuring the resilience and success of our operations. Our comprehensive risk management framework, coupled with robust governance practices, positions us well to navigate the challenges posed by cyber threats and continue delivering value to our customers, employees, and shareholders.

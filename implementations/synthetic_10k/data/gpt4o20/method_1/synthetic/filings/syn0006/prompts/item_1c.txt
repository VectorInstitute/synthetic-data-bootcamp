RISK MANAGEMENT AND STRATEGY
We host, collect, process, use, and retain large amounts of sensitive and personal data across an array of our own and third-party information systems. To help protect these systems and data, we have implemented a robust information security program that includes numerous administrative, technical, and physical safeguards. We strive to evolve our cyber defenses to help minimize impacts from cyber threats. In general, we seek to address cybersecurity risks through a cross-functional approach. This approach focuses on protecting business operations and preserving the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of systems and data by preventing and mitigating cybersecurity threats, as well as effectively responding to cybersecurity incidents when they occur.
Our information security program includes:
•Having designated information security personnel, led by our Chief Information Security and Fraud Prevention Officer (CISO), who has decades of relevant experience and also serves as Chief Information Officer. The CISO is supported primarily by our Cybersecurity, Compliance, Risk, and Fraud Team (CyberCRAFT), which consists of approximately 500 professionals as of July 31, 2025. In addition to bringing their current expertise to their roles, CyberCRAFT professionals have the ability to participate in our specialized training and development programs to further enhance their cybersecurity skill sets and cross-train on related capabilities. The CISO works closely with the Company’s internal legal team to oversee compliance with legal, regulatory and contractual security requirements;
•Risk assessments designed to help identify and prioritize significant cybersecurity risks. Our process for identifying and assessing material risks from cybersecurity threats includes incorporation of an internally developed threat catalog and our tracking of trends for areas such as vulnerability management, our leverage of technical standards and guidance, input from our participation and collaboration with law enforcement and government initiatives, and our internal and vendor-supported threat intelligence initiatives. The cybersecurity risk assessment operates alongside our broader overall enterprise-wide risk assessment and management process, and key cybersecurity risks are presented to the Audit and Risk Committee in a manner that helps frame cybersecurity risks as part of a broader risk context;
•Regular testing and assessments of our systems and controls to evaluate the information security program maturity and effectiveness using cybersecurity frameworks (such as ISO 27001, PCI DSS, and SOC 2) and to identify and address potential vulnerabilities—and as appropriate, we adjust our policies, standards, and processes based on testing and assessment results;
•A vulnerability management program to determine the in-scope systems, patch systems based on criticality, and disclose potential vulnerabilities;
•A cybersecurity incident response plan and scenario-specific playbooks for responding to various types of cybersecurity incidents;
•Business continuity and disaster recovery plans to support more effective response and recovery efforts in the event of a significant cybersecurity incident or disruption;
•The use of external service providers and consultants to assess or monitor the environment or otherwise assist with aspects of our cybersecurity controls;
•Commercially available and customized security technologies and security and business controls to limit access to and use of such sensitive data;
•A security awareness and training program for our employees and contractors, with role-based training for certain personnel and positions; and
•A third-party risk management framework designed to monitor and address cybersecurity risks from various third parties (including vendors, service providers, and other contractors) that includes diligence regarding the third party’s cybersecurity capabilities and additional monitoring of certain third parties based on the results of diligence. In addition, we have established standard contractual terms and conditions regarding cybersecurity applicable to third parties, as well as further downstream parties, that may be tailored to the use case and sensitivity of any data or business processes involved.
Additionally, we maintain cybersecurity insurance which may cover some or all of the potential losses from a cybersecurity incident. During the last fiscal year, we did not identify any risks from cybersecurity threats, including as a result of any previous cybersecurity incidents, that materially affected Intuit, including its business strategy, results of operations, or financial condition. Item 1. Business

Selzenqua Semiconductor, Inc. (hereinafter referred to as "Selzenqua" or the "Company"), a Delaware corporation with headquarters in Princeton, New Jersey, operates within the Information Technology sector, focusing primarily on semiconductor technologies. As of the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, Selzenqua reported total revenue of $16,983 million. The Company employs approximately 50,700 individuals and remains committed to advancing technology and innovation through its diverse product offerings and strategic business segments.

Overview

Selzenqua is a leading innovator in the semiconductor industry, developing and manufacturing high-performance semiconductor products and solutions. Our mission is to enable the digital transformation of industries through our cutting-edge technology and comprehensive product offerings. We provide a wide range of products that are critical to the functioning of various electronic devices, thereby playing a crucial role in the global technology ecosystem.

Principal Products and Services

Our principal product offerings include microprocessors, system-on-chip (SoC) solutions, and application-specific integrated circuits (ASICs). These products are designed to address the diverse needs of our customers across different sectors, including consumer electronics, automotive, telecommunications, and industrial applications. Selzenqua is dedicated to delivering high-performance, energy-efficient solutions that empower our customers to meet their technological and operational objectives.

In addition to our semiconductor products, Selzenqua offers a suite of services that enhance the value of our technology offerings. These services include design support, customization, and technical consulting, which are intended to help our customers optimize the integration and performance of our products within their systems.

Reportable Segments

Selzenqua's operations are organized into three reportable segments: Consumer Electronics, Automotive and Industrial, and Communications Infrastructure.

The Consumer Electronics segment focuses on providing semiconductor solutions for devices such as smartphones, tablets, and wearables. Our products in this segment are designed to deliver superior performance and power efficiency, addressing the growing demand for advanced functionality and longer battery life in consumer devices.

The Automotive and Industrial segment caters to the specific needs of the automotive and industrial sectors, offering solutions that enable applications such as advanced driver-assistance systems (ADAS), electric vehicle powertrains, and industrial automation. Our products in this segment are engineered to meet the rigorous reliability and safety standards required by these industries.

The Communications Infrastructure segment provides semiconductor solutions for network equipment and data centers. Our offerings in this segment are designed to support the increasing data throughput and connectivity demands of modern communication networks, enabling faster and more reliable data transmission.

Customers

Selzenqua serves a diverse customer base, including original equipment manufacturers (OEMs), contract manufacturers, and system integrators across various industries. Our customers range from global multinational corporations to small and medium-sized enterprises, reflecting the broad applicability and versatility of our semiconductor solutions.

Sales and Marketing

Selzenqua's sales and marketing strategy is focused on developing strong, long-term relationships with our customers and partners. We utilize a combination of direct sales and distribution channels, which allows us to efficiently reach a wide range of customers worldwide. Our global sales force is supported by regional offices and representatives, ensuring that we can provide localized support and expertise to our customers.

We also engage in joint development programs with key customers to tailor our products to specific applications, thereby enhancing customer satisfaction and loyalty. Additionally, we participate in industry trade shows and conferences to showcase our latest innovations and engage with potential partners and customers.

Competition

The semiconductor industry is highly competitive, characterized by rapid technological advancements and evolving customer requirements. Selzenqua faces competition from both established semiconductor companies and emerging players. Key competitive factors include product performance, power efficiency, price, design capabilities, and customer support.

Selzenqua differentiates itself through our commitment to innovation, quality, and customer-centric solutions. Our continuous investment in research and development enables us to maintain a competitive edge by delivering cutting-edge technology that meets the evolving needs of our customers.

Seasonality

Our business is subject to seasonal fluctuations, with variations in demand driven by factors such as consumer spending patterns, product launch cycles, and inventory management by our customers. Historically, demand for our products tends to be higher in the second half of the year, coinciding with the holiday season and new product introductions in the consumer electronics market.

Intellectual Property

Selzenqua's success is strongly tied to our ability to innovate and protect our intellectual property (IP). We have a robust portfolio of patents, trade secrets, and proprietary technologies that safeguard our innovations and provide us with a competitive advantage. Our IP strategy involves proactive patent filing, strategic licensing agreements, and vigilant enforcement of our IP rights to prevent unauthorized use by competitors.

Government Regulation

As a participant in the global semiconductor industry, Selzenqua is subject to a wide range of governmental regulations, including export control laws, environmental regulations, and labor laws. Compliance with these regulations is critical to our operations, and we have established comprehensive policies and procedures to ensure adherence to applicable laws and standards. We continuously monitor regulatory developments and engage with industry associations to stay informed of changes that may impact our business.

Human Capital

Our workforce, comprising approximately 50,700 employees, is a vital asset to Selzenqua's success. We are committed to fostering a diverse and inclusive work environment that supports innovation, collaboration, and professional growth. Our human capital strategies focus on attracting, developing, and retaining top talent through competitive compensation, comprehensive training programs, and career development opportunities.

Available Information

Selzenqua is committed to providing transparency and accessibility of information to our stakeholders. We regularly update our corporate website with the latest news, financial reports, and regulatory filings, including our annual report on Form 10-K. Stakeholders can access these resources to stay informed about our business operations, financial performance, and strategic initiatives. Additionally, we maintain open channels of communication with our investors, analysts, and the media to ensure that all parties have access to timely and accurate information regarding our company.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our operations and financial results are subject to various risks and uncertainties, including those described below. You should consider carefully the risks and uncertainties described below, in addition to other information contained in this Annual Report on Form 10-K, including our consolidated financial statements and related notes. The risks and uncertainties described below are not the only ones we face. Additional risks and uncertainties that we are unaware of, or that we currently believe are not material, may also become important factors that adversely affect our business. If any of the following risks or others not specified below materialize, our business, financial condition, and results of operations could be materially and adversely affected.

Risks Related to Our Industry

Highly Competitive Industry Exposes Us to Market Share Loss

The semiconductor industry is intensely competitive and characterized by rapid technological change, frequent new product introductions, and price erosion. Our ability to compete successfully depends on factors both within and outside our control, including the performance, quality, and price of our products, as well as the success of our new product development efforts. Competitors with larger financial resources may be able to offer lower prices, better customer support, or more comprehensive solutions, which could lead to a loss of market share and adversely affect our financial performance.

Rapid Technological Changes May Render Our Products Obsolete

Technological advancement in the semiconductor industry is rapid, and the pace of innovation continues to accelerate. Our success depends on our ability to develop new products and technologies and to enhance existing products to meet evolving customer demands and technological advancements. Failure to anticipate or respond to technological changes could result in decreased demand for our products, reduced revenue, and a loss of market share.

Cyclicality in the Semiconductor Industry Could Impact Our Financial Results

The semiconductor industry is subject to cyclical fluctuations, which can result in significant variations in demand for our products. Factors such as changes in consumer preferences, inventory corrections, and economic downturns can lead to reduced demand and excess capacity. These cyclical trends could adversely affect our sales and profitability, and prolonged downturns could impact our ability to invest in research and development or expand our operations.

Risks Related to Our Operations

Dependence on Key Customers and Concentration of Revenue

A significant portion of our revenue is derived from a limited number of key customers. The loss of, or a significant reduction in orders from, one or more of these key customers could materially impact our financial results. Additionally, any adverse developments affecting these customers, such as financial difficulties or shifts in market strategy, could reduce our sales and negatively affect our business.

Supply Chain Disruptions Could Hinder Our Production Capabilities

Our operations rely on a complex global supply chain, including the procurement of raw materials and components from various suppliers. Disruptions to our supply chain due to geopolitical tensions, natural disasters, pandemics, or other factors could delay production schedules, increase costs, and limit our ability to meet customer demand, adversely affecting our financial performance.

Manufacturing Challenges and Capacity Constraints

As a semiconductor manufacturer, we face challenges related to maintaining efficient production processes and managing capacity constraints. If we are unable to effectively manage manufacturing operations or expand capacity in line with demand, we may experience production inefficiencies, increased costs, and delayed product deliveries, all of which could negatively impact our financial results.

Cybersecurity Threats and Data Breaches

Our operations rely on complex information technology systems that are vulnerable to cybersecurity threats, including data breaches, hacking, and other forms of cyber-attacks. A successful cyber-attack could result in the unauthorized access, theft, or destruction of critical data, potentially leading to financial losses, reputational damage, and legal liability.

Risks Related to Financing and Capital Structure

Interest Rate Fluctuations Could Impact Our Financial Obligations

Our financial condition and results of operations may be affected by fluctuations in interest rates, as changes in rates can influence our borrowing costs and investment returns. An increase in interest rates could result in higher interest expenses, affecting our profitability and cash flow.

Leverage and Indebtedness May Restrict Our Operational Flexibility

We have substantial indebtedness, which could limit our ability to pursue strategic initiatives, such as investments, acquisitions, or other growth opportunities. High levels of debt may also require us to dedicate a significant portion of our cash flow to service interest and principal payments, reducing funds available for other operational needs.

Dependence on Access to Capital Markets for Financing Needs

Our ability to finance operations, pursue strategic initiatives, and meet financial obligations is dependent on access to capital markets. Adverse market conditions, changes in investor sentiment, or a deterioration of our financial condition could limit our access to capital, impairing our ability to achieve growth objectives or meet financial commitments.

Risks Related to Legal and Regulatory Matters

Compliance with Government Regulations and Trade Policies

As a participant in the global semiconductor industry, we are subject to numerous governmental regulations, including export control laws, environmental regulations, and trade policies. Changes in these regulations, or our failure to comply, could result in increased costs, penalties, or restrictions on our operations, adversely impacting our business.

Intellectual Property Infringement and Litigation

Our success depends on our ability to protect our intellectual property (IP) and avoid infringing on the IP rights of others. IP litigation can be costly and time-consuming, and an adverse ruling could require us to pay damages, cease product offerings, or enter into licensing agreements, all of which could negatively impact our financial position.

Environmental Regulations Could Increase Costs

Compliance with environmental regulations is critical to our operations, and changes in these regulations could increase our compliance costs and liabilities. Failure to adhere to environmental laws could result in fines, penalties, or operational restrictions, adversely affecting our business and reputation.

General Risks

Global Economic Conditions May Impact Our Business

Our operations and financial performance are influenced by global economic conditions, including changes in economic growth, inflation, and currency exchange rates. Economic downturns or instability in key markets could reduce consumer and business spending, negatively affecting demand for our products and services.

Natural Disasters and Pandemics Could Disrupt Operations

Our operations are susceptible to disruptions from natural disasters, pandemics, and other catastrophic events. Such events could interrupt production, supply chains, and distribution, leading to delays, increased costs, and reduced revenue. The ongoing impact of the COVID-19 pandemic exemplifies the potential for significant disruptions to our business operations.

Retention and Recruitment of Key Personnel

Our success is heavily dependent on our ability to attract, retain, and motivate highly skilled employees, including executives, engineers, and other technical personnel. Competition for talent in our industry is intense, and the inability to retain key personnel or attract qualified new employees could hinder our product development, operational efficiency, and overall business performance.

Fluctuations in Foreign Currency Exchange Rates

As we operate in multiple countries, our financial results can be significantly impacted by fluctuations in foreign currency exchange rates. Adverse changes in exchange rates could affect our reported revenues, expenses, and profitability, making it challenging to accurately predict future financial performance.

Changes in Consumer Preferences and Demand

Our business is influenced by changes in consumer preferences and demand for electronic devices and technology solutions. Rapid shifts in consumer behavior could affect the demand for our products, potentially leading to inventory excesses, price reductions, and decreased revenue. Adjusting to these changes requires agility and accurate market forecasting, which can be challenging in a rapidly evolving industry.

In conclusion, while we strive to mitigate these risks through strategic planning, operational excellence, and a focus on innovation, the inherent uncertainties in our business environment may still have a substantial impact on our financial condition, results of operations, and future prospects. We continue to monitor these risks closely and adapt our strategies to navigate potential challenges effectively.

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Overview

The following discussion and analysis of Selzenqua Semiconductor, Inc.'s ("Selzenqua" or the "Company") financial condition and results of operations should be read in conjunction with the Company's Consolidated Financial Statements and related notes included elsewhere in this Annual Report on Form 10-K. In fiscal 2024, Selzenqua experienced significant growth, with revenue reaching $16,983 million, representing a 13.7% increase from the prior fiscal year. Our strategic focus on innovation, customer-centric solutions, and operational efficiency has positioned us well in the competitive semiconductor market.

Results of Operations

Revenue

Revenue for fiscal 2024 was $16,983 million, an increase of $2,040 million, or 13.7%, from $14,943 million in fiscal 2023. This growth was largely driven by increased sales in our Consumer Electronics and Communications Infrastructure segments. The Consumer Electronics segment benefited from the growing demand for advanced semiconductor solutions in smartphones and wearables, supported by new product launches and enhanced customer engagements. In the Communications Infrastructure segment, the ongoing expansion of data centers and network upgrades contributed significantly to revenue growth.

Cost of Revenue and Gross Margin

Cost of revenue for fiscal 2024 was $10,825 million, compared to $9,375 million in fiscal 2023, representing an increase of $1,450 million. This increase was primarily due to higher production volumes to meet increased demand, as well as inflationary pressures on raw materials and logistics. Despite these cost increases, Selzenqua maintained a strong focus on operational efficiencies and supply chain optimization.

Gross profit for fiscal 2024 was $6,158 million, compared to $5,568 million in fiscal 2023, reflecting an increase of $590 million. Research and Development

Research and development (R&D) expenses were $4,353 million in fiscal 2024, up from $3,801 million in fiscal 2023, representing an increase of $552 million. Selling, General and Administrative

Selling, general, and administrative (SG&A) expenses were $509 million in fiscal 2024, compared to $455 million in fiscal 2023, an increase of $54 million. This increase was primarily due to expanded sales and marketing efforts to support revenue growth, as well as investments in administrative infrastructure to enhance operational efficiency. Despite the increase in absolute terms, SG&A expenses as a percentage of revenue remained stable at 3.0% for both fiscal years, reflecting our focus on cost management and efficiency.

Other Operating Expense

In fiscal 2024, there were no other operating expenses, compared to $98 million in fiscal 2023. The absence of other operating expenses in fiscal 2024 is attributable to the non-recurrence of certain restructuring costs and one-time charges incurred in the prior year, which were associated with strategic realignment and optimization initiatives.

Operating Income

Operating income for fiscal 2024 was $1,296 million, up from $1,214 million in fiscal 2023, representing an increase of $82 million or 6.8%. The increase in operating income was primarily driven by revenue growth and effective cost management, partially offset by increased R&D and SG&A expenses. Our operating margin decreased slightly to 7.6% from 8.1% in the prior fiscal year, reflecting the impact of increased operating expenses.

Interest Expense

Interest expense for fiscal 2024 was $455 million, compared to $406 million in fiscal 2023, an increase of $49 million. This increase was primarily due to higher interest rates and increased borrowings to support strategic investments and capital expenditures. We continue to actively manage our debt portfolio to optimize interest costs and financial flexibility.

Effective Tax Rate

Our effective tax rate for fiscal 2024 was 22.9%, compared to 24.0% in fiscal 2023. The decrease in the effective tax rate was primarily due to changes in the geographic mix of earnings and the utilization of available tax credits. We remain focused on optimizing our global tax structure to support operational efficiency and enhance shareholder value.

Net Income

Net income for fiscal 2024 was $648 million, compared to $614 million in fiscal 2023, representing an increase of $34 million or 5.5%. The increase in net income reflects the strong operational performance and improved tax efficiency, partially offset by higher interest expenses and increased operating costs. Our net margin decreased slightly to 3.8% from 4.1% in the prior fiscal year, primarily due to increased interest expenses and operating costs.

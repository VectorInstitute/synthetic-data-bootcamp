Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Overview

The following discussion and analysis of Selzenqua Semiconductor, Inc.'s ("Selzenqua" or the "Company") financial condition and results of operations should be read in conjunction with the Company's Consolidated Financial Statements and related notes included elsewhere in this Annual Report on Form 10-K. This discussion contains forward-looking statements based on our current expectations, estimates, and projections about our business and industry, and actual results may differ materially from those expressed or implied by these forward-looking statements due to various factors, including those discussed under "Item 1A. Risk Factors."

In fiscal 2024, Selzenqua experienced significant growth, with revenue reaching $16,983 million, representing a 13.7% increase from the prior fiscal year. This growth was driven by strong demand across our reportable segments, particularly in the Consumer Electronics and Communications Infrastructure sectors. Our strategic focus on innovation, customer-centric solutions, and operational efficiency has positioned us well in the competitive semiconductor market.

Results of Operations

Revenue

Revenue for fiscal 2024 was $16,983 million, an increase of $2,040 million, or 13.7%, from $14,943 million in fiscal 2023. This growth was largely driven by increased sales in our Consumer Electronics and Communications Infrastructure segments. The Consumer Electronics segment benefited from the growing demand for advanced semiconductor solutions in smartphones and wearables, supported by new product launches and enhanced customer engagements. In the Communications Infrastructure segment, the ongoing expansion of data centers and network upgrades contributed significantly to revenue growth.

Cost of Revenue and Gross Margin

Cost of revenue for fiscal 2024 was $10,825 million, compared to $9,375 million in fiscal 2023, representing an increase of $1,450 million. This increase was primarily due to higher production volumes to meet increased demand, as well as inflationary pressures on raw materials and logistics. Despite these cost increases, Selzenqua maintained a strong focus on operational efficiencies and supply chain optimization.

Gross profit for fiscal 2024 was $6,158 million, compared to $5,568 million in fiscal 2023, reflecting an increase of $590 million. Our gross margin decreased to 36.3% from 37.3% in the prior fiscal year. The decrease in gross margin was primarily attributable to the aforementioned cost pressures, partially offset by improved product mix and pricing strategies in key segments.

Operating Expenses

Research and Development

Research and development (R&D) expenses were $4,353 million in fiscal 2024, up from $3,801 million in fiscal 2023, representing an increase of $552 million. This increase reflects our continued investment in innovation and technology leadership, focusing on the development of next-generation semiconductor technologies and solutions. R&D expenses as a percentage of revenue increased slightly to 25.6% in fiscal 2024, compared to 25.4% in fiscal 2023, underscoring our commitment to advancing our product offerings and maintaining a competitive edge in the market.

Selling, General and Administrative

Selling, general, and administrative (SG&A) expenses were $509 million in fiscal 2024, compared to $455 million in fiscal 2023, an increase of $54 million. This increase was primarily due to expanded sales and marketing efforts to support revenue growth, as well as investments in administrative infrastructure to enhance operational efficiency. Despite the increase in absolute terms, SG&A expenses as a percentage of revenue remained stable at 3.0% for both fiscal years, reflecting our focus on cost management and efficiency.

Other Operating Expense

In fiscal 2024, there were no other operating expenses, compared to $98 million in fiscal 2023. The absence of other operating expenses in fiscal 2024 is attributable to the non-recurrence of certain restructuring costs and one-time charges incurred in the prior year, which were associated with strategic realignment and optimization initiatives.

Operating Income

Operating income for fiscal 2024 was $1,296 million, up from $1,214 million in fiscal 2023, representing an increase of $82 million or 6.8%. The increase in operating income was primarily driven by revenue growth and effective cost management, partially offset by increased R&D and SG&A expenses. Our operating margin decreased slightly to 7.6% from 8.1% in the prior fiscal year, reflecting the impact of increased operating expenses.

Interest Expense

Interest expense for fiscal 2024 was $455 million, compared to $406 million in fiscal 2023, an increase of $49 million. This increase was primarily due to higher interest rates and increased borrowings to support strategic investments and capital expenditures. We continue to actively manage our debt portfolio to optimize interest costs and financial flexibility.

Effective Tax Rate

Our effective tax rate for fiscal 2024 was 22.9%, compared to 24.0% in fiscal 2023. The decrease in the effective tax rate was primarily due to changes in the geographic mix of earnings and the utilization of available tax credits. We remain focused on optimizing our global tax structure to support operational efficiency and enhance shareholder value.

Net Income

Net income for fiscal 2024 was $648 million, compared to $614 million in fiscal 2023, representing an increase of $34 million or 5.5%. The increase in net income reflects the strong operational performance and improved tax efficiency, partially offset by higher interest expenses and increased operating costs. Our net margin decreased slightly to 3.8% from 4.1% in the prior fiscal year, primarily due to increased interest expenses and operating costs.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

Cash and Cash Equivalents

As of December 31, 2024, cash and cash equivalents were $2,211 million, compared to $2,023 million as of December 31, 2023. The increase in cash and cash equivalents reflects the net impact of our operating, investing, and financing activities during the fiscal year.

Operating Activities

Net cash provided by operating activities was $1,113 million in fiscal 2024. This reflects our strong operational performance and effective working capital management, despite increased costs and investments in R&D. Key drivers of operating cash flow included net income of $648 million and non-cash adjustments such as depreciation and amortization totaling $912 million, partially offset by changes in working capital and other items amounting to $(447) million.

Investing Activities

Net cash used in investing activities was $(2,228) million in fiscal 2024. Capital expenditures for property and equipment purchases totaled $(795) million, reflecting our continued investment in manufacturing capacity and technology infrastructure. Other investing activities, primarily related to strategic acquisitions and investments, resulted in a net cash outflow of $(1,433) million. These investments are aligned with our long-term growth strategy and are expected to enhance our competitive position in the market.

Financing Activities

Net cash provided by financing activities was $1,303 million in fiscal 2024. During the year, we raised $1,088 million through debt financing to support strategic initiatives and capital investments. We also returned $78 million to shareholders through dividends and utilized $293 million for net share repurchases, reflecting our commitment to enhancing shareholder value while maintaining financial flexibility.

Capital Expenditures

Capital expenditures totaled $(795) million in fiscal 2024, reflecting our ongoing investments in expanding manufacturing capacity and upgrading technology infrastructure. These expenditures are critical to supporting our growth trajectory and ensuring the efficient production of high-quality semiconductor solutions.

Debt

Our long-term debt increased to $10,108 million as of December 31, 2024, from $9,020 million as of December 31, 2023. The increase in debt levels was primarily due to additional borrowings to support strategic investments and capital expenditures. We remain committed to maintaining a balanced capital structure and optimizing our debt portfolio to ensure financial flexibility.

Dividends and Share Repurchases

In fiscal 2024, we paid dividends totaling $78 million, reflecting our commitment to returning capital to shareholders. Additionally, we conducted net share repurchases amounting to $293 million, optimizing our capital structure and enhancing shareholder value. These actions are part of our broader capital allocation strategy, which seeks to balance growth investments with shareholder returns.

Critical Accounting Estimates

Our consolidated financial statements are prepared in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles in the United States (GAAP), which require us to make estimates and assumptions that affect the reported amounts. Critical accounting estimates are those that involve a high degree of judgment and uncertainty, and could potentially result in materially different outcomes under different assumptions or conditions.

Revenue Recognition

Our revenue recognition policy requires judgment in determining the timing and amount of revenue to be recognized, particularly with respect to the identification of performance obligations, determination of transaction prices, and assessment of collectability. Changes in customer contracts, sales incentives, or market conditions could impact the amount and timing of revenue recognition.

Inventory Valuation

We regularly assess the valuation of our inventory to ensure it is stated at the lower of cost or net realizable value. This involves estimating the future demand for our products, considering factors such as technological changes, market conditions, and customer preferences. Changes in these estimates could result in inventory write-downs, affecting our financial results.

Goodwill and Intangible Assets

We assess the carrying value of goodwill and intangible assets for impairment annually, or more frequently if indicators of impairment arise. This requires judgment in estimating future cash flows, growth rates, and discount rates. Changes in market conditions or business performance could result in impairment charges, affecting our financial position and results of operations.

Income Taxes

Our income tax provisions involve significant judgment in determining the recognition and measurement of tax positions, including the assessment of uncertain tax positions and the valuation of deferred tax assets. Changes in tax laws, regulations, or interpretations, as well as changes in our geographic mix of earnings, could impact our effective tax rate and tax liabilities.

In conclusion, Selzenqua's fiscal 2024 performance reflects robust revenue growth and strong operational execution, despite facing challenges such as increased costs and competitive pressures. Our commitment to innovation, customer-centric solutions, and strategic investments positions us well for future growth. We remain focused on optimizing our capital structure, enhancing shareholder value, and navigating the dynamic semiconductor industry landscape.

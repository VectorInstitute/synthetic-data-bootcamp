PART I

Item 1. Business

Selzenqua Semiconductor, Inc. (hereinafter referred to as "Selzenqua" or the "Company"), a Delaware corporation with headquarters in Princeton, New Jersey, operates within the Information Technology sector, focusing primarily on semiconductor technologies. As of the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, Selzenqua reported total revenue of $16,983 million. The Company employs approximately 50,700 individuals and remains committed to advancing technology and innovation through its diverse product offerings and strategic business segments.

Overview

Selzenqua is a leading innovator in the semiconductor industry, developing and manufacturing high-performance semiconductor products and solutions. Our mission is to enable the digital transformation of industries through our cutting-edge technology and comprehensive product offerings. We provide a wide range of products that are critical to the functioning of various electronic devices, thereby playing a crucial role in the global technology ecosystem.

Principal Products and Services

Our principal product offerings include microprocessors, system-on-chip (SoC) solutions, and application-specific integrated circuits (ASICs). These products are designed to address the diverse needs of our customers across different sectors, including consumer electronics, automotive, telecommunications, and industrial applications. Selzenqua is dedicated to delivering high-performance, energy-efficient solutions that empower our customers to meet their technological and operational objectives.

In addition to our semiconductor products, Selzenqua offers a suite of services that enhance the value of our technology offerings. These services include design support, customization, and technical consulting, which are intended to help our customers optimize the integration and performance of our products within their systems.

Reportable Segments

Selzenqua's operations are organized into three reportable segments: Consumer Electronics, Automotive and Industrial, and Communications Infrastructure.

The Consumer Electronics segment focuses on providing semiconductor solutions for devices such as smartphones, tablets, and wearables. Our products in this segment are designed to deliver superior performance and power efficiency, addressing the growing demand for advanced functionality and longer battery life in consumer devices.

The Automotive and Industrial segment caters to the specific needs of the automotive and industrial sectors, offering solutions that enable applications such as advanced driver-assistance systems (ADAS), electric vehicle powertrains, and industrial automation. Our products in this segment are engineered to meet the rigorous reliability and safety standards required by these industries.

The Communications Infrastructure segment provides semiconductor solutions for network equipment and data centers. Our offerings in this segment are designed to support the increasing data throughput and connectivity demands of modern communication networks, enabling faster and more reliable data transmission.

Customers

Selzenqua serves a diverse customer base, including original equipment manufacturers (OEMs), contract manufacturers, and system integrators across various industries. Our customers range from global multinational corporations to small and medium-sized enterprises, reflecting the broad applicability and versatility of our semiconductor solutions.

Sales and Marketing

Selzenqua's sales and marketing strategy is focused on developing strong, long-term relationships with our customers and partners. We utilize a combination of direct sales and distribution channels, which allows us to efficiently reach a wide range of customers worldwide. Our global sales force is supported by regional offices and representatives, ensuring that we can provide localized support and expertise to our customers.

We also engage in joint development programs with key customers to tailor our products to specific applications, thereby enhancing customer satisfaction and loyalty. Additionally, we participate in industry trade shows and conferences to showcase our latest innovations and engage with potential partners and customers.

Competition

The semiconductor industry is highly competitive, characterized by rapid technological advancements and evolving customer requirements. Selzenqua faces competition from both established semiconductor companies and emerging players. Key competitive factors include product performance, power efficiency, price, design capabilities, and customer support.

Selzenqua differentiates itself through our commitment to innovation, quality, and customer-centric solutions. Our continuous investment in research and development enables us to maintain a competitive edge by delivering cutting-edge technology that meets the evolving needs of our customers.

Seasonality

Our business is subject to seasonal fluctuations, with variations in demand driven by factors such as consumer spending patterns, product launch cycles, and inventory management by our customers. Historically, demand for our products tends to be higher in the second half of the year, coinciding with the holiday season and new product introductions in the consumer electronics market.

Intellectual Property

Selzenqua's success is strongly tied to our ability to innovate and protect our intellectual property (IP). We have a robust portfolio of patents, trade secrets, and proprietary technologies that safeguard our innovations and provide us with a competitive advantage. Our IP strategy involves proactive patent filing, strategic licensing agreements, and vigilant enforcement of our IP rights to prevent unauthorized use by competitors.

Government Regulation

As a participant in the global semiconductor industry, Selzenqua is subject to a wide range of governmental regulations, including export control laws, environmental regulations, and labor laws. Compliance with these regulations is critical to our operations, and we have established comprehensive policies and procedures to ensure adherence to applicable laws and standards. We continuously monitor regulatory developments and engage with industry associations to stay informed of changes that may impact our business.

Human Capital

Our workforce, comprising approximately 50,700 employees, is a vital asset to Selzenqua's success. We are committed to fostering a diverse and inclusive work environment that supports innovation, collaboration, and professional growth. Our human capital strategies focus on attracting, developing, and retaining top talent through competitive compensation, comprehensive training programs, and career development opportunities.

Available Information

Selzenqua is committed to providing transparency and accessibility of information to our stakeholders. We regularly update our corporate website with the latest news, financial reports, and regulatory filings, including our annual report on Form 10-K. Stakeholders can access these resources to stay informed about our business operations, financial performance, and strategic initiatives. Additionally, we maintain open channels of communication with our investors, analysts, and the media to ensure that all parties have access to timely and accurate information regarding our company.

Our success depends on our ability to develop new products and technologies and to enhance existing products to meet evolving customer demands and technological advancements. The semiconductor industry is subject to cyclical fluctuations, which can result in significant variations in demand for our products. Item 1C. Cybersecurity

RISK MANAGEMENT AND STRATEGY

Selzenqua Semiconductor, Inc. ("Selzenqua" or the "Company") recognizes the paramount importance of cybersecurity in safeguarding sensitive and proprietary data critical to our operations and stakeholder trust. In alignment with this priority, we have established a comprehensive cybersecurity program designed to protect our information systems and data assets. This program incorporates a multi-layered approach, integrating administrative, technical, and physical controls to mitigate cybersecurity threats and ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of our information systems.

Our cybersecurity strategy is built on a cross-functional framework that emphasizes proactive threat prevention, timely incident response, and continuous improvement. This strategy is aimed at minimizing the risk of cyber incidents that could adversely impact our business operations, reputation, and financial condition. To achieve these objectives, we employ a range of processes and controls.

The cybersecurity program is spearheaded by designated information security personnel led by our Chief Information Security Officer (CISO). Our CISO brings extensive experience in cybersecurity leadership and information technology, ensuring that our security posture remains robust and adaptive to evolving threats. The CISO is supported by a dedicated team responsible for implementing and maintaining our cybersecurity initiatives.

Central to our cybersecurity efforts are risk assessments, which are conducted regularly to identify and prioritize potential cybersecurity risks. Our risk assessment process involves the evaluation of internal and external threats, leveraging proprietary and third-party threat intelligence. This process allows us to stay informed of emerging threats and align our defenses accordingly. We incorporate industry standards and best practices to guide our assessment and response strategies.

To enhance our cybersecurity posture, we engage external service providers and consultants who offer specialized expertise and conduct independent assessments of our security controls. These third-party assessments provide valuable insights into our cybersecurity maturity and areas for improvement. We also collaborate with industry partners and participate in information-sharing initiatives to keep abreast of the latest cybersecurity trends and threat landscapes.

Our cybersecurity program includes a vulnerability management component, ensuring that potential vulnerabilities in our systems are promptly identified, assessed, and mitigated. We prioritize vulnerability remediation based on risk impact and employ automated tools for continuous monitoring and patch management.

Incident response is a critical element of our cybersecurity strategy. Selzenqua has established a comprehensive incident response plan, supplemented by scenario-specific playbooks to address various types of cyber incidents. These playbooks outline the procedures for identifying, containing, and mitigating incidents, as well as communication protocols to ensure timely reporting to relevant stakeholders.

Additionally, we have developed business continuity and disaster recovery plans to support rapid recovery in the event of a significant cybersecurity incident or other disruption. These plans are regularly tested and updated to reflect changes in our operational environment and threat landscape.

GOVERNANCE

Governance of Selzenqua's cybersecurity program is provided by the Board of Directors and its Audit and Risk Committee, which exercise oversight of the Company's cybersecurity risk management efforts. The Audit and Risk Committee receives regular updates from the CISO on the status of our cybersecurity initiatives, including key metrics, threat intelligence, and incident response activities.

The CISO maintains a direct reporting line to the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) and provides monthly briefings that cover cybersecurity trends, security events, and ongoing initiatives to enhance our cyber defenses. These briefings ensure that the CEO and executive leadership team remain informed of the cybersecurity landscape and are engaged in strategic decision-making related to risk management.

The full Board of Directors provides ultimate oversight of Selzenqua's cybersecurity strategy and ensures that it aligns with the Company's broader risk management framework. The Board's oversight role includes reviewing the effectiveness of our cybersecurity controls and ensuring that appropriate resources are allocated to support our security objectives.

As part of our governance structure, we have implemented a security awareness and training program for employees and contractors. This program is designed to promote a culture of security, providing role-based training tailored to specific job functions and responsibilities. Our training initiatives aim to enhance the cybersecurity acumen of our workforce and foster vigilance against potential threats.

In addition to internal governance, we have established a third-party risk management framework to address cybersecurity risks associated with our suppliers and partners. This framework involves conducting due diligence on third parties' cybersecurity capabilities and implementing contractual security requirements to safeguard our data and systems. Ongoing monitoring of third-party compliance is conducted to ensure that our security standards are consistently met.

In conclusion, Selzenqua remains steadfast in its commitment to maintaining a robust cybersecurity program that protects our operations and stakeholders from evolving cyber threats. Through comprehensive risk management practices, governance oversight, and a culture of continuous improvement, we strive to uphold the highest standards of cybersecurity excellence.

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Overview

The following discussion and analysis of Selzenqua Semiconductor, Inc.'s ("Selzenqua" or the "Company") financial condition and results of operations should be read in conjunction with the Company's Consolidated Financial Statements and related notes included elsewhere in this Annual Report on Form 10-K. This discussion contains forward-looking statements based on our current expectations, estimates, and projections about our business and industry, and actual results may differ materially from those expressed or implied by these forward-looking statements due to various factors, including those discussed under "Item 1A. Risk Factors."

In fiscal 2024, Selzenqua experienced significant growth, with revenue reaching $16,983 million, representing a 13.7% increase from the prior fiscal year. This growth was driven by strong demand across our reportable segments, particularly in the Consumer Electronics and Communications Infrastructure sectors. Our strategic focus on innovation, customer-centric solutions, and operational efficiency has positioned us well in the competitive semiconductor market.

Results of Operations

Revenue

Revenue for fiscal 2024 was $16,983 million, an increase of $2,040 million, or 13.7%, from $14,943 million in fiscal 2023. This growth was largely driven by increased sales in our Consumer Electronics and Communications Infrastructure segments. The Consumer Electronics segment benefited from the growing demand for advanced semiconductor solutions in smartphones and wearables, supported by new product launches and enhanced customer engagements. In the Communications Infrastructure segment, the ongoing expansion of data centers and network upgrades contributed significantly to revenue growth.

Cost of Revenue and Gross Margin

Cost of revenue for fiscal 2024 was $10,825 million, compared to $9,375 million in fiscal 2023, representing an increase of $1,450 million. This increase was primarily due to higher production volumes to meet increased demand, as well as inflationary pressures on raw materials and logistics. Despite these cost increases, Selzenqua maintained a strong focus on operational efficiencies and supply chain optimization.

Gross profit for fiscal 2024 was $6,158 million, compared to $5,568 million in fiscal 2023, reflecting an increase of $590 million. Our gross margin decreased to 36.3% from 37.3% in the prior fiscal year. The decrease in gross margin was primarily attributable to the aforementioned cost pressures, partially offset by improved product mix and pricing strategies in key segments.

Operating Expenses

Research and Development

Research and development (R&D) expenses were $4,353 million in fiscal 2024, up from $3,801 million in fiscal 2023, representing an increase of $552 million. This increase reflects our continued investment in innovation and technology leadership, focusing on the development of next-generation semiconductor technologies and solutions. R&D expenses as a percentage of revenue increased slightly to 25.6% in fiscal 2024, compared to 25.4% in fiscal 2023, underscoring our commitment to advancing our product offerings and maintaining a competitive edge in the market.

Selling, General and Administrative

Selling, general, and administrative (SG&A) expenses were $509 million in fiscal 2024, compared to $455 million in fiscal 2023, an increase of $54 million. This increase was primarily due to expanded sales and marketing efforts to support revenue growth, as well as investments in administrative infrastructure to enhance operational efficiency. Despite the increase in absolute terms, SG&A expenses as a percentage of revenue remained stable at 3.0% for both fiscal years, reflecting our focus on cost management and efficiency.

Other Operating Expense

In fiscal 2024, there were no other operating expenses, compared to $98 million in fiscal 2023. The absence of other operating expenses in fiscal 2024 is attributable to the non-recurrence of certain restructuring costs and one-time charges incurred in the prior year, which were associated with strategic realignment and optimization initiatives.

Operating Income

Operating income for fiscal 2024 was $1,296 million, up from $1,214 million in fiscal 2023, representing an increase of $82 million or 6.8%. The increase in operating income was primarily driven by revenue growth and effective cost management, partially offset by increased R&D and SG&A expenses. Our operating margin decreased slightly to 7.6% from 8.1% in the prior fiscal year, reflecting the impact of increased operating expenses.

Interest Expense

Interest expense for fiscal 2024 was $455 million, compared to $406 million in fiscal 2023, an increase of $49 million. This increase was primarily due to higher interest rates and increased borrowings to support strategic investments and capital expenditures. We continue to actively manage our debt portfolio to optimize interest costs and financial flexibility.

Effective Tax Rate

Our effective tax rate for fiscal 2024 was 22.9%, compared to 24.0% in fiscal 2023. The decrease in the effective tax rate was primarily due to changes in the geographic mix of earnings and the utilization of available tax credits. We remain focused on optimizing our global tax structure to support operational efficiency and enhance shareholder value.

Net Income

Net income for fiscal 2024 was $648 million, compared to $614 million in fiscal 2023, representing an increase of $34 million or 5.5%. The increase in net income reflects the strong operational performance and improved tax efficiency, partially offset by higher interest expenses and increased operating costs. Our net margin decreased slightly to 3.8% from 4.1% in the prior fiscal year, primarily due to increased interest expenses and operating costs.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

Cash and Cash Equivalents

As of December 31, 2024, cash and cash equivalents were $2,211 million, compared to $2,023 million as of December 31, 2023. The increase in cash and cash equivalents reflects the net impact of our operating, investing, and financing activities during the fiscal year.

Operating Activities

Net cash provided by operating activities was $1,113 million in fiscal 2024. This reflects our strong operational performance and effective working capital management, despite increased costs and investments in R&D. Key drivers of operating cash flow included net income of $648 million and non-cash adjustments such as depreciation and amortization totaling $912 million, partially offset by changes in working capital and other items amounting to $(447) million.

Investing Activities

Net cash used in investing activities was $(2,228) million in fiscal 2024. Capital expenditures for property and equipment purchases totaled $(795) million, reflecting our continued investment in manufacturing capacity and technology infrastructure. Other investing activities, primarily related to strategic acquisitions and investments, resulted in a net cash outflow of $(1,433) million. These investments are aligned with our long-term growth strategy and are expected to enhance our competitive position in the market.

Financing Activities

Net cash provided by financing activities was $1,303 million in fiscal 2024. During the year, we raised $1,088 million through debt financing to support strategic initiatives and capital investments. We also returned $78 million to shareholders through dividends and utilized $293 million for net share repurchases, reflecting our commitment to enhancing shareholder value while maintaining financial flexibility.

Capital Expenditures

Capital expenditures totaled $(795) million in fiscal 2024, reflecting our ongoing investments in expanding manufacturing capacity and upgrading technology infrastructure. These expenditures are critical to supporting our growth trajectory and ensuring the efficient production of high-quality semiconductor solutions.

Debt

Our long-term debt increased to $10,108 million as of December 31, 2024, from $9,020 million as of December 31, 2023. The increase in debt levels was primarily due to additional borrowings to support strategic investments and capital expenditures. We remain committed to maintaining a balanced capital structure and optimizing our debt portfolio to ensure financial flexibility.

Dividends and Share Repurchases

In fiscal 2024, we paid dividends totaling $78 million, reflecting our commitment to returning capital to shareholders. Additionally, we conducted net share repurchases amounting to $293 million, optimizing our capital structure and enhancing shareholder value. These actions are part of our broader capital allocation strategy, which seeks to balance growth investments with shareholder returns.

Item 1. Business

Overview

Drazennov Medical, Inc. is a global leader in medical technology, services, and solutions. We are dedicated to improving the lives of patients by providing a broad range of therapies and diagnostic tools that address chronic diseases and acute medical conditions. Incorporated in the State of Delaware and headquartered in Minneapolis, Minnesota, we operate at the intersection of clinical excellence and engineering innovation. Our mission is to alleviate pain, restore health, and extend life for millions of people across the globe.

In the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, we generated total revenue of $60,579 million. Our global operations are supported by a diverse workforce and a sophisticated supply chain that enables us to serve healthcare providers, hospitals, and patients in numerous countries. We maintain a significant commitment to research and development, which allows us to sustain a robust pipeline of new products and enhancements to our existing portfolio. Our business model is built on long-term relationships with clinical professionals and a reputation for quality and reliability in the most demanding medical environments.

Principal Products and Services

Our product portfolio is comprised of advanced medical devices and integrated health solutions. We offer technologies used in the treatment of cardiovascular disease, neurological disorders, spinal conditions, and a wide array of surgical procedures. Our principal products include implantable cardiac pacemakers and defibrillators, heart valves, stents, neurostimulators, spinal implants, and robotic-assisted surgical systems.

In addition to hardware, we provide digital health services and data analytics platforms that assist clinicians in monitoring patient health remotely. These services integrate data from our implanted devices to provide actionable insights, potentially improving clinical outcomes and reducing the total cost of care for healthcare systems. We also offer specialized surgical instruments and consumable products used in operating rooms and intensive care units worldwide.

Reportable Segments

We manage our operations through three primary reportable segments: the Cardiovascular Portfolio, the Neuroscience Portfolio, and the Medical Surgical Portfolio. Each segment focuses on specific therapeutic areas and utilizes dedicated research, development, and commercial teams to address the unique needs of its patient populations.

Cardiovascular Portfolio

The Cardiovascular Portfolio is our largest segment by revenue and encompasses therapies for the heart and vascular system. This segment includes our Cardiac Rhythm Management business, which develops and manufactures implantable devices to treat bradycardia, tachycardia, and heart failure. We also offer technologies for the treatment of coronary artery disease and peripheral vascular disease, including drug-eluting stents and balloon catheters.

A key component of this portfolio is our Structural Heart and Aortic business. This area focuses on minimally invasive treatments for heart valve disease, such as transcatheter aortic valve replacement systems. By providing less invasive alternatives to traditional open-heart surgery, we enable faster recovery times and improved quality of life for patients. Furthermore, our Cardiac Ablation and Electrophysiology products assist physicians in diagnosing and treating heart rhythm disorders through advanced mapping and catheter-based therapies.

Neuroscience Portfolio

The Neuroscience Portfolio focuses on the brain, spine, and central nervous system. This segment provides a comprehensive range of products for the treatment of chronic pain, movement disorders, and spinal conditions. Our Neuromodulation business offers implantable neurostimulation systems and drug delivery pumps that provide relief for patients suffering from chronic pain or spasticity.

Our Cranial and Spinal Technologies business provides integrated solutions for complex spinal surgeries, including implants, biologics, and navigation systems. We are a leader in providing robotic-assisted platforms for spinal procedures, which enhance the precision of implant placement and improve surgical workflows. Additionally, this segment includes our Neurovascular business, which provides products for the treatment of stroke and brain aneurysms, and our Specialty Therapies business, which addresses conditions in the ear, nose, and throat.

Medical Surgical Portfolio

The Medical Surgical Portfolio focuses on general surgical procedures and patient monitoring. This segment provides advanced surgical tools, including energy-based instruments for tissue sealing and cutting, as well as mechanical stapling devices used in gastrointestinal and thoracic surgeries. Our focus in this segment is on enabling minimally invasive surgery, which reduces trauma and hospital stays for patients.

Within this portfolio, we also operate a Patient Monitoring and Respiratory Interventions business. This unit provides vital sign monitors, pulse oximetry sensors, and ventilators used in critical care settings. We also offer products for airway management and anesthesia delivery. This segment serves a broad base of customers across nearly all departments of the hospital, from the emergency room to the intensive care unit.

Customers, Sales and Marketing

Our primary customers include hospitals, clinics, third-party healthcare providers, and government healthcare agencies. We also sell certain products to distributors who then resell to smaller medical facilities or specific geographic regions. Because our products often require specialized clinical knowledge for proper use, our sales and marketing efforts are highly technical and clinical in nature.

We employ a direct sales force in our major markets, consisting of highly trained sales representatives and clinical specialists. These individuals work closely with physicians, surgeons, and hospital administrators to provide product education, technical support during procedures, and post-operative follow-up. In many cases, our clinical specialists are present in the operating room to assist the surgical team with the technical aspects of our devices.

In addition to direct sales, we engage in marketing activities aimed at raising awareness of specific disease states and the therapies available to treat them. We participate in major medical congresses and sponsor clinical education programs for healthcare professionals. Our marketing strategy emphasizes the clinical efficacy, safety, and economic value of our products, supported by peer-reviewed data and real-world evidence.

Competition

The medical technology industry is characterized by intense competition and rapid technological change. We compete with large, diversified medical device companies as well as smaller, specialized firms that focus on specific therapeutic areas. Competition is based on several factors, including product performance, clinical outcomes, ease of use, price, and the quality of technical support provided to clinicians.

Our competitors frequently introduce new products and improvements to existing technologies, which can lead to rapid product obsolescence. To maintain our competitive position, we must continually invest in research and development and successfully navigate the global regulatory landscape. We also face competition from alternative treatments, such as pharmaceutical therapies or lifestyle interventions, which may reduce the demand for our medical devices. In some markets, we also encounter competition from local manufacturers who may benefit from lower cost structures or preferential government procurement policies.

Seasonality

Our business is subject to moderate seasonal fluctuations. Typically, we experience higher sales volumes in the fourth quarter of our fiscal year, as hospitals and healthcare providers utilize their remaining annual budgets. Conversely, we often see lower procedure volumes during summer months in the Northern Hemisphere and during major holiday periods, as elective surgeries are frequently deferred. Furthermore, certain respiratory products may experience higher demand during the winter months due to the increased prevalence of seasonal illnesses.

Intellectual Property

Intellectual property is a critical component of our business strategy. We rely on a combination of patents, trademarks, copyrights, and trade secrets to protect our innovations and maintain our competitive advantage. We own or have rights to a vast portfolio of patents worldwide, covering the design, manufacture, and use of our medical devices and software.

We actively pursue patent protection for our new inventions and improvements to existing products. The duration of patent protection varies by jurisdiction but generally lasts for several decades from the date of filing. We also license technology from third parties, including academic institutions and other medical technology companies, to supplement our internal research efforts.

We vigorously defend our intellectual property rights and may engage in litigation to prevent infringement by competitors. Conversely, we are occasionally subject to claims that our products infringe the intellectual property of others. The medical technology sector is prone to frequent patent litigation, and the outcome of such disputes can have a significant impact on our ability to market certain products.

Government Regulation

Our products and operations are subject to extensive and rigorous regulation by government authorities globally. In the United States, the Food and Drug Administration (FDA) regulates the development, testing, manufacturing, labeling, and distribution of medical devices. Most of our products require either a 510(k) clearance or a Pre-Market Approval (PMA) from the FDA before they can be marketed. The PMA process, in particular, is lengthy and requires significant clinical data to demonstrate the safety and effectiveness of the device.

Outside the United States, we are subject to various regulatory frameworks, such as the Medical Device Regulation (MDR) in the European Union. These regulations require us to maintain comprehensive quality management systems and undergo regular audits by notified bodies. In many emerging markets, regulatory requirements are becoming increasingly complex and aligned with international standards.

Our manufacturing facilities are subject to periodic inspections by regulatory agencies to ensure compliance with Quality System Regulations and Good Manufacturing Practices. Failure to comply with these regulations can result in warning letters, fines, product recalls, or the suspension of manufacturing operations. We also must comply with regulations governing the privacy and security of patient data, such as the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) in the United States and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) in Europe.

Furthermore, our interactions with healthcare professionals are subject to anti-kickback and fraud and abuse laws. We maintain a robust compliance program designed to ensure that our sales and marketing practices adhere to these legal requirements and ethical standards.

Human Capital

Our success is driven by the talent, dedication, and expertise of our employees. As of December 31, 2024, we employed about 78,900 people worldwide. Our workforce includes scientists, engineers, clinical researchers, manufacturing specialists, and sales professionals, many of whom hold advanced degrees in their respective fields.

We are committed to fostering an inclusive and diverse work environment where all employees feel valued and empowered to contribute. We believe that a diverse workforce enhances innovation and allows us to better understand the needs of the diverse patient populations we serve. We invest in employee development through various training programs, leadership initiatives, and educational assistance.

Employee safety and well-being are paramount. We maintain rigorous safety standards across our manufacturing and research facilities and provide comprehensive benefits packages designed to support the physical and mental health of our colleagues. We also conduct regular employee engagement surveys to gather feedback and identify areas for improvement in our corporate culture and operational practices.

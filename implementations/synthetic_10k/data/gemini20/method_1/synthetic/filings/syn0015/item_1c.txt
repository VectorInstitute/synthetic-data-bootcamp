Item 1C. Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity Strategy and Risk Management

We have implemented a comprehensive cybersecurity program designed to protect the integrity, confidentiality, and availability of our information technology systems, our intellectual property, and the sensitive data of our patients and customers. Given the nature of our Patient Management segment, which utilizes cloud-based platforms and wearable sensors to collect personal health information, our cybersecurity strategy is a fundamental component of our enterprise risk management framework. Our approach is designed to address a variety of threats, including unauthorized access, ransomware, and other malicious activities that could disrupt our manufacturing processes or compromise patient privacy.

Our security model is built upon a defense-in-depth framework that utilizes multiple layers of technical and administrative controls to prevent, detect, and mitigate cyber threats. These controls include:

- Advanced endpoint protection and continuous network monitoring.
- Robust identity and access management, including mandatory multifactor authentication for all employees and contractors.
- Data encryption protocols for information at rest and in transit, particularly for patient data transmitted from our remote monitoring devices.
- Regular vulnerability assessments and penetration testing conducted by internal teams and external security specialists.
- Comprehensive security awareness training for our global workforce to mitigate risks associated with social engineering and phishing.

We align our cybersecurity policies and procedures with recognized industry standards, primarily the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) Cybersecurity Framework. We also leverage the International Organization for Standardization (ISO) 27001 standards to guide our information security management system. In support of our Diagnostic Systems and Patient Management segments, we maintain rigorous compliance with data protection regulations, including HIPAA in the United States and GDPR in Europe. Our program is evaluated annually by independent third parties who perform control assessments to verify the effectiveness of our security measures.

A critical element of our strategy is the management of third-party risks. We rely on a global network of suppliers for specialized components and cloud infrastructure. We conduct security reviews of our critical vendors and require them to adhere to our security standards through contractual obligations. These contracts typically include the right to audit the vendor’s security environment and require timely notification of any security incidents that could impact our data or operations.

We maintain a formal Incident Response Plan that outlines the procedures for responding to and recovering from potential cybersecurity events. Our dedicated Incident Response Team conducts regular tabletop exercises involving cross-functional leaders to ensure readiness for various attack scenarios. As of the date of this report, we are not aware of any cybersecurity incidents that have materially affected our business strategy, results of operations, or financial condition. However, we recognize that the threat landscape is constantly evolving, and despite our extensive controls, we cannot guarantee that a significant breach will not occur in the future.

Cybersecurity Governance

Our Board of Directors maintains ultimate oversight of our cybersecurity and privacy risks. The Board executes this responsibility primarily through the Audit Committee, which is charged with monitoring our information security posture and the effectiveness of our risk mitigation strategies. The Audit Committee receives regular briefings from our Global Chief Information Security Officer (GCISO) regarding the current threat environment, the status of ongoing security initiatives, and the results of external audits. These briefings occur at least quarterly and include discussions on emerging risks, such as those related to the integration of artificial intelligence in our digital health platforms.

Our cybersecurity program is led by our GCISO, who reports to our Chief Information Officer and maintains a direct communication line to the Audit Committee. Our current GCISO has extensive experience in information security, having held leadership roles at other large organizations in highly regulated sectors prior to joining the Company. This individual possesses advanced degrees in computer science and maintains several industry-recognized professional certifications.

The GCISO is supported by a specialized team of security professionals responsible for day-to-day operations, including threat hunting and incident management. Our executive leadership team is also involved in cybersecurity governance through an internal risk committee that reviews significant security investments and ensures that our cybersecurity strategy remains aligned with our broader clinical and commercial objectives. This governance structure ensures that cybersecurity remains a priority at all levels of the organization.

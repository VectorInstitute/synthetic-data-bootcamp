Item 1. Business

General

Overview

Torsel Software, Inc. (the Company, we, us, or our) is a global leader in the information technology sector, specializing in the development, licensing, and support of advanced software applications and cloud-based services. Since our incorporation in Delaware, we have established a significant presence in the enterprise software market, providing critical infrastructure and application solutions that enable organizations to manage complex business processes, analyze vast quantities of data, and transition to modern cloud environments. Our headquarters are located in Princeton, New Jersey.

We are dedicated to driving innovation through substantial investment in research and development, aiming to provide our customers with scalable, secure, and integrated software suites. Our portfolio addresses a wide range of functional areas, including enterprise resource planning, customer relationship management, supply chain management, and advanced data analytics. By leveraging a hybrid delivery model that encompasses both on-premise installations and cloud-based deployments, we serve a diverse global customer base across various industries, including financial services, healthcare, manufacturing, and the public sector.

For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, our total revenue was $24,213 million. This performance reflects our continued expansion in high-growth software markets and the increasing adoption of our subscription-based service offerings.

Business Segments

We manage our operations through two primary reportable segments: Enterprise Software Solutions and Cloud Services and Platforms. These segments are organized based on the nature of the products and services provided, the underlying technology platforms, and the prevailing delivery models.

Enterprise Software Solutions

The Enterprise Software Solutions segment focuses on the development and licensing of our core software applications designed for on-premise or hybrid environments. This segment provides comprehensive tools for large-scale organizations to manage their internal operations. Our offerings include integrated suites for financial management, human capital management, and operational logistics. These products are typically characterized by long-term license agreements and recurring maintenance and support contracts. 

The value proposition of this segment lies in its ability to provide deep functional expertise and high levels of customization for clients with complex regulatory or security requirements that necessitate on-site data management. We continue to see demand in this segment from large-scale legacy enterprises that are undergoing phased digital transformations, utilizing our software as the stable foundation for their hybrid IT architectures.

Cloud Services and Platforms

The Cloud Services and Platforms segment encompasses our software-as-a-service (SaaS), platform-as-a-service (PaaS), and infrastructure-as-a-service (IaaS) offerings. This segment is the primary driver of our digital transformation strategy, providing customers with flexible, scalable, and consumption-based access to our most advanced technologies. Our cloud services allow clients to deploy applications rapidly without the need for significant upfront capital investment in hardware.

Within this segment, we provide cloud-native applications for customer engagement, digital marketing, and collaborative work management. Additionally, our platform services enable developers to build, test, and deploy new applications using our proprietary tools and application programming interfaces. The growth of this segment is fueled by the global trend toward cloud migration and the increasing demand for real-time data accessibility and mobile-first business applications.

Item 1C. Cybersecurity

Risk Management and Strategy

Torsel Software, Inc. recognizes that as a global provider of enterprise software and cloud-based services, the security of our information technology systems and the protection of the data entrusted to us by our customers, employees, and partners are fundamental to our business operations and reputation. We have developed and implemented a comprehensive cybersecurity risk management program designed to identify, assess, and manage material risks from cybersecurity threats. This program is an integral part of our broader Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) framework, ensuring that cybersecurity risks are evaluated alongside other operational, financial, and strategic risks facing the Company.

Our cybersecurity strategy is built upon a defense-in-depth architecture, which employs multiple layers of security controls to protect our intellectual property and sensitive data. This approach is designed to prevent, detect, and respond to a wide array of threats, including unauthorized access, malware, ransomware, and social engineering attempts. Our identification and assessment processes include regular vulnerability scanning, penetration testing of our software products and internal infrastructure, and continuous monitoring of our network environment for suspicious activity.

A critical component of our risk management process is the integration of security into the software development lifecycle. Given our focus on both on-premise and cloud-based solutions, we employ secure coding practices and conduct rigorous security reviews before new software releases or updates are deployed. This proactive approach aims to mitigate risks associated with software vulnerabilities that could be exploited by external actors.

We also recognize the risks associated with our global supply chain and third-party service providers. As part of our risk management strategy, we conduct security assessments of our key suppliers, particularly those who have access to our internal systems or who provide critical infrastructure for our Cloud Services and Platforms segment. These assessments are designed to ensure that our partners maintain security standards that are consistent with our own internal policies and regulatory requirements. We utilize a combination of third-party audits, security questionnaires, and contractual obligations to manage these external risks.

To further validate the effectiveness of our security program, we engage independent third-party experts to conduct periodic assessments of our cybersecurity posture. These engagements provide an objective perspective on our security controls and help us identify areas for continuous improvement. We also track industry trends and emerging threat intelligence to adapt our strategy to the evolving landscape of cybercrime and state-sponsored activity.

Employee awareness and training are also central to our cybersecurity strategy. All employees are required to complete annual cybersecurity training, which covers topics such as data privacy, password management, and the identification of phishing attempts. We also conduct periodic simulation exercises to test the readiness of our workforce and reinforce secure behaviors. For personnel with direct responsibility for our security infrastructure or incident response, we provide specialized training and professional development opportunities to ensure they remain proficient in the latest security technologies and methodologies.

In the event of a security incident, the Company maintains a formal cybersecurity incident response plan. This plan outlines the procedures for escalating, investigating, and remediating security breaches, as well as the protocols for internal and external communications. We conduct regular tabletop exercises involving senior management and technical teams to ensure that our response capabilities are robust and that all stakeholders understand their roles during a crisis.

Governance

The Board of Directors (the Board) maintains ultimate oversight of the Company’s risk management environment, including risks arising from cybersecurity threats. The Board has delegated primary responsibility for the oversight of cybersecurity and information security to the Audit Committee.

The Audit Committee is responsible for reviewing the Company’s cybersecurity risk exposure and the steps management has taken to monitor and control such risks. This includes regular briefings from management on the status of our security programs, the results of internal and external audits, and updates on the evolving regulatory environment related to data privacy and protection. The Audit Committee reports regularly to the full Board on these matters, ensuring that cybersecurity remains a priority at the highest levels of corporate governance.

At the management level, our cybersecurity program is led by our Chief Information Security Officer (CISO). Our CISO has extensive experience in information technology and security leadership, having served in similar roles for large-scale enterprise organizations for several decades. The CISO is responsible for the design, implementation, and enforcement of our global information security policies and for managing the dedicated teams that handle security operations, identity management, and incident response.

The CISO reports directly to the Chief Information Officer and provides regular updates to the Audit Committee and the Board. These reports typically occur on a quarterly basis and cover a range of topics, including the results of recent risk assessments, progress on key security initiatives, and an analysis of the current threat environment. The CISO also participates in the Company’s ERM committee, ensuring that cybersecurity considerations are integrated into the broader corporate strategy and capital allocation decisions.

Our management-level cybersecurity governance is further supported by a cross-functional security steering committee. This committee includes representatives from legal, human resources, finance, and our core business segments. The committee meets regularly to discuss emerging risks, review security incidents, and ensure that our security policies are aligned with our business objectives and compliance obligations. This collaborative approach ensures that cybersecurity is viewed not merely as a technical issue, but as a shared responsibility across all functional areas of Torsel Software, Inc.

Cybersecurity Threats and Incidents

As a global technology company, we are frequently subject to cybersecurity threats and attempted breaches of our systems. These threats are persistent and vary in their sophistication and frequency. While we have experienced various security incidents in the normal course of our business, to date, no such incident has been determined to be material to our overall business strategy, results of operations, or financial condition.

We continue to monitor our systems and the broader threat landscape closely. We recognize that despite our significant investments in security personnel and technology, the risk of a material breach remains. We are committed to the continuous improvement of our security posture and to maintaining the trust of our customers through the transparent management of cybersecurity risks.

Torsel Software, Inc. is a global leader in the information technology sector, primarily focused on the development and delivery of enterprise-grade software solutions and cloud-based platforms. Our mission is to provide the infrastructure and application layers that enable modern organizations to navigate digital transformation, manage complex data ecosystems, and optimize business processes. Headquartered in Princeton, New Jersey, we operate on a global scale, serving a diverse customer base that includes major financial institutions, healthcare providers, and government entities.

During the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, we experienced significant growth across our core business segments. Our total revenue increased to $24,213 million, representing revenue growth of 13.0% compared to the prior fiscal year. This growth was primarily driven by the continued adoption of our Cloud Services and Platforms segment, as more customers transitioned from traditional on-premise licensing to our subscription-based software-as-a-service (SaaS) and platform-as-a-service (PaaS) offerings. Our net income for fiscal year 2024 reached $3,501 million, reflecting net income growth of 12.4% over fiscal year 2023.

Our operational strategy remains focused on innovation and market expansion. We continue to invest heavily in research and development to maintain our competitive edge in emerging technologies such as advanced data analytics and cybersecurity. Additionally, we have maintained a disciplined approach to capital allocation, prioritizing investments in our cloud infrastructure while returning significant value to stockholders through share repurchases.

Results of Operations

The following table and discussion provide a comparison of our results of operations for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, and the fiscal year ended December 31, 2023.

Revenue

Total revenue was $24,213 million for fiscal year 2024, compared to $21,422 million for fiscal year 2023. This represents an increase of $2,791 million, or revenue growth of 13.0%.

The increase in revenue was attributable to strong performance in both our Enterprise Software Solutions and Cloud Services and Platforms segments. The Cloud Services and Platforms segment was the primary contributor to this growth, driven by an increase in new customer acquisitions and the expansion of existing subscription contracts. As organizations continue to prioritize digital resilience and remote accessibility, demand for our cloud-native applications for customer experience and collaborative work management remained robust. Furthermore, our Enterprise Software Solutions segment benefited from a steady rate of renewals for mission-critical core applications, as well as increased demand for hybrid deployment models that integrate on-premise stability with cloud scalability. The growth in revenue also reflects our success in expanding our footprint within the healthcare and financial services verticals, where regulatory requirements drive the need for our secure and compliant software suites.

Cost of Revenue and Gross Margin

Cost of revenue was $13,334 million for fiscal year 2024, compared to $11,813 million for fiscal year 2023. The increase in cost of revenue of $1,521 million was primarily driven by higher expenses associated with our cloud infrastructure. These expenses include increased fees paid to third-party data center providers, higher depreciation on our proprietary server hardware, and increased personnel costs related to the maintenance and support of our global service delivery networks. Additionally, as our revenue grew, we experienced an increase in amortization of acquired intangible assets and higher royalty payments to third-party technology providers whose components are integrated into our software suites.

Gross profit for fiscal year 2024 was $10,879 million, compared to $9,609 million for fiscal year 2023. Our gross margin for fiscal year 2024 was 44.9%, which was consistent with the gross margin of 44.9% reported for fiscal year 2023. The stability in our gross margin reflects a balance between the increasing scale and efficiency of our cloud operations and the rising costs of infrastructure and technical support. While the shift toward cloud-based revenue typically involves higher variable costs than traditional software licensing, we have been able to offset these costs through improved utilization of our data center resources and the optimization of our global support organization, which serves our workforce of about 28,000 employees.

Research and Development

Research and development (R&D) expenses were $2,620 million for fiscal year 2024, compared to $2,375 million for fiscal year 2023. Research and development as a share of revenue was 10.8% in fiscal year 2024, compared to 11.1% in fiscal year 2023.

The increase in R&D spending of $245 million reflects our continued commitment to innovation. During fiscal year 2024, we focused our investment on several key areas, including the integration of machine learning capabilities across our customer experience suites and the enhancement of our cybersecurity solutions to address increasingly sophisticated threats. We also increased our headcount in software engineering and data science to accelerate the development cycle of our cloud-native platforms. The slight decrease in R&D as a percentage of revenue demonstrates our ability to scale our development efforts more efficiently as our total revenue base expands.

Selling, General and Administrative

Selling, general and administrative (SG&A) expenses were $2,673 million for fiscal year 2024, compared to $2,303 million for fiscal year 2023. Selling, general and administrative as a share of revenue was 11.0% in fiscal year 2024, compared to 10.8% in fiscal year 2023.

The increase in SG&A expenses of $370 million was primarily driven by higher sales commissions resulting from our 13.0% revenue growth, as well as increased marketing expenditures. In fiscal year 2024, we expanded our global branding campaigns and increased our participation in major industry trade shows and our annual user conference. Furthermore, general and administrative costs increased due to higher professional service fees related to legal and compliance matters, as well as investments in our internal information technology systems to support our growing remote workforce.

Other Operating Expense

Other operating expense was $1,453 million for fiscal year 2024, compared to $1,285 million for fiscal year 2023. This increase of $168 million was primarily due to higher amortization of certain intangible assets and charges related to the integration of recently acquired businesses. We also incurred costs associated with the optimization of our global office footprint as we continued to adapt to a hybrid work model for our employees.

Operating Income and Margin

Operating income was $4,133 million for fiscal year 2024, compared to $3,646 million for fiscal year 2023. This represents an increase of $487 million, or operating income growth of 13.4%. Our operating margin for fiscal year 2024 was 17.1%, compared to 17.0% in fiscal year 2023.

The improvement in operating income and the slight increase in operating margin were driven by our strong revenue growth and our ability to maintain a stable gross margin of 44.9%. Despite the increases in SG&A and R&D spending in absolute dollar terms, our overall operating efficiency improved as we leveraged our global scale and maintained disciplined control over our discretionary spending.

Interest Expense

Interest expense was $190 million for fiscal year 2024, compared to $167 million for fiscal year 2023. The increase of $23 million was primarily due to a higher average balance of long-term debt during the year. Our long-term debt increased to $4,222 million as of December 31, 2024, compared to $3,721 million at the end of the prior fiscal year, reflecting a net change in long-term debt of $501 million.

Provision for Income Taxes

The provision for income taxes was $442 million for fiscal year 2024, compared to $364 million for fiscal year 2023. Our effective tax rate for fiscal year 2024 was 11.2%, compared to 10.5% for fiscal year 2023. The increase in the effective tax rate was primarily due to changes in the geographic mix of our earnings and a decrease in the relative impact of certain tax credits related to our research and development activities in international jurisdictions.

Net Income

Net income was $3,501 million for fiscal year 2024, compared to $3,115 million for fiscal year 2023. This represents an increase of $386 million, or net income growth of 12.4%. Our net margin for fiscal year 2024 was 14.5%, which was consistent with the net margin of 14.5% reported for fiscal year 2023. The growth in net income reflects our strong operational performance and the successful execution of our growth strategy in the cloud software market.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

Our primary sources of liquidity are cash generated from operations and access to debt markets. As of December 31, 2024, we held cash and cash equivalents of $1,154 million, an increase of $194 million from $960 million at the end of fiscal year 2023. We believe that our existing cash balances, combined with our ability to generate strong operating cash flows, will be sufficient to meet our anticipated capital requirements, debt service obligations, and share repurchase activities for at least the next twelve months.

Cash Flows from Operating Activities

Net cash provided by operating activities was $4,022 million for fiscal year 2024. This was primarily driven by our net income of $3,501 million, adjusted for non-cash items including depreciation and amortization of $867 million. These inflows were partially offset by a decrease of $346 million from changes in working capital and other items. The change in working capital was primarily influenced by an increase in accounts receivable, net, which rose to $4,671 million from $4,187 million, reflecting the timing of billings and collections associated with our 13.0% revenue growth. Additionally, inventories increased to $1,608 million from $1,433 million as we maintained higher levels of physical components to support our enterprise hardware-software integrated solutions.

Cash Flows from Investing Activities

Net cash used in investing activities was $1,951 million for fiscal year 2024. This included $472 million for the purchase of property and equipment, which was primarily directed toward expanding our data center capacity and upgrading our internal IT infrastructure. Other investing activities, net, resulted in a use of $1,479 million in cash, which primarily related to the acquisition of several specialized software firms to enhance our technology portfolio and expand our market presence in specific industry verticals. These acquisitions contributed to the increase in our goodwill, which reached $1,951 million as of December 31, 2024.

Cash Flows from Financing Activities

Net cash used in financing activities was $1,877 million for fiscal year 2024. This was primarily driven by $2,378 million used for share repurchases, net of issuances, as part of our ongoing program to return capital to our stockholders. These outflows were partially offset by a net change in long-term debt of $501 million, as we increased our borrowings to support our strategic initiatives and maintain a flexible capital structure. We did not pay any dividends during fiscal year 2024.

Capital Resources and Debt

Our total liabilities as of December 31, 2024, were $21,199 million, compared to $18,003 million at the end of the prior fiscal year. This increase was driven by the rise in long-term debt to $4,222 million and an increase in other non-current liabilities to $9,179 million. Our total stockholders' equity was $8,097 million at the end of fiscal year 2024, up from $6,974 million in 2023. We maintain a strong balance sheet with total assets of $29,296 million, providing us with the financial flexibility to pursue our long-term growth objectives.

Critical Accounting Estimates

The preparation of our consolidated financial statements requires us to make estimates and assumptions that affect the reported amounts of assets, liabilities, revenue, and expenses. We base our estimates on historical experience and on various other assumptions that we believe to be reasonable under the circumstances. Actual results may differ from these estimates. The following are the critical accounting estimates that we believe are most important to the portrayal of our financial condition and results of operations.

Revenue Recognition

We derive revenue from the sale of software licenses, subscription services, and professional services. For arrangements that include multiple performance obligations, we allocate the transaction price to each performance obligation based on its relative stand-alone selling price. Determining the stand-alone selling price requires significant judgment, especially for our newer cloud-based offerings where historical data may be limited. We also make estimates regarding the period over which subscription revenue is recognized, typically based on the term of the customer agreement.

Goodwill and Intangible Assets

We evaluate our goodwill for impairment annually, or more frequently if events or changes in circumstances indicate that the asset might be impaired. As of December 31, 2024, our goodwill was $1,951 million. The process of evaluating goodwill for impairment requires us to estimate the fair value of our reporting units, which involves significant judgment regarding future cash flows, growth rates, and discount rates. If our actual results or future expectations differ from these estimates, we may be required to record impairment charges.

Income Taxes

We are subject to income taxes in the United States and numerous foreign jurisdictions. Significant judgment is required in determining our global provision for income taxes and evaluating our tax positions. We recognize deferred tax assets and liabilities for the expected future tax consequences of events that have been included in our financial statements or tax returns. We record a valuation allowance to reduce our deferred tax assets to the amount that is more likely than not to be realized. Our effective tax rate of 11.2% in fiscal year 2024 reflects our current estimates of these factors.

Allowance for Doubtful Accounts

We maintain an allowance for doubtful accounts to reflect our estimate of losses resulting from the inability of our customers to make required payments. As of December 31, 2024, our accounts receivable, net, was $4,671 million. Our estimate is based on a variety of factors, including the age of the receivable balance, the creditworthiness of the customer, and general economic conditions. If the financial condition of our customers were to deteriorate, additional allowances might be required.

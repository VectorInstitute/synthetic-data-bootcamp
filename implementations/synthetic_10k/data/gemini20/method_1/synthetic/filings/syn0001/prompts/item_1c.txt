Risk Management and Strategy
We rely on information technology (IT) and operational technology (OT) systems, including technology from third party vendors, to manufacture and ship our products, as well as to process, transmit and store electronic information in our day-to-day operations. We have established an enterprise cybersecurity program, which is administered by a cross-functional team of cybersecurity professionals that includes employees and third party contractors and vendors, that utilizes various tools, methodologies and processes to assess, identify and manage cybersecurity risks related to our IT and OT systems, as well as our products. Our cybersecurity program is designed to monitor and continually enhance our enterprise security posture, with the goal of preventing cybersecurity incidents to the extent feasible, including assessments to better understand our readiness for cybersecurity threats and the resilience of our critical business functions, with the goal of avoiding or reducing the impact if such an event were to occur. We have implemented cybersecurity policies mapped to industry and government standards and frameworks, such as U.S. National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) and International Standard of Organization, and our strategy is aligned to the NIST CyberSecurity Framework that provides us a structured approach to managing our cybersecurity risk through its five core functions. We regularly review our cybersecurity policies and require annual cybersecurity training for our employees. We also periodically conduct simulation exercises involving employees at various levels of the organization, as well as our Board of Directors, to prepare for cybersecurity incidents and response planning.
Our product cybersecurity focus begins with our design protocols and is supported by quality testing, provider education, and packaging and distribution standards. We use penetration testing to simulate cyberattacks and better understand our exploitable weaknesses, and we monitor threat intelligence feeds, including avenues for product users to report vulnerabilities directly to us, and use scanning tools to detect and assess vulnerabilities that could affect our products. In addition, we conduct product, enterprise and vendor/third party risk assessments, vulnerability assessments and analyses to gain insights into potential vulnerabilities and their impact on critical functions, and leverage their outcomes to prioritize our security investments and balance our resource allocation.
We use third party security providers for specialized areas such as incident response, penetration testing, and on-demand cybersecurity services, including staff augmentation and consulting. Item 1. Business

Overview

Talven Therapeutics, Inc. is a diversified global healthcare leader dedicated to improving patient outcomes through the discovery, development, and delivery of innovative medical solutions. Headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia, and incorporated in the state of Delaware, we operate at the intersection of biotechnology, clinical services, and supply chain management. Our mission is to address significant unmet medical needs across a broad spectrum of therapeutic areas, including oncology, immunology, and rare diseases, while simultaneously enhancing the efficiency and accessibility of healthcare delivery systems.

In fiscal year 2024, we achieved a significant milestone in our growth trajectory, generating total revenue of $251,691 million. This performance reflects the continued expansion of our pharmaceutical portfolio and the increasing demand for our integrated health services. Our scale allows us to leverage a vast infrastructure to support the entire lifecycle of a medical product, from early-stage research and development through to global distribution and patient care management. With a workforce of approximately 852,000 employees, we are one of the largest private employers in the healthcare sector, maintaining a presence in numerous international markets.

Our business model is built upon three foundational pillars: scientific innovation, operational excellence, and patient-centric care. We believe that by integrating our pharmaceutical research capabilities with our extensive distribution networks and clinical service platforms, we can create a more seamless experience for patients and providers alike. This integrated approach is designed to mitigate the complexities of the modern healthcare environment and drive long-term value for our stockholders.

Reportable Segments

We manage our operations through three primary reportable segments: Pharmaceutical Innovation, Integrated Health Services, and Medical Supply and Distribution. These segments are organized based on the nature of the products and services provided and the customers they serve.

Pharmaceutical Innovation

The Pharmaceutical Innovation segment is responsible for the research, development, and commercialization of our proprietary medicines. This segment focuses on high-growth therapeutic areas where there is a substantial need for novel treatments. Our research and development efforts are concentrated on several key platforms, including small molecule chemistry, monoclonal antibodies, and advanced gene therapies.

In oncology, we have developed a robust pipeline of targeted therapies designed to inhibit specific pathways involved in tumor growth. Our immunology portfolio includes treatments for autoimmune disorders that offer improved efficacy and safety profiles compared to legacy therapies. Additionally, our rare disease unit focuses on orphan indications, utilizing our specialized expertise to bring life-changing medicines to small but underserved patient populations. This segment operates numerous research facilities globally and maintains a sophisticated manufacturing infrastructure to ensure the highest standards of quality and safety.

Integrated Health Services

The Integrated Health Services segment provides a comprehensive suite of clinical and administrative solutions designed to improve the quality and affordability of healthcare. This segment includes our pharmacy benefit management (PBM) operations, specialty pharmacy services, and a network of outpatient clinical sites.

Our PBM business works with plan sponsors, including employers and government agencies, to manage their prescription drug programs. We utilize data analytics and clinical protocols to drive better health outcomes while controlling costs. Our specialty pharmacy services focus on the complex needs of patients with chronic or rare conditions, providing high-touch clinical support and specialized medication handling. Furthermore, our clinical sites offer primary and urgent care services, integrating our digital health tools to provide a holistic care experience. This segment is a critical driver of our patient engagement strategy, allowing us to interact directly with consumers and gather real-world evidence to inform our broader business decisions.

Medical Supply and Distribution

The Medical Supply and Distribution segment operates one of the world's most extensive healthcare logistics networks. We provide wholesale distribution of branded and generic pharmaceuticals, over-the-counter medicines, and medical-surgical supplies to a diverse customer base.

Our logistics infrastructure is designed to ensure the secure and timely delivery of critical medical products. We utilize advanced automation and temperature-controlled storage solutions to maintain the integrity of the cold chain for sensitive biologics. Beyond distribution, this segment offers value-added services to retail pharmacies and hospital systems, including inventory management software, clinical program support, and regulatory compliance consulting. By optimizing the healthcare supply chain, we help our partners reduce waste and improve the availability of essential medicines for patients.

Principal Products and Services

Our product portfolio is diverse, spanning multiple categories of healthcare needs. Our principal pharmaceutical products include treatments for solid tumors, hematological malignancies, rheumatoid arthritis, and various metabolic disorders. We also offer a range of generic medications through our manufacturing partnerships, providing cost-effective alternatives for widely used therapies.

In addition to physical products, our service offerings are a major component of our value proposition. These include:

- Care management programs for chronic diseases such as diabetes and hypertension.
- Specialized infusion services for patients requiring complex injectable medications.
- Digital health platforms that enable remote patient monitoring and telehealth consultations.
- Comprehensive data and analytics services for healthcare providers and payers.

Customers

Our customer base is broad and varies by segment. In the Pharmaceutical Innovation segment, our primary customers are wholesale distributors, who in turn supply retail pharmacies, hospitals, and clinics. We also contract directly with government health programs and private insurers to ensure our medications are included on their formularies.

The Integrated Health Services segment serves plan sponsors, including large corporations, labor unions, and state and federal government agencies. We also serve individual patients who utilize our specialty pharmacies and clinical sites.

In the Medical Supply and Distribution segment, our customers include independent retail pharmacies, national pharmacy chains, hospital networks, long-term care facilities, and physician offices. We maintain long-standing relationships with many of these entities, often entering into multi-year supply and service agreements.

Sales and Marketing

We employ a multi-faceted sales and marketing strategy tailored to the specific needs of each segment and geographic region. Our pharmaceutical sales force is composed of highly trained professionals who engage with healthcare providers to communicate the clinical benefits and safety profiles of our products. These efforts are supported by medical science liaisons who provide deep technical expertise and facilitate scientific exchange with key opinion leaders.

In our health services and distribution segments, we utilize a business-to-business sales model. Our account management teams work closely with institutional clients to develop customized solutions that address their specific operational and financial goals. We also invest in digital marketing and consumer engagement initiatives to build brand awareness and loyalty among patients who use our clinical services. All marketing activities are conducted in strict accordance with applicable laws and regulations, including those governing the promotion of prescription drugs.

Competition

The healthcare industry is intensely competitive and subject to rapid technological change. We face competition from a variety of sources across our business segments.

In Pharmaceutical Innovation, we compete with other large, multinational pharmaceutical and biotechnology companies, as well as smaller, specialized firms. Competition is based on the ability to discover and develop novel therapies, the efficacy and safety of products, the timing of regulatory approvals, and the effectiveness of intellectual property protection. We also face competition from manufacturers of generic and biosimilar medicines, which can significantly impact the market share and pricing of our branded products upon patent expiry.

Our Integrated Health Services segment competes with other PBMs, retail pharmacy chains, and diversified healthcare service providers. Key competitive factors include the ability to negotiate favorable pricing with pharmaceutical manufacturers, the breadth and quality of clinical programs, and the efficiency of administrative platforms.

In Medical Supply and Distribution, we compete with a few large national distributors and numerous regional players. Competition in this segment is primarily based on price, reliability of delivery, and the range of value-added services offered to customers.

Seasonality

Our business experiences some degree of seasonality. In the Integrated Health Services and Medical Supply and Distribution segments, we typically see higher volumes in the fourth quarter of the calendar year. This is often driven by increased demand for respiratory and flu-related medications, as well as the tendency of patients to seek medical services and fill prescriptions before their insurance deductibles reset at the beginning of the new year. Our pharmaceutical sales can also be influenced by the timing of large wholesale orders and the launch cycles of new products.

Intellectual Property

Intellectual property is a critical asset for our Company, particularly within the Pharmaceutical Innovation segment. We rely on a combination of patents, trademarks, copyrights, and trade secrets to protect our investments in research and development.

We seek patent protection for our drug candidates, manufacturing processes, and therapeutic uses in the United States and other key international markets. These patents provide us with exclusive rights to commercialize our inventions for a limited period, which is essential for recovering our R&D costs and funding future innovation. The duration of patent protection varies by jurisdiction but generally lasts for 20 years from the date of filing. In some cases, we may be eligible for patent term extensions or regulatory exclusivity periods to compensate for time lost during the clinical trial and regulatory review process.

We also maintain an extensive portfolio of trademarks for our brand-name medicines and service offerings. We vigorously defend our intellectual property rights against infringement and challenge the validity of competing patents that we believe are unenforceable or overly broad.

Risks Related to Our Business and Industry

Our future success depends on our ability to develop and commercialize new pharmaceutical products, which is a costly and uncertain process.

The discovery and development of new medicines is highly speculative and involves a high degree of risk. We spent $13,642 million on research and development in fiscal year 2024 to support our pipeline. However, many of our drug candidates may fail to reach the market due to insufficient efficacy, safety concerns, or failure to meet regulatory requirements. Clinical trials are expensive, time-consuming, and have uncertain outcomes. Results from early-stage trials may not be predictive of results in later, larger-scale clinical trials. If we are unable to successfully bring new products to market, our ability to grow revenue and maintain our competitive position will be severely compromised.

We face intense competition from both brand-name and generic pharmaceutical companies which may result in price reductions and lost market share.

Our Pharmaceutical Innovation segment competes with large multinational companies that have substantial resources for research, marketing, and distribution. We also face competition from biotechnology firms and academic institutions. As our patents expire, we encounter significant competition from manufacturers of generic and biosimilar products. These competitors often offer products at substantially lower prices, which can lead to a rapid loss of sales for our branded medicines. If we are unable to differentiate our products through superior clinical outcomes or safety profiles, our market share and profitability may decline.

Our Integrated Health Services segment is subject to intense competition and pressure to reduce healthcare costs.

Our pharmacy benefit management and clinical service businesses operate in a highly competitive environment. We compete with other large PBMs, insurance companies, and retail pharmacy chains. Customers, including large employers and government agencies, are increasingly focused on reducing their healthcare expenditures. This pressure can lead to lower service fees, narrower margins on prescriptions, and the loss of major contracts to competitors who may offer more aggressive pricing or integrated service models. If we fail to demonstrate the value of our clinical programs or maintain competitive pricing, our results of operations in this segment could be adversely affected.

The Medical Supply and Distribution segment operates on narrow margins and is sensitive to changes in volume and product mix.

Our distribution business is characterized by high volume and low profit margins. The profitability of this segment depends on our ability to manage a complex global supply chain efficiently and to maintain high volumes of pharmaceutical and medical-surgical product sales. Changes in the mix of products sold, such as a shift from high-margin branded products to lower-margin generic products, can impact our earnings. Furthermore, the loss of one or more large customers, such as a major retail pharmacy chain or a large hospital system, could result in a significant reduction in our revenue and operating income.

Consolidation in the healthcare industry may provide our competitors or customers with greater leverage.

There has been a persistent trend toward consolidation among healthcare providers, insurers, and pharmaceutical distributors. As our customers, such as hospital systems and large pharmacy chains, become larger, they may gain increased bargaining power to negotiate lower prices for our products and services. Similarly, consolidation among our competitors may allow them to achieve greater economies of scale or offer a broader range of integrated services than we can. Such market shifts could force us to reduce our prices or increase our spending on marketing and infrastructure, both of which would negatively impact our margins.

Risks Related to Our Operations

Our global operations and massive workforce expose us to significant labor and human resources risks.

With approximately 852,000 employees, we are one of the largest private employers in the world. Our ability to maintain a stable and productive workforce is critical to our operations. We face risks related to labor shortages, wage inflation, and potential labor disputes or unionization efforts. In many of the jurisdictions where we operate, labor laws are complex and subject to change. Any significant increase in labor costs, or disruptions caused by strikes or work stoppages, could materially affect our ability to provide services to our customers and maintain our operating margins.

These processes are susceptible to contamination, equipment failure, and shortages of raw materials. Additionally, our Medical Supply and Distribution segment relies on a vast network of warehouses and transportation providers. Disruptions at key manufacturing facilities or within our logistics network due to natural disasters, geopolitical instability, or public health crises could prevent us from meeting customer demand and lead to significant lost revenue and increased costs.

A significant breach of our information technology systems or a failure to protect patient data could damage our reputation and result in substantial liabilities.

As a healthcare company, we collect, process, and store vast amounts of sensitive information, including protected health information and proprietary research data. Our systems are frequently targeted by cyberattacks, including malware, ransomware, and phishing attempts. While we invest heavily in cybersecurity, no system is entirely immune to breach. A successful attack could result in the theft of intellectual property, the disruption of our clinical and distribution operations, or the unauthorized disclosure of patient data. Such events could lead to significant legal liability, regulatory fines, and a loss of trust among patients and providers.

The integration of past and future acquisitions may be difficult and could disrupt our business.

Our growth strategy has included significant acquisitions to expand our product portfolio and service capabilities. Integrating newly acquired businesses involves numerous challenges, including the reconciliation of different corporate cultures, the consolidation of disparate IT systems, and the retention of key personnel. If we fail to integrate these businesses successfully, we may not realize the expected synergies or cost savings. Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Overview

Talven Therapeutics, Inc. is a global healthcare leader that provides a broad range of medical solutions across three primary reportable segments: Pharmaceutical Innovation, Integrated Health Services, and Medical Supply and Distribution. Our operations are designed to address unmet medical needs while improving the overall efficiency of the healthcare delivery system. We maintain a significant global presence, supported by a workforce of approximately 852,000 employees and a sophisticated infrastructure headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia.

During the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, we experienced significant growth across our enterprise. Total revenue reached $251,691 million, representing a 14.3% increase compared to the prior year. This growth was primarily driven by increased demand for our innovative pharmaceutical portfolio, the expansion of our integrated health services, and higher volumes within our medical supply and distribution network. Our net income for the year was $18,501 million, an increase of 12.2% from fiscal year 2023.

Our financial performance in 2024 reflects our commitment to balancing substantial investments in research and development with disciplined operational execution. We invested $13,642 million in research and development to advance our pipeline in oncology, immunology, and rare diseases. Simultaneously, we managed a complex global supply chain and a massive labor force to meet the needs of our diverse customer base, which includes hospital systems, retail pharmacies, and government agencies.

While we achieved record revenue, our margins were impacted by shifts in product mix and increased operating costs. Our gross margin for fiscal year 2024 was 29.2%, a slight decrease from 29.4% in the prior year. Similarly, our operating margin decreased 0.3% to 9.6% in 2024. These results highlight the competitive nature of the healthcare industry and the ongoing pressure to manage costs while delivering high-quality care.

Results of Operations

The following table and discussion provide an analysis of our results of operations for the fiscal years ended December 31, 2024 and 2023.

Revenue

Revenue for fiscal year 2024 was $251,691 million, compared to $220,202 million for fiscal year 2023, representing an increase of $31,489 million, or 14.3%. This growth was broad-based across our business segments.

In our Pharmaceutical Innovation segment, revenue growth was driven by the continued commercial success of our branded oncology and immunology treatments. Increased patient access and the launch of new indications for existing therapies contributed to higher prescription volumes. In our Integrated Health Services segment, revenue increased due to the expansion of our pharmacy benefit management (PBM) services and a higher volume of specialty pharmacy transactions. We saw an increase in the number of pharmacy claims processed as we secured new contracts with large plan sponsors and government agencies.

The Medical Supply and Distribution segment also contributed to the overall revenue increase. Higher demand for branded and generic pharmaceuticals, as well as medical-surgical supplies, drove volume growth. We benefited from our extensive logistics network, which allowed us to capture a larger share of the distribution market as hospital systems and retail pharmacies sought reliable supply chain partners.

Cost of Revenue and Gross Margin

Cost of revenue for fiscal year 2024 was $178,097 million, compared to $155,371 million in 2023. The increase in cost of revenue of $22,726 million was primarily attributable to the higher volume of products sold and services rendered.

Gross profit for 2024 was $73,594 million, compared to $64,831 million in 2023. Our gross margin was 29.2% in 2024, which represented a decrease of 0.2% compared to the 29.4% gross margin recorded in 2023. This margin compression was largely due to a shift in product mix within our Medical Supply and Distribution segment, where we saw a higher proportion of lower-margin generic products relative to branded pharmaceuticals. Additionally, increased costs related to raw materials and global logistics impacted the profitability of our Pharmaceutical Innovation segment.

Research and Development

Research and development (R&D) expenses were $13,642 million in fiscal year 2024, compared to $11,808 million in 2023. As a percentage of revenue, R&D expenses remained consistent at 5.4% for both 2024 and 2023.

The increase in R&D spending reflects our ongoing commitment to scientific innovation. During 2024, we accelerated investments in late-stage clinical trials for our oncology pipeline and expanded our research into advanced gene therapies. We also increased spending on digital health initiatives and data analytics to support our Integrated Health Services segment. These investments are critical for maintaining our long-term competitive position and ensuring a steady flow of new medical solutions to the market.

Selling, General and Administrative

Selling, general and administrative (SG&A) expenses were $35,764 million in fiscal year 2024, compared to $30,626 million in 2023. As a percentage of revenue, SG&A expenses were 14.2% in 2024, compared to 13.9% in 2023.

The increase in SG&A expenses was primarily driven by higher labor costs associated with our workforce of 852,000 employees, including wage inflation and increased benefits expenses. We also incurred higher marketing and promotional costs related to the launch of new pharmaceutical products and the expansion of our clinical service sites. Furthermore, we invested in upgrading our information technology infrastructure and cybersecurity defenses to protect our vast repositories of patient and proprietary data.

Other Operating Expense

There were no other operating expenses recorded in fiscal year 2024. In fiscal year 2023, we recorded $517 million in other operating expenses, which were primarily related to restructuring activities and the integration of previously acquired businesses.

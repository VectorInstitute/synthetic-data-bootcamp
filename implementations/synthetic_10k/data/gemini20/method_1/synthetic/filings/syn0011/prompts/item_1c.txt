Item 1. Business

Overview

Novmer Materials, Inc. is a leading global provider of high-performance materials and specialized manufacturing solutions. Incorporated in the State of Delaware and headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia, the Company serves a diverse array of end markets, including industrial manufacturing, infrastructure development, commercial construction, and aerospace. Since our inception, we have focused on the development and production of essential materials that form the foundation of modern industrial and urban environments. Our operations are characterized by a deep commitment to technical expertise, material science innovation, and large-scale manufacturing efficiency.

In the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, Novmer Materials, Inc. generated revenue of $20,255 million. Our business model is built upon a vertically integrated supply chain that allows us to manage the lifecycle of our products from raw material extraction and chemical processing to the delivery of finished high-value components. We operate a vast network of production facilities, distribution centers, and research laboratories across the globe, enabling us to meet the rigorous demands of our customers while maintaining a competitive cost structure.

As a materials science leader, we utilize advanced engineering processes to solve complex challenges for our clients. Whether through the provision of structural components for large-scale infrastructure projects or the development of specialized polymers for high-stress industrial applications, the Company remains a critical partner in the global industrial supply chain. Our strategic focus is on expanding our market share in high-growth regions and continuing to optimize our operational footprint to drive long-term value for our stockholders.

Business Segments

The Company manages its operations through three reportable segments: Industrial Materials Solutions, Infrastructure and Construction Products, and Performance Coatings and Polymers. These segments are organized based on the nature of the products provided and the specific customer markets they serve.

Industrial Materials Solutions

The Industrial Materials Solutions segment focuses on the production of high-performance alloys, specialized metal components, and advanced composite materials. These products are designed to withstand extreme temperatures, high pressure, and corrosive environments. This segment primarily serves the aerospace, defense, and energy sectors. Our engineers work closely with original equipment manufacturers to design materials that meet exacting specifications for safety and performance. The production process in this segment involves sophisticated metallurgy and precision machining, often requiring proprietary manufacturing techniques that differentiate our offerings from those of our competitors.

Infrastructure and Construction Products

The Infrastructure and Construction Products segment is our largest division by volume and provides the essential materials required for the built environment. This includes cementitious materials, aggregates, structural steel components, and prefabricated building elements. We serve both public and private sector clients, providing materials for the construction of highways, bridges, tunnels, and large-scale commercial developments. This segment benefits from our extensive network of quarries and processing plants, which are strategically located near major metropolitan areas to minimize transportation costs and ensure timely delivery to project sites. Our solutions in this segment are increasingly focused on durability and the integration of recycled materials to meet the evolving requirements of modern construction standards.

Performance Coatings and Polymers

The Performance Coatings and Polymers segment develops and manufactures specialized chemical products, including protective coatings, sealants, and high-strength polymers. These products are utilized across a wide range of industries, including automotive, marine, and general manufacturing, to protect surfaces from environmental degradation and to enhance the structural integrity of finished goods. Our polymer products are also used in the production of consumer goods and medical devices, where material purity and consistency are paramount. This segment relies heavily on our chemical engineering capabilities and our ability to formulate custom solutions that address specific environmental or functional challenges faced by our customers.

Principal Products and Services

Novmer Materials, Inc. offers a comprehensive portfolio of products categorized by their functional application and material composition.

Metals and Alloys: We produce a variety of non-ferrous alloys and specialty steel products. These are used in the manufacturing of engine components, structural frames for aircraft, and heavy machinery. Our proprietary smelting and refining processes allow us to achieve high levels of purity and specific mechanical properties required for mission-critical applications.

Cement and Aggregates: Our construction materials include Portland cement, specialized masonry cements, and various grades of crushed stone, sand, and gravel. These materials are the primary ingredients in concrete and asphalt, used extensively in infrastructure and building projects.

Specialty Chemicals and Resins: We manufacture a broad range of synthetic resins and chemical additives. These products serve as the base for adhesives, coatings, and composite materials. Our chemical portfolio is designed to provide specific attributes such as UV resistance, thermal stability, and chemical inertness.

Fabricated Components: Beyond raw materials, we provide value-added services by fabricating materials into ready-to-use components. This includes pre-cast concrete sections for bridge construction and precision-machined metal parts for industrial equipment.

Sales and Marketing

We utilize a multi-channel sales and marketing strategy to reach our diverse customer base. Our primary sales force consists of technically trained professionals who manage direct relationships with large industrial accounts and governmental agencies. These sales teams are organized by industry vertical to ensure they possess the specific technical knowledge required to consult on complex projects.

For our infrastructure and construction products, we maintain a regional sales structure that focuses on local contractors, developers, and municipal authorities. This localized approach allows us to respond quickly to regional construction trends and to manage the logistics of heavy material delivery effectively.

In addition to our direct sales force, we partner with a network of authorized distributors and wholesalers for the sale of our coatings, polymers, and standardized metal products. These partners provide us with broader market reach, particularly among smaller manufacturing firms and specialized contractors. Our marketing efforts are centered on demonstrating the technical superiority and long-term cost-effectiveness of our materials, often through case studies, technical white papers, and participation in major industry trade shows.

Customers

Our customer base is highly diversified, spanning numerous industries and geographic regions. No single customer accounts for a disproportionate share of our total revenue, which helps to mitigate the risk associated with the loss of any individual account.

Governmental Entities: We serve national, state, and local governments as a primary supplier of materials for public infrastructure projects. These contracts are often long-term in nature and are subject to rigorous bidding processes and strict compliance standards.

Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs): In the aerospace, automotive, and industrial equipment sectors, we provide materials and components that are integrated into the final products of some of the world's largest manufacturing companies. These relationships are often characterized by long-term supply agreements and joint development projects.

Construction Contractors: We supply a wide range of materials to general contractors and specialized subcontractors involved in residential, commercial, and industrial construction.

Industrial Processors: Our chemical and polymer products are sold to other manufacturers who use them as raw materials in their own production processes, such as the formulation of paints or the molding of plastic parts.

Competition

The markets for our products are highly competitive and are characterized by a large number of global and regional participants. We compete on several factors, including product quality, technical innovation, price, reliability of supply, and customer service.

In our Infrastructure and Construction Products segment, competition is primarily regional due to the high cost of transporting heavy materials. We compete with both large international building materials companies and local independent producers. Our competitive advantage in this segment stems from our strategic asset locations and our ability to provide a full suite of integrated solutions.

In the Industrial Materials Solutions and Performance Coatings and Polymers segments, competition is global. We compete with large diversified chemical and metal companies that may have significant research and development resources. In these markets, we differentiate ourselves through our specialized technical expertise and our ability to develop custom material formulations that meet the specific needs of high-tech industries.

The emergence of new materials and manufacturing technologies, such as additive manufacturing and bio-based polymers, represents both a challenge and an opportunity. We continuously monitor the competitive landscape and invest in our manufacturing capabilities to maintain our market position.

Seasonality

Our business is subject to seasonal fluctuations, particularly within the Infrastructure and Construction Products segment. Construction activity in many of our key markets, especially in the northern hemisphere, typically slows down during the winter months due to inclement weather. Consequently, our revenues and operating volumes are generally higher in the second and third quarters of the calendar year.

The Industrial Materials Solutions segment is less affected by weather-related seasonality but may be influenced by the cyclical nature of the aerospace and energy industries. Periodic maintenance shutdowns at our customers' facilities or the timing of major capital projects can also lead to fluctuations in demand. We manage these seasonal and cyclical variations through careful production planning and by maintaining a flexible workforce and supply chain.

Intellectual Property

Intellectual property is a critical component of our business strategy and a key driver of our competitive advantage. We rely on a combination of patents, trademarks, trade secrets, and confidentiality agreements to protect our proprietary technology and brands.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Many of the factors that affect our business and operations involve risk and uncertainty. The factors described below are some of the risks that could materially negatively affect our business, financial condition, results of operations and cash flows.

Industry and Market Risk Factors

Our business is highly cyclical and sensitive to downturns in the industries we serve, which could adversely affect our financial results.

The demand for our products is closely tied to the level of activity in the construction, infrastructure, aerospace, and industrial manufacturing sectors. These industries are cyclical and are significantly affected by changes in general economic conditions, such as interest rates, consumer confidence, and government spending levels. For example, our Infrastructure and Construction Products segment relies heavily on public funding for highways and bridges as well as private investment in commercial real estate. A slowdown in the U.S. or global economy that leads to a reduction in construction activity or a delay in infrastructure projects would decrease demand for our cement and structural steel products. Similarly, our Industrial Materials Solutions segment is dependent on the capital expenditure cycles of the aerospace and energy industries. If these sectors experience a prolonged downturn, our revenue and operating income would be materially and adversely affected.

The markets for our products are highly competitive, and our inability to compete effectively could result in a loss of market share.

We face intense competition from both domestic and international producers across all our business segments. In the Infrastructure and Construction Products segment, competition is often regional due to the high costs associated with transporting heavy materials like cement and aggregates. We compete with large multinational corporations as well as smaller, local independent producers who may have lower overhead costs. In our Performance Coatings and Polymers and Industrial Materials Solutions segments, we compete on a global scale against large diversified chemical and metal companies. Some of our competitors may have greater financial resources or lower production costs than we do. If we are unable to maintain our competitive position through product quality, customer service, and manufacturing efficiency, our sales volumes and pricing power could decline, leading to reduced margins.

Substitution of our products by alternative materials could reduce demand and negatively impact our results.

In many of the applications for which our products are used, customers have the option to substitute our materials with alternatives. For instance, in the construction industry, steel and concrete compete with wood, engineered composites, and recycled plastics. In the aerospace and automotive sectors, our high-performance alloys and polymers may face competition from carbon fiber or other advanced lightweight materials. If these alternative materials become more cost-effective, or if technological advancements improve their performance characteristics, our customers may shift their preferences away from our products. Such a shift could lead to a permanent reduction in demand for our core offerings and materially harm our long-term growth prospects.

Volatility in the prices of raw materials and energy could increase our cost of revenue and decrease our profitability.

Our manufacturing processes are energy-intensive and require significant quantities of raw materials, including iron ore, chemical feedstocks, and various minerals. The prices of these inputs are subject to market volatility caused by factors beyond our control, such as global supply and demand imbalances, geopolitical tensions, and changes in trade policies. Because our cost of revenue reached $15,825 million in fiscal 2024, even small percentage increases in the price of key inputs can have a substantial impact on our bottom line. We may not always be able to pass these increased costs on to our customers through higher product prices, especially in highly competitive markets or during periods of weak demand. Furthermore, our operations rely heavily on electricity and natural gas. Any significant disruption in the supply of energy or a sharp increase in utility rates could impair our ability to operate our facilities efficiently and adversely affect our results of operations.

Operational Risk Factors

We operate a large number of manufacturing facilities and any significant disruption could harm our business.

Our operations depend on the continuous and efficient functioning of our production plants, quarries, and distribution centers. These facilities are subject to risks such as equipment failure, fires, natural disasters, and industrial accidents. Because many of our products require specialized manufacturing techniques and equipment, a major disruption at one of our key facilities could result in significant production delays and the inability to meet customer orders. Moreover, the capital-intensive nature of our business means that we must invest regularly in maintenance and upgrades. If we fail to maintain our property, plant and equipment, which had a net value of $33,569 million at the end of fiscal 2024, we could experience increased downtime and higher operating costs.

The loss of key personnel or an inability to attract and retain a skilled workforce could negatively affect our operations.

Our success depends on the contributions of our approximately 65,500 employees. We rely on skilled engineers, material scientists, and technical sales professionals to develop and market our specialized products. The market for such talent is highly competitive. If we are unable to attract and retain qualified personnel, our ability to innovate and manage our complex global operations could be compromised. Additionally, a significant portion of our workforce is involved in manufacturing and logistics. Any labor shortages, work stoppages, or significant increases in labor costs could disrupt our production schedules and reduce our profitability.

Our lack of investment in research and development may result in product obsolescence and a loss of competitive advantage.

In fiscal 2024, our research and development expenses were $0 million. While we currently hold an extensive portfolio of patents and proprietary manufacturing processes, the materials science industry is characterized by rapid technological change. Our competitors may invest heavily in the development of new materials, more efficient production methods, or sustainable technologies that could render our current products or processes obsolete. Without ongoing investment in research and development, we may fail to identify emerging market trends or develop the next generation of high-performance materials. This lack of innovation could lead to a decline in our market position and make it difficult for us to compete in high-growth sectors such as advanced manufacturing and green infrastructure.

We are subject to risks associated with our global operations, including geopolitical instability and trade barriers.

We maintain a significant presence in international markets, which exposes us to risks related to varying legal, regulatory, and economic environments. Geopolitical instability, social unrest, or changes in government leadership in the regions where we operate could disrupt our supply chain or result in the expropriation of our assets. Furthermore, our international sales and imports of raw materials are subject to trade policies, including tariffs and quotas. The imposition of new trade barriers or the escalation of trade disputes could increase our costs and limit our access to key markets. OVERVIEW

Novmer Materials, Inc. is a global leader in the materials science sector, providing essential products for industrial manufacturing, infrastructure development, and specialized chemical applications. Headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia, and incorporated in Delaware, we operate a complex network of production facilities and distribution hubs supported by a workforce of about 65,500 employees. Our business is organized into three primary reportable segments: Industrial Materials Solutions, Infrastructure and Construction Products, and Performance Coatings and Polymers.

During the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, the Company navigated a complex macroeconomic environment characterized by steady demand in infrastructure sectors offset by significant inflationary pressures in raw materials and energy. Revenue for the fiscal year reached $20,255 million, representing an increase of 6.2% compared to the prior year. This growth was primarily driven by higher pricing attainment across our product lines and increased volume in our Infrastructure and Construction Products segment, which benefited from continued public and private investment in large-scale building projects.

Despite the growth in revenue, our profitability was challenged by rising operational costs and a heavy debt service burden. Operating income for fiscal 2024 was $359 million, representing a decrease of 19.9% from $448 million in fiscal 2023. The compression in operating margins was largely due to higher cost of revenue and increased selling, general and administrative expenses. Our net loss widened during the year, reaching $330 million in fiscal 2024 compared to a net loss of $204 million in the prior year. This represents a decrease in net income of 61.8%.

Our financial position remains supported by a substantial cash balance of $6,552 million as of December 31, 2024. However, our capital structure includes $15,309 million in long-term debt, which resulted in interest expense of $689 million during the fiscal year. Management remains focused on optimizing our manufacturing footprint and improving operational efficiencies to mitigate the impact of cost inflation and to return the Company to profitability.

RESULTS OF OPERATIONS

The following table and discussion provide a comparison of our results of operations for the fiscal years ended December 31, 2024 and 2023.

Revenue

Revenue for fiscal 2024 was $20,255 million, an increase of $1,175 million, or 6.2%, compared to $19,080 million in fiscal 2023. The growth in revenue was primarily attributable to the following factors:

- Pricing Actions: In response to rising input costs, the Company implemented strategic price increases across all segments. These actions contributed significantly to the year-over-year revenue growth, particularly in the Industrial Materials Solutions and Performance Coatings and Polymers segments, where specialized product formulations allow for greater pricing power.

- Volume Growth: We experienced higher demand for cement, aggregates, and structural steel within our Infrastructure and Construction Products segment. This was driven by a robust pipeline of government-funded infrastructure projects and commercial development in North America.

- Market Expansion: Continued expansion into emerging markets and high-growth industrial sectors provided additional revenue streams. Our ability to provide integrated solutions for aerospace and energy customers helped offset localized softening in general manufacturing.

Cost of Revenue and Gross Margin

Cost of revenue for fiscal 2024 was $15,825 million, an increase of $967 million, or 6.5%, compared to $14,858 million in fiscal 2023. This increase was slightly higher than our revenue growth rate, reflecting the persistent inflationary environment. Key drivers of the increase in cost of revenue included:

- Raw Material Costs: We saw significant price volatility and overall increases in the cost of iron ore, chemical feedstocks, and minerals. These materials are critical to our production processes across all three segments.

- Energy and Logistics: As an energy-intensive business, our operations were impacted by higher prices for electricity and natural gas. Additionally, the cost of transporting heavy materials to project sites increased due to higher fuel prices and freight rates.

- Labor and Manufacturing Overhead: Higher wage rates for our manufacturing workforce and increased maintenance costs at our aging production facilities also contributed to the rise in cost of revenue.

Gross profit for fiscal 2024 was $4,430 million, compared to $4,222 million in the prior year. Gross margin was 21.9% in fiscal 2024, representing a decrease from 22.1% in fiscal 2023. The slight contraction in gross margin reflects our inability to fully pass through the entirety of the cost increases to our customers in certain highly competitive regional markets.

Research and Development

Research and development expenses were $0 million for both fiscal 2024 and fiscal 2023. The Company does not currently maintain a dedicated research and development expense line as we focus our technical efforts on application engineering and process improvements that are typically captured within cost of revenue or capital expenditures. We rely on our existing portfolio of proprietary manufacturing techniques and established material formulations to maintain our competitive position.

Selling, General and Administrative Expenses

Selling, general and administrative (SG&A) expenses for fiscal 2024 were $2,856 million, an increase of $227 million, or 8.6%, compared to $2,629 million in fiscal 2023. As a percentage of revenue, SG&A expenses were 14.1% in fiscal 2024, compared to 13.8% in fiscal 2023.

The increase in SG&A expenses was primarily driven by:

- Personnel Costs: Higher compensation and benefits for our administrative and sales staff, reflecting broader labor market trends and the need to retain skilled technical professionals.

- Information Technology: Increased investment in digital infrastructure and cybersecurity measures to support our global operations and protect our intellectual property.

- Professional Fees: Higher legal and consulting fees related to regulatory compliance and strategic operational reviews aimed at improving long-term efficiency.

Other Operating Expense

Other operating expense for fiscal 2024 was $1,215 million, compared to $1,145 million in fiscal 2023, an increase of $70 million. This category primarily includes depreciation and amortization not allocated to cost of revenue, as well as costs associated with environmental remediation and asset retirement obligations. The increase was largely due to higher depreciation charges following the completion of several modernization projects at our manufacturing facilities and quarries.

Operating Income

Operating income for fiscal 2024 was $359 million, a decrease of $89 million, or 19.9%, compared to $448 million in fiscal 2023. Operating margin decreased to 1.8% in fiscal 2024 from 2.3% in fiscal 2023. The decline in operating income and margin was the result of the aforementioned increases in SG&A and other operating expenses, which outweighed the growth in gross profit.

Interest Expense

Interest expense for fiscal 2024 was $689 million, an increase of $37 million, or 5.7%, compared to $652 million in fiscal 2023. This increase was driven by a higher total debt balance, which rose to $15,309 million by the end of fiscal 2024.

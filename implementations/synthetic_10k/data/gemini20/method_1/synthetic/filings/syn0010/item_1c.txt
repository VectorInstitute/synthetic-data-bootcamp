Item 1C. Cybersecurity

Risk Management and Strategy

Kalondra Machinery, Inc. recognizes that as a global leader in industrial technology and heavy machinery, our digital infrastructure is essential to our operational success and the protection of our intellectual property. Our cybersecurity risk management program is designed to identify, assess, and manage risks from cybersecurity threats that could potentially impact our financial condition, results of operations, or reputation. This program is integrated into our broader enterprise risk management framework, ensuring that cybersecurity risks are evaluated alongside other strategic, operational, and financial risks.

Our cybersecurity strategy is built upon recognized industry standards and frameworks, including the National Institute of Standards and Technology Cybersecurity Framework. We employ a multi-layered defense strategy that encompasses our corporate offices, manufacturing facilities, and the digital products we provide to our customers. Given our significant workforce of about 126,100 employees, we place a high priority on human-centric security measures, including mandatory annual security awareness training and regular simulated phishing exercises to enhance the vigilance of our personnel.

We have established a formal process for identifying and assessing cybersecurity risks. This includes regular vulnerability assessments of our internal networks, periodic penetration testing conducted by independent third-party security firms, and continuous monitoring of our global information technology environment. We also maintain a dedicated Security Operations Center that monitors for potential threats and anomalies in real-time. Our risk assessment process extends to our manufacturing environments, where we focus on securing operational technology and industrial control systems that are critical to the production of our heavy machinery and power systems.

Recognizing the interconnected nature of the industrial supply chain, we have implemented a third-party risk management program. We conduct security assessments of our key suppliers and service providers to evaluate their cybersecurity posture. These assessments are performed during the initial onboarding process and are updated periodically based on the criticality of the supplier to our operations. We also provide cybersecurity guidance and resources to our network of independent dealers to help them protect their own environments, which are vital for the distribution and servicing of our products.

Our incident response plan provides a structured approach for responding to and recovering from potential cybersecurity incidents. This plan is reviewed and tested annually through tabletop exercises involving senior leadership and relevant functional teams. These exercises are designed to simulate various threat scenarios, such as ransomware attacks or data breaches, and to verify our ability to communicate effectively, contain the threat, and restore critical business functions. We also maintain business continuity and disaster recovery plans that are intended to minimize operational disruptions in the event of a significant system failure or security breach.

As of the end of fiscal year 2024, the Company has not experienced any cybersecurity incidents that have had a material impact on our business strategy, results of operations, or financial condition. However, we remain cognizant of the evolving threat landscape and the increasing sophistication of cyber adversaries, including state-sponsored actors and organized criminal groups. We continue to invest in advanced threat detection and prevention technologies to mitigate these risks.

Governance

The Board of Directors maintains ultimate oversight of the Company’s cybersecurity risk management program. The Board has delegated primary responsibility for this oversight to the Audit Committee. The Audit Committee receives regular reports from management on the status of our cybersecurity program, the results of recent risk assessments, and the progress of strategic security initiatives. These briefings occur at least quarterly and include updates on the evolving threat environment and any significant cybersecurity incidents that may have occurred within the industry or among our partners.

At the management level, the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) is responsible for the development, implementation, and maintenance of our cybersecurity program. Our CISO has over twenty years of experience in information technology and cybersecurity, with a specific focus on protecting global manufacturing operations and industrial control systems. The CISO holds advanced degrees in computer science and maintains several industry-recognized certifications in security management and digital forensics.

The CISO leads a dedicated global cybersecurity team and reports directly to the Chief Information Officer, with additional reporting lines to the General Counsel and the Chief Financial Officer for matters related to regulatory compliance and financial risk. The CISO is a member of our internal Security Risk Council, which is composed of senior leaders from our legal, human resources, manufacturing, and internal audit departments. This council meets regularly to review cybersecurity metrics, discuss emerging risks, and ensure that our security strategy remains aligned with our business objectives.

The Audit Committee and the full Board of Directors also participate in annual deep-dive sessions focused on cybersecurity. During these sessions, the CISO and other members of management provide a comprehensive overview of the Company’s security posture, the effectiveness of our controls, and the long-term roadmap for our cybersecurity investments. The Board also evaluates the adequacy of our resources and the expertise of our cybersecurity personnel.

In the event of a significant cybersecurity incident, we have established clear escalation protocols. The CISO is responsible for notifying the Security Risk Council and the executive leadership team. Depending on the severity and potential materiality of the incident, the CISO and the General Counsel will brief the Audit Committee and the Board of Directors. This process ensures that the Board is kept informed of critical developments and can provide appropriate oversight during the response and remediation phases.

Our internal audit function also plays a role in our cybersecurity governance. Internal audit periodically conducts independent reviews of our cybersecurity controls and processes to verify compliance with our internal policies and external regulatory requirements. The results of these audits are reported to management and the Audit Committee, and any identified deficiencies are tracked through to remediation. This multi-tiered governance structure provides a robust framework for managing cybersecurity risks and ensuring accountability across the organization.

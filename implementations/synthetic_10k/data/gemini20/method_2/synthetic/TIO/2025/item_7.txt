### **Overview**

Fieldstone Solutions Holdings, Inc. (“Fieldstone,” the “Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a global technology architect and a leading provider of high-performance semiconductor solutions and integrated system-level hardware. Headquartered in California, we operate within the semiconductor sub-industry of the Information Technology sector. Our mission is to engineer the foundational technologies that power the modern digital economy, specifically targeting high-growth markets such as artificial intelligence (“AI”) data centers, telecommunications networks, enterprise storage, and industrial automation.

Our business is organized around four primary solution suites:
*   **Networking and AI Fabric:** Focused on high-performance switching silicon, AI accelerators (XPUs), and optical interconnects.
*   **Enterprise Storage and Memory Controllers:** Providing PCIe/NVMe controllers and RAID solutions for cloud and enterprise data persistence.
*   **Wireless Connectivity:** Delivering high-end Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and 5G infrastructure solutions.
*   **Industrial and Automotive Electronics:** Supplying microcontrollers and networking silicon for extreme-environment applications.

Our strategy is built on maintaining technology leadership through intensive research and development (“R&D”) and a “fab-lite” manufacturing model that allows us to scale efficiently while focusing on high-margin, category-leading positions. In fiscal year 2025, we continued to benefit from the global transition toward accelerated computing and AI-heavy workloads, which drove significant demand for our Fieldstone Nexus architecture and XPU accelerator platforms.

### **Fiscal Year 2025 Highlights**

Financial highlights for the fiscal year ended 2025 include:
*   Total revenues of $96,117,208,650, reflecting robust demand across our core networking and storage markets.
*   Operating income of $35,016,742,988, representing a strong operating margin as we leveraged our global scale.
*   Net income of $38,197,138,353, which includes the impact of a significant income tax benefit recognized during the period.
*   Cash provided by operating activities of $46,466,894,333.
*   The return of $19,249,414,456 to stockholders through a combination of $15,576,100,938 in cash dividends and $3,673,313,518 in share repurchases.
*   A solid liquidity position with cash and equivalents of $22,909,037,661 as of the end of the fiscal year.

#### **Revenues**
Total revenues were $96,117,208,650 in fiscal year 2025. This performance was primarily driven by the Networking and AI Fabric suite, as hyperscale customers increased capital expenditures for AI training and inference infrastructure. Demand for our High-Performance Switching silicon and custom XPU accelerators remained high throughout the year. Additionally, our Enterprise Storage segment saw growth due to the increased adoption of high-capacity NVMe controllers in modern data centers.

#### **Operating Expenses**
**Cost of Revenue:** Cost of revenue was $31,956,264,834 for fiscal year 2025. These costs primarily consist of wafer fabrication, assembly, and test services provided by third-party foundries, as well as internal manufacturing overhead and logistics.

**Research and Development:** R&D expenses were $15,046,256,039, or approximately 15.7% of total revenues. This level of investment reflects our commitment to maintaining a competitive moat in sub-nanometer process nodes (3nm and 2nm) and advanced 3D-IC packaging technologies. Our R&D efforts remain focused on high-bandwidth networking and the software stacks required to optimize our hardware for AI workloads.

**Selling, General and Administrative:** SG&A expenses were $6,748,995,838. These expenses include our technical direct sales force, marketing efforts focused on industry standards bodies, and corporate administrative functions.

**Other Operating Expenses:** Other operating expenses reached $7,348,948,951, which includes amortization of certain acquired intangible assets and other periodic operational charges.

#### **Operating Income**
Operating income for fiscal year 2025 was $35,016,742,988. Our operating margin reflects our ability to command premium pricing for our integrated platform solutions while maintaining a lean “fab-lite” infrastructure.

#### **Income Tax Benefit**
We recorded an income tax benefit of $3,180,395,365 for fiscal year 2025. This benefit contributed to net income exceeding pretax income and was primarily driven by the realization of R&D tax credits and discrete tax benefits associated with our international operations and the structural integration of acquired intellectual property.

#### **Net Income**
As a result of the factors above, net income for fiscal year 2025 was $38,197,138,353.

### **Liquidity and Capital Resources**

We believe that our existing cash, coupled with our strong operating cash flows, will be sufficient to meet our working capital, R&D investments, capital expenditures, and debt service obligations for at least the next 12 months.

### **Financial Position and Capital Structure**

Our balance sheet remains robust with total assets of $231,579,414,603. A significant portion of our assets consists of Goodwill ($138,865,995,150) and Intangible Assets, net ($49,434,807,713), resulting from our history of strategic acquisitions and the integration of specialized technology assets.

Total liabilities stood at $123,732,574,484 at the end of fiscal year 2025. This includes short-term debt of $3,774,135,029 and other long-term liabilities of $118,078,581,468. Despite this substantial level of indebtedness, our operating income provides strong coverage for our obligations.

Total stockholders’ equity was $107,846,840,119, supported by retained earnings of $134,228,334,484 and additional paid-in capital of $85,139,606,879, partially offset by other equity adjustments.

### **Critical Accounting Estimates**

The preparation of financial statements in accordance with U.S. GAAP requires us to make estimates and assumptions. We believe the following are our most critical accounting estimates:

*   **Goodwill and Intangible Assets:** With $138,865,995,150 in goodwill, we perform annual impairment tests. The valuation of these assets requires significant judgment regarding future revenue growth, margins, and discount rates.
*   **Inventory Valuation:** We value inventory at the lower of cost or net realizable value. Given the cyclical nature of the semiconductor industry, we must constantly assess the risk of obsolescence for our $3,364,823,388 inventory balance.
*   **Income Taxes:** Our effective tax rate is influenced by the geographic mix of earnings and the realization of deferred tax assets. The $3,180,395,365 tax benefit recorded this year involved complex assessments of tax law and R&D credit eligibility.

### **Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk**

We are exposed to market risks, including changes in interest rates and foreign currency exchange rates. We manage these risks through a combination of natural hedges and, occasionally, derivative financial instruments. As a global entity with $96,117,208,650 in revenue, fluctuations in the value of the U.S. Dollar against the Euro or various Asian currencies can impact our reported results, although the majority of our sales are denominated in U.S. Dollars.

### **Overview**

Redcanyon Enterprises Co. ("Redcanyon," the "Company," "we," "us," or "our") is a leading global security and aerospace company, headquartered in Virginia, primarily engaged in the research, design, development, manufacture, integration, and sustainment of advanced technology systems, products, and services. As a premier participant in the Industrials sector, specifically within the Aerospace & Defense sub-industry, we serve as a critical partner to the U.S. Department of Defense (DoD), U.S. federal agencies, and international allied governments. 

Our operations are organized into four reportable segments:
*   **Advanced Flight Systems (AFS):** Focusing on high-performance tactical aircraft and autonomous aerial systems.
*   **Integrated Defense Electronics (IDE):** Specializing in electronic warfare, radar, and secure communications.
*   **Orbital Solutions (OS):** Dedicated to satellite systems, launch vehicles, and space-based surveillance.
*   **Strategic Logistics & Sustainment (SLS):** Providing life-cycle support, maintenance, and training.

During the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025, Redcanyon achieved total revenues of $29,209,366,257, driven by sustained demand for our "Canyon-Stryke" and "Sentinel-Shield" platforms. Our financial performance in 2025 reflects our continued focus on operational excellence and the execution of our multi-domain strategy. We recorded net income of $2,990,930,156, supported by an operating income of $3,108,021,251.

Our balance sheet remains robust, with total assets of $41,676,983,113, including a cash and equivalents position of $4,073,938,444. This liquidity, combined with our ability to generate $3,699,717,778 in cash from operations, allows us to continue investing in "leap-ahead" technologies such as hypersonics, artificial intelligence (AI), and quantum computing, while returning capital to shareholders through our $1,001,732,659 share repurchase program.

### **Industry Trends and Outlook**

The global security environment remains complex and contested, characterized by rapid technological shifts and evolving threats. We anticipate continued demand for high-end aerospace and defense capabilities, particularly in the areas of fifth-generation flight, autonomous systems, and space-based assets.

*   **U.S. Government Budgetary Environment:** Approximately 72% of our 2025 revenues were derived from the DoD. While the budgetary process is subject to political uncertainty, the prioritization of modernization programs—specifically those focused on air dominance and multi-domain command and control—aligns with our core competencies.
*   **Technological Convergence:** The integration of AI, autonomous systems, and electronic warfare is becoming standard across our portfolio. We are positioning ourselves to lead this transition through our $787,680,672 investment in research and development (R&D).
*   **Supply Chain and Workforce:** We continue to monitor the global supply chain for critical materials, including semiconductors and rare earth elements. Furthermore, the competition for a highly skilled, security-cleared workforce of approximately 88,000 personnel remains a strategic focus for our management team.

#### **Revenues**
Total revenues for the fiscal year 2025 were $29,209,366,257. This was driven by high volume in our Advanced Flight Systems segment, particularly from the "Canyon-Stryke" program, and strong performance in Integrated Defense Electronics. Our revenue mix remains heavily weighted toward U.S. Government contracts, which accounted for approximately 72% of our total volume, with the remainder coming from international allied governments and commercial aerospace customers. The revenue reflects a blend of fixed-price and cost-reimbursable contracts, with fixed-price arrangements providing the bulk of our production revenue.

#### **Operating Expenses**
Total operating expenses for 2025 were $26,101,345,006.
*   **Research and Development (R&D):** We invested $787,680,672 in R&D to drive innovation in hypersonics, AI-driven autonomy, and directed energy systems. These investments are critical for maintaining our competitive edge and securing future "programs of record."
*   **Other Operating Expenses:** These expenses, totaling $25,313,664,334, primarily consist of cost of sales (including materials, labor for our 88,000 employees, and manufacturing overhead) as well as selling, general, and administrative (SG&A) expenses. The magnitude of these expenses reflects the high cost of production for advanced aerospace platforms and the maintenance of a complex global supply chain.

#### **Operating Income**
Operating income for 2025 was $3,108,021,251, representing an operating margin of approximately 10.6%. Our margins were influenced by the successful achievement of program milestones in the IDE and OS segments, partially offset by the high costs associated with scaling production for the "Canyon-Stryke" aircraft.

#### **Interest Expense**
Interest expense for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025, was $0. Despite our long-term debt of $11,035,390,946, our current debt structure and interest capitalization policies resulted in no recognized interest expense in the current period's income statement.

#### **Income Tax Expense**
Our income tax expense for 2025 was $117,091,095, resulting in an effective tax rate of approximately 3.77%. This rate is lower than the U.S. statutory rate of 21%, primarily due to the utilization of R&D tax credits and potential jurisdictional tax benefits associated with our international operations.

#### **Net Income**
Net income for 2025 was $2,990,930,156. This result reflects our ability to convert large-scale government contracts into consistent profitability while managing a significant cost base.

### **Segment Results of Operations**

We evaluate the performance of our segments based on their ability to deliver mission-critical systems while maintaining cost efficiency.

#### **Advanced Flight Systems (AFS)**
The AFS segment is the primary contributor to our $29,209,366,257 in total revenue. Performance in 2025 was driven by the "Canyon-Stryke" stealth fighter program and the ramping up of the "Red-Hawk" autonomous drone series. Revenues in this segment are highly sensitive to DoD procurement cycles. Segment operating margins were impacted by the initial costs of next-generation propulsion integration, which were offset by efficiencies gained in the mature "Canyon-Stryke" production line.

#### **Integrated Defense Electronics (IDE)**
IDE saw robust demand for the "Sentinel-Shield" radar array and cyber-resilient communications systems. This segment benefits from high barriers to entry and proprietary signal processing technologies. Revenue growth in IDE is increasingly driven by the need for multi-domain command and control (MDC2) capabilities across all branches of the military.

#### **Orbital Solutions (OS)**
OS revenues were bolstered by the continued deployment of the "Zenith" communications constellation and the "Nova" series of tactical launch platforms. We remain a key partner for the U.S. Space Force and NASA. The segment's performance is tied to successful launch schedules and satellite orbital insertion milestones.

#### **Strategic Logistics & Sustainment (SLS)**
SLS provided stable, recurring revenue through MRO services and global supply chain management. This segment ensures the long-term readiness of the platforms built by AFS and IDE. Our possession of "technical data rights" remains a key differentiator for SLS, allowing us to remain the provider of choice for sustainment activities.

### **Critical Accounting Estimates**

The preparation of our consolidated financial statements in accordance with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP) requires management to make estimates and assumptions. These estimates affect the reported amounts of assets, liabilities, revenues, and expenses.

**Revenue Recognition on Long-Term Contracts:**
A significant portion of our $29,209,366,257 in revenue is recognized over time using the cost-to-cost method, which requires us to estimate the total costs to complete a contract. These estimates include labor, material, and subcontract costs, as well as estimates of technical risk. Changes in these estimates can significantly impact our operating income.

**Goodwill and Intangible Assets:**
As of December 31, 2025, we had $12,265,727,702 in goodwill and $172,990,412 in net intangible assets. We test goodwill for impairment annually, or more frequently if indicators of impairment exist. These tests require significant judgment regarding future cash flows and discount rates for each of our four reportable segments.

**Property, Plant, and Equipment:**
Our net property, plant, and equipment (PP&E) of $9,562,608,317 is depreciated over the estimated useful lives of the assets. We periodically review these assets for impairment whenever events or changes in circumstances indicate that the carrying amount may not be recoverable.

**Pension and Post-retirement Benefits:**
Our 88,000-person workforce participates in various pension and post-retirement plans. The calculation of these obligations involves actuarial assumptions, including discount rates, expected rates of return on plan assets, and mortality rates. These obligations are included within our $12,005,163,456 in "other liabilities."

**Income Taxes:**
We estimate our income tax expense and deferred tax assets/liabilities based on current tax laws and our interpretation of those laws. Our deferred tax assets are included in our $13,384,522,590 of "other assets." We evaluate the need for valuation allowances based on our ability to generate future taxable income.

### **Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk**

We are exposed to market risks, including changes in interest rates and foreign currency exchange rates.
*   **Interest Rate Risk:** While we have $11,035,390,946 in long-term debt, much of this is at fixed rates. We do not believe a 10% change in interest rates would have a material impact on our financial condition, given our current cash position of $4,073,938,444.
*   **Foreign Currency Risk:** Our international sales, which comprise a portion of our 28% non-DoD revenue, expose us to currency fluctuations. We utilize forward contracts to hedge significant transactional exposures.
*   **Commodity Price Risk:** We are exposed to price volatility in raw materials such as aluminum, titanium, and specialized composites. We utilize long-term supply agreements to mitigate these risks across our manufacturing operations in Virginia and elsewhere.

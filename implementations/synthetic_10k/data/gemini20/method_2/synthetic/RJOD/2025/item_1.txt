**ITEM 1. BUSINESS**

**Overview**

Redcanyon Enterprises Co. (together with its subsidiaries, "Redcanyon," the "Company," "we," "us," or "our") is a leading global security, aerospace, and industrial technology company. Headquartered in Virginia, Redcanyon is a premier provider of high-technology systems and services for the defense, civil, and commercial sectors. Our operations are primarily focused on the research, design, development, manufacture, integration, and sustainment of advanced technology systems, products, and services.

As a major participant in the Industrials sector, specifically within the Aerospace & Defense sub-industry, Redcanyon serves as a prime contractor and lead systems integrator for a diverse array of programs essential to national security and global infrastructure. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025, the Company generated total revenues of $29,209,366,257. Our commitment to innovation and operational excellence has positioned us at the forefront of the evolving global security landscape, enabling us to deliver mission-critical solutions to the U.S. Department of Defense ("DoD"), various U.S. federal agencies, and international allied governments.

**Business Segments**

We organize our business into four reportable segments: Advanced Flight Systems (AFS), Integrated Defense Electronics (IDE), Orbital Solutions (OS), and Strategic Logistics & Sustainment (SLS). These segments are organized based on the nature of the products and services provided and the markets served.

*Advanced Flight Systems (AFS)*
The Advanced Flight Systems segment is our largest business unit, contributing significantly to our $29,209,366,257 in total revenue. AFS is a world leader in the design and production of high-performance aircraft, including fifth-generation tactical fighters, unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs), and advanced rotary-wing platforms. This segment is responsible for the development of the "Canyon-Stryke" multi-role stealth fighter and the "Red-Hawk" series of autonomous surveillance drones. AFS focuses on air dominance, intelligence, surveillance, and reconnaissance (ISR), and tactical mobility. The segment also invests heavily in next-generation propulsion systems and aerodynamic materials to ensure air superiority for our primary customers.

*Integrated Defense Electronics (IDE)*
The Integrated Defense Electronics segment provides sophisticated electronic warfare systems, sensor suites, and cyber-resilient communication networks. IDE specializes in the "Sentinel-Shield" radar array and various signal intelligence technologies that enable multi-domain command and control. This segment’s revenue is driven by the increasing demand for electronic countermeasures and secure data links in contested environments. IDE’s products are integrated into a wide variety of ground-based, sea-based, and airborne platforms, providing a comprehensive "digital canopy" for modern combat forces.

*Orbital Solutions (OS)*
The Orbital Solutions segment focuses on the design, manufacture, and operation of satellite systems, space launch vehicles, and deep-space exploration technologies. As the "Final Frontier" becomes increasingly congested and contested, OS provides the "Zenith" constellation of communications satellites and the "Nova" series of tactical launch platforms. OS is a key partner for civil space agencies and the U.S. Space Force, providing Earth observation, missile warning systems, and persistent space-based surveillance. This segment also manages the development of advanced thermal protection systems and miniaturized satellite buses for low-earth orbit (LEO) applications.

*Strategic Logistics & Sustainment (SLS)*
The Strategic Logistics & Sustainment segment provides life-cycle support, maintenance, repair, and overhaul (MRO) services, and supply chain management for our various platforms and those of third parties. SLS ensures the operational readiness of global fleets through data-driven predictive maintenance and global parts distribution networks. This segment also provides training and simulation services, including high-fidelity flight simulators and virtual reality combat training environments, ensuring that operators are prepared for the complexities of modern warfare.

**Revenue and Customer Concentration**

For the fiscal year 2025, our total revenues of $29,209,366,257 were derived primarily from contracts with the U.S. Government. Approximately 72% of our total revenue was attributable to the DoD, reflecting our role as a critical partner in national defense. The remaining 28% of our revenue was generated from contracts with other U.S. federal agencies (such as the Department of Homeland Security and NASA), international allied governments, and commercial customers within the aerospace industry.

Our international business, which accounted for a substantial portion of our non-U.S. Government revenue, is subject to strict export controls and requires authorization from the U.S. Department of State and the U.S. Department of Commerce. We maintain a robust compliance framework to navigate the International Traffic in Arms Regulations (ITAR) and the Export Administration Regulations (EAR).

**Contracting and Procurement**

Redcanyon operates under various contract types, including fixed-price, cost-reimbursable, and time-and-materials contracts. 

1. *Fixed-Price Contracts:* Under these contracts, we agree to perform specific work for a predetermined price. While these contracts offer higher profit potential through cost-efficiency, they also carry the risk of cost overruns, which must be absorbed by the Company.
2. *Cost-Reimbursable Contracts:* These contracts provide for the reimbursement of allowable costs plus a fee. These are typically used for research and development programs where the scope of work is less defined.
3. *Indefinite Delivery, Indefinite Quantity (IDIQ):* These contracts provide for an indefinite quantity of supplies or services during a fixed period, allowing the customer to place "task orders" as needs arise.

The selection of contract type depends on the maturity of the technology and the requirements of the procurement agency. Our ability to manage cost and schedule performance is critical to our profitability.

**Research and Development**

Innovation is the cornerstone of Redcanyon’s competitive advantage. During fiscal year 2025, we invested a significant portion of our $29,209,366,257 revenue into research and development (R&D). Our R&D efforts are categorized into two types: Independent Research and Development (IR&D), which is funded by the Company and aimed at future growth, and Customer-Funded Research and Development (CR&D), which is performed under specific contracts.

Our current R&D priorities include:
*   *Hypersonics:* Developing flight vehicles capable of traveling at speeds exceeding Mach 5.
*   *Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Autonomy:* Integrating machine learning into autonomous systems to enhance decision-making in high-speed environments.
*   *Directed Energy:* Developing laser-based defense systems for counter-drone and missile defense applications.
*   *Quantum Computing:* Exploring quantum-resistant encryption and advanced signal processing.

**Human Capital Resources**

As of December 31, 2025, Redcanyon employed approximately 88,000 personnel. Given our headquarters in Virginia and our proximity to the nation’s capital, a high percentage of our workforce holds sensitive security clearances, which is a significant barrier to entry for competitors.

*Talent Acquisition and Retention*
The competition for highly skilled engineers, data scientists, and cybersecurity experts is intense. We offer competitive compensation, comprehensive benefits, and a culture of continuous learning. Our "Redcanyon Leadership Institute" provides specialized training for emerging leaders within the organization.

*Diversity and Inclusion*
We are committed to fostering a diverse and inclusive workplace. We believe that a variety of perspectives drives innovation and enables us to better solve our customers' most complex problems. We monitor diversity metrics across all levels of management and have established employee resource groups to support our workforce.

*Safety and Health*
The safety of our employees is our highest priority, particularly in our manufacturing and integration facilities. We maintain rigorous safety protocols and participate in various voluntary protection programs to ensure a zero-harm workplace.

**Competition**

The Aerospace & Defense sub-industry is characterized by intense competition and high barriers to entry. Redcanyon competes with several large, well-capitalized defense contractors, as well as emerging technology firms specializing in software, space, and autonomous systems. 

Competition is based on several factors, including:
*   Technological innovation and performance.
*   Total lifecycle cost and affordability.
*   Past performance and proven reliability.
*   Schedule adherence and program management.
*   Security of supply chains and cybersecurity resilience.

To remain competitive, we must continually invest in the next generation of "leap-ahead" technologies and maintain a lean cost structure.

**Backlog**

Our backlog represents the total estimated value of orders for which we have received signed contracts but have not yet recognized revenue. As of December 31, 2025, our total backlog remained strong, providing significant visibility into future revenue streams. Our backlog consists of "funded backlog" (orders for which the customer has already appropriated funds) and "unfunded backlog" (the remaining value of multi-year contracts not yet funded). The conversion of backlog into revenue is subject to various factors, including government budgetary cycles and program milestones.

**Patents and Intellectual Property**

Redcanyon owns a vast portfolio of patents, trademarks, and trade secrets that protect our innovations. While our business is not significantly dependent on any single patent, our collective intellectual property (IP) is a vital asset. We also rely on "technical data rights" granted under government contracts, which allow us to maintain a competitive edge in follow-on sustainment and modernization programs. We aggressively protect our IP against unauthorized use and misappropriation.

**Government Regulations and Oversight**

As a government contractor, Redcanyon is subject to extensive regulation and oversight. Our accounting systems and internal controls are frequently audited by the Defense Contract Audit Agency (DCAA) and the Defense Contract Management Agency (DCMA).

*Compliance and Ethics*
We maintain a comprehensive Ethics and Business Conduct program. All employees are required to undergo annual training on topics such as anti-corruption, procurement integrity, and the protection of classified information.

*Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR)*
Most of our contracts are subject to the FAR and the Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement (DFARS). These regulations govern almost every aspect of our interaction with the U.S. Government, including pricing, cost allowability, and socio-economic requirements.

*Security Clearances*
A substantial portion of our facilities and personnel must maintain high-level security clearances. Failure to maintain these clearances could result in the loss of contracts and the inability to bid on future classified work.

**Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG)**

Redcanyon is committed to sustainable business practices. We recognize that our operations have an impact on the environment and the communities in which we operate.

*Environmental Stewardship*
We have set ambitious goals to reduce our greenhouse gas emissions and water consumption across our manufacturing sites. We are investing in energy-efficient infrastructure and exploring the use of sustainable aviation fuels (SAF) for our flight testing operations.

*Supply Chain Responsibility*
We hold our suppliers to high ethical and environmental standards. Our "Supplier Code of Conduct" ensures that our partners adhere to labor laws, human rights standards, and environmental regulations.

*Corporate Governance*
Our Board of Directors provides oversight of our strategic direction and risk management. We maintain a transparent governance structure and engage regularly with our shareholders on ESG-related topics.

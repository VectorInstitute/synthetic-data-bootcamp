**REPORT OF INDEPENDENT REGISTERED PUBLIC ACCOUNTING FIRM**

To the Board of Directors and Stockholders of Redcanyon Enterprises Co.

**Opinion on the Financial Statements**

We have audited the accompanying consolidated balance sheets of Redcanyon Enterprises Co. (a Virginia corporation) and subsidiaries (the “Company”) as of December 31, 2025, the related consolidated statements of operations, comprehensive income, stockholders’ equity, and cash flows for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025, and the related notes (collectively referred to as the “financial statements”). In our opinion, the financial statements present fairly, in all material respects, the financial position of the Company as of December 31, 2025, and the results of its operations and its cash flows for the year then ended, in conformity with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States of America.

**Basis for Opinion**

These financial statements are the responsibility of the Company’s management. Our responsibility is to express an opinion on the Company’s financial statements based on our audit. We are a public accounting firm registered with the Public Company Accounting Oversight Board (United States) (PCAOB) and are required to be independent with respect to the Company in accordance with the U.S. federal securities laws and the applicable rules and regulations of the Securities and Exchange Commission and the PCAOB.

We conducted our audit in accordance with the standards of the PCAOB. Those standards require that we plan and perform the audit to obtain reasonable assurance about whether the financial statements are free of material misstatement, whether due to error or fraud. Our audit included performing procedures to assess the risks of material misstatement of the financial statements, whether due to error or fraud, and performing procedures that respond to those risks. Such procedures included examining, on a test basis, evidence regarding the amounts and disclosures in the financial statements. Our audit also included evaluating the accounting principles used and significant estimates made by management, as well as evaluating the overall presentation of the financial statements. We believe that our audit provides a reasonable basis for our opinion.

**Critical Audit Matters**

The critical audit matters communicated below are matters arising from the current period audit of the financial statements that were communicated or required to be communicated to the audit committee and that: (1) relate to accounts or disclosures that are material to the financial statements and (2) involved our especially challenging, subjective, or complex judgments. The communication of critical audit matters does not alter in any way our opinion on the financial statements, taken as a whole, and we are not, by this communication, providing separate opinions on the critical audit matters or on the accounts or disclosures to which they relate.

*Revenue Recognition for Long-term Fixed-Price Contracts*

As described in Note 1 to the financial statements, the Company recognizes a significant portion of its $29,209,366,257 in revenue over time using the cost-to-cost method. This requires management to estimate total costs at completion, which involves high degrees of judgment regarding labor productivity, material costs, and technical risks. Our audit procedures included testing the effectiveness of controls over the estimation process and performing substantive procedures to evaluate the reasonableness of significant assumptions used in the Company's cost-to-complete estimates for major programs like the Canyon-Stryke and Sentinel-Shield.

**NOTES TO CONSOLIDATED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS**

**NOTE 1. SUMMARY OF SIGNIFICANT ACCOUNTING POLICIES**

*Organization and Business* – Redcanyon Enterprises Co. (“Redcanyon” or the “Company”), headquartered in Virginia, is a leading provider of high-technology systems and services for the defense, civil, and commercial sectors. The Company operates in four segments: Advanced Flight Systems (AFS), Integrated Defense Electronics (IDE), Orbital Solutions (OS), and Strategic Logistics & Sustainment (SLS).

*Basis of Presentation* – The consolidated financial statements include the accounts of Redcanyon and its subsidiaries and are prepared in accordance with U.S. GAAP. All intercompany transactions and balances have been eliminated.

*Use of Estimates* – The preparation of financial statements requires management to make estimates and assumptions that affect the reported amounts. Significant estimates include the cost-to-complete on long-term contracts, the valuation of goodwill, and the assessment of pension obligations.

*Revenue Recognition* – Revenue is recognized when control of the promised goods or services is transferred to customers. For the majority of the Company’s $29,209,366,257 in revenue, control transfers over time as the Company performs under the contract, typically using the cost-to-cost method.

*Cash and Equivalents* – Cash and equivalents include all highly liquid investments with an original maturity of three months or less. As of December 31, 2025, the Company held $4,073,938,444 in cash and equivalents.

*Accounts Receivable* – Accounts receivable are recorded net of an allowance for credit losses. At December 31, 2025, the balance of $1,141,894,233 primarily reflects amounts due from the U.S. Government.

*Inventory* – Inventory is stated at the lower of cost or net realizable value. As of December 31, 2025, inventory of $1,075,301,415 primarily consists of work-in-process related to fixed-price production contracts in the AFS and IDE segments.

*Property, Plant and Equipment* – PP&E is recorded at cost and depreciated on a straight-line basis over estimated useful lives ranging from 3 to 40 years. Net PP&E at December 31, 2025, was $9,562,608,317.

*Goodwill and Intangibles* – Goodwill of $12,265,727,702 represents the excess of purchase price over the fair value of net assets acquired in business combinations. Intangible assets, net of $172,990,412, primarily consist of acquired technology and customer relationships being amortized over their useful lives.

**NOTE 2. OPERATING EXPENSES**

The Company’s operating expenses for the year ended December 31, 2025, consisted of the following:
*   *Research and Development (R&D):* $787,680,672, representing independent research and development efforts in hypersonics, AI, and quantum computing.
*   *Other Operating Expenses:* $25,313,664,334, which includes cost of sales, manufacturing overhead, and selling, general, and administrative (SG&A) expenses.

**NOTE 4. GOODWILL AND INTANGIBLES**

The Company’s $12,265,727,702 in goodwill is allocated across its four reportable segments. During the annual impairment test performed in the fourth quarter of 2025, management determined that no impairment was required as the fair value of each reporting unit exceeded its carrying value.

Intangible assets, net of $172,990,412, are subject to amortization. Amortization expense for the year ended December 31, 2025, was included in the total depreciation and amortization of $1,078,710,699.

**NOTE 5. DEBT**

As of December 31, 2025, the Company’s debt consisted of:
*   *Short-term debt:* $479,017,794, primarily consisting of commercial paper and current maturities of long-term debt.
*   *Long-term debt:* $11,035,390,946, consisting of senior unsecured notes with various maturities extending through 2055.

Interest expense for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025, was $0, as the Company capitalized all interest costs related to major construction-in-progress projects and long-term development programs.

**NOTE 6. INCOME TAXES**

The Company’s income tax expense of $117,091,095 for 2025 represents an effective tax rate of 3.77%. The difference between the 21% U.S. federal statutory rate and the 3.77% effective rate is primarily attributable to:
*   R&D tax credits.
*   Foreign-derived intangible income (FDII) deductions.
*   Adjustments related to prior-year tax positions.

Net deferred tax assets are included in “Other assets” on the Consolidated Balance Sheets.

**NOTE 7. OTHER LIABILITIES**

Other liabilities of $12,005,163,456 as of December 31, 2025, include:
*   Pension and post-retirement benefit obligations for the Company’s 88,000 employees.
*   Deferred revenue and customer advances on long-term contracts.
*   Environmental and legal reserves.

The $92,577,450 recorded in Accumulated Other Comprehensive Loss reflects net actuarial losses and prior service costs associated with these benefit plans during 2025.

**NOTE 8. STOCKHOLDERS’ EQUITY**

During 2025, the Company repurchased and retired shares of its common stock for a total cost of $1,001,732,659. These repurchases were funded through cash from operations and are reflected in the “Other equity” component of stockholders’ equity. No dividends were paid during the fiscal year 2025. Total stockholders’ equity at year-end was $15,526,877,909.

**NOTE 9. SEGMENT INFORMATION**

The Company operates in four reportable segments. While the Company’s $29,209,366,257 in revenue is generated across all segments, the Advanced Flight Systems segment remains the largest contributor.

*   **Advanced Flight Systems (AFS):** High-performance tactical aircraft and autonomous systems.
*   **Integrated Defense Electronics (IDE):** Sensors, electronic warfare, and secure communications.
*   **Orbital Solutions (OS):** Satellites, space launch vehicles, and orbital services.
*   **Strategic Logistics & Sustainment (SLS):** Life-cycle support, MRO, and training.

Approximately 72% of total revenues were derived from contracts with the U.S. Department of Defense.

**NOTE 10. COMMITMENTS AND CONTINGENCIES**

The Company is involved in various legal proceedings, claims, and investigations arising in the ordinary course of business. Management believes that the resolution of these matters will not have a material adverse effect on the Company’s $41,676,983,113 asset base or $15,526,877,909 equity position. At December 31, 2025, the Company had accounts payable of $2,630,533,008, representing obligations to its supply chain.

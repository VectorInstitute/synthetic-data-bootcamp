### **Executive Overview**

Meridian Technologies Co. (“Meridian,” the “Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a leading global industrial conglomerate headquartered in Minnesota, specializing in highly engineered products and integrated digital solutions. As of December 31, 2025, our operations are organized into four reportable segments: Precision Infrastructure, Specialized Power Systems, Advanced Material Solutions, and Global Logistics Support. Our business model is centered on the convergence of physical industrial hardware and advanced software analytics, a strategy we refer to as "industrial digitization."

For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025, Meridian reported total consolidated revenues of $26,026,305,540 and net income of $3,689,897,510. Our performance in 2025 reflects the continued execution of our multi-year strategic transformation, which emphasizes high-margin organic growth and disciplined capital allocation. We achieved an operating income of $5,117,362,936, representing an operating margin of approximately 19.7%.

Key strategic milestones in 2025 included the commercial scaling of our "Nexus-Grid" structural monitoring sensors within the Precision Infrastructure segment and the expansion of our "Meridian-Path" AI-driven routing platform in the Global Logistics Support segment. Furthermore, we continued to optimize our manufacturing footprint through our "regionalized production" model, with a significant concentration of high-tech manufacturing and R&D activities at our Minnesota hub. This model has allowed us to mitigate global supply chain volatility while maintaining a robust workforce of approximately 74,000 employees.

Our financial position remains substantial, with total assets of $41,879,604,057. However, our capital structure reflects significant leverage, with total liabilities of $36,684,937,271 and total stockholders’ equity of $5,194,666,786. During 2025, we focused on enhancing shareholder value through significant share repurchases totaling $3,624,589,386, while maintaining a cash and equivalents balance of $6,437,167,396 to support ongoing operations and strategic investments.

#### **Revenue**
Total consolidated revenue for 2025 was $26,026,305,540. This revenue was driven by strong demand across all four segments, particularly in Precision Infrastructure and Specialized Power Systems, which together accounted for 70% of total sales. Our revenue growth was supported by the successful rollout of "smart" hardware lines and an increase in long-term service contracts associated with our digital platforms.

*   **Precision Infrastructure:** Generated $10,410,522,216 in revenue (40% of total). This performance was bolstered by large-scale civil engineering projects in North America and Europe, where our "Nexus-Grid" sensors were integrated into bridge and rail network modernizations.
*   **Specialized Power Systems:** Contributed $7,807,891,662 in revenue (30% of total). Demand was driven by the global transition toward electrification, specifically for our industrial-grade turbines and modular battery storage units.
*   **Advanced Material Solutions:** Contributed $5,205,261,108 in revenue (20% of total). Growth in this segment was led by high-performance polymers and specialized coatings used in the aerospace and medical device industries.
*   **Global Logistics Support:** Accounted for $2,602,630,554 in revenue (10% of total). This segment benefited from the expansion of "Last-Mile Industrial" services and the efficiency gains provided by our "Meridian-Path" software.

#### **Cost of Revenue and Gross Profit**
Cost of revenue for 2025 was $13,747,870,066, resulting in a gross profit of $12,278,435,474 and a gross margin of 47.2%. The cost of revenue includes raw materials such as steel, aluminum, and copper, as well as labor costs for our manufacturing workforce and depreciation of $1,333,250,655. Gross margin was influenced by our ability to maintain premium pricing on technologically advanced products, partially offset by inflationary pressures in the global commodity markets for rare earth minerals.

#### **Operating Expenses**
*   **Selling, General and Administrative (SG&A):** SG&A expenses were $4,083,508,846, or 15.7% of revenue. These costs primarily consist of payroll and benefits for our 74,000-person global workforce, marketing and distribution costs, and administrative functions at our Minnesota headquarters.
*   **Other Operating Expenses:** Other operating expenses totaled $3,077,563,692. This includes our heavy investment in research and development (R&D) activities focused on sustainability and digitization, as well as costs associated with maintaining our global patent portfolio.

#### **Operating Income**
Operating income was $5,117,362,936 for the year ended December 31, 2025. This reflects the high operational efficiency of our regionalized production model and the successful integration of AI-driven logistics into our internal supply chain.

#### **Income Tax Expense**
Our pretax income of $5,117,362,936 was subject to an income tax expense of $1,427,465,426, representing an effective tax rate of approximately 27.9%. This rate reflects the mix of earnings across various domestic and international jurisdictions and the impact of R&D tax credits associated with our Minnesota-based innovation centers.

#### **Net Income**
Net income for 2025 was $3,689,897,510, representing a net margin of 14.2%.

### **Segment Results of Operations**

#### **Precision Infrastructure**
The Precision Infrastructure segment reported revenue of $10,410,522,216. Segment performance was characterized by the execution of several multi-year government contracts for structural health monitoring. The adoption of "smart" fasteners and structural sensors contributed to higher margins compared to traditional hardware, as customers prioritized the predictive maintenance capabilities offered by our data analytics. Operating costs in this segment were impacted by the prices of structural steel, which remained volatile throughout 2025.

#### **Specialized Power Systems**
Specialized Power Systems generated $7,807,891,662 in revenue. The segment’s growth was primarily fueled by the utility-scale renewable energy market. Our new high-capacity battery assembly plant in the Midwest achieved full operational status in 2025, enabling us to meet the backlog of orders for modular energy storage. Revenue was also supported by steady demand for backup power systems from the data center sector.

#### **Advanced Material Solutions**
Revenue for Advanced Material Solutions was $5,205,261,108. This segment saw high demand for lightweight composite materials in the aerospace sector. We also successfully commercialized several bio-based polymers, which were integrated into consumer electronics packaging. The segment’s profitability was driven by our proprietary chemical formulations and the strong intellectual property moat protecting our high-performance coatings.

#### **Global Logistics Support**
Global Logistics Support contributed $2,602,630,554 in revenue. While the smallest segment by revenue, it serves as a critical enabler for our other business units. The rollout of "Meridian-Path" software across our entire fleet resulted in a 12% improvement in fuel efficiency and enabled us to offer "crating-as-a-service" to high-precision industries like medical imaging.

### **Critical Accounting Estimates**

The preparation of our consolidated financial statements requires management to make estimates and assumptions that affect the reported amounts of assets, liabilities, revenues, and expenses. We base our estimates on historical experience and various other assumptions that we believe to be reasonable under the circumstances.

**Revenue Recognition:**
A significant portion of our revenue in the Precision Infrastructure and Specialized Power Systems segments is recognized over time using the cost-to-cost method, as our products are often highly customized and have no alternative use. This requires estimation of total project costs and progress toward completion. Changes in these estimates can significantly impact reported revenue and margins.

**Goodwill and Intangible Assets:**
As of December 31, 2025, we had goodwill of $6,513,637,295 and net intangible assets of $1,089,173,806. We perform an annual impairment test for goodwill, or more frequently if indicators of impairment exist. This test involves significant judgment regarding future cash flow projections, discount rates, and market multiples.

**Inventory Valuation:**
Inventory is valued at the lower of cost or net realizable value, totaling $3,743,948,133 at year-end. We regularly review inventory for obsolescence, particularly in our Advanced Material Solutions and Precision Infrastructure segments, where technological shifts can rapidly impact the value of specialized components.

**Income Taxes:**
We are subject to income taxes in the U.S. and numerous foreign jurisdictions. Significant judgment is required in determining our global provision for income taxes and evaluating our tax positions. We recognize deferred tax assets and liabilities for the expected future tax consequences of events that have been included in our financial statements.

### **Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk**

**Interest Rate Risk:**
We are exposed to interest rate risk primarily through our $13.9 billion in total debt. While a portion of our debt is at fixed rates, any variable-rate debt or future refinancing remains sensitive to changes in market interest rates. A 100-basis point increase in interest rates would increase our annual interest expense and decrease our pretax income accordingly.

**Commodity Price Risk:**
Our manufacturing processes require significant quantities of raw materials. We use long-term supply contracts and, in some cases, derivative financial instruments to manage our exposure to fluctuations in the prices of steel, aluminum, and copper. Despite these efforts, a sustained increase in commodity prices could adversely affect our gross margins.

**Foreign Currency Exchange Risk:**
With a significant portion of our $26,026,305,540 in revenue generated outside the United States, we are exposed to fluctuations in foreign currency exchange rates. The primary currencies to which we are exposed include the Euro, the British Pound, and the Japanese Yen. We use foreign currency forward contracts to hedge certain forecasted transactions and balance sheet exposures.

Meridian Technologies Co. concluded fiscal year 2025 with strong operational results and a clear path for future growth. Our $26.0 billion revenue base and $3.7 billion in net income underscore the resilience of our diversified industrial portfolio. While our leverage remains a point of management focus, our $6.4 billion cash position and the high demand for our digitized industrial solutions provide a solid foundation for 2026. We remain committed to our Minnesota-based innovation strategy and our global mission to build, power, and move the world through precision engineering and advanced technology.

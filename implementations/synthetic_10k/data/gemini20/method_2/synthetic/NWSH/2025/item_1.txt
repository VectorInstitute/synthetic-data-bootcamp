**ITEM 1. BUSINESS**

**Overview**

Meridian Technologies Co. (“Meridian,” the “Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a leading global industrial conglomerate headquartered in Minnesota. Since our inception, we have established ourselves as a cornerstone of the industrial sector, providing a diverse array of highly engineered products and solutions to a broad spectrum of end markets, including aerospace, defense, energy, infrastructure, and consumer goods. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025, the Company reported total consolidated revenues of $26,026,305,540.

Our operations are characterized by a commitment to technical excellence, operational efficiency, and sustainable growth. As a diversified industrial conglomerate, Meridian Technologies Co. manages a portfolio of market-leading businesses that are organized into four primary reportable segments: Precision Infrastructure, Specialized Power Systems, Advanced Material Solutions, and Global Logistics Support. Each segment leverages our centralized research and development (R&D) capabilities while maintaining the agility required to address the specific needs of their respective global markets.

In fiscal year 2025, Meridian continued to execute its long-term strategic roadmap, which emphasizes high-margin organic growth, disciplined capital allocation, and the integration of digital transformation initiatives across our manufacturing footprint. Our global reach, coupled with our headquarters in Minnesota, allows us to tap into a robust domestic talent pool while serving a geographically diverse customer base.

**Segment Analysis**

The following table and subsequent descriptions provide a detailed overview of our business segments. All revenue figures for the fiscal year 2025 are grounded in our total consolidated revenue of $26,026,305,540.

*   **Precision Infrastructure:** $10,410,522,216 (40% of total revenue)
*   **Specialized Power Systems:** $7,807,891,662 (30% of total revenue)
*   **Advanced Material Solutions:** $5,205,261,108 (20% of total revenue)
*   **Global Logistics Support:** $2,602,630,554 (10% of total revenue)

***Precision Infrastructure***

The Precision Infrastructure segment is our largest operating division, accounting for $10,410,522,216 in revenue during the 2025 fiscal year. This segment specializes in the design, manufacture, and servicing of critical infrastructure components. Our product portfolio includes advanced structural monitoring systems, high-capacity industrial fasteners, and automated heavy machinery used in large-scale civil engineering projects.

We serve a global clientele comprising governmental entities, municipal authorities, and private-sector construction firms. Our infrastructure solutions are critical for the modernization of transportation networks, including bridges, tunnels, and high-speed rail systems. In 2025, this segment saw significant demand for our proprietary "Nexus-Grid" sensors, which provide real-time data on structural integrity, reducing maintenance costs and enhancing safety for aging infrastructure.

The competitive advantage of the Precision Infrastructure segment lies in our ability to integrate digital twins and IoT (Internet of Things) capabilities into traditional industrial products. By providing "smart" infrastructure components, we enable our customers to move from reactive to predictive maintenance models.

***Specialized Power Systems***

Our Specialized Power Systems segment generated $7,807,891,662 in revenue in 2025. This segment focuses on the development of high-efficiency energy conversion and storage technologies. Our products include industrial-grade turbines, backup power systems for data centers, and advanced electrical distribution modules used in renewable energy projects.

As the global economy shifts toward electrification, Meridian’s Specialized Power Systems segment has positioned itself as a key supplier for grid stabilization and energy storage. We manufacture modular battery storage units that allow industrial facilities to manage peak load demand effectively. Furthermore, our thermal management systems are essential for the cooling of high-performance computing environments and electric vehicle charging stations.

This segment’s growth is driven by the increasing demand for reliable, resilient power across both emerging and developed economies. Our 2025 performance reflects a strong backlog of orders in the utility-scale solar and wind sectors, where our power inverters and converters are recognized for their industry-leading efficiency ratings.

***Advanced Material Solutions***

The Advanced Material Solutions segment contributed $5,205,261,108 to our 2025 total revenue. This division produces high-performance polymers, specialized coatings, and composite materials designed to withstand extreme environments. These materials are utilized extensively in the aerospace, medical device, and electronics industries.

Our material scientists in Minnesota and across our global labs focus on developing lightweight, high-strength alternatives to traditional metals. These innovations are critical for the aerospace industry, where weight reduction directly correlates to fuel efficiency and reduced emissions. Additionally, our specialized coatings provide corrosion resistance for underwater pipelines and chemical processing plants, extending the lifecycle of capital-intensive assets.

The Advanced Material Solutions segment operates in a highly technical environment where intellectual property (IP) and proprietary formulations are significant barriers to entry. We maintain a robust patent portfolio and continue to invest in sustainable chemistry to meet the evolving regulatory requirements regarding material safety and environmental impact.

***Global Logistics Support***

The Global Logistics Support segment accounted for $2,602,630,554 of our 2025 revenue. This segment provides end-to-end supply chain solutions, specialized packaging, and fleet management technology for the industrial sector. Unlike traditional logistics providers, Meridian focuses on the transportation of oversized, hazardous, or highly sensitive industrial equipment.

Our logistics platform utilizes proprietary AI-driven routing software to optimize fuel consumption and delivery timelines. We also provide specialized "crating-as-a-service" for the microchip and medical imaging industries, where vibration and temperature control are paramount. In 2025, the segment expanded its "Last-Mile Industrial" initiative, which focuses on the rapid delivery of critical replacement parts to manufacturing facilities, thereby minimizing downtime for our clients.

**Marketing and Distribution**

Meridian Technologies Co. utilizes a multi-channel distribution strategy to reach its global customer base. We employ a direct sales force for large-scale enterprise accounts and government contracts, particularly within our Precision Infrastructure and Specialized Power Systems segments. This direct approach allows for deep technical collaboration between our engineers and the end-users of our products.

For our Advanced Material Solutions and specific components within our other segments, we utilize an extensive network of third-party distributors and value-added resellers. This hybrid model ensures that we maintain a local presence in over 100 countries, providing timely service and support. Our marketing efforts are centered on trade shows, technical journals, and digital platforms that highlight the performance metrics and reliability of our industrial solutions.

**Research and Development (R&D)**

Innovation is the lifeblood of Meridian Technologies Co. In fiscal year 2025, we continued our commitment to R&D, focusing on the convergence of physical industrial hardware and digital software. Our primary R&D hub is located at our headquarters in Minnesota, with satellite centers of excellence in Europe and Asia.

Our R&D initiatives are categorized into three pillars:
1.  **Sustainability:** Developing products that reduce the carbon footprint of our customers.
2.  **Digitization:** Integrating sensors and AI into all hardware lines to provide actionable data.
3.  **Efficiency:** Refining manufacturing processes to reduce waste and energy consumption.

By maintaining a high level of R&D investment relative to our $26,026,305,540 revenue, we ensure that our product offerings remain at the cutting edge of the industrial conglomerate sector, preventing commoditization and supporting our premium pricing strategy.

**Manufacturing and Raw Materials**

Meridian operates a global network of manufacturing facilities, with a significant concentration of high-tech production in Minnesota and the wider Midwestern United States. Our manufacturing strategy focuses on "regionalized production," where we manufacture goods close to the markets where they are sold to reduce logistics costs and exposure to trade volatility.

The primary raw materials used in our operations include steel, aluminum, copper, various resins, and rare earth minerals for our power systems. While we source these materials from a diverse group of suppliers to mitigate risk, we are subject to price fluctuations in the global commodities markets. To combat this, we utilize long-term supply contracts and hedging strategies when appropriate. In 2025, we saw a stabilization of supply chain lead times, though we remain vigilant regarding the geopolitical factors that influence the availability of specialized components.

**Competition**

The industrial conglomerate landscape is intensely competitive. Meridian Technologies Co. competes with both large multi-industry corporations and specialized niche players. We compete on the basis of product reliability, technological innovation, price, and the quality of our after-market service.

In the Precision Infrastructure space, competition is primarily driven by the ability to secure large-scale government tenders. In Specialized Power Systems, the competition focuses on energy efficiency ratings and the speed of integration into existing grids. Our Advanced Material Solutions segment competes on the basis of proprietary chemical formulations and performance specifications. We believe our diversified portfolio and our $26,026,305,540 revenue base provide us with the economies of scale and financial stability necessary to maintain a dominant market position.

**Intellectual Property**

We rely on a combination of patents, trademarks, copyrights, and trade secrets to protect our innovations. As of December 31, 2025, Meridian Technologies Co. holds thousands of active patents worldwide. Our intellectual property strategy is designed to protect our R&D investments and provide us with a competitive moat in high-growth sectors such as structural health monitoring and advanced energy storage. We aggressively defend our IP rights against infringement to maintain the value of our brand and technology.

**Human Capital Resources**

At Meridian Technologies Co., we recognize that our employees are our most valuable asset. As of the end of fiscal year 2025, we employed approximately 74,000 individuals globally. Our human capital strategy is centered on four key areas:

*   **Safety:** The safety of our workforce is our highest priority. We maintain rigorous safety protocols across all manufacturing sites. In 2025, our Total Recordable Incident Rate (TRIR) remained significantly below the industry average for industrial conglomerates.
*   **Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion (DEI):** We are committed to fostering a diverse workforce that reflects the global communities we serve. Our Minnesota headquarters serves as a hub for our leadership development programs aimed at increasing the representation of underrepresented groups in executive and engineering roles.
*   **Training and Development:** We invest heavily in the continuous learning of our staff. Through "Meridian University," our internal training platform, employees have access to thousands of courses on technical skills, leadership, and digital literacy.
*   **Recruitment:** We maintain strong partnerships with leading universities, particularly in Minnesota and the surrounding region, to recruit the next generation of engineers, data scientists, and business leaders.

**Regulatory Matters**

Our operations are subject to extensive and evolving federal, state, and international laws and regulations. These include environmental regulations, labor laws, health and safety standards, and trade policies.

*   **Environmental Regulations:** We are subject to laws governing air emissions, wastewater discharge, and the management of hazardous materials. Meridian is committed to environmental stewardship and has set a goal to reduce our operational greenhouse gas emissions by 50% by 2035. Compliance with these regulations requires significant ongoing capital expenditures, which are factored into our long-term financial planning.
*   **Trade and Export Controls:** Given our global footprint and our work in the defense and aerospace sectors, we must comply with strict export control laws. We maintain a robust compliance department to ensure that our international sales do not violate trade sanctions or national security protocols.
*   **Data Privacy:** As we integrate more digital and IoT solutions into our products, we are increasingly subject to data privacy laws such as the GDPR in Europe and various state-level regulations in the U.S. We have implemented comprehensive data security measures to protect both Company and customer information.

**Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG)**

Meridian Technologies Co. is dedicated to a holistic approach to ESG. We believe that sustainable business practices are fundamentally linked to long-term shareholder value. Our ESG framework is overseen by the Board of Directors and focuses on reducing our environmental footprint, enhancing social equity within our workforce, and maintaining the highest standards of corporate governance.

In 2025, we published our fifth annual Sustainability Report, highlighting our progress in transitioning to renewable energy sources for our manufacturing plants and our initiatives to reduce plastic waste in our Global Logistics Support segment. Our governance structure ensures transparency and accountability, with a focus on ethical conduct and risk management.

**Seasonality**

While the diverse nature of our industrial conglomerate generally mitigates the impact of seasonality, certain segments do experience fluctuations. The Precision Infrastructure segment typically sees higher activity in the second and third quarters of the year, coinciding with the peak construction season in the Northern Hemisphere. Conversely, the Specialized Power Systems segment often experiences increased demand in the fourth quarter as industrial clients utilize their remaining annual capital budgets for grid upgrades and backup power installations.

**Backlog**

As of December 31, 2025, Meridian Technologies Co. maintained a robust backlog of unfilled orders. This backlog represents firm orders from customers for which revenue has not yet been recognized. The strength of our backlog, particularly in the Precision Infrastructure and Specialized Power Systems segments, provides us with significant visibility into our revenue stream for the upcoming fiscal year. We believe the quality and diversity of our backlog reflect the essential nature of our products in the global industrial economy.

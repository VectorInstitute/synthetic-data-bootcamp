**Item 1. Business**

**Introduction**

Amberwood Robotics Co. (“Amberwood,” the “Company,” “we,” “our” or “us”) is a leading high-tech retailer and distributor of automotive replacement parts, maintenance items, and advanced vehicle diagnostic equipment. Founded on the principle of integrating precision technology into the automotive aftermarket, we have grown from a specialized Tennessee-based operation into a premier national provider of automotive solutions. For the fiscal year ended 2025, the Company generated total revenues of $16,591,900,553.

As of the conclusion of fiscal year 2025, we operated an extensive network of retail locations across the United States, with our corporate headquarters located in Tennessee. Our stores serve both DIY (“do-it-yourself”) and DIFM (“do-it-for-me”) customers, providing a comprehensive catalog of products ranging from traditional hard parts—such as engines, batteries, and brake systems—to sophisticated electronic sensors and robotic diagnostic tools designed for modern smart-vehicles.

A core component of our growth strategy is the Amberwood Professional program. This commercial sales initiative provides rapid delivery of precision parts and specialized diagnostic hardware to local and regional repair hubs, fleet operators, and independent service stations. Our professional-grade inventory is supported by our proprietary “AmberLink” platform, an integrated shop management and parts procurement software suite that allows commercial clients to manage high-volume repairs with technical precision.

While we are a leader in the retail space, Amberwood Robotics Co. does not perform automotive repairs or installation services directly. Our business model is centered on the retail and wholesale distribution of products and the technological infrastructure that supports modern vehicle maintenance.

**Human Capital Resources**

At Amberwood Robotics Co., we believe our human capital is our most vital asset. Our culture is built upon a commitment to technical expertise, reliability, and what we call the “Amberwood Standard”: Precision, Service, and Innovation. These values guide our interactions with our customers and our colleagues, ensuring that every individual who walks through our doors receives expert guidance.

As of the end of fiscal year 2025, we employed approximately 128,000 team members. Our workforce is strategically distributed to maximize operational efficiency, with approximately 90% of our employees serving in store-level roles or field supervision. The remaining 10% are dedicated to our advanced distribution centers, automated logistics hubs, and our store support center in Tennessee.

We emphasize a diverse and inclusive workplace where technical skill and customer-centricity are rewarded. We provide competitive compensation and comprehensive benefits packages designed to attract and retain top talent in a competitive retail landscape. We have no collective bargaining agreements in the U.S. and consider our relations with our employees to be excellent.

**Training & Development**

The complexity of modern automotive systems requires a highly skilled workforce. To meet this demand, we invest heavily in the “Amberwood Academy,” our internal training and certification program. This initiative offers career pathways for employees to transition from entry-level sales roles to specialized technical consultants or store management.

Our training curriculum focuses on:
*   **Technical Proficiency:** Extensive education on the latest hybrid and electric vehicle (EV) components and robotic diagnostic tools.
*   **Leadership Excellence:** Advanced management training for district and regional oversight roles.
*   **Customer Experience:** Soft-skills training to ensure that technical knowledge is communicated effectively to DIY customers.

We believe that providing clear avenues for internal promotion is essential to our long-term success. A significant portion of our senior leadership team consists of individuals who began their careers in our retail stores or distribution centers.

**Recognition**

The “Amberwood Standard” is reinforced through a robust recognition system. We celebrate employees who demonstrate exceptional commitment to safety, technical accuracy, and customer satisfaction. Our “Precision Award” is the highest honor bestowed upon employees who solve complex customer challenges or contribute to significant operational improvements. We also recognize milestones in tenure, celebrating the deep institutional knowledge that our long-term team members provide.

**Health and Safety**

The safety of our employees and customers is our paramount concern. Given the nature of automotive retail—which involves handling heavy components and hazardous materials such as batteries and chemicals—we maintain rigorous safety protocols. Our “Safety-First Initiative” involves regular audits of store conditions, comprehensive training on equipment handling, and a proactive reporting system for potential hazards. We are committed to maintaining a workspace that meets or exceeds all federal and state safety regulations.

Our store operations are supported by a state-of-the-art logistics network that ensures high in-stock levels for both common maintenance items and specialized components.

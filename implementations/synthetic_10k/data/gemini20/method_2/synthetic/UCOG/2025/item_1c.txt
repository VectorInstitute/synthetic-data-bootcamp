**Item 1C. Cybersecurity**

**Risk Management and Strategy**

*Program and Framework*

At Amberwood Robotics Co., we recognize that as a technology-integrated retailer, our digital infrastructure is as critical to our success as our physical store footprint. We have developed and implemented a comprehensive cybersecurity risk management program designed to protect the "AmberLink" platform, our proprietary robotic diagnostic tools, and the sensitive data of our 128,000 team members and millions of customers. Our cybersecurity program is grounded in the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (the "NIST CSF"), which provides a structured approach to identifying, protecting, detecting, responding to, and recovering from cybersecurity threats.

Our strategy is built on the principle of "Technical Precision," aligning with the Amberwood Standard. We utilize a multi-layered defense-in-depth architecture that incorporates advanced encryption, multi-factor authentication, and automated endpoint detection and response systems. Given our $15,883,805,319 asset base—much of which is managed through the AmberLink digital ecosystem—maintaining the integrity and availability of our systems is a primary operational priority.

*Assessment and Mitigation*

The Company regularly engages in proactive measures to evaluate the effectiveness of our security controls. These measures include:
*   **Continuous Monitoring:** Our dedicated Security Operations Center (SOC), headquartered at our Store Support Center in Tennessee, monitors our network traffic and system logs in real-time to detect anomalous activity.
*   **Vulnerability Management:** We perform regular automated vulnerability scanning across our 6,314 retail locations and our automated distribution centers.
*   **Threat Hunting and Testing:** We engage independent, third-party cybersecurity experts to perform annual penetration testing and red-team simulations to identify potential weaknesses in our "AmberLink" platform and our corporate network.
*   **Incident Response:** We maintain a formal Cyber Incident Response Plan (CIRP) that outlines specific protocols for containment, eradication, and recovery in the event of a security breach. This plan is tested through regular tabletop exercises involving senior leadership and cross-functional teams.

*Third-Party Risk Management*

As a distributor with a complex global supply chain, we are exposed to risks originating from our vendors and service providers. Our third-party risk management program involves a rigorous security assessment during the procurement process. We evaluate the cybersecurity posture of our software-as-a-service (SaaS) providers, hardware manufacturers, and logistics partners to ensure their standards are commensurate with the NIST CSF. For critical vendors supporting the AmberLink platform, we require regular audits, such as SOC 2 Type II reports, and maintain contractual rights to assess their security controls.

*Education and Training*

We believe that human capital is a vital component of our cybersecurity defense. Leveraging the "Amberwood Academy," we have integrated mandatory cybersecurity awareness training into our curriculum for all 128,000 team members. This training focuses on phishing recognition, secure password practices, and the proper handling of sensitive customer information. Specialized training is provided to our technical consultants who interface with robotic diagnostic hardware to ensure that these advanced tools do not become vectors for unauthorized access to vehicle telematics.

**Cybersecurity Risks**

As of the conclusion of fiscal year 2025, the Company has not experienced a material cybersecurity incident, and we have not disclosed any such incidents in prior filings. However, like other large-scale retailers and distributors, we are subject to constant threats from sophisticated actors, including ransomware attacks, business email compromise, and distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attempts. 

Any significant disruption to our information technology systems could prevent us from fulfilling orders, managing our $4,906,875,511 in inventory, or operating the AmberLink platform that supports our professional sales program. Such an event could lead to a loss of customer trust, significant legal and remediation costs, and a material adverse impact on our $16,591,900,553 annual revenue base. For a more detailed discussion of these risks, please see "Item 1A. Risk Factors—The failure of our 'AmberLink' platform or other critical information technology systems could disrupt our operations."

**Governance**

*Board Oversight*

Cybersecurity is a central component of the Company’s enterprise risk management framework. Reflecting the high-tech nature of our operations, the full Board of Directors maintains primary oversight of cybersecurity risks. This top-level oversight ensures that security initiatives are aligned with the Company’s long-term strategic goals and capital allocation priorities. 

The Board is supported in this role by the Audit Committee. While the full Board focuses on strategic alignment and major risk exposure, the Audit Committee provides detailed oversight of the technical and financial aspects of the program. This includes reviewing the results of internal and external audits, assessing the adequacy of our cybersecurity insurance coverage, and monitoring the Company’s compliance with emerging data privacy regulations.

*Management’s Role*

The execution of our cybersecurity program is led by our Chief Information Security Officer (CISO). Our CISO is a seasoned professional with over two decades of experience in information security, specifically within the retail and industrial automation sectors. The CISO holds advanced certifications, including the Certified Information Systems Security Professional (CISSP), and reports directly to the Chief Information Officer (CIO).

The CISO provides quarterly reports to the Audit Committee and an annual comprehensive briefing to the full Board of Directors. These reports cover:
*   The current threat landscape facing the automotive retail and robotics industries.
*   Progress against the NIST CSF benchmarks.
*   Results of recent penetration tests and vulnerability assessments.
*   Updates on the "Amberwood Academy" security training participation and effectiveness.
*   Status of the security roadmap and planned investments in defensive technologies.

In the event of a significant cybersecurity incident, the CISO is empowered to activate the Cyber Incident Response Team (CIRT) and initiate the communication protocol with the Board and executive leadership to ensure rapid decision-making and transparency. We believe this governance structure ensures that cybersecurity remains a high-priority topic at the most senior levels of Amberwood Robotics Co.

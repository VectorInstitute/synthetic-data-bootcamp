**ITEM 1A. RISK FACTORS**

*Investment in our securities involves a high degree of risk. You should carefully consider the risks and uncertainties described below, together with all of the other information in this Annual Report on Form 10-K, including our consolidated financial statements and related notes, and "Management’s Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations." The risks described below are not the only risks we face. Additional risks and uncertainties not currently known to us or that we currently deem to be immaterial may also materially and adversely affect our business, financial condition, results of operations, or cash flows. In any such case, the trading price of our common stock could decline, and you could lose all or part of your investment.*

**Summary of Risk Factors**

Our business is subject to numerous risks and uncertainties, including those outside of our control, that could cause our actual results to be harmed. These risks include, but are not limited to, the following:

*   **Economic and Industry Risks:** Volatility in the global and domestic economy; fluctuations in interest rates and fuel prices; and the cyclical nature of the automotive retail industry.
*   **Financial and Liquidity Risks:** Our substantial indebtedness, totaling $14,836,983,822 in total liabilities against $302,795,588 in stockholders’ equity; our ability to maintain floorplan financing; and the potential for impairment of our $15,139,779,410 in total assets.
*   **Operational Risks:** Dependence on relationships with vehicle manufacturers (OEMs); volatility in the used vehicle market; and our ability to maintain the "Bluestone Apex" technological advantage.
*   **Technological and Privacy Risks:** Cybersecurity threats to our proprietary data; failures in our omnichannel sales platform; and evolving data privacy regulations in Tennessee and nationally.
*   **Legal and Regulatory Risks:** Changes in automotive franchise laws; environmental liabilities associated with our service and collision centers; and strict compliance requirements for consumer finance and insurance products.

### **Risks Related to Our Substantial Indebtedness and Financial Condition**

**Our high level of indebtedness could adversely affect our financial health and prevent us from fulfilling our obligations.**

As of December 31, 2024, Bluestone Analytics Inc. has total liabilities of $14,836,983,822 and total stockholders’ equity of only $302,795,588. This represents a highly leveraged capital structure. Our significant level of debt could have important consequences for our investors, including:
*   Limiting our ability to obtain additional financing to fund future working capital, capital expenditures, acquisitions, or other general corporate requirements;
*   Requiring a substantial portion of our cash flows from operations to be dedicated to the payment of interest and principal on our indebtedness, thereby reducing the funds available for operations or other purposes;
*   Increasing our vulnerability to general adverse economic and industry conditions; and
*   Limiting our flexibility in planning for, or reacting to, changes in our business and the industry in which we operate.

If our $18,529,777,843 in annual revenue were to decline due to market conditions, our ability to service this $14.8 billion in liabilities would be severely compromised.

**Our thin equity cushion of $302,795,588 makes us highly susceptible to asset devaluations.**

Our total assets are valued at $15,139,779,410. Given that our total stockholders’ equity is $302,795,588, even a minor decrease in the value of our assets—approximately 2%—could result in negative stockholders’ equity. Such a decrease could occur due to a variety of factors, including a sharp decline in the market value of our used vehicle inventory, impairment of goodwill or intangible assets following an acquisition, or a decrease in the value of our real estate holdings in Tennessee and other markets. If our asset value declines significantly, we could violate financial covenants in our credit agreements, which could result in an acceleration of our massive debt obligations.

**We rely heavily on floorplan financing to purchase our vehicle inventory.**

Like most automotive retailers, we fund the purchase of our new and used vehicle inventory through floorplan financing. If our primary lenders were to reduce our credit lines or increase the interest rates associated with these facilities, our ability to maintain the inventory levels necessary to generate our $18.5 billion in revenue would be hampered. A significant portion of our $14,836,983,822 in liabilities is comprised of these short-term, asset-backed borrowings. Any disruption in the credit markets could make it difficult or impossible to refinance these obligations on favorable terms, or at all.

### **Risks Related to Our Industry and Economic Conditions**

**The automotive retail industry is sensitive to economic conditions and consumer spending.**

Our business, particularly our Retail Vehicle Operations segment, is highly dependent on consumer discretionary spending. Economic factors such as inflation, unemployment levels, and consumer confidence levels significantly impact whether individuals choose to purchase new or used vehicles. While our Lifecycle Support and Analytics segment provides some insulation, as consumers may repair rather than replace vehicles during downturns, a major recession would likely lead to a decline in our total revenue of $18,529,777,843 and could materially impact our $15.1 billion asset base.

**Fluctuations in interest rates can reduce our profitability and consumer demand.**

As a company with $14.8 billion in liabilities, we are highly sensitive to interest rate fluctuations. Rising interest rates increase our borrowing costs, particularly for our floorplan financing. Additionally, most of our customers rely on financing to purchase vehicles. As interest rates rise, the monthly cost of a vehicle loan increases, which can deter potential buyers or force them to purchase lower-margin vehicles. This dual impact of higher internal costs and lower consumer demand could compress our margins and reduce our overall profitability.

**Our geographic concentration in Tennessee and the Southeast makes us vulnerable to regional economic shifts and natural disasters.**

While our Tennessee headquarters and regional concentration provide operational efficiencies, they also expose us to localized risks. Economic downturns specific to the Southeastern United States, or natural disasters such as severe storms and flooding common to the region, could disrupt our logistics hub or damage our physical dealership locations. Any prolonged disruption to our Tennessee-based operations could hinder our ability to manage our nationwide data network and satisfy our substantial financial obligations.

### **Risks Related to Our Business Operations and Strategy**

**We are dependent on our relationships with Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs).**

Our Retail Vehicle Operations rely on franchise agreements with various OEMs. These agreements grant us the right to sell new vehicles and provide warranty service. However, OEMs exercise significant control over our operations, including setting facility standards, mandating inventory levels, and determining vehicle allocations. If an OEM were to terminate a franchise agreement, or if a major manufacturer were to experience a prolonged strike or production shortage, our ability to generate revenue would be adversely affected. Furthermore, any move by OEMs toward a direct-to-consumer sales model that bypasses the traditional dealer network could fundamentally threaten our business model.

**Our "Bluestone Apex" analytics engine may fail to accurately predict market trends.**

A core pillar of our strategy is the use of our proprietary "Bluestone Apex" platform to optimize inventory and pricing. This platform analyzes data to manage a significant portion of our $15,139,779,410 in assets. If the algorithms within Bluestone Apex are based on flawed assumptions, or if they fail to account for unprecedented market shifts (such as sudden changes in fuel prices or regulatory shifts toward electric vehicles), we may find ourselves with an oversupply of slow-moving, depreciating inventory. Failure to accurately forecast demand would lead to higher floorplan interest expenses and lower margins, directly impacting our $302.7 million equity base.

**The used vehicle market is highly volatile.**

Our used vehicle operations are a significant component of our revenue and rely on our ability to acquire inventory at prices that allow for a profitable resale. Used vehicle prices are subject to rapid fluctuations based on supply at auctions, the volume of trade-ins, and the availability of new vehicle incentives. If we misjudge the market and overpay for used inventory, we may be forced to sell those vehicles at a loss or write down the value of our inventory, which would negatively impact our financial condition.

**The "Lifecycle Support and Analytics" segment may not grow as expected.**

We have invested heavily in our service, parts, and collision centers to capture the full lifetime value of the vehicle. However, as vehicles become more sophisticated and potentially transition toward electric powertrains, the nature of maintenance and repair will change. Electric vehicles generally require less routine maintenance than internal combustion engines. If we fail to adapt our service capabilities or if the shift to EVs occurs faster than our "Bluestone Apex" engine predicts, our high-margin lifecycle revenue could decline.

### **Risks Related to Technology, Data Privacy, and Cybersecurity**

**A security breach or failure of our IT systems could disrupt our business and damage our reputation.**

As a data-driven company, we collect and store massive amounts of sensitive information, including customer financial data and proprietary market intelligence. Our "Bluestone Apex" engine is a critical intellectual property asset. A major cybersecurity breach could lead to the theft of this data, litigation from affected customers, and significant regulatory fines. Given our high debt-to-equity ratio, the costs associated with a large-scale data breach could exceed our $302,795,588 in stockholders’ equity, threatening our solvency.

**We face increasing risks related to data privacy and protection laws.**

We are subject to evolving data privacy laws, such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and similar emerging frameworks in Tennessee and other states. These laws grant consumers significant rights over their data and impose strict requirements on how we collect and use information. Compliance with these regulations is costly and complex. Any failure to comply could result in substantial penalties and necessitate changes to our "Bluestone Apex" platform that could reduce its effectiveness.

---

### **Risks Related to Legal, Regulatory, and Compliance Matters**

**We are subject to extensive and changing government regulations.**

Our operations are governed by a web of federal, state, and local laws. These include consumer protection laws (such as the Truth in Lending Act), environmental regulations regarding the handling of hazardous materials in our service centers, and state franchise laws. Changes in these laws—such as stricter emissions standards or new restrictions on how we price F&I products—could increase our operating costs or reduce our revenue.

**Environmental liabilities could adversely affect our financial results.**

Our service and collision centers handle various hazardous substances. We are subject to environmental laws that impose strict, and in some cases joint and several, liability for the cost of investigating and cleaning up contaminated properties. Even if we are in compliance with current regulations, the discovery of historical contamination at a newly acquired or existing site could result in significant remediation costs that would negatively impact our $15.1 billion asset value.

**Our F&I operations are subject to intense regulatory scrutiny.**

A significant portion of our profitability is derived from the sale of financing and insurance products. These activities are scrutinized by the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB) and state regulators. If new regulations are enacted that cap the commissions we can earn on these products or change the way they are sold, our margins would be significantly compressed, making it more difficult to support our $14.8 billion liability structure.

### **General Risks Related to Our Common Stock**

**Our stock price may be volatile, and you could lose your entire investment.**

The market price of our common stock is likely to be highly volatile due to our high leverage and the cyclical nature of the automotive industry. With a stockholders' equity of only $302,795,588 against a revenue base of $18,529,777,843, any small shift in operational efficiency or market sentiment can have a magnified effect on our perceived value. Investors should be aware that the high level of debt ($14,836,983,822) means that in a liquidation scenario, stockholders would only be entitled to residual assets after all creditors are paid in full, which could result in zero recovery for equity holders.

**We do not expect to pay dividends in the foreseeable future.**

Given our high level of indebtedness and our strategy of using cash flows to fund acquisitions and technology development, we do not currently anticipate paying any cash dividends on our common stock. Investors seeking current income should look elsewhere, and the return on investment will depend solely on the appreciation of our stock price, which is subject to the numerous risks outlined above.

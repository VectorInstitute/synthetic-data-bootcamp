**ITEM 1C. CYBERSECURITY**

**Risk Management and Strategy**

As a data-driven automotive retailer that leverages the proprietary "Bluestone Apex" analytics platform to manage $15,139,779,410 in total assets, Bluestone Analytics Inc. recognizes that cybersecurity is a fundamental component of our operational resilience and long-term value creation. Our business model, which generated $18,529,777,843 in revenue during fiscal year 2024, is predicated on the seamless integration of digital sales environments, vehicle telematics, and complex financial data. Consequently, the protection of our technological infrastructure and the sensitive consumer data we process is a primary strategic priority.

Our cybersecurity risk management program is integrated into our broader enterprise risk management framework. We have developed and implemented a cybersecurity program designed to identify, assess, and manage material risks from cybersecurity threats. This program is grounded in the NIST Cybersecurity Framework, which provides a structured approach to identifying, protecting, detecting, responding to, and recovering from cyber-related events. Given our headquarters in Tennessee and our decentralized operational structure across various regional dealerships, our strategy focuses on maintaining a centralized security posture that protects both our Tennessee-based data hubs and our localized retail nodes.

The core elements of our cybersecurity strategy include:

*   **Continuous Monitoring and Threat Detection:** We employ a multi-layered defense strategy that includes automated monitoring of our network traffic, endpoint detection and response (EDR) solutions, and real-time analysis of system logs. These tools are specifically tuned to protect the "Bluestone Apex" engine, ensuring the integrity of the data that drives our inventory and pricing decisions.
*   **Third-Party Risk Management:** Our business involves extensive interactions with third parties, including automotive OEMs, financial institutions for our F&I operations, and various software providers. We conduct cybersecurity due diligence on our significant third-party service providers and include security requirements in our contractual agreements to mitigate risks originating from our supply chain.
*   **Incident Response Planning:** We maintain a comprehensive Cybersecurity Incident Response Plan (CIRP) that outlines the protocols for responding to and recovering from potential security breaches. This plan is tested through periodic tabletop exercises involving cross-functional teams from IT, legal, operations, and communications.
*   **Employee Training and Awareness:** We recognize that human error remains a significant vulnerability. Our "Bluestone University" platform includes mandatory cybersecurity training for all employees, focusing on phishing awareness, password hygiene, and the secure handling of customer financial information.

As of the date of this report, Bluestone has not experienced a cybersecurity incident that has materially affected, or is reasonably likely to materially affect, our business strategy, results of operations, or financial condition. However, we remain cognizant that a major security breach could have devastating consequences. With a total stockholders’ equity of only $302,795,588 against liabilities of $14,836,983,822, the costs associated with a large-scale data breach—including regulatory fines, litigation, and the potential loss of our proprietary "Bluestone Apex" intellectual property—could materially impair our solvency and our ability to service our $14.8 billion in debt obligations.

**Governance**

Our Board of Directors (the “Board”) maintains ultimate oversight of the Company's risk management, with cybersecurity serving as a critical focal point of their reviews. Recognizing the technical complexity of modern cyber threats, the Board is supported in this duty by the Audit Committee.

*   **Board of Directors Role:** The full Board receives regular updates on the Company’s cybersecurity posture and the evolving threat landscape. These updates include discussions on strategic investments in security technology, the results of external audits, and the alignment of our cybersecurity initiatives with the NIST Cybersecurity Framework. The Board’s oversight ensures that cybersecurity risk is considered within the context of our $18.5 billion annual operation and our highly leveraged capital structure.
*   **Audit Committee Role:** The Audit Committee is specifically charged with the more granular oversight of our cybersecurity risk management processes. The Committee reviews quarterly reports regarding our security metrics, the results of internal and external vulnerability assessments, and the status of our compliance with evolving data privacy regulations in Tennessee and other states. The Audit Committee also oversees our "Bluestone Apex" security protocols to ensure the algorithms that manage our $4.88 billion in inventory remain protected from tampering or unauthorized access.
*   **Management’s Role:** Our cybersecurity program is led by our Chief Information Security Officer (CISO), who has over two decades of experience in information technology and security within the retail and financial services sectors. The CISO is responsible for the day-to-day implementation of our security policies and reports directly to the Audit Committee on a quarterly basis. Our senior management team, including the CEO and CFO, is involved in the Cybersecurity Steering Committee, which meets monthly to evaluate emerging risks and allocate resources toward high-priority security initiatives.

In the event of a significant cybersecurity incident, management is required to follow an escalation protocol that ensures the CISO, the Audit Committee, and the Board are informed in a timely manner. This protocol facilitates the rapid assessment of the incident's materiality and ensures that disclosure obligations are met in accordance with SEC requirements.

Our governance structure is designed to provide a "top-down" culture of security that reaches every level of our Tennessee-based executive team and our nationwide network of dealerships. By treating cybersecurity as a board-level issue, we ensure that the protection of our $15.1 billion asset base is prioritized alongside our growth and profitability objectives. While we have not disclosed a material cybersecurity incident to date, our governance and risk management frameworks are designed to be proactive and adaptive, reflecting the critical role that technology plays in our $18.5 billion automotive retail ecosystem.

### **Executive Overview**

Bluestone Analytics Inc. (“Bluestone,” the “Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a Tennessee-based transformative force in the automotive retail sector. Our 2024 fiscal year was characterized by the aggressive scaling of our data-centric business model, which integrates traditional automotive dealership operations with our proprietary “Bluestone Apex” analytics platform. We operate in a highly competitive and cyclical Consumer Discretionary environment, specifically within the Automotive Retail sub-industry, where we have successfully leveraged our Tennessee headquarters as a strategic logistics and technological hub to drive significant top-line growth and operational efficiency.

For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, the Company achieved total revenues of $18,529,777,843, representing a milestone in our expansion strategy. This performance was underpinned by robust demand in our Retail Vehicle Operations and an increasing contribution from our high-margin Lifecycle Support and Analytics segment. Our net income for the year reached $2,625,875,247, reflecting our ability to maintain discipline in our cost structure even as we expanded our market footprint.

Our operational philosophy is centered on “Volume, Velocity, and Value.” By utilizing the Bluestone Apex engine, we have optimized our inventory turnover and precision-priced our offerings, which is reflected in our total assets of $15,139,779,410, of which $4,882,231,701 is comprised of inventory. However, our financial strategy in 2024 also involved significant capital allocation toward shareholders, including $3,200,289,873 in share repurchases, which has resulted in a highly leveraged capital structure with total stockholders’ equity of $302,795,588 against total liabilities of $14,836,983,822.

**Key Performance Indicators and 2024 Highlights**

*   **Revenue Growth:** Our total revenue of $18,529,777,843 was driven by a combination of organic growth at existing Tennessee and regional locations and the strategic integration of newly acquired dealerships.
*   **Operating Efficiency:** We achieved an operating income of $4,294,588,789. Our operating margin was bolstered by the successful containment of selling, general, and administrative (“SG&A”) expenses at $5,901,686,575 and a net benefit in other operating expenses of $240,929,582.
*   **Data Integration:** The Bluestone Apex platform now influences over 90% of our used vehicle acquisition and pricing decisions, contributing to our $4.88 billion inventory valuation and reducing the average days-to-turn across our network.
*   **Capital Allocation:** We prioritized returning value to shareholders, deploying $3,200,289,873 into our share repurchase program, funded by our strong cash flow from operations of $3,207,792,907 and strategic financing activities.

### **Critical Accounting Estimates**

The preparation of financial statements in conformity with U.S. GAAP requires management to make estimates and assumptions that affect the reported amounts of assets and liabilities and the disclosure of contingent assets and liabilities at the date of the financial statements. We base our estimates on historical experience, the Bluestone Apex data trends, and various other assumptions we believe to be reasonable under the circumstances.

**Inventory Valuation**

Our inventory, valued at $4,882,231,701 as of December 31, 2024, consists primarily of new and used vehicles, as well as parts and accessories. Inventory is stated at the lower of cost or net realizable value. We utilize the Bluestone Apex engine to monitor real-time market data, auction results, and regional demand shifts, particularly in our core Tennessee markets. If market conditions were to decline precipitously, or if the Apex engine were to miscalculate future demand, we could be required to record significant write-downs. A 1% decrease in the net realizable value of our inventory would result in an approximate $48.8 million charge to cost of revenue.

**Goodwill and Intangible Assets**

As of December 31, 2024, we carried $243,849,920 in goodwill on our balance sheet. This goodwill originated from our historical acquisitions of franchised dealerships and our proprietary technology platform. We perform an annual impairment test, or more frequently if indicators of impairment exist. Our evaluation involves comparing the fair value of our reporting units to their carrying values. Given our thin equity cushion of $302,795,588, any impairment of goodwill could have a disproportionate impact on our financial stability and our compliance with debt covenants.

**Revenue Recognition**

We recognize revenue when control of the promised goods or services is transferred to our customers in an amount that reflects the consideration we expect to be entitled to in exchange for those goods or services. For vehicle sales, this generally occurs at the time of delivery and signing of the final contract. For our Lifecycle Support segment, revenue is recognized as services are performed or parts are delivered. We also earn commissions from third-party lenders and insurance providers (F&I), which are recognized at the time of the vehicle sale, net of estimated chargebacks.

**Income Taxes**

We are subject to income taxes in the United States and specifically in the State of Tennessee. For 2024, our income tax expense was $1,668,713,542 on pretax income of $4,294,588,789, resulting in an effective tax rate of approximately 38.8%. This rate reflects the federal statutory rate, state taxes, and adjustments for permanent differences. Our deferred tax assets and liabilities are measured using enacted tax rates expected to apply to taxable income in the years in which those temporary differences are expected to be recovered or settled.

**Revenue Analysis**

Total revenue for 2024 was $18,529,777,843. This revenue is generated across our two primary segments: Retail Vehicle Operations and Lifecycle Support and Analytics.

*   *Retail Vehicle Operations:* This segment accounted for the vast majority of our total revenue. New vehicle sales remained steady, supported by our strong franchise relationships, while used vehicle sales saw significant volume increases. Our Bluestone Apex engine allowed us to identify "hot" inventory in the Tennessee and Southeast corridors, moving vehicles to locations where they could command higher transaction prices.
*   *Lifecycle Support and Analytics:* This segment contributed significantly to our gross profit. The high-margin nature of our service, parts, and collision operations provided a critical buffer against the tighter margins seen in new vehicle retail. Our analytics division also generated revenue through third-party data licensing to fleet managers.

**Gross Profit and Cost of Revenue**

Our cost of revenue for 2024 was $8,574,432,061, resulting in a consolidated gross profit of $9,955,345,782, or a gross margin of 53.7%. This high margin level for an automotive retailer is primarily attributable to:
1.  The higher concentration of high-margin service and F&I revenue.
2.  The efficiency gains from our Bluestone Apex platform, which reduced the cost of acquisition for used inventory.
3.  Our strategic focus on the Lifecycle Support segment, where margins are inherently higher than in vehicle hardware sales.

**Operating Expenses**

*Selling, General and Administrative (SG&A):* SG&A expenses were $5,901,686,575 for the year. This included personnel costs for our dealership staff, corporate overhead at our Tennessee headquarters, marketing and advertising expenditures, and the ongoing maintenance and development costs for our IT infrastructure. As a percentage of total revenue, SG&A was 31.8%, reflecting our continuous efforts to scale our administrative functions through automation and centralized data processing.

*Other Operating Expenses (Income), net:* We recorded a net benefit of $240,929,582 in this category. This was primarily driven by non-recurring gains on the sale of several non-core real estate parcels in the Southeast and a favorable settlement related to a legacy supply chain dispute. This net gain directly increased our operating income for the period.

**Operating Income and Net Income**

Operating income was $4,294,588,789 for the fiscal year 2024. Notably, the company recorded $0 in interest expense for the period, as our interest-bearing obligations were managed through high-velocity cash rotations and our financing strategy was structured to capitalize interest or offset it through our investment activities.

After accounting for an income tax expense of $1,668,713,542, we achieved a net income of $2,625,875,247. This represents a net margin of 14.2%, which we believe is industry-leading and validates our data-integrated retail model.

### **Segment Performance**

**Retail Vehicle Operations**

The Retail Vehicle Operations segment remains our largest revenue driver. In 2024, the segment focused on optimizing the mix between new and used units. While the new vehicle market faced some supply chain constraints early in the year, our used vehicle operations reached record volumes. The integration of trade-in data with our Bluestone Apex engine allowed us to offer competitive valuations to customers while maintaining a target spread on resale. The F&I component of this segment also performed exceptionally well, as we utilized data to tailor financing and protection products to individual customer profiles, increasing the attachment rate of our high-margin VPPs.

**Lifecycle Support and Analytics**

This segment, though smaller in revenue than Retail Operations, provided a substantial portion of our $4.29 billion operating income. Our service centers in Tennessee and surrounding states saw increased throughput due to our predictive maintenance alerts. Our "Bluestone Certified" parts business also expanded its reach to independent repair shops, leveraging our scaled purchasing power with OEMs to maintain a competitive pricing advantage. The Analytics division continued to refine its B2B offerings, providing residual value forecasting that is becoming an industry standard for regional banks and insurance providers.

**Balance Sheet and Capital Structure**

Our balance sheet as of December 31, 2024, reflects a highly leveraged position:

*   **Total Assets:** $15,139,779,410. This is anchored by our property, plant, and equipment (net) of $5,426,829,563 and our inventory of $4,882,231,701. Other significant assets include $3,759,249,838 in "Other Assets," which includes our deferred tax assets and regional operating leases.
*   **Total Liabilities:** $14,836,983,822. Our largest liabilities are our long-term debt of $7,665,483,624 and our accounts payable of $6,290,996,188. The accounts payable figure primarily reflects our obligations to OEMs and other suppliers for our vehicle inventory.
*   **Total Stockholders’ Equity:** $302,795,588. Our equity base is thin relative to our total assets, largely due to our negative retained earnings of $3,625,544,811, which is a direct result of our historical and current-year share repurchase programs. Additional paid-in capital stands at $1,637,364,163, and accumulated other comprehensive income is a loss of $345,901,316.

This capital structure indicates that we are highly reliant on the continued generation of strong cash flows from operations to service our $14.8 billion in liabilities. Any significant disruption to our $18.5 billion revenue stream could put pressure on our ability to maintain this level of leverage.

**Long-Term Debt and Floorplan Financing**

Our long-term debt of $7.67 billion consists of a mix of senior secured notes and revolving credit facilities. These facilities are used to fund our acquisitions and general corporate purposes. Additionally, our accounts payable of $6.29 billion includes significant amounts related to our floorplan financing. We rely on these facilities to maintain our $4.88 billion inventory. Most of these facilities are asset-backed and are sensitive to interest rate fluctuations. Although we recorded $0 in interest expense in 2024, future periods may see significant interest costs if we are unable to maintain our current cash rotation efficiency or if market rates rise substantially.

### **Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk**

We are exposed to various market risks, including interest rate risk and credit risk.

**Interest Rate Risk**

Our primary interest rate risk relates to our $14.8 billion in total liabilities, specifically our floorplan financing and revolving credit facilities. While we effectively managed interest costs to zero in the current year through our cash management strategies, a 100-basis point increase in interest rates on our variable-rate debt could increase our annual interest expense by approximately $80 million, assuming constant debt levels. We use the Bluestone Apex engine to model these scenarios and adjust our inventory levels and cash reserves accordingly.

**Credit Risk**

We are exposed to credit risk through our accounts receivable, which totaled $531,186,271 as of December 31, 2024. These receivables are primarily due from OEMs for incentives and warranty work, and from third-party financial institutions for F&I commissions. We perform regular credit evaluations of these entities and maintain an allowance for doubtful accounts. Historically, our credit losses have been minimal due to the high credit quality of our OEM and financial institution partners.

**Used Vehicle Market Risk**

Our used vehicle inventory of approximately $4.88 billion is subject to market value fluctuations. A sudden decrease in used vehicle prices, perhaps driven by an influx of new vehicle supply or an economic downturn, could lead to inventory impairments. We mitigate this risk by maintaining a high velocity of inventory turnover, guided by the real-time data analytics provided by our Tennessee-based Bluestone Apex team.

### **Conclusion**

Bluestone Analytics Inc. delivered a strong financial performance in 2024, characterized by $18,529,777,843 in revenue and $2,625,875,247 in net income. Our success is a testament to the power of our data-driven model and our strategic positioning within the Tennessee automotive market. However, our highly leveraged balance sheet, with $14.8 billion in liabilities against $302.8 million in equity, requires us to maintain a rigorous focus on operational excellence and cash flow generation. As we look forward to 2025, we remain committed to refining the Bluestone Apex engine and scaling our Lifecycle Support and Analytics segment to drive long-term value for our shareholders.

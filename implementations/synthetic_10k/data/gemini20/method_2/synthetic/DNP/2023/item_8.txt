To the Stockholders and Board of Directors of Silverlake Materials Holdings, Inc.

**Opinion on the Financial Statements**

We have audited the accompanying consolidated balance sheets of Silverlake Materials Holdings, Inc. (the "Company") as of December 31, 2023, and the related consolidated statements of operations, comprehensive income, stockholders’ equity, and cash flows for the fiscal year then ended, and the related notes (collectively referred to as the "financial statements"). In our opinion, the financial statements present fairly, in all material respects, the financial position of the Company as of December 31, 2023, and the results of its operations and its cash flows for the year then ended, in conformity with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States of America.

**Basis for Opinion**

These financial statements are the responsibility of the Company’s management. Our responsibility is to express an opinion on the Company’s financial statements based on our audit. We are a public accounting firm registered with the Public Company Accounting Oversight Board (United States) ("PCAOB") and are required to be independent with respect to the Company in accordance with the U.S. federal securities laws and the applicable rules and regulations of the Securities and Exchange Commission and the PCAOB.

We conducted our audit in accordance with the standards of the PCAOB. Those standards require that we plan and perform the audit to obtain reasonable assurance about whether the financial statements are free of material misstatement, whether due to error or fraud. Our audit included performing procedures to assess the risks of material misstatement of the financial statements, whether due to error or fraud, and performing procedures that respond to those risks. Such procedures included examining, on a test basis, evidence regarding the amounts and disclosures in the financial statements. Our audit also included evaluating the accounting principles used and significant estimates made by management, as well as evaluating the overall presentation of the financial statements. We believe that our audit provides a reasonable basis for our opinion.

NOTES TO CONSOLIDATED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS**

**Note 1. Basis of Presentation and Summary of Significant Accounting Policies**

*Description of Business*
Silverlake Materials Holdings, Inc. ("Silverlake" or the "Company") is a Colorado-headquartered global provider of sustainable metal, glass, and plastic container solutions. The Company operates 84 manufacturing facilities across North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific. Silverlake’s primary operations involve the design, manufacture, and sale of high-performance packaging for the beverage, food, spirits, and pharmaceutical sectors.

*Basis of Consolidation*
The consolidated financial statements include the accounts of Silverlake and its wholly owned subsidiaries. All intercompany transactions and balances have been eliminated in consolidation. These financial statements have been prepared in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States ("U.S. GAAP").

*Use of Estimates*
The preparation of financial statements requires management to make estimates and assumptions that affect the reported amounts of assets and liabilities and the disclosure of contingent assets and liabilities at the date of the financial statements, and the reported amounts of revenues and expenses during the reporting period. Actual results could differ from those estimates. Significant estimates include the valuation of goodwill, the useful lives of property, plant, and equipment, inventory obsolescence, and the valuation of deferred tax assets.

*Cash and Cash Equivalents*
The Company considers all highly liquid investments with an original maturity of three months or less at the date of purchase to be cash equivalents. As of December 31, 2023, the Company held $510,971,135 in cash and equivalents.

*Accounts Receivable*
Accounts receivable are stated at net realizable value. The Company maintains an allowance for doubtful accounts for estimated losses resulting from the inability of its customers to make required payments. As of December 31, 2023, accounts receivable, net, was $1,159,406,365.

*Inventory*
Inventory is valued at the lower of cost or net realizable value, with cost determined using the first-in, first-out (FIFO) or average cost methods. Inventory consists of raw materials (primarily aluminum, resins, and glass-making minerals), work-in-progress, and finished goods. As of December 31, 2023, total inventory was $1,333,872,520.

*Property, Plant and Equipment*
Property, plant, and equipment ("PP&E") are stated at cost less accumulated depreciation. Depreciation is calculated using the straight-line method over the estimated useful lives of the assets, which range from 15 to 40 years for buildings and 3 to 20 years for machinery and equipment. In 2023, the Company invested $953,392,382 in capital expenditures to upgrade its 84 facilities.

*Goodwill and Intangible Assets*
Goodwill represents the excess of the purchase price over the fair value of net assets acquired in business combinations. Goodwill is not amortized but is tested for impairment annually or more frequently if events indicate potential impairment. As of December 31, 2023, goodwill was $3,231,087,238. Intangible assets, net, totaled $829,983,809 and primarily consist of customer relationships and proprietary technology, which are amortized over their estimated useful lives.

**Note 2. Revenue Recognition**

The Company recognizes revenue in accordance with ASC 606, *Revenue from Contracts with Customers*. Revenue is recognized when control of the promised goods is transferred to the customer, which typically occurs upon shipment from the Company’s manufacturing facilities.

The Company’s revenue of $9,578,919,389 for the year ended December 31, 2023, was derived from its three reportable segments. Many of the Company’s contracts include "pass-through" provisions for raw material and energy price volatility. Revenue is recorded net of sales taxes, returns, and allowances.

**Note 3. Income Taxes**

Income tax expense for the fiscal year 2023 was $629,373,266, representing an effective tax rate of 56.6%. This rate differs from the U.S. statutory rate of 21% primarily due to:
*   Higher tax rates in certain foreign jurisdictions where the Company operates a portion of its 84 facilities.
*   The impact of non-deductible expenses and valuation allowances on deferred tax assets in specific markets.
*   State and local taxes, net of federal benefit.

The Company records deferred tax assets and liabilities for the expected future tax consequences of temporary differences between the financial reporting and tax bases of assets and liabilities.

**Note 4. Debt and Financing Arrangements**

As of December 31, 2023, the Company’s total liabilities included $712,818,390 in short-term debt and $5,225,110,902 in long-term debt. The Company’s debt is primarily comprised of senior secured notes and revolving credit facilities.

During 2023, the Company’s interest expense was $0. This was the result of interest capitalization related to several major multi-year expansion projects at the Colorado Global Innovation Center and European manufacturing hubs, which offset the gross interest incurred during the period. The Company used $415,220,000 for net debt repayments during the year as part of its deleveraging strategy.

**Note 5. Stockholders' Equity**

*Common Stock*
The Company has authorized 500,000,000 shares of common stock, $0.01 par value. As of December 31, 2023, 115,428,800 shares were issued and outstanding.

*Share Repurchases*
In 2023, the Company repurchased $2,403,229 of its common stock in open market transactions. These shares were subsequently retired.

*Accumulated Other Comprehensive Loss*
As of December 31, 2023, the accumulated other comprehensive loss ("AOCI") was $755,256,617. This balance is primarily composed of cumulative foreign currency translation adjustments resulting from the Company's significant international footprint.

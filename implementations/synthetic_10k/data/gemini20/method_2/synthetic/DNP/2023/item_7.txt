### **Overview**

Silverlake Materials Holdings, Inc. ("Silverlake," "the Company," "we," "us," or "our") is a Colorado-headquartered global provider of sustainable packaging solutions, operating within the Metal, Glass & Plastic Containers sub-industry of the Materials sector. We specialize in the design and manufacture of high-performance containers for the beverage, food, spirits, and pharmaceutical industries. As of December 31, 2023, we operated a network of 84 manufacturing facilities across North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific, employing approximately 22,000 people.

Our business strategy is centered on "Sustainability-Driven Innovation," focusing on the transition toward a circular economy. This involves significant investments in material science to increase the use of infinitely recyclable aluminum and glass, as well as developing high-recycled-content plastics. During the fiscal year ended December 31, 2023, we generated total revenues of $9,578,919,389 and achieved a net income of $481,982,008.

### **2023 Performance Highlights**

The fiscal year 2023 was characterized by robust demand for our premium packaging solutions, particularly in the Silverlake Metal Beverage Packaging and Silverlake Glass Solutions segments. Despite global macroeconomic volatility and fluctuating energy costs, our operational excellence initiatives and strategic "pass-through" pricing mechanisms allowed us to maintain healthy margins. Key financial metrics for the year include:

*   **Total Revenues:** $9,578,919,389
*   **Operating Income:** $1,111,355,274
*   **Net Income:** $481,982,008
*   **Cash Flow from Operating Activities:** $1,688,569,008
*   **Capital Expenditures:** $953,392,382

Our balance sheet remains a focal point of management’s strategy, as we balance significant growth-oriented assets—including $3,231,087,238 in goodwill and $14,633,397,493 in total assets—against a substantial liability profile of $12,028,898,687. Our liquidity position remained stable, ending the year with $510,971,135 in cash and equivalents.

### **Macroeconomic Trends and Industry Factors**

The materials and packaging industry is currently influenced by several key factors that impacted our 2023 results and are expected to continue influencing our performance:

1.  **Raw Material Volatility:** The costs of aluminum sheet, soda ash, silica sand, and petroleum-based resins are subject to global market fluctuations. We utilized long-term supply contracts and pricing adjustments to manage the impact of these costs.
2.  **Energy Intensity:** Our manufacturing processes, particularly in glass melting and metal curing, are energy-intensive. While energy costs in North America remained relatively stable, our European operations faced higher electricity and natural gas prices, which we addressed through efficiency upgrades and renewable energy sourcing.
3.  **Sustainability Mandates:** Increasing regulatory pressure in Europe (via the PPWR) and consumer demand in North America for "plastic-free" or "high-PCR" (post-consumer recycled) options have driven our R&D spend and capital allocation toward sustainable technologies.
4.  **Interest Rates and Inflation:** While our reported interest expense for the fiscal year 2023 was $0 due to the specific structure of our current debt instruments and capitalization of interest on major projects, we remain sensitive to the broader inflationary environment which impacts labor and logistical costs.

#### **Revenues**
Total revenues for 2023 were $9,578,919,389. This performance was driven by strong volume growth in our Metal Beverage Packaging segment, where the global shift toward aluminum cans over single-use plastics continued to accelerate. Revenue was also bolstered by "pass-through" price increases implemented to offset the rising cost of raw materials such as aluminum and soda ash. Geographically, North America remained our largest market, followed by Europe, where premium glass solutions for the spirits industry saw steady demand.

#### **Operating Expenses**
*   **Selling, General and Administrative (SG&A):** SG&A expenses were $412,616,782, or approximately 4.3% of total revenues. This reflects our commitment to maintaining a lean corporate structure and leveraging AI-driven supply chain optimizations to manage our 84 global facilities.
*   **Research and Development (R&D):** We invested $37,958,479 in R&D during 2023. These efforts were primarily focused at our Global Innovation Center in Colorado, targeting "lightweighting" technologies to reduce material consumption and the development of proprietary coatings that enhance the shelf life of beverages without the use of controversial chemicals.
*   **Other Operating Expenses:** This category, totaling $8,016,988,854, primarily consists of our cost of goods sold (COGS), including raw materials, direct labor, and energy costs. The scale of these expenses reflects the high-volume, capital-intensive nature of the metal and glass container manufacturing process. In 2023, these costs were impacted by increased logistics and freight rates globally.

#### **Operating Income**
Operating income was $1,111,355,274 for the year. Our operating margin was approximately 11.6%, supported by high capacity utilization across our 84 plants and successful cost-containment measures within our "Zero Waste" initiative.

#### **Income Tax Expense**
Income tax expense was $629,373,266 for the fiscal year 2023. This reflects an effective tax rate of approximately 56.6%. This rate is significantly higher than the statutory U.S. federal rate due to several factors, including the impact of non-deductible expenses, valuation allowances in certain foreign jurisdictions, and the geographic mix of earnings across the 84 facilities we operate worldwide.

### **Segment Discussion**

While we report consolidated results, our management evaluates performance across three primary segments: Metal Beverage Packaging, Glass Solutions, and Specialty Plastic & Closures.

**Silverlake Metal Beverage Packaging**
This segment was the primary driver of our $9,578,919,389 in revenue. The segment benefited from the continued premiumization of the craft beer and energy drink markets. Demand for 12oz and 16oz aluminum cans remained resilient. Operating margins in this segment were supported by the high recyclability of aluminum, which allows us to utilize more cost-effective recycled aluminum sheet when available.

**Silverlake Glass Solutions**
Our glass segment focused on high-end flint and amber glass containers. This segment faced higher energy costs due to the continuous operation of furnaces, but successfully offset these costs through multi-year contracts with blue-chip spirits producers. The segment continues to invest in furnace technology that incorporates higher percentages of recycled cullet.

**Silverlake Specialty Plastic & Closures**
This segment focuses on high-barrier containers and child-resistant closures. In 2023, we saw increased demand from the pharmaceutical and personal care sectors. We are actively transitioning this segment toward PCR (post-consumer recycled) resins to align with our sustainability strategy.

### **Critical Accounting Estimates**

The preparation of financial statements in conformity with U.S. GAAP requires management to make estimates and assumptions that affect the reported amounts of assets and liabilities. We believe the following accounting estimates are the most critical to understanding our financial condition.

**Goodwill and Intangible Assets**
As of December 31, 2023, we carried $3,231,087,238 in goodwill and $829,983,809 in net intangible assets. We test goodwill for impairment annually, or more frequently if events indicate a potential impairment. Our impairment test involves comparing the fair value of our reporting units to their carrying values. The fair value is estimated using a combination of the income approach (discounted cash flows) and the market approach. Given the significant balance, a downturn in the global packaging market or a rise in discount rates could lead to a material impairment charge.

**Inventory Valuation**
Our inventory of $1,333,872,520 consists of raw materials (aluminum, resins, glass cullet), work-in-progress, and finished containers. Inventory is stated at the lower of cost or net realizable value. We regularly review inventory for obsolescence or slow-moving items, particularly in our Specialty Plastic & Closures segment where product designs change more frequently.

**Revenue Recognition**
We recognize revenue when control of the promised goods is transferred to our customers, typically upon shipment from one of our 84 facilities. Revenue is measured as the amount of consideration we expect to receive in exchange for those goods, net of returns and allowances. Because many of our products are manufactured under long-term contracts with specific customer designs, we must accurately estimate variable consideration and potential returns.

**Income Taxes**
We are subject to income taxes in the U.S. and numerous foreign jurisdictions. Significant judgment is required in determining our global provision for income taxes. The $629,373,266 tax expense for 2023 reflects our current estimate of these obligations. We evaluate the realizability of our deferred tax assets on a jurisdictional basis and establish valuation allowances when it is more likely than not that some portion of the assets will not be realized.

### **Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk**

**Raw Material Price Risk**
We are exposed to price volatility in aluminum and plastic resins. We manage this through "pass-through" clauses in our customer contracts, which allow us to adjust our selling prices based on changes in certain material indices. For materials where pass-through is not possible, we may use derivative financial instruments to hedge our exposure.

**Foreign Currency Exchange Risk**
With operations in Europe and Asia-Pacific, a portion of our $9,578,919,389 in revenue is denominated in Euro, British Pound, and other currencies. Fluctuations in these currencies against the U.S. Dollar can impact our reported results. We utilize forward contracts to mitigate some of this risk, but we remain exposed to long-term structural shifts in exchange rates.

**Interest Rate Risk**
While our interest expense was $0 in 2023, our $12,028,898,687 in total liabilities includes debt that may be subject to variable interest rates in future periods. We monitor the interest rate environment closely to determine the appropriate mix of fixed and floating-rate debt for our capital structure.

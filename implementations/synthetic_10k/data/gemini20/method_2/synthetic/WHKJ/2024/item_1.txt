**ITEM 1. BUSINESS**

**General**

Stonegate Materials Corp. (“Stonegate,” the “Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a global leader in the design, manufacture, and distribution of a wide array of paper and plastic packaging solutions. Incorporated and headquartered in Ireland, Stonegate serves a diverse set of end-markets including food and beverage, healthcare, personal care, household products, and industrial applications. Our mission is to provide innovative, sustainable packaging materials that enhance our customers' brands while minimizing environmental impact.

As of the fiscal year ended 2024, Stonegate Materials Corp. generated total revenues of $20,489,294,899. Our global operations are supported by an expansive network of manufacturing facilities, distribution centers, and research and development hubs located across North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific, and Latin America. Our strategic focus remains on leveraging our materials science expertise to transition the global economy toward circular packaging solutions.

**Our Operating Segments**

We manage our business through three primary reportable segments: Industrial Paper Packaging, Consumer Plastic Solutions, and Sustainable Feedstock & Recycling. These segments are structured based on the nature of the products provided, the production processes employed, and the markets served.

*Industrial Paper Packaging*

The Industrial Paper Packaging segment is our largest business unit by revenue, focusing on the production of containerboard, corrugated containers, and high-strength paper-based protective packaging. This segment serves as the backbone of global e-commerce and logistics, providing durable and recyclable shipping solutions for everything from heavy machinery to consumer electronics. 

Our containerboard mills utilize both virgin fiber and recycled content to produce linerboard and medium. These materials are then converted in our network of corrugated box plants into custom-engineered packaging. We specialize in "right-size" packaging technology, which reduces waste and shipping costs for our customers. In fiscal 2024, this segment’s contributions were vital to achieving our consolidated revenue of $20,489,294,899, reflecting the high demand for fiber-based shipping materials in the digital economy.

*Consumer Plastic Solutions*

The Consumer Plastic Solutions segment designs and manufactures rigid and flexible plastic packaging for the consumer-packaged goods ("CPG") industry. Our products include high-barrier films for food preservation, medical-grade blisters and vials, and lightweight bottles for beverages and home care products. 

We utilize a variety of polymers, including polyethylene (PE), polypropylene (PP), and polyethylene terephthalate (PET). A significant portion of our research and development budget within this segment is dedicated to the development of monomaterial films—plastic structures made of a single polymer type that are significantly easier to recycle than traditional multi-layer laminates. We also incorporate post-consumer recycled ("PCR") resins into our plastic products to meet the evolving sustainability goals of our global customer base.

*Sustainable Feedstock & Recycling*

Stonegate operates one of the world's most sophisticated recovery networks for paper and plastic. The Sustainable Feedstock & Recycling segment collects, sorts, and processes secondary materials. These materials are either used internally by our other segments to manufacture new packaging or sold to third-party manufacturers. 

By controlling our own feedstock, we mitigate the volatility of the global commodities market and ensure a steady supply of high-quality recycled content. Our recycling facilities utilize advanced spectroscopic sorting technology to separate different grades of paper and polymer types, ensuring that the purity of our recycled outputs meets the rigorous standards required for food-contact packaging.

**Strategic Priorities**

Stonegate’s long-term value creation strategy is built upon four pillars: Innovation in Materials Science, Operational Excellence, Portfolio Optimization, and Sustainability Leadership.

*Innovation in Materials Science:* We believe that the future of packaging lies in the molecular engineering of materials. We are investing heavily in bio-based polymers and aqueous coatings that can replace traditional plastic barriers in paper packaging. Our goal is to make all Stonegate products either fully recyclable, reusable, or compostable by 2030.

*Operational Excellence:* To maintain our competitive edge, we employ standardized manufacturing processes across our global footprint. This includes the implementation of Stonegate Production System (SPS), a proprietary methodology based on lean manufacturing principles designed to reduce downtime, minimize energy consumption, and maximize throughput.

*Portfolio Optimization:* We continuously evaluate our business units to ensure they align with our high-margin, high-growth objectives. This involves strategic acquisitions of specialized packaging firms and the divestiture of non-core assets that do not meet our internal benchmarks for return on invested capital.

*Sustainability Leadership:* As a materials company based in Ireland, we are subject to some of the world’s most stringent environmental regulations. We view this as a competitive advantage. By leading the industry in carbon reduction and circularity, we position ourselves as the partner of choice for multinational brands facing pressure from consumers and regulators to reduce their plastic footprint.

**Manufacturing and Supply Chain**

Stonegate operates 242 manufacturing facilities worldwide. Our manufacturing process begins with the procurement of raw materials, including wood fiber, plastic resins, and chemicals. 

In our paper operations, wood fiber is sourced through long-term supply contracts with managed forests and through the purchase of recycled paper. Our mills convert these fibers into large rolls of paper, which are then shipped to our converting plants. At the converting plants, the paper is corrugated, printed, and cut into final packaging products.

In our plastic operations, we utilize extrusion, blow molding, and injection molding processes. We maintain a diverse supplier base for our resin requirements to avoid over-reliance on any single provider. Given the global nature of our $20,489,294,899 revenue base, we utilize a sophisticated logistics network to manage the flow of goods, balancing localized production for regional customers with global export hubs for specialty components.

**Raw Materials and Energy**

The primary raw materials used in our business are wood fiber (virgin and recycled) and various plastic resins. The prices for these materials are subject to significant cyclicality and are influenced by global supply and demand balances, energy costs, and geopolitical events.

Energy is a significant component of our manufacturing costs, particularly in our paper mills. We utilize a mix of electricity, natural gas, and biomass. In several of our European facilities, we have installed co-generation plants that allow us to produce our own electricity and steam, increasing our energy efficiency and reducing our carbon intensity. We also engage in financial hedging activities to manage our exposure to fluctuations in energy and raw material prices.

**Competition**

The paper and plastic packaging industry is highly competitive and fragmented. We compete with large multinational corporations, as well as regional and local manufacturers. Competition is based on several factors, including price, product quality, service, innovation, and sustainability profile.

In the Industrial Paper Packaging segment, competition is primarily driven by cost efficiency and the ability to provide a reliable supply of high-volume containers. In the Consumer Plastic Solutions segment, competition is more focused on technical capabilities, such as barrier properties and aesthetic design, as well as the ability to incorporate recycled content into the final product. Stonegate differentiates itself through its integrated business model, which allows us to offer both paper and plastic solutions, providing an unbiased approach to our customers' packaging challenges.

**Research and Development**

Our research and development (“R&D”) efforts are centered on creating the next generation of sustainable materials. We operate three "Centers of Excellence" in Ireland, the United States, and Singapore. These facilities are staffed by materials scientists, chemists, and packaging engineers.

Recent R&D milestones include the development of a fully recyclable paper-based bottle and a proprietary method for depolymerizing plastic waste into its original monomers. We believe our commitment to R&D is essential for maintaining our market share and achieving the scale necessary to support our $20,489,294,899 in annual revenues.

**Intellectual Property**

Stonegate relies on a combination of patents, trademarks, trade secrets, and confidentiality agreements to protect our intellectual property. We hold thousands of patents globally covering our manufacturing processes, product designs, and material formulations. While our patents are an important asset, we believe that our technical expertise, customer relationships, and brand reputation are equally vital to our competitive position.

**Human Capital Resources**

As of December 31, 2024, Stonegate employed approximately 48,000 people worldwide. Our employees are our most valuable asset, and we are committed to providing a safe, inclusive, and rewarding work environment.

*Health and Safety:* The safety of our employees is our top priority. We maintain rigorous safety protocols at all our manufacturing sites and track metrics such as Total Recordable Incident Rate (TRIR) and Days Away, Restricted or Transferred (DART). We strive for a "Zero Harm" culture.

*Diversity and Inclusion:* We believe that a diverse workforce fosters innovation. We have implemented programs to increase the representation of women and underrepresented groups in our leadership ranks. Our Irish headquarters serves as a hub for our global diversity initiatives, ensuring that our corporate culture reflects the global markets we serve.

*Training and Development:* We invest in the continuous learning of our employees through Stonegate Academy, an internal platform providing training in technical skills, leadership, and sustainability.

**Government Regulation**

Our operations are subject to extensive environmental, health, and safety laws and regulations in every jurisdiction where we operate. These include regulations governing air emissions, water discharges, waste management, and the chemicals used in our manufacturing processes.

In the European Union, we are particularly impacted by the Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation (PPWR) and the Single-Use Plastics Directive. These regulations mandate higher recycling rates and the reduction of certain plastic formats. We have proactively adjusted our product portfolio to comply with these requirements. In the United States, we are subject to the regulations of the Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) and the Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA). Compliance with these regulations requires significant capital expenditures and ongoing operating costs, which are factored into our long-term financial planning.

**Operational Details and Market Dynamics**

*Geography and Global Reach*

While headquartered in Ireland, Stonegate Materials Corp. is inherently global. Our revenue of $20,489,294,899 is geographically diversified, with North America accounting for approximately 42%, Europe for 35%, and the remainder coming from high-growth markets in Asia and Latin America. This diversification helps insulate the Company from regional economic downturns and allows us to capture growth in emerging economies where the middle class is expanding and demand for packaged consumer goods is rising.

In Ireland, we benefit from a sophisticated legal and financial infrastructure, as well as access to a highly skilled workforce within the European Union. Our Irish operations serve as the central hub for our European logistics and our global treasury functions.

*Detailed Segment Analysis: Industrial Paper Packaging*

The Industrial Paper Packaging segment operates across the entire value chain of fiber-based packaging. Our mills are equipped with state-of-the-art paper machines that produce high-performance linerboard. We have transitioned several of our mills to utilize 100% recycled fiber, aligning with the "Circular Economy" mandates prevalent in our European markets.

The converting side of this segment produces everything from standard shipping containers to complex point-of-purchase displays. We utilize high-speed flexographic and digital printing to provide our customers with vibrant, brand-aligned packaging. The rise of e-commerce has necessitated the development of "Frustration-Free Packaging," which we design to be easy to open while providing maximum protection during the rigors of the small-parcel delivery chain.

*Detailed Segment Analysis: Consumer Plastic Solutions*

In our plastic operations, we focus on high-value applications where the unique properties of plastic—such as barrier protection, clarity, and durability—are essential. For example, our healthcare packaging provides sterile barriers for surgical instruments and moisture-sensitive medications. In the food sector, our multi-layer barrier films significantly extend the shelf life of proteins and produce, reducing global food waste—a key sustainability metric.

We are a leader in the development of "Tethered Caps" and other design modifications required by the EU Single-Use Plastics Directive. By integrating these features into our high-volume production lines, we help our customers maintain regulatory compliance without sacrificing performance or consumer convenience.

*Detailed Segment Analysis: Sustainable Feedstock & Recycling*

This segment acts as a strategic hedge for the entire corporation. By collecting over 6 million tons of paper and plastic annually, we secure a significant portion of our internal needs for recycled content. Our recycling operations include large-scale Material Recovery Facilities (MRFs) where we process municipal and industrial waste streams.

We are currently investing in "Chemical Recycling" technologies. Unlike traditional mechanical recycling, which can degrade the quality of plastic over time, chemical recycling breaks plastic down into its basic chemical building blocks. This allows for the creation of "virgin-quality" recycled plastic that is suitable for sensitive applications like infant formula packaging or medical devices.

**Sustainability and Environmental Stewardship**

Sustainability is not merely a department at Stonegate; it is integrated into our core business strategy. Our $20,489,294,899 in revenue is increasingly derived from products that offer a superior environmental profile compared to traditional alternatives.

*Climate Action:* We have set science-based targets to reduce our Scope 1 and Scope 2 greenhouse gas emissions by 40% by 2030, using 2020 as a base year. We are achieving this through investments in energy efficiency, the transition to renewable energy sources, and the modernization of our vehicle fleet.

*Water Stewardship:* Packaging manufacturing, particularly paper production, is water-intensive. We have implemented closed-loop water systems in our mills, which allow us to recycle and reuse water multiple times before it is treated and returned to the environment.

*Waste Reduction:* We are committed to a "Zero Waste to Landfill" goal for all our manufacturing sites. By finding secondary markets for our manufacturing byproducts—such as using lime mud from our paper mills as a soil conditioner for local farmers—we reduce our environmental footprint and create additional value streams.

### **Overview**

Stonegate Materials Corp. (“Stonegate,” the “Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a leading global provider of sustainable packaging solutions, specializing in paper and plastic products for a diverse array of end-markets. Headquartered in Ireland and operating 242 manufacturing facilities worldwide, we provide essential packaging for the food and beverage, healthcare, personal care, and industrial sectors. Our business is organized into three reportable segments: Industrial Paper Packaging, Consumer Plastic Solutions, and Sustainable Feedstock & Recycling.

Fiscal year 2024 was characterized by resilient demand across our core segments, offset by significant volatility in input costs and a complex global macroeconomic environment. We achieved total revenues of $20,489,294,899, reflecting our market-leading position and the successful implementation of pricing actions intended to mitigate inflationary pressures. However, our margins were compressed by elevated costs of revenue, which totaled $18,183,605,081 for the year. Despite these headwinds, Stonegate maintained a strong liquidity position, ending the year with $640,245,230 in cash and equivalents and generating $1,567,004,669 in cash from operating activities.

Our strategic focus remains on the transition toward circularity. We are investing heavily in materials science to develop recyclable and compostable alternatives to traditional packaging. During 2024, we continued to optimize our portfolio, which is reflected in our "other operating expenses" line item as a net credit of $1,092,277,444, primarily driven by the strategic divestiture of certain non-core assets and the realization of gains on asset disposals.

### **Key Trends Affecting Our Business**

Several macro-level trends influenced our financial performance in 2024 and are expected to continue impacting our results:

1.  **Raw Material and Energy Volatility:** The costs of wood fiber and plastic resins (PE, PP, and PET) represent the largest component of our $18,183,605,081 cost of revenue. During 2024, we experienced periodic supply chain constraints and price spikes in energy, particularly in our European operations, where natural gas prices remained sensitive to geopolitical tensions.
2.  **Sustainability and Regulation:** Increasing regulatory pressure, such as the EU Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation (PPWR), is driving a shift toward fiber-based packaging and higher recycled content requirements. This trend benefits our Industrial Paper Packaging and Sustainable Feedstock & Recycling segments but requires significant R&D and capital investment in our Consumer Plastic Solutions segment.
3.  **E-commerce Growth:** The continued expansion of global e-commerce continues to drive demand for our corrugated containers and protective paper packaging. This growth is a primary driver of our revenue base in North America and Europe.
4.  **Global Economic Conditions:** Interest rate environments and currency fluctuations impact our $23,528,234,266 liability structure and the translation of our international earnings into U.S. dollars. With 35% of our revenue generated in Europe, the Euro-to-Dollar exchange rate remains a critical factor in our reported results.

#### **Revenues**
For the fiscal year 2024, Stonegate recorded total revenues of $20,489,294,899. This revenue performance was supported by strong volume demand in our Industrial Paper Packaging segment, which benefited from a rebound in global manufacturing and sustained e-commerce activity. In our Consumer Plastic Solutions segment, revenue was bolstered by high-value applications in the healthcare and food preservation sectors. Geographically, North America accounted for approximately 42% of our revenue, while Europe contributed 35%, with the remainder coming from Asia-Pacific and Latin America.

#### **Cost of Revenue**
Cost of revenue for fiscal 2024 was $18,183,605,081, representing approximately 88.7% of total revenues. The high cost of revenue relative to sales reflects the intensive nature of our manufacturing processes and the significant impact of raw material inflation. We saw higher prices for virgin wood pulp and recycled linerboard, as well as increased costs for chemical additives used in our high-barrier plastic films. Logistics and freight costs also remained elevated throughout the first half of the year, though they began to stabilize in the fourth quarter.

#### **Selling, General and Administrative (SG&A) Expenses**
SG&A expenses were $2,435,429,988 in 2024, or approximately 11.9% of total revenues. These costs include corporate overhead, R&D expenditures related to our "Centers of Excellence" in Ireland and abroad, and the administrative costs associated with managing our 48,000-person workforce. We also incurred significant information technology costs as we modernized our enterprise resource planning (ERP) systems across our global footprint to improve supply chain visibility.

#### **Other Operating Expenses (Income)**
We recorded a net credit of $1,092,277,444 in other operating expenses. This substantial gain was primarily attributable to the execution of our portfolio optimization strategy, which included the sale of non-core industrial assets and the settlement of a favorable insurance claim related to a prior-year facility disruption. This credit was a significant driver of our operating income for the period.

#### **Operating Income**
Operating income for 2024 was $962,537,274. While our gross margins were pressured by the $18,183,605,081 in cost of revenue, the $1,092,277,444 in other operating income and disciplined management of SG&A helped us maintain a positive operating margin.

#### **Interest Expense**
For fiscal year 2024, the Company reported zero interest expense on the income statement. This is primarily due to the capitalization of interest related to major long-term construction projects and the structure of our internal financing during the reporting period.

#### **Income Tax Expense**
Income tax expense was $691,862,293 on pretax income of $962,537,274, resulting in an effective tax rate of approximately 71.9%. This rate is significantly higher than the statutory rate in Ireland, primarily due to the impact of non-deductible expenses, valuation allowances on certain deferred tax assets in foreign jurisdictions, and the tax impact of the $1,092,277,444 gain reported in other operating income.

#### **Net Income**
As a result of the factors above, Stonegate Materials Corp. realized net income of $270,674,981 for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024.

### **Segment Results of Operations**

#### **Industrial Paper Packaging**
Our Industrial Paper Packaging segment serves as the primary driver of our $20,489,294,899 revenue base. In 2024, this segment focused on meeting the demand for sustainable shipping solutions. We observed a shift in customer preference toward "right-size" packaging, which reduced material usage but required more complex engineering. Segment performance was impacted by the high cost of recycled fiber, though our integrated recycling network helped mitigate some of these costs.

#### **Consumer Plastic Solutions**
In this segment, we focus on high-barrier and medical-grade packaging. Revenues remained stable as our CPG and healthcare customers maintained consistent demand for specialized films and bottles. The segment's R&D team achieved a major milestone in 2024 with the launch of our first 100% PCR-content healthcare vial. However, the segment faced headwinds from the EU Single-Use Plastics Directive, which required capital expenditures to modify production lines for tethered closures.

#### **Sustainable Feedstock & Recycling**
This segment operates as both an internal supplier and a third-party seller of secondary materials. In 2024, the segment processed over 6 million tons of material. While the market prices for recycled commodities were volatile, the segment provided a strategic hedge for our Industrial Paper Packaging segment, ensuring a consistent supply of feedstock amid global shortages.

### **Critical Accounting Policies and Estimates**

The preparation of our consolidated financial statements requires us to make estimates and judgments that affect the reported amounts of assets, liabilities, revenues, and expenses. We base our estimates on historical experience and on various other assumptions that we believe to be reasonable under the circumstances. Actual results may differ from these estimates.

**Goodwill and Intangible Assets**
Our $6,727,882,081 in goodwill is tested for impairment at least annually. This process requires significant judgment, including the estimation of future cash flows and the determination of appropriate discount rates. A decline in the projected performance of our Consumer Plastic Solutions segment, for instance, could lead to a material impairment of this asset.

**Revenue Recognition**
We recognize revenue when control of the promised goods is transferred to our customers, typically upon shipment or delivery. Our $20,489,294,899 in revenue is recorded net of estimated rebates, discounts, and returns, which require management to make estimates based on historical trends and current contract terms.

**Inventory Valuation**
Inventory is valued at the lower of cost or net realizable value. Given the volatility in resin and fiber prices, we must constantly assess the carrying value of our $2,944,239,093 in inventory to ensure it reflects current market conditions.

**Income Taxes**
We are subject to income taxes in numerous jurisdictions, with our primary tax residency in Ireland. The calculation of our $691,862,293 tax expense involves complex interpretations of tax laws and the assessment of the recoverability of deferred tax assets.

### **Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk**

**Commodity Price Risk:**
We are exposed to price fluctuations in wood fiber, plastic resins, and energy. We use a combination of indexed customer contracts and financial hedging instruments to manage this risk. A 10% increase in resin prices, if not passed through to customers, would have a significant impact on our $18,183,605,081 cost of revenue.

**Foreign Currency Exchange Risk:**
Because we report in U.S. dollars but generate 35% of our revenue in Europe (primarily in Euros) and have significant operations in other regions, we are subject to translation and transaction risk. We manage this through natural hedges—matching local currency revenues with local currency costs—and through the use of forward exchange contracts.

**Interest Rate Risk:**
Our $11,609,870,992 in total debt includes both fixed and floating rate instruments. While our interest expense for 2024 was effectively zero due to capitalization and internal structures, future fluctuations in interest rates could impact our cost of borrowing and the valuation of our pension liabilities.

### **Outlook**

Looking ahead to fiscal 2025, Stonegate Materials Corp. remains committed to our four strategic pillars: Innovation, Operational Excellence, Portfolio Optimization, and Sustainability Leadership. We expect demand for our products to remain stable, though we anticipate continued pressure from raw material costs. Our focus will be on further integrating our Sustainable Feedstock & Recycling segment to secure high-quality recycled content, which is essential for meeting both regulatory requirements and the sustainability goals of our global customers.

With a robust balance sheet featuring $38,216,064,255 in total assets and a disciplined approach to capital allocation, we believe we are well-positioned to navigate the evolving packaging landscape. We will continue to evaluate strategic M&A opportunities that can enhance our technical capabilities and help us maintain our leadership in the materials sector. Our goal remains the creation of long-term value for our shareholders while leading the transition to a more circular and sustainable global economy.

**ITEM 1. BUSINESS**

**Overview**

Stonegate Systems Holdings, Inc. (“Stonegate,” the “Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a leading multi-line insurance and financial services holding company incorporated under the laws of the State of Delaware and headquartered in New York, New York. Since our inception, we have established ourselves as a cornerstone of the financial services sector, providing a diverse array of insurance products and services to individual, commercial, and institutional clients across the globe.

For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025, Stonegate Systems Holdings, Inc. generated total revenues of $18,321,342,217. Our operations are characterized by a disciplined approach to underwriting, a sophisticated risk management framework, and a commitment to long-term value creation for our shareholders. We operate through a decentralized structure that empowers our various business units to respond with agility to local market conditions while benefiting from the capital strength, investment expertise, and technological infrastructure of the parent organization.

As a multi-line insurer, our portfolio is intentionally diversified to mitigate the impact of localized economic downturns or specific catastrophic events. Our business is organized into four primary reportable segments: Commercial Property & Casualty, Personal Insurance Solutions, Global Life and Annuity, and Stonegate Reinsurance Services. Each segment contributes to a balanced revenue stream, with our total consolidated revenue of $18,321,342,217 reflecting the scale and reach of our integrated platform.

**Our Business Segments**

***Commercial Property & Casualty (CP&C)***

Our Commercial Property & Casualty segment provides a broad range of insurance products to small businesses, mid-market enterprises, and large multinational corporations. This segment is our largest by premium volume and is a significant driver of our $18,321,342,217 in total revenue. We offer specialized coverage tailored to specific industry verticals, including construction, manufacturing, technology, healthcare, and professional services.

Key products within CP&C include:
*   **General Liability:** Protection against third-party claims for bodily injury and property damage.
*   **Commercial Property:** Coverage for physical assets, including buildings, equipment, and inventory, against perils such as fire, windstorm, and theft.
*   **Workers' Compensation:** Mandatory coverage providing medical benefits and wage replacement to employees injured in the course of employment.
*   **Professional Liability:** Specialized "Errors and Omissions" (E&O) and "Directors and Officers" (D&O) coverage for corporate leadership and professional practitioners.
*   **Specialty Lines:** Including cyber risk insurance, environmental liability, and marine and aviation coverage.

Our CP&C segment utilizes advanced predictive analytics to price risk accurately and maintain a competitive combined ratio. We distribute these products primarily through a network of independent brokers, regional agencies, and specialized wholesale producers.

***Personal Insurance Solutions (PIS)***

The Personal Insurance Solutions segment focuses on providing essential protection to individuals and families. This segment leverages our "Stonegate Direct" digital platform alongside traditional agency channels to offer a seamless customer experience.

Key products include:
*   **Automobile Insurance:** Coverage for physical damage and liability for private passenger vehicles.
*   **Homeowners Insurance:** Protection for primary residences, condominiums, and renters, including personal property and liability coverage.
*   **Specialty Personal Lines:** Coverage for high-value items such as jewelry, fine art, and recreational vehicles (boats, motorcycles).

PIS relies heavily on our proprietary "Sentinel" underwriting engine, which incorporates real-time data feeds to adjust pricing based on shifting risk profiles. This segment is a critical component of our $18,321,342,217 revenue base, providing stable, recurring cash flows.

***Global Life and Annuity (GLA)***

Our Global Life and Annuity segment provides financial security and retirement planning products. We aim to help our clients manage the risks of mortality and longevity through a suite of protected-growth and income-generating solutions.

Key products include:
*   **Term and Whole Life Insurance:** Providing death benefits and, in the case of whole life, cash value accumulation for beneficiaries.
*   **Fixed and Variable Annuities:** Retirement savings vehicles that offer tax-deferred growth and various payout options, including guaranteed lifetime income.
*   **Group Benefits:** Life and disability insurance programs offered through employers.

The GLA segment manages a significant investment portfolio, where the "spread" between investment returns and credited interest rates to policyholders serves as a primary source of earnings.

***Stonegate Reinsurance Services (SRS)***

SRS operates as a global provider of reinsurance capacity to other insurance companies. By assuming a portion of the risks written by primary insurers, SRS helps stabilize the broader insurance market while earning premium income that contributes to our total revenue of $18,321,342,217.

SRS provides both treaty and facultative reinsurance across property, casualty, and specialty lines. Our reinsurance operations are managed with a strict focus on capital preservation and are supported by high-grade collateral and retrocessional protections.

**Our Strategy**

Stonegate’s primary objective is to deliver industry-leading risk-adjusted returns while maintaining the highest standards of financial solvency. Our strategy is built upon four strategic pillars:

1.  **Underwriting Excellence and Discipline:** We prioritize profitability over market share. Our underwriters are empowered with state-of-the-art analytical tools but are required to adhere to rigorous internal standards. We continuously monitor our aggregate exposure to catastrophic events (CAT) and adjust our risk appetite based on evolving climate data and economic forecasts.
2.  **Digital Transformation and Innovation:** We are investing heavily in the "Stonegate NextGen" initiative, a multi-year digital transformation project designed to automate claims processing, enhance customer self-service capabilities, and utilize artificial intelligence (AI) for more precise risk graduation. This focus on technology is aimed at reducing our expense ratio and improving our competitive position in a rapidly evolving marketplace.
3.  **Capital Optimization:** We manage our capital dynamically, allocating resources to the business lines with the highest expected returns on equity (ROE). Our $18,321,342,217 revenue figure allows us the scale to self-fund significant growth initiatives while maintaining a robust dividend policy and engaging in opportunistic share repurchases.
4.  **Strategic M&A and Partnership:** We actively seek to expand our footprint through the acquisition of high-quality insurance portfolios and agencies that complement our existing lines of business. We focus on "bolt-on" acquisitions that can be integrated into the Stonegate infrastructure to realize immediate cost and revenue synergies.

**Investment Operations**

The management of our investment portfolio is central to our business model. As an insurance company, we collect premiums in advance of paying claims, creating a "float" that we invest to generate additional income. Our investment strategy is governed by our Investment Committee and focuses on capital preservation, liquidity, and long-term yield.

As of December 31, 2025, our investment portfolio is primarily comprised of:
*   **Fixed Maturity Securities:** Including U.S. Treasuries, municipal bonds, and high-investment-grade corporate bonds. This represents the core of our portfolio, intended to match the duration of our insurance liabilities.
*   **Equity Securities:** A diversified portfolio of common and preferred stocks aimed at providing long-term capital appreciation.
*   **Alternative Investments:** Including private equity, real estate, and hedge funds, which offer non-correlated returns to the broader public markets.

The income generated from this portfolio, combined with our underwriting results, constitutes the $18,321,342,217 in total revenues reported for the fiscal year. We maintain a conservative investment posture, with a significant majority of our fixed-income assets rated "A" or higher by major credit rating agencies.

**Competition**

The insurance and financial services industry is highly competitive. We compete with a broad range of domestic and international insurers, reinsurers, and financial services firms. Our competitors include:
*   **Global Multi-line Insurers:** Large organizations with similar geographic and product breadth.
*   **Specialty Carriers:** Niche players that focus on specific risks or industries.
*   **Direct-to-Consumer Insurtechs:** Technology-driven startups that utilize low-overhead models to offer competitive pricing in personal lines.
*   **Captive Insurance Companies:** Self-insurance vehicles formed by large corporations.

We compete based on several factors, including financial strength ratings (e.g., A.M. Best, S&P, Moody’s), pricing, product features, reputation, quality of service, and the strength of our distribution networks. Our ability to report $18,321,342,217 in revenue in such a competitive environment is a testament to our brand equity and operational efficiency.

**Regulation**

Stonegate Systems Holdings, Inc. and its subsidiaries are subject to extensive regulation and supervision in the jurisdictions in which they operate. In the United States, insurance is primarily regulated at the state level.

***State Regulation***

Our U.S. insurance subsidiaries are licensed and regulated by the insurance departments of the states in which they are domiciled and where they conduct business. These regulations are designed primarily to protect policyholders rather than shareholders or creditors. State insurance laws and regulations generally cover:
*   **Solvency Standards:** Minimum capital and surplus requirements, often based on Risk-Based Capital (RBC) formulas.
*   **Rate and Form Filing:** Requirements to file and, in many cases, obtain approval for insurance rates and policy forms.
*   **Investment Restrictions:** Limitations on the types and amounts of investments that insurance companies can hold.
*   **Market Conduct:** Regulations governing sales practices, advertising, claims handling, and underwriting.
*   **Dividend Limitations:** Restrictions on the ability of insurance subsidiaries to pay dividends to the parent holding company without prior regulatory approval.

The New York Department of Financial Services (NYDFS) serves as the lead regulator for several of our key subsidiaries, given our headquarters' location.

***Holding Company Acts***

As a holding company, Stonegate is subject to the Insurance Holding Company System Acts in various states. These laws require us to register with state insurance departments and provide periodic disclosure regarding our capital structure, ownership, and intercompany transactions (such as service agreements and tax-sharing arrangements). Transactions between Stonegate and its insurance subsidiaries must be fair and reasonable and may require prior notice or approval.

***International Regulation***

Our international operations are subject to the laws and regulations of the countries in which they operate, including oversight by the Prudential Regulation Authority (PRA) and the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) in the United Kingdom, and various European Union regulatory bodies under the Solvency II framework. Solvency II imposes stringent capital requirements and risk management standards across our European entities.

***Cybersecurity and Data Privacy***

Given the sensitive nature of the personal and financial information we handle, we are subject to increasingly rigorous data privacy and cybersecurity regulations. These include the NYDFS Cybersecurity Regulation (23 NYCRR 500), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) in Europe. Compliance with these regulations requires significant ongoing investment in our information technology infrastructure and internal controls.

**Seasonal Impacts**

Our business can be subject to seasonal fluctuations. For example, our Commercial and Personal Property lines may experience higher claims volume during the hurricane season in the Atlantic (typically June through November) or during severe winter weather events in the Northern Hemisphere. Additionally, our GLA segment often sees increased annuity sales activity in the fourth quarter as clients engage in year-end financial planning. However, these seasonal variations generally do not have a disproportionate impact on our total annual revenue of $18,321,342,217.

**Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG)**

Stonegate is committed to sustainable business practices. We recognize that climate change poses a significant risk to the insurance industry, particularly through the increased frequency and severity of weather-related events. We have integrated climate risk assessments into our underwriting and investment processes.

Our "Green Stonegate" initiative focuses on:
*   **Sustainable Underwriting:** Offering premium discounts for "green" buildings and renewable energy projects.
*   **Responsible Investment:** Increasing our allocation to "green bonds" and ESG-rated funds within our investment portfolio.
*   **Operational Footprint:** Reducing our corporate carbon footprint through energy-efficient office spaces and a flexible hybrid work model that reduces employee commuting.

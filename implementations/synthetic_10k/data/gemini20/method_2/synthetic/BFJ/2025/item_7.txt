### **Executive Overview**

Stonegate Systems Holdings, Inc. (“Stonegate,” the “Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a New York-based multi-line insurance and financial services holding company. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025, our operations were defined by a commitment to underwriting discipline, the continued rollout of our “Stonegate NextGen” digital transformation initiative, and a focus on capital optimization through strategic share repurchases.

During the 2025 fiscal year, Stonegate achieved total revenues of $18,321,342,217 and net income of $2,160,945,770. Our performance reflects the inherent strength of our diversified business model, which spans Commercial Property & Casualty (“CP&C”), Personal Insurance Solutions (“PIS”), Global Life and Annuity (“GLA”), and Stonegate Reinsurance Services (“SRS”).

Our financial position remains robust, with total assets of $125,286,265,226 and total stockholders’ equity of $32,947,209,984 as of December 31, 2025. Despite a challenging macroeconomic environment characterized by inflationary pressures on claims costs and market volatility impacting our investment portfolio, our consolidated results demonstrate the efficacy of our “Sentinel” underwriting engine and our ability to generate significant cash flow from operations, which totaled $2,423,959,314 for the year.

**Key Financial Highlights for Fiscal Year 2025:**
*   Total revenues of $18,321,342,217.
*   Net income of $2,160,945,770, or approximately 11.8% of total revenue.
*   Operating cash flows of $2,423,959,314, driven by strong premium collections and investment income.
*   Execution of $4,361,348,895 in share repurchases, reflecting our commitment to returning excess capital to shareholders.
*   Total assets of $125,286,265,226, supported by a cash and equivalents balance of $1,140,871,605.

#### **Revenue Analysis**

Consolidated revenue for the year ended December 31, 2025, was $18,321,342,217. This revenue is primarily derived from insurance premiums, policy fees, and net investment income generated from our $125,286,265,226 asset base.

*   **Premiums and Policy Fees:** The majority of our revenue was generated through our four primary business segments. In our CP&C and PIS segments, we saw stable policy retention rates, bolstered by the integration of the “Sentinel” underwriting engine, which allowed for more precise risk graduation and competitive pricing.
*   **Net Investment Income:** As an insurer, we manage a significant portfolio of investments. The income from these investments is a critical component of our $18,321,342,217 revenue figure. Throughout 2025, we navigated a fluctuating interest rate environment that affected the yields on our fixed-maturity securities.

#### **Operating Expenses**

Total operating expenses for 2025 were $15,286,748,433, consisting of selling, general and administrative (SG&A) expenses and other operating expenses.

*   **Selling, General and Administrative (SG&A):** SG&A expenses were $3,039,298,451, representing 16.6% of total revenue. These costs include compensation and benefits for our approximately 14,200 employees, commissions paid to our independent broker and agency network, and technology expenditures related to the “Stonegate NextGen” initiative. We continue to focus on expense management, aiming to leverage our digital transformation to reduce the administrative burden of policy issuance and claims intake.
*   **Other Operating Expenses:** Other operating expenses, which primarily consist of policyholder benefits and claims losses, totaled $12,247,449,982. This represents the single largest cost for the Company, emphasizing the importance of our underwriting discipline. During 2025, these expenses were impacted by “social inflation” and rising costs for construction and medical services, which increased the average severity of claims in our CP&C and PIS segments.

#### **Interest Expense**

Interest expense for the year was $299,219,392. This cost is associated with our $6,402,885,077 in long-term debt. We utilize long-term debt as part of our capital structure to optimize our weighted average cost of capital while maintaining the flexibility to fund strategic initiatives.

#### **Income Tax Expense**

Our income tax expense for fiscal year 2025 was $574,428,622 on pretax income of $2,735,374,392. This results in an effective tax rate of approximately 21.0%. This rate is consistent with the U.S. federal statutory rate, adjusted for state taxes in New York and other jurisdictions, as well as tax-exempt interest income from our municipal bond holdings.

### **Segment Results of Operations**

We monitor the performance of our four reportable segments to evaluate the health of our diversified business model.

#### **Commercial Property & Casualty (CP&C)**
The CP&C segment remains the cornerstone of our revenue generation. In 2025, the segment focused on high-margin specialty lines, including cyber risk and professional liability. The “Sentinel” system has been instrumental in identifying emerging risks in the mid-market enterprise sector, allowing us to adjust premiums ahead of the market. However, this segment felt the impact of rising reinsurance costs, which influenced our net retention strategies.

#### **Personal Insurance Solutions (PIS)**
In the PIS segment, the “Stonegate Direct” platform saw increased adoption, contributing to the stability of our $18,321,342,217 revenue base. While automobile and homeowners lines are subject to intense price competition, our use of real-time data feeds enabled us to maintain a balanced combined ratio despite the volatility of localized weather events.

#### **Global Life and Annuity (GLA)**
The GLA segment benefited from an aging demographic seeking guaranteed lifetime income products. Sales of fixed and variable annuities remained steady. The segment’s profitability is closely tied to the “spread” between the returns on our $125,286,265,226 in assets and the interest credited to policyholders. In 2025, we managed this spread through a conservative asset-liability matching strategy.

#### **Stonegate Reinsurance Services (SRS)**
SRS provided critical capacity to the global insurance market. While SRS contributes significantly to our overall revenue, it is also the segment most exposed to catastrophic volatility. In 2025, SRS’s performance was supported by a lack of singular, massive global catastrophes, though we remain vigilant regarding the aggregate impact of mid-sized events.

### **Critical Accounting Estimates**

The preparation of financial statements in conformity with U.S. GAAP requires management to make estimates and assumptions that affect the reported amounts. The following are the estimates we consider most critical to an understanding of our financial condition and results of operations.

#### **Reserves for Losses and Loss Adjustment Expenses**
A significant portion of our $85,936,170,165 in other liabilities relates to reserves for unpaid losses. These reserves represent estimates of the ultimate cost of all reported and unreported claims. The process of estimating these reserves is inherently uncertain and involves complex actuarial projections. Assumptions regarding medical cost inflation, legal trends, and the frequency of catastrophic events are used to determine the adequacy of these reserves. A change in these assumptions could materially impact our net income of $2,160,945,770.

#### **Valuation of Investments**
With $120,477,604,676 in other assets, primarily investments, the valuation of these instruments is critical. While many of our securities are valued based on quoted market prices, our alternative investments and certain illiquid fixed-income securities require the use of significant judgment and unobservable inputs.

#### **Goodwill Impairment Testing**
We evaluate our $2,540,919,699 in goodwill for impairment annually, or more frequently if indicators of impairment exist. This process involves estimating the fair value of our reporting units using discounted cash flow models and market multiples.

#### **Recoverability of Deferred Tax Assets**
We assess the need for a valuation allowance against our deferred tax assets based on the likelihood of realizing future tax benefits. Our assessment includes projections of future taxable income and the evaluation of tax-planning strategies.

### **Market Risk**

Stonegate is exposed to various market risks, primarily interest rate risk, credit risk, and equity price risk.

*   **Interest Rate Risk:** Our fixed-maturity investment portfolio is sensitive to changes in interest rates. A rise in rates generally decreases the market value of these assets, as reflected in our negative AOCI of $3,401,258,681.
*   **Credit Risk:** We are exposed to the risk that issuers of the securities in our $125,286,265,226 asset base will default. We mitigate this through a conservative investment policy focusing on high-investment-grade instruments.
*   **Inflation Risk:** Inflation impacts the cost of claims in our CP&C and PIS segments. We manage this risk by incorporating inflationary expectations into our “Sentinel” underwriting models and adjusting premiums accordingly.

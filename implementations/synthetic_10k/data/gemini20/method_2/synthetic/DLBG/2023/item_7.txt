**ITEM 7. MANAGEMENT’S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS OF FINANCIAL CONDITION AND RESULTS OF OPERATIONS**

*The following discussion and analysis of our financial condition and results of operations should be read in conjunction with our consolidated financial statements and related notes included elsewhere in this Annual Report on Form 10-K. This discussion contains forward-looking statements that involve risks and uncertainties. Our actual results could differ materially from those discussed below. Factors that could cause or contribute to such differences include, but are not limited to, those identified below and those discussed in the section titled "Risk Factors" under Part I, Item 1A of this report.*

### **Overview**

Ironwood Resources Corp. (“Ironwood,” the “Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a global leader in the financial services sector, headquartered in Illinois. We operate at the intersection of capital allocation and asset safekeeping, providing a comprehensive suite of services through our two primary business segments: Investment Management and Custody & Trust Services. Our business model is designed to generate durable fee-based revenue and net interest income by leveraging our "Ironwood Nexus" technology platform and our deep expertise in fiduciary oversight.

For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2023, Ironwood generated total revenues of $8,038,599,569 and net income of $2,299,536,868. Our performance in 2023 was characterized by the resilience of our integrated platform amidst a complex global macroeconomic environment, marked by shifting interest rate regimes and volatility in equity and fixed-income markets.

As an Illinois-based institution with a global footprint, we manage a massive balance sheet that reflects our role as a premier custodian. As of December 31, 2023, our total assets were $160,771,991,387, primarily driven by other assets totaling $153,681,110,474, which largely consist of client-related balances and investment securities held to support our custody and trust operations. Our capital position remains robust, with total stockholders’ equity of $14,117,000,176.

### **2023 Market Environment and Strategic Focus**

The global financial markets in 2023 presented both challenges and opportunities for Asset Management & Custody Banks. Central bank policies, particularly in the United States, led to a higher-for-longer interest rate environment, which influenced client behavior and asset valuations.

*   **Market Volatility:** Fluctuations in global equity and bond markets impacted our Assets Under Management (AUM) and Assets Under Custody or Administration (AUC/A). Despite these fluctuations, our diversified product suite—ranging from fundamental equity to alternative investments—allowed us to maintain a stable revenue base.
*   **Interest Rate Dynamics:** The interest rate environment was a primary driver of our interest expense, which totaled $4,823,159,741 for the year. This expense reflects the cost of maintaining the $146,654,991,211 in total liabilities, which include client deposits and other short-term funding instruments essential to our custody operations.
*   **Technological Integration:** We continued to invest in "Ironwood Nexus," our proprietary technology platform. This platform is critical for driving operational efficiencies and providing clients with real-time data analytics. These investments are reflected in our capital expenditures of $198,384,173 during the year.

#### **Total Revenues**
For the fiscal year 2023, total revenues were $8,038,599,569. Our revenue streams are diversified across our two segments:

1.  **Investment Management Fees:** Derived from managing assets for institutional and individual clients. These fees are typically based on a percentage of AUM. Performance fees, which are generally recognized in the fourth quarter, also contributed to this total.
2.  **Custody and Trust Service Fees:** Earned through the safekeeping, settlement, and administration of client assets. These fees are driven by AUC/A levels and transaction volumes.
3.  **Net Interest Income:** While not broken out separately from total revenue in the primary income statement summary, the interplay between our gross yields on "Other Assets" ($153,681,110,474) and our "Interest Expense" ($4,823,159,741) is a critical component of our profitability.

The scale of our revenue in 2023 demonstrates the stickiness of our client relationships and our ability to attract new flows into high-margin products, such as our alternative investment strategies and ESG-integrated solutions.

#### **Interest Expense**
Interest expense for 2023 was $4,823,159,741. This represents the interest paid on our $146,654,991,211 in total liabilities. Given our role as a custody bank, a significant portion of our liabilities consists of interest-bearing client deposits. The elevated interest rate environment throughout 2023 necessitated higher payouts to maintain deposit stability and remain competitive in the market. Efficient management of this expense is a key priority for our treasury and risk management teams in Illinois.

#### **Pretax Income and Operating Margin**
Pretax income for the year was $3,215,439,828. Our pretax margin (pretax income divided by total revenues) was approximately 40.0%. This margin reflects the inherent scalability of our fee-based business model. Despite the substantial interest expense, our ability to control other operational costs (leveraging "Ironwood Nexus" for automation) allowed us to retain a significant portion of our gross revenue as pretax profit.

#### **Income Tax Expense**
Income tax expense was $915,902,960 for the fiscal year 2023. This results in an effective tax rate of approximately 28.5%. This rate reflects the blended statutory rates of the various jurisdictions in which we operate, including U.S. federal and state taxes (most notably in Illinois) and international taxes from our global operations in Europe and Asia.

#### **Net Income**
Net income for 2023 was $2,299,536,868. This result underscores the strength of Ironwood's diversified financial platform. The net income figure includes the impact of non-cash charges, such as depreciation and amortization of $1,087,044,472, which is associated with our property, plant, and equipment and intangible assets.

### **Segment Discussion**

#### **Investment Management**
Our Investment Management segment focused on navigating volatile market conditions in 2023. Equity and Fixed Income strategies remained the core of our AUM, but we saw significant growth in our Alternative Investments sub-segment. The demand for private credit and real estate solutions increased as institutional investors sought yields that were less correlated with public markets. Our "Ironwood Academy" helped train our advisors to better position these complex products for our global client base.

#### **Custody & Trust Services**
The Custody & Trust Services segment benefited from high transaction volumes and the stability of AUC/A. Our global custody network, spanning over 100 markets, continued to provide essential infrastructure for our clients. We successfully integrated several large-scale fund administration mandates in 2023, which bolstered our fee-based revenue. Our securities lending program remained a key differentiator, helping clients generate additional returns on their assets while providing us with incremental service fees.

### **Financial Condition, Liquidity, and Capital Resources**

As of December 31, 2023, Ironwood maintained a strong financial position, supported by a large asset base and a solid equity foundation.

#### **Balance Sheet Analysis**
*   **Total Assets:** Our total assets reached $160,771,991,387. The largest component, "Other Assets," at $153,681,110,474, includes our investment securities portfolio, client receivables, and other financial instruments. We also hold $5,787,758,603 in cash and equivalents, providing ample immediate liquidity.
*   **Property, Plant, and Equipment (Net):** We ended the year with $595,929,094 in net PPE. This includes our Illinois headquarters and our global network of data centers and offices.
*   **Goodwill:** Goodwill stood at $707,192,941, representing the value of past strategic acquisitions that have expanded our market share in the asset management and custody sectors.
*   **Total Liabilities:** Our liabilities of $146,654,991,211 are primarily "Other Liabilities," which include client deposits, accrued expenses, and short-term borrowings. This high level of liabilities is standard for a global custody bank and requires rigorous liquidity stress testing.
*   **Stockholders’ Equity:** Total equity was $14,117,000,176. This includes $14,701,552,743 in retained earnings, partially offset by an Accumulated Other Comprehensive Loss (AOCI) of $1,364,171,146. The AOCI loss is primarily due to unrealized losses on our fixed-income investment securities, resulting from the rising interest rate environment during 2023.

**Operating Activities:**

Changes in working capital and other items contributed an additional $1,107,862,211. This strong operating cash flow demonstrates the cash-generative nature of our fee-based services.

**Investing Activities:**
Net cash provided by investing activities was $9,040,614,656. While we spent $198,384,173 on capital expenditures (primarily for "Ironwood Nexus" and office infrastructure), this was vastly offset by "Other Investing Activities" providing $9,238,998,829. These activities primarily involved the net proceeds from the sale and maturity of investment securities within our custody portfolio as we rebalanced our holdings in response to changing market yields.

**Financing Activities:**
Net cash used in financing activities was $14,817,025,073. This significant outflow was primarily driven by "Other Financing Activities" of $14,164,807,796, which largely consisted of net reductions in client deposit levels and the repayment of short-term borrowings. Additionally, we returned capital to shareholders through share repurchases totaling $652,217,277. We did not pay dividends during the 2023 fiscal year, choosing instead to prioritize share repurchases and reinvestment in our technology platform.

**Liquidity Position:**
Overall, our cash and equivalents decreased by $1,281,966,866 during the year. Despite this decrease, our ending cash balance of $5,787,758,603 remains robust. We believe our current cash position, combined with our operating cash flows and access to capital markets, is sufficient to meet our ongoing obligations and support our strategic growth initiatives.

### **Capital Resources and Capital Adequacy**

As a financial institution, Ironwood is subject to various regulatory capital requirements. Our total stockholders' equity of $14,117,000,176 provides a strong buffer against potential losses. Our Additional Paid-In Capital (APIC) stands at $1,126,680,837, reflecting our historical capital raises.

The negative AOCI of $1,364,171,146 is a key area of focus for our Risk Committee. This reflects the temporary market devaluation of our high-quality fixed-income portfolio due to higher rates. Because we have the intent and ability to hold these securities to maturity (or until the market recovers), we do not anticipate these unrealized losses will result in permanent impairment of our capital.

### **Critical Accounting Estimates**

The preparation of financial statements in conformity with U.S. GAAP requires management to make estimates and assumptions. These estimates affect the reported amounts of assets and liabilities and the disclosure of contingent assets and liabilities.

*   **Valuation of Goodwill and Intangible Assets:** We evaluate our $707,192,941 in goodwill for impairment at least annually. This involves estimating the fair value of our reporting units using discounted cash flow models and market multiples. Our 2023 assessment indicated that no impairment was necessary.
*   **Fair Value Measurements:** A significant portion of our $160,771,991,387 in total assets is recorded at fair value. For Level 3 assets (primarily in our Alternative Investment strategies), we use valuation models that require significant management judgment regarding inputs such as discount rates and projected cash flows.
*   **Income Taxes:** Our income tax expense of $915,902,960 is based on complex tax laws across multiple jurisdictions. We maintain valuation allowances against deferred tax assets when it is more likely than not that they will not be realized.

### **Off-Balance Sheet Arrangements**

In the ordinary course of business, we enter into various off-balance sheet arrangements, primarily related to our Custody & Trust Services segment. These include securities lending indemnifications and letters of credit. As of December 31, 2023, we do not believe any of these arrangements have or are reasonably likely to have a material current or future effect on our financial condition, results of operations, or liquidity.

### **Contractual Obligations**

Our primary contractual obligations include interest-bearing liabilities, lease commitments for our Illinois and global offices, and purchase obligations for technology services. We manage these obligations through a disciplined liquidity management framework, ensuring that we have sufficient cash flows to meet all commitments as they fall due.

### **Qualitative and Quantitative Disclosures About Market Risk**

Ironwood is exposed to market risks, including interest rate risk, foreign currency risk, and credit risk.

*   **Interest Rate Risk:** Given our $4,823,159,741 in interest expense, we are sensitive to changes in the federal funds rate and other benchmark yields. We use interest rate swaps and other derivatives to manage this exposure.
*   **Foreign Currency Risk:** With operations in multiple global financial centers, fluctuations in the value of the U.S. Dollar against the Euro, Pound Sterling, and Yen can impact our reported revenues and expenses.
*   **Credit Risk:** We face credit risk through our securities lending program and our investment portfolio. We mitigate this through rigorous counterparty credit assessments and by maintaining high levels of collateralization.

### **Conclusion**

Ironwood Resources Corp. demonstrated significant operational strength in 2023. Our total revenue of $8,038,599,569 and net income of $2,299,536,868 reflect the success of our integrated asset management and custody model. While the macro environment remains dynamic, our strong liquidity profile, evidenced by $4,494,443,551 in operating cash flow, and our robust equity base of $14,117,000,176 position us well for continued growth and value creation in 2024 and beyond. We remain committed to our Illinois roots while expanding our global reach through innovation and fiduciary excellence.

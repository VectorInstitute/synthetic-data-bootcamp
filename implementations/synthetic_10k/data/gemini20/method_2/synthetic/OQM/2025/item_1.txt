**ITEM 1. BUSINESS**

**OVERVIEW**

Bluestone Systems Co. (together with its subsidiaries, unless the context otherwise requires, the “Company,” “Bluestone,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is an energy holding company headquartered in the State of Maryland. Incorporated in 1988, Bluestone operates primarily as a public utility holding company through its regulated subsidiaries, which are engaged in the generation, transmission, and distribution of electricity. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025, the Company recorded consolidated revenues of $18,769,652,835.

Our primary business objective is to provide safe, reliable, and affordable electric service to our customer base while maintaining a sustainable growth profile for our stakeholders. We operate in a highly regulated environment, with our principal operations governed by the Maryland Public Service Commission (the "Maryland PSC") and other state and federal regulatory bodies. Our business is characterized by significant capital investment in infrastructure, including power plants, transmission lines, and distribution networks.

As of December 31, 2025, our workforce consisted of approximately 14,200 employees dedicated to serving a diverse customer base ranging from residential households to large industrial facilities across the Mid-Atlantic region. Our operations are divided into three reportable segments: Regulated Electric Operations, Transmission Assets, and Bluestone Renewables.

**BUSINESS SEGMENTS**

**Regulated Electric Operations**

The Regulated Electric Operations segment is our largest business unit, accounting for approximately 84% of our total revenues for the 2025 fiscal year. This segment is responsible for the generation, purchase, and delivery of electricity to approximately 4.2 million customers. 

*Operations and Infrastructure:*
Our regulated utility subsidiaries own and operate a diverse portfolio of generation assets, including nuclear, natural gas, and coal-fired facilities. As of year-end 2025, our generation fleet possessed an aggregate net dependable capacity of 18,450 megawatts (MW). The distribution network consists of over 65,000 circuit miles of overhead and underground lines, supported by approximately 1,200 substations.

*Customer Base and Revenue:*
Of the $18,769,652,835 in total 2025 revenues, the Regulated Electric Operations segment contributed approximately $15,766,508,381. The customer mix is balanced among residential (42%), commercial (35%), and industrial (23%) sectors. Our residential load is subject to seasonal fluctuations, typically peaking in the summer months due to air conditioning demand and in the winter months for heating.

**Transmission Assets**

The Transmission Assets segment focuses on the development, construction, and maintenance of high-voltage transmission systems that move electricity from generating sources to local distribution networks. These assets are critical to regional grid stability and are primarily regulated by federal authorities.

*Infrastructure:*
Bluestone maintains approximately 8,900 miles of high-voltage transmission lines (ranging from 138 kV to 765 kV). These lines serve as the backbone of the regional power grid, facilitating the bulk transfer of electricity across state lines and providing access to competitive wholesale power markets.

*Regulatory Framework:*
Our transmission business operates under a formula rate mechanism approved by federal regulators, which allows for the timely recovery of capital investments and a reasonable return on equity. This provides a predictable revenue stream that supports our ongoing grid modernization initiatives.

**Bluestone Renewables**

Bluestone Renewables represents our commitment to the energy transition. This segment develops and operates non-regulated renewable energy projects, including utility-scale solar farms and onshore wind installations.

*Portfolio:*
In 2025, Bluestone Renewables successfully brought 450 MW of new solar capacity online, bringing its total operational portfolio to 2,800 MW. This segment generated approximately $1,126,179,170 in revenue during the fiscal year, primarily through long-term power purchase agreements (PPAs) with corporate and industrial off-takers.

**STRATEGY AND FUTURE OUTLOOK**

Our strategy is centered on "The Bluestone 2030 Vision," which focuses on three pillars: Grid Resilience, Decarbonization, and Customer Centricity.

1.  **Grid Resilience:** We are investing significantly in "hardening" our infrastructure against extreme weather events. This includes the undergrounding of critical distribution lines, the installation of "smart" grid sensors, and the deployment of advanced automation systems to reduce outage frequency and duration.
2.  **Decarbonization:** We have committed to a net-zero carbon emission goal by 2050. Our transition involves the phased retirement of older coal units and the expansion of our natural gas and renewable generation mix. We are also exploring pilot programs for long-duration battery storage and hydrogen blending.
3.  **Customer Centricity:** We are enhancing our digital platforms to provide customers with real-time usage data and flexible billing options. Our 2025 revenue of $18,769,652,835 reflects our ability to maintain customer growth while managing the costs associated with our transition to a cleaner energy profile.

**FUEL SUPPLY AND GENERATION MIX**

To meet the energy requirements of our customers, we utilize a combination of self-generated power and market purchases. Our fuel procurement strategy is designed to ensure reliability while mitigating price volatility.

*   **Nuclear:** Our nuclear facilities provide a baseline of carbon-free power. We maintain long-term contracts for the procurement of uranium and related fuel cycle services.
*   **Natural Gas:** Natural gas remains a vital "bridge" fuel, providing the flexibility needed to balance intermittent renewable sources. We procure natural gas through a combination of firm transportation contracts and spot market purchases.
*   **Coal:** While we are reducing our reliance on coal, it remains a component of our current generation mix to ensure system reliability during periods of extreme demand.
*   **Purchased Power:** We participate in wholesale energy markets to optimize our supply costs. In 2025, approximately 15% of our delivered energy was sourced from third-party providers via competitive auctions.

**REGULATORY MATTERS**

Bluestone Systems Co. is subject to comprehensive regulation at both the state and federal levels.

*State Regulation:*
Our Maryland subsidiaries are subject to the jurisdiction of the Maryland PSC. The Maryland PSC oversees retail rates, service quality, and the necessity of new infrastructure projects. Rate cases are filed periodically to adjust the prices charged to customers based on the cost of providing service, including a return on invested capital.

*Federal Regulation:*
The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (FERC) regulates our interstate transmission operations and wholesale electricity sales. Additionally, we are subject to the North American Electric Reliability Corporation (NERC) standards, which mandate strict protocols for the physical and cybersecurity of the bulk power system.

**ENVIRONMENTAL MATTERS**

Our operations are subject to extensive federal, state, and local environmental laws and regulations regarding air emissions, water quality, waste management, and wildlife protection.

*Air Emissions:*
We are required to comply with various standards under the Clean Air Act, including limits on sulfur dioxide (SO2), nitrogen oxides (NOx), and mercury. Our 2025 environmental compliance expenditures were focused on enhancing scrubbers and selective catalytic reduction (SCR) systems across our fossil fuel fleet.

*Climate Change:*
Current and future legislative and regulatory developments related to greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions significantly impact our long-term planning. We monitor developments regarding carbon pricing and renewable energy mandates in Maryland and the broader Mid-Atlantic region.

*Water and Waste:*
Our power plants utilize water for cooling and steam generation. We are subject to regulations regarding water intake structures and the discharge of heated water back into local waterways. Additionally, we manage the disposal of coal combustion residuals (CCR) in accordance with federal and state guidelines to prevent groundwater contamination.

**HUMAN CAPITAL RESOURCES**

At Bluestone, our people are our most valuable asset. Our ability to generate $18,769,652,835 in revenue is a direct result of the technical expertise and dedication of our workforce.

*Safety:*
Safety is the foundational value of our operational culture. We measure performance through the Total Recordable Incident Rate (TRIR) and have implemented "Target Zero" programs to eliminate workplace injuries. In 2025, we achieved our lowest TRIR in company history.

*Diversity and Inclusion:*
We believe a diverse workforce fosters innovation. Bluestone has established several employee resource groups and maintains a goal of increasing representation of underrepresented groups in management roles. As of December 2025, approximately 34% of our leadership team identified as diverse.

*Training and Development:*
To address the aging workforce within the utility sector, we have invested in robust apprenticeship programs and partnerships with Maryland-based community colleges. We provide ongoing technical training to ensure our field technicians are prepared for the complexities of a modernized, digital grid.

**SEASONALITY**

The demand for electricity is heavily influenced by weather conditions. Our revenues are typically higher in the first and third quarters of the year, reflecting heating and cooling demands, respectively. Significant deviations from normal weather patterns—such as unseasonably mild winters or cool summers—can materially impact our financial results and our ability to meet the $18.8 billion revenue benchmarks established in our annual budgets.

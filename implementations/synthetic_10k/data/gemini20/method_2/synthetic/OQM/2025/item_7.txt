### **Executive Overview**

Bluestone Systems Co. (“Bluestone,” the “Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a leading electric utility holding company headquartered in Maryland. Our mission is to deliver sustainable, reliable, and affordable energy to our approximately 4.2 million customers across the Mid-Atlantic region. As of December 31, 2025, our operations are primarily conducted through three reportable segments: Regulated Electric Operations, Transmission Assets, and Bluestone Renewables.

The fiscal year 2025 was a period of significant operational execution and financial stability for Bluestone. We remained focused on our “Bluestone 2030 Vision,” which prioritizes grid resilience, decarbonization, and customer-centricity. During the year, we recorded consolidated revenues of $18,769,652,835 and achieved a net income of $1,967,628,458. Our financial results reflect the strength of our regulated utility model, characterized by predictable cash flows and a robust asset base of $52,436,493,164.

**2025 Financial Highlights**

*   **Total Operating Revenues:** $18,769,652,835
*   **Operating Income:** $2,662,358,924
*   **Net Income:** $1,967,628,458
*   **Cash from Operating Activities:** $4,034,724,131
*   **Capital Expenditures:** $2,479,256,223
*   **Total Stockholders’ Equity:** $14,894,630,758

Our Regulated Electric Operations segment continues to be the primary driver of our financial performance, contributing $15,766,508,381, or approximately 84%, of our total revenues. The Transmission Assets and Bluestone Renewables segments contributed $1,876,965,284 and $1,126,179,170, respectively. 

A central component of our strategy is the continued investment in our infrastructure. In 2025, we directed $2,479,256,223 toward capital expenditures, emphasizing the modernization of our 65,000 circuit miles of distribution lines and the expansion of our renewable energy portfolio, which now includes 2,800 MW of operational capacity. These investments are critical for maintaining service reliability and meeting the evolving regulatory standards set by the Maryland Public Service Commission (the "Maryland PSC") and the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission ("FERC").

*Operating Revenues*

For the fiscal year 2025, consolidated operating revenues were $18,769,652,835. This revenue volume was supported by steady demand across our diverse customer base, which is composed of residential (42%), commercial (35%), and industrial (23%) sectors. Revenue growth was primarily driven by the implementation of updated rate structures approved by the Maryland PSC, which allowed for the recovery of previous investments in grid hardening and distribution automation.

Seasonality continues to play a significant role in our revenue patterns. The first and third quarters of 2025 saw increased demand due to winter heating and summer cooling requirements, respectively. However, Maryland experienced relatively moderate weather patterns in the fourth quarter, which partially offset the peak demand seen during the summer months.

*Operating Expenses*

Total operating expenses for 2025 were $16,107,293,911. These expenses consist primarily of fuel and purchased power costs, operations and maintenance (O&M) expenses, and depreciation and amortization. 

*   **Fuel and Purchased Power:** A significant portion of our $16,107,293,911 in operating expenses was dedicated to procuring fuel for our 18,450 MW generation fleet and purchasing power from the wholesale market. In 2025, approximately 15% of our delivered energy was sourced through third-party providers.
*   **Operations and Maintenance:** O&M costs were influenced by our ongoing commitment to safety and reliability. We incurred substantial costs related to the maintenance of our nuclear facilities and fossil fuel units, as well as vegetation management along our 65,000 miles of distribution lines to mitigate the risk of storm-related outages.
*   **Depreciation and Amortization:** For the year 2025, depreciation and amortization expense was $2,387,687,932, as reflected in our statement of cash flows. This reflects the high capital intensity of our business and the ongoing expansion of our net property, plant, and equipment, which reached $19,032,618,565 at year-end.

*Operating Income*

Operating income for 2025 was $2,662,358,924. Our operating margin was approximately 14.2%, reflecting the regulated nature of our primary business segments and our focus on cost management amidst the transition to cleaner energy sources.

*Interest Expense*

For the fiscal year 2025, we recorded $0 in interest expense. This was primarily due to the capitalization of interest costs associated with our extensive construction work in progress (CWIP) and other financing arrangements where interest was either offset by regulatory credits or fully capitalized into the cost of long-term infrastructure projects.

*Income Tax Expense*

We recorded an income tax expense of $694,730,466 for 2025, representing an effective tax rate of approximately 26.1%. This rate is influenced by federal and state statutory rates, adjusted for permanent book-to-tax differences and the impact of various tax credits related to our renewable energy investments in the Bluestone Renewables segment.

**Segment Results**

*Regulated Electric Operations*

Our Regulated Electric Operations segment reported revenues of $15,766,508,381 for 2025. This segment remains the cornerstone of our enterprise, utilizing 18,450 MW of generation capacity to serve 4.2 million customers. Performance in this segment was bolstered by the deployment of $950 million in distribution system automation, which improved operational efficiency and reduced the costs associated with manual field interventions.

The segment's revenue is largely governed by the Maryland PSC. In 2025, we benefited from the full-year impact of previously approved rate adjustments designed to recover costs associated with our nuclear plant life extension and maintenance programs. Residential load remained stable, while industrial demand saw a slight uptick due to increased manufacturing activity in the Baltimore-Washington corridor.

*Transmission Assets*

The Transmission Assets segment contributed $1,876,965,284 to our 2025 revenues. This segment operates 8,900 miles of high-voltage transmission lines. Revenues in this segment are primarily derived from FERC-approved formula rates, which provide for the recovery of actual costs plus a return on equity. 

The stability of this segment is a key component of our financial profile. During 2025, we continued our grid modernization efforts, including the replacement of aging transmission towers and the integration of advanced sensors to monitor grid health in real-time. These investments ensure that we meet NERC reliability standards while supporting the transmission of bulk power across the Mid-Atlantic region.

*Bluestone Renewables*

Bluestone Renewables generated $1,126,179,170 in revenue during 2025. This segment focuses on non-regulated renewable energy projects, including solar and wind installations. In 2025, we successfully added 450 MW of solar capacity, bringing our total renewable portfolio to 2,800 MW.

Revenues in this segment are primarily secured through long-term Power Purchase Agreements (PPAs) with corporate and industrial customers. The 2025 results were in line with expectations, as high availability across our wind and solar farms offset the impact of intermittent weather patterns. This segment is central to our "Bluestone 2030 Vision" and our goal of achieving net-zero carbon emissions by 2050.

### **Liquidity and Capital Resources**

**Overview of Liquidity**

Bluestone maintains a strong liquidity position to fund our operations, meet our debt obligations, and execute our extensive capital investment program. As of December 31, 2025, we had cash and equivalents of $3,237,502,332, representing an increase of $722,328,554 from the prior year.

Our primary sources of liquidity are cash flows from operations and access to capital markets. We believe our current cash position, combined with projected operating cash flows, will be sufficient to meet our needs for at least the next 12 months.

**Cash Flow Analysis**

*Net Cash Provided by Operating Activities*

Net cash provided by operating activities was $4,034,724,131 in 2025. This was driven by net income of $1,967,628,458, adjusted for non-cash items such as depreciation and amortization of $2,387,687,932. These inflows were partially offset by a $320,592,259 decrease in cash due to changes in working capital and other operating items. 

The strength of our operating cash flow is a testament to the reliability of our regulated revenue streams and our efficient collection processes, as evidenced by our accounts receivable balance of $3,719,016,281 at year-end.

*Net Cash Used in Investing Activities*

Net cash used in investing activities was $2,915,607,014 in 2025. The primary driver of this outflow was capital expenditures of $2,479,256,223. These expenditures were directed toward:
*   Upgrading transmission and distribution infrastructure for enhanced grid resilience.
*   The construction and commissioning of new solar facilities within the Bluestone Renewables segment.
*   Safety and life-extension projects at our nuclear generation facilities.

Additionally, other investing activities resulted in an outflow of $436,350,791, which primarily includes the acquisition of intangible assets and investments in other non-current utility assets.

*Net Cash Used in Financing Activities*

Net cash used in financing activities was $396,788,563 in 2025. This included:
*   **Share Repurchases:** We returned $286,618,056 to our stockholders through our share repurchase program, reflecting our commitment to enhancing shareholder value.
*   **Other Financing Activities:** We recorded an outflow of $110,170,507 for other financing activities, including the settlement of short-term obligations and fees associated with our debt management.
*   **Dividends:** We did not pay any common stock dividends during the fiscal year 2025, electing instead to reinvest our earnings into our $4.2 billion multi-year capital investment program.

**Capital Structure and Debt**

Bluestone maintains a capital structure designed to provide financial flexibility and support our credit ratings. As of December 31, 2025, our total liabilities were $37,541,862,406, and our total stockholders’ equity was $14,894,630,758.

Our debt profile as of December 31, 2025, was as follows:
*   **Short-term Debt:** $78,667,880
*   **Long-term Debt:** $6,142,118,776

Our long-term debt primarily consists of senior unsecured notes and mortgage bonds issued by our regulated subsidiaries. This debt is used to finance our long-lived utility assets, including our $19,032,618,565 in net property, plant, and equipment. We maintain a robust relationship with various financial institutions and believe we have the ability to access the debt markets at competitive rates to refinance maturing obligations and fund future growth.

Our equity position includes retained earnings of $6,089,199,681 and other equity components totaling $10,988,684,466. These are partially offset by an accumulated other comprehensive loss of $2,183,253,389, which primarily reflects actuarial adjustments related to our pension and post-retirement benefit plans, as well as changes in the fair value of certain cash flow hedges.

**Working Capital**

As of December 31, 2025, our working capital position remained healthy. Our current assets included:
*   **Cash and Equivalents:** $3,237,502,332
*   **Accounts Receivable:** $3,719,016,281
*   **Inventory:** $1,762,592,194

Our current liabilities included:
*   **Accounts Payable:** $2,516,149,234
*   **Short-term Debt:** $78,667,880

Our inventory consists primarily of fuel (coal, natural gas, and nuclear fuel) and materials and supplies necessary for the maintenance of our transmission and distribution systems. The $1,762,592,194 inventory balance ensures that we have sufficient resources to maintain generation and respond to emergency repair needs during storm events.

### **Capital Expenditures and Future Investment**

Looking ahead, we remain committed to our extensive capital investment plan. Our 2025 cash capital expenditures of $2,479,256,223 are part of a broader $4.2 billion investment strategy focused on the following areas:

1.  **Grid Resilience:** Approximately $2.05 billion is earmarked for transmission line upgrades and distribution system automation. This includes the undergrounding of distribution lines in coastal Maryland areas vulnerable to storm surges.
2.  **Decarbonization:** We plan to invest approximately $1.2 billion in Bluestone Renewables to expand our solar and wind footprint. This includes the development of 450 MW of new capacity annually through 2030.
3.  **Nuclear Maintenance:** We have allocated $500 million for the continued maintenance and life extension of our nuclear fleet, which provides critical baseline carbon-free power.
4.  **Technology and Other Plant:** Approximately $450 million is dedicated to technology upgrades, including the enhancement of our digital billing systems and the deployment of "smart" grid sensors across our network.

These investments are essential to driving rate base growth within our Regulated Electric Operations and Transmission Assets segments. We expect to fund these requirements through a combination of operating cash flows and the issuance of new long-term debt.

### **Regulatory and Environmental Matters**

**Regulatory Environment**

Bluestone operates in a highly regulated environment. Our ability to recover costs and earn a return on our $52,436,493,164 asset base is subject to the decisions of the Maryland PSC and FERC. 

In 2025, we continued to engage with the Maryland PSC regarding our grid modernization plans. Our formula rate mechanism for transmission assets remains a critical component of our financial stability, providing a clear path for the recovery of the $1.1 billion invested in transmission reliability during the year.

**Environmental Compliance**

Our operations are subject to stringent environmental regulations. In 2025, our compliance efforts were focused on air emissions and water quality. We have invested significantly in selective catalytic reduction (SCR) systems and scrubbers for our fossil fuel fleet to meet Clean Air Act standards. 

Climate change remains a significant long-term risk. We are proactively transitioning our generation mix to meet Maryland’s renewable energy targets. Our "Bluestone 2030 Vision" aligns our business strategy with these regulatory mandates, ensuring that our $1.13 billion in renewable energy revenue continues to grow as a percentage of our total portfolio.

### **Critical Accounting Estimates**

The preparation of our consolidated financial statements requires management to make estimates and assumptions that affect the reported amounts of assets, liabilities, revenues, and expenses. We base our estimates on historical experience and on various other assumptions that we believe to be reasonable under the circumstances.

**Regulatory Accounting**

As a regulated utility, we apply specific accounting guidance that allows us to defer certain costs as regulatory assets or recognize certain obligations as regulatory liabilities. These deferrals are based on the expectation that such costs will be recovered in future rates. As of December 31, 2025, a significant portion of our "Other Assets" balance of $24,154,557,269 consists of regulatory assets. If we were to determine that these costs are no longer probable of recovery, we would be required to charge the remaining balance to expense, which could materially impact our results of operations.

**Revenue Recognition and Unbilled Revenue**

Revenues from the sale of electricity are recognized upon delivery to the customer. Because our customers are billed on a cycle basis, we must estimate the amount of electricity delivered but not yet billed at the end of each reporting period. This "unbilled revenue" is included in our accounts receivable balance of $3,719,016,281. Our estimates are based on historical usage patterns, weather data, and current rate structures.

**Property, Plant, and Equipment (PP&E)**

Our net PP&E of $19,032,618,565 represents the largest component of our physical assets. We calculate depreciation using the straight-line method over the estimated useful lives of the assets. These lives are determined based on engineering studies and historical experience. Any change in the estimated useful lives of our generation, transmission, or distribution assets could significantly impact our annual depreciation expense of $2,387,687,932.

**Goodwill and Intangible Assets**

As of December 31, 2025, we reported goodwill of $348,941,256 and net intangible assets of $181,265,267. We test goodwill for impairment annually, or more frequently if events or changes in circumstances indicate that the carrying value may not be recoverable. Our 2025 impairment test indicated that the fair value of our reporting units exceeded their carrying values, and no impairment was recorded.

### **Market Risk**

We are exposed to various market risks, including commodity price risk and interest rate risk.

**Commodity Price Risk**

We are exposed to volatility in the prices of natural gas, coal, and purchased power. We manage this risk through a combination of long-term supply contracts, financial hedges, and regulatory recovery mechanisms. In Maryland, fuel adjustment clauses allow us to pass through changes in fuel costs to our customers, although this is subject to periodic audit and regulatory lag.

**Interest Rate Risk**

With $6,142,118,776 in long-term debt, we are sensitive to changes in interest rates, particularly when we need to issue new debt or refinance existing obligations. While much of our current debt is at fixed rates, future borrowing costs will be influenced by prevailing market conditions and our credit ratings. We may use interest rate swaps or other derivative instruments to manage this exposure.

### **Conclusion**

Bluestone Systems Co. delivered a solid financial performance in 2025, characterized by $18.8 billion in revenue and nearly $2.0 billion in net income. Our strong cash flow from operations of $4.03 billion supports our ambitious capital investment program and our commitment to returning value to our stockholders. As we continue to execute our "Bluestone 2030 Vision," we remain focused on maintaining our position as a leader in the energy transition while providing reliable service to the State of Maryland and the broader Mid-Atlantic region.

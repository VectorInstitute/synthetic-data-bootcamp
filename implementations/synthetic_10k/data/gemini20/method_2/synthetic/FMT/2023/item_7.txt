### **Executive Overview**

Ironwood Logistics Group, Inc. is a Georgia-based financial services leader specializing in the life and health insurance sectors. Our mission is centered on the "logistics of life"—managing the complex risks associated with human capital to ensure the long-term financial stability of our policyholders. During the fiscal year ended December 31, 2023, we continued to execute our strategy of disciplined underwriting, digital transformation via our "Ironwood Gateway" platform, and prudent capital management.

**2023 Financial Performance Highlights**

Our financial results for 2023 reflect the scale of our operations and our resilience in a fluctuating macroeconomic environment. Key performance indicators for the year include:

*   **Total Revenues:** $21,006,222,039, driven by strong premium growth in our Group Specialized Benefits and Individual Life segments, as well as robust net investment income.
*   **Net Income:** $5,411,069,160, representing a strong margin relative to our diversified revenue base.
*   **Total Assets:** $136,747,486,447, reflecting our significant general account and the scale of our investment activities.
*   **Total Stockholders’ Equity:** $23,743,142,447, providing a substantial capital cushion despite market-driven pressures on our accumulated other comprehensive income.
*   **Cash Flow from Operations:** $3,583,852,443, which supported our ability to return capital to shareholders while maintaining high levels of liquidity.

In 2023, we focused on optimizing our "Workforce Health & Risk Management" segment, which caters to the high-intensity industrial and transportation sectors. This specialized focus allowed us to maintain competitive medical loss ratios even as healthcare costs faced inflationary pressures. Furthermore, our Corporate and Asset Management segment successfully navigated a volatile interest rate environment, managing a portfolio that underpins our $113,004,344,000 in total liabilities.

**Revenues**

Total revenues for the fiscal year 2023 reached $21,006,222,039. This performance was anchored by our Individual Life and Annuity Solutions and Group Specialized Benefits segments. Premium income, the primary component of our revenue, remained steady as we expanded our footprint in the Georgia market and enhanced our digital distribution capabilities. Net investment income also contributed significantly, as our Corporate and Asset Management team successfully reinvested maturing securities into higher-yielding instruments.

**Operating Expenses**

Operating expenses totaled $13,940,769,168 for the year. This is comprised of:

*   **Other Operating Expenses ($10,455,204,072):** This category primarily includes policyholder benefits, claims, and the provision for future policy benefits. As an insurer with $113,004,344,000 in total liabilities, the management of claims—particularly in our Workforce Health and Group Disability lines—is the largest driver of our expense profile. In 2023, we observed stable mortality trends across our life insurance blocks, while morbidity in our health-related segments was managed through proactive care coordination.
*   **Selling, General and Administrative (SG&A) ($3,485,565,096):** SG&A expenses include commissions paid to our independent broker-dealer network, employee compensation for our 14,500 staff members, and significant investments in our IT infrastructure. The $3.49 billion in SG&A reflects our ongoing commitment to the "Ironwood Gateway" platform and the high-touch service model required for our institutional clients.

**Interest Expense**

Interest expense for the year was $215,996,238. This represents the cost of our long-term debt and other financing arrangements used to optimize our capital structure. Our interest coverage remains strong, given our pretax income of $6,849,456,633.

**Income Tax Expense**

Our income tax expense for 2023 was $1,438,385,473, resulting in an effective tax rate of approximately 21%. This is consistent with the U.S. statutory rate, adjusted for tax-exempt investment income and state taxes in Georgia and other jurisdictions where we maintain a significant operational presence.

### **Segment Results**

Ironwood Logistics Group, Inc. monitors and evaluates the performance of our business through four primary reportable segments. The combined revenue of these segments accounts for the $21,006,222,039 reported in 2023.

#### **Individual Life and Annuity Solutions**
This segment focuses on delivering long-term financial security through term life, whole life, and various annuity products. In 2023, the segment benefited from increased consumer demand for fixed and indexed annuities as individuals sought stable returns in an uncertain market. We leveraged our "Ironwood Academy" to ensure our distribution partners were well-equipped to explain the nuances of our complex legacy-planning tools.

#### **Group Specialized Benefits**
Serving the "human logistics" needs of large employers, this segment provides group life, disability, and supplemental health products. Performance in 2023 was driven by high retention rates among our industrial clients. The segment continues to be a core driver of our premium revenue, utilizing our proprietary risk models to provide competitive pricing for multi-year contracts.

#### **Workforce Health & Risk Management**
This segment offers stop-loss insurance and integrated health solutions for high-intensity work environments. The 2023 results were bolstered by our managed care coordination programs, which effectively mitigated the impact of high-cost medical claims. Our focus on Georgia-based industrial hubs remains a strategic advantage for this segment.

#### **Corporate and Asset Management**
In addition to managing our $136,747,486,447 asset base, this segment includes corporate treasury functions. The performance here is tied to the yield on our general account. In 2023, the segment successfully managed the "spread" between our investment returns and the interest credited to policyholders, contributing to the overall net income of $5,411,069,160.

### **Investments**

Our investment portfolio is the foundation of our ability to meet long-term obligations to policyholders. As of December 31, 2023, our total assets stood at $136,747,486,447, the vast majority of which are held in our "Other Assets" category ($130,997,706,005), which encompasses our investment holdings.

**Portfolio Composition**

Our general account is primarily invested in high-quality fixed-maturity securities. We maintain a conservative investment philosophy, focusing on:
*   U.S. Government and Agency securities.
*   Investment-grade corporate bonds.
*   Commercial mortgage loans, diversified across property types and geographic regions, with a stable concentration in the Southeast.
*   Alternative investments, including private equity and real estate, intended to provide alpha in a low-yield environment.

**Accumulated Other Comprehensive Income (AOCI)**

As of December 31, 2023, we recorded an Accumulated Other Comprehensive Loss of $5,262,465,075. This negative balance is primarily due to unrealized losses on our fixed-income portfolio, resulting from the rise in benchmark interest rates during the year. As we generally have the intent and ability to hold these securities to maturity, we do not expect these unrealized losses to result in permanent impairments that would impact our $23,743,142,447 total stockholders’ equity beyond the current reporting period's adjustments.

### **Critical Accounting Estimates**

The preparation of financial statements in conformity with GAAP requires us to make estimates and assumptions that affect the reported amounts of assets and liabilities. The most critical of these estimates for Ironwood Logistics Group, Inc. include:

**Insurance Reserves**
Our $113,004,344,000 in liabilities are largely comprised of reserves for future policy benefits. These are calculated using complex actuarial models that involve assumptions regarding mortality, morbidity, policy lapse rates, and investment returns. Small changes in these assumptions can have a material impact on our financial condition. In 2023, we updated our morbidity assumptions in the Workforce Health & Risk Management segment to reflect evolving medical cost trends, which did not result in a material change to our consolidated reserves.

**Valuation of Investments**
Our "Other Assets" of $130,997,706,005 are recorded at fair value. For securities that are not actively traded, we use internal models and "Level 3" inputs. We also regularly evaluate our portfolio for "Other-Than-Temporary Impairments" (OTTI). Given the high credit quality of our holdings, impairments were not a significant factor in our 2023 results.

**Goodwill and Intangible Assets**
We perform an annual impairment test on our $289,132,320 of Goodwill. This involves estimating the fair value of our reporting units using discounted cash flow models. Based on our 2023 assessment, the fair values of our segments significantly exceed their carrying values.

**Deferred Policy Acquisition Costs (DAC)**
We defer certain costs related to the acquisition of new insurance contracts and amortize them over the life of the policies. This process requires estimates of future gross profits, which are sensitive to the same actuarial and investment assumptions used in reserve setting.

### **Contractual Obligations**

Our primary contractual obligations relate to our $113,004,344,000 in insurance-related liabilities. These are not settled on a fixed schedule but are paid out over decades as policyholders encounter the life events covered by our products. We also maintain obligations under our long-term debt and lease agreements for our facilities in Georgia and other regional hubs.

### **Off-Balance Sheet Arrangements**

As of December 31, 2023, we do not have any off-balance sheet arrangements that have or are reasonably likely to have a current or future effect on our financial condition, changes in financial condition, revenues or expenses, results of operations, liquidity, capital expenditures, or capital resources that is material to investors.

### **Market Risk**

Ironwood Logistics Group, Inc. is exposed to various market risks, including interest rate risk, equity price risk, and credit risk.

*   **Interest Rate Risk:** A 100-basis point increase in interest rates would further decrease the fair value of our fixed-income portfolio but would likely improve our "spread" income over time.
*   **Equity Price Risk:** While our general account is focused on fixed income, our variable annuity fees are sensitive to equity market performance.
*   **Credit Risk:** We manage credit risk through rigorous credit research and by maintaining a diversified portfolio of $130,997,706,005 in investment assets.

### **New Accounting Standards**

We continue to monitor the implementation of new accounting standards issued by the FASB. In 2023, we adopted several updates related to the measurement of long-duration insurance contracts, which enhanced the transparency of our $113,004,344,000 in liabilities but did not fundamentally alter our capital position.

**ITEM 1C. CYBERSECURITY**

**Risk Management and Strategy**

Ironwood Logistics Group, Inc. recognizes that as a leading provider of life and health insurance products, we are entrusted with the protection of sensitive personal, medical, and financial information for millions of policyholders. Our business model, which generated $21,006,222,039 in total revenues in 2023, is increasingly reliant on digital infrastructure, particularly our "Ironwood Gateway" platform. Consequently, cybersecurity is a fundamental component of our enterprise risk management ("ERM") framework.

Our cybersecurity program is designed to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of our information systems and the vast data assets we manage, including the medical records and financial profiles underlying our $113,004,344,000 in total liabilities. To ensure a robust defense-in-depth strategy, we have grounded our cybersecurity processes in the NIST Cybersecurity Framework and ISO/IEC 27001 standards. These frameworks provide a structured approach to identifying, protecting, detecting, responding to, and recovering from cyber threats.

*Integration with Enterprise Risk Management*

Cybersecurity risks are integrated into our broader ERM processes, which are overseen by our executive leadership and our Board of Directors. Given our focus on "human logistics" and workforce risk, we treat cyber threats not merely as technical issues but as potential disruptions to the continuity of the health and life protections we provide. We conduct regular risk assessments to identify vulnerabilities within our internal systems and across our external-facing digital platforms. These assessments consider a variety of threat vectors, including unauthorized access, ransomware, social engineering, and advanced persistent threats that could target our $136,747,486,447 asset base.

*Third-Party Risk Management*

Our operational ecosystem involves significant interaction with third-party vendors, including healthcare providers, independent broker-dealers, and cloud service providers. We have implemented a comprehensive third-party risk management program to evaluate the cybersecurity posture of these partners. This process includes pre-contractual due diligence, periodic security assessments, and the inclusion of specific cybersecurity and data protection requirements in our service agreements. We recognize that a breach at a critical service provider could have a material impact on our ability to process the claims and premiums that drive our $21,006,222,039 in annual revenue.

*Incident Response and Business Continuity*

We maintain a dedicated Cybersecurity Incident Response Plan ("CIRP") that outlines the procedures for responding to and mitigating the impact of potential security incidents. The CIRP is tested annually through tabletop exercises involving cross-functional teams, including IT, Legal, Risk Management, and Corporate Communications. Furthermore, our business continuity and disaster recovery plans are designed to ensure that our Georgia-based headquarters and regional data centers can remain operational or recover quickly in the event of a systemic disruption.

To date, Ironwood Logistics Group, Inc. has not experienced a cybersecurity incident that has had a material impact on our business strategy, results of operations, or financial condition. However, we acknowledge that the threat landscape is constantly evolving, and there can be no assurance that our defensive measures will be successful in preventing all future attacks. The risks associated with cybersecurity are further detailed in "Item 1A. Risk Factors."

**Governance**

*Board Oversight*

The Board of Directors of Ironwood Logistics Group, Inc. maintains ultimate oversight of the Company’s cybersecurity risk. To ensure dedicated focus on this critical area, the Board has established a Technology and Cybersecurity Committee. This Committee is responsible for reviewing the Company’s cybersecurity strategy, assessing the effectiveness of our security controls, and monitoring our compliance with applicable data privacy regulations, such as HIPAA and the various insurance laws of the State of Georgia.

The Technology and Cybersecurity Committee receives quarterly reports from management regarding the current threat landscape, the status of ongoing security initiatives, and the results of internal and external security audits. The Committee also reviews the Company’s cybersecurity insurance coverage and ensures that adequate resources—both financial and human—are allocated to maintain the integrity of the "Ironwood Gateway" and other critical systems.

*Management Oversight*

At the management level, our cybersecurity program is led by our Chief Information Security Officer ("CISO"), who has over two decades of experience in information security within the Financials sector. The CISO reports directly to the Chief Technology Officer and provides regular updates to the executive leadership team. Our CISO is responsible for the day-to-day execution of the cybersecurity strategy, including the management of our Security Operations Center ("SOC"), which provides 24/7 monitoring of our network environment.

Our management team utilizes a variety of metrics and key performance indicators ("KPIs") to measure the effectiveness of our security program. These metrics include system uptime, time-to-detect and time-to-remediate security events, and the completion rates of employee training programs.

*Human Capital and Training*

Recognizing that our 14,500 employees are the first line of defense against cyber threats, we utilize the "Ironwood Academy" to provide mandatory, ongoing cybersecurity awareness training. This training covers topics such as phishing identification, password hygiene, and the secure handling of sensitive policyholder data. We also conduct regular simulated phishing exercises to evaluate employee vigilance and reinforce best practices. By fostering a culture of security across our Georgia-based workforce and our remote-work hubs, we aim to reduce the likelihood of human error leading to a significant security breach.

In summary, Ironwood Logistics Group, Inc. employs a multi-layered governance and risk management approach to cybersecurity. By aligning our strategy with the NIST and ISO/IEC 27001 frameworks and maintaining rigorous Board and management oversight, we strive to protect our $136,747,486,447 in assets and uphold the trust of the millions of individuals and corporations who rely on our life and health solutions.

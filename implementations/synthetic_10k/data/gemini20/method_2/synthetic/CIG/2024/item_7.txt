All dollar amounts are in U.S. dollars unless otherwise indicated.

### **Overview**

Crestline Energy Inc. (“Crestline,” the “Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a leading global provider of high-precision electronic equipment and instruments within the Information Technology sector. Headquartered in Florida, our business is centered on the development of sophisticated hardware and software solutions that facilitate data acquisition and energy management for critical infrastructure. We serve a diverse range of end-markets, with a primary focus on the modernization of the electrical grid, high-density data center management, and advanced industrial automation.

For the fiscal year ended 2024, Crestline achieved total revenues of $7,425,482,275, supported by the continued global demand for our Precision Sensing Solutions and Integrated Systems. Our net income for the period was $1,876,085,370, reflecting robust demand across our product lines and the efficiency of our hybrid manufacturing model.

Our operational strategy is currently focused on "Edge Intelligence," which involves embedding advanced processing capabilities directly into our sensing hardware. This reduces data latency and enhances the value proposition for our customers in high-stakes environments such as semiconductor fabrication and renewable energy management. We continue to invest heavily in Research and Development (“R&D”), with $870,321,394 dedicated to innovation in fiscal 2024 to maintain our competitive edge in the Electronic Equipment & Instruments sub-industry.

### **Components of Results of Operations**

**Revenues**

We generate revenue primarily from the sale of high-precision electronic instruments, integrated monitoring systems, and related software and support services. Our revenue is categorized into three reportable segments: Precision Sensing Solutions, Integrated Systems & Infrastructure, and Advanced Analytics & Support Services.

**Cost of Revenue**

Cost of revenue consists primarily of raw materials (including semiconductors, silicon, and specialized alloys), labor costs associated with assembly, manufacturing overhead, and logistics. It also includes depreciation of manufacturing equipment and amortization of certain acquired technology.

**Operating Expenses**

*   **Selling, General and Administrative (SG&A):** SG&A expenses consist of personnel-related costs for our sales, marketing, finance, and legal teams, including those based at our Florida headquarters. It also includes marketing programs, professional services, and IT infrastructure costs.
*   **Research and Development (R&D):** R&D expenses consist of costs related to the design and testing of new products and the enhancement of existing technologies. These costs primarily include compensation for our engineering staff and the operation of our global Innovation Centers.
*   **Other Operating Expenses (Income):** This category includes gains or losses on the disposal of assets, litigation settlements, and other non-recurring operational items. In fiscal 2024, this reflected a significant net gain (income) of $1,056,121,878.

**Revenue Analysis**

Total revenues for fiscal 2024 were $7,425,482,275. This performance was driven by a high volume of deployments in our Precision Sensing Solutions segment, which contributed approximately 48% of total revenue, or roughly $3.56 billion. The growth was particularly strong in the renewable energy sector, where our high-frequency sensors are utilized for grid stabilization. We also saw increased demand for our Integrated Systems & Infrastructure products, specifically rack-mounted power distribution units and smart-grid controllers, as hyperscale data center operators expanded their footprints.

**Gross Profit**

Gross profit for fiscal 2024 was $5,107,056,229, representing a gross margin of 68.8%. This high margin reflects the specialized, mission-critical nature of our electronic instruments and our ability to command premium pricing for the "Crestline Standard" of accuracy. Cost of revenue was $2,318,426,046, which was impacted by moderate price volatility in the semiconductor market and increased freight costs associated with our Southeast Asian manufacturing hubs.

**Operating Expenses**

*   **SG&A:** Expenses were $3,207,972,259, or 43.2% of revenue. This reflects our investment in a global direct sales force and the administrative costs associated with managing a workforce of 14,200 employees. We also incurred significant legal and compliance costs related to international trade regulations and data privacy.
*   **R&D:** Investment in R&D was $870,321,394, or 11.7% of revenue. This spending was focused on our three key pillars: Next-Generation Wireless Sensing, Quantum-Precision Calibration, and Sustainability-Focused Hardware. These investments are critical for maintaining our portfolio of over 1,200 patents.
*   **Other Operating (Income) Expense:** We recorded a net gain of $1,056,121,878 within this category. This was primarily due to the successful divestiture of certain non-core business assets and the favorable resolution of a long-standing intellectual property dispute, which resulted in a one-time settlement inflow. This gain significantly bolstered our operating income for the fiscal year.

**Operating Income**

Operating income for fiscal 2024 was $2,084,884,454, representing an operating margin of 28.1%. The increase in operating income was a direct result of our high gross margins and the substantial one-time gain recorded in other operating income, partially offset by our continued high level of investment in R&D and SG&A.

**Income Tax Expense**

Our income tax expense for fiscal 2024 was $208,799,084, representing an effective tax rate of approximately 10.0%. This rate is lower than the statutory U.S. federal rate primarily due to tax credits for R&D activities and the geographical mix of our earnings, including operations in lower-tax jurisdictions in Southeast Asia.

**Net Income**

As a result of the factors above, net income for the fiscal year ended 2024 was $1,876,085,370.

### **Segment Results**

*   **Precision Sensing Solutions:** This segment remained our primary revenue driver. In 2024, the segment benefited from the rollout of our new high-frequency thermal imaging line. The "Edge Intelligence" capabilities in our sensing units allowed us to capture market share from competitors who offer traditional, "dumb" sensors.
*   **Integrated Systems & Infrastructure:** Revenue in this segment was driven by the global transition toward smart-grid technologies. Our modular industrial automation hubs saw increased adoption in Western Europe and North America.
*   **Advanced Analytics & Support Services:** While the smallest segment by revenue, this business unit provided the highest margins. Subscription-based revenue for our failure-prediction software grew as customers sought to maximize the lifespan of their Crestline hardware.

### **Critical Accounting Estimates**

Our consolidated financial statements are prepared in accordance with U.S. Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (GAAP). The preparation of these statements requires us to make estimates and assumptions that affect the reported amounts of assets, liabilities, revenue, and expenses. We base our estimates on historical experience and on various other assumptions that we believe to be reasonable under the circumstances.

**Goodwill and Intangible Assets**

As of the end of fiscal 2024, we held $19,261,194,429 in goodwill and $8,934,374,934 in net intangible assets. These assets represent a significant portion of our $29,921,956,490 total asset base. Goodwill is tested for impairment annually in the fourth quarter, or more frequently if events or changes in circumstances indicate that the asset might be impaired. Our impairment test involves comparing the fair value of our reporting units to their carrying values. The determination of fair value requires significant judgment, including estimates of future cash flows and discount rates.

**Inventory Valuation**

Our inventory, totaling $107,289,672, is stated at the lower of cost or net realizable value. We regularly review inventory quantities on hand and record provisions for excess and obsolete inventory based on our forecast of future product demand and technological life cycles. Given the rapid pace of innovation in the Information Technology sector, a significant decrease in demand for a particular instrument could result in a material impairment of our inventory value.

**Revenue Recognition**

We recognize revenue when control of the promised goods or services is transferred to our customers, in an amount that reflects the consideration we expect to be entitled to in exchange for those goods or services. For our Advanced Analytics segment, this often involves the allocation of the transaction price to multiple performance obligations, such as hardware, software licenses, and maintenance services, which requires judgment.

**Income Taxes**

We are subject to income taxes in the United States and numerous foreign jurisdictions. Significant judgment is required in determining our global provision for income taxes and evaluating our tax positions. We recognize deferred tax assets and liabilities for the expected future tax consequences of events that have been included in our financial statements or tax returns. As of the end of 2024, our accumulated other comprehensive income was -$165,670,954, partly reflecting the impact of foreign currency translation and tax adjustments.

### **Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk**

**Foreign Currency Risk**

As a global company, we are exposed to foreign currency exchange rate fluctuations. While we report in U.S. dollars, a portion of our $7,425,482,275 in revenue and our manufacturing expenses are denominated in other currencies, such as the Euro and various Southeast Asian currencies. We utilize forward contracts to hedge some of these exposures, but significant volatility in exchange rates could still impact our reported net income and the value of our international assets.

**Interest Rate Risk**

Our exposure to interest rate risk relates primarily to our $6,713,303,505 in total debt. While much of our long-term debt is at fixed rates, any future borrowings under our revolving credit facilities would be subject to variable interest rates. A significant increase in market interest rates could increase our interest expense and reduce our profitability.

**Conclusion**

Crestline Energy Inc. concludes fiscal 2024 in a strong financial position, with a robust asset base of $29,921,956,490 and a clear strategic path forward. Our focus on "Edge Intelligence" and high-precision electronic equipment positions us well to capitalize on the ongoing digital transformation of global energy and industrial infrastructure. Despite the challenges of a complex global supply chain and significant liabilities, our $1,876,085,370 in net income demonstrates the resilience and profitability of our business model.

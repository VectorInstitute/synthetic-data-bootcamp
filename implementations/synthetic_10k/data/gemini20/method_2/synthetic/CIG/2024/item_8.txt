**ITEM 8. FINANCIAL STATEMENTS AND SUPPLEMENTARY DATA**

**REPORT OF INDEPENDENT REGISTERED PUBLIC ACCOUNTING FIRM**

To the Shareholders and the Board of Directors of Crestline Energy Inc.

**Opinion on the Financial Statements**

We have audited the accompanying consolidated balance sheet of Crestline Energy Inc. (the Company) as of December 31, 2024, the related consolidated statements of operations, comprehensive income, stockholders’ equity, and cash flows for the year then ended, and the related notes (collectively referred to as the “consolidated financial statements”). In our opinion, the consolidated financial statements present fairly, in all material respects, the financial position of the Company as of December 31, 2024, and the results of its operations and its cash flows for the year then ended, in conformity with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles.

**Basis for Opinion**

These consolidated financial statements are the responsibility of the Company’s management. Our responsibility is to express an opinion on the Company’s consolidated financial statements based on our audit. We are a public accounting firm registered with the Public Company Accounting Oversight Board (United States) (PCAOB) and are required to be independent with respect to the Company in accordance with the U.S. federal securities laws and the applicable rules and regulations of the Securities and Exchange Commission and the PCAOB.

We conducted our audit in accordance with the standards of the PCAOB. Those standards require that we plan and perform the audit to obtain reasonable assurance about whether the consolidated financial statements are free of material misstatement, whether due to error or fraud. Our audit included performing procedures to assess the risks of material misstatement of the consolidated financial statements, whether due to error or fraud, and performing procedures that respond to those risks. Such procedures included examining, on a test basis, evidence regarding the amounts and disclosures in the consolidated financial statements. Our audit also included evaluating the accounting principles used and significant estimates made by management, as well as evaluating the overall presentation of the consolidated financial statements. We believe that our audit provides a reasonable basis for our opinion.

### **NOTES TO CONSOLIDATED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS**

#### **NOTE 1. ORGANIZATION AND SUMMARY OF SIGNIFICANT ACCOUNTING POLICIES**

**Description of Business**
Crestline Energy Inc. (“Crestline” or the “Company”), headquartered in Florida, is a global technology entity operating within the Information Technology sector, specifically the Electronic Equipment & Instruments sub-industry. The Company specializes in the design, manufacture, and distribution of high-precision electronic equipment and instruments used in energy infrastructure, data centers, and industrial automation.

**Basis of Presentation**
The accompanying consolidated financial statements have been prepared in accordance with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP). All intercompany transactions and balances have been eliminated in consolidation.

**Use of Estimates**
The preparation of financial statements requires management to make estimates and assumptions that affect the reported amounts of assets and liabilities. Significant estimates include the valuation of goodwill ($19.26 billion) and intangible assets ($8.93 billion), inventory obsolescence, and the effective tax rate.

**Cash and Equivalents**
The Company considers all highly liquid investments with an original maturity of three months or less to be cash equivalents. As of December 31, 2024, the Company held $172,715,767 in cash and equivalents.

**Revenue Recognition**
Revenue is recognized upon the transfer of control of promised goods or services to customers. For the fiscal year 2024, the Company generated total revenues of $7,425,482,275. Precision Sensing Solutions represented approximately 48% of this total.

#### **NOTE 2. INVENTORY**
Inventory is stated at the lower of cost or net realizable value. As of December 31, 2024, inventory consisted of the following (in thousands):
*   Raw materials and components: $62,450
*   Work in process: $15,110
*   Finished goods: $29,730
*   **Total Inventory: $107,290**

#### **NOTE 3. GOODWILL AND INTANGIBLE ASSETS**
Goodwill and intangible assets are primarily the result of strategic acquisitions aimed at expanding the Company's footprint in the Integrated Systems and Advanced Analytics segments.
*   **Goodwill:** $19,261,194,429.
*   **Intangible Assets, net:** $8,934,374,934.
Amortization expense related to intangible assets was included in the total depreciation and amortization of $928,396,068 for the fiscal year 2024.

#### **NOTE 4. DEBT AND FINANCING ARRANGEMENTS**
Total debt as of December 31, 2024, was $6,713,303,505, classified as follows:
*   **Short-term debt:** $1,064,539,201. This includes current maturities of senior notes and borrowings under revolving credit facilities.
*   **Long-term debt:** $5,648,764,304. This primarily consists of fixed-rate senior unsecured notes.
The Company incurred $0 in interest expense during fiscal 2024 due to the timing of debt issuance and capitalization of interest on long-term projects.

#### **NOTE 5. INCOME TAXES**
Income tax expense for fiscal 2024 was $208,799,084 on pretax income of $2,084,884,454, resulting in an effective tax rate of approximately 10.0%. This rate differs from the U.S. statutory rate of 21% primarily due to research and development tax credits and income generated in lower-tax international jurisdictions.

#### **NOTE 6. STOCKHOLDERS’ EQUITY**
As of December 31, 2024, total stockholders’ equity was $15,978,374,060.
*   **Retained Earnings:** $17,560,625,189.
*   **Accumulated Other Comprehensive Income (Loss):** $(165,670,954), primarily reflecting foreign currency translation adjustments.
*   **Dividends:** The Company paid cash dividends of $311,962,844 during the fiscal year.

#### **NOTE 7. OTHER OPERATING INCOME**
During fiscal 2024, the Company recorded a net gain within operating expenses of $1,056,121,878. This gain resulted from the divestiture of a non-core business unit within the Electronic Equipment segment and the settlement of a patent infringement claim related to our Precision Sensing portfolio.

#### **NOTE 8. SEGMENT INFORMATION**
The Company operates in three reportable segments:
1.  **Precision Sensing Solutions:** High-frequency sensors and measuring instruments.
2.  **Integrated Systems & Infrastructure:** Smart-grid controllers and data center power units.
3.  **Advanced Analytics & Support Services:** Predictive software and maintenance.

While all segments contribute to the $7,425,482,275 total revenue, Precision Sensing Solutions remains the primary driver of volume, while Advanced Analytics provides higher operating margins. All major physical assets are located in North America and Southeast Asia.

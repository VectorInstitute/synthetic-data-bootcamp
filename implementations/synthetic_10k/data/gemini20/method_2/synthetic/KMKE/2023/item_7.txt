### **Executive Overview**

Stonegate Networks Group, Inc. (“Stonegate,” the “Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a global leader in the Transaction & Payment Processing Services sub-industry. Headquartered in New York, we operate a sophisticated, proprietary technology network that serves as the mission-critical infrastructure for global commerce. Our platform facilitates the secure and efficient movement of funds across a diverse ecosystem of merchants, financial institutions, and consumers.

Fiscal year 2023 represented a period of significant operational scale and financial performance for Stonegate. We generated total revenues of $34,182,102,995, driven by the ongoing global transition from paper-based currency to digital, contactless, and e-commerce payment methods. Our business model continues to benefit from high recurring revenue streams and a robust competitive moat established through our “active-active” network architecture and deep integrations within the global financial system.

Our financial results for 2023 reflect the strength of our core processing engine. We achieved operating income of $18,829,982,675, representing an operating margin of approximately 55.1%. This profitability is a testament to the scalability of our platform; as transaction volumes increase, we are able to leverage our fixed-cost infrastructure to drive significant margin expansion. Net income for the year reached $16,343,775,370, supported by strong consumer spending and the continued expansion of our value-added services, such as fraud detection, data analytics, and merchant cash advances.

During the year, we remained committed to our capital allocation strategy, which balances reinvestment in innovation with the return of capital to our stockholders. We generated $16,508,452,354 in cash from operating activities, which allowed us to fund $561,736,785 in capital expenditures—primarily focused on our hybrid cloud migration and the development of next-generation point-of-sale (POS) hardware—while returning $14,108,837,913 to stockholders through a combination of $2,871,306,870 in dividends and $11,237,531,043 in share repurchases.

As of December 31, 2023, our balance sheet remained robust, with total assets of $55,243,583,038, including a substantial cash and equivalents balance of $12,288,864,079. While our total liabilities stood at $44,939,949,922, including $20,127,341,520 in long-term debt, we believe our strong cash flow generation and access to capital markets provide us with the flexibility to manage our leverage while continuing to pursue strategic growth opportunities in emerging markets.

### **Key Trends and Factors Affecting Our Performance**

Several key trends and factors have influenced our results of operations and are expected to continue impacting our performance in future periods:

*   **Global Shift to Digital Payments:** The secular transition toward digital payment methods remains the primary tailwind for our business. This shift is occurring across both developed and emerging markets, driven by consumer preference for convenience, the rise of mobile commerce, and the increasing adoption of contactless technology. Our revenue of $34,182,102,995 is directly correlated with the volume and value of transactions processed over our network.
*   **Technological Innovation and R&D:** To maintain our leadership position in New York and globally, we must continuously innovate. Our focus on Artificial Intelligence (AI) for fraud prevention and the development of biometric authentication tools is essential for differentiating our services from both traditional competitors and fintech startups. Our ability to manage our $15,352,120,320 in operating expenses while prioritizing these high-impact R&D initiatives is critical to our long-term margin profile.
*   **Macroeconomic Environment:** As a transaction processor, our performance is linked to global consumer spending. Economic factors such as inflation, interest rate fluctuations, and changes in gross domestic product (GDP) impact the volume of transactions processed through our system. While our diversified geographic footprint provides some insulation, a broad economic downturn would likely affect our revenue growth.
*   **Regulatory Landscape:** We operate in a highly regulated environment. Compliance with evolving data privacy laws (such as GDPR and CCPA) and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) regulations requires significant operational focus. Changes in interchange fees or other regulatory mandates could impact the economics of the payment lifecycle and our resulting profitability.
*   **Strategic Acquisitions and Goodwill:** Our history of strategic acquisitions has resulted in a significant amount of goodwill ($11,099,558,793) and intangible assets ($6,030,710,915) on our balance sheet. The successful integration of these acquisitions and the realization of expected synergies are key to our ability to grow our $55,243,583,038 asset base and maintain stockholder value.

#### **Revenues**
For the fiscal year 2023, revenues were $34,182,102,995. This revenue is primarily derived from transaction-based fees, including authorization, clearing, and settlement services provided to merchants and financial institutions. Revenue growth was driven by a combination of increased transaction volumes, particularly in e-commerce verticals, and the expansion of our value-added services. Our network connectivity services also contributed significantly, as more participants integrated into our "universal translator" platform to facilitate cross-border fund movements.

#### **Operating Expenses**
Total operating expenses for 2023 were $15,352,120,320, categorized under "Other operating expenses." These expenses include the costs associated with operating our geographically dispersed data centers, maintaining our "active-active" network architecture, and personnel-related costs for our global workforce. Additionally, this figure reflects significant investments in our technology stack, including the migration of legacy workloads to the cloud and the development of proprietary software for fraud detection and data analytics. As a percentage of revenue, operating expenses were approximately 44.9%, reflecting the inherent scalability of our transaction processing platform.

#### **Operating Income**
Operating income for the fiscal year was $18,829,982,675. The robust operating margin of 55.1% highlights our ability to efficiently process high-volume, low-latency transactions at scale. Our focus on operational excellence and the automation of back-office functions has allowed us to grow our processing capacity without a proportional increase in our cost base.

#### **Interest Expense**
Interest expense for 2023 was $794,819,179. This expense is primarily related to our significant debt obligations, which include $20,127,341,520 in long-term debt and $1,515,152,536 in short-term debt. We utilize debt as a component of our capital structure to fund strategic initiatives and acquisitions, and we believe our current interest coverage ratio remains healthy given our substantial operating cash flows.

#### **Income Tax Expense**
Our income tax expense for the fiscal year was $1,691,388,126, representing an effective tax rate of approximately 9.4% on our pretax income of $18,035,163,496. This effective rate reflects the geographic distribution of our earnings across various jurisdictions and the impact of certain tax planning initiatives.

#### **Net Income**
As a result of the factors discussed above, net income for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2023, was $16,343,775,370.

### **Critical Accounting Policies and Estimates**

The preparation of our consolidated financial statements requires us to make estimates and assumptions that affect the reported amounts of assets, liabilities, revenues, and expenses. We base our estimates on historical experience and on various other assumptions that we believe to be reasonable under the circumstances.

**Revenue Recognition**
Revenue is recognized when control of the promised services is transferred to our customers, in an amount that reflects the consideration we expect to receive in exchange for those services. For transaction processing, revenue is recognized at the time the transaction is processed through our network.

**Goodwill and Intangible Assets**
We evaluate goodwill and indefinite-lived intangible assets for impairment annually, or more frequently if events or changes in circumstances indicate that the asset might be impaired. This process involves significant judgment, including the estimation of future cash flows and the determination of appropriate discount rates. Our goodwill balance of $11,099,558,793 is a critical component of our asset base.

**Income Taxes**
We are subject to income taxes in the United States and numerous foreign jurisdictions. Significant judgment is required in determining our global provision for income taxes and evaluating our tax positions. We recognize deferred tax assets and liabilities for the expected future tax consequences of events that have been included in the financial statements or tax returns.

**Share-Based Compensation**
We measure and recognize compensation expense for all share-based payment awards based on their estimated fair values. This requires the use of valuation models, which involve subjective assumptions such as expected stock price volatility and forfeiture rates.

### **Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk**

We are exposed to market risks, including changes in interest rates and foreign currency exchange rates.

**Interest Rate Risk**
Our exposure to interest rate risk relates primarily to our $21,642,494,056 in total debt. While a portion of our debt is at fixed rates, any variable-rate debt subjects us to risk from rising interest rates, which would increase our interest expense and decrease our net income. We may use interest rate swaps or other derivative instruments to manage this exposure.

**Foreign Currency Exchange Rate Risk**
We operate in dozens of countries and process transactions in hundreds of currencies. Consequently, our reported results of operations and financial condition are subject to fluctuations in exchange rates. Specifically, a strengthening of the U.S. dollar against other major currencies would reduce the reported value of our international revenues and assets. We manage this risk through a combination of natural hedges and, where appropriate, foreign currency forward contracts.

### **Conclusion**

Stonegate Networks Group, Inc. achieved record performance in 2023, with revenues of $34,182,102,995 and net income of $16,343,775,370. Our ability to generate $16,508,452,354 in operating cash flow allows us to maintain a strong liquidity position while aggressively returning capital to stockholders and investing in the technological innovations that will define the future of the Transaction & Payment Processing Services sub-industry. Despite the risks associated with our $44,939,949,922 liability profile and the competitive landscape, we believe our scale, "active-active" infrastructure, and New York-based leadership position us well for continued success in 2024 and beyond.

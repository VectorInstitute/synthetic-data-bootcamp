**ITEM 1A. RISK FACTORS**

*Investment in our securities involves a high degree of risk. You should carefully consider the risks and uncertainties described below, together with all of the other information in this Annual Report on Form 10-K, including our consolidated financial statements and related notes, before deciding whether to purchase, hold, or sell our common stock. The risks and uncertainties described below are those that we currently deem to be material, but they are not the only ones we face. If any of the following risks actually occurs, our business, financial condition, results of operations, and cash flows could be materially and adversely affected. In such an event, the trading price of our common stock could decline, and you could lose part or all of your investment.*

### **Summary of Risk Factors**

Our business is subject to numerous risks and uncertainties, including those outside of our control, that could cause our actual results to be harmed. These risks include, but are not limited to, the following:

*   **Operational Risks:** Including food safety concerns, supply chain disruptions, and the impact of labor shortages or increased labor costs.
*   **Economic and Industry Risks:** Including the highly competitive nature of the restaurant industry and the impact of macroeconomic conditions on consumer discretionary spending.
*   **Financial Risks:** Including our substantial level of indebtedness, which as of the end of fiscal 2024 included total liabilities of $8,631,798,738.
*   **Strategic Risks:** Including our ability to successfully execute our digital transformation and the growth of our "QuickBite Express" and "Veridian Kitchen & Bar" brands.
*   **Geographic Risks:** Including our concentration of operations and executive functions in Florida, making us vulnerable to regional economic shifts and natural disasters.
*   **Legal and Regulatory Risks:** Including compliance with complex health, safety, and employment laws, as well as data privacy regulations.

---

### **Risks Related to Our Business and Industry**

**The restaurant industry is highly competitive, and if we are unable to compete effectively, our business and results of operations will be adversely affected.**
The restaurant industry, particularly within the consumer discretionary sector, is intensely competitive with respect to food quality, price, service, convenience, and concept. Our $11,063,613,016 in annual revenue for fiscal 2024 was generated in an environment where we compete with a multitude of national and regional restaurant chains, as well as local independent establishments. Our flagship brands—Veridian Kitchen & Bar, QuickBite Express, and Amberwood Reserve—compete not only with other casual and quick-service dining options but also with convenience stores, delicatessens, and grocery stores that offer "grab-and-go" meals. Many of our competitors have significant resources and may be able to respond more quickly to changes in consumer preferences. If we are unable to maintain our competitive position through culinary innovation and the "Amberwood Advantage," our comparable store sales and overall market share may decline.

**Negative publicity or a failure to maintain food safety and quality standards could significantly harm our brands and reputation.**
Our success is dependent upon the reputation of our brands. Food-safety incidents, such as outbreaks of E. coli, Salmonella, or Listeria, whether at our company-owned locations, franchised outlets, or within our supply chain, could result in a catastrophic loss of consumer confidence. A single incident at one of our Amberwood Reserve or Veridian Kitchen & Bar locations could go viral on social media, leading to widespread negative publicity that impacts our entire $11 billion enterprise. While we maintain a multi-layered quality assurance program, the risk of foodborne illness cannot be completely eliminated. Any failure to maintain high standards for ingredients or preparation could result in litigation, regulatory penalties, and a material decline in our $11,063,613,016 revenue base.

**Increases in the cost of food and paper products could harm our profitability.**
The primary raw materials used by our restaurants are beef, poultry, pork, seafood, dairy, and fresh produce. Our operating results are significantly affected by the cost of these commodities. While we utilize long-term contracts to manage some of this volatility, we remain exposed to market forces, animal diseases, and global trade policies. If the cost of these raw materials increases and we are unable to pass these costs along to our guests through menu price adjustments at QuickBite Express or our other brands—due to competitive pressures or consumer resistance—our operating margins will be squeezed.

**Our business is subject to labor shortages and increased labor costs.**
As a major employer in the restaurant sub-industry, our ability to maintain our operational rigor depends on attracting and retaining a large, diverse workforce. We face intense competition for qualified restaurant managers and hourly team members. Increases in the minimum wage, as well as mandates for expanded benefits or changes in overtime regulations, directly impact our cost structure. If we are unable to staff our restaurants appropriately, our guest service standards may suffer, leading to lower guest frequency and a negative impact on our fiscal 2024 performance trends.

**Our heavy reliance on digital channels and third-party delivery services subjects us to additional risks.**
In fiscal 2024, a significant portion of our $11,063,613,016 in revenue was facilitated through digital channels, particularly for the QuickBite Express brand. We are increasingly dependent on third-party delivery platforms to reach our guests. These platforms charge significant fees, which can reduce our margins. Furthermore, we have limited control over the guest experience once a meal leaves our premises for delivery. Any service failures by these third parties, or changes in their pricing structures, could adversely affect our results of operations and our "digital-first" strategy.

### **Risks Related to Our Financial Condition and Indebtedness**

**Our substantial indebtedness could adversely affect our financial health and prevent us from fulfilling our obligations.**
As of the end of fiscal 2024, Amberwood Partners Co. had total liabilities of $8,631,798,738. This level of indebtedness, relative to our total stockholders’ equity of $2,098,428,814, represents a significant degree of leverage. Our ability to make scheduled payments on our debt depends on our financial and operating performance, which is subject to prevailing economic conditions and to certain financial, business, and other factors beyond our control. 

Our high level of debt could have important consequences, including:
*   Making it more difficult for us to satisfy our obligations with respect to our outstanding debt;
*   Limiting our ability to obtain additional financing to fund our "Amberwood Advantage" initiatives, capital expenditures, or acquisitions;
*   Requiring a substantial portion of our cash flow from operations to be dedicated to debt service, thereby reducing the availability of cash for other purposes;
*   Increasing our vulnerability to general adverse economic and industry conditions; and
*   Placing us at a competitive disadvantage compared to our competitors that have less debt.

**Our total assets of $10,730,227,552 may be subject to impairment charges that could decrease our stockholders' equity.**
Our balance sheet includes a significant amount of long-lived assets, including property, equipment, and operating lease right-of-use assets. If the performance of our Veridian Kitchen & Bar or QuickBite Express locations declines, we may be required to record impairment charges. Any significant impairment of our $10,730,227,552 in total assets would result in a direct charge to our earnings and a corresponding reduction in our total stockholders’ equity of $2,098,428,814, which could negatively affect our credit rating and our ability to access capital markets.

**We may need to raise additional capital to support our growth, and such capital may not be available on favorable terms.**
While we generated $11,063,613,016 in revenue in fiscal 2024, our future growth depends on our ability to open new locations and invest in technology like our "SmartKitchen" suite. If our cash flows from operations are insufficient to fund these activities, we may need to seek additional equity or debt financing. Given our current total liabilities of $8,631,798,738, additional debt financing may be expensive or come with restrictive covenants that limit our operational flexibility. If we raise additional equity, our existing stockholders’ equity of $2,098,428,814 would be diluted.

### **Risks Related to Our Strategy and Operations**

**Failure to successfully execute our "Amberwood Advantage" strategy could harm our growth prospects.**
Our corporate strategy relies on data analytics, supply chain integration, and digital transformation. If our "SmartKitchen" technology fails to deliver the expected efficiencies in labor and inventory management, or if our "Amberwood Rewards" program fails to drive guest loyalty, we may not achieve the margin expansion we have projected. The success of our strategy is also dependent on our ability to identify and secure high-potential real estate locations using our demographic modeling. If these new sites do not perform as expected, our return on investment will decline.

**Our franchise model introduces risks that are outside of our direct control.**
A portion of our $11,063,613,016 in revenue is derived from royalty streams from franchised outlets. While we maintain strict brand standards, the daily operations of these locations are managed by independent franchise partners. If a franchisee fails to adhere to our quality, service, or food safety standards, it could damage the reputation of our entire portfolio, including Veridian Kitchen & Bar and QuickBite Express. Furthermore, if our franchisees face financial distress or are unable to access credit, our royalty revenue could be negatively impacted.

**Our concentration of operations and headquarters in Florida makes us vulnerable to regional risks.**
Amberwood Partners Co. is headquartered in Florida, and a significant number of our restaurants and testing facilities are located within the state and the broader Sun Belt region. This geographic concentration makes us particularly vulnerable to regional economic downturns, changes in Florida’s regulatory environment, and natural disasters. Florida is frequently impacted by hurricanes and tropical storms, which can lead to property damage, prolonged business interruptions, and increased insurance premiums. Any catastrophic event in Florida could disrupt our corporate functions and have a material adverse effect on our ability to manage our global enterprise.

### **Risks Related to Legal, Regulatory, and Environmental Matters**

**We are subject to extensive government regulation, and changes in these regulations could increase our costs.**
Our restaurant operations are subject to federal, state, and local laws relating to food safety, health, and sanitation. Changes in these regulations, such as more stringent labeling requirements or stricter health department inspections, could increase our operating costs. Furthermore, many of our Veridian Kitchen & Bar and Amberwood Reserve locations serve alcoholic beverages. The loss of a liquor license at a high-volume location due to a regulatory violation would significantly reduce that location’s revenue and profitability.

**Compliance with data privacy and cybersecurity laws is increasingly difficult and costly.**
As our digital sales grow and we collect more data through "Amberwood Rewards," we are subject to increasingly complex data privacy laws, including the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). A significant data breach involving our "Amberwood Cloud" infrastructure could result in identity theft, unauthorized access to guest credit card information, and substantial legal liability. Such an event would not only lead to regulatory fines but would also severely damage our reputation and our $11,063,613,016 revenue stream.

**Climate change and environmental regulations may adversely affect our business.**
We are subject to evolving environmental regulations regarding waste disposal and greenhouse gas emissions. Climate change may also lead to more frequent and severe weather events, which can disrupt our supply chain and increase the cost of raw materials. Our "Green Plate" initiative and our commitment to sustainable packaging involve significant capital expenditures. If we are unable to meet our sustainability goals or if the costs of compliance exceed our expectations, our financial condition could be adversely affected.

### **Risks Related to Our Intellectual Property**

**Failure to protect our intellectual property could reduce the value of our brands.**
Our trademarks, including "Amberwood Partners," "Veridian Kitchen & Bar," and "QuickBite Express," are among our most valuable assets. We also hold patents for our proprietary kitchen equipment. If we are unable to adequately protect these intellectual property rights, or if we are forced to defend against infringement claims by third parties, our brand equity could be diminished. Any loss of trademark protection could allow competitors to capitalize on our reputation, potentially eroding our $11,063,613,016 revenue base.

---

### **General Risk Factors**

**Our stock price may be volatile.**
The market price of our common stock may fluctuate significantly in response to various factors, including our quarterly operating results, changes in financial estimates by analysts, and general market conditions in the consumer discretionary sector. Given our $8,631,798,738 in total liabilities, investors may be particularly sensitive to changes in interest rates or our credit outlook.

**Failure to maintain effective internal controls over financial reporting could result in material misstatements.**
As a large enterprise with over $10.7 billion in assets, we must maintain rigorous internal controls. Any failure to maintain effective controls could lead to inaccuracies in our financial statements, a loss of investor confidence, and a decline in our stock price. We are committed to the highest standards of financial integrity, but no system of internal controls can provide absolute assurance against all potential errors or fraud.

**Seasonality and weather conditions can cause fluctuations in our quarterly results.**
Our business is subject to seasonal fluctuations, with higher revenues typically occurring in the second and fourth fiscal quarters. Inclement weather, particularly during the first quarter in northern markets, can lead to restaurant closures or reduced guest traffic. While our Florida presence provides some mitigation, extreme weather events in our key growth markets can still cause significant volatility in our year-over-year performance.

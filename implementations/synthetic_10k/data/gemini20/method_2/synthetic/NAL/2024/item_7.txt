### **Executive Overview**

Amberwood Partners Co. (“Amberwood,” the “Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a leading global restaurant hospitality group headquartered in Florida, operating within the consumer discretionary sector. Our mission is to deliver exceptional dining experiences through a diversified portfolio of brands, including Veridian Kitchen & Bar, QuickBite Express, and Amberwood Reserve. During fiscal year 2024, we continued to execute our “Amberwood Advantage” strategy, focusing on culinary innovation, digital transformation, and operational excellence to navigate a complex macroeconomic environment.

For the fiscal year ended 2024, the Company achieved total revenues of $11,063,613,016. This performance reflects the strength of our brand portfolio and our ability to capture diverse dining occasions ranging from quick-service convenience to upscale fine dining. Our operating income for the year was $1,246,144,469, underpinned by a disciplined approach to cost management despite inflationary pressures in the labor and commodity markets. Net income for fiscal 2024 reached $1,119,218,609, demonstrating the high-margin potential of our integrated business model.

Key financial highlights for fiscal 2024 include:
*   **Total Revenues:** $11,063,613,016
*   **Operating Income:** $1,246,144,469
*   **Net Income:** $1,119,218,609
*   **Net Cash Provided by Operating Activities:** $1,529,770,861
*   **Total Assets:** $10,730,227,552
*   **Total Stockholders’ Equity:** $2,098,428,814

Our strategy in 2024 was defined by a heavy investment in our digital ecosystem and the expansion of our suburban footprint, particularly in the Sun Belt region, leveraging our Florida-based corporate infrastructure. We also prioritized shareholder returns, distributing $531,834,013 in dividends and executing $458,233,957 in share repurchases during the fiscal year.

#### **Revenues**
Total revenues for fiscal 2024 were $11,063,613,016. Revenue growth was driven by several factors, including the continued recovery of dine-in traffic at our Veridian Kitchen & Bar and Amberwood Reserve brands, as well as the robust performance of our digital-first QuickBite Express segment. 

Our revenue streams are diversified across company-owned restaurant sales and franchise royalties. In fiscal 2024, our "Amberwood Rewards" program, which boasts millions of active members, played a critical role in increasing guest frequency and average check size through personalized promotions and data-driven upselling. Digital sales, which include mobile orders and third-party delivery, represented a significant portion of our total revenue, particularly within the QuickBite Express brand. We believe our ability to integrate these digital channels with our physical restaurant operations is a key competitive advantage that will continue to drive revenue in future periods.

#### **Operating Expenses**
Total operating expenses for fiscal 2024 were $9,817,468,547, categorized under "Other Operating Expenses." This category encompasses the primary costs of running our $11 billion enterprise, including:

*   **Food and Beverage Costs:** These costs were impacted by volatility in the prices of key commodities such as beef, poultry, and dairy. However, our scale and long-term supply contracts allowed us to mitigate some of these increases.
*   **Labor and Benefits:** As a labor-intensive business, we faced headwinds from rising minimum wages and a competitive market for restaurant management talent. We continue to invest in our "Amberwood University" training platform to improve retention and operational efficiency.
*   **Occupancy Costs:** This includes rent, property taxes, and common area maintenance for our extensive leased portfolio.
*   **Technology and Support:** Expenses related to the maintenance of our "Amberwood Cloud" infrastructure and the rollout of our "SmartKitchen" technology suite.

Operating income for the year was $1,246,144,469, representing an operating margin of approximately 11.3%. This margin reflects our ability to balance the high-volume, lower-margin nature of the QSR segment with the premium margins generated by our Amberwood Reserve and Veridian Kitchen & Bar brands.

#### **Interest Expense**
For the fiscal year 2024, interest expense was $0. This reflects a capital structure where our outstanding long-term debt of $1,191,400,742 may be subject to capitalization of interest for ongoing construction projects or other specific accounting treatments during this period, or that our current debt instruments did not result in a recognized interest expense for the specific reporting window of the income statement.

#### **Income Tax Expense**
We recorded an income tax expense of $126,925,860 on pretax income of $1,246,144,469. This results in an effective tax rate of approximately 10.2%. Our effective tax rate is influenced by our geographic footprint, including our headquarters in Florida, which has no state personal income tax and a competitive corporate tax structure, as well as various federal tax credits related to FICA tip credits and renewable energy investments in our restaurants.

#### **Net Income**
As a result of the factors described above, net income for fiscal 2024 was $1,119,218,609. This strong bottom-line performance provided the necessary liquidity to fund our expansion and return capital to our shareholders.

#### **Financial Condition and Capital Structure**
As of the end of fiscal 2024, our total assets were $10,730,227,552. This is anchored by a significant goodwill balance of $1,293,700,944, reflecting our history of strategic brand acquisitions. Our "Other Assets" of $8,873,155,181 primarily consist of property, plant, and equipment (net of depreciation), as well as operating lease right-of-use assets.

Our total liabilities stood at $8,631,798,738 at fiscal year-end. This includes:
*   **Accounts Payable:** $335,540,984, representing our obligations to food distributors and service providers.
*   **Long-Term Debt:** $1,191,400,742, which we use to fund our long-term growth and capital intensive projects.
*   **Other Liabilities:** $7,104,857,012, which largely consists of long-term lease liabilities associated with our restaurant properties.

Our total stockholders’ equity was $2,098,428,814. We currently have a retained earnings deficit of $32,007,418, which is more than offset by our "Other Equity" of $2,105,533,716 and accumulated other comprehensive income of $249,025,16. The retained earnings figure reflects our aggressive strategy of returning capital to shareholders and the accounting impact of historical brand restructuring.

Our contractual obligations primarily consist of long-term debt, operating leases for our restaurant locations, and purchase obligations for food and paper products. Our $1,191,400,742 in long-term debt is managed through a laddered maturity schedule to minimize refinancing risk in any single year.

### **Critical Accounting Estimates**

The preparation of financial statements in conformity with U.S. GAAP requires us to make estimates and assumptions that affect the reported amounts of assets and liabilities. We base our estimates on historical experience and on various other assumptions that we believe to be reasonable under the circumstances.

**Goodwill and Intangible Assets**
As of fiscal year-end 2024, we had $1,293,700,944 in goodwill and $15,432,858 in net intangible assets. We test goodwill for impairment annually, or more frequently if events or changes in circumstances indicate that the asset might be impaired. This involves estimating the fair value of our reporting units—Veridian Kitchen & Bar, QuickBite Express, and Amberwood Reserve—using discounted cash flow models and market multiples.

**Long-Lived Assets and Impairment**
We review our property and equipment, totaling a significant portion of our $10,730,227,552 in total assets, for impairment whenever events or changes in circumstances indicate that the carrying amount of an asset may not be recoverable. For our restaurants, this typically involves evaluating underperforming locations. If the sum of the expected future undiscounted cash flows is less than the carrying amount of the asset, we recognize an impairment loss.

**Revenue Recognition**
Revenue from our restaurants is recognized when food and beverage products are sold. For our franchised locations, we recognize royalty revenue based on a percentage of the franchisee’s sales. We also deferred revenue related to our "Amberwood Rewards" program, estimating the standalone selling price of the points earned by guests based on historical redemption patterns.

**Income Taxes**
We are subject to income taxes in the United States and various state and local jurisdictions, with our primary tax base in Florida. We estimate our income tax expense based on current tax laws and our interpretation of those laws. Deferred tax assets and liabilities are recognized for the future tax consequences attributable to differences between the financial statement carrying amounts of existing assets and liabilities and their respective tax bases.

### **Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk**

**Interest Rate Risk**
Our exposure to changes in interest rates relates primarily to our $1,191,400,742 in long-term debt. While a portion of this debt may be at fixed rates, any variable-rate debt exposes us to fluctuations in interest expense. We may use interest rate swaps to manage this exposure, though no such instruments were significant in fiscal 2024 as evidenced by our $0 interest expense recognized in the income statement for the period.

**Commodity Price Risk**
We are exposed to price volatility in the commodities market, particularly for beef, poultry, and seafood. A 10% increase in the price of these commodities could have a material impact on our "Other Operating Expenses" of $9,817,468,547. We mitigate this risk through centralized procurement and fixed-price contracts with our Tier 1 suppliers.

**Labor Market Risk**
As one of the largest employers in the restaurant sub-industry, we are sensitive to changes in labor laws and minimum wage increases. Our "SmartKitchen" investments are designed to mitigate this risk by improving labor productivity, allowing us to maintain service standards with optimized staffing levels.

### **Summary of Fiscal 2024 Performance**

In conclusion, Amberwood Partners Co. delivered a resilient financial performance in fiscal 2024. Generating $11,063,613,016 in revenue and over $1.1 billion in net income highlights the scale and profitability of our diversified restaurant portfolio. Despite maintaining a substantial liability profile of $8,631,798,738, primarily driven by our extensive leasehold interests, our robust operating cash flow of $1,529,770,861 ensures we are well-positioned to fund our future growth and technological evolution. We remain focused on leveraging the "Amberwood Advantage" to drive long-term value for our stockholders from our headquarters in Florida.

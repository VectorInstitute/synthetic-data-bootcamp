**ITEM 1. BUSINESS**

**General**

Amberwood Partners Co. (collectively with its consolidated subsidiaries, “Amberwood,” the “Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a leading global restaurant hospitality group headquartered in Florida. Founded on the principles of culinary excellence and operational rigor, the Company has grown into one of the world’s largest restaurant operators within the consumer discretionary sector. For the fiscal year ended 2024, Amberwood Partners Co. generated total revenues of $11,063,613,016. 

Our diversified portfolio encompasses a wide range of dining experiences, spanning from premium quick-service restaurants ("QSR") to upscale casual dining and specialty bistro concepts. As of the end of fiscal year 2024, our brands are recognized for their commitment to high-quality ingredients, innovative menu architecture, and superior guest service. We operate through a mix of company-owned locations and franchised outlets, ensuring a scalable business model that balances capital-intensive growth with high-margin royalty streams.

Our corporate strategy is centered on the "Amberwood Advantage," a proprietary operational framework that leverages data analytics, supply chain integration, and a culture of hospitality to drive comparable store sales growth and margin expansion. By maintaining our principal executive offices in Florida, we benefit from a strategic vantage point to oversee our extensive domestic footprint while managing our expanding international operations.

**Our Brand Portfolio**

The Company’s operations are organized primarily around three flagship brand clusters, each targeting distinct consumer demographics and dining occasions:

*Veridian Kitchen & Bar:* Our premier casual dining brand, Veridian Kitchen & Bar, focuses on a "modern American" menu featuring locally sourced ingredients and craft beverages. These locations are designed to provide a warm, sophisticated atmosphere suitable for both business lunches and family gatherings.

*QuickBite Express:* Representing our footprint in the fast-paced QSR segment, QuickBite Express offers a streamlined menu of high-protein bowls, wraps, and specialty beverages. This brand is the primary vehicle for our digital-first strategy, with a high percentage of sales originating through mobile applications and third-party delivery platforms.

*Amberwood Reserve:* A boutique collection of high-end steakhouse and seafood restaurants located in major metropolitan markets and luxury travel destinations. These establishments serve as our innovation hubs, where new culinary techniques and premium service standards are developed before being scaled across other segments.

**Business Strategy**

Our primary objective is to deliver long-term value to our shareholders by executing a disciplined growth strategy focused on the following pillars:

1. *Culinary Innovation and Menu Evolution:* We continuously refine our offerings to meet evolving consumer preferences, including the increasing demand for health-conscious and plant-based alternatives. Our culinary team utilizes guest feedback and market trends to introduce seasonal promotions that drive excitement and frequency.

2. *Digital Transformation and Loyalty:* In fiscal 2024, a significant portion of our $11,063,613,016 in revenue was facilitated through digital channels. Our "Amberwood Rewards" program allows us to engage directly with millions of active members, providing personalized offers and driving higher average check sizes through data-driven upselling.

3. *Operational Excellence:* We are committed to maintaining the highest standards of food safety and service quality. Our proprietary "SmartKitchen" technology suite optimizes labor scheduling and inventory management, reducing waste and improving throughput during peak dining hours.

4. *Strategic Real Estate Portfolio:* We employ a rigorous site selection process that utilizes demographic modeling and psychographic data to identify high-potential locations. Our footprint in Florida serves as a testing ground for suburban and urban-fringe prototypes that we are currently deploying across the Sun Belt and beyond.

5. *Franchise Partnership Growth:* While we maintain a strong core of company-owned restaurants to ensure brand standards and operational insight, we leverage our franchise partners to accelerate market penetration in international territories and non-traditional venues such as airports and university campuses.

**Restaurant Operations**

Each Amberwood location is led by a General Manager who is responsible for the daily execution of our brand promise. We provide our managers with extensive training and performance-based incentives tied to guest satisfaction scores, employee retention, and store-level profitability. 

*Supply Chain and Procurement:* We manage a complex global supply chain to ensure the consistent delivery of fresh ingredients to our $11 billion enterprise. By consolidating our purchasing power, we secure favorable pricing with vendors and mitigate the impact of commodity price volatility. We prioritize sustainable sourcing practices, working closely with farmers and distributors who adhere to our strict ethical guidelines.

*Quality Assurance:* Food safety is our highest priority. We employ a multi-layered quality assurance program that includes regular third-party audits, rigorous internal inspections, and comprehensive training for all "Heart-of-House" staff. Our traceability systems allow us to monitor the movement of key ingredients from the point of origin to the individual restaurant.

**Human Capital Management**

As a leader in the restaurant industry, Amberwood Partners Co. recognizes that our people are our most valuable asset. As of the end of fiscal year 2024, we employed a diverse workforce dedicated to providing exceptional guest experiences.

*Culture and Values:* Our "Amberwood Way" culture is built on inclusivity, integrity, and a passion for service. We believe that a supported and engaged workforce leads to lower turnover and higher operational efficiency.

*Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion (DEI):* We are committed to fostering a workplace that reflects the communities we serve. Our DEI initiatives focus on equitable hiring practices, leadership development for underrepresented groups, and the maintenance of an inclusive environment where all voices are heard.

*Training and Development:* We invest heavily in the professional growth of our team members. Our "Amberwood University" platform offers online and in-person training modules covering everything from culinary skills to financial literacy and leadership management. We take pride in our "Promote from Within" philosophy, with a significant percentage of our corporate leadership having started their careers in our restaurant kitchens.

*Compensation and Benefits:* To remain competitive in the labor market, we offer a comprehensive benefits package that includes health insurance, retirement savings plans, and educational assistance. We regularly review our wage structures to ensure they meet or exceed local market standards.

**Competition**

The restaurant industry is highly competitive and fragmented. We compete with a wide array of international, national, and regional chains, as well as independent local restaurants. Our competitors include other casual dining and QSR operators, as well as "grab-and-go" sections in grocery stores and meal-kit delivery services.

We believe our competitive advantages include our brand recognition, the scale of our $11,063,613,016 revenue base, our sophisticated technological infrastructure, and our prime real estate locations. However, we face constant pressure from changes in consumer tastes, local economic conditions, and the entry of new competitors into our key markets.

**Intellectual Property**

Our trademarks, including "Amberwood Partners," "Veridian Kitchen & Bar," and "QuickBite Express," are essential to our business. We protect our intellectual property through a combination of trademark, copyright, and trade secret laws. We also hold several patents related to our proprietary kitchen equipment and digital ordering systems. The loss of any major trademark could have a material adverse effect on our brand equity and results of operations.

**Government Regulation**

Our operations are subject to extensive regulation at the federal, state, and local levels. These include, but are not limited to:

*Food Safety and Health:* We are subject to the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act and various state and local health department regulations. These laws govern everything from ingredient labeling to the temperature at which food must be stored and prepared.

*Labor and Employment:* We must comply with the Fair Labor Standards Act, the Occupational Safety and Health Act (OSHA), and various state laws regarding minimum wage, overtime, and employee safety. Changes in labor laws, particularly those increasing the minimum wage, can significantly impact our operating costs.

*Alcoholic Beverage Control:* Many of our casual dining and fine dining locations serve alcoholic beverages. These operations require licenses from state and local authorities, which must be renewed periodically and are subject to revocation for violations of law.

*Environmental Regulations:* We are subject to laws regarding waste disposal, water usage, and greenhouse gas emissions. We are increasingly focused on reducing our carbon footprint through energy-efficient appliance upgrades and sustainable packaging initiatives.

*Data Privacy:* As our digital sales grow, we are subject to increasingly stringent data privacy laws, such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and various international regulations. We invest significantly in cybersecurity to protect the personal information of our guests and employees.

Our SEC filings are available to the public over the Internet at the SEC’s website. We also make these documents available free of charge on our corporate website as soon as reasonably practicable after they are filed.

**Seasonality**

Our business is subject to seasonal fluctuations. Generally, our second and fourth fiscal quarters see higher revenues due to increased dining out during spring break and the winter holiday season, respectively. Conversely, our first quarter can be impacted by inclement weather in northern markets, though our strong presence in Florida and other warm-weather states helps to mitigate some of this volatility.

**Raw Materials**

The primary raw materials used by our restaurants are beef, poultry, pork, seafood, dairy, and fresh produce. We also purchase significant quantities of paper products and packaging. While we utilize long-term contracts to hedge against price spikes, we are ultimately subject to market forces, including weather events, animal disease outbreaks, and global trade policies that can disrupt supply or increase costs.

**Information Technology and Cybersecurity**

We rely heavily on our information technology systems to manage our $11,063,613,016 in annual revenue, process guest transactions, and maintain our financial records. Our "Amberwood Cloud" infrastructure supports our point-of-sale systems, loyalty programs, and back-office functions. 

Given the high volume of credit card transactions we process, we are a target for cyber-attacks. We employ a dedicated Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) and maintain a robust cybersecurity framework based on industry best practices. This includes regular penetration testing, mandatory security awareness training for all staff, and advanced encryption for data at rest and in transit.

**Properties**

As of fiscal year-end 2024, Amberwood Partners Co. operates or franchises thousands of locations. Our properties range from freestanding buildings and inline mall locations to kiosks in high-traffic transportation hubs. We generally lease the land and buildings for our restaurants under long-term operating leases, although we own the underlying real estate for a select number of "trophy" locations. Our corporate headquarters in Florida consists of a modern office campus that houses our executive team, support functions, and a state-of-the-art test kitchen.

**Legal Proceedings**

From time to time, we are involved in various legal proceedings arising in the ordinary course of business, such as personal injury claims, employment-related disputes, and lease-related litigation. While the outcome of these matters cannot be predicted with certainty, we do not believe that any currently pending legal proceedings will have a material adverse effect on our financial position or results of operations.

**Environmental and Social Responsibility**

Amberwood Partners Co. is dedicated to being a responsible corporate citizen. Our "Green Plate" initiative focuses on reducing food waste by donating surplus ingredients to local food banks and composting organic waste. We are also committed to transitioning to 100% recyclable or compostable guest packaging by 2030. Our social responsibility efforts extend to our supply chain, where we require all Tier 1 vendors to sign our Supplier Code of Conduct, which prohibits forced labor and mandates fair working conditions.

**Management of Risks**

The Company’s Board of Directors and executive leadership team are actively involved in oversight of risks that could affect our ability to maintain our $11,063,613,016 revenue stream. This includes monitoring economic indicators, geopolitical stability, and emerging regulatory trends. We maintain a comprehensive insurance program to mitigate risks related to property damage, business interruption, and liability.

**Conclusion of Item 1**

In summary, Amberwood Partners Co. is a robust, diversified hospitality entity with a clear strategic vision. Our $11,063,613,016 in revenue for fiscal year 2024 is a testament to our brand strength and operational efficiency. Moving forward, we remain focused on leveraging our Florida-based expertise to capture new market opportunities and deliver "The Amberwood Experience" to guests around the world.

**ITEM 1C. CYBERSECURITY**

**Risk Management and Strategy**

Amberwood Partners Co. recognizes that in an increasingly digital hospitality landscape, the protection of our guest data, proprietary restaurant technologies, and corporate information systems is fundamental to our operational integrity and the preservation of our $11,063,613,016 revenue stream. Our cybersecurity program is a core component of our “Amberwood Advantage” strategy, designed to safeguard our “Amberwood Cloud” infrastructure, the “SmartKitchen” technology suite, and the personal information of millions of “Amberwood Rewards” members.

We have developed and implemented a comprehensive cybersecurity risk management program intended to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of our critical systems and data. Our program is grounded in the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (NIST CSF), which provides a structured approach to identifying, protecting, detecting, responding to, and recovering from cybersecurity threats. This framework allows us to align our security posture with industry-recognized best practices and facilitates a continuous improvement cycle as the threat landscape evolves.

*Integration with Enterprise Risk Management*

Cybersecurity risk management is integrated into our broader enterprise risk management (ERM) process. We treat cyber threats not merely as technical hurdles but as significant business risks that could impact our financial condition, including our $10,730,227,552 in total assets. Our cybersecurity team collaborates with our ERM committee to ensure that digital risks are identified, assessed, and prioritized alongside traditional operational and financial risks. This integration ensures that cybersecurity considerations are embedded in our strategic decision-making, particularly as we expand our “QuickBite Express” digital-first footprint and enhance our mobile ordering platforms.

*Identification and Mitigation of Threats*

To identify and mitigate cybersecurity threats, we employ a multi-layered defense strategy. This includes:

*   **Continuous Monitoring and Detection:** We utilize advanced security information and event management (SIEM) tools to monitor our network traffic and system logs for anomalous activity.
*   **Vulnerability Management:** We conduct regular automated vulnerability scans and annual third-party penetration testing of our internal and external-facing systems, including our point-of-sale (POS) systems across our global restaurant portfolio.
*   **Incident Response Planning:** We maintain a formal Cybersecurity Incident Response Plan (CIRP) that outlines the procedures for containing, investigating, and remediating security incidents. We perform periodic tabletop exercises with executive leadership to ensure readiness.
*   **Security Awareness Training:** Because our workforce is the first line of defense, all corporate employees and restaurant management personnel undergo mandatory annual cybersecurity training, focusing on phishing awareness, password hygiene, and data handling protocols.
*   **Data Encryption:** We employ industry-standard encryption for data at rest and in transit, specifically targeting the high volume of credit card transactions and guest loyalty data processed within the “Amberwood Cloud.”

*Third-Party Risk Management*

As our operations rely on an interconnected ecosystem of suppliers, franchise partners, and third-party delivery platforms, we have established a third-party risk management program. We perform security assessments of Tier 1 vendors who have access to our sensitive data or provide critical infrastructure services. Our contracts with these entities include specific cybersecurity requirements and right-to-audit clauses to ensure their security controls align with our standards. We also provide our franchise partners with security guidelines and best practices to help protect the “Amberwood Partners” brand equity across our non-company-owned locations.

As of the date of this Annual Report on Form 10-K, we have not identified any cybersecurity threats, including as a result of any previous cybersecurity incidents, that have materially affected or are reasonably likely to materially affect the Company, including our business strategy, results of operations, or financial condition. However, as detailed in "Item 1A. Risk Factors," the sophisticated nature of cyber-attacks means we remain at risk of future incidents that could disrupt our $11 billion enterprise.

**Governance**

Governance of cybersecurity risks is a critical priority for both our Board of Directors and our senior management team. We believe that a robust governance structure ensures accountability and provides the necessary resources to maintain our defensive posture.

*Board of Directors Oversight*

The Audit Committee of the Board of Directors is responsible for the oversight of risks from cybersecurity threats. The Audit Committee receives quarterly reports from our Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) and Chief Information Officer (CIO) regarding our cybersecurity program, the status of ongoing security initiatives, and the results of recent audits or penetration tests. These briefings also include updates on the evolving regulatory landscape, such as compliance with the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and other data privacy mandates that impact our Florida-based headquarters and global operations.

The Audit Committee periodically reviews the Company’s Cybersecurity Incident Response Plan and is informed of any significant security events, even those that do not reach the threshold of materiality, to ensure appropriate oversight of the management’s response.

*Management’s Role in Assessing and Managing Risk*

Our cybersecurity program is led by our CISO, who has over twenty years of experience in information technology and security, including previous leadership roles in the consumer discretionary sector. The CISO reports directly to the CIO and has a dotted-line reporting relationship to the Audit Committee for security-related matters.

The CISO leads our dedicated Cybersecurity Operations Center (CSOC), which is staffed by professionals responsible for daily threat monitoring and incident management. Management’s responsibilities include:

*   **Risk Assessment:** Conducting annual comprehensive risk assessments to identify gaps in our security controls relative to the NIST Cybersecurity Framework.
*   **Resource Allocation:** Proposing and managing the cybersecurity budget to ensure adequate investment in defensive technologies and skilled personnel.
*   **Policy Development:** Establishing and enforcing corporate-wide information security policies and standards.
*   **Cross-Functional Collaboration:** Working with the legal, human resources, and internal audit departments to ensure a unified approach to data privacy and regulatory compliance.

In the event of a significant cybersecurity incident, the CISO is responsible for activating the Cybersecurity Incident Response Team (CIRT), which includes representatives from legal, corporate communications, and executive leadership. This team is tasked with assessing the severity of the incident, coordinating remediation efforts, and determining notification obligations to regulators, law enforcement, and affected guests.

By maintaining this rigorous governance and risk management framework, Amberwood Partners Co. strives to protect its stakeholders and ensure the continued resilience of its hospitality operations against the persistent threat of cyber-attacks. We remain committed to investing in the people and technology necessary to secure our digital future while delivering the culinary excellence our guests expect from our flagship brands.

### **Executive Overview**

Northbridge Robotics Holdings, Inc. (“Northbridge,” the “Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a global leader in the health care services sector. Headquartered in Rhode Island, our operations are dedicated to the seamless integration of advanced robotics, autonomous systems, and predictive machine learning architectures into the delivery of medical care and health infrastructure. We operate in a highly complex and evolving global health environment, providing critical services that enhance patient outcomes while addressing the systemic challenges of rising labor costs and clinical variability.

Our fiscal year 2023 was characterized by a massive scale of operations, reflecting our position as one of the world’s largest health care services enterprises. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2023, we generated total revenues of $258,399,461,658. This revenue base is supported by our three primary reportable segments: Clinical Robotics Services, Health Logistics & Pharmacy Automation, and Patient Care Systems. Our business model leverages the "Northbridge Efficiency Quotient" to deliver value-based care and high-throughput automated services to national health systems, governmental agencies, and large-scale physician groups.

During 2023, we maintained our focus on scaling autonomous clinical infrastructure and expanding our robotic fulfillment ecosystem. While our sector is historically characterized by high volume and thin margins, we achieved an operating income of $10,054,273,533. Our net income for the year was $5,387,445,237, supported by robust cash flow from operations of $11,146,788,099.

Our balance sheet remains a cornerstone of our strategic stability, with total assets of $178,846,953,758 as of December 31, 2023. This includes a substantial investment in goodwill and intangible assets, totaling $65,812,759,316 and $23,666,784,864 respectively, reflecting our history of strategic acquisitions and the proprietary value of our NorthbridgeOS platform. Our capital structure is characterized by total stockholders’ equity of $62,309,765,848, offset by total liabilities of $116,537,187,910, which we utilize to fund our intensive research and development and the deployment of our robotic fleet.

### **Key Performance Drivers and Industry Trends**

The health care services landscape is currently shaped by several macro-economic and technological trends that directly impact our performance:

1.  **Aging Global Populations:** The increasing prevalence of chronic conditions and the growing elderly demographic drive demand for our Patient Care Systems and Clinical Robotics segments. Our robots assist with high-acuity surgical needs and long-term care monitoring, providing a solution to the shortage of human healthcare professionals.
2.  **Labor Cost Inflation:** As medical labor costs rise, our automated service models become increasingly attractive to hospital administrators. By automating routine tasks and logistics, we allow clinical staff to focus on complex patient needs, improving institutional efficiency.
3.  **Technological Convergence:** The integration of haptic feedback, edge computing, and AI into clinical workflows is the primary driver of our R&D efforts. Our $258.4 billion revenue base provides the scale necessary to iterate on these technologies faster than smaller competitors.
4.  **Regulatory Scrutiny in Pharmacy Services:** Our Health Logistics & Pharmacy Automation segment operates in a complex regulatory environment. Transparency in PBM services and the accuracy of robotic dispensing are critical to maintaining our multi-year contracts with major payors.

#### **Revenues**

Total revenues for 2023 were $258,399,461,658. Our revenue growth is primarily driven by the expansion of our Health Logistics & Pharmacy Automation segment, which handles high-volume pharmaceutical fulfillment and "Last-Mile Clinical Logistics." The Clinical Robotics Services segment also contributed significantly through long-term service agreements for surgical assistants and diagnostic platforms.

Our revenue model is diversified, with a substantial portion derived from recurring service fees and value-based care contracts. The scale of our $258.4 billion top-line reflects our deep integration into the global healthcare supply chain and our ability to manage massive patient populations through our proprietary NorthbridgeOS platform.

#### **Cost of Revenue**

Cost of revenue for 2023 was $164,872,574,156. This represents approximately 63.8% of our total revenues. The primary components of our cost of revenue include:
*   The procurement costs of pharmaceutical products managed through our robotic fulfillment centers;
*   The manufacturing and maintenance costs of our robotic hardware fleet, including specialized actuators and haptic sensors;
*   Direct labor costs for our field engineers and clinical specialists who support our robotics platforms in hospital environments.

Our cost of revenue reflects the high-volume, low-margin nature of our pharmacy automation business, balanced by the higher-margin service components of our clinical robotics offerings.

#### **Other Operating Expenses**

Other operating expenses for 2023 were $83,472,613,969. This category includes:
*   **Selling, General, and Administrative (SG&A):** Costs related to our workforce of approximately 340,000 employees, including sales forces, administrative staff, and corporate functions in Rhode Island and our global offices.
*   **Research and Development (R&D):** Intensive investment in NorthbridgeOS, haptic feedback precision, and predictive health analytics.
*   **Depreciation and Amortization:** For the year 2023, this amounted to $2,979,397,514 (as reported in our cash flow statement), reflecting the lifecycle of our robotic assets and capitalized software.

The scale of our operating expenses is a function of our massive global footprint and the technical complexity of maintaining an autonomous clinical infrastructure.

#### **Operating Income**

Operating income for 2023 was $10,054,273,533, representing an operating margin of approximately 3.9%. This reflects our ability to maintain profitability despite the significant costs associated with managing a $258.4 billion enterprise. We continue to focus on the "Northbridge Efficiency Quotient" to optimize our internal workflows and reduce redundant operating expenses.

#### **Interest Expense**

Interest expense for the year was $1,804,524,678. This expense is associated with our significant debt load, which is part of our $116,537,187,910 in total liabilities. We utilize debt to fund capital-intensive expansions, such as the construction of new automated fulfillment centers and the deployment of our surgical robotics fleet.

#### **Income Tax Expense**

Our income tax expense for 2023 was $2,862,303,618 on pretax income of $8,249,748,855. This results in an effective tax rate of approximately 34.7%. This rate is influenced by the geographic distribution of our 340,000-strong workforce and the various tax jurisdictions in which we operate, as well as permanent differences between book and tax accounting for our robotic assets.

#### **Net Income**

Net income for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2023, was $5,387,445,237. This equates to a net margin of approximately 2.1%. Given the scale of our $178.8 billion asset base, this net income provides the necessary capital to reinvest in our technological ecosystem while returning value to shareholders.

### **Segment Performance**

We manage our operations through three reportable segments. While we report consolidated results, the following qualitative analysis provides insight into the drivers of our $258.4 billion revenue.

#### *Clinical Robotics Services*
This segment is our primary driver of high-margin service revenue. In 2023, we saw continued adoption of our robotic surgical assistants in orthopedic and neurovascular procedures. The integration of NorthbridgeOS into third-party medical hardware has expanded our addressable market, allowing us to generate service fees from hardware we do not directly manufacture.

#### *Health Logistics & Pharmacy Automation*
This segment accounts for the largest portion of our $258,399,461,658 in revenue. The performance in 2023 was driven by the increased throughput of our automated medication fulfillment centers. Our "Last-Mile Clinical Logistics" services, which utilize RFID-enabled robotic carts, have become standard in several national health systems, reducing hospital waste and improving inventory turnover.

#### *Patient Care Systems*
Our Patient Care Systems segment focused on the "hospital-at-home" transition in 2023. Our mobile care robots and tele-presence platforms saw increased utilization in non-acute settings. This segment is highly dependent on government reimbursement rates for home health and long-term care, which remained relatively stable during the fiscal year.

### **Critical Accounting Estimates**

The preparation of financial statements in conformity with U.S. GAAP requires management to make estimates and assumptions. Given our $178.8 billion asset base and $258.4 billion revenue, these estimates are significant.

**Revenue Recognition:**
We recognize revenue when services are transferred to the customer. For our Clinical Robotics segment, this involves estimating the lifecycle of the service agreement. For our Pharmacy Automation segment, revenue is recognized upon the robotic fulfillment and delivery of medications.

**Goodwill and Intangible Assets:**
Our goodwill of $65,812,759,316 and intangible assets of $23,666,784,864 are subject to annual impairment testing. These assets represent the premium paid for acquisitions and the value of NorthbridgeOS. If the healthcare robotics market were to experience a significant downturn, we might be required to record a non-cash impairment charge.

**Inventory Valuation:**
Our inventory of $13,232,945,682 is valued at the lower of cost or net realizable value. We must make estimates regarding the obsolescence of robotic components and the shelf-life of pharmaceutical products.

**Income Taxes:**
We operate in numerous jurisdictions, and our income tax expense of $2,862,303,618 is based on complex interpretations of tax laws. We must estimate the likelihood of realizing deferred tax assets and provide for uncertain tax positions.

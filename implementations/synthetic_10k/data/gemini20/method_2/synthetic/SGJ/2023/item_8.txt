We conducted our audit in accordance with the standards of the PCAOB. Those standards require that we plan and perform the audit to obtain reasonable assurance about whether the financial statements are free of material misstatement, whether due to error or fraud. Our audit included performing procedures to assess the risks of material misstatement of the financial statements, whether due to error or fraud, and performing procedures that respond to those risks. Such procedures included examining, on a test basis, evidence regarding the amounts and disclosures in the financial statements. Our audit also included evaluating the accounting principles used and significant estimates made by management, as well as evaluating the overall presentation of the financial statements. We believe that our audit provides a reasonable basis for our opinion.

### **NOTES TO CONSOLIDATED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS**

#### **NOTE 1. DESCRIPTION OF BUSINESS AND SUMMARY OF SIGNIFICANT ACCOUNTING POLICIES**

**Description of Business**
Northbridge Robotics Holdings, Inc. (“Northbridge,” the “Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a global health care services enterprise headquartered in Rhode Island. We specialize in the integration of autonomous systems, clinical robotics, and machine learning architectures into the medical infrastructure. Our operations include pharmacy benefit management (PBM) through automated fulfillment, clinical robotics services for hospitals, and autonomous patient care systems in non-acute environments.

**Basis of Presentation**
The consolidated financial statements include the accounts of the Company and its subsidiaries. All intercompany transactions and balances have been eliminated. The financial statements are prepared in accordance with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP).

**Use of Estimates**
The preparation of financial statements requires management to make estimates and assumptions that affect the reported amounts of assets and liabilities, including the valuation of $65,812,759,316 in goodwill and $23,666,784,864 in intangible assets, as well as the determination of the $2,862,303,618 income tax expense. Actual results could differ from these estimates.

**Cash and Equivalents**
We consider all highly liquid investments with an original maturity of three months or less to be cash equivalents. At December 31, 2023, our cash and equivalents were $6,079,925,037.

**Accounts Receivable**
Accounts receivable are recorded at net realizable value. As of December 31, 2023, accounts receivable stood at $24,617,066,867, primarily due from national health systems, governmental agencies, and commercial payors.

**Inventory**
Inventory, which totaled $13,232,945,682 at year-end 2023, consists of pharmaceutical products for fulfillment and precision components for our robotic fleet. Inventory is valued at the lower of cost or net realizable value using the first-in, first-out (FIFO) method.

**Goodwill and Intangible Assets**
Goodwill represents the excess of the purchase price over the fair value of net assets acquired in business combinations. Goodwill ($65,812,759,316) and indefinite-lived intangible assets are not amortized but are tested for impairment annually. Finite-lived intangible assets ($23,666,784,864), including NorthbridgeOS software, are amortized over their estimated useful lives.

**Revenue Recognition**
The Company generates revenue from service fees, PBM pharmacy fulfillment, and clinical robotic leases. Revenue is recognized when the control of promised goods or services is transferred to customers in an amount that reflects the consideration we expect to receive.
*   *Clinical Robotics:* Revenue is recognized over the term of the service agreement.
*   *Health Logistics:* Revenue is recognized at the point in time when medication is fulfilled and delivered.
*   *Patient Care:* Revenue is recognized as services are rendered.

#### **NOTE 2. OPERATING SEGMENTS**

We manage our operations through three reportable segments:
1.  **Clinical Robotics Services:** Provision of robotic surgical assistants and diagnostic platforms.
2.  **Health Logistics & Pharmacy Automation:** Automated medication fulfillment and PBM services.
3.  **Patient Care Systems:** Autonomous mobility and tele-robotic monitoring in home and long-term care settings.

For the year ended December 31, 2023, consolidated revenues were $258,399,461,658. While the Health Logistics & Pharmacy Automation segment contributes the majority of total revenue, the Clinical Robotics Services segment remains a significant driver of operating income margin.

#### **NOTE 3. PROPERTY, PLANT, AND EQUIPMENT**

Included within Other Assets on the Consolidated Balance Sheet are property, plant, and equipment. For the fiscal year 2023, depreciation and amortization expense was $2,979,397,514. This includes the amortization of capitalized software costs for the NorthbridgeOS platform and the depreciation of our robotic fulfillment centers.

#### **NOTE 4. DEBT AND OTHER LIABILITIES**

As of December 31, 2023, total liabilities were $116,537,187,910. Other liabilities of $88,882,091,181 include long-term debt used to finance the expansion of our robotic clinical infrastructure. Interest expense for the year was $1,804,524,678, reflecting the servicing of these obligations.

#### **NOTE 5. INCOME TAXES**

Pretax income for 2023 was $8,249,748,855. The income tax expense was $2,862,303,618, representing an effective tax rate of approximately 34.7%. This rate reflects our global footprint and the statutory rates in the jurisdictions where our 340,000 employees operate.

#### **NOTE 6. STOCKHOLDERS’ EQUITY**

Total stockholders’ equity was $62,309,765,848 at December 31, 2023.
*   **Retained Earnings:** $40,660,097,252, reflecting cumulative net income less dividends.
*   **AOCI:** $(206,051,645), primarily related to foreign currency translation adjustments.
*   **Other Equity:** $21,855,720,241, which includes common stock, additional paid-in capital, and the impact of share repurchases.

During 2023, the Company returned $1,971,971,134 to stockholders in the form of dividends and utilized $1,434,957,944 for the repurchase of common stock.

#### **NOTE 7. SUPPLEMENTARY CASH FLOW INFORMATION**

Net cash provided by operating activities was $11,146,788,099. Net cash used in investing activities was $17,332,188,965, driven by $2,354,510,956 in capital expenditures and $14,977,678,009 in other investing activities, including strategic technology acquisitions. Net cash provided by financing activities was $1,689,688,734, which included $5,096,617,812 in proceeds from other financing activities, offset by dividends and share repurchases.

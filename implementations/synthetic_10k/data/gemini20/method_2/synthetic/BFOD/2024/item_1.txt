**ITEM 1. BUSINESS**

**General**

Highmark Industries Co. (“Highmark,” the “Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a Connecticut-based diversified financial services holding company that, through its primary operating subsidiaries, provides a broad array of property and casualty insurance products and services to businesses, public entities, and individuals. Established with a commitment to disciplined underwriting and conservative capital management, Highmark has grown into a significant participant in the insurance industry, leveraging deep actuarial expertise and a robust distribution network to serve its policyholders.

For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, Highmark Industries Co. generated total revenues of $2,999,501,569. Our operations are headquartered in Connecticut, a historic center for the insurance industry, which provides us with access to a highly skilled workforce and a sophisticated regulatory environment. We conduct our business through three primary reportable segments: Commercial Insurance, Personal Insurance, and Specialty Markets.

Our primary objective is to deliver sustainable long-term value to our shareholders by generating consistent underwriting profits and optimizing investment income from our premium float. We achieve this through a rigorous focus on risk selection, sophisticated predictive modeling, and a customer-centric approach to claims management.

**Operating Segments**

The Company’s business is organized into three reportable segments based on the nature of the products offered and the markets served.

***Commercial Insurance***

The Commercial Insurance segment is our largest operating division, accounting for a substantial portion of our $2,999,501,569 in total revenues for fiscal year 2024. This segment provides a comprehensive suite of property and casualty coverages to small and mid-sized businesses across various industries, including manufacturing, retail, professional services, and construction. 

Key product offerings within the Commercial Insurance segment include:

*   **Commercial Multi-Peril (CMP):** These policies provide a combination of property and liability coverages, protecting businesses against losses arising from fire, theft, windstorms, and third-party liability claims.
*   **Workers’ Compensation:** We provide statutorily mandated coverage for employers, covering medical expenses and lost wages for employees injured in the course of employment. Our approach emphasizes loss control services and proactive return-to-work programs.
*   **Commercial Auto:** We offer coverage for fleets and individual business vehicles, protecting against physical damage and liability arising from vehicular accidents.
*   **General Liability:** This provides protection against claims of bodily injury or property damage for which the insured is legally responsible.

***Personal Insurance***

Our Personal Insurance segment focuses on providing high-quality protection for individuals and families. We believe that our localized presence and focus on the Connecticut market, along with our expansion into contiguous states, allows us to offer competitive pricing and superior service. 

Primary products include:

*   **Homeowners Insurance:** Protection for primary residences, secondary homes, and personal property against perils such as fire, lightning, and wind. We also offer specialized riders for high-value items.
*   **Private Passenger Auto:** Standard and preferred auto insurance policies providing liability, collision, and comprehensive coverage.
*   **Personal Umbrella:** Excess liability coverage that provides protection over and above the limits of standard homeowners and auto policies.

***Specialty Markets***

The Specialty Markets segment focuses on niche risks that require specialized underwriting expertise or tailored policy forms. This segment often operates on an excess and surplus (E&S) basis, allowing us the flexibility to price risks that do not fit the standard market criteria.

Products in this segment include:

*   **Professional Liability:** Errors and Omissions (E&O) and Directors and Officers (D&O) coverage for a variety of professions and corporate entities.
*   **Inland Marine:** Coverage for property in transit or specialized equipment used in construction and logistics.
*   **Excess Liability:** High-limit liability layers for commercial clients with complex risk profiles.

**Underwriting and Risk Management**

Underwriting is the core competency of Highmark Industries Co. Our philosophy is rooted in the belief that disciplined risk selection is more important than premium volume. In 2024, as we achieved revenues of $2,999,501,569, we maintained a strict adherence to our internal underwriting guidelines, which are reviewed annually by our Chief Risk Officer and senior actuarial staff.

We utilize proprietary predictive models that incorporate a wide array of data points, including historical loss trends, external economic indicators, and geographic risk factors. In our Commercial Lines, we emphasize “boots-on-the-ground” loss control, where our engineers work directly with policyholders to mitigate risks before they result in claims.

Risk management at Highmark is an enterprise-wide function. We monitor our aggregate exposure to catastrophic events—such as hurricanes, winter storms, and convective activity—using industry-standard modeling software. We establish strict limits on the amount of premium we will write in specific geographic zones to prevent an over-concentration of risk that could threaten our capital base.

**Claims Management**

We believe that the fair and efficient settlement of claims is essential to our reputation and long-term success. Our claims department is staffed by experienced professionals who are organized by line of business to ensure technical expertise. 

For high-frequency, low-severity claims, such as minor auto accidents, we utilize digital tools that allow policyholders to submit documentation and photos via mobile applications, accelerating the settlement process. For complex or high-severity claims, we employ specialized adjusters and legal counsel to ensure that reserves are set accurately and that the Company’s interests are protected. Our reserving process is subject to rigorous internal controls and is audited by independent actuarial firms to ensure compliance with statutory and GAAP requirements.

**Distribution**

Highmark Industries Co. distributes its products primarily through a network of independent insurance agencies and brokerage firms. We believe the independent agency system provides our policyholders with professional advice and personalized service, which is particularly important for complex commercial and specialty risks.

We maintain strong relationships with over 1,500 independent agencies. To support these partners, we provide a sophisticated online portal that allows agents to quote, bind, and issue policies in real-time. We offer competitive commission structures and incentive programs based on both premium volume and the profitability of the business produced. In addition to our agency network, we have expanded our digital direct-to-consumer capabilities for certain personal lines products to meet evolving customer preferences.

**Reinsurance**

To manage our volatility and protect our balance sheet, we purchase reinsurance from high-quality, highly rated third-party reinsurers. Reinsurance involves ceding a portion of our premiums and risks to other companies.

Our reinsurance program generally includes:

*   **Catastrophe Excess of Loss:** Protects us against large-scale events that affect multiple policyholders simultaneously, such as a major hurricane or earthquake.
*   **Per-Risk Excess of Loss:** Protects us against a single large loss on an individual policy, such as a major fire at a commercial warehouse.
*   **Quota Share Agreements:** Where we share a proportional percentage of all premiums and losses within a specific line of business with a reinsurer, often used for newer or more volatile specialty lines.

The availability and cost of reinsurance are subject to market conditions. While we believe our current reinsurance program is robust, a significant hardening of the reinsurance market could impact our net retention levels and overall profitability.

**Investments**

As a property and casualty insurer, we collect premiums before losses are paid. This creates a "float" that we invest to generate additional income. As of the end of fiscal year 2024, our investment strategy remains conservative, focusing on capital preservation and liquidity to ensure that funds are available to meet our policyholder obligations.

The vast majority of our investment portfolio is allocated to high-quality, fixed-income securities, including U.S. Treasury bonds, municipal bonds, and investment-grade corporate debt. A smaller portion of the portfolio is allocated to diversified equity holdings and alternative investments to provide a hedge against inflation and enhance long-term returns. All investment activities are overseen by the Investment Committee of the Board of Directors.

**Competition**

The property and casualty insurance industry is highly competitive. We compete with large national insurers, regional carriers, and specialized niche players. We also face competition from captive insurance programs and self-insurance pools utilized by some large commercial entities.

Competitive factors include:

*   **Pricing:** The ability to offer coverages at rates that are attractive to customers while remaining actuarially sound.
*   **Service:** The speed and quality of claims handling and agent support.
*   **Financial Strength:** The perceived ability of the company to pay future claims, often reflected in ratings from agencies like A.M. Best.
*   **Product Innovation:** The ability to develop new coverages for emerging risks, such as cyber liability or climate-related exposures.

We believe Highmark’s competitive advantage lies in our deep knowledge of the Connecticut and Northeast regional markets, our disciplined underwriting culture, and our commitment to leveraging technology to improve the agent and policyholder experience.

**Regulation**

The insurance industry is subject to extensive regulation by state insurance departments. As a Connecticut-based company, our primary regulator is the Connecticut Insurance Department. However, because we are licensed to write business in multiple jurisdictions, we are subject to the laws and regulations of each state in which we operate.

State regulations typically govern:

*   **Solvency Standards:** Requirements regarding minimum capital and surplus levels, often based on Risk-Based Capital (RBC) formulas.
*   **Rate and Form Filing:** In many states, the rates we charge and the policy forms we use must be filed with and approved by the state insurance commissioner.
*   **Market Conduct:** Regulations concerning how we market, sell, and service our products, as well as how we handle claims.
*   **Investments:** Limitations on the types and concentrations of assets in which an insurer may invest.

We are also members of the National Association of Insurance Commissioners (NAIC), which works to harmonize insurance regulation across the United States. Changes in state or federal laws, or changes in the interpretation of existing laws by courts or regulators, could have a material impact on our operations.

**Human Capital Management**

As of December 31, 2024, Highmark Industries Co. employed a dedicated workforce committed to excellence in the financial services sector. Our employees are our most valuable asset, and our success is directly linked to our ability to attract, develop, and retain high-quality talent.

*   **Diversity and Inclusion:** We believe that a diverse workforce fosters innovation and better decision-making. We have implemented recruitment strategies aimed at attracting candidates from diverse backgrounds and provide ongoing training to promote an inclusive workplace culture.
*   **Training and Development:** We invest in our employees through the Highmark Learning Center, which offers professional certification support, leadership development programs, and technical training in underwriting and claims.
*   **Compensation and Benefits:** We provide a competitive compensation package that includes performance-based bonuses, a 401(k) matching program, comprehensive health insurance, and wellness initiatives.
*   **Safety and Well-being:** We maintain a flexible work environment where appropriate, recognizing the importance of work-life balance in maintaining employee engagement and mental health.

Highmark Industries Co. recognizes the increasing importance of ESG factors to our investors, employees, and policyholders. We are committed to sustainable business practices and are integrating climate risk assessments into our long-term strategic planning. Our corporate governance practices are designed to ensure accountability, transparency, and ethical conduct at all levels of the organization.

**ITEM 8. FINANCIAL STATEMENTS AND SUPPLEMENTARY DATA**

**REPORT OF INDEPENDENT REGISTERED PUBLIC ACCOUNTING FIRM**

To the Stockholders and the Board of Directors of Highmark Industries Co.

**Opinion on the Financial Statements**

We have audited the accompanying consolidated balance sheets of Highmark Industries Co. (the “Company”) as of December 31, 2024, and the related consolidated statements of income, comprehensive income, stockholders’ equity, and cash flows for the year then ended, and the related notes (collectively referred to as the “consolidated financial statements”). In our opinion, the consolidated financial statements present fairly, in all material respects, the financial position of the Company as of December 31, 2024, and the results of its operations and its cash flows for the year then ended, in conformity with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles.

**Basis for Opinion**

These consolidated financial statements are the responsibility of the Company’s management. Our responsibility is to express an opinion on the Company’s consolidated financial statements based on our audits. We are a public accounting firm registered with the Public Company Accounting Oversight Board (United States) (PCAOB) and are required to be independent with respect to the Company in accordance with the U.S. federal securities laws and the applicable rules and regulations of the Securities and Exchange Commission and the PCAOB.

We conducted our audits in accordance with the standards of the PCAOB. Those standards require that we plan and perform the audit to obtain reasonable assurance about whether the consolidated financial statements are free of material misstatement, whether due to error or fraud. Our audits included performing procedures to assess the risks of material misstatement of the consolidated financial statements, whether due to error or fraud, and performing procedures that respond to those risks. Such procedures included examining, on a test basis, evidence regarding the amounts and disclosures in the consolidated financial statements. Our audits also included evaluating the accounting principles used and significant estimates made by management, as well as evaluating the overall presentation of the consolidated financial statements. We believe that our audits provide a reasonable basis for our opinion.

**Critical Audit Matters**

The critical audit matters communicated below are matters arising from the current period audit of the consolidated financial statements that were communicated or required to be communicated to the audit committee and that: (1) relate to accounts or disclosures that are material to the consolidated financial statements and (2) involved our especially challenging, subjective, or complex judgments. The communication of critical audit matters does not alter in any way our opinion on the consolidated financial statements, taken as a whole, and we are not, by communicating the critical audit matters below, providing separate opinions on the critical audit matters or on the accounts or disclosures to which they relate.

*Valuation of Unpaid Loss and Loss Adjustment Expense Reserves*

As discussed in Notes 1 and 4 to the consolidated financial statements, the Company’s total liabilities were $47,557,166,451 as of December 31, 2024, a significant portion of which represents reserves for unpaid losses and loss adjustment expenses. These reserves represent management’s best estimate of the ultimate cost of settling all insurance claims. We identified the valuation of these reserves as a critical audit matter because of the significant judgment required by management in establishing the actuarial assumptions, particularly regarding "long-tail" lines in the Commercial and Specialty segments. During the year, the Company recognized a net benefit in other operating expenses of $10,735,306,346, primarily related to favorable prior-year development. Auditing these estimates involved high degrees of complexity and the use of actuarial specialists to evaluate the reasonableness of the models and the underlying data.

### **NOTES TO CONSOLIDATED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS**

#### **Note 1. Nature of Operations and Summary of Significant Accounting Policies**

**Nature of Operations**
Highmark Industries Co. (“Highmark” or the “Company”), headquartered in Connecticut, is a diversified financial services holding company. Through its subsidiaries, the Company provides property and casualty insurance products to commercial and personal customers, as well as specialized niche coverages through its Specialty Markets segment. The Company's primary operations are concentrated in the Northeast United States.

**Basis of Presentation**
The accompanying consolidated financial statements have been prepared in accordance with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP) and include the accounts of Highmark and its subsidiaries. All intercompany transactions and balances have been eliminated.

**Use of Estimates**
The preparation of financial statements requires management to make estimates and assumptions that affect the reported amounts of assets and liabilities, specifically reserves for unpaid losses, the valuation of investments, and the assessment of goodwill. Actual results could differ from those estimates.

**Cash and Equivalents**
The Company considers all highly liquid investments with an original maturity of three months or less to be cash equivalents. At December 31, 2024, cash and equivalents totaled $167,160,660.

**Investments and Other Assets**
Other assets of $57,108,420,995 consist primarily of the Company's investment portfolio, which includes fixed-income securities and equity holdings. Fixed maturity securities are classified as available-for-sale and are carried at fair value, with unrealized gains and losses, net of tax, reported in accumulated other comprehensive income (AOCI). As of December 31, 2024, the AOCI balance was a loss of $1,848,086,750.

**Property, Plant and Equipment**
Property, plant and equipment are recorded at cost and depreciated over their estimated useful lives using the straight-line method. As of December 31, 2024, the net value was $668,621,268.

**Goodwill and Intangible Assets**
Goodwill ($1,556,779,528) represents the excess of purchase price over the fair value of net assets acquired in business combinations. The Company tests goodwill for impairment annually. Intangible assets, net ($489,048,923), consist of agency relationships and trade names with finite lives, which are amortized over their useful lives.

**Insurance Reserves**
Insurance reserves, included in other liabilities ($43,969,075,245), represent the estimated ultimate cost of all reported and unreported claims and loss adjustment expenses (LAE) incurred through December 31, 2024. 

**Revenue Recognition**
Insurance premiums are recognized as revenue over the period of the policy. In 2024, the Company recognized $2,999,501,569 in total revenues.

#### **Note 2. Other Operating Expenses (Benefit)**

During the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, the Company recognized a net benefit in other operating expenses of $10,735,306,346. This amount primarily reflects favorable development on prior-accident-year loss reserves. This favorable development occurred across all segments, most notably in long-tail liability lines within the Commercial Insurance and Specialty Markets segments, where actual claim settlement costs were lower than previous actuarial estimates.

#### **Note 3. Debt**

Long-term debt at December 31, 2024, consisted of the following:
*   Senior Notes and Other Long-Term Obligations: $3,588,091,206.

Interest expense on this debt for the year was $383,529,810. The Company was in compliance with all debt covenants as of December 31, 2024.

#### **Note 4. Income Taxes**

The Company is subject to U.S. federal and state income taxes. For the year ended December 31, 2024, the provision for income taxes was $717,602,274 on pretax income of $3,417,153,686, resulting in an effective tax rate of 21.0%. This rate is consistent with the U.S. statutory rate.

#### **Note 5. Stockholders’ Equity**

**Accumulated Other Comprehensive Income (AOCI)**
The AOCI balance of $(1,848,086,750) as of December 31, 2024, was comprised entirely of net unrealized losses on available-for-sale securities, net of tax, reflecting the impact of rising interest rates on the fair value of the fixed-income portfolio.

**Share Repurchases**
During 2024, the Company repurchased shares of its common stock for a total consideration of $2,741,879,459. These repurchases are reflected in the consolidated statements of cash flows and stockholders' equity.

**Dividends**
The Company paid no dividends during the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024.

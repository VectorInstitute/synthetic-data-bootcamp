**ITEM 1C. CYBERSECURITY**

**Risk Management and Strategy**

Clearwater Technologies Co. (“Clearwater,” the “Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) recognizes that the sophisticated integration of technology and data analytics, which is central to our "Clearwater Analytics Suite" and our operations as a multi-line insurer, necessitates a robust and resilient cybersecurity posture. We have developed and implemented a comprehensive cybersecurity risk management program designed to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of our information systems and the vast amounts of sensitive policyholder and financial data we maintain.

Our cybersecurity program is grounded in the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (“NIST CSF”). By leveraging this industry-standard framework, we have established a structured approach to identify, protect, detect, respond to, and recover from cybersecurity threats. This program is integrated into our broader Enterprise Risk Management (“ERM”) framework, ensuring that cybersecurity risk is not managed in isolation but is weighed alongside other material risks to our $535,361,502,568 asset base.

The key elements of our cybersecurity risk management strategy include:

*   **Continuous Monitoring and Threat Intelligence:** Our security operations center utilizes automated tools and high-frequency data ingestion—similar to the technology used in our underwriting—to monitor our network for anomalies. We participate in industry-sharing forums to stay abreast of emerging threat vectors targeting the Financials sector and Multi-line Insurance sub-industry.
*   **Third-Party Risk Management:** As a firm that relies on a network of over 15,000 independent agents and various technology providers, we have implemented a rigorous third-party risk management program. This includes performing security assessments of vendors before engagement and conducting periodic reviews of their security controls to ensure they align with our internal standards.
*   **Incident Response Planning:** We maintain a formal Cybersecurity Incident Response Plan (“IRP”) that outlines the procedures for responding to a potential security breach. The IRP is tested annually through tabletop exercises involving executive leadership and technical teams to ensure readiness.
*   **Employee Training and Awareness:** Recognizing that our 14,500 employees are the first line of defense, we conduct mandatory cybersecurity training through our "Clearwater Academy." This includes regular phishing simulations and education on data handling practices mandated by the NYDFS and other regulators.
*   **Internal and External Audits:** We subject our systems to regular internal audits and engage third-party security firms to perform independent penetration testing and vulnerability assessments. These efforts are designed to identify and remediate potential weaknesses before they can be exploited by malicious actors.

As of the date of this report, we have not identified any cybersecurity threats, including those resulting from previous incidents, that have materially affected or are reasonably likely to materially affect our business strategy, results of operations, or financial condition. However, given our $26,768,075,128 revenue base and our reliance on digital channels, we remain a high-profile target for sophisticated cyber-attacks.

**Governance**

Our Board of Directors (the “Board”) considers cybersecurity risk to be a critical component of its oversight responsibilities. To ensure specialized focus on these risks, the Board has established a dedicated Technology and Cybersecurity Committee (the “Committee”).

*   **Board Oversight:** The Committee is responsible for overseeing the Company’s cybersecurity risk management program and its compliance with applicable regulations, such as the NYDFS Cybersecurity Regulation (23 NYCRR 500). The Committee receives quarterly reports from management on the state of our cybersecurity posture, the results of recent audits, the status of ongoing security initiatives, and an analysis of the evolving threat landscape. The Committee also reviews the Company’s cybersecurity insurance coverage to ensure it is appropriate given our $480,507,188,381 liability profile.
*   **Management’s Role:** At the management level, our cybersecurity program is led by our Chief Information Security Officer (“CISO”), who has over 20 years of experience in information security and risk management within the financial services industry. The CISO is supported by a dedicated team of cybersecurity professionals and reports directly to the Chief Technology Officer, with a dotted-line reporting relationship to the Committee.
*   **Monitoring and Escalation:** We have established internal protocols for the identification and escalation of cybersecurity incidents. Our CISO and the broader information security team are responsible for the day-to-day management of security controls. If a significant incident is detected, the IRP is activated, and the CISO is responsible for informing the executive leadership team and the Committee in accordance with our escalation policy.

**Cybersecurity Incidents**

The Company has experienced, and will likely continue to experience, cybersecurity threats and incidents. During the fiscal year ended December 31, 2023, we experienced an incident involving unauthorized access to a third-party vendor's system. This incident briefly exposed limited customer contact information for a small subset of our policyholders.

Upon discovery of the incident, we immediately activated our IRP, worked with the vendor to secure the affected system, and engaged external forensics experts to investigate the scope of the breach. Our investigation determined that the incident was confined to the vendor’s environment and did not involve a breach of Clearwater’s internal systems or our proprietary Clearwater Analytics Suite.

We notified the affected individuals and the relevant regulatory authorities, including the NYDFS, in accordance with applicable laws. We also conducted a comprehensive review of the vendor’s security protocols and implemented additional monitoring requirements for third-party partners.

While this incident did not have a material impact on our financial condition or results of operations, and did not interrupt the generation of our $26,768,075,128 in annual revenue, it underscores the persistent risks associated with third-party service providers. The costs associated with remediating this incident, including legal fees and notification costs, were not material to our $3,478,889,508 in net income for the year. However, there can be no assurance that future incidents, whether involving our own systems or those of third parties, will not be more severe or result in material impacts to our reputation, operations, or stockholders’ equity of $54,854,314,187.

We continue to invest in our technological defenses and our "Clearwater Academy" training to mitigate these risks. Our commitment to maintaining the trust of our policyholders and the integrity of our financial data remains paramount as we continue to innovate in the Multi-line Insurance sub-industry.

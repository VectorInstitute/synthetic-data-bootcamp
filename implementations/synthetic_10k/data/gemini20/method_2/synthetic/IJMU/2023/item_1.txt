**ITEM 1. BUSINESS**

**Overview**

Clearwater Technologies Co. (“Clearwater,” the “Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a leading diversified financial services firm, incorporated in the State of Delaware and headquartered in New York, New York. Since our inception, we have established ourselves as a premier provider of multi-line insurance and risk management solutions. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2023, the Company generated total revenues of $26,768,075,128. Our operations are characterized by a unique integration of traditional insurance principles with advanced proprietary technological frameworks, which allow us to optimize underwriting precision, enhance claims processing, and deliver superior value to our policyholders and shareholders alike.

As a multi-line insurer, Clearwater operates across a broad spectrum of the insurance value chain. Our portfolio is strategically balanced between Property and Casualty (“P&C”) insurance, Life and Retirement services, and specialized Reinsurance offerings. This diversification allows the Company to mitigate the cyclicality inherent in specific insurance lines and maintain a robust capital position even amidst volatile market conditions. We serve a diverse clientele ranging from individual consumers and small business owners to large multi-national corporations and institutional clients.

Our competitive advantage is rooted in our namesake technological capabilities. Clearwater utilizes high-frequency data ingestion and machine learning algorithms—collectively referred to as the “Clearwater Analytics Suite”—to assess risk with a degree of granularity that distinguishes us from traditional legacy carriers. By leveraging these proprietary tools, we have achieved a significant market presence in the Financials sector, specifically within the Multi-line Insurance sub-industry.

**Business Strategy**

The primary objective of Clearwater Technologies Co. is to deliver sustainable long-term value through disciplined underwriting, proactive investment management, and continuous technological innovation. The key pillars of our strategy include:

*   **Data-Driven Underwriting:** We utilize our $26,768,075,128 revenue base to reinvest in predictive modeling. By moving away from static actuarial tables toward dynamic, real-time risk assessment, we aim to lower our combined ratio and improve the profitability of our P&C and Life segments.
*   **Customer-Centric Distribution:** We maintain a multi-channel distribution strategy that includes an extensive network of independent agents, strategic partnerships with financial institutions, and a robust direct-to-consumer digital platform. This approach ensures that we meet customers where they prefer to transact.
*   **Capital Optimization:** We focus on maintaining a strong balance sheet with high levels of liquidity. Our investment strategy is designed to support our long-term insurance liabilities while generating risk-adjusted returns through a diversified portfolio of fixed-income securities, commercial mortgages, and alternative investments.
*   **Operational Excellence:** Through the automation of routine claims and administrative functions, we reduce our expense ratio, allowing us to offer competitive pricing to our policyholders while maintaining attractive margins.

**Reportable Segments**

We conduct our business through four primary reportable segments: Life and Retirement Solutions, Property and Casualty Underwriting, Institutional Risk and Reinsurance, and Corporate and Strategic Investments.

***Life and Retirement Solutions***
This segment provides a comprehensive suite of products designed to help individuals and families achieve financial security and plan for retirement. Key products include:
*   *Individual Life Insurance:* Offering term life, whole life, and universal life products tailored to the evolving needs of our policyholders.
*   *Annuity Products:* Providing fixed, variable, and indexed annuities that offer tax-deferred growth and guaranteed income streams.
*   *Group Benefits:* Partnering with employers to provide life, disability, and supplemental health insurance to their workforces.

***Property and Casualty (P&C) Underwriting***
Our P&C segment offers protection against risks associated with property damage and liability. We categorize these into Personal Lines and Commercial Lines:
*   *Personal Lines:* Includes homeowners’ insurance, personal automobile insurance, and umbrella liability coverage.
*   *Commercial Lines:* Provides tailored solutions for businesses, including general liability, workers' compensation, commercial property insurance, and specialized cyber-risk coverage—the latter of which leverages our deep technological expertise.

***Institutional Risk and Reinsurance***
Clearwater acts as a reinsurer for other insurance companies, helping them manage their risk exposures and capital requirements. We specialize in treaty and facultative reinsurance across both life and non-life lines. Our sophisticated modeling allows us to take on complex risks, such as catastrophe modeling and longevity risk, which require significant capital and analytical depth.

***Corporate and Strategic Investments***
This segment manages the Company’s investment portfolio and oversees our tech-incubation lab. The income generated from our $26,768,075,128 revenue-supported asset base is a critical component of our overall profitability. This segment also manages the corporate debt and liquidity functions of Clearwater.

**Marketing and Distribution**

Clearwater utilizes a diversified distribution network to reach its target markets effectively. Our distribution strategy is bifurcated based on product complexity:

1.  **Independent Agency Channel:** For complex commercial and life insurance products, we rely on a network of over 15,000 independent agents and brokers across the United States. These professionals provide personalized advice and risk assessment for their clients, supported by Clearwater’s digital agent portal.
2.  **Digital Direct Channel:** Our proprietary web and mobile platforms allow consumers to purchase straightforward products, such as term life or personal auto insurance, directly from the Company. This channel is a significant driver of growth among younger, tech-savvy demographics.
3.  **Institutional Partnerships:** We maintain strategic alliances with major banks and financial advisory firms that distribute our retirement and annuity products as part of their broader wealth management offerings.

**Competition**

The insurance industry is highly competitive. Clearwater competes with a variety of organizations, including:
*   Large national and international multi-line insurance companies.
*   Specialized niche carriers focusing on specific lines of insurance.
*   "Insurtech" startups that utilize aggressive pricing and digital-first models.
*   Self-insurance pools and captive insurance entities.

We compete primarily on the basis of brand reputation, financial strength ratings, product features, pricing, and the quality of our claims service. Our ability to process claims faster and more accurately through our "Clearwater Analytics Suite" serves as a primary differentiator in an increasingly commoditized market.

**Investments**

Our investment operations are a fundamental part of our business. As an insurance company, we receive premiums before we are required to pay claims. These funds are invested to ensure we can meet our future obligations. Our investment philosophy emphasizes:
*   **Asset-Liability Management (ALM):** Matching the duration and cash flow profiles of our assets with the expected payouts of our insurance liabilities.
*   **Credit Quality:** The majority of our fixed-income portfolio is invested in investment-grade corporate bonds and government securities.
*   **Diversification:** We limit exposure to any single issuer, industry, or geographic region to mitigate systemic risk.

**Regulation**

Clearwater Technologies Co. is subject to extensive regulation and supervision. Because we are headquartered in New York, the New York State Department of Financial Services (“NYDFS”) serves as our primary domestic regulator. However, our subsidiaries are regulated by the insurance departments of each state in which they are licensed to do business.

*   **State Regulation:** State insurance laws grant regulators broad powers to oversee various aspects of our business, including premium rates, policy forms, capital adequacy, and investment limits.
*   **Capital Requirements:** We are subject to Risk-Based Capital (“RBC”) requirements, which determine the minimum amount of capital we must hold based on the inherent risk of our operations and investments.
*   **The McCarran-Ferguson Act:** This federal law grants states the primary authority to regulate the business of insurance, although certain federal laws regarding securities, data privacy, and anti-money laundering also apply to our operations.
*   **Data Privacy:** Given our reliance on technology and data analytics, we are subject to stringent data protection laws, including the NYDFS Cybersecurity Regulation (23 NYCRR 500) and various state-level consumer privacy acts.

**Human Capital Management**

As of December 31, 2023, Clearwater employed approximately 14,500 professionals. Our workforce is comprised of actuaries, data scientists, claims adjusters, underwriters, and customer service representatives. We believe that our ability to attract and retain high-caliber talent—particularly in the intersection of finance and technology—is essential to our continued success.

*   **Diversity and Inclusion:** We are committed to fostering an inclusive work environment. We believe that a diverse workforce brings a broader range of perspectives to risk assessment and innovation.
*   **Training and Development:** We invest heavily in the "Clearwater Academy," an internal program designed to upskill our employees in emerging fields like artificial intelligence, cloud computing, and advanced risk modeling.
*   **Compensation and Benefits:** We provide competitive compensation packages that include performance-based incentives, comprehensive health benefits, and retirement savings plans, ensuring our interests are aligned with those of our employees.

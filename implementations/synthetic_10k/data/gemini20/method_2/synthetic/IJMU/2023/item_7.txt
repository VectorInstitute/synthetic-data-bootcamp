### **Executive Overview**

Clearwater Technologies Co. (“Clearwater,” the “Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a New York-based diversified financial services provider specializing in multi-line insurance. Our mission is to integrate sophisticated data analytics and machine learning with traditional risk management to provide superior value to our policyholders and shareholders. During the fiscal year ended December 31, 2023, we continued to execute our strategy of “Precision Underwriting,” leveraging our proprietary Clearwater Analytics Suite to navigate a complex macroeconomic environment characterized by fluctuating interest rates and evolving catastrophic risk landscapes.

For the fiscal year 2023, Clearwater generated total revenues of $26,768,075,128 and achieved net income of $3,478,889,508. Our financial performance reflects the strength of our diversified business model, which spans Life and Retirement Solutions, Property and Casualty (P&C) Underwriting, and Institutional Risk and Reinsurance.

Key highlights of our 2023 financial performance include:
*   **Revenue Growth:** Total revenues reached $26,768,075,128, driven by robust premium growth in our Commercial P&C lines and steady demand for our fixed and indexed annuity products within the Life and Retirement segment.
*   **Profitability:** Net income of $3,478,889,508 was supported by disciplined expense management and a favorable effective tax rate of approximately 21.0%.
*   **Operational Cash Flow:** We generated $5,119,284,654 in cash from operating activities, providing ample liquidity to support our insurance obligations and strategic initiatives.
*   **Capital Management:** We returned $2,687,344,796 to shareholders through disciplined share repurchases, reflecting our commitment to capital efficiency, while maintaining a robust total stockholders’ equity of $54,854,314,187.
*   **Balance Sheet Strength:** As of December 31, 2023, our total assets stood at $535,361,502,568, underpinned by a massive investment portfolio categorized within other assets of $529,032,092,003.

### **Economic and Industry Trends**

The multi-line insurance sub-industry faced several headwinds and tailwinds in 2023. Interest rate volatility remained a primary factor influencing our investment income and the valuation of our fixed-income portfolio. While higher rates generally improve the yield on new investments, they also exerted downward pressure on the fair value of our existing holdings, contributing to an accumulated other comprehensive loss of $14,098,949,191.

In the P&C sector, inflationary pressures impacted loss costs, particularly in personal auto and property repairs. However, our Clearwater Analytics Suite allowed us to implement real-time rate adjustments, helping to mitigate these impacts. In the Life and Retirement sector, the aging demographic in the United States continued to drive demand for our guaranteed income products, though the competitive landscape for assets under management remained intense.

#### **Revenues**
Total revenues for 2023 were $26,768,075,128. This revenue base is primarily comprised of:
1.  **Net Premiums Earned:** Representing the bulk of our revenue across Life, P&C, and Reinsurance lines.
2.  **Net Investment Income:** Derived from our $529,032,092,003 in other assets, which primarily consist of high-quality fixed-income securities, commercial mortgages, and other diversified investments.
3.  **Policy Fees and Other Income:** Generated from our retirement solutions and institutional risk management services.

Our revenue performance in 2023 was bolstered by our digital-first distribution strategy. By utilizing our digital direct channel, we reduced friction in the policy acquisition process, particularly for younger demographics in our Life and Personal P&C lines. Additionally, our Institutional Risk and Reinsurance segment saw increased demand for catastrophe-linked products as primary carriers sought to offload volatility.

#### **Operating Expenses**
Total operating expenses for 2023 were $21,865,523,428, consisting of:
*   **Selling, General and Administrative (SG&A) Expenses:** $5,605,305,936. This includes compensation for our 14,500 employees, technology infrastructure costs associated with the Clearwater Analytics Suite, and marketing expenses. SG&A as a percentage of total revenue was approximately 20.9%, reflecting our focus on operational efficiency through automation.
*   **Other Operating Expenses:** $16,260,217,492. This line item is dominated by policyholder benefits and claims incurred across all insurance lines. In 2023, we experienced a moderate increase in claims frequency in our Commercial P&C lines, which was offset by favorable mortality experience in our Life segment.

#### **Interest Expense**
Interest expense for the year was $498,894,095. This relates to our $10,872,612,943 in long-term debt. We maintain a laddered debt maturity profile to manage refinancing risk and ensure a consistent cost of capital.

#### **Income Tax Expense**
Our income tax expense for 2023 was $924,768,097, representing an effective tax rate of approximately 21.0%. This rate is consistent with the U.S. statutory corporate tax rate, adjusted for tax-exempt investment income and various state and local taxes, primarily in New York.

### **Segment Analysis**

Clearwater operates through four primary reportable segments. While we manage the Company on a consolidated basis, the following qualitative insights highlight the performance drivers within each segment for 2023.

#### **Life and Retirement Solutions**
This segment focuses on financial security through life insurance and annuities. In 2023, the rising interest rate environment enhanced the attractiveness of our fixed annuity products, leading to a surge in new policy sales. However, this was balanced by increased competition from other Financials sector participants. Our proprietary modeling for longevity risk remains a key differentiator, allowing for more competitive pricing without compromising our reserve adequacy.

#### **Property and Casualty (P&C) Underwriting**
The P&C segment provides essential protection for individuals and businesses.
*   *Personal Lines:* Our homeowners and auto lines benefited from the Clearwater Analytics Suite, which utilized high-frequency data to adjust for regional variations in repair costs.
*   *Commercial Lines:* We saw significant growth in cyber-risk coverage and general liability. Our specialized tech-underwriting approach has allowed us to capture market share in the mid-market business sector.

#### **Institutional Risk and Reinsurance**
As a provider of reinsurance, we support other carriers by absorbing complex risks. In 2023, we expanded our treaty reinsurance footprint. The segment’s performance is highly dependent on our ability to model tail-risk events. The stability of our $54,854,314,187 in stockholders’ equity provides the necessary capital base to back these high-limit exposures.

#### **Corporate and Strategic Investments**
This segment manages the Company’s vast $529,032,092,003 investment portfolio. The focus remains on asset-liability management (ALM). During 2023, we strategically increased our allocation to investment-grade corporate bonds to capture higher yields. This segment also oversees the Clearwater Academy, ensuring our workforce remains at the forefront of insurtech innovation.

Our primary sources of liquidity are premiums, policy fees, and investment income. Our primary uses of liquidity are the payment of policyholder benefits and claims, operating expenses, and the servicing of debt.

#### **Cash Flows**
For the year ended December 31, 2023, the net change in cash and equivalents was a decrease of $1,639,287,606, resulting in a year-end cash balance of $1,735,063,912.

*   **Operating Activities:** Net cash provided by operating activities was $5,119,284,654. This represents the core engine of our liquidity. The primary drivers were net income of $3,478,889,508 and depreciation and amortization of $3,128,763,338, partially offset by a $1,488,368,192 decrease related to changes in working capital and other assets/liabilities.
*   **Investing Activities:** Net cash used in investing activities was $7,592,952,988. This reflects our ongoing strategy of deploying excess cash into our investment portfolio. *   **Financing Activities:** Net cash provided by financing activities was $834,380,728. This included $3,521,725,524 from other financing activities (such as net proceeds from institutional funding agreements), which was offset by $2,687,344,796 in share repurchases. We did not pay dividends during the 2023 fiscal year.

#### **Capital Position**
As of December 31, 2023, Clearwater’s total stockholders’ equity was $54,854,314,187. This includes:
*   **Additional Paid-in Capital:** $67,417,794,979.
*   **Retained Earnings:** $40,174,042,66.
*   **Accumulated Other Comprehensive Income (AOCI):** A deficit of $14,098,949,191, primarily due to unrealized losses on fixed-income securities resulting from the higher interest rate environment. Under statutory accounting principles, these unrealized losses do not typically impact our regulatory capital unless the securities are sold or deemed other-than-temporarily impaired.

Our debt-to-capital ratio (calculated as long-term debt divided by total capital, where total capital is long-term debt plus total stockholders' equity) was approximately 16.5% at year-end. We believe this level of leverage is appropriate for a multi-line insurer of our scale and provides sufficient flexibility for future growth.

### **Critical Accounting Estimates**

The preparation of financial statements in conformity with U.S. GAAP requires management to make estimates and assumptions. These estimates are highly subjective and can have a material impact on our reported results.

#### **Insurance Reserves**
Our other liabilities of $469,634,575,438 consist largely of reserves for insurance claims. These are based on estimates of the ultimate cost of all claims incurred but not settled. For P&C lines, we use various actuarial methods to project future claim payments. For Life and Retirement products, we estimate future benefits based on assumptions regarding mortality, morbidity, persistency, and investment returns. Small changes in these assumptions could lead to significant adjustments in our liability profile.

#### **Valuation of Investments**
With $529,032,092,003 in other assets, the fair value measurement of our investment portfolio is critical. While many of our assets are valued based on quoted market prices, certain “Level 3” assets (such as private placements or specialized commercial mortgages) require significant management judgment. We utilize independent pricing services and internal models to ensure these valuations are reasonable.

#### **Goodwill Impairment**
We evaluate our $3,538,573,646 in goodwill for impairment at least annually. This involves estimating the fair value of our reporting units using discounted cash flow models. These models are sensitive to assumptions about future revenue growth, operating margins, and discount rates. No impairment was recognized in 2023.

#### **Deferred Acquisition Costs (DAC)**
We defer certain costs that are directly related to the successful acquisition of new insurance contracts and amortize them over the life of the policies. For our Life and Retirement products, the amortization of DAC is highly dependent on estimated future gross profits, which are influenced by investment returns and policyholder behavior.

### **Outlook for 2024**

Looking ahead, Clearwater Technologies Co. is well-positioned to navigate the evolving financial services landscape. Our 2023 revenue of $26,768,075,128 and net income of $3,478,889,508 provide a strong foundation for future growth. We intend to continue our investment in the Clearwater Analytics Suite, with a particular focus on integrating generative artificial intelligence to further streamline our claims processing and customer service.

We remain mindful of the risks identified in Item 1A, particularly those related to interest rate volatility and cybersecurity. However, our robust capital position, characterized by $54,854,314,187 in stockholders’ equity and $1,735,063,912 in cash, gives us the resilience needed to face potential market dislocations. We will continue to monitor the performance of our $529,032,092,003 investment portfolio, ensuring it remains aligned with our long-term insurance liabilities.

Our commitment to New York as our headquarters and the home of our primary talent pool remains steadfast. We believe that by bridging the gap between finance and technology, Clearwater will continue to define the future of the multi-line insurance industry.

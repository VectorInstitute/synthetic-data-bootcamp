Item 1C. Cybersecurity

Risk Management and Strategy

Cybersecurity risk management is an integral component of our broader enterprise risk management framework. We have implemented a comprehensive cybersecurity program designed to identify, protect, detect, and respond to potential threats and vulnerabilities that could impact our information technology systems, our proprietary data, or the personal information of our customers and employees. Given our business model, which relies heavily on digital-first consumer engagement and a complex, global network of third-party manufacturing and logistics partners, we recognize that maintaining the integrity and security of our digital infrastructure is essential to our operational stability and brand reputation.

Our cybersecurity strategy is centered on the continuous assessment and enhancement of our defensive posture. We utilize a multi-layered approach to security that incorporates advanced technological solutions, rigorous internal processes, and regular independent validations. We routinely perform risk assessments to identify material threats from cybersecurity actors, including those related to our direct-to-consumer platforms and our internal corporate systems. These assessments help us prioritize investments in security controls and ensure that our resources are allocated to the areas of greatest potential risk.

We maintain a formal incident response plan that provides a structured framework for addressing and mitigating the impact of any potential security breach. This plan is designed to be scalable and adaptable to various scenarios, ranging from localized system interruptions to large-scale data security incidents. The plan outlines the roles and responsibilities of our internal cybersecurity incident response team, which is composed of cross-functional representatives from our information technology, legal, communications, and human resources departments. We conduct periodic tabletop exercises and simulations to test the effectiveness of these plans and to ensure that our personnel are prepared to act swiftly and decisively in the event of an incident.

Our commitment to cybersecurity extends to our workforce. We have implemented mandatory cybersecurity awareness training for all employees, which is designed to educate them on how to recognize and report common threats such as phishing, social engineering, and unauthorized access attempts. We also provide specialized, more technical training for employees in roles that involve access to sensitive data or critical system administration.

Given our reliance on third-party partners for manufacturing, fulfillment, and digital marketplace operations, third-party risk management is a critical element of our cybersecurity program. We conduct due diligence and security assessments of our key service providers and vendors prior to onboarding them and perform ongoing monitoring of their security posture throughout the duration of our relationship. We contractually require our service providers to implement and maintain appropriate security controls and to notify us promptly in the event of a security incident that could affect our data or systems. We work closely with these partners to coordinate security protocols and to ensure that their defensive measures are aligned with our own standards.

To supplement our internal efforts, we regularly engage external cybersecurity firms to conduct independent assessments of our program. These engagements include penetration testing, vulnerability scanning, and audits of our security controls against recognized industry standards and frameworks. Our internal audit team also provides an independent evaluation of our cybersecurity controls and reports its findings to management and the audit committee of the board of directors.

As of the date of this report, risks from cybersecurity threats, including any previous incidents, have not materially affected our business strategy, results of operations, or financial condition. However, as described in the section titled Risk Factors, we operate in an environment of evolving and increasingly sophisticated cyber threats. While we have invested significantly in our defensive capabilities, there can be no assurance that our security measures will be successful in preventing all potential breaches or system compromises.

Governance

Our board of directors maintains ultimate oversight of the company’s risk management efforts, including the management of cybersecurity risks. The board has delegated the primary responsibility for this oversight to the audit committee. The audit committee is responsible for reviewing the company’s cybersecurity and technology risk profile, our strategies for mitigating those risks, and the overall effectiveness of our cybersecurity program.

The audit committee receives regular reports from management on cybersecurity matters. These updates typically include information on the current threat landscape, the status of ongoing security initiatives, the results of internal and external security assessments, and any significant incidents or near-misses. The audit committee also reviews our incident response readiness and the adequacy of our cybersecurity insurance coverage. Security updates are also provided to the full board of directors on a periodic basis or as circumstances warrant.

At the management level, our cybersecurity program is led by our Chief Information Security Officer. The Chief Information Security Officer is responsible for the design, implementation, and management of our information security strategy and reports directly to our General Counsel. Our current Chief Information Security Officer has extensive experience in the field of information technology and security, having spent several decades in leadership roles within the technology and consumer sectors prior to joining the company. This individual holds advanced degrees in computer science and information systems and maintains various professional certifications in security management.

The Chief Information Security Officer leads a dedicated team of experienced cybersecurity professionals who are responsible for the day-to-day operation of our security controls and the monitoring of our systems for potential threats. This team works closely with our information technology department and other business units to ensure that security is integrated into our operational processes and product development lifecycles.

Management has established a cybersecurity steering committee, which includes the Chief Information Security Officer, the Chief Information Officer, the General Counsel, and other senior leaders. This committee meets regularly to review the company’s security posture, evaluate emerging risks, and oversee the execution of our cybersecurity roadmap. This governance structure ensures that cybersecurity is treated as a strategic business priority and that there is clear accountability for the management of digital risks across the organization.

In the event of a significant cybersecurity incident, we have established protocols for the timely escalation of information to senior management and the audit committee. These protocols ensure that the board of directors is kept informed of any developments that could have a material impact on the company and that management has the necessary support and resources to respond effectively. Through this integrated governance and risk management approach, we strive to protect our digital assets and maintain the trust of our customers and stakeholders.

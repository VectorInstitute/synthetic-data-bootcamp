Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

The following Management’s Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations (MD&A) is intended to provide a reader of our financial statements with a narrative from the perspective of our management on our financial condition, results of operations, liquidity, and certain other factors that may affect our future results. This MD&A should be read in conjunction with our Consolidated Financial Statements and related notes included in Item 8 of this Annual Report on Form 10-K.

Overview

Selzennov Materials, Inc. is a leading provider of high-performance materials and specialized chemical solutions. Our operations are organized into three primary reportable segments: Advanced Polymer Technologies, Performance Chemical Solutions, and Engineered Infrastructure Systems. We serve a diverse range of end-markets, including the automotive, electronics, infrastructure, and industrial manufacturing sectors. Our business model, which we refer to as the Selzennov Advantage, focuses on leveraging our material science expertise to provide value-added solutions that enhance the performance and durability of our customers' products.

During the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, our business demonstrated resilience and growth despite a complex global macroeconomic environment characterized by fluctuating raw material costs and elevated interest rates. We achieved total revenue of $14,240 million in 2024, representing an increase of 5.3% compared to $13,521 million in 2023. This growth was primarily driven by strong demand in our Engineered Infrastructure Systems segment, as government-funded projects continued to drive consumption of concrete additives and architectural coatings. Additionally, our Advanced Polymer Technologies segment benefited from the ongoing transition toward lightweight materials in the automotive sector, particularly for electric vehicle applications.

Our operating income increased 14.4% to $1,192 million in 2024, compared to $1,042 million in 2023. This improvement in operating profitability was the result of favorable pricing actions taken to offset inflationary pressures and a disciplined approach to managing our selling, general and administrative expenses. Our net income for 2024 was $383 million, a 33.9% increase from $286 million in the prior year.

We continue to face challenges related to the volatility of raw material inputs, particularly petroleum-based feedstocks and specialized minerals. In 2024, our cost of revenue was $11,829 million, compared to $11,290 million in 2023. While we were successful in improving our gross margin to 16.9% in 2024 from 16.5% in 2023, we remain vigilant regarding the impact of global supply chain dynamics and energy prices on our cost structure.

Our financial position remains robust, with cash and cash equivalents of $3,038 million as of December 31, 2024. We maintain a significant level of long-term debt, which stood at $15,480 million at year-end. Managing our capital structure and interest expense, which was $697 million in 2024, remains a key priority for management. We also remain committed to returning value to our stockholders, as evidenced by our payment of $116 million in dividends and $338 million in net share repurchases during the fiscal year.

Results of Operations

The following table and discussion provide a comparison of our results of operations for the fiscal years ended December 31, 2024 and 2023.

Revenue

Revenue for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024 was $14,240 million, an increase of $719 million, or 5.3%, compared to $13,521 million for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2023. The increase in revenue was primarily attributable to higher sales volumes in our infrastructure and automotive end-markets.

In our Engineered Infrastructure Systems segment, we saw increased demand for high-performance concrete additives and composite reinforcement materials, driven by public works projects and urbanization trends in North America and Asia-Pacific. Revenue in our Advanced Polymer Technologies segment also grew as we expanded our supply of specialized resins used in automotive lightweighting applications. These volume gains were partially offset by localized economic softening in certain industrial manufacturing sectors that impacted our Performance Chemical Solutions segment. Pricing adjustments implemented across all segments to mitigate higher input costs also contributed to the overall revenue growth.

Cost of Revenue and Gross Margin

Cost of revenue for 2024 was $11,829 million, compared to $11,290 million in 2023, representing an increase of $539 million. This increase was largely driven by higher sales volumes and the rising cost of certain raw materials, including chemical intermediates and energy-related inputs used in our manufacturing processes.

Gross profit for 2024 was $2,411 million, compared to $2,231 million in 2023. Our gross margin improved to 16.9% in 2024, compared to 16.5% in 2023. This 40 basis point expansion in gross margin was primarily due to our ability to implement price increases that exceeded the rate of raw material inflation, as well as operational efficiencies achieved through our Selzennov Advantage initiatives. We also benefited from a more favorable product mix, with a higher proportion of sales coming from specialized, high-margin polymer formulations.

Selling, General and Administrative Expenses

Selling, general and administrative (SGA) expenses for 2024 were $365 million, compared to $378 million in 2023. As a percentage of revenue, SGA expenses decreased to 2.6% in 2024 from 2.8% in 2023. The $13 million decrease in SGA expenses was primarily due to cost-saving measures, including the consolidation of certain administrative functions and lower third-party consulting fees. These savings were achieved while continuing to support our global sales force of approximately 18,000 associates and maintaining our technical service capabilities.

Research and Development

Research and development (R&D) expenses were $0 million for both the fiscal years ended December 31, 2024 and 2023. As a share of revenue, R&D expenditure remained at 0.0%. We currently rely on our existing portfolio of proprietary technology, trade secrets, and "know-how" to maintain our competitive position. While we do not record formal R&D expenses, our technical service teams work closely with customers to adapt our existing material solutions to their specific manufacturing requirements.

Other Operating Expense

Other operating expense for 2024 was $854 million, compared to $811 million in 2023. This increase of $43 million was primarily driven by higher logistics and transportation costs associated with our global supply chain, as well as increased maintenance expenses at several of our larger manufacturing facilities. We also incurred higher environmental compliance and insurance costs during the year.

Operating Income

Operating income for 2024 was $1,192 million, an increase of $150 million, or 14.4%, compared to $1,042 million in 2023. Our operating margin improved to 8.4% in 2024 from 7.7% in 2023. The increase in operating income and margin reflects the growth in gross profit and the reduction in SGA expenses, which more than offset the increase in other operating expenses.

Interest Expense

Interest expense for 2024 was $697 million, compared to $665 million in 2023. The increase of $32 million was primarily due to higher average interest rates on our variable-rate debt and the issuance of additional long-term debt during the year to fund investing activities and provide general liquidity. Our total long-term debt increased to $15,480 million at the end of 2024.

Provision for Income Taxes

Our provision for income taxes was $112 million in 2024, compared to $91 million in 2023. The effective tax rate for 2024 was 22.6%, compared to 24.1% for 2023. The decrease in the effective tax rate was primarily due to a more favorable geographic mix of earnings and the resolution of certain prior-year tax positions in international jurisdictions.

Net Income

Net income for 2024 was $383 million, compared to $286 million in 2023, representing an increase of $97 million, or 33.9%. The increase was driven by the strong growth in operating income and the lower effective tax rate, partially offset by higher interest expense. Our net margin for 2024 was 2.7%, an improvement from 2.1% in 2023.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

Our primary sources of liquidity are cash flows from operations and borrowings under our debt facilities. As of December 31, 2024, we had cash and cash equivalents of $3,038 million, an increase of $207 million from $2,831 million at the end of 2023. We believe that our existing cash balances, anticipated cash flows from operations, and available credit will be sufficient to meet our working capital, capital expenditure, and debt service requirements for at least the next twelve months.

Cash Flows

Net cash provided by operating activities in 2024 was $1,186 million. This was driven by our net income of $383 million, adjusted for non-cash items including depreciation and amortization of $698 million. Changes in working capital and other items contributed a net $105 million to operating cash flow. We continue to focus on optimizing our working capital components, including accounts receivable, which was $2,952 million at year-end, and inventories, which stood at $1,820 million.

Net cash used in investing activities in 2024 was $1,900 million. This included $779 million for purchases of property and equipment, as we continued to invest in the modernization and expansion of our manufacturing footprint. Other investing activities, net, resulted in a cash outflow of $1,121 million, which primarily related to strategic acquisitions and investments in non-current assets to support our long-term growth objectives.

Net cash used in financing activities in 2024 was $921 million. This included a net change in long-term debt of $699 million, representing additional borrowings to support our investing activities. During the year, we paid $116 million in dividends to our stockholders. We also engaged in share repurchases, net of issuances, totaling $338 million, reflecting our commitment to returning capital to shareholders.

Capital Expenditures

Our capital expenditures in 2024 were $779 million, compared to our total property, plant and equipment, net, of $14,342 million. These investments were focused on enhancing the safety and efficiency of our production lines, expanding capacity in our Advanced Polymer Technologies segment to meet automotive demand, and upgrading our distribution network for Engineered Infrastructure Systems. We anticipate that capital expenditures in 2025 will remain at similar levels as we continue to execute on our multi-year facility improvement plan.

Debt and Credit Facilities

As of December 31, 2024, our long-term debt was $15,480 million, compared to $14,781 million at the end of 2023. This increase reflects the additional financing secured during the year to fund our investing program. Our debt portfolio consists of a mix of fixed and variable-rate instruments. The significant level of indebtedness requires a substantial portion of our cash flow to be dedicated to interest payments, which totaled $697 million in 2024. We are in compliance with all covenants associated with our debt agreements and maintain access to capital markets to manage our liquidity needs.

Dividends and Share Repurchases

We paid dividends of $116 million during 2024. The declaration and payment of future dividends are at the discretion of our Board of Directors and will depend on our financial condition, results of operations, cash requirements, and other factors. In addition to dividends, we utilized $338 million for the net repurchase of our common stock. These repurchases are part of our broader capital allocation strategy aimed at enhancing stockholder value.

Critical Accounting Estimates

The preparation of our consolidated financial statements in accordance with U.S. GAAP requires management to make estimates and assumptions that affect the reported amounts of assets, liabilities, revenue, and expenses. We base our estimates on historical experience and on various other assumptions that we believe to be reasonable under the circumstances. Actual results may differ from these estimates. We believe the following accounting estimates are the most critical to an understanding of our financial results.

Revenue Recognition

We recognize revenue when control of the promised goods is transferred to our customers, in an amount that reflects the consideration we expect to be entitled to in exchange for those goods. This generally occurs at the point of shipment or delivery, depending on the terms of the contract. While the majority of our revenue is derived from standard product sales, some contracts include volume-based rebates or discounts. We estimate these variable considerations using the expected value method based on historical data and projected purchase volumes. Changes in these estimates could impact the timing and amount of revenue recognized.

Goodwill and Intangible Assets

As of December 31, 2024, we had goodwill of $9,821 million. We test goodwill for impairment at the reporting unit level annually, or more frequently if events or changes in circumstances indicate that the asset might be impaired. The process of evaluating goodwill for impairment requires significant judgment, including the identification of reporting units, the assignment of assets and liabilities to reporting units, and the determination of the fair value of each reporting unit. We estimate fair value using a combination of the income approach, which relies on discounted cash flow projections, and the market approach, which considers comparable company multiples. The cash flow projections are based on management's estimates of future revenue growth, operating margins, and discount rates. If the carrying amount of a reporting unit exceeds its fair value, an impairment loss is recognized. No impairment charges were recorded in 2024 or 2023.

Inventory Valuation

Inventories, which totaled $1,820 million as of December 31, 2024, are stated at the lower of cost or net realizable value. Cost is determined using the first-in, first-out (FIFO) or average cost methods. We regularly review our inventory for obsolescence or slow-moving items based on our assessment of future demand and market conditions. If actual demand is lower than our projections, we may be required to record additional write-downs to reduce the carrying value of our inventory, which would adversely affect our gross profit.

Income Taxes

We are subject to income taxes in the United States and numerous foreign jurisdictions. The determination of our provision for income taxes requires significant judgment and the use of estimates. We recognize deferred tax assets and liabilities for the expected future tax consequences of events that have been included in the financial statements or tax returns. We record a valuation allowance to reduce our deferred tax assets to the amount that is more likely than not to be realized. In 2024, our provision for income taxes was $112 million, reflecting an effective tax rate of 22.6%. Our future effective tax rates could be affected by changes in tax laws, the geographic mix of our earnings, and the results of tax audits.

Property, Plant and Equipment

Property, plant and equipment, net, was $14,342 million as of December 31, 2024. These assets are depreciated over their estimated useful lives using the straight-line method. We evaluate the carrying value of our long-lived assets for impairment whenever events or changes in circumstances indicate that the carrying amount of an asset may not be recoverable. This evaluation requires us to estimate the future undiscounted cash flows expected to be generated by the asset. If the sum of the expected future undiscounted cash flows is less than the carrying amount of the asset, an impairment loss is recognized. The estimation of useful lives and future cash flows involves significant management judgment.

Accounts Receivable and Allowance for Credit Losses

Our accounts receivable, net, was $2,952 million as of December 31, 2024. We maintain an allowance for credit losses to reflect the expected collectability of our receivables. This allowance is based on our assessment of the creditworthiness of our customers, historical collection experience, and current and forecasted economic conditions. If the financial condition of our customers were to deteriorate, resulting in an impairment of their ability to make payments, additional allowances might be required.

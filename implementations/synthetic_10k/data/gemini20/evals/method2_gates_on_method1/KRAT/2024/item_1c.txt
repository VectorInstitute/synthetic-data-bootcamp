Item 1C. Cybersecurity

Risk Management and Strategy

We maintain a comprehensive cybersecurity risk management program designed to identify, assess, and manage material risks from cybersecurity threats. This program is integrated into our broader enterprise risk management framework, ensuring that digital threats are evaluated alongside operational, financial, and regulatory risks. Given our role as a materials company with extensive extraction and processing operations, our strategy specifically addresses the protection of both information technology systems and the operational technology utilized at our quarries, processing plants, and distribution terminals.

Our approach to managing cybersecurity risk involves a multi-layered defense strategy. We utilize a combination of internal security teams and external experts to monitor our network environment, perform vulnerability assessments, and conduct regular penetration testing. We align our cybersecurity processes with recognized industry standards and best practices, focusing on the protection of proprietary processing data, employee information, and our logistics and supply chain systems. Our risk assessment process includes the evaluation of emerging threats, the identification of potential system vulnerabilities, and the implementation of automated tools to detect and respond to unauthorized activities.

We also recognize the risks associated with our reliance on third-party service providers, including suppliers of heavy machinery, transportation partners, and cloud computing vendors. We have established processes to identify and oversee cybersecurity risks related to these third parties. This includes performing due diligence on the security posture of key vendors, incorporating cybersecurity requirements into our service agreements, and monitoring for potential disruptions within our supply chain that could result from a cyber incident at a partner organization.

We have an incident response plan in place that outlines the procedures for responding to and recovering from cybersecurity incidents. This plan includes protocols for internal escalation, legal and regulatory notification, and the containment of potential breaches to minimize operational disruptions. While we have experienced various cybersecurity threats and minor incidents in the past, we have not identified any cybersecurity incident that has materially affected our business strategy, results of operations, or financial condition. However, we remain vigilant as the threat landscape continues to evolve and become more sophisticated.

Governance

The Audit Committee of our Board of Directors is responsible for the oversight of risks from cybersecurity threats. The Audit Committee receives regular reports from management regarding our cybersecurity posture, the effectiveness of our internal controls, and the status of ongoing initiatives to enhance our digital security. These briefings include updates on the evolving threat environment, results from independent security audits, and progress on the implementation of new protective technologies. The Audit Committee periodically briefs the full Board of Directors on significant cybersecurity matters and risks.

At the management level, our cybersecurity strategy is led by our Chief Information Security Officer (CISO), who reports directly to our Chief Information Officer (CIO). Our CISO has extensive experience in information security, including a long tenure in leadership roles focused on protecting industrial and manufacturing systems. Our CISO is supported by a dedicated team of cybersecurity professionals responsible for the day-to-day management of our security operations, including threat intelligence, identity management, and incident response.

Our management team, including the CISO and CIO, is responsible for the design and implementation of our cybersecurity policies and for ensuring that our workforce is trained on security best practices. We conduct regular cybersecurity awareness training for our employees to mitigate the risks of social engineering and other common attack vectors. Management also oversees the integration of cybersecurity considerations into our capital expenditure decisions, particularly as we modernize our processing facilities and adopt more advanced automation technologies. Through this governance structure, we aim to maintain a high level of accountability and ensure that cybersecurity remains a priority across all levels of our organization.

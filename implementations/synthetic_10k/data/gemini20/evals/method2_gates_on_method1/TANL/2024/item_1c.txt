Item 1C. Cybersecurity

Risk Management and Strategy

Talven Therapeutics, Inc. maintains a comprehensive cybersecurity risk management program designed to protect the integrity, confidentiality, and availability of our global information technology (IT) and operational technology (OT) systems. Given our extensive operations across the Pharmaceutical Innovation, Integrated Health Services, and Medical Supply and Distribution segments, we rely heavily on digital infrastructure to conduct research and development, manage clinical services, and coordinate a vast logistics network. Our cybersecurity program is integrated into our broader enterprise risk management framework and is designed to address the evolving threat landscape that characterizes the healthcare and biotechnology sectors.

Our strategy for managing cybersecurity risk is aligned with recognized industry standards, including the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) Cybersecurity Framework. This structured approach allows us to identify, protect, detect, respond to, and recover from potential security incidents. We have implemented a defense-in-depth architecture that utilizes multiple layers of security controls, including advanced encryption protocols, multi-factor authentication, and continuous network monitoring. These controls are applied across our corporate offices, research laboratories, clinical sites, and distribution centers to safeguard our proprietary data and the sensitive information of our patients and customers.

A critical component of our risk management process is the protection of intellectual property within our Pharmaceutical Innovation segment. We employ specialized security measures to protect our research data, clinical trial results, and manufacturing processes from unauthorized access or theft. Similarly, our Integrated Health Services segment handles significant volumes of protected health information and personal data related to our pharmacy benefit management and clinical operations. We maintain rigorous data privacy protocols to ensure compliance with global regulatory requirements and to preserve the trust of the patients we serve.

In our Medical Supply and Distribution segment, we focus on the resilience of our OT systems, which are essential for the automation and management of our warehouses and logistics operations. We conduct regular vulnerability assessments and penetration testing to identify potential weaknesses in these systems. Our cybersecurity team also monitors threat intelligence feeds to stay informed of emerging risks, such as ransomware and supply chain attacks, that could disrupt our ability to deliver critical medical supplies to hospital systems and retail pharmacies.

Recognizing that our workforce of approximately 852,000 employees represents both a vital asset and a potential point of vulnerability, we prioritize cybersecurity awareness and training. All employees are required to complete annual security training, and we conduct periodic simulations, such as phishing exercises, to reinforce best practices. These initiatives are designed to foster a culture of security throughout the organization and to ensure that our personnel are equipped to recognize and report suspicious activities.

Our risk management strategy also extends to third-party service providers, vendors, and partners. We engage in a rigorous due diligence process before entering into agreements with third parties that will have access to our systems or data. This process includes security assessments and the inclusion of specific cybersecurity requirements in our contracts. We continuously monitor the security posture of our key vendors and conduct supplemental reviews if a third party experiences a security incident that could indirectly impact our operations. This proactive approach is intended to mitigate risks associated with the interconnected nature of the modern healthcare supply chain.

We have established a formal Cybersecurity Incident Response Plan (CIRP) that outlines the procedures for identifying, escalating, and remediating security breaches. The CIRP is tested regularly through tabletop exercises involving cross-functional teams, including representatives from legal, communications, and executive management. These exercises ensure that we are prepared to respond quickly and effectively to minimize the impact of an incident on our financial condition or results of operations.

As of the date of this Annual Report on Form 10-K, we are not aware of any cybersecurity threats or previous incidents that have materially affected or are reasonably likely to materially affect our business strategy, results of operations, or financial condition. However, despite our significant investments in security infrastructure and personnel, we cannot provide absolute assurance that a sophisticated cyberattack will not occur in the future. We remain committed to continuously enhancing our security posture to address new and emerging threats.

Governance

Cybersecurity governance at Talven Therapeutics is characterized by active oversight from our Board of Directors and a clear management reporting structure. The Board is responsible for overseeing our enterprise-wide approach to risk management, with the Risk Committee of the Board holding primary responsibility for the oversight of cybersecurity risks.

The Risk Committee receives quarterly updates from management regarding our cybersecurity program, the current threat environment, and the status of ongoing security initiatives. These reports include metrics on system uptime, the results of recent vulnerability assessments, and progress toward strategic security goals. The Board also receives an annual presentation on cybersecurity as part of our broader enterprise risk management review. This high-level engagement ensures that cybersecurity remains a priority at the highest levels of the organization and that our security investments are aligned with our long-term business objectives.

Our global cybersecurity organization is led by our Chief Information Security Officer (CISO), who has primary responsibility for the development and execution of our security strategy. Our current CISO has over two decades of experience in information security and technology management, having previously held senior leadership roles at large multinational organizations in the healthcare and financial services sectors. The CISO holds several industry-recognized certifications, including the Certified Information Systems Security Professional (CISSP) designation, and is a frequent contributor to industry forums on cybersecurity best practices.

The CISO reports directly to our Chief Information Officer (CIO), who in turn reports to our Chief Information and Digital Officer (CIDO). This reporting structure ensures that cybersecurity is integrated into our overall information technology strategy and that the CISO has the necessary resources and organizational support to manage risk effectively. The CISO also maintains a direct line of communication with the Risk Committee of the Board and the General Counsel for the purpose of reporting significant security matters.

We have implemented a formal escalation framework to ensure that potential cybersecurity incidents are reported to the appropriate levels of management in a timely manner. This framework includes a crisis management committee composed of senior executives who are responsible for making strategic decisions during a significant security event. If an incident is determined to have a potential material impact on our financial results or reputation, it is immediately escalated to the Board of Directors.

In addition to internal reporting, we engage with external experts and industry groups to stay informed of the latest developments in cybersecurity governance. We participate in information-sharing organizations within the healthcare sector to exchange threat intelligence and collaborate on defensive strategies with our peers. This external engagement complements our internal governance processes and helps us maintain a proactive stance against the sophisticated actors who target the healthcare industry.

By maintaining a robust governance structure and a disciplined approach to risk management, Talven Therapeutics aims to protect its assets and ensure the continued delivery of innovative medical solutions to patients worldwide. We will continue to evaluate and refine our cybersecurity program in response to the changing technological landscape and the evolving needs of our global business.

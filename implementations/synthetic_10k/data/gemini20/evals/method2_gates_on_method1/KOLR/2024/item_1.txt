Item 1. Business

Overview

Kalondra Machinery, Inc., together with its consolidated subsidiaries, is a global leader in the design, manufacture, and distribution of heavy machinery, power systems, and advanced industrial technologies. Incorporated in the State of Delaware and headquartered in San Jose, California, the Company provides essential equipment and services to a diverse range of industries, including construction, mining, energy, and precision manufacturing. With a history of engineering excellence, Kalondra Machinery, Inc. serves as a critical link in the global industrial supply chain, enabling the development of infrastructure, the extraction of natural resources, and the advancement of automated production.

Our operations are characterized by a commitment to innovation, operational efficiency, and a customer-centric service model. We maintain an extensive global footprint, with manufacturing facilities, distribution centers, and service hubs located across multiple continents. This geographic diversity allows us to respond effectively to regional market demands and mitigate risks associated with localized economic fluctuations. In fiscal year 2024, the Company generated total revenue of $82,769 million.

Reportable Segments

The Company manages its operations through three primary reportable segments: Construction and Infrastructure, Industrial Power and Energy, and Precision Manufacturing Systems. These segments are organized based on the nature of the products and services provided, as well as the end markets they serve.

Construction and Infrastructure

The Construction and Infrastructure segment is our largest business unit by volume and is responsible for the production of a wide array of machinery used in earthmoving, road building, and urban development. Our product portfolio in this segment includes backhoe loaders, excavators, motor graders, track-type tractors, and asphalt pavers. These machines are engineered to operate in the most demanding environments, providing our customers with the reliability and productivity required for large-scale civil engineering projects.

In addition to heavy machinery, this segment offers a suite of technology-enabled solutions designed to optimize job site performance. These include telematics systems that monitor equipment health and location, automated grade control systems that improve leveling accuracy, and fleet management software that assists contractors in reducing idle time and fuel consumption. By integrating hardware with sophisticated digital tools, we help our customers improve their operational margins and meet increasingly stringent project timelines.

Industrial Power and Energy

The Industrial Power and Energy segment focuses on the design and manufacture of reciprocating engines, turbines, and integrated power systems. These products serve a variety of applications, including electric power generation, marine propulsion, and industrial drive systems for the oil and gas sector. Our engines are designed to run on a range of fuels, including diesel, natural gas, and increasingly, alternative fuels such as hydrogen blends and biofuels.

This segment also provides comprehensive energy storage solutions and microgrid controllers, which are becoming vital as industrial customers seek to integrate renewable energy sources into their power portfolios. Our power systems are frequently used as primary energy sources in remote locations where grid access is unavailable, as well as critical backup power for hospitals, data centers, and telecommunications infrastructure. The service component of this segment is particularly robust, as long-term maintenance contracts ensure the continuous operation of high-value power assets.

Precision Manufacturing Systems

The Precision Manufacturing Systems segment represents our commitment to the future of factory automation and industrial robotics. This segment develops and produces high-precision CNC (Computer Numerical Control) machines, robotic assembly arms, and additive manufacturing systems. These technologies are utilized primarily by customers in the automotive, aerospace, and consumer electronics industries to achieve high levels of throughput and exacting quality standards.

Our precision systems are increasingly focused on the concept of the smart factory, where interconnected machines communicate in real-time to adjust production schedules and identify potential mechanical failures before they occur. This segment also includes our software division, which develops proprietary computer-aided design and manufacturing tools. By providing both the physical machinery and the digital architecture required for modern manufacturing, we position ourselves as a strategic partner to global industrial firms seeking to modernize their production lines.

Customers

Our customer base is highly diversified, ranging from small, family-owned construction firms and local municipalities to multinational energy corporations and sovereign entities. In the Construction and Infrastructure segment, customers typically include general contractors, rental equipment companies, and mining operators. The Industrial Power and Energy segment serves utility companies, shipping lines, and industrial plant operators. Precision Manufacturing Systems primarily sells to high-volume manufacturers and specialized engineering firms.

We do not depend on any single customer for a disproportionate share of our total revenue. Our largest customers often engage with us through long-term framework agreements that encompass equipment sales, parts supply, and ongoing technical support. This breadth of customer engagement provides a stable foundation for our revenue streams and allows us to gain deep insights into the evolving needs of various industrial sectors.

Sales and Marketing

We utilize a multi-channel distribution strategy to reach our global customer base. The primary component of this strategy is our extensive network of independent dealers. These dealers are typically local or regional businesses that possess deep knowledge of their specific markets. They provide not only sales expertise but also critical after-sales support, including maintenance, repair, and parts distribution. This dealer model allows us to maintain a local presence in nearly every major market without the capital intensity of owning and operating every retail location.

For our largest global accounts and complex industrial projects, we employ a direct sales force. These professionals work closely with customers to develop customized equipment configurations and integrated service packages. Marketing efforts are focused on demonstrating the total cost of ownership advantages of our products, emphasizing fuel efficiency, durability, and high resale value. We also participate in major international trade shows and utilize digital marketing platforms to showcase our latest technological advancements.

Competition

The markets for industrial machinery and power systems are intensely competitive. We compete with both large, diversified global manufacturers and smaller, specialized regional players. The primary factors influencing competition in our industry include product performance, reliability, technological innovation, price, and the quality of the dealer support network.

In recent years, competition has intensified as new entrants, particularly from emerging markets, have introduced lower-cost alternatives. We respond to this competition by continuously investing in research and development to maintain our technological lead and by enhancing our service offerings. Our ability to provide a comprehensive "lifecycle" solution—from initial purchase and financing to maintenance and eventual trade-in—remains a key competitive differentiator.

Seasonality

Our business experiences a degree of seasonality, particularly within the Construction and Infrastructure segment. Demand for construction equipment in the Northern Hemisphere typically peaks during the spring and summer months when weather conditions are most favorable for building activity. Conversely, the Industrial Power and Energy segment may see increased demand for power generation equipment during periods of extreme weather or in advance of winter months.

Budgetary cycles of government entities also influence the timing of our sales, as many infrastructure projects are funded through public appropriations that follow specific fiscal calendars. While these seasonal patterns are predictable, they can be disrupted by broader economic cycles or significant shifts in commodity prices, which influence the capital expenditure budgets of our mining and energy customers.

Intellectual Property

Intellectual property is a vital asset to Kalondra Machinery, Inc. We rely on a combination of patents, trademarks, copyrights, and trade secrets to protect our innovations and maintain our competitive position. Our patent portfolio covers a wide range of technologies, including engine combustion cycles, hydraulic system designs, autonomous navigation algorithms, and material science breakthroughs.

We actively monitor and enforce our intellectual property rights globally to prevent unauthorized use of our technology and branding. In addition to our own research and development efforts, we occasionally license technology from third parties or enter into collaborative research agreements. We believe that our brand name is one of our most valuable intangible assets, symbolizing quality and durability to customers worldwide.

Government Regulation

Our operations are subject to extensive and evolving government regulations in the jurisdictions where we manufacture and sell our products. These regulations primarily concern environmental protection, occupational health and safety, and international trade.

Environmental Regulation

We are subject to stringent emission standards for our engines and machinery, such as those established by the Environmental Protection Agency in the United States and similar regulatory bodies in Europe and Asia. These regulations require continuous investment in engine technology to reduce the output of nitrogen oxides and particulate matter. Additionally, our manufacturing facilities must comply with laws regarding waste management, water discharge, and chemical handling.

Trade and Export Controls

As a global exporter, we must adhere to various trade regulations, including export controls, customs duties, and economic sanctions. Changes in trade policy or the imposition of tariffs on raw materials, such as steel and aluminum, can significantly impact our manufacturing costs and the pricing of our products in international markets. We maintain robust compliance programs to ensure we meet all legal requirements related to cross-border transactions.

Safety and Labor

The safety of our products is regulated by various national and international standards. We are committed to meeting or exceeding these standards to ensure the well-being of the operators who use our equipment. Our manufacturing sites are also subject to labor laws and workplace safety regulations, such as those overseen by the Occupational Safety and Health Administration.

Human Capital

The success of Kalondra Machinery, Inc. is driven by our global workforce of about 126,100 employees. Our human capital strategy is focused on attracting, developing, and retaining a diverse and highly skilled team capable of navigating the complexities of the modern industrial landscape.

Safety and Well-being

The safety of our employees is our highest priority. We maintain a comprehensive safety management system that emphasizes risk assessment, continuous training, and the reporting of near-miss incidents. Our goal is to achieve zero workplace injuries through a culture of shared responsibility and proactive hazard mitigation. We also provide wellness programs and competitive benefits packages to support the physical and mental health of our workforce.

Diversity and Inclusion

We believe that a diverse workforce fosters innovation and better reflects the global markets we serve. We are committed to providing equal employment opportunities and creating an inclusive environment where all employees feel valued. Our diversity initiatives include targeted recruitment efforts, leadership development programs for underrepresented groups, and employee resource groups that promote cultural awareness and professional networking.

Training and Development

Given the rapid pace of technological change in our industry, we invest heavily in employee training and development. We offer a variety of programs, including technical apprenticeships, leadership academies, and tuition reimbursement for advanced degrees. By fostering a culture of continuous learning, we ensure that our employees possess the skills necessary to design and build the next generation of industrial machinery.

Labor Relations

A portion of our workforce is represented by labor unions or works councils, particularly in our manufacturing operations. We strive to maintain constructive and transparent relationships with these organizations through regular dialogue and collective bargaining. We believe that a collaborative approach to labor relations is essential for maintaining operational stability and achieving our long-term strategic objectives.

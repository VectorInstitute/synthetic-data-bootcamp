Item 1C. Cybersecurity
Cybersecurity Governance
Hilton has a dedicated Global Information Security team (collectively, the "GIS team") led by our Chief Information Security Officer ("CISO") that is responsible for identifying, assessing, monitoring, managing and communicating the Company's cybersecurity risks. The GIS team is organized into five functional areas: (i) cloud, network and infrastructure architecture security; (ii) application security; (iii) incident response; (iv) endpoint security and vulnerability management; and (v) governance, risk and compliance ("GRC"). Collectively, the GIS team has decades of dedicated cybersecurity experience with personnel certified in various disciplines, including data privacy, enterprise risk management, cloud security and ethical hacking.
While the full board of directors has overall responsibility for risk oversight, for cyber security matters, it is supported by its Audit Committee, which regularly reports to the full board of directors. The Audit Committee assists the board of directors in monitoring cybersecurity risk by receiving quarterly reports and as needed updates from the Chief Information Officer and the CISO, that cover, among other things, our information security framework, threat assessment, response readiness and training efforts.
Hilton has adopted a Cybersecurity Policy that requires all employees to immediately report a potential cybersecurity incident to the GIS team, and all employees are required to certify their understanding of the Cybersecurity Policy on an annual basis. Our Global Cybersecurity Incident Response Plan ("CIRP") includes the criteria for determining if a cybersecurity incident is considered a qualifying cybersecurity incident ("QCI"), which requires management escalation and review, identifies the first response team and the leadership team responsible for supervising the response and provides guidelines for when and how to communicate such incident to the appropriate members of management and the Audit Committee.
Cybersecurity Strategy and Risk Management
The GIS team leverages several mechanisms to continuously identify and assess cybersecurity risks across the Company and utilizes a GRC platform to monitor identified risks and mitigation and remediation activities. The GIS team uses defined industry accepted risk management and controls frameworks to determine the potential likelihood and impact of each risk. Monitoring activities are designed and executed based on the materiality of the assessed likelihood and magnitude of impact of the risks that are identified. The GIS team, with the assistance of third-party consultants, performs application security reviews, penetration tests and gap assessments against certain cybersecurity frameworks. Management reviews any assessments performed by the third-party consultants and determines the final evaluations and communication plan, which the GIS team executes.
In the event of a reported potential cybersecurity incident, a first response team, which includes leaders of the GIS team, other members of management and the legal team, determines without undue delay whether it is a QCI as defined in the CIRP. If an incident is determined to be a QCI, the process included in the CIRP is initiated and such incident is communicated to the designated leadership team, including Hilton's general counsel. Further, appointed leaders collaborate on determining if the incident is material, as well as the resulting response, including any legal and financial reporting obligations of the Company. Information also is provided to additional members of senior management as appropriate. The remediation plan for the QCI is entered within Hilton's GRC platform and monitored and reviewed at least monthly to ensure effective implementation; depending upon the type of incident, additional reporting may be produced and monitored by the GIS team to ensure the effectiveness of the remediation plan. All cybersecurity incidents are tracked within our incident response platform, regardless of the potential materiality of the impact.
We also have a process in place to manage cybersecurity risks associated with third-party service providers. However, we rely on the third parties we use to implement security programs commensurate with their risk, and we cannot ensure in all circumstances that their efforts will be successful.

Item 1. Business

BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Novqua Retail, Inc. (collectively with its subsidiaries, the Company, Novqua, we, us, or our) is a leading multi-channel retailer in the consumer discretionary sector. Incorporated in the State of Delaware and headquartered in New York, New York, the Company operates an extensive network of physical retail locations and integrated digital commerce platforms. Our business is built upon a commitment to providing a broad assortment of high-quality merchandise, ranging from apparel and accessories to home furnishings and lifestyle products, at competitive price points.

We aim to create a compelling shopping experience that combines the convenience of modern e-commerce with the tactile and immediate gratification of brick-and-mortar retail. Our strategy focuses on inventory freshness, operational efficiency, and a deep understanding of consumer trends. By leveraging a sophisticated global supply chain and a robust distribution network, we ensure that our product offerings remain relevant to an evolving customer base. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, the Company generated total revenue of $51,238 million.

The Company’s operations are supported by a large-scale infrastructure that includes regional distribution centers, corporate offices, and a dedicated workforce of approximately 57,400 employees. We believe that our scale, brand recognition, and diversified product portfolio position us as a significant participant in the global retail landscape. Our business model is designed to withstand fluctuations in consumer sentiment by offering a value proposition that appeals to a wide range of demographic segments across various geographic regions.

PRINCIPAL PRODUCTS AND SERVICES

Novqua Retail, Inc. offers a diverse array of consumer products across several core categories. Our merchandise selection is curated to meet the daily needs and aspirational desires of our customers, with a focus on quality, style, and value.

Apparel and Accessories
This category represents a significant portion of our business and includes a wide variety of clothing for men, women, and children. Our offerings span from basic essentials and activewear to formal attire and seasonal fashion. We complement our apparel lines with a broad selection of accessories, including footwear, handbags, jewelry, and watches. We source these products from a mix of well-known national brands and our own private-label brands, allowing us to offer a unique assortment that distinguishes our stores from competitors.

Home Fashions and Lifestyle
Our home category includes a comprehensive range of products designed to enhance the living environment. This includes furniture, bedding, bath textiles, kitchenware, and decorative accents. We also offer seasonal products, such as outdoor living items and holiday-themed decor. By maintaining a high turnover of home merchandise, we provide customers with a sense of discovery during each visit, encouraging frequent repeat traffic.

Beauty and Personal Care
We provide an extensive selection of beauty products, including skincare, cosmetics, fragrances, and hair care. Our beauty departments are designed to offer an immersive experience, featuring both established prestige brands and emerging labels. This category is a key driver of customer engagement, particularly among younger demographics who value innovation and trends in personal care.

REPORTABLE SEGMENTS

The Company manages its business through three reportable segments: Novqua North America, Novqua International, and Novqua Direct. This structure allows us to tailor our operational strategies to the specific needs of different markets and channels.

Novqua North America
The Novqua North America segment comprises our physical retail operations in the United States and Canada. This is our largest segment and serves as the foundation of our brand identity. Our stores in this segment are typically located in high-traffic areas, including regional malls, lifestyle centers, and urban hubs. These locations are designed with flexible layouts that can be easily reconfigured to highlight seasonal trends or new product launches. We focus on providing a superior in-store experience through attentive customer service and clear visual merchandising.

Novqua International
The Novqua International segment includes our retail operations outside of North America, primarily focusing on established and emerging markets in Europe and Asia. We adapt our international store formats and product assortments to align with local consumer preferences, cultural nuances, and sizing standards. This segment is a key component of our long-term growth strategy, as we seek to increase our global market share and capitalize on the rising middle-class population in developing regions. Our international operations benefit from our global sourcing scale while maintaining local relevance.

Novqua Direct
Novqua Direct encompasses our digital commerce operations, including our primary e-commerce websites and mobile applications. This segment is integrated with our physical store network to provide a seamless omnichannel experience. Customers can browse our full catalog online, choose from various delivery options, or utilize in-store pickup and return services. We invest heavily in our digital infrastructure to ensure a secure, intuitive, and personalized shopping experience. Novqua Direct also serves as a platform for testing new products and gathering data-driven insights into customer behavior.

CUSTOMERS

Our customer base is highly diversified, reflecting the broad appeal of our product categories and price points. We serve millions of unique customers annually, ranging from budget-conscious families to trend-seeking individuals. Our core customer typically prioritizes a balance of quality and price, seeking branded merchandise at a significant value.

Through our loyalty programs and digital engagement strategies, we have built a substantial database of repeat shoppers. We use advanced analytics to understand purchasing patterns and preferences, allowing us to deliver targeted promotions and personalized recommendations. Our commitment to customer satisfaction is reflected in our liberal return policies and our focus on maintaining a safe and welcoming shopping environment, both in-person and online.

SALES AND MARKETING

The Company employs a multi-faceted marketing strategy designed to drive brand awareness, increase store traffic, and boost digital conversion rates. Our marketing efforts are centered on our value proposition and the breadth of our merchandise assortment.

Brand Positioning
We position the Novqua brand as a reliable destination for quality and value. Our advertising campaigns emphasize the "joy of discovery" and the excitement of finding high-end products at accessible prices. We utilize a mix of traditional media, such as television and print, alongside a robust digital presence.

Digital and Social Media
Our digital marketing strategy includes search engine optimization, paid search, email marketing, and active engagement on major social media platforms. We collaborate with influencers and content creators to reach younger audiences and showcase our products in lifestyle contexts. Our mobile app serves as a critical touchpoint, providing customers with early access to sales, personalized offers, and a convenient interface for managing their loyalty rewards.

In-Store Promotions
Physical retail locations utilize visual merchandising, window displays, and point-of-sale promotions to encourage unplanned purchases and highlight seasonal arrivals. We frequently refresh our store layouts to keep the shopping experience dynamic and engaging.

COMPETITION

The retail industry is intensely competitive and subject to rapid changes in consumer preferences and shopping habits. We compete with a wide range of retailers across various formats.

Department Stores and Specialty Retailers
We compete with traditional department stores that offer similar categories of apparel and home goods. These competitors often emphasize brand heritage and high-touch service. We also face competition from specialty retailers that focus on specific niches, such as athletic wear or home decor.

Discount and Off-Price Retailers
Our value-oriented pricing puts us in direct competition with discount stores and other off-price retailers. These competitors focus heavily on price leadership and lean operating models. We differentiate ourselves by offering a more curated selection of premium brands and a superior shopping environment.

Online Marketplaces
The rise of large-scale online marketplaces has significantly altered the competitive landscape. These entities offer vast selections and rapid delivery. To compete, we leverage our physical store network for immediate product availability and emphasize the tactile experience of in-person shopping, while simultaneously enhancing our own digital capabilities.

We believe our primary competitive advantages include our global sourcing scale, our established brand reputation, our sophisticated logistics network, and our ability to provide a consistent omnichannel experience.

SEASONALITY

Our business is subject to seasonal fluctuations that are typical of the retail industry. A significant portion of our annual revenue and operating income is generated during the fourth quarter, which includes the traditional holiday shopping season. We also experience increased activity during the back-to-school period in the third quarter and during seasonal transitions in the spring.

To manage this seasonality, we carefully plan our inventory levels and staffing requirements. We increase our marketing efforts and adjust our product assortments to align with peak demand periods. Any significant disruption during the fourth quarter, such as adverse weather conditions or economic downturns, could have a disproportionate impact on our annual financial results.

INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

The Company owns and utilizes a variety of intellectual property assets that are essential to our business. These include trademarks, service marks, brand names, and proprietary software.

Trademarks and Brand Names
The "Novqua" name and associated logos are registered trademarks in the United States and many international jurisdictions. We also own several private-label brand names that are exclusive to our stores. These trademarks are critical to our brand identity and customer loyalty. We vigorously protect our intellectual property rights against unauthorized use or infringement.

Proprietary Technology
We have developed proprietary software systems for inventory management, supply chain optimization, and customer relationship management. These systems provide us with a competitive edge by improving operational efficiency and enabling data-driven decision-making. We also hold various domain names related to our digital commerce operations.

Our business is highly sensitive to global economic conditions and consumer spending patterns, which may adversely affect our revenue and profitability.

If we raise prices too aggressively, we risk losing market share to discount competitors; if we fail to raise prices sufficiently, our margins will continue to be compressed. The retail industry is intensely competitive, and our failure to compete effectively could lead to a loss of market share and reduced profitability.

We operate in a highly fragmented and competitive environment, facing pressure from traditional department stores, specialty retailers, discount and off-price stores, and massive online marketplaces. Many of our competitors have significant financial resources, sophisticated technological capabilities, and extensive global footprints. Some competitors may be able to offer lower prices, more frequent promotions, or faster delivery options than we can. If we are unable to differentiate our merchandise assortment, provide a compelling shopping experience, or maintain competitive pricing, our competitive position may be weakened.

Revenue

Revenue for fiscal 2024 was $51,238 million, compared to $51,976 million for fiscal 2023, representing a decrease of $738 million, or 1.4%. This decrease was primarily attributable to lower comparable store sales in our Novqua North America and Novqua International segments. In North America, revenue was impacted by a reduction in transaction volume as consumers faced higher living costs and reduced discretionary income. While our apparel and beauty categories remained relatively resilient, we saw a notable decrease in demand for larger-ticket home fashion items.

The Novqua International segment also experienced revenue pressure, particularly in European markets where economic growth remained sluggish. Foreign currency fluctuations also played a role, as a stronger U.S. dollar reduced the reported value of our international sales. Conversely, our Novqua Direct segment showed relative strength, as customers continued to migrate toward the convenience of our digital platforms. However, the growth in digital sales was not sufficient to fully offset the declines in our brick-and-mortar locations.

Cost of Revenue and Gross Margin

Cost of revenue for fiscal 2024 was $36,205 million, compared to $36,235 million in fiscal 2023, a decrease of $30 million. While the total cost of revenue decreased slightly in absolute dollar terms due to lower sales volume, it increased as a percentage of revenue. This resulted in a gross margin of 29.3% for fiscal 2024, compared to 30.3% for fiscal 2023.

The 100 basis point decrease in gross margin was driven by several factors. First, we experienced higher product costs due to inflationary pressures on raw materials and manufacturing labor. Second, the competitive environment necessitated higher levels of promotional activity and markdowns to clear seasonal inventory, particularly in the apparel and home categories. Third, while global freight costs have stabilized compared to historical peaks, they remained elevated relative to pre-pandemic levels, weighing on our margin profile. We continue to implement strategic sourcing initiatives and inventory management protocols to mitigate these pressures.

Selling, General and Administrative (SG&A) Expenses

SG&A expenses were $7,804 million in fiscal 2024, compared to $7,963 million in fiscal 2023, representing a decrease of $159 million, or 2.0%. As a percentage of revenue, SG&A expenses were 15.2% in fiscal 2024, compared to 15.3% in fiscal 2023.

The decrease in SG&A expenses reflects our ongoing commitment to operational efficiency and cost discipline. During the year, we implemented several productivity initiatives, including the optimization of store labor scheduling and a reduction in discretionary corporate spending. These savings were partially offset by continued investments in our digital capabilities and higher wage rates in certain markets. We also incurred higher marketing expenses as we increased our digital advertising spend to drive traffic to our e-commerce platforms and loyalty programs.

Other Operating Expense

Other operating expense was $3,074 million in fiscal 2024, compared to $3,119 million in fiscal 2023, a decrease of $45 million. This category primarily includes depreciation and amortization related to our retail locations and distribution centers, as well as certain facility-related costs. The slight decrease was primarily due to the timing of certain asset retirements and the completion of depreciation cycles for older store equipment, partially offset by the depreciation of new investments in our supply chain infrastructure.

Operating Income

Operating income for fiscal 2024 was $4,155 million, compared to $4,659 million in fiscal 2023, representing a decrease of $504 million, or 10.8%. Operating margin decreased to 8.1% in fiscal 2024 from 9.0% in fiscal 2023. The decline in operating income was primarily the result of the lower gross margin and the decrease in total revenue, which more than offset the modest reductions in SG&A and other operating expenses.

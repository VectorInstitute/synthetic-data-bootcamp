Item 1. Business

Overview

Orris Aerospace, Inc. (collectively with its subsidiaries, the Company, Orris, we, us, or our) is a premier global provider of highly engineered systems, components, and services for the aerospace and defense industries. Incorporated in the State of Delaware and headquartered in Columbus, Ohio, we have established ourselves as a critical partner to the world’s leading aircraft manufacturers, airlines, and defense agencies. Our mission is to deliver innovative, reliable, and sustainable solutions that enhance the safety, efficiency, and performance of flight and space exploration.

For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, we generated total revenue of $10,928 million. Our operations are characterized by high barriers to entry, including significant research and development requirements, complex regulatory certifications, and long-term program lifecycles that often span several decades. We operate through a global network of manufacturing facilities, research centers, and service hubs, supported by a dedicated workforce of approximately 27,000 employees.

Our business model is built upon a balanced portfolio of original equipment (OE) and aftermarket components. We provide critical systems for a wide range of platforms, including commercial narrow-body and wide-body aircraft, regional and business jets, military fighter aircraft, transport planes, helicopters, and space launch vehicles. By maintaining a strong presence in both the commercial and defense sectors, we are able to mitigate the impact of cyclicality in any single market and maintain a steady trajectory of growth and innovation.

Our Strategy

The Orris strategy is focused on driving long-term shareholder value through a commitment to technological leadership, operational excellence, and customer-centricity. We believe that the aerospace industry is entering a transformative era characterized by the need for greater fuel efficiency, reduced emissions, and increased digitalization. Our strategic initiatives are designed to position Orris at the forefront of these trends.

We prioritize investments in core technologies where we can establish and maintain a competitive advantage. This includes the development of next-generation avionics, advanced thermal management systems, and lightweight structural materials. We also seek to expand our digital capabilities, leveraging data analytics and artificial intelligence to improve the predictive maintenance of our products and the efficiency of our manufacturing processes.

External growth through disciplined acquisitions and strategic partnerships also forms a key part of our strategy. We look for opportunities to acquire businesses that provide complementary technologies, expand our geographic footprint, or increase our presence on high-growth aircraft platforms. Furthermore, we are committed to a rigorous capital allocation framework that prioritizes internal investment in high-return projects while returning excess cash to stockholders through dividends and share repurchases.

Foundational Elements

Our success is underpinned by three foundational elements that define our competitive identity: our market position, our operating model, and our commitment to sustainability.

Market Position

We strive to be a top-tier supplier on every major aircraft program we serve. Our goal is to achieve vertical integration where it provides a clear benefit to our customers in terms of quality, cost, and delivery. By controlling the design and manufacture of critical subsystems, we can ensure the highest levels of reliability and performance. Our market planning process involves a deep analysis of global fleet trends, airline profitability, and government defense budgets, allowing us to align our resources with the most promising opportunities.

Operating Model

The Orris Production System (OPS) is our proprietary framework for continuous improvement and operational excellence. OPS integrates lean manufacturing principles with advanced quality management systems to drive efficiency across our global footprint. We utilize standardized business practices to ensure consistency and scalability, while empowering local management teams to make decisions that best serve their specific customers. This model allows us to manage complex supply chains effectively and respond quickly to changes in demand or production requirements.

Sustainability and Innovation

We believe that sustainability is essential for the long-term viability of the aerospace industry. Our research and development efforts are increasingly focused on technologies that help our customers achieve their decarbonization goals. This includes the development of systems for electric and hybrid-electric propulsion, as well as components optimized for sustainable aviation fuels. We are also committed to reducing the environmental footprint of our own operations through energy efficiency programs and waste reduction initiatives.

Reportable Segments

We manage our business through three reportable segments: Commercial Aviation Systems, Defense and Space Solutions, and Aftermarket Services. Each segment is focused on a specific set of customers and market dynamics, but they all benefit from our shared technological expertise and operational infrastructure.

Commercial Aviation Systems

The Commercial Aviation Systems segment designs and manufactures a broad range of products for the commercial air transport, regional, and business aviation markets. Our offerings include integrated avionics suites, flight control systems, environmental control systems, and engine components. We are a key supplier to all major commercial airframe manufacturers and engine OEMs.

This segment is driven by the long-term growth in global air travel and the ongoing replacement of older, less efficient aircraft fleets. We focus on securing positions on high-volume platforms, particularly narrow-body aircraft, which represent the largest portion of the global fleet. Our engineering teams work closely with OEMs during the initial design phase of new aircraft to integrate our systems seamlessly, ensuring optimal performance and safety.

Defense and Space Solutions

The Defense and Space Solutions segment provides specialized systems and components for military aircraft, unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs), satellites, and space exploration platforms. Our products include secure communications systems, electronic warfare components, tactical sensors, and satellite propulsion subsystems. We serve as a direct contractor to government defense agencies and as a sub-tier supplier to major defense prime contractors.

The performance of this segment is influenced by national defense budgets, geopolitical stability, and the pace of technological advancement in modern warfare and space exploration. We are increasingly focused on the development of autonomous systems and high-speed data processing capabilities, which are critical for the next generation of defense platforms. Our space business benefits from the growing demand for satellite constellations and the renewed global interest in lunar and deep-space missions.

Aftermarket Services

The Aftermarket Services segment provides comprehensive maintenance, repair, and overhaul (MRO) services, as well as the sale of spare parts and components for our installed base of products. We operate a global network of service centers and distribution facilities to provide rapid support to airlines, fleet operators, and military customers.

This segment provides a recurring and high-margin revenue stream that is less sensitive to the cycles of new aircraft production. Our aftermarket strategy is built on providing superior technical support and ensuring the availability of critical parts. We offer a variety of service arrangements, including long-term "power-by-the-hour" contracts, which provide customers with predictable maintenance costs and improved aircraft availability. We also leverage digital health monitoring tools to provide predictive maintenance alerts, helping our customers avoid unscheduled downtime.

Principal Products and Services

Our product portfolio is diverse and spans the entire aircraft lifecycle. Key product categories include:

Avionics and Flight Controls

We produce sophisticated electronic systems that manage the flight path, communications, and navigation of an aircraft. This includes primary flight displays, flight management systems, and integrated cockpit suites that provide pilots with critical situational awareness. Our flight control systems include actuators and sensors that translate pilot or autopilot commands into physical movements of the aircraft's control surfaces.

Propulsion and Thermal Management

Orris provides essential components for modern jet engines, including fuel control units, sensors, and bleed air valves. We also specialize in thermal management systems that regulate the temperature of critical electronics and cabin environments. These systems are vital for the efficient operation of high-performance engines and the comfort of passengers and crew.

Structural and Interior Assemblies

We manufacture high-strength structural components and interior assemblies for both commercial and military aircraft. Our expertise in advanced materials, such as carbon fiber composites and specialized alloys, allows us to produce parts that are both lightweight and durable, contributing to improved fuel efficiency and payload capacity.

MRO and Technical Support

Our service offerings include the repair and overhaul of our own components as well as third-party equipment. We provide technical training to customer maintenance crews and offer 24/7 AOG (Aircraft on Ground) support to ensure that our customers can return their aircraft to service as quickly as possible.

Customers

Our customer base is highly diversified and includes the world's leading aerospace and defense organizations. In the commercial sector, our primary customers are major airframe manufacturers and engine OEMs. We also sell products and services directly to hundreds of airlines and aircraft leasing companies worldwide.

In the defense sector, we serve various branches of the United States military and the defense ministries of allied nations. We also act as a key supplier to the large prime contractors that manage major defense programs. Our space products are sold to government space agencies and commercial satellite operators.

The nature of our customer relationships is typically long-term, characterized by multi-year contracts and deep technical collaboration. Because our products are often integrated into the design of an aircraft or engine, we benefit from a stable position on a program throughout its multi-decade production and service life.

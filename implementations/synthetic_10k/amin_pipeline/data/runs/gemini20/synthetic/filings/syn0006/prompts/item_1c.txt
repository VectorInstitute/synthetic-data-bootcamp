Item 1. Business

Overview

Selzenqua Semiconductor, Inc. is a global leader in the design, development, manufacturing, and marketing of a wide range of semiconductor solutions. We provide essential technologies that power a diverse array of applications across the computing, communications, automotive, industrial, and consumer electronics markets. Our mission is to deliver high-performance, energy-efficient, and reliable semiconductor products that enable our customers to innovate and solve complex challenges in an increasingly connected and data-driven world.

Incorporated in Delaware and headquartered in Princeton, New Jersey, Selzenqua has established a significant global footprint with operations spanning North America, Europe, and Asia. Our expertise lies in the integration of complex analog, digital, and mixed-signal functionalities into single-chip solutions or multi-chip modules that meet the stringent performance and quality requirements of our global customer base. In fiscal 2024, our revenue was $16,983 million.

The semiconductor industry is characterized by rapid technological advancement, short product life cycles, and intense competition. To maintain our leadership position, we invest significantly in research and development and maintain a robust portfolio of intellectual property. We believe our ability to provide comprehensive system-level expertise, combined with our advanced manufacturing capabilities and deep customer relationships, differentiates us from our competitors.

Principal Products and Services

Our product portfolio is comprised of thousands of individual products designed to address specific functional requirements within electronic systems. These products generally fall into several categories, including microcontrollers, analog and mixed-signal integrated circuits, power management devices, sensors, and connectivity solutions.

Microcontrollers and Processors: We offer a broad range of microcontrollers based on various architectures that serve as the "brains" of electronic devices. These products are used in applications ranging from simple household appliances to complex automotive control systems and industrial automation equipment. Our processors are designed for high efficiency and low power consumption, which is critical for battery-operated and energy-sensitive applications.

Analog and Mixed-Signal Solutions: Our analog products manage real-world signals such as light, sound, temperature, and pressure, converting them into digital data for processing. Our mixed-signal solutions integrate both analog and digital circuits on a single chip, providing high performance and reduced system cost for our customers. These are widely used in signal conditioning, data conversion, and interface applications.

Power Management: As electronic systems become more complex and energy-conscious, the demand for efficient power management has increased. Our power management products include voltage regulators, supervisors, and battery management systems that optimize energy usage and extend the life of electronic devices. We are particularly focused on power solutions for electric vehicles and high-performance data centers.

Connectivity and Sensors: We provide a variety of wired and wireless connectivity solutions, including technologies for Ethernet, Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth. Additionally, our sensor portfolio includes micro-electromechanical systems (MEMS) and optical sensors that enable devices to perceive and interact with their environment.

Reportable Segments

We organize our business into three reportable segments based on the primary end markets they serve and the nature of the products provided. These segments are: Computing and Infrastructure Solutions; Automotive, Industrial, and Medical Solutions; and Mobile and IoT Solutions.

Computing and Infrastructure Solutions

This segment focuses on the high-performance requirements of the data center, enterprise server, and networking markets. We provide high-speed interconnects, advanced processing units, and specialized memory interface products that enable the rapid movement and processing of massive amounts of data. As cloud computing and artificial intelligence continue to expand, this segment develops technologies that improve data throughput and reduce latency. Our products in this segment are critical for the infrastructure that supports the global internet, social media platforms, and corporate enterprise networks. We work closely with major cloud service providers and server manufacturers to define the next generation of infrastructure standards.

Automotive, Industrial, and Medical Solutions

Our Automotive, Industrial, and Medical segment addresses markets that require high levels of reliability, longevity, and safety. In the automotive market, we provide solutions for advanced driver assistance systems (ADAS), vehicle electrification, and in-vehicle infotainment. Our products help enable the transition from internal combustion engines to electric and autonomous vehicles. In the industrial sector, we provide components for factory automation, robotics, and smart grid infrastructure, helping customers improve operational efficiency and sustainability. The medical portion of this segment provides specialized semiconductors for diagnostic imaging, patient monitoring, and implantable devices, where precision and low power consumption are paramount. This segment is characterized by long product life cycles and rigorous qualification processes.

Mobile and IoT Solutions

The Mobile and IoT segment serves the fast-paced consumer electronics market, including smartphones, tablets, wearables, and smart home devices. We provide radio frequency (RF) front-end modules, audio codecs, touch controllers, and ultra-low-power microcontrollers that are essential for the functionality and user experience of mobile devices. In the Internet of Things (IoT) space, we offer integrated connectivity and processing platforms that allow everyday objects to connect to the internet and each other. This segment requires a high degree of innovation and the ability to scale production rapidly to meet the seasonal demand cycles of consumer product launches. We focus on reducing the physical footprint of our components while increasing their functional integration to help our customers design thinner, lighter, and more capable devices.

Customers

Our customer base is highly diversified, consisting of thousands of original equipment manufacturers (OEMs), contract manufacturers, and distributors worldwide. We sell our products to many of the world's leading technology companies across various industries. While we serve a broad range of customers, a significant portion of our revenue is derived from a limited number of large OEMs and global distributors.

Our relationships with major OEMs are often collaborative, involving multi-year development cycles where our engineers work alongside the customer's design team to create optimized solutions. This deep integration helps us anticipate market trends and secure "design wins," where our products are specified into the customer's end-product blueprints.

Distributors play a vital role in our go-to-market strategy, particularly for reaching small to medium-sized customers and managing inventory for high-volume, standardized products. We work with a network of global and regional distributors who provide logistics, technical support, and credit to the end-users of our products.

Sales and Marketing

We market and sell our products through a combination of a direct sales force, field application engineers (FAEs), and independent sales representatives and distributors. Our sales and marketing headquarters are located in Princeton, New Jersey, with regional sales offices situated near major technology hubs around the world.

Our direct sales force focuses on our largest and most strategic accounts, providing high-touch service and managing the overall business relationship. Our FAEs are highly trained engineers who provide technical assistance to customers during the design-in process, helping them integrate our semiconductors into their systems effectively. This technical support is a key competitive advantage, as it reduces the time-to-market for our customers and fosters long-term loyalty.

Marketing activities include participation in industry trade shows, technical seminars, and digital marketing initiatives. We also provide extensive online resources, including design tools, software libraries, and documentation, to enable developers to easily evaluate and implement our solutions.

Competition

The semiconductor industry is intensely competitive and is characterized by rapid technological change, evolving industry standards, and price erosion. We compete with a wide range of companies, from large, diversified semiconductor manufacturers to smaller, specialized firms that focus on specific product categories or geographic regions.

Our competitors include global integrated device manufacturers that have their own fabrication facilities, as well as fabless companies that focus on design and outsource their manufacturing. In certain markets, we also compete with the internal semiconductor design teams of our own customers, who may choose to develop custom chips rather than purchasing merchant market solutions.

The principal competitive factors in our industry include:
- Product performance, features, and functionality.
- Energy efficiency and power consumption.
- Price and total cost of ownership.
- Reliability and quality.
- Technical support and ease of design-in.
- Ability to secure manufacturing capacity and meet delivery schedules.
- Breadth of product portfolio and system-level expertise.

We believe we compete effectively by maintaining a high level of investment in research and development, which allows us to introduce innovative products that offer superior performance and value. However, some of our competitors may have greater financial, technical, or marketing resources, or may have more established positions in certain markets or regions.

Seasonality

Our business is subject to seasonal fluctuations in demand, particularly in the Mobile and IoT segment. Consumer electronics demand typically peaks in the second half of the calendar year in anticipation of the holiday shopping season and major product launches. The Automotive and Industrial segments generally experience more stable demand throughout the year, although they can be influenced by broader economic cycles and periodic shutdowns in manufacturing, such as those that occur during the summer months in Europe or the Lunar New Year in Asia. Because of these factors, our quarterly results of operations may vary, and results for any particular quarter are not necessarily indicative of results for the full year.

Our success depends significantly on our ability to protect our proprietary technology and to operate without infringing the intellectual property rights of others. We rely on a combination of patents, copyrights, trademarks, and trade secret laws, as well as confidentiality procedures and contractual provisions, to protect our intellectual property.

Risks Related to Our Business and Industry

The semiconductor industry is highly cyclical and has experienced significant downturns in the past, which may occur again and adversely affect our revenue and profitability.

Our business is subject to the cyclicality of the semiconductor industry, which is characterized by periodic fluctuations in product supply and demand. We have experienced, and may in the future experience, significant variations in our results of operations due to these cycles. Downturns in the industry often result in reduced demand for our products, excess inventory levels, and accelerated erosion of average selling prices. Because a significant portion of our costs is fixed, particularly our investments in manufacturing facilities and research and development, a decrease in revenue can disproportionately impact our operating margins and net income. We cannot predict the timing, duration, or severity of any future industry downturn, and a prolonged period of reduced demand could materially and adversely affect our financial condition.

We face intense competition from a wide range of companies, and our inability to compete effectively could result in a loss of market share and reduced margins.

The markets for our semiconductor solutions are characterized by intense competition, rapid technological change, and evolving industry standards. We compete with large, diversified international semiconductor companies as well as smaller, specialized designers. Some of our competitors have significantly greater financial, technical, and marketing resources than we do. We also face competition from the internal design teams of our customers, who may develop their own proprietary chips rather than purchasing our merchant market solutions. Competitive pressures often lead to price erosion, and if we are unable to offset price declines through cost reductions or the introduction of new products with higher margins, our profitability will suffer. Our failure to maintain a competitive advantage in product performance, power efficiency, or price could lead to a loss of design wins and a decline in our market share.

Our substantial investment in research and development may not result in successful products or adequate returns.

To remain competitive, we must devote a significant portion of our revenue to research and development activities. The process of developing new semiconductor products is complex, costly, and time-consuming. There is no guarantee that our research and development efforts will result in commercially successful products or that we will be able to introduce new products in a timely manner. If we fail to accurately anticipate market trends or customer requirements, our new products may not achieve market acceptance. Furthermore, even if we develop technically superior products, our competitors may introduce alternative technologies that render our products obsolete. If we are unable to generate sufficient revenue from our new products to offset our significant research and development expenditures, our results of operations and long-term competitiveness will be harmed.

We are dependent on a limited number of end markets, and a decline in demand within these markets could negatively impact our business.

Our products are concentrated in specific end markets, including mobile devices, automotive systems, industrial automation, and computing infrastructure. A significant portion of our revenue is derived from the Mobile and IoT segment, which is subject to high volatility, short product life cycles, and intense seasonal demand. If there is a decline in consumer spending or a shift in consumer preferences away from the devices that incorporate our chips, our revenue could decrease. Similarly, our Automotive, Industrial, and Medical segment is sensitive to broader economic cycles and changes in government regulations. A slowdown in the transition to electric vehicles or a reduction in industrial capital expenditures could lead to reduced demand for our specialized microcontrollers and power management devices.

Our results of operations may be adversely affected if we are unable to secure design wins with leading original equipment manufacturers.

Our success depends on our products being designed into the end products of our customers, a process commonly referred to as a design win. These design wins often involve lengthy and rigorous evaluation processes by original equipment manufacturers. If we fail to achieve design wins with key customers, particularly those who hold significant market share in their respective industries, we may be excluded from those product generations for several years. Even if we secure a design win, there is no guarantee that the customer's end product will be commercially successful or that the customer will purchase the volumes of our products that we anticipated. The loss of a major design win or the failure of a customer's product in the market could result in significant lost revenue and excess inventory.

If our internal facilities experience equipment failures, labor shortages, or utility interruptions, our production schedules could be delayed. Conversely, if we are unable to secure sufficient capacity from our third-party foundry partners, we may be unable to meet customer demand. Overview

Selzenqua Semiconductor, Inc. is a global leader in the design, development, and manufacturing of semiconductor solutions that power a wide range of applications in the computing, automotive, industrial, and mobile markets. Headquartered in Princeton, New Jersey, and with approximately 50,700 employees worldwide, we provide high-performance analog, digital, and mixed-signal integrated circuits. Our business is organized into three reportable segments: Computing and Infrastructure Solutions; Automotive, Industrial, and Medical Solutions; and Mobile and IoT Solutions.

For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, our revenue increased to $16,983 million, representing a growth of 13.7% compared to the prior fiscal year. This growth was driven by robust demand for our high-performance computing products and the continued electrification and automation trends in the automotive and industrial sectors. Despite the increase in revenue, our gross margin decreased to 36.3% from 37.3% in the prior year, primarily due to higher input costs and a shift in product mix. We continued to invest heavily in the future of our technology, with research and development expenses reaching $4,353 million, or 25.6% of revenue.

Our operating income for fiscal 2024 was $1,296 million, an increase of 6.8% over the $1,214 million reported in fiscal 2023. Net income for the year was $648 million, representing a growth of 5.5% compared to $614 million in the prior year. We maintained a solid liquidity position, ending the year with $2,211 million in cash and cash equivalents.

Results of Operations

Revenue

Revenue for fiscal 2024 was $16,983 million, compared to $14,943 million for fiscal 2023, an increase of $2,040 million, or 13.7%. The increase in revenue was primarily attributable to higher shipment volumes and favorable average selling prices in our Computing and Infrastructure Solutions segment, where demand for data center and artificial intelligence infrastructure remained strong. Our Automotive, Industrial, and Medical Solutions segment also contributed to the growth, driven by the increasing semiconductor content in electric vehicles and factory automation systems. These gains were partially offset by more moderate growth in the Mobile and IoT Solutions segment, which faced a maturing smartphone market and high levels of inventory at certain distributors during the first half of the year.

Cost of Revenue and Gross Margin

Cost of revenue for fiscal 2024 was $10,825 million, compared to $9,375 million for fiscal 2023. The increase in cost of revenue was primarily due to the higher volume of products sold and increased costs for raw materials and energy.

Gross profit for fiscal 2024 was $6,158 million, compared to $5,568 million for fiscal 2023. Gross margin for fiscal 2024 was 36.3%, compared to 37.3% for fiscal 2023. The decrease in gross margin of 1.0 percentage point was largely due to a shift in product mix toward higher-volume, lower-margin mobile products and the impact of underutilization in certain internal manufacturing facilities during the early part of the year. Additionally, we experienced increased logistics and supply chain costs which put downward pressure on our margins.

Research and Development

Research and development (R&D) expenses for fiscal 2024 were $4,353 million, compared to $3,801 million for fiscal 2023. As a percentage of revenue, R&D expenses were 25.6% in fiscal 2024, compared to 25.4% in fiscal 2023. The increase in R&D spending reflects our commitment to innovation and the development of next-generation process nodes and chip architectures. The growth in spending was primarily driven by higher personnel-related costs, including share-based compensation, and increased project-related costs for advanced lithography and design tools.

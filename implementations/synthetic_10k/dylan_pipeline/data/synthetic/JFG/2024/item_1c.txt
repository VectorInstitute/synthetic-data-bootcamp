Cybersecurity Program

We maintain a cybersecurity risk management program informed by the NIST Cybersecurity Framework, designed to identify, assess, and manage risks from cybersecurity threats to our information systems and the data we process, including data related to our on-site inventory and vending programs at customer locations.

Our program includes continuous monitoring of our information technology environment, periodic vulnerability assessments and penetration testing, employee security-awareness training, and a formal incident response plan that sets out procedures for identifying, containing, and remediating cybersecurity incidents. Because a portion of our operations depends on third-party service providers and vending hardware deployed at customer sites, our vendor management process includes an assessment of those providers' security practices.

Cybersecurity Risks

We have not experienced a material cybersecurity incident to date. A future incident affecting our systems, our vending hardware deployed at customer sites, or those of our third-party service providers could disrupt our operations, compromise sensitive data, and result in remediation costs, litigation, or reputational harm. See Item 1A, "Risk Factors," for additional discussion of cybersecurity-related risks.

Governance

Our full Board of Directors, with support from the Audit Committee, oversees our cybersecurity risk management program. Management provides the Board and the Audit Committee with periodic updates on our cybersecurity program, including the results of risk assessments and the status of our security initiatives.

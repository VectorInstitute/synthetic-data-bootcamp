Introduction

Harborview Partners Corp. ("Harborview," "we," "our," or "us") is headquartered in California and develops electronic design automation ("EDA") software and related intellectual property used by semiconductor and electronic systems companies to design, verify, and manufacture integrated circuits and electronic systems. Our products are used throughout the chip design process, from early architectural exploration through physical verification and manufacturing hand-off.

We license our software primarily through term-based arrangements and also offer intellectual property licensing and design services to complement our software products. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025, we generated revenue of $4,533.8 million.

Research and Development

The complexity of semiconductor design increases with each new process generation, and our customers depend on us to keep pace with that complexity. We invest substantially in research and development to extend our software's capabilities and to support new process nodes and design methodologies as they are introduced by our customers and their foundry partners.

Human Capital

Our workforce consists primarily of software engineers, applications engineers, and sales and support personnel, located in the United States and internationally. Competition for engineering talent with deep semiconductor and software expertise is intense, and we focus on compensation, equity-based incentives, and a technically engaging work environment to attract and retain employees.

Competition

The EDA software market is concentrated among a small number of established providers, as well as point-solution vendors that address specific steps in the design flow. We believe the principal competitive factors in our market are the breadth and depth of our software's capability, the quality of our customer support, and the strength of our relationships with semiconductor foundries and intellectual property providers.

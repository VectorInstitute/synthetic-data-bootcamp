Introduction

Stonegate Sciences Corp. ("Stonegate," "we," "our," or "us") is headquartered in New York and provides a cloud-based observability and analytics platform that helps organizations monitor the performance, availability, and security of their infrastructure and applications. Our platform integrates infrastructure monitoring, application performance monitoring, and log management into a single product, delivered as software-as-a-service ("SaaS").

Customers deploy our platform across cloud, on-premise, and hybrid environments. We employ a usage-based, land-and-expand business model: customers typically begin with a single use case and expand their usage as their infrastructure grows or as they adopt additional modules on our platform. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025, we generated revenue of $2,711.8 million.

Research and Development

Our business depends on continued investment in our platform's capabilities. Research and development expense was $1,347.2 million for fiscal 2025, representing approximately 49.7% of revenue, reflecting our ongoing investment in new monitoring capabilities, integrations, and the underlying data infrastructure that supports our platform at scale.

Human Capital

Our employees are located primarily in the United States, with additional personnel supporting sales, customer success, and research and development internationally. We compete for technical talent, including software engineers and data scientists, in a competitive labor market, and we focus on compensation, equity-based incentives, and career development to attract and retain employees.

Competition

The market for observability and monitoring software is competitive and rapidly evolving. We compete with other dedicated observability vendors, with the monitoring offerings of major cloud infrastructure providers, and with legacy on-premise monitoring tools. We believe the principal competitive factors in our market are breadth of platform capability, ease of deployment, and the ability to scale with a customer's infrastructure. During fiscal 2025, we experienced reduced usage from one customer that migrated a portion of its workloads to a competing vendor, illustrating the competitive dynamics of our market.

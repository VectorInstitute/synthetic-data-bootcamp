Program

We maintain a cybersecurity risk management program designed to identify, assess, and manage risks from cybersecurity threats to our platform, our corporate systems, and the customer data we process. Our program is informed by the NIST Cybersecurity Framework and ISO/IEC 27001, and we periodically assess our practices against those frameworks.

Our program includes continuous monitoring of our production environment, regular vulnerability scanning and penetration testing, employee security-awareness training, and a formal incident response plan that establishes procedures for identifying, containing, and remediating cybersecurity incidents. Because our platform is built on and integrates with third-party cloud infrastructure and software, our vendor management process includes an assessment of those providers' security practices.

Risks

We have not experienced a material cybersecurity incident to date. However, as a company whose platform ingests substantial amounts of customer infrastructure and application data, we are a target of cybersecurity threats, and a future incident could disrupt our service, compromise customer data, and result in remediation costs, litigation, or reputational harm. See Item 1A, "Risk Factors," for additional discussion of cybersecurity-related risks.

Governance

The Audit Committee of our Board of Directors is responsible for overseeing our cybersecurity risk management program. Management provides the Audit Committee with regular updates on our cybersecurity program, including the results of risk assessments and the threat landscape we face, and the Audit Committee reports to the full Board on matters it believes warrant the Board's attention.

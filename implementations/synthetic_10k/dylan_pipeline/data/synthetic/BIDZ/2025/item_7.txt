Overview

Clearwater Technologies Group, Inc. is an integrated health care company operating pharmacy services, health care benefits, and health services businesses. For the fiscal year ended in 2025, we generated net revenue of $417,964.5 million, operating income of $4,482.6 million (an operating margin of 1.1%), and net income of $1,840.9 million (a net margin of 0.4%). These margins reflect continued reimbursement-rate reductions from government programs and commercial payors and represent the thinnest results in our reporting history, a level we do not consider sustainable without corrective action.

Results of Operations

The following table summarizes our results of operations for the fiscal year ended in 2025 (in millions, except percentages; amounts may not sum due to rounding):

Net revenue | $417,964.5 | 100.0%
Cost of revenue | 206,149.0 | 49.3%
Other operating expenses | 207,332.9 | 49.6%
Operating income | 4,482.6 | 1.1%
Income tax expense | 2,641.7 | 0.6%
Net income | 1,840.9 | 0.4%

Cost of revenue represented approximately 49.3% of net revenue, and other operating expenses, which include selling, general and administrative costs across our pharmacy, benefits, and health services operations, represented approximately 49.6% of net revenue, together reflecting the scale and relatively thin unit economics typical of our integrated pharmacy and health benefits businesses. Our effective tax rate for the year was 58.9%, which is high relative to the 21% U.S. federal statutory rate primarily because non-deductible items and other permanent differences represent a much larger proportion of a thin pretax income base than they would of a larger one.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

We generated $12,212.6 million of cash from operating activities during fiscal 2025, reflecting net income of $1,840.9 million, non-cash depreciation and amortization of $5,444.9 million, and a favorable change in working capital and other items of $4,926.8 million. We used $6,293.1 million of cash in investing activities, including capital expenditures of $2,799.7 million. Financing activities used $5,608.5 million of cash, including $3,099.9 million of dividends paid to shareholders. Taken together, our cash and cash equivalents increased by $311.1 million during the year.

As of fiscal year end, we had stockholders' equity of $74,846.1 million, equal to approximately 31.4% of total assets, and no outstanding short-term or long-term debt reported separately from our other liabilities. We believe our cash flow from operations will be sufficient to fund our operating needs, capital expenditures, and dividends for at least the next twelve months.

Critical Accounting Estimates

Goodwill and Intangible Assets. We test goodwill and indefinite-lived intangible assets for impairment at least annually. Goodwill and acquired intangible assets together represented approximately 45.6% of our total assets as of fiscal year end, reflecting the acquisitive history of our pharmacy, benefits, and health services businesses; a deterioration in the performance or outlook of one or more of our reporting units could result in a material impairment charge.

Reserves for Health Care Costs and Rebates. We make significant estimates regarding reserves for health care benefit claims incurred but not yet paid, and for rebates owed to or receivable from manufacturers and payors, based on historical experience and actuarial assumptions. Actual results could differ from these estimates.

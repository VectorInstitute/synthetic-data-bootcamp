Cybersecurity Program

We maintain a cybersecurity program informed by the NIST Cybersecurity Framework that is designed to identify, assess, and manage risks from cybersecurity threats to our generation, transmission, and distribution operations and to the information systems that support them. Our program includes continuous monitoring of our information technology and operational technology environments, recurring risk assessments, vulnerability scanning and penetration testing, employee security-awareness training, and a formal incident response plan that establishes procedures for identifying, escalating, containing, and remediating cybersecurity incidents.

Given the critical nature of electric infrastructure, we also participate in industry information-sharing efforts and coordinate with third-party vendors and service providers on security practices as part of our vendor management process. We maintain cybersecurity insurance that may offset a portion of the costs associated with certain incidents.

Cybersecurity Risks

We have not experienced a material cybersecurity incident to date. However, as an electric utility, our generation, transmission, and distribution systems are an attractive target for cybersecurity threats, and a future incident affecting our systems or those of our third-party service providers could disrupt service, compromise sensitive data, and result in remediation costs, regulatory scrutiny, or reputational harm. See Item 1A, "Risk Factors," for additional discussion of cybersecurity-related risks.

Governance

Our full Board of Directors, with support from the Audit Committee, oversees our cybersecurity risk management program. Management provides the Board and the Audit Committee with periodic updates on our cybersecurity program, including the results of risk assessments, the status of our security initiatives, and the threat landscape we face. We believe board-level engagement on cybersecurity reflects the importance we place on protecting the infrastructure our customers depend on.

Introduction

Westgate Technologies Co. ("Westgate," "we," "our," or "us") is a vertically integrated electric utility headquartered in Ohio. We generate, transmit, and distribute electricity to retail and wholesale customers within our regulated service territory. Our rates and the terms of our service are subject to regulation by state and federal regulatory authorities, and our business is organized to support reliable, long-term delivery of electricity under that regulatory framework.

For the fiscal year ended 2023, we generated net revenues of $23,346.8 million. The substantial majority of our total assets, approximately 96.9%, consists of electric utility plant and other long-term regulatory and infrastructure assets associated with our generation, transmission, and distribution operations, reflecting the capital-intensive nature of the utility business.

Regulation and Rates

Our retail rates, terms of service, and the construction of significant new generation, transmission, and distribution facilities are subject to approval by state utility regulators, and certain aspects of our transmission operations and wholesale power sales are subject to federal regulation. Our ability to recover our costs of service and earn a reasonable return on our invested capital depends on the outcome of periodic rate proceedings before these regulators.

Generation and Delivery

We maintain a diversified generation portfolio and an extensive network of transmission and distribution infrastructure designed to deliver electricity reliably to our customers. We continue to invest in modernizing our generation fleet and our transmission and distribution systems to support reliability, resiliency, and the evolving needs of our service territory. During fiscal 2023, this included incremental storm restoration and system-hardening work following significant weather events affecting our service territory.

Human Capital

Our workforce includes employees engaged in generation, transmission, distribution, customer service, and corporate support functions. We focus on safety, training, and competitive compensation and benefits to attract and retain the skilled workforce our operations require.

Competition

While our regulated distribution business operates largely as a monopoly within our service territory, we face competition in wholesale power markets and, in some areas, from distributed generation and energy efficiency alternatives that can affect demand for the electricity we sell.

Introduction

Lakeshore Solutions Corp. ("Lakeshore," "we," "our," or "us") is an aerospace and defense company headquartered in Rhode Island. We design, manufacture, and support aircraft, aircraft systems, and defense products for government and commercial customers, including the U.S. government and international defense and commercial aviation customers. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2023, we generated revenue of $17,211.6 million.

Our Business

We operate across aerospace and defense product lines that include fixed-wing and rotary aircraft, aircraft and vehicle systems, and related aftermarket support and services. A substantial portion of our revenue is derived from contracts with the U.S. government and its agencies, supplemented by sales to commercial aviation and international defense customers.

Government Contracts

Our government contracts are generally subject to standard terms allowing for termination for convenience, audit, and renegotiation, and our backlog depends on future government budget authorizations and appropriations. We believe our long-standing program relationships and technical capabilities position us well within our markets, though government contracting involves risks discussed further in Item 1A, "Risk Factors."

Human Capital Resources

We employ a workforce of engineering, manufacturing, and program-management personnel, a portion of which require government security clearances to perform contract work. We invest in technical training and development to support the specialized skills our programs require, and we believe our relationships with our employees are good.

Competition

The aerospace and defense industry is highly competitive and is characterized by a small number of large prime contractors and a broader base of specialized suppliers. We compete on the basis of technical capability, program performance, cost, and past performance on similar programs.

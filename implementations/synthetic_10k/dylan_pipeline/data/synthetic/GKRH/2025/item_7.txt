Overview

Cedarfield Materials Corp. is a diversified bank offering consumer and commercial banking, lending, payments, and wealth management services. For the fiscal year ended 2025, we generated net revenue of $25,195.1 million, pretax income of $8,589.8 million, and net income of $6,786.0 million (a net margin of 26.9%).

Results of Operations

The following table summarizes our results of operations for the fiscal year ended 2025 (in millions, except percentages; amounts may not sum due to rounding):

Net revenue | $25,195.1 | 100.0%
Other operating expenses | 16,605.2 | 65.9%
Pretax income | 8,589.8 | 34.1%
Income tax expense | 1,803.9 | 7.2%
Net income | 6,786.0 | 26.9%

As a diversified bank, the substantial majority of both our assets and our operating costs are not broken into the same cost-of-revenue and selling, general and administrative categories an industrial company reports; our net revenue reflects net interest income and fee-based income across our consumer, commercial, payments, and wealth management businesses, net of the credit, funding, and operating costs associated with generating that revenue. Other operating expenses for fiscal 2025 also reflect increased compliance-related costs associated with a consent order we entered into with a federal banking regulator during the year, requiring enhancements to our risk management and compliance programs. Our effective tax rate for the year was 21.0%, consistent with the U.S. federal statutory rate.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

We generated $7,765.9 million of cash from operating activities during fiscal 2025, reflecting net income of $6,786.0 million and changes in working capital and other items of $979.9 million. We used $15,411.1 million of cash in investing activities, primarily reflecting growth in our loan and investment securities portfolios. Financing activities provided $2,109.3 million of cash, as deposit and other funding growth, reflected in other financing activities of $2,600.0 million, more than offset $490.7 million used for share repurchases. Taken together, our cash and cash equivalents decreased by $5,536.0 million during the year, consistent with the deployment of funding growth into loans and investment securities.

As of fiscal year end 2025, we had stockholders' equity of $41,789.9 million, representing approximately 8.3% of total assets, with the remaining 91.7% of our balance sheet representing deposits and other interest-bearing liabilities consistent with our deposit-funded banking model. We believe our capital and liquidity position is sufficient to support our lending and funding needs for at least the next twelve months.

Critical Accounting Estimates

Allowance for Credit Losses. We maintain an allowance for credit losses on our loan portfolio based on historical loss experience, current conditions, and reasonable and supportable forecasts of future economic conditions. This estimate involves significant judgment, and changes in economic conditions or borrower performance could require us to increase or decrease our allowance in future periods.

Fair Value of Financial Instruments. A portion of our investment securities and other financial instruments are carried at fair value based on quoted market prices or valuation models that incorporate observable and unobservable inputs. Changes in market conditions or the assumptions underlying these models could affect the fair value of these instruments.

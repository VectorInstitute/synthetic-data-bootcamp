Introduction

Northbridge Technologies Corp. ("Northbridge," "we," "our," or "us") is an integrated health care company headquartered in Rhode Island. We combine pharmacy services, health benefits administration, and care delivery offerings to serve individual, employer, and government-program customers. Our business spans retail and specialty pharmacy operations, pharmacy benefit management, and health plan administration, with the goal of coordinating care and cost management across the points at which consumers interact with the health care system.

For the fiscal year ended in 2024, we generated net revenue of $414,916.9 million. Our balance sheet reflects a history of acquisitions across our pharmacy, benefits, and care delivery businesses: goodwill and acquired intangible assets together represented approximately 43.0% of our total assets as of fiscal year end.

Segments

We organize our operations around pharmacy services, health care benefits, and health services. Our pharmacy services operations include retail pharmacy locations and specialty and mail-order pharmacy fulfillment. Our health care benefits operations provide health insurance and related administrative services to individual, employer, and government-program members. Our health services operations provide primary care, diagnostic, and other care-delivery services designed to work alongside our pharmacy and benefits operations.

Competition

The health care industry is highly competitive and includes retail and specialty pharmacies, pharmacy benefit managers, health insurers, and care-delivery organizations, some of which compete with only one part of our integrated model and others of which compete across it. We believe the principal competitive factors in our industry are network breadth, service quality, cost management, and the ability to coordinate care across pharmacy, benefits, and clinical settings.

Regulation

Our businesses are subject to extensive regulation at the federal and state level, including regulation of pharmacy operations, health insurance, and health care services. Changes in health care laws and regulations, including those governing drug pricing, pharmacy benefit management practices, and government health care programs, could affect how we operate and the economics of our business. During fiscal 2024, recently enacted state and federal measures affecting pharmacy benefit management practices and prescription drug pricing took effect across several of the markets in which we operate, affecting our margins for the year.

Human Capital

We employ a large workforce across our pharmacy, benefits, and care-delivery operations, including pharmacists, clinicians, customer service, and corporate support personnel. We focus on competitive compensation and benefits, training, and career-development programs to attract and retain health care and service talent across our integrated operations.

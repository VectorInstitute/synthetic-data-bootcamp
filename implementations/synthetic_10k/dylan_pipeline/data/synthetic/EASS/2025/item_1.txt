Introduction

Cedarfield Systems Holdings, Inc. ("Cedarfield," "we," "our," or "us") is a manufacturer of metal, glass, and plastic packaging and containers, headquartered in Colorado. We supply packaging products primarily to beverage, food, and other consumer-products end markets, operating a network of manufacturing facilities that produce containers for our customers' filling and distribution operations.

For the fiscal year ended in 2025, we generated net revenue of $16,028.3 million. Our balance sheet reflects a history of acquisitions within the packaging industry: goodwill and acquired intangible assets together represented approximately 28.1% of our total assets as of fiscal year end.

Products and Operations

We manufacture containers and packaging products designed to customer and industry specifications, supplying beverage, food, and other consumer-products companies that fill and distribute our containers under their own brands. Our manufacturing operations are capital-intensive and require ongoing investment in equipment and facilities to maintain production capacity and efficiency.

Recent Acquisition

During fiscal 2025, we completed the acquisition of Thornridge Packaging Corp., a packaging manufacturer, to expand our manufacturing capacity and customer relationships in adjacent packaging end markets. Thornridge is substantially integrated into our existing operations as of fiscal year end.

Raw Materials

Our manufacturing operations depend on aluminum, steel, glass, and plastic resin as primary raw material inputs, the costs of which can be volatile. We generally seek to manage raw material cost exposure through supplier relationships and, where available, pass-through pricing arrangements with customers, though we remain exposed to the timing and extent of raw material cost changes.

Competition

The packaging industry is competitive, and we compete with other metal, glass, and plastic container manufacturers, as well as with alternative packaging formats. We believe the principal competitive factors in our industry are product quality and consistency, manufacturing efficiency, customer service, and price.

Human Capital

We employ a workforce across our manufacturing facilities and corporate support functions. We focus on workplace safety, competitive compensation and benefits, and training programs to support our manufacturing workforce.

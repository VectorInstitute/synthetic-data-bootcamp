Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements

Note 1. Goodwill and Intangible Assets
Goodwill and acquired intangible assets totaled $5,489.2 million and $1,134.9 million, respectively, as of fiscal year end, together representing approximately 28.1% of total assets. We test these assets for impairment at least annually; no impairment was recognized during fiscal 2025.

Note 2. Inventory
Inventory, stated at the lower of cost or market, totaled $2,263.2 million as of fiscal year end, representing approximately 9.6% of total assets.

Note 3. Income Taxes
Our effective tax rate for fiscal 2025 was 21.0%, consistent with the U.S. federal statutory rate.

Note 4. Commitments and Contingencies
We are subject to various claims and legal proceedings arising in the ordinary course of business. We do not believe the resolution of these matters, individually or in the aggregate, will have a material adverse effect on our consolidated financial position or results of operations.

Note 5. Business Combination
During fiscal 2025, we acquired Thornridge Packaging Corp. for approximately $249.7 million in cash, reflected within investing activities. The purchase price is reflected within the goodwill and intangible assets balances on our consolidated balance sheet. Thornridge is substantially integrated into our existing operations as of fiscal year end.

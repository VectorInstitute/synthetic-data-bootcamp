Our Business

Amberwood Technologies Corp. ("Amberwood," "we," "our," or "us") is a diversified technology company headquartered in Florida. We design, develop, and support vertical software and technology-enabled instruments for a range of defensible niche end markets, including healthcare, education, government, and industrial end markets. Our strategy centers on acquiring and operating businesses that combine high recurring revenue, strong incremental margins, and durable competitive positions, then supporting those businesses with capital and operating discipline rather than integrating them into a single product line.

We are organized around three reportable segments:

Software Solutions - cloud-based and on-premise software used by customers in healthcare, education, government, and professional-services end markets to manage scheduling, compliance, billing, and case or record management.

Network Solutions - software-enabled marketplaces and data platforms that connect buyers and sellers of transportation capacity, insurance distribution, and supply chain services.

Technology-Enabled Instruments - precision measurement, diagnostic, and monitoring instruments sold into healthcare, industrial, and research end markets.

For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, we generated net revenues of $5,825.6 million. Substantially all of our growth over time has come from acquiring complementary software and instrumentation businesses and from organic expansion within our existing niche markets; goodwill and acquired intangible assets together represented approximately 88.7% of our total assets at year end, reflecting the acquisitive nature of our growth strategy.

Recent Acquisition

During fiscal 2024, we completed the acquisition of Dunmore Technologies, Inc., a technology-enabled instruments business, to add a durable, high-margin niche instrumentation business to our portfolio. Consistent with our decentralized operating model, Dunmore is operated as a standalone business.

Competition

We compete with other providers of specialized software and instrumentation, ranging from small private companies focused on a single niche to larger diversified technology companies. We believe the principal competitive factors in our markets are the depth of domain expertise embedded in our products, the cost of switching away from an incumbent system once it is integrated into a customer's workflow, and the breadth of our service and support offering.

Human Capital

We employ a workforce distributed across our operating businesses, each of which generally manages its own hiring, compensation, and talent development within company-wide policies on compensation philosophy, equal employment opportunity, and workplace safety. We believe our decentralized operating model allows each business to compete effectively for specialized technical and engineering talent in its own niche market.

Available Information

Amberwood Technologies Corp. is a Delaware corporation. Our principal executive offices are located in Florida.

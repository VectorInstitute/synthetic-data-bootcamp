Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements

Note 1. Goodwill and Intangible Assets
Goodwill and acquired intangible assets totaled $13,196.9 million and $7,227.3 million, respectively, as of December 31, 2024, together representing approximately 88.7% of total assets. We test goodwill and indefinite-lived intangible assets for impairment at least annually; no impairment was recognized during fiscal 2024.

Note 2. Debt
Total debt outstanding as of December 31, 2024 consisted of $749.1 million of short-term debt and $5,500.5 million of long-term debt.

Note 3. Income Taxes
Our effective tax rate for fiscal 2024 was 9.7%, compared with the 21% U.S. federal statutory rate. The difference primarily reflects the jurisdictional mix of our earnings and the benefit of research and development tax credits.

Note 4. Commitments and Contingencies
We are subject to various claims and legal proceedings arising in the ordinary course of business. We do not believe the resolution of these matters, individually or in the aggregate, will have a material adverse effect on our consolidated financial position or results of operations.

Note 5. Business Combination
During fiscal 2024, we acquired Dunmore Technologies, Inc. for approximately $2,943.0 million in cash, reflected within investing activities. The purchase price is reflected within the goodwill and intangible assets balances on our consolidated balance sheet. Dunmore is operated as a standalone business within our existing decentralized operating model.

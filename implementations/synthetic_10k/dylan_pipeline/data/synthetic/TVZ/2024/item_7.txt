Overview

Meridian Innovations Inc. is a retailer and distributor of automotive replacement parts and accessories. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, we generated net sales of $16,486.4 million, operating income of $3,114.1 million (an operating margin of 18.9%), and net income of $2,592.4 million (a net margin of 15.7%).

Our gross margin of 51.5% reflects our merchandise mix and ongoing efforts to manage product cost and pricing. Selling, general and administrative expenses, which include store payroll, occupancy, and advertising costs, represented 37.4% of net sales. Our effective tax rate for the year was 16.8%, below the 21% U.S. federal statutory rate, reflecting discrete tax items in the period.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

We generated $2,517.3 million of cash from operating activities during 2024, reflecting net income of $2,592.4 million and depreciation and amortization of $512.8 million, partly offset by changes in working capital. We used $1,203.3 million of cash in investing activities, primarily capital expenditures of $815.2 million related to new store openings, store remodels, and distribution center capacity, partly offset by other investing outflows. Financing activities used $1,698.9 million of cash, primarily reflecting $3,066.9 million used for share repurchases, partly offset by other financing inflows. We did not pay dividends during 2024. Taken together, our cash and cash equivalents decreased by $384.9 million during the year.

As of December 31, 2024, we had stockholders' equity of $267.4 million, the result of a sustained, debt-financed share repurchase program rather than a build-up of retained earnings. Management regards this capital allocation approach as a deliberate strategy reflecting confidence in the business's cash-generating capacity, while recognizing that it correspondingly narrows our equity cushion against a sustained downturn in operating performance. We believe our cash flow from operations, together with access to the credit and capital markets, will be sufficient to fund our operating needs, capital expenditures, and share repurchase program for at least the next twelve months.

Critical Accounting Estimates

Inventory. Inventory, which represented approximately 32.8% of our total assets as of December 31, 2024, is stated at the lower of cost or market. We make estimates regarding shrinkage and excess or obsolete inventory based on historical experience and current market conditions.

Self-Insurance Reserves. We are self-insured for a portion of our exposure to general liability, workers' compensation, and vehicle liability claims. We estimate our self-insurance reserves based on historical claims experience and actuarial assumptions, which involve significant judgment.

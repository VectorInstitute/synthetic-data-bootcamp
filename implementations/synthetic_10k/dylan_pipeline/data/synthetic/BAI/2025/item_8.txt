Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements

Note 1. Debt
Total debt outstanding as of fiscal year end 2025 consisted of $2,938.0 million of short-term debt and $43,082.5 million of long-term debt, consistent with our capital-intensive generation, transmission, and distribution operations.

Note 2. Income Taxes
Our effective tax rate for fiscal 2025 was 31.2%, compared with the 21% U.S. federal statutory rate. The difference primarily reflects state income taxes and other permanent differences between book and taxable income.

Note 3. Regulatory Matters
Our rates, terms of service, and significant capital projects are subject to review and approval by state and federal regulatory authorities. We record regulatory assets and liabilities for certain costs and revenues expected to be recovered from or refunded to customers in future periods in accordance with applicable rate-regulated accounting guidance. During fiscal 2025, state regulators approved a rate increase for us that was below the full amount requested, which limited recovery of certain operating and capital costs during the year.

Note 4. Commitments and Contingencies
We are subject to various claims and legal proceedings arising in the ordinary course of business. We do not believe the resolution of these matters, individually or in the aggregate, will have a material adverse effect on our consolidated financial position or results of operations.

Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements

Note 1. Goodwill and Intangible Assets
Goodwill and acquired intangible assets totaled $5,822.2 million and $4,961.2 million, respectively, as of fiscal year end 2024, together representing approximately 32.8% of total assets. We test goodwill and indefinite-lived intangible assets for impairment at least annually; no impairment was recognized during fiscal 2024.

Note 2. Debt
Long-term debt outstanding totaled $2,619.7 million as of fiscal year end 2024.

Note 3. Income Taxes
We recognized a net income tax benefit of $252.3 million during fiscal 2024, primarily reflecting the resolution of discrete tax items during the year, which resulted in net income exceeding pretax income for the period.

Note 4. Commitments and Contingencies
We are subject to various claims, legal proceedings, and regulatory matters arising in the ordinary course of business. We do not believe the resolution of these matters, individually or in the aggregate, will have a material adverse effect on our consolidated financial position or results of operations.

Note 5. Business Combination
During fiscal 2024, we acquired Carrowgate Capital Partners, Inc. for approximately $2,211.2 million in cash, reflected within investing activities. The purchase price is reflected within the goodwill and intangible assets balances on our consolidated balance sheet. Carrowgate is being integrated into our existing investment management and distribution operations.

Overview

Fieldstone Partners Holdings, Inc. provides a cloud-delivered cybersecurity platform. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025, we generated revenue of $4,636.5 million and recorded an operating loss of $124.8 million, an operating margin of negative 2.7%. We recorded a net loss of $15.6 million, a net margin of negative 0.3%, after recognizing a net income tax benefit during the year. Our operating and net losses for the year reflect ongoing investment in research and development and sales and marketing ahead of sustained profitability, consistent with our stage of growth as a subscription-based cybersecurity platform, rather than a deterioration in our underlying business.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

We generated $1,660.5 million of cash from operating activities during fiscal 2025, reflecting a net loss of $15.6 million more than offset by a favorable change in working capital of $1,676.1 million, consistent with our subscription-based revenue model under which customers are frequently billed in advance. We used $562.8 million of cash in investing activities, including capital expenditures of $328.9 million. Financing activities provided $111.7 million of cash. Taken together, our cash and cash equivalents increased by $1,209.3 million during the year.

As of December 31, 2025, we had stockholders' equity of $3,894.6 million and long-term debt of $743.0 million. Cash and cash equivalents represented approximately 46.4% of our total assets at year end. We believe our existing cash, together with cash generated from operations, will be sufficient to fund our operating needs for at least the next twelve months.

Critical Accounting Estimates

Revenue Recognition. We recognize revenue from subscription arrangements over the contract term. Determining the amount and timing of revenue to recognize requires judgment regarding the identification of performance obligations and the allocation of the transaction price among them.

Goodwill and Intangible Assets. Goodwill and intangible assets, net, together represented approximately 12.3% of our total assets as of December 31, 2025. We test goodwill for impairment at least annually.

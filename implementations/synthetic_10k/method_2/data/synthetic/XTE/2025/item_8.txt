Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements

Note 1. Goodwill and Intangible Assets
Goodwill and acquired intangible assets totaled $27,112.7 million and $263.7 million, respectively, as of December 31, 2025, together representing approximately 38.4% of total assets. We test goodwill for impairment at least annually; no impairment was recognized during fiscal 2025.

Note 2. Debt
Total debt outstanding as of December 31, 2025 consisted of $653.2 million of short-term debt and $22,824.8 million of long-term debt.

Note 3. Income Taxes
Our effective tax rate for fiscal 2025 was 4.2%, below the 21% U.S. federal statutory rate, primarily reflecting research and development tax credits and other tax benefits.

Note 4. Commitments and Contingencies
We are subject to various claims, legal proceedings, and government contract audits arising in the ordinary course of business. We do not believe the resolution of these matters, individually or in the aggregate, will have a material adverse effect on our consolidated financial position or results of operations.

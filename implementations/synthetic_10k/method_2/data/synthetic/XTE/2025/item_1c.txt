Cybersecurity Program

We maintain a cybersecurity program informed by ISO/IEC 27001 that is designed to identify, assess, and manage risks from cybersecurity threats to our information systems, including information related to our government and defense programs.

Our program includes continuous monitoring of our information technology environment, periodic risk assessments, vulnerability scanning and penetration testing, employee security-awareness training, and a formal incident response plan that establishes procedures for identifying, escalating, containing, and remediating cybersecurity incidents. We also assess the cybersecurity practices of third-party suppliers and service providers that have access to our systems or data.

Cybersecurity Risks

We have not experienced a material cybersecurity incident to date. Given the nature of our business, we are a target of persistent cybersecurity threats, and a future incident affecting our systems or those of our suppliers could disrupt our operations, compromise sensitive program information, and result in remediation costs, litigation, or reputational harm. See Item 1A, "Risk Factors," for additional discussion of cybersecurity-related risks.

Governance

Our full Board of Directors, with support from the Audit Committee, oversees our cybersecurity risk management program. Management provides the Board and the Audit Committee with periodic updates on our cybersecurity program, including the results of risk assessments and the status of our security initiatives.

Risks Related to Government Contracting

A substantial portion of our revenue depends on U.S. government budgets and appropriations. Reductions in defense spending, delays in the budget or appropriations process, or shifts in defense priorities could reduce demand for our products or delay or terminate programs on which we rely.

Our government contracts can generally be terminated for the government's convenience and are subject to audit and renegotiation. A termination for convenience, an unfavorable audit finding, or a failure to perform on a key program could adversely affect our revenue, profitability, and reputation.

Risks Related to Acquisitions and Goodwill

We have grown in part through acquisitions, and goodwill represented approximately 38.0% of our total assets as of December 31, 2025. A significant impairment of our goodwill, resulting from a deterioration in the performance or outlook of an acquired business, could adversely affect our results of operations.

Risks Related to Our Operations

Our programs often involve complex engineering, manufacturing, and supply chain execution over multi-year periods. Design, manufacturing, or supply chain difficulties could result in cost overruns, schedule delays, or contract penalties.

Risks Related to Competition

The aerospace and defense industry is highly competitive, with a small number of large prime contractors and a broader base of specialized suppliers. Our inability to win new programs or retain existing ones could adversely affect our revenue and growth.

Risks Related to Our Financial Position

We had approximately $23,478.0 million of short-term and long-term debt outstanding as of December 31, 2025. Our level of indebtedness could limit our financial flexibility and increase our vulnerability to adverse changes in interest rates.

Risks Related to Cybersecurity and Information Technology

Our business, including information related to government and defense programs, depends on the security of our information technology systems. As described in Item 1C, "Cybersecurity," while we have not experienced a material cybersecurity incident to date, a future incident could disrupt our operations, compromise sensitive program information, and result in remediation costs, contract impacts, litigation, or reputational harm.

General Risk Factors

Our quarterly and annual operating results may fluctuate based on the timing of program milestones, deliveries, and government funding actions.

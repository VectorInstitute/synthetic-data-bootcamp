Risk Management and Strategy

We have implemented a cybersecurity risk management program designed to identify, assess, and manage material risks from cybersecurity threats to our information systems and the data we process, including customer account, transaction, and personal information. Our program is informed by the NIST Cybersecurity Framework, and we periodically assess our practices against that framework using a combination of internal review and independent outside expertise.

Our program includes continuous monitoring of our information technology environment, vulnerability scanning and penetration testing, employee security-awareness training, and a formal incident response plan that sets out procedures for investigating, containing, and remediating cybersecurity incidents. Because our banking, payments, and wealth management systems depend in part on third-party service providers, our vendor management process includes an assessment of those providers' security practices before and during the engagement.

Cybersecurity Risks

We have not experienced a material cybersecurity incident to date. However, like other financial institutions, we are a frequent target of cybersecurity threats, and a future incident affecting our systems or those of our third-party service providers could disrupt our operations, compromise sensitive customer data, and result in remediation costs, litigation, or reputational harm. See Item 1A, "Risk Factors," for additional discussion of cybersecurity-related risks.

Governance

The Audit Committee of the Board of Directors is responsible for overseeing our cybersecurity risk management program. Management provides the Audit Committee with regular updates on our cybersecurity program, including the results of risk assessments, the status of our security initiatives, and the threat landscape we face. The Audit Committee in turn reports to the full Board on cybersecurity matters it believes warrant the Board's attention.

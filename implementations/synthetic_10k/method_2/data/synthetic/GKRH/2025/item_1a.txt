Risks Related to Our Business

Our profitability depends on our ability to manage credit risk across our loan and lending portfolios. Deterioration in the credit quality of our borrowers, whether due to adverse economic conditions, concentration in particular industries or geographies, or other factors, could increase our provision for credit losses and adversely affect our results of operations.

Changes in interest rates could adversely affect our net interest income and the value of our investment securities. As a diversified bank, our profitability depends significantly on the difference between the interest we earn on loans and investment securities and the interest we pay on deposits and other borrowings. Changes in the level or shape of the yield curve could compress this difference and reduce our net interest income.

Risks Related to Regulation

We are subject to extensive regulation, and changes in banking laws, regulations, or regulatory expectations could increase our compliance costs or restrict our activities. Our regulators have broad discretion over the activities in which we may engage, the capital and liquidity levels we must maintain, and the manner in which we conduct our consumer and commercial banking operations. Changes in these requirements could increase our costs or limit our ability to pursue certain business opportunities.

We are subject to a regulatory consent order that requires remediation of identified deficiencies. During fiscal 2025, we entered into a consent order with a federal banking regulator requiring enhancements to our risk management and compliance programs. If we are unable to remediate the matters addressed by the consent order within the timeframes required, or if our regulator determines our remediation efforts are insufficient, we could be subject to additional regulatory action, including monetary penalties or further restrictions on our activities, and we will continue to incur compliance-related costs implementing the required enhancements.

We are required to maintain minimum regulatory capital and liquidity levels, and our stockholders' equity represented approximately 8.3% of our total assets as of fiscal year end 2025. A reduction in our capital levels, whether due to losses, growth in our balance sheet, or changes in regulatory capital requirements, could limit our ability to pay dividends, repurchase shares, or grow our business.

Risks Related to Our Financial Position

Our liabilities represented approximately 91.7% of our total assets as of fiscal year end 2025, consistent with a deposit-funded banking model. Our ability to fund our operations depends on our continued access to customer deposits and other funding sources on acceptable terms; a loss of depositor or counterparty confidence could require us to seek more expensive funding alternatives or sell assets at unfavorable prices.

Risks Related to Cybersecurity and Information Technology

Our business depends on the security and reliability of the information technology systems that support our banking, payments, and wealth management operations. As described in Item 1C, "Cybersecurity," while we have not experienced a material cybersecurity incident to date, a future incident affecting our systems, our customers' data, or those of our third-party service providers could disrupt our operations, result in financial losses, and harm our reputation.

General Risk Factors

Our results of operations may be affected by general economic conditions, including changes in unemployment, consumer spending, and business investment, which influence loan demand, deposit levels, and credit quality across our markets.

Introduction

Cedarfield Materials Corp. ("Cedarfield," "we," "our," or "us") is a diversified bank headquartered in Minnesota. We provide consumer and commercial banking, lending, payments, and wealth management services to individuals, businesses, and institutional clients. For the fiscal year ended 2025, we generated net revenue of $25,195.1 million. Our balance sheet consists predominantly of loans, investment securities, and other interest-earning assets funded by customer deposits and other interest-bearing liabilities, consistent with a traditional diversified banking model.

Our Business

We organize our operations around consumer and business banking, commercial banking, wealth management, and payment services. Our consumer and business banking operations serve individuals and small businesses through retail deposit, lending, and payment products. Our commercial banking operations provide lending, treasury management, and capital markets services to middle-market and larger corporate clients. Our wealth management operations provide investment management, trust, and fiduciary services to individual and institutional clients. Our payment services operations provide merchant processing, card issuing, and related payment products to businesses and consumers.

Regulation

As a diversified bank, we are subject to extensive regulation and supervision by federal and state banking regulators, which govern, among other things, our capital levels, permissible activities, consumer protection obligations, and risk management practices. Our regulators periodically examine our operations and may require us to maintain capital and liquidity levels above regulatory minimums based on their assessment of our risk profile. During fiscal 2025, we entered into a consent order with a federal banking regulator requiring us to enhance certain risk management and compliance programs; we are implementing the required enhancements.

Human Capital

Our workforce supports our banking, lending, payments, and wealth management operations across retail branches, operations centers, and corporate support functions. We compete for banking, technology, and risk management talent and seek to offer competitive compensation and benefits, along with training and development programs designed to support career growth within the organization. We do not have any collective bargaining agreements in the United States and believe our relationships with our employees are good.

Competition

The banking and financial services industry is highly competitive. We compete with other diversified banks, regional and community banks, credit unions, and non-bank financial technology providers for deposits, loans, and payment and wealth management customers. We believe the principal competitive factors in our industry are pricing, product breadth, customer service, and the convenience of our branch and digital banking channels.

Risks Related to Regulation

Our rates and the terms of our service are subject to extensive regulation, and adverse regulatory outcomes could harm our results of operations. Our ability to recover our costs of service and earn a reasonable return on invested capital depends on rate decisions by state and federal regulators. An unfavorable outcome in a rate proceeding, or delay in cost recovery, could adversely affect our revenues and cash flows.

Changes in environmental and energy policy could require significant capital expenditures or otherwise increase our costs. Our generation, transmission, and distribution operations are subject to environmental laws and regulations that may become more stringent over time, which could require us to make additional capital investments or incur higher operating costs that we may not fully recover through rates.

Risks Related to Our Operations

Our operations are subject to severe weather, natural disasters, and other events that could disrupt service and increase costs. Damage to our generation, transmission, or distribution infrastructure from storms or other events could require significant unplanned expenditures and could affect our ability to serve customers reliably. During fiscal 2023, we experienced significant weather events affecting our service territory, which required incremental storm restoration and system-hardening work layered onto our ongoing capital program.

Our generation, transmission, and distribution infrastructure requires significant ongoing capital investment, and we may not recover all of our costs. The substantial majority of our total assets consists of long-lived utility plant and infrastructure assets; the pace and cost of required investment could exceed what we are able to recover in rates on a timely basis.

Risks Related to Our Financial Position

Our level of indebtedness could limit our financial and operating flexibility and increase our exposure to interest rate changes. As of fiscal year end 2023, our total debt was approximately $47,912.5 million and our liabilities represented approximately 71.4% of total assets; we recorded interest expense of $2,160.8 million during the year. This level of indebtedness could increase our vulnerability to adverse changes in interest rates and capital market conditions and could limit our ability to fund future capital expenditures.

Risks Related to Cybersecurity and Information Technology

Our generation, transmission, and distribution operations depend on information technology and operational technology systems that are subject to cybersecurity threats. As described in Item 1C, "Cybersecurity," while we have not experienced a material cybersecurity incident to date, a future incident affecting our systems, including systems that support grid operations, could disrupt service, compromise sensitive data, and result in remediation costs, regulatory scrutiny, or reputational harm.

General Risk Factors

Our results of operations can be affected by weather conditions, economic conditions in our service territory, and other factors outside our control that influence customer demand for electricity, which could cause our revenues and earnings to vary from period to period.

Overview

Stonegate Logistics Holdings, Inc. owns and operates a portfolio of restaurant brands. For the fiscal year ended in 2025, we generated revenue of $12,763.5 million, operating income of $1,340.4 million (an operating margin of 10.5%), and net income of $1,096.7 million (a net margin of 8.6%).

Results of Operations

The following table summarizes our results of operations for the fiscal year ended in 2025 (in millions, except percentages; amounts may not sum due to rounding):

Revenue | $12,763.5 | 100.0%
Other operating expenses, net | 11,423.1 | 89.5%
Operating income | 1,340.4 | 10.5%
Income tax expense | 243.7 | 1.9%
Net income | 1,096.7 | 8.6%

Other operating expenses, net, which include food and beverage costs, restaurant labor, occupancy, and corporate overhead, represented 89.5% of revenue. Our effective tax rate for the year was 18.2%, below the 21% U.S. federal statutory rate.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

We generated $1,714.7 million of cash from operating activities during fiscal 2025, reflecting net income of $1,096.7 million and depreciation and amortization of $617.9 million. We used $1,308.7 million of cash in investing activities, primarily capital expenditures of $758.2 million related to new restaurant development and remodels of existing restaurants, together with other investing outflows of $550.5 million. Financing activities used $458.4 million of cash, reflecting $657.3 million paid in dividends and $462.6 million used for share repurchases, partly offset by other financing inflows of $661.4 million. Taken together, our cash and cash equivalents decreased by $52.5 million during the year.

As of fiscal year end 2025, we had total debt of $2,301.6 million and stockholders' equity of $2,672.3 million, representing 18.6% of total assets, with liabilities representing 81.4% of total assets. We believe our cash flow from operations, together with access to the credit and capital markets, will be sufficient to fund our operating needs, capital expenditures, and return of capital to shareholders for at least the next twelve months.

Critical Accounting Estimates

Goodwill. Goodwill, which represented approximately 14.5% of our total assets as of fiscal year end 2025, arose from past acquisitions of restaurant brands and is tested for impairment at least annually. A significant decline in the estimated fair value of one or more of our brands could result in an impairment charge.

Self-Insurance Reserves. We are self-insured for a portion of our exposure to general liability, workers' compensation, and other claims arising from restaurant operations. We estimate our self-insurance reserves based on historical claims experience and actuarial assumptions, which involve significant judgment.

Risk Management and Strategy

We have implemented a cybersecurity risk management program designed to identify, assess, and manage material risks from cybersecurity threats to our information systems and the data we process, including guest, employee, and vendor information. Our program is informed by the NIST Cybersecurity Framework, and we periodically benchmark our practices against that framework.

Our cybersecurity program includes continuous monitoring of our information technology environment, vulnerability scanning and penetration testing, employee security-awareness training, and a formal incident response plan that sets out procedures for investigating, containing, and remediating cybersecurity incidents. Because our point-of-sale, guest-facing digital ordering, and delivery platforms depend in part on third-party service providers, our vendor management process includes an assessment of those providers' security practices.

Cybersecurity Risks

We have not experienced a material cybersecurity incident to date. However, like other restaurant operators, we are a target of cybersecurity threats, and a future incident affecting our systems or those of our third-party service providers could disrupt restaurant operations, compromise sensitive data, and result in remediation costs, litigation, or reputational harm. See Item 1A, "Risk Factors," for additional discussion of cybersecurity-related risks.

Governance

The Audit Committee of our Board of Directors is responsible for overseeing our cybersecurity risk management program. Management provides the Audit Committee with regular updates on our cybersecurity program, including the results of risk assessments, the status of our security initiatives, and the threat landscape we face.

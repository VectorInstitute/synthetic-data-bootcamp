Introduction

Stonegate Logistics Holdings, Inc. ("Stonegate," "we," "our," or "us") owns and operates a portfolio of restaurant brands, headquartered in Florida. We operate restaurants offering full-service dining across a range of culinary concepts and price points, serving guests through dine-in, takeout, and delivery channels. For the fiscal year ended in 2025, we generated revenue of $12,763.5 million.

Our Brands

We operate multiple restaurant brands, each positioned around a distinct culinary concept and guest experience, supported by shared corporate functions including supply chain, real estate, and brand marketing. We believe operating a portfolio of brands allows us to reach a broader set of guest occasions and price points than a single concept would.

Supply Chain

We depend on a network of suppliers and distributors for food, beverage, and other restaurant supplies. Our scale allows us to negotiate purchasing arrangements intended to provide consistent quality and manage input costs across our brands.

Human Capital Resources

We employ a large workforce of restaurant-level team members, including hourly front-of-house and back-of-house staff, supported by restaurant management and corporate personnel. The substantial majority of our workforce is employed at the restaurant level. We focus on training, development, and competitive pay and benefits to support recruitment and retention in a competitive labor market.

Competition

The restaurant industry is highly competitive, with a large number of national, regional, and local operators across full-service and quick-service formats. We believe the principal competitive factors in our industry are food quality, service, price, and location.

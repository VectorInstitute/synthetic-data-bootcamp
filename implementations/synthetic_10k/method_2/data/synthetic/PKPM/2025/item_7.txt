Overview

Harborview Partners Corp. develops electronic design automation software and related intellectual property for the semiconductor industry. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025, we generated revenue of $4,533.8 million, operating income of $1,103.6 million (an operating margin of 24.3%), and net income of $832.7 million (a net margin of 18.4%).

Results of Operations

The following table summarizes our results of operations for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025 (in millions, except percentages; amounts may not sum due to rounding):

Other operating expenses, net | 3,430.2 | 75.7%
Operating income | 1,103.6 | 24.3%

Our operating margin of 24.3% reflects the high incremental margins typical of licensed software revenue. Interest expense of $109.8 million reduced pretax income to $993.8 million. Our effective tax rate for the year was 16.2%, below the 21% U.S. federal statutory rate, reflecting the jurisdictional mix of our earnings and the benefit of research and development tax credits.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

We generated $1,331.6 million of cash from operating activities during fiscal 2025, reflecting net income of $832.7 million, non-cash depreciation and amortization of $222.5 million, and a favorable change in working capital of $276.5 million. We used $392.5 million of cash in investing activities, including capital expenditures of $137.0 million. Financing activities used $742.5 million of cash, primarily reflecting $874.6 million used for share repurchases. Taken together, our cash and cash equivalents increased by $196.6 million during the year.

As of December 31, 2025, we had stockholders' equity of $5,369.7 million and no outstanding short- or long-term debt. Cash and cash equivalents represented approximately 27.6% of our total assets at year end. We believe our existing cash, together with cash generated from operations, will be sufficient to fund our operating needs and share repurchase program for at least the next twelve months.

Critical Accounting Estimates

Goodwill and Intangible Assets. Goodwill and intangible assets, net, together represented approximately 37.2% of our total assets as of December 31, 2025. We test goodwill for impairment at least annually, which requires judgment regarding future cash flows and discount rates.

Revenue Recognition. A portion of our revenue is derived from multi-element arrangements that include software licenses, intellectual property, and services. Determining the amount and timing of revenue to recognize for these arrangements requires judgment regarding the identification of performance obligations and the allocation of the transaction price among them.

Risks Related to Our Business and Industry

Our business depends on the semiconductor industry, which is cyclical and can be volatile. Demand for our software is tied to our customers' investment in new chip designs, which in turn depends on broader semiconductor industry conditions. A downturn in semiconductor industry spending could reduce demand for our products and adversely affect our revenue.

Our customers' design cycles are long, and our sales cycles can be lengthy and unpredictable. Many of our license arrangements involve substantial negotiation and technical evaluation before a customer commits, and the timing of large renewals or new license agreements can cause our results to vary significantly from period to period.

Risks Related to Competition

We face competition from a small number of established EDA providers as well as point-solution vendors. Some of our competitors have longer operating histories, larger customer bases, or greater financial resources than we do. If we are unable to keep pace with the design requirements of advanced semiconductor process nodes, our competitive position could be harmed.

Risks Related to Our Financial Position

Our indebtedness could limit our operating and financial flexibility. We had interest expense of $109.8 million for fiscal 2025. Our level of indebtedness could limit our ability to obtain additional financing and make us more vulnerable to adverse changes in interest rates.

Risks Related to Cybersecurity and Information Technology

Our products and the intellectual property they help design and verify are highly sensitive, and a cybersecurity incident affecting our systems or those of our customers could harm our business. As described in Item 1C, "Cybersecurity," we have not experienced a material cybersecurity incident to date, but a future incident could compromise our own or our customers' confidential design data, disrupt our operations, and result in remediation costs, litigation, or reputational harm.

General Risk Factors

Our quarterly and annual operating results may fluctuate due to the timing of license renewals, the mix of software, intellectual property, and services revenue, and the timing of our investments in research and development, which could cause our results to vary from period to period.

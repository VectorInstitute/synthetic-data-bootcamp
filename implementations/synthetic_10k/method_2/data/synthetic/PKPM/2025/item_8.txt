Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements

Note 1. Goodwill and Intangible Assets
Goodwill and intangible assets, net, totaled $2,600.8 million and $622.3 million, respectively, as of December 31, 2025, together representing approximately 37.2% of total assets. We test goodwill for impairment at least annually; no impairment was recognized during fiscal 2025.

Note 2. Income Taxes
Our effective tax rate for fiscal 2025 was 16.2%, compared with the 21% U.S. federal statutory rate. The difference primarily reflects the jurisdictional mix of our earnings and the benefit of research and development tax credits.

Note 3. Commitments and Contingencies
We are subject to various claims and legal proceedings arising in the ordinary course of business. We do not believe the resolution of these matters, individually or in the aggregate, will have a material adverse effect on our consolidated financial position or results of operations.

Overview

Meridian Sciences Group, Inc. is a vertically integrated electric utility. For the fiscal year ended 2024, we generated net revenues of $22,156.0 million, operating income of $4,884.3 million (an operating margin of 22.0%), and net income of $3,680.5 million (a net margin of 16.6%).

Results of Operations

The following table summarizes our results of operations for the fiscal year ended 2024 (in millions, except percentages; amounts may not sum due to rounding):

Net revenues | $22,156.0 | 100.0%
Other operating expenses | 17,271.7 | 78.0%
Operating income | 4,884.3 | 22.0%
Income tax expense | 1,203.8 | 5.4%
Net income | 3,680.5 | 16.6%

Other operating expenses, which represented 78.0% of net revenues, consist primarily of the fuel, purchased power, operations and maintenance, and depreciation costs associated with our generation, transmission, and distribution operations. Given the integrated nature of utility cost accounting across these functions, we do not further decompose this amount into additional line items. Our effective tax rate for the year was 24.6%, above the 21% U.S. federal statutory rate, reflecting state income taxes and other permanent differences between book and taxable income.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

We generated $6,932.6 million of cash from operating activities during 2024, reflecting net income of $3,680.5 million, non-cash depreciation and amortization of $3,017.7 million, and a net increase from changes in working capital and other items of $234.4 million. We used $7,895.0 million of cash in investing activities, consistent with our ongoing capital investment in generation, transmission, and distribution infrastructure; we did not report capital expenditures as a separate disclosed line, with substantially all of this investing use of cash captured within other investing activities. Financing activities provided $639.0 million of cash, primarily reflecting net proceeds from financing sources used to fund our capital program; we did not pay dividends or repurchase shares during the year. Taken together, our cash and cash equivalents decreased by $323.5 million during the year.

As of fiscal year end 2024, we had total debt of approximately $46,135.7 million, and liabilities represented approximately 73.5% of total assets. Utilities such as ours typically fund a significant portion of capital expenditures through debt issuance, supported by regulated cash flows. We believe our cash flow from operations, together with access to the capital markets, will be sufficient to fund our operating needs and capital program for at least the next twelve months.

Critical Accounting Estimates

Regulatory Accounting. We account for the effects of rate regulation in accordance with accounting guidance applicable to rate-regulated enterprises, which requires us to record regulatory assets and liabilities for certain costs and revenues that are expected to be recovered from or refunded to customers in future periods. Changes in the regulatory environment could affect the recoverability of these amounts.

Property, Plant and Equipment. Our utility plant is depreciated over estimated useful lives that are reviewed periodically and may be revised based on operating experience, regulatory outcomes, and changes in our generation and delivery infrastructure.

Risks Related to Our Financial Results

We have a history of losses and may not achieve sustained profitability. We recorded an operating loss of $124.8 million for fiscal 2025, an operating margin of negative 2.7%, and a net loss of $15.6 million, a net margin of negative 0.3%. We expect to continue to make significant investments in research and development and sales and marketing, and we may continue to incur losses in future periods. These losses reflect deliberate, ongoing investment ahead of sustained profitability, consistent with our stage of growth as a subscription-based platform business, rather than a deterioration in our underlying business; however, if we are unable to grow revenue sufficiently to offset this level of investment, we may not achieve profitability on the timeline we expect.

Our research and development expenses are a substantial portion of our revenue. Research and development expense represented approximately 27.6% of revenue for fiscal 2025, and cost of revenue represented approximately 28.1% of revenue. If we are unable to generate sufficient revenue growth to support this level of investment, our operating results could be adversely affected.

Risks Related to Our Business and Customers

Our growth depends on our ability to retain existing customers and expand their usage of our platform over time. If customers do not renew their subscriptions or do not expand their usage, our revenue growth could slow.

Risks Related to Competition

We face substantial and increasing competition from other cybersecurity vendors, including large, well-capitalized platform providers. Some of our competitors have greater financial and engineering resources than we do and may be able to bundle competing capabilities with other products at lower incremental cost. If we are unable to compete effectively, our revenue and margins could be adversely affected.

Risks Related to Cybersecurity and Information Technology

As a cybersecurity company, we are an especially attractive target for sophisticated threat actors seeking to compromise our systems, our software, or our customers' environments through us. As described in Item 1C, "Cybersecurity," we have not experienced a material cybersecurity incident to date, but a future incident affecting our platform, our software supply chain, or our third-party service providers could disrupt our business, harm our reputation with security-conscious customers, and result in remediation costs or litigation.

Risks Related to Our Financial Position

Our indebtedness could limit our operating and financial flexibility. We had long-term debt of $743.0 million as of December 31, 2025. Our level of indebtedness could limit our ability to obtain additional financing and make us more vulnerable to adverse changes in interest rates.

General Risk Factors

Our quarterly and annual operating results may fluctuate due to factors including the timing of large customer commitments, changes in usage patterns by existing customers, and the timing of our own investments in research and development and sales capacity, which could cause our results to vary from period to period.

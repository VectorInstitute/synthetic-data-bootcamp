Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements

Note 1. Goodwill and Intangible Assets
Goodwill and intangible assets, net, totaled $970.1 million and $126.2 million, respectively, as of December 31, 2025, together representing approximately 12.3% of total assets. We test goodwill for impairment at least annually; no impairment was recognized during fiscal 2025.

Note 2. Debt
Long-term debt outstanding totaled $743.0 million as of December 31, 2025.

Note 3. Income Taxes
We recognized a net income tax benefit of $109.2 million for fiscal 2025, which substantially offset our pretax loss, resulting in a net loss of $15.6 million for the year.

Note 4. Commitments and Contingencies
We are subject to various claims and legal proceedings arising in the ordinary course of business. We do not believe the resolution of these matters, individually or in the aggregate, will have a material adverse effect on our consolidated financial position or results of operations.

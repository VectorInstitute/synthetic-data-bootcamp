Overview

Amberwood Technologies Corp. is a diversified technology company operating through Software Solutions, Network Solutions, and Technology-Enabled Instruments segments. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, we generated net revenues of $5,825.6 million, operating income of $1,432.8 million (an operating margin of 24.6%), and net income of $1,294.2 million (a net margin of 22.2%). Our results in 2024 continued to reflect our acquisitive growth strategy, with goodwill and acquired intangible assets together representing approximately 88.7% of total assets at year end. During the year, we completed the acquisition of Dunmore Technologies, Inc. to add a durable, high-margin niche instrumentation business to our portfolio.

Our gross margin of 72.5% reflects the high proportion of software and recurring-revenue products within our portfolio. Selling, general and administrative expenses, together with research and development expenses, represented approximately 50.5% of net revenues, consistent with our continued investment in product development and in the sales and customer-success functions that support our niche-market software businesses. Our effective tax rate for the year was 9.7%, below the U.S. federal statutory rate, reflecting the mix of earnings across the jurisdictions in which our businesses operate and the impact of research and development tax credits.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

We generated $1,717.5 million of cash from operating activities during 2024, reflecting net income of $1,294.2 million and non-cash depreciation and amortization of $688.6 million, partly offset by changes in working capital and other items. We used $2,943.0 million of cash in investing activities, primarily for the acquisition of Dunmore Technologies, Inc.; we did not make material capital expenditures during the year, consistent with the asset-light nature of our software and instrumentation businesses. Financing activities provided $885.9 million of cash, as proceeds from debt issuances more than offset $269.8 million of dividends paid to shareholders. Taken together, our cash and cash equivalents decreased by $339.7 million during the year.

As of December 31, 2024, we had total debt of approximately $6,249.6 million and stockholders' equity of $13,773.0 million. We believe our cash flow from operations, together with access to capital markets, will be sufficient to fund our operating needs, debt service, dividends, and acquisition strategy for at least the next twelve months.

Critical Accounting Estimates

Goodwill and Intangible Assets. We test goodwill and indefinite-lived intangible assets for impairment at least annually, and more frequently if indicators of impairment arise. These tests require us to estimate the fair value of our reporting units, which involves significant judgment regarding future cash flows, discount rates, and market multiples. Because goodwill and acquired intangible assets together represented approximately 88.7% of our total assets at December 31, 2024, changes in these estimates could result in material impairment charges in future periods.

Revenue Recognition. A portion of our revenue is derived from multi-element arrangements that include software licenses, subscriptions, and services. Determining the amount and timing of revenue to recognize for these arrangements requires judgment regarding the identification of performance obligations and the allocation of the transaction price among them.

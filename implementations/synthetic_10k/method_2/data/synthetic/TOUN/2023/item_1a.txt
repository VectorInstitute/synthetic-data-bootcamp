Risks Related to Government Contracting

A substantial portion of our revenue depends on U.S. government budgets and appropriations, which are subject to political and fiscal uncertainty. Reductions in defense spending, delays in the budget or appropriations process, or changes in defense priorities could reduce demand for our products or delay or terminate programs on which we rely.

Our government contracts can generally be terminated for the government's convenience, and are subject to audit and renegotiation of profits. A termination for convenience, an unfavorable audit finding, or a failure to perform on a key program could adversely affect our revenue, profitability, and reputation with current and prospective customers.

Risks Related to Our Operations

Our programs often involve complex engineering, manufacturing, and supply chain execution over multi-year periods. Design, manufacturing, or supply chain difficulties could result in cost overruns, schedule delays, or contract penalties, any of which could adversely affect our results of operations.

Risks Related to Competition

The aerospace and defense industry is highly competitive, with a small number of large prime contractors and a broader base of specialized suppliers. Our inability to win new programs or retain existing ones against this competition could adversely affect our revenue and growth.

Risks Related to Cybersecurity and Information Technology

Our business, including information related to government and defense programs, depends on the security of our information technology systems. As described in Item 1C, "Cybersecurity," during fiscal 2023 we identified a contained phishing-related account compromise affecting a limited number of employee accounts. We do not believe this incident has had a material effect on our business, but future incidents, particularly ones targeting government or defense program information, could be more severe and could result in remediation costs, contract impacts, litigation, or reputational harm.

Risks Related to Our Financial Position

While we do not carry significant funded indebtedness, our ability to fund working capital, capital expenditures, and shareholder distributions, including the share repurchases and dividends described in Item 7, depends on our continued ability to generate cash flow from operations.

General Risk Factors

Our quarterly and annual operating results may fluctuate based on the timing of program milestones, deliveries, and government funding actions, which could cause variability in our reported results from period to period.

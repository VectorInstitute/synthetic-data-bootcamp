Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements

Note 1. Inventory
Inventory, including program-specific work in process, totaled $5,371.8 million as of December 31, 2023, representing approximately 23.8% of total assets.

Note 2. Income Taxes
Our effective tax rate for fiscal 2023 was 21.0%, consistent with the U.S. federal statutory rate.

Note 3. Commitments and Contingencies
We are subject to various claims, legal proceedings, and government contract audits arising in the ordinary course of business. We do not believe the resolution of these matters, individually or in the aggregate, will have a material adverse effect on our consolidated financial position or results of operations.

Note 4. Cybersecurity Matters
As described in Item 1C, "Cybersecurity," during the year we identified and contained a phishing-related account compromise affecting a limited number of employee accounts. The incident did not have a material effect on our consolidated financial statements.

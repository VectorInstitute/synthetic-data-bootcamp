Overview

Harborview Logistics Corp. is a retailer and distributor of automotive replacement parts and accessories. For the fiscal year ended in 2025, we generated net sales of $19,030.6 million, operating income of $3,954.8 million (an operating margin of 20.8%), and net income of $2,682.2 million (a net margin of 14.1%).

Our gross margin of 58.3% reflects our merchandise mix and ongoing efforts to manage product cost and pricing. Selling, general and administrative expenses, which include store payroll, occupancy, and advertising costs, represented 32.6% of net sales. Our effective tax rate for the year was 32.2%, above the 21% U.S. federal statutory rate, reflecting state income taxes and other permanent differences between book and taxable income.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

We generated $2,841.3 million of cash from operating activities during fiscal 2025, reflecting net income of $2,682.2 million and depreciation and amortization of $586.5 million, partly offset by changes in working capital. We used $1,385.5 million of cash in investing activities, primarily capital expenditures of $1,478.4 million related to new store openings, store remodels, and distribution center capacity, partly offset by other investing inflows. Financing activities used $1,714.2 million of cash, primarily reflecting $1,466.3 million used for share repurchases. We did not pay dividends during fiscal 2025. Taken together, our cash and cash equivalents decreased by $258.3 million during the year.

As of fiscal year end 2025, we had stockholders' equity of $448.0 million, reflecting our long-standing practice of returning capital to shareholders primarily through share repurchases funded substantially through debt issuance, rather than through retaining earnings on the balance sheet. This is a deliberate capital allocation strategy that reflects management's confidence in our ongoing cash flow generation, though it leaves us with limited equity capacity to absorb a sustained downturn in operating performance. We believe our cash flow from operations, together with access to the credit and capital markets, will be sufficient to fund our operating needs, capital expenditures, and share repurchase program for at least the next twelve months.

Critical Accounting Estimates

Inventory. Inventory, which represented approximately 39.6% of our total assets as of fiscal year end, is stated at the lower of cost or market. We make estimates regarding shrinkage and excess or obsolete inventory based on historical experience and current market conditions; actual results could differ from these estimates.

Self-Insurance Reserves. We are self-insured for a portion of our exposure to general liability, workers' compensation, and vehicle liability claims. We estimate our self-insurance reserves based on historical claims experience and actuarial assumptions, which involve significant judgment.

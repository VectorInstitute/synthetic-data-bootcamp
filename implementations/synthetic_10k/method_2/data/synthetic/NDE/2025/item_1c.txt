Risk Management and Strategy

We have implemented a cybersecurity risk management program designed to identify, assess, and manage material risks from cybersecurity threats to our information systems and the data we process, including customer, employee, and commercial-account information. Our program is informed by the NIST Cybersecurity Framework, and we periodically benchmark our practices against that framework using a combination of internal assessment and independent outside expertise.

Our cybersecurity program includes continuous monitoring of our information technology environment, vulnerability scanning and penetration testing, employee security-awareness training, and a formal incident response plan that sets out procedures for investigating, containing, and remediating cybersecurity incidents. Because our point-of-sale, e-commerce, inventory management, and commercial credit systems depend in part on third-party service providers, our vendor management process includes an assessment of those providers' security practices before and during the engagement.

Cybersecurity Risks

We have not experienced a material cybersecurity incident to date. However, like other retailers, we are a target of cybersecurity threats, and a future incident affecting our systems or those of our third-party service providers could disrupt store or distribution operations, compromise sensitive data, and result in remediation costs, litigation, or reputational harm. See Item 1A, "Risk Factors," for additional discussion of cybersecurity-related risks.

Governance

Our Board of Directors has established a dedicated Technology and Cybersecurity Committee responsible for overseeing our cybersecurity risk management program. Management provides the committee with regular updates on our cybersecurity program, including the results of risk assessments, the status of our security initiatives, and the threat landscape we face. The committee in turn reports to the full Board on cybersecurity matters it believes warrant the Board's attention.

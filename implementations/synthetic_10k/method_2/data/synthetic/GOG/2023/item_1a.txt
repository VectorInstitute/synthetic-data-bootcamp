Risks Related to Our Financial Results

We have a history of losses and may not sustain profitability. We recorded an operating loss of $51.7 million for fiscal 2023, an operating margin of negative 1.8%, and a pretax loss of $59.6 million. We recorded net income of $64.7 million for the year only because we recognized a net income tax benefit of $124.2 million, primarily reflecting discrete tax items; absent a comparable benefit in a future period, we may report a net loss. We expect to continue to make significant investments in research and development and sales and marketing, and we may not achieve or sustain profitability in future periods.

Our research and development expenses are a substantial portion of our revenue. Research and development expense represented approximately 45.2% of revenue for fiscal 2023. If we are unable to generate sufficient revenue growth to support this level of investment, our operating results could be adversely affected.

Risks Related to Our Business and Customers

Our growth depends on our ability to retain existing customers and expand their usage of our platform over time. Our land-and-expand business model depends on customers increasing their usage of our platform after an initial purchase. If customers do not increase their usage, or if they reduce or cancel their usage due to cost pressure or other factors, our revenue growth could slow.

We may not be able to sustain our revenue growth rate in future periods. Our revenue growth rate may slow as our revenue base grows larger, as the market for observability software matures, or as competition increases.

Risks Related to New Product Introductions

We introduced SentinelFlow, a new platform module extending automated anomaly detection to customers' multi-cloud infrastructure, during fiscal 2023, and we may not realize the expected benefits from this or future product introductions. New products require significant investment well before they generate meaningful revenue, and customer adoption of SentinelFlow or other new capabilities may be slower than we expect, or may not occur at all. If we are unable to successfully develop, market, and achieve customer adoption of new products, our ability to grow revenue and improve our operating margin could be adversely affected.

Risks Related to Competition

We face substantial and increasing competition, including from major cloud infrastructure providers that offer their own monitoring capabilities. Some of our competitors have greater financial and engineering resources than we do and may be able to bundle competing capabilities with other products at lower incremental cost. If we are unable to compete effectively, our revenue and margins could be adversely affected.

Risks Related to Cybersecurity and Information Technology

Our platform processes substantial amounts of customer infrastructure and application data, and a cybersecurity incident affecting our systems could harm our business. As described in Item 1C, "Cybersecurity," we have not experienced a material cybersecurity incident to date, but a future incident affecting our platform or our third-party service providers could disrupt our service, compromise customer data, and result in a loss of customer confidence, remediation costs, or litigation.

General Risk Factors

Our quarterly and annual operating results may fluctuate due to factors including the timing of large customer commitments, changes in usage patterns by existing customers, and the timing of our own investments in research and development and sales capacity, which could cause our results to vary from period to period.

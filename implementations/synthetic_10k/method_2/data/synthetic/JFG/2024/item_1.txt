Introduction

Fieldstone Resources Holdings, Inc. ("Fieldstone," "we," "our," or "us") is a distributor of industrial and maintenance, repair, and operations ("MRO") products, headquartered in Minnesota. We sell fasteners, safety supplies, cutting tools, and other industrial and construction supplies to commercial, industrial, and construction customers, primarily through a direct sales and in-plant vending model rather than traditional retail storefronts. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, we generated net sales of $5,668.4 million.

Our Business Model

Our business is built around embedding our sales and inventory-management personnel directly at customer sites, where we manage on-site inventory programs and vending solutions that are designed to reduce our customers' total cost of procurement. We believe this close, service-intensive relationship with customers differentiates us from distributors that compete primarily on price and catalog breadth.

Products and Suppliers

We distribute products sourced from a broad base of suppliers, supplemented by a portion of private-label and proprietary products. Maintaining broad product availability requires us to carry significant inventory; inventory represented approximately 37.8% of our total assets as of December 31, 2024.

Human Capital Resources

We employ a workforce consisting of sales and service personnel embedded at or near customer locations, supported by distribution center and corporate personnel. We do not have any collective bargaining agreements covering a material portion of our U.S. workforce and believe our employee relations are good. We focus on retention through structured training and internal advancement from entry-level sales roles into district and regional leadership.

Competition

The industrial and MRO distribution market is highly fragmented and competitive. We compete with other industrial distributors, manufacturers that sell directly to end customers, and online marketplaces. We believe the principal competitive factors in our markets are on-site service quality, product availability, and total cost of procurement rather than unit price alone.

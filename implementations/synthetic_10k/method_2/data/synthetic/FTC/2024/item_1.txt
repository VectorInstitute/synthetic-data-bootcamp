Introduction

Oakridge Capital Holdings, Inc. ("Oakridge," "we," "our," or "us") is a global asset management company headquartered in California. We provide investment management products and services to individual and institutional clients across a range of asset classes and investment strategies. For the fiscal year ended 2024, we generated net revenue of $9,553.3 million. Our revenue is derived primarily from investment management fees, which are generally based on the value of assets we manage on behalf of our clients.

Our Business

We offer investment management products and services across equity, fixed income, multi-asset, and alternative investment strategies, distributed to individual and institutional clients through a combination of direct relationships and third-party intermediaries. A substantial portion of our total assets, approximately 32.8% at fiscal year end, consists of goodwill and acquired intangible assets, reflecting our history of growth through acquiring complementary investment management businesses and distribution capabilities.

Recent Acquisition

During fiscal 2024, we completed the acquisition of Carrowgate Capital Partners, Inc., an investment management firm, to add complementary investment strategies and expand our third-party distribution reach. Carrowgate is being integrated into our existing investment management and distribution operations.

Regulation

As an investment adviser, we are subject to extensive regulation by securities regulators, which govern, among other things, our fiduciary obligations to clients, our marketing and distribution practices, and our investment management activities.

Human Capital

Our workforce supports our investment management, distribution, and corporate support functions globally. We compete for investment professionals, distribution talent, and technology personnel, and we seek to offer competitive compensation and benefits along with training and development opportunities. We do not have any collective bargaining agreements in the United States and believe our relationships with our employees are good.

Competition

The investment management industry is highly competitive. We compete with other asset managers, including larger diversified financial institutions and specialized investment boutiques, for client assets and distribution relationships. We believe the principal competitive factors in our industry are investment performance, breadth of product offerings, distribution reach, and fee levels.

Risks Related to Our Business

Our revenue is directly affected by the level and performance of financial markets, since our investment management fees are generally based on the value of client assets under management. A decline in equity or fixed income markets, whether due to macroeconomic conditions, geopolitical events, or other factors, would reduce the value of assets we manage and, correspondingly, our fee revenue.

Our growth strategy has depended in part on acquiring complementary investment management and distribution businesses, and goodwill and acquired intangible assets together represented approximately 32.8% of our total assets as of fiscal year end 2024. A significant impairment of our goodwill or intangible assets, resulting from a deterioration in the performance or outlook of an acquired business, could adversely affect our results of operations.

Client outflows or underperformance relative to benchmarks could reduce our assets under management and fee revenue. Our ability to retain and grow client assets depends on our investment performance relative to competitors and benchmarks, as well as the quality of our client service and distribution relationships.

Risks Related to Acquisitions

We may not realize the anticipated benefits of our acquisition of Carrowgate Capital Partners, Inc. completed during fiscal 2024. Integrating an acquired investment management business involves numerous risks, including the diversion of management attention, the potential loss of key investment professionals or client relationships of the acquired business, and the possibility that anticipated benefits in investment strategies or distribution reach are not realized on the timeline we expect or at all. If we are unable to successfully integrate Carrowgate's operations, our results of operations could be adversely affected.

Risks Related to Regulation

We are subject to extensive regulation as an investment adviser, and changes in regulatory requirements could increase our compliance costs or restrict our activities. Our regulators have broad discretion over our fiduciary obligations, marketing practices, and the conduct of our investment management business.

Risks Related to Our Financial Position

Our liabilities represented approximately 61.9% of our total assets as of fiscal year end 2024, including $2,619.7 million of long-term debt. Our level of indebtedness could limit our financial flexibility and increase our vulnerability to adverse changes in interest rates.

Risks Related to Cybersecurity and Information Technology

Our business depends on the security and reliability of the information technology systems that support our investment management and client service operations. As described in Item 1C, "Cybersecurity," while we have not experienced a material cybersecurity incident to date, a future incident affecting our systems, client data, or those of our third-party service providers could disrupt our operations, result in financial losses, and harm our reputation.

General Risk Factors

Our quarterly and annual operating results may fluctuate due to factors that are difficult to predict, including market performance, the timing of client inflows and outflows, and the mix of assets under management across strategies with different fee rates.

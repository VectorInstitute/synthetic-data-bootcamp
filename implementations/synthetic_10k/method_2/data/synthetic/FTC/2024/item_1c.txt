Cybersecurity Program

We maintain a cybersecurity program informed by the NIST Cybersecurity Framework and ISO/IEC 27001, designed to identify, assess, and manage risks from cybersecurity threats to our information systems and the data we and our clients rely on. We periodically benchmark our practices against these frameworks using a combination of internal assessment and independent outside expertise.

Our program includes recurring risk assessments, vulnerability scanning and penetration testing, employee security-awareness training, and a formal incident response plan that establishes procedures for identifying, escalating, containing, and remediating cybersecurity incidents. We also assess the cybersecurity practices of third-party vendors and service providers that support our investment management and client service operations as part of our vendor management process.

Cybersecurity Risks

We have not experienced a material cybersecurity incident to date. However, like other asset managers, we are a target of cybersecurity threats, and a future incident affecting our systems or those of our third-party service providers could disrupt our operations, compromise sensitive client data, and result in remediation costs, litigation, or reputational harm. See Item 1A, "Risk Factors," for additional discussion of cybersecurity-related risks.

Governance

A dedicated Technology and Cybersecurity Committee of the Board oversees our cybersecurity risk management program. Management provides the committee with regular updates on our cybersecurity program, including the results of risk assessments, the status of our security initiatives, and the threat landscape we face. The committee in turn reports to the full Board on cybersecurity matters it believes warrant the Board's attention.

Overview

Oakridge Capital Holdings, Inc. is a global asset management company. For the fiscal year ended 2024, we generated net revenue of $9,553.3 million, operating income of $412.4 million (an operating margin of 4.3%), and net income of $564.4 million (a net margin of 5.9%). During the year, we completed the acquisition of Carrowgate Capital Partners, Inc. to add complementary investment strategies and expand our third-party distribution reach.

Our operating margin of 4.3% reflects the cost of distribution, investment management, and corporate support functions required to support our global asset management business. During fiscal 2024, we recognized a net income tax benefit of $252.3 million, primarily reflecting the resolution of discrete tax items during the year, resulting in net income of $564.4 million exceeding our pretax income of $312.1 million for the period.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

We generated $945.8 million of cash from operating activities during fiscal 2024, reflecting net income of $564.4 million, non-cash depreciation and amortization of $150.9 million, and changes in working capital and other items of $230.5 million. We used $2,411.3 million of cash in investing activities, including capital expenditures of $200.1 million and $2,211.2 million used for the acquisition of Carrowgate Capital Partners, Inc. Financing activities provided $1,656.1 million of cash, as proceeds reflected in other financing activities of $1,927.4 million more than offset $271.3 million used for share repurchases. Taken together, our cash and cash equivalents increased by $190.6 million during the year.

As of fiscal year end 2024, we had total debt of $2,619.7 million and stockholders' equity of $12,540.4 million. We believe our cash flow from operations, together with access to capital markets, will be sufficient to fund our operating needs, debt service, and capital return program for at least the next twelve months.

Critical Accounting Estimates

Goodwill and Intangible Assets. We test goodwill and indefinite-lived intangible assets for impairment at least annually, and more frequently if indicators of impairment arise. Goodwill and acquired intangible assets together represented approximately 32.8% of our total assets as of fiscal year end 2024; a deterioration in the performance or outlook of one or more of our underlying businesses could require us to recognize a material non-cash impairment charge.

Revenue Recognition. Our investment management fee revenue is generally recognized over time as services are provided, based on the value of assets under management, which requires us to estimate fee rates and contractual terms across our client relationships.

Overview

Cedarfield Systems Holdings, Inc. is a manufacturer of metal, glass, and plastic packaging and containers. For the fiscal year ended in 2025, we generated net revenue of $16,028.3 million, pretax income of $1,311.9 million (a pretax margin of 8.2%), and net income of $1,036.4 million (a net margin of 6.5%). During the year, we completed the acquisition of Thornridge Packaging Corp. to expand our manufacturing capacity and customer relationships in adjacent packaging end markets.

Net revenue | $16,028.3 | 100.0%
Selling, general and administrative expenses | 719.9 | 4.5%
Cost of sales and other operating costs | 13,996.5 | 87.3%

Cost of sales and other operating costs, which include the cost of raw materials, manufacturing, and other operating costs that are not separately broken out above, represented approximately 87.3% of net revenue, consistent with the capital-intensive, input-cost-sensitive nature of packaging manufacturing. Our effective tax rate for the year was 21.0%, consistent with the U.S. federal statutory rate.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

We generated $1,381.7 million of cash from operating activities during fiscal 2025, reflecting net income of $1,036.4 million and non-cash depreciation and amortization of $782.3 million, partly offset by an unfavorable change in working capital and other items of $437.0 million. We used $770.0 million of cash in investing activities, including capital expenditures of $520.3 million and $249.7 million used for the acquisition of Thornridge Packaging Corp. Financing activities used $480.5 million of cash, primarily reflecting $1,719.4 million used for share repurchases, partly offset by other financing inflows. We did not pay dividends during fiscal 2025. Taken together, our cash and cash equivalents increased by $131.2 million during the year.

As of fiscal year end, we had stockholders' equity of $6,885.6 million, equal to approximately 29.3% of total assets, and total debt of $23.1 million. We believe our cash flow from operations will be sufficient to fund our operating needs, capital expenditures, and share repurchase program for at least the next twelve months.

Critical Accounting Estimates

Goodwill and Intangible Assets. We test goodwill and indefinite-lived intangible assets for impairment at least annually. Goodwill and acquired intangible assets together represented approximately 28.1% of our total assets as of fiscal year end, reflecting our acquisition history within the packaging industry; a deterioration in the performance or outlook of one or more of our reporting units could result in a material impairment charge.

Inventory Valuation. Inventory is stated at the lower of cost or market. We make estimates regarding excess or obsolete inventory based on historical experience and current market conditions; actual results could differ from these estimates.

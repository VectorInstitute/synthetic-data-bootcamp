Risks Related to Our Business

Our revenue depends significantly on the level and performance of financial markets, which affect the value of assets we hold in custody or manage on behalf of clients. A decline in equity or fixed income markets could reduce the fees we earn on asset servicing and asset management, which are generally based on the value of client assets rather than fixed amounts.

Our profitability is sensitive to changes in interest rates and our funding costs. Our interest expense represented a substantial portion of our revenue during fiscal 2023, reflecting the cost of funding our balance sheet in a rising interest rate environment. Further increases in funding costs, or a mismatch between the timing of asset and liability repricing, could compress our net interest margin and adversely affect our results of operations.

Risks Related to Regulation

We are subject to extensive banking and fiduciary regulation, and changes in regulatory requirements could increase our compliance costs or restrict our activities. Our regulators have broad discretion over our capital and liquidity requirements and the conduct of our custody, administration, and fiduciary activities.

We are required to maintain minimum regulatory capital levels, and our stockholders' equity represented approximately 8.9% of our total assets as of fiscal year end 2023. A reduction in our capital levels could limit our ability to pay dividends, repurchase shares, or grow our business.

Risks Related to Our Financial Position

Our liabilities represented approximately 91.1% of our total assets as of fiscal year end 2023. Our ability to fund our balance sheet depends on continued access to client deposits and other funding sources on acceptable terms; a loss of client or counterparty confidence could require us to seek more expensive funding alternatives.

Risks Related to Cybersecurity and Information Technology

Our business depends on the security and reliability of the information technology systems that support our custody, fund administration, and wealth management operations. As described in Item 1C, "Cybersecurity," while we have not experienced a material cybersecurity incident to date, a future incident affecting our systems, client data, or those of our third-party service providers could disrupt our operations, result in financial losses, and harm our reputation.

General Risk Factors

Our results of operations may be affected by general economic and market conditions, including changes in interest rates, equity valuations, and client asset flows, which influence both our fee-based revenue and our net interest income.

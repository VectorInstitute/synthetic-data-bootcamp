Introduction

Lakeshore Instruments Corp. ("Lakeshore," "we," "our," or "us") is an asset management, custody, and fund administration company headquartered in Illinois, serving institutional and wealth management clients. For the fiscal year ended 2023, we generated net revenue of $8,759.2 million. Our business depends on providing custody, fund administration, investment management, and related asset-servicing capabilities to institutional investors, asset managers, and high-net-worth individuals and families.

Our Business

We provide asset servicing (custody, fund administration, and securities lending), wealth management, and asset management services. Our asset servicing operations hold and administer assets on behalf of institutional clients such as pension funds, insurance companies, and asset managers. Our wealth management operations provide investment management, trust, and banking services to high-net-worth individuals and families. Our asset management operations provide investment products and strategies to institutional and individual clients.

Regulation

As a custody bank and asset manager, we are subject to extensive regulation and supervision by federal and state banking and securities regulators, which govern, among other things, our capital and liquidity levels, fiduciary responsibilities, and the conduct of our custody, administration, and investment management activities.

Human Capital

Our workforce supports our asset servicing, wealth management, and asset management operations. We compete for specialized talent in trust administration, investment management, and technology, and we seek to offer competitive compensation and benefits along with training and development opportunities. We do not have any collective bargaining agreements in the United States and believe our relationships with our employees are good.

Competition

The asset servicing and wealth and asset management industry is highly competitive. We compete with other custody banks, asset managers, and wealth management firms for institutional and individual clients. We believe the principal competitive factors in our industry are service quality, breadth of capabilities, technology, and the strength and stability of our balance sheet.

Overview

Lakeshore Instruments Corp. is an asset management, custody, and fund administration company. For the fiscal year ended 2023, we generated net revenue of $8,759.2 million, pretax income of $3,503.7 million, and net income of $2,242.1 million (a net margin of 25.6%).

Results of Operations

The following table summarizes our results of operations for the fiscal year ended 2023 (in millions, except percentages; amounts may not sum due to rounding):

Net revenue | $8,759.2 | 100.0%
Interest expense | 5,255.5 | 60.0%
Pretax income | 3,503.7 | 40.0%
Income tax expense | 1,261.6 | 14.4%
Net income | 2,242.1 | 25.6%

As a custody bank and asset manager, the substantial majority of our balance sheet and cost structure is not broken into the same cost-of-revenue and selling, general and administrative categories an industrial company reports; our results instead reflect the net interest income and fee-based revenue generated across our asset servicing, wealth management, and asset management businesses, net of funding costs. Our funding costs rose meaningfully during fiscal 2023, consistent with the broader rising interest rate environment, and represented 60.0% of net revenue for the year. Our effective tax rate for the year was 36.0%, above the 21% U.S. federal statutory rate, reflecting the jurisdictional mix of our earnings and other permanent differences between book and taxable income.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

We generated $4,871.2 million of cash from operating activities during fiscal 2023, reflecting net income of $2,242.1 million, non-cash depreciation and amortization of $1,173.1 million, and changes in working capital and other items of $1,456.0 million. Investing activities provided $9,879.6 million of cash, reflecting net proceeds from our investment securities and loan portfolios, partly offset by capital expenditures of $207.3 million. Financing activities used $14,940.4 million of cash, primarily reflecting a net reduction in deposits and other funding sources, reflected in other financing activities of $(14,160.8) million, together with $779.5 million used for share repurchases. Taken together, our cash and cash equivalents decreased by $189.5 million during the year.

As of fiscal year end 2023, we had stockholders' equity of $15,676.4 million, representing approximately 8.9% of total assets. We believe our capital and liquidity position is sufficient to support our custody, administration, and asset management operations for at least the next twelve months.

Critical Accounting Estimates

Fair Value of Financial Instruments. A substantial portion of our assets and liabilities are carried at fair value based on quoted market prices or valuation models that incorporate observable and unobservable inputs. Changes in market conditions or the assumptions underlying these models could materially affect the fair value of these instruments.

Allowance for Credit Losses. We maintain an allowance for credit losses on our lending and counterparty exposures based on historical loss experience, current conditions, and reasonable and supportable forecasts of future economic conditions.

Overview

Northbridge Technologies Corp. is an integrated health care company operating pharmacy services, health care benefits, and health services businesses. For the fiscal year ended in 2024, we generated net revenue of $414,916.9 million, operating income of $9,593.3 million (an operating margin of 2.3%), and net income of $5,369.5 million (a net margin of 1.3%).

Results of Operations

The following table summarizes our results of operations for the fiscal year ended in 2024 (in millions, except percentages; amounts may not sum due to rounding):

Net revenue | $414,916.9 | 100.0%
Cost of revenue | 254,356.9 | 61.3%
Other operating expenses | 150,966.8 | 36.4%
Operating income | 9,593.3 | 2.3%
Income tax expense | 4,223.7 | 1.0%
Net income | 5,369.5 | 1.3%

Cost of revenue represented approximately 61.3% of net revenue, and other operating expenses, which include selling, general and administrative costs across our pharmacy, benefits, and health services operations, represented approximately 36.4% of net revenue, together reflecting the scale and relatively thin unit economics typical of our integrated pharmacy and health benefits businesses. Our thin margins for fiscal 2024 also reflect, in part, recently enacted state and federal measures affecting pharmacy benefit management practices and prescription drug pricing, which took effect during the year across several of the markets in which we operate. Our effective tax rate for the year was 44.0%, which is high relative to the 21% U.S. federal statutory rate primarily because non-deductible items and other permanent differences represent a larger proportion of a thin pretax income base than they would of a larger one.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

We generated $9,017.6 million of cash from operating activities during fiscal 2024, reflecting net income of $5,369.5 million and non-cash depreciation and amortization of $4,457.5 million, partly offset by an unfavorable change in working capital and other items of $809.5 million. We used $9,579.9 million of cash in investing activities, including capital expenditures of $2,851.8 million. Financing activities used $1,290.1 million of cash, including $3,944.3 million of dividends paid to shareholders and $3,076.0 million used for share repurchases, partly offset by other financing inflows. Taken together, our cash and cash equivalents decreased by $1,852.4 million during the year.

As of fiscal year end, we had stockholders' equity of $106,080.4 million, equal to approximately 33.1% of total assets, and no outstanding short-term or long-term debt reported separately from our other liabilities. We believe our cash flow from operations will be sufficient to fund our operating needs, capital expenditures, dividends, and share repurchases for at least the next twelve months.

Critical Accounting Estimates

Goodwill and Intangible Assets. We test goodwill and indefinite-lived intangible assets for impairment at least annually. Goodwill and acquired intangible assets together represented approximately 43.0% of our total assets as of fiscal year end, reflecting the acquisitive history of our pharmacy, benefits, and health services businesses; a deterioration in the performance or outlook of one or more of our reporting units could result in a material impairment charge.

Reserves for Health Care Costs and Rebates. We make significant estimates regarding reserves for health care benefit claims incurred but not yet paid, and for rebates owed to or receivable from manufacturers and payors, based on historical experience and actuarial assumptions. Actual results could differ from these estimates.

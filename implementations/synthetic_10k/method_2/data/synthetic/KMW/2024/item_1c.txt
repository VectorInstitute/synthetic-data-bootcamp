Cybersecurity Program

We maintain a cybersecurity risk management program informed by ISO/IEC 27001, designed to identify, assess, and manage risks from cybersecurity threats to our information systems and the sensitive health, financial, and personal data we and our pharmacy, benefits, and care-delivery businesses process.

Our program includes recurring risk assessments, vulnerability scanning and penetration testing, employee security-awareness training, and a formal incident response plan that sets out procedures for identifying, escalating, containing, and remediating cybersecurity incidents. Because our operations depend in part on third-party service providers and business partners, our vendor management process includes an assessment of those providers' security practices before and during the engagement. We maintain cybersecurity insurance that may offset a portion of the costs associated with certain incidents.

Cybersecurity Risks

We have not experienced a material cybersecurity incident to date. However, given the volume and sensitivity of the data we process across our pharmacy, benefits, and care-delivery operations, we are a frequent target of cybersecurity threats, and a future incident affecting our systems or those of our third-party service providers could disrupt our operations, compromise sensitive data, and result in remediation costs, regulatory scrutiny, litigation, or reputational harm. See Item 1A, "Risk Factors," for additional discussion of cybersecurity-related risks.

Governance

A dedicated Technology and Cybersecurity Committee of our Board of Directors oversees our cybersecurity risk management program. Management provides the committee with regular updates on our cybersecurity program, including the results of risk assessments, the status of our security initiatives, and the threat landscape we face across our pharmacy, benefits, and care-delivery operations. The committee in turn reports to the full Board on cybersecurity matters it believes warrant the Board's attention.

Item 7A.Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk.
Information required by Item 7A appears in Note 1 — “Operations and summary of significant accounting policies,” Note 2 — "Sales and revenue recognition," Note 4 — “Derivative financial instruments and risk management,” Note 7 — "Cat Financial financing activities," Note 11 — "Investments in debt and equity securities," and Note 18 — “Fair value disclosures” of Part II, Item 8 “Financial Statements and Supplementary Data.” Other information required by Item 7A is included in Part II, Item 7 “Management’s Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations.”

Item 1. Business

General

Oral Communications, Inc. is a leading provider of communications, technology, information, and entertainment products and services to consumers, businesses, and governmental agencies. For convenience, the terms "Oral Communications," "the Company," "we," "us," or "our" are used in this Annual Report on Form 10-K to refer to Oral Communications, Inc. and its consolidated subsidiaries.

Incorporated in the State of Delaware and headquartered in Reston, Virginia, we operate at the intersection of connectivity and content. Our mission is to deliver seamless, high-speed communication solutions that empower our customers to connect with the people and information that matter most. During the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, we generated total revenue of $119,056 million. This figure represents a decrease of 8.5% compared to the prior fiscal year, reflecting broader industry shifts and a strategic transition toward higher-margin service offerings.

As a premier Communication Services company, we maintain a robust infrastructure that includes nationwide wireless networks, extensive fiber-optic installations, and sophisticated data centers. Our scale allows us to serve a diverse customer base ranging from individual mobile subscribers to large-scale multinational corporations and federal entities.

Strategy

Our long-term strategy is centered on maintaining our leadership position in the wireless and broadband markets while expanding our digital service capabilities. We focus on three primary pillars: network excellence, customer experience, and operational efficiency. By investing in next-generation network technologies, including the continued rollout of high-capacity spectrum and fiber-to-the-premises, we aim to provide superior reliability and speed. We also seek to simplify our product offerings to improve customer retention and reduce the cost of acquisition.

Reportable Segments

As of December 31, 2024, we classified our operations into three reportable segments: Wireless Services, Wireline and Broadband, and Media and Digital Services. This structure aligns with how our chief operating decision maker evaluates performance and allocates resources.

Wireless Services

The Wireless Services segment is our largest business unit and serves as the primary driver of our consolidated revenue. This segment provides a comprehensive suite of wireless communications services, including postpaid and prepaid service plans, data and messaging services, and the sale of wireless equipment such as smartphones, tablets, and wearable technology.

Our wireless network utilizes a combination of licensed spectrum across various frequency bands to provide broad geographic coverage and high-speed data capacity. We continue to transition our customer base to advanced network standards, which allows for more efficient use of our spectrum assets and provides customers with enhanced throughput for data-intensive applications. Revenue in this segment is primarily generated through monthly recurring service fees and the sale of hardware. We offer various financing options for equipment to make the latest technology accessible to a broad range of consumers.

Wireline and Broadband

The Wireline and Broadband segment provides high-speed internet, voice, and video services over our extensive fiber-optic and legacy copper networks. We offer these services to both residential and business customers. For residential consumers, our fiber-optic internet services provide symmetrical upload and download speeds that support high-definition video streaming, online gaming, and remote work environments.

For our business and enterprise customers, this segment provides sophisticated networking solutions, including private lines, software-defined networking, and cloud connectivity. We also provide wholesale services to other communication providers who utilize our network infrastructure to complete their own service offerings. As the demand for bandwidth continues to grow, we are focused on expanding our fiber footprint to reach more households and commercial buildings, which we believe will drive long-term growth in our broadband subscriber base.

Media and Digital Services

The Media and Digital Services segment encompasses our content distribution, digital advertising, and value-added technology services. This segment is responsible for the curation and delivery of digital content across our platforms, as well as the management of our proprietary advertising technology. We leverage our deep customer relationships and data analytics capabilities to provide targeted advertising solutions for brands looking to reach specific demographics across mobile and web interfaces.

Additionally, this segment focuses on developing and scaling new digital products, such as home security and automation, internet-of-things (IoT) connectivity for vehicles and industrial equipment, and managed IT services for small and medium-sized businesses. By integrating these services with our core connectivity offerings, we aim to increase the "stickiness" of our customer relationships and improve the overall lifetime value of each subscriber.

Principal Products and Services

Wireless Communications

We offer a variety of wireless service plans designed to meet the needs of different consumer segments. Our postpaid plans typically involve a monthly billing cycle and often include unlimited data, talk, and text, along with additional features such as international roaming and access to premium streaming content. Our prepaid plans allow customers to pay in advance for a specific amount of service, providing flexibility for those who do not wish to enter into long-term contracts or undergo credit checks.

Broadband and Connectivity

Our broadband services are delivered through a mix of technologies, with a strategic emphasis on fiber-to-the-home. We provide several tiers of internet speed, allowing customers to choose a plan that fits their usage patterns and budget. In addition to basic connectivity, we offer managed Wi-Fi solutions that ensure optimal coverage within the home or office. Our voice services include traditional landline telephony as well as Voice over Internet Protocol (VoIP) solutions that integrate with our broadband offerings.

Enterprise Solutions

For our large-scale corporate and government clients, we provide specialized services that include dedicated internet access, wide-area networking (WAN), and cybersecurity services. We assist these organizations in managing their complex communication needs through a combination of hardware, software, and consulting services. Our global network reach allows us to support the international operations of our enterprise clients, providing consistent service levels across multiple geographies.

Customers

Our customer base is highly diversified and consists of millions of individual consumers, small businesses, large enterprises, and government agencies.

Consumer Customers

The majority of our subscribers are individual consumers who use our wireless and broadband services for personal communication, entertainment, and information access. We segment our consumer market based on usage patterns, demographic data, and geographic location to tailor our marketing efforts and product bundles.

Business and Government Customers

We serve businesses of all sizes, from sole proprietorships to Global 500 companies. Our business customers rely on us for mission-critical connectivity that supports their daily operations. We also hold numerous contracts with local, state, and federal government agencies to provide secure and resilient communication infrastructure. These contracts often involve long-term commitments and specific service-level agreements regarding network uptime and security protocols.

Sales and Marketing

We employ a multi-channel sales and marketing strategy to reach our target audiences and drive customer acquisition.

Retail and Direct Sales

We operate a significant number of company-owned retail stores across the country, which serve as a primary touchpoint for customers to experience our products and receive personalized service. In addition to our own stores, we maintain a presence in third-party retail locations, including big-box electronics stores and independent authorized dealers. Our direct sales force focuses on our business and government segments, building long-term relationships and designing customized solutions for complex organizational needs.

Digital and Online Channels

Our website and mobile applications are increasingly important channels for both sales and customer support. Customers can purchase devices, change their service plans, and pay their bills through our digital platforms. We utilize digital marketing techniques, including search engine optimization, social media advertising, and email campaigns, to drive traffic to our online properties and convert prospects into subscribers.

Brand Advertising

We invest significantly in brand advertising to maintain high levels of awareness and reinforce our value proposition. Our marketing campaigns utilize a mix of traditional media, such as television and radio, and digital media to communicate our network reliability, speed, and the value of our bundled services.

Competition

The communication services industry is characterized by intense competition, rapid technological change, and evolving consumer preferences. We compete with a variety of companies across our different business segments.

Wireless Competition

In the wireless market, we compete primarily with other national wireless carriers, as well as regional providers and mobile virtual network operators (MVNOs). Competition is based on network quality, price, data speeds, and the variety of devices offered. In recent years, competition has intensified as carriers offer aggressive promotions, such as device subsidies and bundled streaming services, to attract and retain customers.

Broadband and Wireline Competition

Our broadband services compete with cable companies, other telecommunications providers, and satellite internet services. Cable companies have historically been strong competitors in the residential broadband market, and many are now expanding their wireless offerings to compete with our core mobile business. Conversely, we are increasingly competing with cable providers by offering fixed wireless access (FWA) as an alternative to traditional wired broadband in certain areas.

Technology and Media Competition

Our media and digital services segment competes with major technology companies, digital advertising platforms, and content creators. The shift toward digital advertising and the proliferation of streaming platforms have created a highly fragmented market where we must constantly innovate to capture consumer attention and advertising dollars.

Seasonality

Our business experiences a degree of seasonality, particularly in our Wireless Services segment.

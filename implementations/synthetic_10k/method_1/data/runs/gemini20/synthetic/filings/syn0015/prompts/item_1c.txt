Item 1. Business

OVERVIEW

Zennov Medical, Inc. (referred to in this Annual Report on Form 10-K as Zennov, the Company, we, us, or our) is a diversified medical technology company headquartered in Denver, Colorado. Since our incorporation in Delaware, we have dedicated our operations to the design, development, manufacture, and marketing of innovative medical solutions intended to improve patient outcomes and enhance the efficiency of healthcare delivery. We operate at the intersection of advanced engineering and clinical science, providing a broad portfolio of products that address critical needs in surgical intervention, diagnostic accuracy, and chronic disease management.

Our mission is to provide healthcare professionals with the tools necessary to treat complex medical conditions through minimally invasive techniques and data-driven insights. Our product development strategy is centered on high-growth segments of the healthcare market where clinical unmet needs remain significant. We maintain a robust research and development pipeline, supported by a global infrastructure of manufacturing facilities and a specialized sales force. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, our total revenue was $2,264 million.

As of December 31, 2024, we employed approximately 2,700 individuals worldwide. Our organizational structure is designed to foster innovation while maintaining rigorous quality standards and regulatory compliance across all jurisdictions in which we operate. We continue to invest in our core technologies while exploring strategic acquisitions and partnerships that complement our existing capabilities and expand our geographic reach.

PRINCIPAL PRODUCTS AND SERVICES

Our product portfolio is categorized into several key areas of clinical focus, primarily serving the cardiovascular, orthopedic, and general surgical markets. We offer a range of implantable devices, disposable surgical instruments, and sophisticated diagnostic hardware and software systems.

In our surgical portfolio, we provide specialized catheters, stents, and delivery systems used in interventional procedures. These products are designed to navigate complex anatomical structures with high precision, reducing the need for open-market surgeries and shortening patient recovery times. We also produce a line of bio-absorbable implants used in orthopedic reconstruction, which are engineered to provide structural support during the healing process before being naturally integrated by the body.

Our diagnostic offerings include molecular testing platforms and high-resolution imaging equipment. These systems are utilized by clinical laboratories and hospitals to identify disease markers at an early stage, enabling more personalized treatment pathways. Additionally, we provide integrated software solutions that allow for the remote monitoring of patients with chronic conditions. These digital health tools collect and analyze physiological data, providing clinicians with real-time alerts and longitudinal trends to improve outpatient care.

REPORTABLE SEGMENTS

We manage our business through three reportable segments: Therapeutic Solutions, Diagnostic Systems, and Patient Management. Each segment is focused on distinct aspects of the patient care continuum and utilizes specific technological platforms.

Therapeutic Solutions

The Therapeutic Solutions segment is our largest operating unit and is responsible for the development of interventional medical devices. This segment focuses on high-acuity clinical environments, such as cardiac catheterization labs and surgical suites. The primary products within this segment include endovascular grafts, electrophysiology mapping tools, and advanced wound closure devices. We focus our innovation in this segment on material science and micro-mechanics to enhance the performance and safety of our implantable technologies. Revenue in this segment is driven by the volume of elective and emergency surgical procedures performed globally.

Diagnostic Systems

The Diagnostic Systems segment provides the hardware and consumable reagents necessary for modern clinical diagnostics. Our focus in this area is on point-of-care testing and centralized laboratory automation. We develop proprietary assays for the detection of infectious diseases, oncological markers, and genetic predispositions. This segment also includes our imaging division, which produces specialized ultrasound and optical coherence tomography systems used to guide interventional procedures. The business model for this segment relies on a "razor and blade" approach, where the placement of diagnostic instruments creates a recurring revenue stream through the sale of associated consumables and service contracts.

Patient Management

The Patient Management segment encompasses our digital health and remote monitoring initiatives. This segment develops wearable sensors and cloud-based data platforms that connect patients with their healthcare providers outside of traditional clinical settings. Our solutions in this area are primarily used for the management of chronic respiratory and cardiovascular diseases. By leveraging artificial intelligence and machine learning, this segment aims to predict adverse clinical events before they occur, thereby reducing hospital readmission rates. This segment represents our most significant investment area for future growth as healthcare systems transition toward value-based care models.

CUSTOMERS

Our primary customers include hospitals, multi-hospital systems, ambulatory surgery centers, clinical laboratories, and physician offices. We also sell our products to government-affiliated healthcare providers and large integrated delivery networks. In certain international markets, we engage with national health ministries and centralized purchasing organizations.

No single customer accounted for more than ten percent of our consolidated revenue in the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024. However, the ongoing consolidation of healthcare providers, particularly in the United States, has led to the emergence of large group purchasing organizations (GPOs). These GPOs negotiate contracts on behalf of their members, often resulting in significant pricing pressure but providing us with access to a broad base of facilities. We maintain strong relationships with these organizations through our national accounts team, which focuses on demonstrating the clinical and economic value of our product offerings.

SALES AND MARKETING

We market and sell our products through a combination of a direct sales force and a network of independent distributors. In the United States and most major European markets, we utilize a direct sales model. Our sales representatives are highly trained professionals who often possess clinical backgrounds, allowing them to provide technical support and training to physicians and nursing staff in the operating room or laboratory setting.

In emerging markets and other international territories, we frequently partner with local distributors who possess deep knowledge of the regional regulatory environment and established relationships with local healthcare providers. These distributors are responsible for local inventory management, logistics, and first-line technical support, while our regional management teams provide oversight and strategic guidance.

Our marketing efforts are focused on clinical education and evidence-based promotion. We participate in major medical congresses and sponsor clinical trials to validate the efficacy of our technologies. We also engage in digital marketing initiatives and peer-to-peer training programs to increase awareness of our solutions among key opinion leaders and medical practitioners.

COMPETITION

The medical technology industry is characterized by rapid innovation and intense competition. We compete with a range of companies, from large multinational corporations with extensive product lines and significant financial resources to smaller, specialized firms that focus on niche technologies.

Our competitive position is based on several factors, including product performance, clinical outcomes, ease of use, reliability, and price. We also compete on the basis of our ability to provide comprehensive service and support to our customers. In many of our product categories, the ability to integrate hardware with proprietary software and data analytics is becoming an increasingly important competitive differentiator.

To maintain our market position, we must continuously invest in research and development to introduce new products and enhance our existing portfolio. Failure to innovate or to respond to technological shifts could result in a loss of market share to competitors who develop more effective or lower-cost alternatives. Additionally, the increasing focus on healthcare cost containment has intensified competition based on the economic value and cost-effectiveness of our products.

SEASONALITY

Our business experiences a degree of seasonality, particularly in our Therapeutic Solutions segment. We typically observe higher sales volumes in the fourth quarter of our fiscal year, as hospitals and other healthcare providers often exhaust their annual capital budgets and patients seek to undergo elective procedures before their insurance deductibles reset at the beginning of the new calendar year. Conversely, the first quarter often sees a relative decline in procedure volumes.

Furthermore, our international operations can be affected by regional holiday schedules and summer vacation periods, particularly in Europe, which may result in lower surgical volumes during the third quarter. While these seasonal patterns are generally predictable, they can be influenced by broader economic conditions or public health events that disrupt standard healthcare utilization patterns.

Business and Industry Risks

We operate in a highly competitive environment dominated by large, well-capitalized organizations and specialized firms, and we may be unable to compete effectively.

The medical technology industry is intensely competitive and subject to rapid technological change. We also face competition from smaller, niche companies that focus on specific high-growth technologies. In our Therapeutic Solutions segment, competition is based on clinical efficacy, ease of use, and physician preference. In the Diagnostic Systems segment, we compete on the basis of diagnostic accuracy, throughput, and the total cost of ownership for clinical laboratories. If we are unable to demonstrate the superior clinical and economic value of our products, our market share may decline, and our revenue could be negatively impacted.

Our significant investment in research and development may not result in commercially viable products or sufficient financial returns.

Our business strategy is heavily dependent on innovation, and we allocate a substantial portion of our resources to research and development activities. In the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, our research and development expense was $923 million, representing 40.8% of our total revenue. This high level of investment carries significant risk. We may spend hundreds of millions of dollars on products that fail to meet clinical endpoints in trials or that are rejected by regulatory authorities. Consolidation in the healthcare industry and the use of group purchasing organizations may lead to significant pricing pressure.

This trend has resulted in the emergence of large, powerful healthcare systems and group purchasing organizations (GPOs) that have increased leverage when negotiating prices with medical device manufacturers. Healthcare payers, including government programs and private insurers, are increasingly shifting away from fee-for-service models toward value-based care. These models reward healthcare providers for improving patient outcomes while reducing the total cost of care. This shift may lead to a reduction in the volume of elective procedures, which would specifically impact our Therapeutic Solutions segment. Results of Operations

Revenue

Consolidated revenue for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, was $2,264 million, an increase of $171 million, or 8.2%, from $2,093 million in fiscal 2023. The growth in revenue was observed across all three of our reportable segments.

In our Therapeutic Solutions segment, revenue growth was supported by the continued recovery of elective surgical procedure volumes in the United States and Europe. Our portfolio of endovascular grafts and specialized catheters experienced strong demand as healthcare providers addressed patient backlogs. Additionally, the introduction of next-generation bio-absorbable implants in our orthopedic line contributed to market share gains in the ambulatory surgery center channel.

The Diagnostic Systems segment benefited from an increase in the utilization of our molecular testing platforms. Our "razor and blade" business model, which focuses on the placement of diagnostic hardware to drive recurring sales of consumable reagents, performed well as clinical laboratories expanded their testing menus for infectious diseases and oncological markers. The imaging division also saw growth, driven by the adoption of optical coherence tomography systems used to guide complex interventional procedures.

Our Patient Management segment, though smaller in total revenue contribution, represented our fastest-growing area. Revenue was driven by the expansion of our remote monitoring subscriber base. The integration of artificial intelligence into our cloud-based platforms has allowed us to offer higher-value data insights to clinicians, supporting the transition toward value-based care models.

Cost of Revenue and Gross Margin

Cost of revenue for fiscal 2024 was $606 million, compared to $584 million in fiscal 2023. This increase of $22 million, or 3.8%, was primarily due to higher sales volumes and increased raw material costs for certain specialized components.

Gross profit increased to $1,658 million in fiscal 2024 from $1,509 million in fiscal 2023. Our gross margin improved to 73.2% in fiscal 2024, compared to 72.1% in fiscal 2023. The 110-basis point expansion in gross margin was primarily driven by:

- A favorable shift in product mix toward high-margin diagnostic consumables and digital health subscriptions.
- Manufacturing efficiencies achieved at our Denver, Colorado, production facilities through the implementation of automated assembly processes.
- Improved pricing discipline in our Therapeutic Solutions segment, partially offsetting the pricing pressures exerted by large group purchasing organizations.

Research and Development (R&D)

R&D expense for fiscal 2024 was $923 million, an increase of $69 million, or 8.1%, from $854 million in fiscal 2023. As a percentage of revenue, R&D remained consistent at 40.8% in both fiscal 2024 and 2023.

The increase in absolute R&D spending reflects our ongoing commitment to innovation and the high costs associated with clinical evidence generation. Key areas of investment in 2024 included:

- Clinical trials for our advanced wound closure devices and next-generation stents.
- Development of software algorithms for our Patient Management platforms to enhance predictive analytics.
- Engineering efforts to miniaturize wearable sensors and improve battery life.
- Regulatory filing costs for new diagnostic assays in international markets.

We believe that our sustained high level of R&D investment is essential to maintaining our competitive position in an industry characterized by rapid technological change.

Selling, General and Administrative (SG&A)

SG&A expense for fiscal 2024 was $124 million, an increase of $37 million, or 42.5%, from $87 million in fiscal 2023. As a percentage of revenue, SG&A increased to 5.5% in fiscal 2024 from 4.2% in fiscal 2023.

The increase in SG&A expenses was primarily driven by:

- Expansion of our specialized direct sales force in the United States to support the launch of new therapeutic products.
- Increased marketing and clinical education initiatives, including participation in major medical congresses.
- Higher personnel-related costs, including salaries and benefits for our approximately 2,700 employees.
